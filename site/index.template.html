About the project

Better questions.
Evidence in the open.

EMC Research is an independent computational research project focused on extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma, a rare soft-tissue cancer.

These papers share analyses, hypotheses and their limits to support further research and collaboration. Computational predictions require experimental validation.
