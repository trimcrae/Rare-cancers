Deprioritize development of the transferable external fusion-design benchmark. The [verified investigation](feasibility.md) found useful measured evidence but selectively reported parent outcomes and no complete negative-design panels meeting the contract. This is a judgement about attainable contribution, not an exhaustive claim of literature absence. Preserve the 63 design/condition rows; no fitted model or submission package exists.

Reopen when a concrete new source supplies sufficiently comparable, chemically specified multi-design fusion/both-parent measurements including failures across independent target families. An inaccessible thesis is a named lead, not justification for repeated broad searches. Do not relax the paired-outcome requirement to manufacture a design paper.

The next leading prospect is response-outcome recoverability and decision error, rank 3 in the existing portfolio. Its potential contribution is independently demonstrating when denominator, response-definition or time-rule ambiguity changes a defensible interpretation of a study. The current 552-arm manuscript does not establish that contribution: DCR-minus-ORR arithmetic and calling zero response uninformative are insufficient. Its broader evidence-synthesis utility and attainable source-document validation justify one bounded pilot, while weaker EMC specificity and uncertain novelty remain limitations. Trial discoverability remains lower ranked because a validated denominator and ordinary-search baseline are unestablished. No portfolio rewrite or new process framework is needed.

Next decisive question: can a small prespecified source audit identify reproducible, consequential interpretation changes after correcting response definitions, arm identity, denominators or unevaluable patients, with actual study-defined decision criteria? If it yields only extraction errors or missing-field counts without a distinct methodological contribution, deprioritize it too. Use a new fresh-context worker with its own bounded goal. The coordinator full preprint-readiness goal stays active; ASO/Qeios/NAT package and publication authority remain unchanged.
