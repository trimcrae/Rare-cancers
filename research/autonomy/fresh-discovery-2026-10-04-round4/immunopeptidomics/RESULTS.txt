2026-10-04 20:30 UTC. Bounded direct-immunopeptidomics gate. No new disease finding.

Decision: SHELVE a standalone paper from this pilot. No authenticated EMC HLA-bound peptide measurement was identified in the primary cohorts actually evaluated here. This is a scoped eligibility result, not evidence that EMC presents no antigens or that no public EMC ligandome exists. The independent atlas/repository challenge is interrupted and incomplete, so comprehensive public-evidence coverage cannot be claimed. No biological contrast was run, no statistical result or manuscript produced, and no invented negative hypothesis is advanced as a discovery.

Actual evaluation:
1. Chondrosarcoma2026, DOI10.1016/j.jbo.2026.100780: methods explicitly identify JJ012 and CH2879 as conventional primary central chondrosarcoma, grade2 male39 and grade3 female35, respectively. Both untreated/IFNg-treated model conditions belong to those models. Direct class-I ligand measurements cannot be transferred to EMC. The63 clinical expression cases are conventional chondrosarcoma and do not turn their RNA into measured ligandomes.
2. Pediatric2021, DOI10.1016/j.celrep.2021.110047 / PXD017130: the actual complete peptide supplement contains eight assay tables, HOS/U2OS/SAOS2/A673 each measured by panMHC-I and FLAG:HLA*A2:01 IP. These are osteosarcoma/Ewing models, not EMC. Four models and eight assay tables are not eight independent donors; broad657-patient RNA profiling is not657 ligandome specimens. The repository describes5%FDR; no EMC quantitative pilot was warranted regardless of that threshold.
3. Pleural2022, DOI10.1136/jitc-2021-003733 / PXD027766: all14 individual Table1 patients checked. Nine malignant diagnoses are lung adenocarcinoma, squamous carcinoma or endometrioid carcinoma; five are heart/kidney failure. Pleural fluid, bulk-cell, culture and other conditions remain attached to the same patient. None is labelled EMC. A SAGE1 'sarcoma antigen' peptide does not make its carcinoma donor a sarcoma.
4. PXD046803 / DOI10.1038/s41598-025-96877-9: direct HLA-I extraction is from SK-N-MC/HLA-A*02:01 Ewing cells before/after IFNg. Conditions and six source experiments are not new EMC donors. PXD027309 is Ewing total-proteome digestion with a neo-peptide motivation; prediction of possible HLA presentation is not direct EMC ligand elution. PXD030304 is ProCan949 whole-cell SWATH proteomics, not HLA immunoprecipitation, despite an H-EMC-SS catalogue crosslink.
5. JCI2023 DOI10.1172/jci.insight.170324: one author-labelled EMC101 preserved, male74 primary inguinal lesion, no chemotherapy and radiation862days before surgery. The human epitope analysis is WES/NetMHC prediction, not HLA-eluted MS. TableS2 supplies partially ambiguous inferred HLA types. No individual101 CD91/flow/TCR numerical crosswalk is supplied in inspected text, mainTables1-3 or full15-page supplement; group plots and request-only underlying data cannot support a new EMC immune-context conclusion. NR4A3 confirmation is not supplied in that case row. These limitations do not erase the author's histological EMC label.

Repository trace: all returned PRIDE metadata for sarcoma107 (100+7 pagination), chondrosarcoma7, myxoid5, extraskeletal2 and EMC32, plus exact model/fusion aliases, were retained and screened, yielding149unique project records. Metadata screening is not149 biological dataset evaluations and query silence is not absence of a sample within a broadly labelled study. The explicit exclusions above rest on actual source methods and sample/condition definitions. Model alias queries and indexed literature searches supplement, but do not replace, the interrupted broad-atlas review.

Scientific usefulness: no measured EMC antigen source was demonstrated to change an antigen experiment. The JCI101 HLA row is a published genotype observation with ambiguity and no new presentation/immune-outcome relationship. Normal atlases, other sarcomas, source inventories and a failed discovery search cannot fill the missing EMC measurement. No quantitative pilot should be manufactured from them.

Reopening: an authenticated EMC specimen/model with publicly measured HLA-bound peptide data and interpretable assay/error/HLA provenance; then prospectively specify a biologically useful contrast, all eligible cases/conditions, same-allele and normal controls, donor independence and uncertainty before peptide values. An existing published list alone is insufficient. Pending actual atlas/source interpretation must be resolved before claiming coverage or advancing a paper. This does not close the overall research campaign.
