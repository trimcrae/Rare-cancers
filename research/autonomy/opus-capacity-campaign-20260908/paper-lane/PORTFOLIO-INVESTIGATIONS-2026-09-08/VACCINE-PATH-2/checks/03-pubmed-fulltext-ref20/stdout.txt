Verbatim, Methods "Vaccine design and peptide synthesis":
  "The amino acid sequence spanning the type 1 EWSR1-FLI1 fusion breakpoint (EWSR1:Exon 7 -
   FLI1:Exon 6) was used to generate four overlapping 17-mer peptides by a sliding-window approach,
   limiting contiguous residues from either fusion partner to a maximum of ten. To ensure broad
   applicability, the vaccine was designed without HLA restriction."
Verbatim, Methods "Immune monitoring":
  "This assay primarily detects functional, non-anergic memory T-cells in an HLA-independent manner."
Verbatim, Results "Vaccine-induced T-cell responses":
  "By month 26, polyfunctional responses were observed against all four peptides (E1-E4) ...
   Although a single CD8+ response was transiently detectable, the vaccine-induced immunity was
   dominated by durable CD4+ T-cell activity."
Verbatim, Discussion:
  "The dominance of CD4+ responses and the relative absence of CD8+ reactivity may reflect the class
   II binding properties of the selected peptides and limited class I presentation in Ewing sarcoma."
The E1-E4 sequences themselves are in a Table not carried in the PMC text body; lengths (17) and the
absence of any HLA restriction are stated in the text and are what the admissibility test needs.
