# PUB-STRATEGY-ARCH — what the strategy buys, measured where it can be

⛔ Not medical advice, not a trial-matching service. Everything below is a statement about
registry text on the retrieval dates recorded in the cited deposits. No patient, enrolment,
referral, treatment or outcome is involved, and nothing here says any trial would accept
any patient.

## 1 · The question

The reachability paper's central methodological claim (§4) is a **tradeoff**: a keyword-built
reachability map is worse than no map, because a hit that sends a patient toward a trial whose
posted criteria exclude them costs more than the hit is worth. The paper states this as
*"an argument from the adjudications rather than a quantity measured here."*

**Question.** Over the records this programme has actually adjudicated by reading their posted
eligibility text, what are the **error rates** of the two screens the paper contrasts — a
histology-name screen (what a patient runs) and a molecular-language screen (what the paper
recommends building) — and which half of the paper's asymmetry argument does public registry
data support at all?

## 2 · Paper-level merit

Patient relevance is direct and needs no bench: the paper's remedies (§6) tell tool-builders to
read criteria rather than match strings, and that instruction is currently backed by two worked
anecdotes. Turning it into a **screen-error benchmark with a stated denominator** is the
difference between an argument and an instrument specification — and it is the form other
people can reuse, because the ground truth is per-record and the screen predicate is explicit.
The non-trivial contribution is that the two halves of the tradeoff turn out to have
**different evidentiary status**: the *rate* is measurable from committed data today, and the
*cost* is not measurable from registry data at all, by any amount of further fetching.

## 3 · The exact evidence gap, and what makes it new

The programme has adjudicated eligibility text for these records one at a time
(`fet-fusion-trial-eligibility-2026-08-07.json`, `emc-trial-reachability-adjudication-2026-08-09.json`)
and has reported them as **counts** — "two admit, two refuse". Nothing has ever scored a screen
**against** those adjudications, so §4's tradeoff has no numerator, no denominator and no
lexicon. That is distinct from held/closed work in this endpoint: RT-SCHEDULING is closed
`definitional` (a pool the evidence contract refuses), RT-SEQUENCING closed `instrument_limit`
(a record that cannot support an ordering); neither concerns the reachability instrument.
It is also distinct from the 2026-09-05 portfolio recommendation, whose five ranked prospects
are all expression / mechanism / degrader work and none of which touches trial reachability.

## 4 · The bounded step taken

Built a per-record **keyword-screen benchmark** from the two committed deposits and scored three
a-priori lexicons against the eligibility-read verdicts, in two strata.

**Artifact.** `keyword-screen-benchmark.json` (8 scored records, per-record visible fields, hits,
verdict and the deposit field that licenses each label), generated by
`keyword_screen_benchmark.py`; ground truth checked by `verify_ground_truth.py`.

**Screen predicate.** Case-insensitive substring match over `brief_title` + `official_title` +
`listed_conditions` only — the fields a string matcher sees. Eligibility text is deliberately
withheld from the screen. Ground truth = the admit/refuse verdict reached by *reading* the
posted eligibility text, transcribed from the deposit.

### Result

| screen | stratum | n | TP | FP | TN | FN | precision | recall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| histology-name | all adjudicated | 8 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 4 | undefined | **0.00** |
| molecular-language | all adjudicated | 8 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 | **0.50** | 0.75 |
| union | all adjudicated | 8 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 | 0.50 | 0.75 |
| histology-name | recruiting **and** interventional | 5 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 2 | undefined | **0.00** |
| molecular-language | recruiting **and** interventional | 5 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 0 | **0.50** | **1.00** |

**Two findings.**

1. **The paper's core claim now has a rate, not just instances.** Across every adjudicated
   record, a histology-name screen returns **none** of the four whose posted criteria admit this
   disease — recall 0.00. On the slice a patient could actually join (recruiting and
   interventional) it misses both admitting trials.
2. **The recommended fix carries a measured error rate of one in two.** A molecular-language
   screen recovers every admitting recruiting interventional record in this pool (recall 1.00)
   and **half of what it returns would refuse the patient on posted criteria** (precision 0.50;
   the false positives are NCT06094101 and NCT07188532, the paper's two adjudicated refusals).
   So §4's warning is not rhetorical: it is the *modal* outcome of the screen §6 recommends
   building, and any such tool needs the per-trial read as a second stage rather than as advice.

**Validation.** `verify_ground_truth.py` re-derives every label from the deposit's own wording
and exits non-zero on any mismatch — 8/8 licensed, 0 failures (`checks/04-…`). The screen is
falsifiable by construction: a lexicon change re-scores it, which is why three lexicons are
reported rather than one.

**Provenance.** Inputs are the two committed deposit files named in §7 of the manuscript.
Nothing was fetched. No shared file, graph, registry or acceptance criterion was touched; all
writes are inside this lane directory.

## 5 · Limitations — stated, not managed

* **n = 8, and it is a floor, not a sample.** The candidate pool came from screens one of which
  returned at its page limit. These are rates *over the adjudicated set*, not registry-wide
  rates, and no confidence interval is quoted because the set is not a random sample of anything.
* **The histology recall of 0.00 is scoped to the adjudicated set.** NCT06239272 — the recruiting
  trial that *does* list the histology among 42 conditions — is deliberately **not** in the set,
  because these two deposits carry no eligibility-read verdict for it. Including it would give
  the histology screen a true positive. The manuscript already carries that counterexample, and
  this benchmark must not be read as contradicting it.
* **A registry record is not a protocol**, and an "admits" label is a reading of posted criteria,
  never a trial team's decision.
* **The cost half of the asymmetry is not measured here and cannot be.** See §6.

## 6 · The genuine no-go, and the actual missing dependency

Two adjacent questions were attempted and are recorded as **negative**:

* **The instrument question the manuscript flags as untested** — whether `query.term` reaches
  eligibility text, and whether a free-text search on the disease name would surface
  NCT05918640 / NCT06571734 — **could not be run.** `clinicaltrials.gov:443` is refused at this
  sandbox's egress proxy (`CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403`, `checks/01-…`). No offline
  substitute exists: the repository holds **URL manifests but no stored registry payloads**, so
  the index behaviour cannot be reconstructed from committed data. The missing dependency is
  concrete and small — one CI-routed fetch of two `query.term` requests — and it is named here
  rather than worked around; no refusal was proxied or reworded.
* **The cost of a false hit is not a registry quantity at all.** Precision is measurable;
  what a wrong hit costs the person it reaches requires referral, contact or enrolment outcome
  data that no public trial registry holds. Further registry fetching cannot close it, and this
  programme should stop implying that it might. The honest form of §4 is: *a measured
  50% refusal rate among the map's own hits, and an unmeasured cost per refusal.*

## 7 · Stop condition

Stop here. The benchmark is complete over every record with an eligibility-read verdict, and the
denominator cannot grow without new per-trial retrievals, which this sandbox cannot perform.
Do **not** enlarge the set by labelling unadjudicated records from titles — that is the exact
failure the benchmark measures. Next credible work, in order: (1) route the two `query.term`
probes through the existing `fetch-literature.yml` CI path and re-score; (2) if and only if that
succeeds, extend adjudication to the nine molecularly-defined records so the denominator grows
by reading rather than by inference.

## 8 · Unapplied suggestion for the manuscript (NOT applied)

§4's sentence *"That asymmetry is an argument from the adjudications rather than a quantity
measured here"* is now half-measurable. A one-line replacement is available if the paper owner
wants it, but **no edit was made** to `research/manuscripts/care-delivery/emc-trial-reachability.md`
or to any shared file; this lane owns none of them.
