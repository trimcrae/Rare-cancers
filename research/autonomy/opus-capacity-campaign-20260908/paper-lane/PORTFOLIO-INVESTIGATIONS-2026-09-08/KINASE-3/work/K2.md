# KINASE-2 — label-permutation null for the two-platform concordance argument

Baseline `git rev-parse HEAD` at start of work: `a11a1ea76be68909ae90a568742fb5d19e0713cf`
(2026-09-09T00:11:02Z). The sole input, `research/modalities/emc-expression-panels.json`, is
**byte-identical** to the copy lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS` read at its baseline `b6baa1b7`
(`git diff b6baa1b7 HEAD -- <path>` is empty), so the two nulls are built on the same numbers.
Writes confined to this directory. No `git add`/`commit`/`push`, no `preflight.sh`, no subagent,
no worktree, no repo copy, no network, no GPU, no paid compute.

**Declared before any number was computed** (`PREREGISTRATION.md`, written 00:11Z):
**2000 permutations, seed 20260909**, arm sizes held fixed, primary frame = the same 431 genes,
statistics fixed in advance, **no outcome preferred**. **Measured runtime of the full generator:
3.2 s** (2000 permutations × 2 platforms × 2 frames), well inside the "permutation over committed
summary matrices, not a heavy simulation" bound.

## 1 · The question this lane was given

Lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS` measured that **65.2% of randomly drawn genes already move the same way in
EMC on both array platforms**, and could not distinguish two explanations:

* **(a)** the two series are simply correlated with each other — shared batch, shared tissue
  composition, shared probe behaviour — so *any* gene tends to agree;
* **(b)** EMC is a coherent contrast and agreement carries real signal.

Permuting the EMC-vs-comparator **labels** within each platform destroys (b) while leaving the
gene-level data, the missingness pattern, the arm sizes and each platform's own structure intact.
The gap between the two nulls is the quantity nobody had.

## 2 · What was run

`label_permutation_null.py` (this directory), one read-only pass over the committed artifact.
Per permutation the EMC/comparator labels are reassigned at random within each platform
independently — **GPL6244 6 EMC / 29 comparator, GPL3290 10 EMC / 6 comparator**, sizes fixed,
missing values staying attached to their sample — and the Welch t, the direction-concordance rate
and the joint statistic are recomputed. Lead genes keep the observed t values lane
`PUB-KINASE-LEADS` used (`genome_wide_null.placed_wanted_genes`), so only the *null* changes.

Reproduction is exact: `checks/06-reproduce-lane1-gene-resampling/` compares this lane's
gene-resampling column against lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS`'s committed artifact field by field —
**431 genes, 281 concordant, 0.652, and all thirteen placed joint p values agree to <1e-9;
0 mismatches, exit 0.**

## 3 · ⭐ Result 1 — the 65.2% is the contrast, not the correlation

| Frame | statistic | **gene-resampling null** (real labels, random genes) | **label-permutation null** (real genes, random labels) |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary (431 genes in both background draws) | direction-concordance rate | **0.6520** (281/431) | **0.4990** mean, sd 0.0374, 95% [0.4226, 0.5724], **max over 2000 permutations 0.6328** |
| primary | Pearson r(t GPL6244, t GPL3290) | **+0.3869** (Spearman +0.3956) | **−0.0011** mean (97.5th pct +0.169) |
| secondary (413 curated per-sample genes) | concordance rate | **0.6344** (262/413) | **0.5022** mean, sd 0.0426, max 0.6320 |
| secondary | Pearson r | +0.4248 | +0.0027 |

**No permutation out of 2000, on either frame, reached the observed concordance**
(p ≤ 0.0005, i.e. (0+1)/(2000+1)); the observed value is ~4.1 sd above the permuted mean.
Seed stability (`checks/04-…`, five seeds × 500 permutations): permuted mean stays in
**0.4957–0.5013** — the gap is not a sampling accident.

**Explanation (a) is refuted in its literal form.** With the labels shuffled, the two platforms'
gene-level t statistics are **uncorrelated** (r ≈ 0). Nothing about these two series makes an
arbitrary gene agree across them; the agreement exists only when the real EMC-vs-comparator
labels are in place. The 65.2% is a property of the **contrast**, not of the series pair.

⚠ **What kind of contrast, answered in part (lane `EXPR-COMPOSITION`, 2026-09-09).** That the 65.2 %
is contrast-borne does not make it EMC biology. Holding a frozen 20-marker tumour/stroma composition
score constant takes the primary frame to **0.5668** (56.1 % of the excess over 0.5 removed) and the
secondary frame to **0.5981** (28.3 %); cross-platform Pearson r falls 0.405 → 0.251 and
0.447 → 0.281. The unadjusted values in the table above are the correct description of the raw
contrast and are retained. The residual **survives** (adjusted-null p = 0.022 and 0.0085 against the
unadjusted p ≤ 0.0005), so this attenuates the reading and does **not** refute it. ⛔ Composition and
biology are not separable here even in principle — a stromal score in a mesenchymal tumour is partly
the tumour's own programme — so **0.561 is an upper bound on the composition share, not an estimate,
and the surviving p values are conservative.**

## 5 · ⭐ Result 3 — what this does to lane PUB-KINASE-LEADS's conclusions

**Neither pre-named branch occurred.** The label-permutation null neither resembles the
gene-resampling null nor is flatter than it: it is **much steeper** (permuted concordance 0.499 vs
observed 0.652; permuted joint p far smaller). The pre-registered dichotomy assumed the 65.2%
baseline might be nuisance correlation. It is not. This is reported as found.

**Survives, and is now explained rather than merely observed.** *"Concordant on both platforms" is
not by itself evidence for a gene.* Two of three genes clear it — and we now know **why**: the
EMC-vs-comparator contrast in these designs is **broad**, moving a large fraction of the
transcriptome consistently in both series. ⚠ And at most about half of that breadth, on the primary
frame, is attributable to a frozen tumour/stroma composition score (concordance 0.6521 → 0.5668);
the rest survives the adjustment at p = 0.022. The operational consequence for the paper is unchanged:
every unqualified "higher in EMC on both platforms" still needs a strength term and a p beside it.

**Strengthens.** The 431-gene frame is doing honest work: because its baseline is real shared
signal, **the gene-resampling null is conservative, not lenient**. RET at p = 0.0742 and NDRG1 at
p = 0.0882 are being judged against a bar that already contains the effect they are being tested
for. Any referee objection that the concordance null was rigged against the leads by an artefactual
65% baseline is answered — the baseline is earned. ⚠ **Qualified, 2026-09-09:** "earned" means
label-borne, not composition-free. Up to 56.1 % of the primary-frame excess is carried by a frozen
tumour/stroma marker score, so part of the bar the leads are judged against is a composition
contrast; the bar remains conservative for the leads either way, and the residual contrast survives
adjustment (p = 0.022). Also strengthened: **GFRA2 remains the most
extreme gene in the set under both nulls** (p = 0.039 and 0.00006), so lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS`'s
finding that *the RET route's negative arm is better supported than its positive arm* is
null-independent.

**Weakens — a correction this lane owes to lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS`.** That lane wrote that the
instrument "cannot cleanly resolve even the disease-defining driver," reading NR4A3's p = 0.156 as
a **sensitivity** ceiling. Against a no-contrast null, NR4A3 reaches **p = 0.0083** — the instrument
does detect its shift, reliably. The correct statement is a **specificity/crowding** ceiling: NR4A3
is real but **ordinary**, sitting behind an estimated **~2,200 of the 14,403 genes readable on the
smaller platform** that are at least as concordant and at least as strong. The same re-reading
applies to RET (**~1,070 genes** ahead of or level with it) and NDRG1 (**~1,270**). The bound on the
expression arm is not "too noisy to see anything"; it is **"too many genes move for any one of them
to be attributed to anything."** That is a different sentence, and the sharper one.

**Unchanged.** SGK1, ROS1 and the Ku subunits remain indistinguishable from ordinary genes under
both nulls (SGK1 discordant, p 0.383 / 0.122). ALK remains an absent reading on GPL3290.

## 6 · The residual ambiguity this permutation does *not* resolve — stated, not worked around

Label permutation destroys **every** contrast-level explanation at once, including confounding
ones. It therefore splits explanation (a) into two, and closes only the first:

* **(a1) label-independent inter-series correlation** — shared probe behaviour, shared array
  processing that would make any gene agree regardless of grouping. **Refuted:** permuted
  r(t1, t2) ≈ 0.
* **(a2) a shared contrast-level confound** — e.g. both series' EMC arms being matrix-rich,
  low-cellularity myxoid tissue against differently composed comparator arms (LGFMS/desmoid/
  fibrosarcoma vs DFSP/GIST). This is **preserved by the real labels and destroyed by permutation
  exactly as EMC biology is**, so this analysis **cannot** separate it from (b). **It remains
  open**, and it is the more plausible driver of a contrast broad enough to move two genes in
  three. Nothing here licenses reading a small label-permutation p as EMC-specific biology.
  ⚠ **Partly closed, 2026-09-09, and against the biological reading** — lane `EXPR-COMPOSITION`
  measured a per-sample frozen 20-marker composition proxy (Spearman with the EMC label −0.578 on
  GPL6244, −0.504 on GPL3290) and held it constant: primary-frame concordance 0.6521 → 0.5668,
  secondary 0.6368 → 0.5981, with a passed negative control (a permuted score returns 0.6469,
  sd 0.0135; 0 of 500 shuffled draws reached 0.5668). The residual survives at p = 0.022 / 0.0085.
  ⛔ (a2) is **bounded, not eliminated**: the proxy is not a measured cell fraction, and because a
  stromal programme in a mesenchymal tumour is partly the tumour's own, the 56.1 % figure is an
  **upper bound** on the composition share and the surviving p values are **conservative**.

and per lead gene, with the crowding scaling and the seed-stability block.
**Generators.** `label_permutation_null.py` (both nulls), `seed_stability_and_crowding.py`,

**Validation / baselines.** (1) Exact reproduction of lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS`'s gene-resampling
column, 0 mismatches (`checks/06`). (2) Independent secondary frame — 413 curated genes with
committed per-sample z, a different frame *and* a different data path into the same series —
reproduces both nulls (0.6344 vs 0.5022) and the whole qualitative picture. (3) Seed stability
across five seeds (`checks/04`). (4) Regeneration with the crowding column changed **no**
previously reported value (`checks/05`, diff shows additions only). (5) Lane
`PUB-KINASE-LEADS`'s own validation of the recomputed Welch t against the artifact's genome-wide
null (max |Δt| 0.002 / 0.001) carries over unchanged, since the statistic is the same code path.

**Provenance.** Sole input `research/modalities/emc-expression-panels.json`, `generated_utc`
2026-08-29T12:51:32+00:00, committed, read at HEAD `a11a1ea7`; background seeds
`20260829:GSE24369_series_matrix.txt.gz` and `20260829:GSE4303-GPL3290_series_matrix.txt.gz`.
No new data, no network call, no GPU, no paid API.

**Limitations.**
- The permuted concordance mean of 0.499 is close to the **analytic** expectation of 0.5 under
  sign symmetry; the permutation's real contributions are the *dispersion* (sd 0.037, so the
  observed 0.652 is ~4.1 sd out and beyond all 2000 draws) and the pooled min|t| distribution that
  places the leads. It is not a surprising number, and it is not presented as one.
- **GPL3290's exact label space is only C(16,10) = 8008 assignments**; 2000 draws hit 1768 distinct
  ones. The permutation null there is a sample of a small exact space, so its tail is granular.
  GPL6244's space is 1,623,160 (1997 distinct drawn).
- **Resolution.** The gene-resampling p cannot resolve below ~1/431 = 0.0023. The
  label-permutation p is pooled over 858,122 draws but those draws are **not independent** — 431
  genes reappear in every permutation, and genes on an array are correlated — so its small values
  (6e-05) are *not* 1-in-16,000-style evidence and must not be read as such.
- The **crowding counts (~568 … ~8622) are extrapolations**, the gene-resampling rate scaled by the
  smaller platform's 14,403 scored symbols. They are an order-of-magnitude statement, **not** a
  multiplicity correction; none is applied anywhere here, by design.
- **No multiple-testing correction.** Thirteen genes were placed; that is not adjusted for.
- Every value is **transcript abundance** from **bulk archival tissue**, undeconvolved. It is silent
  on activation, phosphorylation, protein, and drug response, and cannot address lead 1's
  "activated", lead 4's IHC, or any IC50.
- The GSE24369 comparator-count discrepancy flagged by P4 (memo 36, artifact 29) is **unresolved**;
  this analysis uses the artifact's own `comparator_gsms` (n = 29), as lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS` did,
  and does not adjudicate the memo.
- Nothing here establishes efficacy, safety, selectivity, a therapeutic window, target attribution
  or clinical readiness for any agent in any disease, and nothing here is advice about a patient.
- Harmless `RuntimeWarning: Mean of empty slice` lines appear in stderr: on GPL3290, 1252 of 3000
  background genes have at least one missing sample, so some permutations leave a group empty for
  a gene. Those genes are excluded from that permutation by the `n >= 2` validity mask, which is
  the same rule the observed computation uses. Preserved in `checks/*/stderr.txt`.

**Stop condition (set in the prereg, before running).** Stop once both nulls are computed on the
primary frame, the recomputed observed statistics reproduce lane `PUB-KINASE-LEADS` exactly, and
the secondary frame either reproduces or breaks the headline — or stop immediately and report the
discrepancy if reproduction failed. **Met, on the reproducing branch.**
