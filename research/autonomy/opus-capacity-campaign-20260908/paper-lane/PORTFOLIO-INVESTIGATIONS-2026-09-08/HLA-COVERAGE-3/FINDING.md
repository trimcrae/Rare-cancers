# HLA-COVERAGE-3 — the drift is purely additive, its direction is fixed, its size is UNKNOWN, and it would break one generated table rather than merely date it

Follow-through on **HLA-COVERAGE-2**, which found the producer/artifact divergence; grounded in
**PUB-HLA-COVERAGE** (the AFND mirror snapshot), **EPITOPE-BENCHMARK** (n = 15 validated
fusion-junction class I epitopes) and **NEOANTIGEN-3** (the panel omits HLA-C).

Writes confined to this directory. Nothing added, committed or pushed; `scripts/preflight.sh` not
run; **no producer executed**; no manuscript, graph entry or committed artifact modified; no
subagents; no network; **$0**.

⛔ **Route B8 (HLA-C) is CLOSED and was not approached.** The producer was not run over HLA-C or
over anything else. No HLA-C frequency was fetched, proxied, substituted or invented; none exists in
this checkout. Every quantity requiring one is **UNKNOWN**, never zero.

⛔ **No claim about immunogenicity, presentation, efficacy, safety, selectivity, therapeutic window
or clinical readiness is made anywhere in this lane.**

## 1 · The question

> `research/modalities/coverage_scan.py` now defines a 52-allele panel while every committed
> artifact records 34. Without running the producer: exactly which committed values would still
> reproduce, which would change, and which cannot be determined offline — and in which direction?

## 2 · Merit

The vaccine-path paper's §8 states that every figure in Sections 2 and 3 "is generated by a script
in `research/modalities/` and is committed as a JSON artifact beside it, with one exception, named
because a blanket claim with an exception in it is worse than no claim". That is the paper's own
standard, and under the current producer it has a second exception. A reader who runs the committed
script to check the committed numbers gets different numbers, and nothing in the paper warns them.
Establishing precisely **which** numbers, and how far the divergence propagates, converts a vague
reproducibility worry into a bounded, checkable statement a paper owner can act on. Patient
relevance is indirect and honest: this changes whether a coverage figure is checkable, not whether
any patient benefits.

## 3 · The evidence gap, and what distinguishes it from prior work

HLA-COVERAGE-2 **observed** the divergence in one paragraph and stopped there — deliberately, since
resolving it by running the producer is route B8. It did not enumerate the consumers, did not
establish any direction, and did not test whether the committed 34 is a subset of the current 52 or
a different list. The unfinished inputs, named exactly: `PANEL_AB`/`PANEL_C` in `coverage_scan.py`;
`panel` in `epitope-allele-matrix.json` and `epitope-allele-loose-matrix.json`; `panel_size` in
`coverage-curve.json`; `n_panel` in `predictor-concordance.json`; `_panel` in
`vaccine-threshold-calibration.json`; `panel()` in `predictor_concordance.py`; `panel_block()` in
`research/manuscripts/vaccine_path_tables.py`; and the two guards in
`research/manuscripts/tests/test_vaccine_path_numbers.py`.

## 4 · Step taken

Two offline generators over read-only inputs. `rederive.py` re-derives HLA-COVERAGE-2's three key
facts from the underlying artifacts with an independent implementation of the coverage formula.
`staleness_map.py` parses `coverage_scan.py` with `ast` — **never importing and never executing it**
— reads every committed consumer, and emits a 48-row per-key verdict table.

## 5 · Results

### (a) All three of HLA-COVERAGE-2's key facts reproduce — `checks/01`, exit 0

1. **The ten-allele MHCflurry panel**, verbatim from `_predictor.alleles` in
   `research/modalities/fusion-breakpoint-neoantigens.json` (sha256 `ae1ba4f7…`, 46,191 bytes):
   `HLA-A*01:01, A*02:01, A*03:01, A*11:01, A*24:02, B*07:02, B*08:01, B*15:01, B*35:01, B*44:02`.
   **n = 10, loci A and B, HLA-C count 0.** MHCflurry 2.1.4, models release 2.2.0, lengths 8–11,
   strong ≤ 0.5 presentation percentile. Reproduces.
2. **The 34-vs-52 divergence.** Producer `PANEL_AB` = 34, `PANEL_C` = 18, `PANEL` = **52** (loci
   A/B/C). `epitope-allele-matrix.json` `panel` = **34** (A/B, and `alleles_without_a_model` absent);
   `epitope-allele-loose-matrix.json` `panel` = **34**; `coverage-curve.json` `panel_size` = **34**.
   Reproduces.
3. **The headline numbers.** Rebuilding both allele sets from the breakpoint records' own `binders`
   list and applying `1 − ∏(1 − af)²` to `hla-coverage.json`'s own frequencies:
   `coverage_e7e3_public` committed **0.0851**, re-derived **0.0851**;
   `coverage_any_strong_binder_allele` committed **0.2737**, re-derived **0.2737**. Both reproduce
   digit for digit, and both allele sets match the committed lists exactly.

**One correction of emphasis, not of digits.** These two headline numbers are **not** downstream of
`coverage_scan.py` at all — they come from `hla_coverage.py` and the ten-allele junction screen. The
drift cannot move them. The broad-panel figures (30.4%, 12.3%, 2.0%) are the ones at risk.

### (b) The drift is purely additive — a fact no prior lane established

The committed 34-allele panel is **element-for-element identical to the producer's current
`PANEL_AB`** (`checks/01`, `producer_AB_equals_committed_panel: true`). No A/B allele was changed,
reordered or removed; the entire divergence is the appended `PANEL_C` block. This is what makes the
directional argument in (d) available at all.

### (c) The staleness map — `STALENESS-MAP.md` and `staleness-map.json`, 48 rows

**REPRODUCES 4 · CHANGES 20 · CHANGES_DIRECTION 20 · UNDETERMINABLE 3 · AFFECTED_CLAIM 1.**
Four tiers: what `coverage_scan.py` writes; what reads what it writes (a **second-order** chain — 
`coverage_threshold_curve.py`, `predictor_concordance.py`, `vaccine_threshold_calibration.py` and
`stage0_vaccine_item_provenance.py` all take the panel from the committed matrix, not from

* **§7's decoy-null denominator changes deterministically.** "0.644% of **59,160** peptide-allele

  The **rate** 0.644% over a C-inclusive panel is UNKNOWN.
* **`test_vaccine_path_numbers.py` is the guard that would catch this**, binding
  `r"a (\d+)-allele screen of the same peptides"` to `len(matrix["panel"])` at every site. It must
  not be weakened. Its sibling `test_the_broad_panel_adds_exactly_the_allele_the_paper_says_it_adds`

### (d) What the change does to the arithmetic — direction fixed, magnitude UNKNOWN

Read from the source, not run:

* `predict_matrix()` calls `predictor.predict(alleles={a: [a] for a in usable})` — **one
  single-allele sample per allele**. Each row's `best_allele` is that allele and its percentile is
  calibrated per allele, so **adding panel alleles adds rows and cannot alter or remove an existing
  one**. `strong_binders` and `presenting_alleles` are therefore **monotone non-decreasing** in the
  panel.
* `greedy_curve()` keeps only alleles with a non-`None` AFND frequency and accumulates
  `1 − ∏(1 − af)²`, which is **non-decreasing**. So `global_max_coverage`, the threshold curve's
  value at 0.5, and every derived coverage percentage can **rise or stay equal, and cannot fall**.
* **The magnitude is UNKNOWN and cannot be made known here.** It needs HLA-C allele frequencies.
  `coverage-threshold-curve.json`'s `allele_frequencies` block is A/B only; there is no HLA-C
  frequency table anywhere in this checkout; acquiring one is route B8. **UNKNOWN, not zero.**
* **The hinge is `alleles_without_a_model`.** If MHCflurry 2.1.4 carries no HLA-C models, all 18 C
  alleles are excluded before scoring and every `CHANGES_DIRECTION` row resolves to *unchanged* —
  while the artifacts still differ by the panel, the `_note` and two newly emitted keys. If it does
  carry them, the direction is up by an unknown amount. **Which is the case is UNKNOWN** and
  settling it is route B8. Note the producer's own comment already refuses the other error: an
  allele with no model "IS NOT AN ALLELE THAT FAILS TO PRESENT".

### (e) One latent defect, found by reading the generator

`vaccine_path_tables.panel_block()` renders Table 2's header as
`f"The {len(panel)}-allele class I panel"` — it would read **52** — but the body emits **only**
`HLA-A` and `HLA-B` rows; there is no HLA-C branch. The 18 C alleles would be **silently dropped**,
so the table would list **34 alleles under a 52-allele heading**, and the caption's hard-coded
"it carries no HLA-C and no class II allele" would become **false** with nothing to catch it. This
should be fixed **before** any re-run, not after.

### (f) Ordering remains UNKNOWN

All six files land in the squashed `-s ours` merge `14a3f172d`. Whether the producer was broadened
after the artifacts were written, or the artifacts came from an older producer, **cannot be dated
from this history**, and no ordering is asserted. Independently corroborating: `systems/graph/artifact-refs.json`'s
`coverage-scan.json` disposition entry, read 2026-09-08, already **withdraws** an earlier claim that
re-running `coverage_scan.py` offline reproduces these two artifacts byte-for-byte. This lane is the
concrete reason that withdrawal was right.
