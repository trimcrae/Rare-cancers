# DEGRADER-3 — dependency resolution on the covalent axis's missing reference

## 0 · Scope ceiling, stated first

RSA is a geometric quantity and a competition ratio is a mass-spectrometry intensity ratio.
**Nothing in this lane is an EMC efficacy, safety, selectivity, therapeutic-window or
clinical-readiness claim.** There is no wet lab. No docking, no structure prediction, no new
sampling, no GPU; the roadmap's no-GPU deferral was not touched. No shared file was edited.

## 1 · The question

DEGRADER-2 closed the exposure question and named the one remaining dependency:
**`STOP_NO_REFERENCE`, the missing covalent-ligandability reference.** So: *what exactly is that
reference — the quantity, its units, the comparison it would enable — and is any part of it
obtainable through the admitted PubMed/PMC route?* A well-evidenced "no" is a valid answer.

## 2 · Paper-level merit

The covalent axis is the only selectivity axis in this paper that does not inherit the free-energy
engine's unvalidated status, and DEGRADER-2 sharpened it to an exact number (dominance 0.9993). That
number is a **geometric ranking** and the paper knows it. The instrument that adjudicates the axis,
`V17` / `C7`, has exactly one positive control and **fails** it. Until a labelled reference exists,
the axis cannot rise above "geometry only" no matter how many frames are counted — so specifying the
reference precisely, and settling by attempt rather than by assumption which parts of it are
reachable, is the highest-value $0 step left on this program. Patient relevance is indirect but real:
this is the axis a future NR4A3 degrader's paralogue-sparing argument would be designed around.

## 3 · The exact evidence gap

* **Round 1 (PUB-DEGRADER)** split published quantiles by replica. **Round 2 (DEGRADER-2)** recomputed
  RSA per frame from 225 committed conformers and falsified the no-overlap sentence. **Both** ended by

* **The gap this lane addresses:** `STOP_NO_REFERENCE` was recorded as a verdict string. Its
  **quantity was never defined from a retrieved source**, its **bound was never stated** (how many
  tables were actually read, and how many were not), and **no one had tested whether the admitted

* **Not re-opened:** every free-energy construct, the `C7` cutoff itself, R1–R4, B1/B2/B4/B8/B9, the
  `metad` and 8XTT subsets, the deferred degrader-optimisation prospect. No new sampling of any kind.

## 5 · Step taken, part B — the reference, specified

Artifact: `artifacts/missing-covalent-ligandability-reference.json`.

**The quantity.** A **competition ratio `R`** — dimensionless. Retrieved verbatim from the aggregate's
open-access article: ligandability is *"generated by comparing relative labeling by equimolar
iodoacetamide in the presence and absence of electrophilic compound"*, and *"cysteines were
categorized as liganded if they had at least two ratios R ≥ 4 (hit fragments) and one ratio between
0.5 and 2 (control fragments)"* — with the cutoff study-specific (3 in one constituent study, 5 in
another). The secondary quantity is a reactivity ratio from a 100 µM / 10 µM probe comparison, where
*"Hyperreactive cysteines are defined as those with R values <2"*. `R` is **not** an affinity, a rate
constant or a free energy, and is **not commensurable with an RSA**.

**The join key.** `UniProtKBID_CYS#` at Q92570 (NR4A3) C397/C420/C559, P22736 (NR4A1) C551/C505,
P43354 (NR4A2) C534 — the residue set already fixed in `cys_chemoproteomics_precheck.py`.

**The comparison it would enable.** A labelled known-answer set for `V17`, converting the C551 false
negative from a standalone anecdote into a **measured recovery rate** with a denominator, and
allowing the threshold-free rank to be scored as a classifier.

**What the manuscript cannot say now:** that `V17`/`C7` has any measured sensitivity or specificity;
that C397's 0.9993 dominance corresponds to any measured ligandability; that the paralogues' low
exposure corresponds to measured non-ligandability. **What it could say with the reference:** V17's
recovery rate on n labelled family cysteines; whether C397 is covered by any public study at all — a
coverage result publishable in either direction. **What it still would not license:** anything about
adduct formation, cellular selectivity, degradation, efficacy, safety or a therapeutic window. A
`REFERENCE_FOUND` outcome would establish only that a known-answer set exists (roadmap §2.3 ceiling).

## 8 · What would be required, by which route, and is that route closed

**Minimum sufficient read:** the aggregate's `Ligandable` / `Identifiers` rows (or Nat Biotechnol 2020
S4/S7) filtered to the three accessions, returning per matched cysteine the `UniProtKBID_CYS#` site,
the identified / hyperreactive / ligandable flags, and the competition ratio `R` with its study of
origin and that study's own cutoff. **A sufficient negative** is the same read returning zero rows
across all nine constituent studies — a reference-absent result *with a denominator*, strictly
stronger than today's verdict and publishable on its own.

**The one route that could do it** is the GitHub Actions CI escape hatch, which the precheck artifact
already names: *"Dispatch this same script from a GitHub Actions runner, which has unrestricted
egress. The source registry is already resolved … so the follow-up is a mechanical dispatch."*

**Is it closed? Not globally — closed to this lane, and the distinction is the finding.** A dispatch
needs a commit/push or an Actions write, which this lane is forbidden; CI dispatch is shared state,
reserved to the parent; and direct egress is fenced here. **The blocker is authority and lane scope,
not money and not feasibility** — the dispatch is $0, no GPU, no paid API. It is a live, cheap,
mechanical follow-up for whoever owns shared state, and it is the *only* identified route to a value.
