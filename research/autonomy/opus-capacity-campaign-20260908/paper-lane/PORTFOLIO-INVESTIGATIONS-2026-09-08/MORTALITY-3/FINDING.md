# MORTALITY-3 — re-derive first, then write the diff nobody had written

## 1. The question

**Do MORTALITY-2's load-bearing numbers reproduce under an independent re-run, and if they do, what
exact unapplied change to `research/manuscripts/emc-mortality-mechanisms-paper.md` §4.2 states the
composition-effect result honestly without reviving anything already rejected?**

## 2. Merit

MORTALITY-2 reached a decisive result — the reported EMC-vs-comparator excess in terminal-event
mentions is a **composition effect**, not a reporting difference — and then wrote **no manuscript
diff**. A finding that never reaches the prose it corrects does not change what a reader is told.
The manuscript's §4.2 still generalises from §3.1 to *ultra-rare cancers generally*; the only
comparator ever measured does not support that generalisation in either direction. Getting that
sentence right costs a paragraph and prevents a reviewer-visible overclaim. Nothing here touches a
patient, a cause of death, a survival curve or a clinical decision.

## 3. The evidence gap this closes

Two gaps, both concrete:

1. **MORTALITY-2's numbers had never been re-derived by anyone else.** A single lane wrote the
   classifier, ran it, and reported the result. This lane re-ran it from copied-and-hash-verified
   sources into its own capture directory.
2. **No diff existed.** Both sibling lanes explicitly say so ("This lane wrote no diff to the
   manuscript"). The paper owner had a recommendation in prose and nothing applicable.

## 4. Step 1 — independent re-derivation. **All six load-bearing numbers reproduce, digit for digit.**

The six MORTALITY-2 scripts and `handcheck-labels.json` were copied into this lane and hashed
against the originals **before** running (`frozen-source-hashes.txt`, `checks/01`–`06`). All seven
files are byte-identical; the frozen classifier's sha256 is
`9f00b7243bfb43fd4cba126144da712d93e143a62a7fba6ab3ad82cceb5be3a3`, which is the value MORTALITY-2
reports, and `genre_stratified_rates.py` re-printed that same hash at analysis time in this lane's
own run. Scripts write beside themselves, which is why they were run from a copy: **no MORTALITY-2
file or capture was written to or overwritten** (`checks/07` compares, it does not replace).

Beyond agreement to the printed digits, **all six regenerated JSON artifacts are byte-identical to
MORTALITY-2's** (`checks/07`), including the seeded misclassification simulation and the 162-paper
reclassification sweep. Nothing was re-tuned; no lexicon, matcher or threshold was touched.

**One wording nuance, recorded rather than smoothed over.** The artifact's own field shows
`"strata_used": 2` for the strict instrument's "five-stratum" test: three of the five strata contain
zero flagged sentences on both sides, carry no information, and are dropped by the exact conditional
convolution as degenerate. The test *is* stratified on the five pre-specified study types and the
p-value is unchanged by this; but "five-stratum" describes the stratification, not the number of
informative strata. The diff below therefore says "stratified on the five study types" rather than
implying five informative comparisons. This is a labelling nuance, **not** a discrepancy — no number
failed to reproduce.

## 5. Step 2 — the diff. `section-4.2-composition-effect.diff`, **unapplied**

Generated by `make_diff.py` (`checks/10`), which asserts its anchor paragraph occurs exactly once
and never writes to the manuscript. It replaces the three-line generalisation paragraph at the end
of §4.2 with three paragraphs, and touches nothing else in the file.

How it meets each required property:

* **(a) null reported as a null, not equality** — "This is a null, and it is reported as a null
  rather than as equality. Within case reports the counts are 4 events against 2; a genuine two-fold
  difference in either direction is entirely compatible with them, so the honest statement is that
  no contrast is supported, not that none exists."
* **(b) sentence unit ≠ patient unit, kept** — "a matched sentence is not a death and a death is not
  a patient, so none of those counts is a count of patients and none assigns a cause to anyone."
* **(c) instrument stated with its measured error** — "inferred from titles and journal names alone,
  with no publication-type field and no full text available for the comparator, by a single
  unblinded reader; on a seeded 40-paper hand check the rule agreed overall in 70 per cent of cases
  (28 of 40), with case-report precision 1.00 (9 of 9) and recall 0.90 (9 of 10)."
* **(d) no rejected reading revived** — the text assigns no cause of death, reads no survival curve,
  restates no competing-share or ceiling figure, and makes no claim about any other disease's
  literature. The retained half of the old sentence is kept strictly to *this* disease: "determines
  what **this disease's** evidence base can answer."
* **(e) §3.1 untouched** — the single hunk starts at line 400, inside §4.2. §3.1 ends at line 282.

## 7. Step 4 — every other site that quotes the crude figures

Swept for `4/116`, `3/461`, `3.45`, `0.65 %`, `5.3-fold`, `p = 0.033`, `0.03304`, `10.34`, `3.47`
(`checks/08`, repo-wide and deliberately over-broad; `checks/09`, targeted). Result:

**In scope and fixed by this diff — one site.** The manuscript is the only paper-side site, and it
does **not** currently quote any of these numbers at all: the crude contrast was produced by the
sibling investigation lanes and never entered the paper. The diff *introduces* 3.45 / 0.65 / 0.033
in §4.2 and immediately retires them in the same passage, which is why they must appear there.

**Not a site — `research/manuscripts/pinned-figures.json`.** No entry quotes any of these values;
the only `3.45`/`terminal` hits in the file belong to the unrelated ASO parent-null arm. Confirmed
in `checks/09` section B.

**Not a site — generated artifacts.** The repo-wide sweep returned 601 lines, of which every hit is
a coincidental numeric substring in unrelated modality/docking JSON
(`nr4a3-fusion-targets-inputs.json`, `emc-hypoxia-null-background.json`, and similar). None concerns
this corpus. `checks/08` is retained in full.

**Live quotations, all inside the two sibling investigation records** — `PUB-MORTALITY-MECHANISM/FINDING.md:88`
and `MORTALITY-2/FINDING.md:16,45,94,101,102`. These are **dated investigation records, not paper
prose**, and MORTALITY-2's own text already supersedes the earlier lane's reading in place. Naming
them **explicitly as out of scope**: a worker must not edit another lane's directory, and rewriting
a historical record to match a later result is the wrong repair.

**Two adjacent sites named explicitly, for the owner, NOT included in the diff:**

1. **§6 Conclusion, line ~500** — "cause-of-death recording should be treated as a measurement that
   determines what its evidence base can answer." It quotes no number and makes no cross-disease
   comparison, so it is not falsified by this result; but it is the same generalisation in softer
   form, and the owner may want it scoped to this disease for consistency with the new §4.2. Left
   alone deliberately rather than silently.
2. **`research/manuscripts/claim-coverage.json` — a real downstream consequence.** It records
   `emc-mortality-mechanisms-paper.md`: `sentences 182`, `with_a_number 54`. `claim_coverage.py`
   carries a `STALE_HEADER` guard and the census/guard-corpus pair is enforced by tests under
   `research/manuscripts/tests/`. **Applying this diff will make that artifact stale until it is
   regenerated** by the owner running the census. This lane did not regenerate it — that is a write
   outside the lane — and did not weaken or bypass the guard. Named so it is not discovered as a
   surprise CI failure. (`checks/12`, `checks/13`.)

## 8. Artifact · validation · provenance · limitations · stop condition

**Artifact** · `section-4.2-composition-effect.diff` (unapplied, applies clean at exit 0) and
`make_diff.py` that builds it; the re-derived JSON set and `frozen-source-hashes.txt` as the
re-derivation evidence.
**Validation** · byte-identity of all six regenerated artifacts against MORTALITY-2's, plus digit-level
agreement of every printed load-bearing quantity; `git apply --check` with its real exit code.
**Provenance** · `research/literature/emc-mortality-probe.json` (committed 2026-09-04) and
`research/manuscripts/emc-terminal-events-classified.json`, read read-only through MORTALITY-2's
unmodified scripts. No network, no retrieval, no cohort, no GPU, no paid API.
**Limitations** · This lane re-derived; it did not re-design. A reproduction of a computation is not
independent evidence *about the world* — the classifier, the lexicon, the corpus and the single
unblinded hand reading are all the same ones, with the same limits MORTALITY-2 records (§7 there),
and every one of those limits carries into the proposed prose. The prose is a scope narrowing and
introduces no new measurement. Nothing here is a patient count, a cause of death, or any clinical,
efficacy, safety or selectivity claim.
**Stop condition** · **Met.** The re-derivation either reproduced or it did not; it did, so the diff
was written, proved to apply, and the quotation sweep completed.
**Forward stop** · Do not apply the diff, do not stage it, and do not regenerate `claim-coverage.json`
from this lane. The paper owner and the parent decide. Advancing the underlying science further
requires a study-type-matched comparator drawn at design time and hand reading at the patient unit,
neither obtainable without new retrieval.
