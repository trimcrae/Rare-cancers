## S1 — Published intervals that carry no denominator in their own artifact

- **Question.** Across the provenance-carrying artifacts under `research/`, how many published confidence
  intervals are emitted **without the (k, n) they rest on** in the same object; and does any of them reach a
  manuscript or other outward-facing document?
- **Scientific purpose.** An interval whose numerator and denominator are absent from its own artifact cannot
  be recomputed, audited or falsified by any reader — and if it reaches prose it propagates a number nobody can
  check. This is a property of the reported science, not of a gate.
- **Actual inputs.** `research/modalities/hla-coverage.json` (`_af_info_from_acc`, `hla_coverage.py:210-217`),
  `research/modalities/nr4a3-tcip-reach.json` (`by_linker_atoms`, `nr4a3_tcip_reach.py:828-840`), then the
  remaining provenance-carrying artifacts under `research/`. Read-only.
- **Distinction from prior work.** W89 measured artifact-vs-generator staleness for 15 `wilson()` modules and
  **named this as the question it could not settle** (58 of 116 interval blocks unauditable by construction).
  W80 compared implementations; W20g is scoped to one fourth-cohort artifact; W08b re-adjudicated one abstract
  census. None enumerates denominator-less intervals corpus-wide, and none asks whether any reaches prose.
- **Method constraint carried forward:** resolve the denominator **by key within the enclosing object, never by
  proximity or number-presence** — W57 measured that a number-presence screen grades a known-stale case as
  corroborated.
- **Finite acceptance.** A count of interval blocks with and without a resolvable (k, n), by artifact; and for
  every denominator-less block, whether the value appears in any `research/manuscripts/**` document.
- **Stop.** When that census is complete, or at ~40 tool calls / ~40 minutes, whichever first.

## S2 — What a reporting standard would have to require for a published curve to be reconstructable

- **Question.** In the retained synthetic digitization control, how does reconstruction error depend on the
  **density and anchoring of the numbers-at-risk table** — i.e. what would a journal have to print for a
  survival figure to be reconstructable at a stated error bound?
- **Scientific purpose.** Study development. The programme has already measured that the two largest reachable
  series in this disease print **no** numbers-at-risk row, and that the standard admissibility metric does not
  bound reading error. What has never been characterised is the **requirement**: how much risk-table detail is
  enough. That is a statement addressed to reporting practice, and it is reusable by any rare disease.
- **⛔ Explicit scope bar.** This is **NOT** an inverse-baseline application and **NOT** a retry of any closed
  recurrence or IPD gate. It applies no inversion to any real published curve, produces **no** patient-level
  dataset, pools nothing, and makes no clinical statement. It characterises an instrument on synthetic inputs
  whose ground truth is known by construction.
- **Actual inputs.** `research/modalities/km-digitization-error.json` (16 scenarios, 12 read / 4 refused;
  `worst_max_abs_curve_error` 0.0636, off-step 0.0035, and its own `⛔_direction_of_the_bound` stating the
  figures bound error **from below**), plus the control's generator source read for write-freedom first.
- **Distinction from prior work.** P1 verified that the control exists and reported its headline; it did not
  vary risk-table density and did not derive a requirement. No retained report does.
- **Finite acceptance.** Either a table of reconstruction error against risk-table density/anchoring with the
  direction-of-bound caveat attached, **or** — if the retained control carries no density-varying arm — the
  **exact dependency that is missing**, named to the field, with the branch stopped there.
- **Stop.** On either outcome above. A documented "the retained control cannot answer this" is a success.

## S3 — Do two committed artifacts ever disagree about the same cohort's counts?

- **Question.** Where more than one committed artifact quotes counts for the **same** published cohort, do the
  counts agree? For each disagreement found, which side matches the source, and can the source settle it at $0?
- **Scientific purpose.** A denominator that two committed homes describe differently is a defect in the
  reported science: every downstream proportion inherits whichever copy its consumer happened to read. This has
  now surfaced **twice by accident in one day** — P4 found `emc-sgk1-lane-assessment.md:233` describing GSE24369
  as 36 comparators against `emc-expression-panels.json`'s 29 (and **29 is what every published score used**),
  and P6 found Agaram *Hum Pathol* 2014 recorded as n=26 while its three named partners sum to 24, with 2 cases
  unexplained. Two independent accidental hits imply a class worth enumerating deliberately.
- **Actual inputs.** Committed cohort-count carriers under `research/modalities/` and `research/manuscripts/`
  (`emc-clinical-registry.json`, `emc-site-curation.json`, `emc-surgical-quality.json`,
  `emc-expression-panels.json`, `hormone-partner-lane.json`, `nr4a3-emc-biology-evidence.md`,
  `emc-sgk1-lane-assessment.md`, and any other file quoting the same `sourceId`/PMID). Key the comparison on the
  cohort identifier, never on a bare number.
- **Retrieval, bounded and already-admitted only.** Where a disagreement is found **and** the primary is
  open-access, the PubMed MCP may be used to read that one source and say which side matches — the same route
  W07g already used in this campaign. **Not authorized:** any paywalled retry, any source on the closed list
  (Pazopanib, Sunitinib 2014, Wagner, CTARC, Trabectedin/RT), any GEO header re-read or new dataset admission,
  any full-genome access probe, PMID 22592656, and any retrieval that would resume held work. A source that is
  not open-access is **UNKNOWN**, and the branch stops there with the exact dependency named.
- **Distinction from prior work.** W05/W05b/W05g/W05h and W07g concern **patient overlap between different
  cohorts** and **cohort composition against full text**; W20g is scoped to one fourth-cohort artifact. None
  asks whether two committed files disagree **numerically about the same cohort**.
- **Finite acceptance.** A list of (cohort, artifact A count, artifact B count) disagreements; for each, which
  consumers read which side; and for those resolvable at $0, which side the source supports.
- **Stop.** When the carriers are enumerated and each disagreement has a verdict or a named blocking
  dependency; ~40 calls / ~40 minutes.
