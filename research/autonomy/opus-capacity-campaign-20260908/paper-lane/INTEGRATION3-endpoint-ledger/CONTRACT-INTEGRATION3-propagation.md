## The admission test for "genuine contradiction"

> Two demonstrably **mutually exclusive** assertions about the **same object** at the **same
> scope**, such that no reading of this cache can make both true.

A candidate that needs an unproved mapping to become incompatible, or that opposes an inference
against a source field, **fails** and is recorded as an unresolved tension with its full evidence
and what would settle it. **Zero admitted contradictions is a permissible result. There is no
quota, and a low count is not a success criterion** — it means less is established, not that the
records agree.

exit 1; every run directory carries its real exit code; v1 and v2 re-hash unchanged.
