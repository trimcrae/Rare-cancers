## The nine findings, as root maps them

**F01 (P1).** The remaining absent or unmet selecting states are **not measured by RNA**, and the
unique-survivor comparison applies **inconsistent standards** to the unresolved biomarkers.

**F02 (P1).** The observed genome-wide mixture is **descriptive background** — it is **not** a
validated null and **not** proof of a negative. A small *t* is **not** equivalence and **not**
biological absence. ⚠ The **old statistics seat's contrary inference is rejected too**: preserve that
history as history, and do **not** read it as clearance.

**F03 (P2).** The PRC2 core **includes EZH2**, so the full statistic cannot be attributed to the
remaining genes once EZH2 is excluded. Relative arrays are **not** an absolute detection floor.

**F04 (P2).** The omitted **SFT** targeted-gene values **EXIST in the frozen offline cache**. The
reviewer ran a **bounded offline sensitivity**, not a full enlarged genome-wide background. ⛔ There is
**no source loss** here and **no new fetch is needed**.

**F05 (P2).** The guardian **mean is correct**, and **BCL2L2 does NOT invalidate it**. What is needed
is coherent reporting of the component reversals, MDM2, the second sensitiser mean, and the limits of
the priming inference.

**F06 (P2).** Effective **N**, coverage, missingness, comparator composition, the meaning of the array
SD, normalisation and the reproduction dependencies are **insufficiently specified**. ⭐ The inspected
Welch unit is **SAMPLES, not genes** — there is **no gene-pseudoreplication finding**, and none is to
be asserted.

**F07 (P1).** The required basal p53-output score and the general class-selection framework are
**unsupported by the sources**. Cancer lineages are **not** a tumour-versus-normal safety or
selectivity statement. Narrow the unsupported targeted-agent, clinical-use and comparative-source
claims — ⛔ **without inventing a regulatory verification**.

**F08 (P2).** Orthogonal protein or functional results **may change the interpretation while the RNA
result stands**; they are **not necessarily RNA falsifiers**. Cellularity and confounding remain
unresolved.

**F09 (P2).** The **retrospective selection from a broader pool** and the **qualitative grading** must
be made explicit. The five classes are **not** an established prospective confirmatory selection and
**not** a validated ranking.
