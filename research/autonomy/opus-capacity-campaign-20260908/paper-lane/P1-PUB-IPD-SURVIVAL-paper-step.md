## Results

### What the reachable literature contains

Sixteen candidate series were assembled from the extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma clinical registry and graded for whether their survival figures could be obtained and read (`research/literature/emc-km-admissibility-2026-08-27.json`, `totals`, `by_verdict`). Eleven of the sixteen could not be retrieved at all at zero cost. The reasons are ordinary and are recorded per row in `research/modalities/emc-ipd-survival.json` (`candidate_sources[].reachability_2026_08_25`): five are graded closed by Unpaywall and have no free route; three are graded open access at the publisher and were nonetheless answered HTTP 403 on every route attempted, including a headless browser, so that a human with a browser can read them and this programme cannot; two returned HTTP 500 from their PubMed Central routes and were left unresolved; one is a conference abstract. The largest series in the field, a 270-patient SEER analysis (`candidate_sources[0]`, `unpaywall_oa_status: "gold"`), falls in the second group: its figures were never seen, and this paper makes no claim about them.

Figures were obtained and read for five papers, fifteen figures in total, of which ten carry a caption naming a survival endpoint (`emc-km-admissibility-2026-08-27.json`, `totals.figures_read_whose_caption_names_a_survival_endpoint`). Those five papers together describe 314 patients.

### The decisive negative

Of the fifteen figures read, **two print a numbers-at-risk row, nine do not, and four are undetermined** (`research/modalities/km-risk-row-detection.json`, `_totals`). The nine are not distributed evenly. They are concentrated in the two largest series whose figures this programme could actually retrieve. The 171-patient national registry study `masunaga2025` prints three survival figures — disease-specific survival by distant metastases at diagnosis, local recurrence-free survival by (neo)adjuvant radiotherapy, and disease-specific survival by chemotherapy in advanced disease — and the detector returns `absent` for all three (`km-risk-row-detection.json`, `sources[]`, pages 5, 7 and 10; input digest `pdf_sha256 4ae16c8e3b901398a2c881007d64d74ce94176f997f2b3d069ac17c2de00461a`). The 59-patient two-institution series `chiusole2020` prints four overall-survival figures and the detector returns `absent` for all four (same artifact, pages 4, 4, 5 and 5; digest `6f0b024ddaa9c65700ea46f2c89c3120f8ecb1c10ef3a6ee820dc91dc7e0a788`). Seven curves, 230 patients between the two denominators, and not one numbers-at-risk row.

Without that row the per-interval censored count is not identifiable, and the reconstruction is refused rather than admitted with a caveat (`research/modalities/emc-ipd-survival.json`, `quality_floor.rationale`; the governing condition is §2.7(a) of `systems/POLICY-evidence.md`). We state plainly what this is and is not. It is a statement about what a journal printed beneath an axis. It is not a criticism of either study, it does not bear on either study's conclusions, and it says nothing about the patients in them (`emc-km-admissibility-2026-08-27.json`, `⛔_a_refusal_is_about_reporting`). The consequence is nonetheless total: the largest reconstructable evidence in this disease is unreconstructable for a reporting reason and not a scientific one.

**There is no pooled time-to-event dataset at the end of this analysis.** That is the finding.

### What could be recovered, and by which instrument

Three fragments survive, and no two of them may be merged.

**One curve inverts.** `stacchiotti2013anthracycline` (`PMC3879193`) prints one Kaplan-Meier figure, of progression-free survival on anthracycline-based chemotherapy in eleven patients with NR4A3-confirmed disease, and it prints a numbers-at-risk row beneath it — one of only two positives in the whole census (`km-risk-row-detection.json`, `sources[]`, page 5). Reading it with the method of Guyot et al. (BMC Med Res Methodol 2012;12:9; `emc-ipd-survival.json`, `method`) recovers **eleven patients, nine events and two censorings** (`emc-ipd-survival.json`, `reconstructions[0]`), with a maximum absolute deviation from the digitized curve of 0.0454 against a floor of 0.05 (`quality[0]`). The reconstructed median progression-free survival is **7.98 months** (`pooled.median_survival = 7.984996`) against the **8 months** the figure's own caption prints — a quantity the reconstruction never saw and the only check in this programme that tests a reading rather than an arithmetic (`research/modalities/km-figure-readings.json`, `recipes[0].external_check`).

That agreement required a judgement, and we report it rather than absorb it. The figure's time axis begins at 2 rather than 0, and the first event falls on the origin, so the printed "10 at risk at t = 2" is a post-event count while Guyot's recursion needs the number at risk while the curve is still at S = 1. Taken verbatim, the reconstruction misses the floor and returns a median contradicting the paper's own caption. Anchored at N = 11 immediately before t = 2, it clears the floor and reproduces the caption. Both tables are retained in the artifact (`km-figure-readings.json`, `recipes[0].risk_table_printed` and `risk_table_anchored`); a printed number is never silently overwritten. The strongest corroboration is internal to the pixels rather than external: the curve's first two steps are read at 0.9097 and 0.8188, where a cohort of eleven predicts 0.9091 and 0.8182 and a cohort of ten predicts 0.900 and 0.800. Against the worst off-step reading error our control measures anywhere, 0.0035, N = 11 misses by 0.0007 and N = 10 by 0.0188, so the figure itself discriminates the two.

**A figure that is not a Kaplan-Meier curve carried more.** `martinbroto2020immunosarc1` (`PMC7674086`) prints **no Kaplan-Meier curve at all**; its Figure 3 is a per-patient swimmer plot, coloured by histology (`emc-ipd-survival.json`, `candidate_sources[].figure_finding.km_figures = 0`). A swimmer plot needs no numbers-at-risk row because it needs no inversion: each bar already is one patient's progression-free survival, with censoring drawn as an arrow. Read at the pixel level, the figure yields all 49 patients of the trial's analysis population, 37 events and 12 censorings, and separating the extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma bars by colour yields **four patients**: 19.3 months censored, 16.3 months censored, 15.5 months with an event, and 11.6 months censored (`research/modalities/km-swimmer-readings.json`, `subgroup.patients`). The claim ceiling on those four is severe and is stated in the artifact: they are identified by bar colour inside a mixed-histology trial, in a subgroup the paper did not analyse and did not pre-specify, so they support a statement about what those four patients' figures show and nothing about efficacy, about this disease as a population, or about any comparison between histologies (`km-swimmer-readings.json`, `⛔_claim_ceiling`).

**Two patients needed no instrument.** `morioka2016trabectedin` (`PMC4946242`) prints per-subject efficacy in Table 2. Two of the five patients in its trabectedin arm are identified as extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma, with progression-free survival of 13.0 and 7.4 months and overall survival of 26.4 and 10.4 months, both uncensored, both with stable disease as best overall response (`emc-ipd-survival.json`, `printed_patient_level_data.rows`, verified against the PDF text layer of page 4 rather than a page raster). Its Kaplan-Meier figure does print a numbers-at-risk row and is admitted by the census, and we deliberately did not invert it: the arm is not an extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma cohort, and inverting a curve to recover what a table already prints adds error for nothing (`candidate_sources[].figure_finding.⛔_it_is_not_an_EMC_cohort`, `⭐_no_reconstruction_needed`).

Seventeen patients in total, from three papers, on two different endpoints, under three different treatments and lines of therapy. `systems/POLICY-evidence.md` forbids merging them with each other or with any reconstructed curve, and we do not. The `pooled` block of the generated artifact contains one curve from one series and carries its own disclaimer: its median is that series' median on anthracyclines, not this disease's median survival (`emc-ipd-survival.json`, `pooled.⛔_this_is_not_a_pool`).

### The admissibility metric does not bound the reading

The metric that admitted the one reconstructed curve — maximum absolute deviation between the reconstructed step function and the digitized curve — was believed in this programme to bound digitization error. It does not. Against synthetic cohorts whose patient-level data is known exactly (`research/modalities/km-digitization-error.json`), the metric compares the reconstruction with the digitized curve rather than with the truth, so an error that moves the input moves both sides of the comparison together. The discriminating pair is direct: a clean render gives a true error against the known cohort of 0.0292 with an internal deviation of 0.0183, while a render whose axis anchor is displaced by one pixel gives a true error of 0.0636 with an internal deviation of 0.0159 (`control.finding.the_discriminating_pair`). **The true error more than doubled while the admissibility statistic fell.** What the metric does catch is a reading so wrong that no cohort could have produced it — a deliberately mis-tuned matcher drove the internal deviation to about 1.0 and the floor refused it. Its blindness is to moderate and systematic error, which is the realistic kind.

Across sixteen scenarios, twelve were read and four refused, with a worst curve error of 0.0636, a worst off-step error of 0.0035, a worst event-count delta of 0, and a worst censoring delta of 8 against the exact-coordinate baseline (`control.summary`). The direction of this bound is fixed and must not be read the other way: a synthetic render is easier than a journal figure, so these figures bound reading error **from below**. The control can refute a reader and cannot certify one (`⛔_direction_of_the_bound`). A curve admitted on the deviation statistic alone has had its arithmetic checked and its reading checked by nothing; the reading needs its own evidence, which for the one curve reported here is the paper's printed median, and for the swimmer plot is five printed quantities.

One further loss is a property of publication rather than of any reader: censorings occurring after the last numbers-at-risk time are not identifiable from a published figure at all, because the risk table stops and the algorithm has nothing to distribute them against (`control.exact_coordinates_baseline.⭐_what_this_baseline_already_loses`). In the exact-coordinate baseline that is seven of the cohort's censorings, with zero coordinate error anywhere.

## Limitations

This study reads figures; it produces no patients and no follow-up. Every value reported here is a re-expression of an already-published figure and inherits every selection effect, referral pattern and treatment allocation of the series it came from (`emc-ipd-survival.json`, `what_this_is`); none of it asserts efficacy, safety, selectivity or clinical readiness for any therapy, and nothing here is medical advice. The census is bounded by what could be retrieved at zero cost on two specified dates, so eleven of sixteen candidate series — including the largest, a 270-patient SEER analysis that Unpaywall grades gold open access — are absent because of access and network outcomes, not because of anything about the papers; their figures were never seen and the risk-row question is unasked for them rather than answered, and a later retrieval could change the census in either direction. Correspondingly, the "two largest" framing above is exact only over series whose figures were retrieved and read. The seventeen patient records recovered come from three papers on two endpoints under three treatment settings and are not pooled and must not be pooled; the eleven-patient reconstruction is one series' own progression-free survival on anthracyclines and its 7.98-month median is not this disease's median survival; the four swimmer-plot patients are a colour-identified, non-pre-specified subgroup of a mixed-histology trial; and the two transcribed patients are one arm of a five-patient stratum. The eleven-patient reconstruction rests on anchoring the numbers-at-risk table at N = 11 rather than on the printed first row, a judgement supported by three corroborations but a judgement nonetheless, and the verbatim alternative is retained. The digitization-error control is synthetic and bounds reading error from below only; it does not model textured backgrounds, grossly misidentified anchors, non-linear axes, or curves that reach S = 0. Re-derivation is uneven: the stacchiotti2013 figure is committed and its reading re-runnable, whereas the swimmer plot is CC BY-NC 4.0, is not committed, and its four readings rest on the five external checks plus a recipe naming the URL, page and resolution rather than on a re-run from this repository. Finally, the retrieval rounds recorded in the admissibility artifact reference a literature cache branch that is not present in this checkout; the per-figure verdicts, captions and input digests are committed here, but the fetch itself is not reproducible from this repository alone.
