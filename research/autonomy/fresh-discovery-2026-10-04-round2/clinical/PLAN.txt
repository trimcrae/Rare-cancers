Fresh discovery round 2: clinical natural history. 4 October 2026.

Question and estimand before patient-level table inspection
Among patients with pathologically authenticated EMC initially treated for
localized disease, does remaining free of distant metastasis for five years
identify a substantially lower subsequent five-year distant-metastasis risk?
Primary estimand: cumulative incidence of first distant metastasis during years
5 to 10 conditional on alive and metastasis-free at year 5, treating death without
metastasis as a competing event. Local recurrence does not itself exclude a patient
from this metastasis-free estimand, but must be tracked if the source allows it.
The phrase disease-free is therefore not interchangeable with metastasis-free.
Secondary descriptive quantity only if competing events cannot be recovered:
conditional net metastasis-free survival S(10)/S(5), explicitly identified as a
Kaplan-Meier re-expression, not real-world cumulative incidence.

Prior knowledge and motivation
Late EMC recurrence is established, including long-term clinical cohorts and the
repository's prior recurrence-timing work. Initial search exposed Ogura2012 printed
5/10-year metastasis-free survival 89/61%, Drilon2008 crude recurrence outcomes,
and Chiusole2020 long follow-up. A ratio of those already published percentages is
not by itself a worthwhile new contribution. The intended addition would require
authentic denominators and timing showing how prognosis changes with elapsed
event-free time, credible uncertainty, and corroboration across eligible cohorts.

Sampling and safeguards
Search all identifiable relevant EMC primary cohorts, their supplements and public
individual tables, including broader sarcoma cohorts. Do not select on a late event
or pool case reports as incidence samples. Record all eligible observations and
unresolved access. Distinguish original diagnosis, definitive surgery, referral and
metastatic-treatment clocks. Follow-up duration is not an event time. Distinguish
metastatic presentation from subsequent first distant metastasis. Check recurrence
definitions, surveillance detection, competing death and cohort reuse before pooling.
No borrowing other sarcomas as EMC observations. No clinical recommendations.

Defeaters and decision
No patient-specific metastasis times or reliable published conditional risk-set
data, unresolvable risk sets, material missing outcomes, heavy cohort overlap, or
prior art already establishing the same useful inference defeats advancement.
Do not reconstruct censoring times merely from final status. If the only feasible
calculation restates known late recurrence without new disease knowledge, shelve
this standalone question and identify a scientifically different feasible question.
No full manuscript or promotion before worthwhile contribution and complete coverage.
