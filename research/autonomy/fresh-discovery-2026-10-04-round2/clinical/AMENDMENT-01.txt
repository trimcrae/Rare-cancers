Appended 4 October 2026 after the frozen conditional-risk pilot; no change to PLAN.

Interpretation and next bounded gate
The Mayo landmark calculation has only two late events and very wide uncertainty.
It describes known persistent late risk without establishing clinically useful
prognostic change. Drilon's published 5/10-year distant recurrence-free estimates
already imply a similar conditional net quantity. No standalone advancement is
justified by new arithmetic alone. Public Ogura/Paioli tables were examined next:
they contain aggregate characteristics/survival, not recoverable individual risk
sets. Paioli explicitly includes 13 locally recurrent presentations, so its clock
and target population cannot silently be combined with untreated localized cohorts.

A different question before relevant table values
Newly found primary study DOI10.1007/s00256-025-05050-w has 44 EMC pretreatment MRIs
and reports an association between solid (>80%) enhancement and metastatic disease
(abstract p=.035). It mixes preoperative nodal and pulmonary findings with subsequent
pulmonary metastases. Does solid enhancement predict FUTURE distant metastasis among
patients without distant metastases at the MRI, rather than merely distinguish
patients who already have metastatic disease? This is an unanswered extension;
the original abstract reports association, not a proven prospective predictor.

Feasibility gate and analysis if feasible
Retrieve actual public tables/supplements. Require patient-level pretreatment MRI
classification, contemporaneous distant metastasis status, first subsequent distant
event time, final follow-up and competing death status. A table giving ever/never
metastasis without temporal crosswalk cannot answer this question. If sufficient,
freeze comparison before inspecting individual values: all initially nonmetastatic
eligible EMC patients, solid versus mixed/sparse, time since pretreatment MRI to
first distant metastasis; competing death handled explicitly. No cutoff search,
no lumping regional nodes into distant disease without source definitions, no
regression with an inadequate number of events. Would be exploratory, requiring
independent data to validate. If temporal crosswalk or full counts are inaccessible,
record unfinished idea and stop; do not treat it as a negative biomarker study.
