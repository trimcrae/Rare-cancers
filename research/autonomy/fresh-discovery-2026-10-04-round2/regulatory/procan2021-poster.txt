LARGE SCALE PROTEOMICS NOW 
POSSIBLE - PROCAN TO DEVELOP PAN 
CANCER DATABASE
• Recent advances in technology allow high throughput 
proteomics; 1000s samples, 1000s of proteins, 6-8 hours
• ProCan- International Centre for the Proteome of Human 
Cancer.
• 6 mass spectrometers, multi disciplinary 
team of >30 including pathologist, 
proteomicists, engineers, data 
scientists and oncologists.
• Aim to produce proteogenomic 
report that increases the accuracy of 
treatment decisions in real time (e.g. 
36-48 hours)
PROJECT AIMS
Aim: To assess the proteomic 
landscape of sarcoma
To investigate if:
1. Histological subtypes have unique 
proteomic signatures
2. New classifications emerge
3. Proteomic signatures differentiate 
disease behaviour within subtypes
• 203 samples of 178 patients
• from International Sarcoma 
Kindred Study
• >30 histological subtypes
• Central pathology review (not 
sarcoma specific)
• Using high throughput mass 
spectrometry
DIA: Data Independent Acquisition

LMS PROTEOMIC SIGNATURE 
HIGHLY DISCRIMINATES LMS 
SAMPLES FROM NON-LMS 
• Use of machine learning to assess predictive 
performance of LMS signature  to classify LMS 
sample from non-LMS samples
• Mean ROC AUC 0.97
Method: Random forest classifier with 1000 trees. 1000 monte-carlo experiments: 70/30 
train-test set. Results are averaged over all runs
TOP 2 PROTEINS CAN DISCRIMINATE 
MAJORITY OF LMS PROTEINS FROM 
NON-LMS
• Top discriminating proteins 
(top 20/25/50 etc) identified 
through machine learning.
• Using single top protein, 
90% LMS samples could be

0.9).
• Using 2 proteins from top 50 
 96.5% (AUC 0.965)
• If validated, potential

• Proteomic landscape of multiple sarcoma 
subtypes  (>30) with high throughput proteomics 
undertaken.
• Signatures, or individual proteins, may be able

• Other subtypes have heterogenous proteomic

Limitations
• Small numbers of individual subtypes.
• Findings require validation.
CONCLUSION
• Validate findings with independent cohorts.
• Expand analysis of subtypes within this cohort in greater 
depth.
• Develop LMS diagnostic assay of key proteins.
• Proteomic signatures of outcome across multiple 
sarcoma subtypes (e.g. predict relapse)
• Through Local biobank at Chris O Brien Lifehouse and 
international collaborators.
• ProCan has capacity for multiple large sarcoma studies and is 
keen to collaborate (econnolly@cmri.org.au).
