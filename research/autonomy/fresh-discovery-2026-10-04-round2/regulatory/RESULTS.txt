Fresh EMC discovery round 2: regulatory and mTOR model evidence
Decision: shelve standalone regulatory-program and mTOR-response papers on the
current evidence. No new demonstrated disease finding survives this lane.
Date: 4 October 2026. No manuscript drafted or publication action taken.

Regulatory question and value gate
Can an authentic EMC tumor/model perturbation reveal a previously unrecognized
fusion-driven regulatory program, with tissue corroboration that survives culture
and cell-composition alternatives? The public measurements recovered here do not
supply that experiment. Filion2009 already measured direct PPARG promoter activation
in engineered cells, EMC PPARG/protein and NDRG2 staining, and SGK1 isoform differences
(DOI10.1002/path.2445). Brenca2019 already established fusion-partner-associated axon
guidance expression in12 EMC tumors and an engineered-cell SEMA3C mechanism
(DOI10.1002/path.5284). Neither is a new authentic EMC knockdown or chromatin dataset.
The proposed PPARG/adipocyte-program expression question also overlaps the project's
public August23 direct-target/gene-set paper at
https://aixiv.science/abs/aixiv.260823.000001. It was rejected before new values;
repairing that paper or reranking genes is outside this discovery objective.

copy_number0. This lane asked whether authentic EMC genotype-matched models supply
mTOR response corroboration, freezing MTOR-PLAN before re-reading compound values.

All40 Bangerter2022 screen compounds (USZ20-EMC1), the separate USZ22 validations,

Clinical search produced potentially relevant, incompletely authenticated evidence
Merimsky2008 reports a rapamycin/cyclophosphamide response in 'myxoid chondrosarcoma';
full diagnostic/site/fusion details remain unavailable. Quek2011 has an indexed
myxoid-chondrosarcoma shrinkage observation under everolimus+figitumumab, but the
individual eligibility, outcome and molecular details could not be recovered.
IND206 reports one EMC response specifically to sunitinib, with no recoverable EMC
recipient denominator or individual outcome on temsirolimus; its registry currently
has no posted results. These sources block any claim of complete clinical mTOR
coverage. Combination effects, ambiguous disease labels and missing genotypes
cannot be converted into TSC2-specific sensitivity or resistance.

Reopening conditions
For regulatory biology: authentic EMC fusion perturbation or an authenticated
sample-linked public chromatin/isoform/methylation dataset with a worthwhile new
question; mapping the7 ZullowRNAs to specimens, original measurements and technical
batches would make a validation route usable but would not itself create novelty.
For TSC2: independently confirmed tumor-specific functional loss with relevant
pathway measurement and/or a genotype-linked authentic EMC perturbation or drug
response. The genomic recurrence observation's value is for the lead to assess;
this packet supplies no therapeutic inference. No TMEM266 or DDR revival.

Final bounded protein/spatial/H19 extension, 4 October 2026
Decision: shelve standalone protein/regulatory/H19 papers; no newly demonstrated
EMC biology. The final source batch ended at identity and scientific-value gates.
Burns2023 all11 histology categories exhaust321 tumors without an EMC category.
Sarquarium2024 DatasetEV1 enumerates all17 models, none authenticated as EMC.
Tang2024 all272 clinical rows were evaluated, including all8 otherFS:7 identify
other entities, while SS-5 has unspecified subtype and remains unresolved.
No protein-expression ranking was performed in these non-authenticated cohorts.
Ngo2025 spatial/single-cell measurements concern epithelioid sarcoma; the EMC
mention concerns an external bulk comparison, not an EMC spatial measurement.
ProCan2021 poster and PanAtlas2025 still have unresolved sample-level eligibility.
Those are visible incomplete coverage, not evidence that public EMC proteomics
does not exist. No global discovery-exhaustion claim follows from this round.

The H19 study(DOI10.1002/cam4.71305) describes EMC in two TMA sets, but its reported
tissue analysis pools rare entities into Others(26/150) with no individual EMC
identities, scores or outcomes. Its data statement links CCLE rather than the
individual tissue observations, and the public fullXML contains no supplements.
Gapmer experiments are restricted to SW872/SW982. Its MUG-EMCS label conflicts
with the model creator's explicit extraskeletal mesenchymal chondrosarcoma label.
No NR4A3 authentication recovered; this model cannot establish EMC H19 dependence.
These are source/identity limits, not evidence of H19 absence in authentic EMC.

Contrary mTOR evidence reported by the independent genomic worker is retained:

The separate genomic packet binds these primary sources; this lane has not
independently counted the poster. Do not state absence of other public mTOR events.
No source supplies a paired TSC2-genotype and authentic EMC drug-response bridge.
