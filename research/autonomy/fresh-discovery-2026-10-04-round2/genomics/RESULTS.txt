2026-10-04 ROUND 2 GENOMICS — SCIENTIFIC DECISION

Decision: SHELVE the standalone secondary-genotype/TSC2 paper. This is a completed bounded analysis, not completion of the discovery campaign or a claim that public evidence has been exhausted. No new publication-worthy EMC finding is advanced.

Question and original contribution sought
Do purportedly actionable secondary alterations recur in authentic EMC across independent cohorts, beyond the already reported TP53 result, sufficiently to change what researchers test next? PLAN.txt predates the new case-level analysis; AMENDMENT-01 records the exploratory TSC2 follow-up after its discovery, and AMENDMENT-02 records the all-public-cBio structural-event search. The submitted CSPG4 letter remains the contribution reference, not a hypothesis to repair. Root identified its exact submitted source at git097f863ab83d242022e05637da39bafbad03b4f2, research/release-candidates/preprint-short-formats-20260924/cspg4-figure1-revision/letter.md; TMEM266 remains shelved.

What was actually evaluated
All 75 final EMC profiles and all 49 secondary-event rows in the original Foundation7494 supplement were evaluated. Thirty-four profiles have at least one such row. These are source calls, including possible germline variants, not 49 established somatic drivers. All 75 have reported NR4A3-associated rearrangements (63 EWSR1,12 TAF15); an extra NR4A3-PGR source-labelled leiomyosarcoma is retained separately. All genes were retained, including low-frequency and unfavorable calls. No disease-wide prevalence is estimated by pooling cohorts.

The seemingly interesting TSC2 result did not survive scrutiny as a new, established recurrent loss. Source2691 (male62, lymph node) has TSC2 Q955*, VAF0.4922, plus FGFR1 N546K and other calls; source4926 (male51, soft tissue, purity20%) has reported TSC2 copy number0. Both have TAF15-NR4A3. Distinct ages/tissues and the study's patient-level design support two source patients, but no cross-cohort identity crosswalk exists. Exploratory 2/12 versus0/63 partner comparison gives two-sided Fisher p0.02378; testing all38 observed secondary genes gives Bonferroni p0.9038, and the465-gene family gives1. It does not establish partner enrichment.

More decisively, Q955* is ClinVar VCV821922, currently uncertain significance in the germline record. The submission rationale and Ekong2016 (10.1002/humu.22951) show that exon25/26 is alternatively omitted from functional TSC2 isoforms. This does not prove benignity in EMC; it means a stop annotation alone cannot establish functional TSC2 loss. The source lacks EMC isoform/allele-specific RNA or protein measurements and matched normal for this call. Copy-number0 in the other specimen remains a reported deletion, not independently verified protein loss or a drug-response biomarker.

Novelty also fails: the original Foundation Figure2A numerical source explicitly already reports EMC TSC2 frequency0.0266666667 (2/75). The presence of these events was not undisclosed. The original Caris2026 abstract already reports recurrent FBXW7 mutations(n2), PTEN deletions(n3), ERCC2 deletions(n3), and more secondary alterations in non-EWSR1 tumours. Thus 'secondary recurrence beyond TP53' is already prior art. The independently inspected BostonGeneSITC2024 poster reports three combined TSC1/2 mutation/loss EVENTS in EMC n10, plus one PIK3CA mutation and one BRCA2 mutation/loss event. These favorable pathway observations are retained, but combined genes/events without variants or patient mapping cannot establish three TSC2-deficient independent patients.

The full externally defined KEGG PI3K-AKT/mTOR union retains all seven Foundation event-bearing cases, including AKT1 E17K, PTEN frameshift, NRAS Q61L, FGFR1 N546K, MYC and MCL1 amplifications, and the TSC2 calls. Broad pathway membership neither establishes pathway activation nor makes these heterogeneous lesions equivalent. Authentic-model follow-up by the independent functional worker found no matched TSC2 genotype–rapalog-response bridge; NCC-EMC1-C1 screening has rapalog measurements but no reported TSC2 genotype. No therapeutic inference is made.

Additional analyses and contrary observations
The all-cBio search queried all223 public structural-variant profiles for NR4A3 and320 mutation profiles from studies lacking structural-variant profiles for legacy fusion encoding:111 structural events and737 NR4A3 small-variant records, with no fusion-like mutation record. Most eligible events are Foundation/MSK/Davis releases of already evaluated patients. Non-EMC events in lung/glioma/hepatocellular/rectal tumours were checked against actual clinical labels. This query does not discover all EMC: untested fusions, NR4A2 variants, source-labelled EMC without fusion records and hidden annotations remain outside its ascertainment.

OrigiMed2022 has two EMC-labelled specimens: P4093 has EWSR1-NR4A3 and PTEN Y27C; P5337 has PIK3CA H1047L, TP53 R280S and FANCM D1544N with no released NR4A3 event. A third specimen,P6101, has EWSR1-NR4A3 and CREBBP N1343Kfs*16 but is labelled chondroblastic osteosarcoma. All were retained and all available small-variant/CNA/SV records evaluated. The discordant specimen was not silently reclassified, and missing fusion in P5337 was not called misdiagnosis. No TSC2/IDH2/ARID1A/NTRK3 secondary event was reported in these three profiles. Empty CNA events do not prove every locus is copy-neutral. Source sequencing uses matched blood, but the API labels variant status NA; primary methods and per-assay panel coverage were checked.

Six EMC rows in the distinct Japanese study10.1111/cas.70214 were evaluated across all small-variant columns: patient602 FANCA S849fs*40 and U2AF1 S34F; patient1028 FAS Q196*; no small variants listed for327,533,977,1036. No candidate IDH2/ARID1A/NTRK3/TSC2 small variant appears. The source separately reports all six diagnostic fusions in aggregate; it does not provide a complete individual CNA/fusion crosswalk in this table. This study is not DOI10.1002/cam4.71098, whose overlap remains unresolved.

The six Davis2017 patients' complete published somatic tables contain no candidate TSC2/IDH2/ARID1A/NTRK3 call. MO1222 has TSC2 W477C in the GERMLINE table, with normal VAF46.6%, tumour52.5% and no reported LOH; it is not somatic TSC2 validation. Frozen/FFPE MO1023 tables are one donor. The MSK all-gene reuse confirms ARID1A Q1142* in one donor, with no released defining fusion support in that specimen and unresolved overlap with GENIE; it is not an independent replication of the GENIE aggregate.

The Lisbon2022 sample29 already reports fusion-confirmed EMC with PTEN exons1–4 deletion and BCOR loss. Its four listed systemic regimens progressed, and no rapalog was administered. This is additional published secondary-genotype evidence, not a new outcome association. Zou2023(one donor/three lesions plus normal), He2022(one donor/four sampled conditions), rare NR4A2-defined tumours and other broader-study evidence are evaluated or explicitly staged in COVERAGE.txt.

Scientific value
The analysis prevents a misleading new claim—two TAF15-associated TSC2 losses and consequent rapalog sensitivity—but does not overturn an important published EMC therapeutic claim. Literal secondary-event presence, source qualification and a careful negative comparison are useful research records; by themselves they do not justify a new EMC paper. Foundation tissue labels lack treatment timing, disease stage, histological grade and outcomes, so a burden-versus-metastasis or untreated-versus-treated analysis is not supported. No substitute phenotype was invented.

Promising but unfinished
The GENIE NTRK3/IDH2/ARID1A question remains unresolved because actual individual IDs, alleles, fusion partner/support, panels and cross-release overlap are not available through the unauthenticated public route. The actual three published supplements were recovered and evaluated; they are aggregate, not missing downloads. The BostonGene TSC1/2 events and original Caris per-case PTEN/FBXW7/ERCC2 calls could sharpen an authentic-EMC pathway hypothesis if individual genotype/assay/donor information and a relevant functional or clinical measurement become public. A citation or more counted events would not alone reopen the paper.

No source count or all-profile query is claimed as new disease knowledge. Suitable accessible material still unevaluated is listed as pending and blocks manuscript advancement. Root owns the next discovery round.
