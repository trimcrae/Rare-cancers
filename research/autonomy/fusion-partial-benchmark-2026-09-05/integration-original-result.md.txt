The retained Lee 2023 partial benchmark is quantitatively feasible. All 31 disclosed duplex designs are retained, with 31 fusion means, 30 measured-parent means and 61 plotted standard deviations. SS #4's parent mean is censored by the plot ceiling and remains unknown above a conservative lower reading bound of 1.9744. All 31 second-parent outcomes remain unknown. This is a completed numerical checkpoint for independent coordinator verification, not a manuscript, preprint-readiness declaration or a relaxation of the historical both-parent feasibility requirement.

The two frozen descriptors do not show a common ordering pattern across the screens. GC has weak fusion associations with opposite signs. Terminal composition asymmetry has a moderate negative association with remaining B4N fusion expression, but a weaker SS fusion association whose sign can change when reading uncertainty is doubled. The stronger positive SS parent/GC association is descriptive and excludes the censored design. These overlapping tiled designs do not identify a causal sequence rule, a calibrated predictor or an independent target-family validation.

| Screen / endpoint | Recovered means / disclosed designs | GC rho with remaining expression | Terminal asymmetry rho with remaining expression |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| B4N / fusion | 15 / 15 | 0.119 | -0.546 |
| B4N / measured parent | 15 / 15 | 0.226 | -0.256 |
| SS / fusion | 16 / 16 | -0.241 | -0.199 |
| SS / measured parent | 15 / 16 | 0.638 | 0.322 |

Rho is Spearman with average tied ranks. Lower plotted relative expression means more suppression; correlation with the explicitly derived index `1 - remaining expression` has the opposite sign. Neither index is asserted to be percent knockdown: source captions do not establish the exact Ct transformation. No p-values, statistical confidence intervals, trained weights, pooling across targets or population inference were used. Plotted SDs are retained separately and do not enter the reading-uncertainty simulation. Raw replicate-level variability and correlation are unavailable, so these associations are not tested against biological uncertainty.

Each range is the observed minimum/maximum over 10,000 seeded uniform perturbations, not an exhaustive bound or confidence interval. Four-pixel native-image half-widths correspond to +/-0.0211 B4N and +/-0.0256 SS plotted units. At those widths, ambiguous pair orderings number 3/105 for B4N fusion, 14/105 for B4N parent, 22/120 for SS fusion and 13/105 for the 15 recovered SS parent means. With doubled widths the counts are 12/105, 27/105, 37/120 and 23/105, respectively. Close design rankings therefore cannot all be resolved, even before biological variation is considered. The SS parent #4 censored observation is retained in coverage but excluded from these numeric summaries; no value was imputed.

The screen includes clear losses of the actually measured parent. For example B4N #15 has plotted fusion expression about 0.074 (reading range 0.053-0.095) and BRD4 about 0.347 (0.326-0.368). B4N #9 has fusion about 0.137 (0.116-0.158) and BRD4 about 0.779 (0.758-0.800). These are source-figure readings, not new experiments or statistical claims of selective sparing. SS #9 has measured SS18 about 0.737 (0.712-0.763); the missing SSX1 outcome cannot be treated as spared. Selected protein or separate-cell-line observations are not substituted for these RNA endpoints.
