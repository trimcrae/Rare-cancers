Both tables explicitly label sense and antisense 5-prime to 3-prime and print lowercase `tt` after 19 RNA bases. Methods section 4 identifies these as 3-prime dTdT overhangs. The isolated whitespace before SS #6 sense `tt` is removed for machine parsing; the original text and PDF are retained. All 31 cores have length 19 and the reported antisense equals the reverse complement of reported sense. Both strands retain their printed orientation. No other chemical modification is specified for these screening duplexes. The later in-vivo Accell B4N #9 reagent is not assigned to the screen's chemistry. The commercial NUTM1 positive-control pool is not an additional screened design or a parent-expression outcome.

B4N: article methods section 4 and S5 caption establish HCC2429, 50 nM, 72 h, transient Neon transfection, qRT-PCR. Article results identify BRD4 exon 11 (NM_058243.2) fused to NUTM1 exon 3 (NM_001284292.2). Main Fig. 2 caption in the XML identifies wild BRD4 C-terminal primers, fusion junction primers and GUSB internal control for its selected-design panel. S5 itself calls its bars average Ct values relative to NC, while its axis calls them relative mRNA expression. No exact delta-Ct/exponentiation formula is reported in the inspected methods/captions. Therefore the retained unit is the plotted relative-expression scale, not an asserted quantitative fold change or percent knockdown. GUSB's use in the main selected panel is contextual support, not an invented explicit statement in S5. Methods section 5 states triplicate qRT-PCR for all samples; S5 labels SD. Biological versus technical replicate identity and raw replicate values are unresolved.

SS: methods section 4 and S6A caption establish 50 nM, 72 h, qRT-PCR, SS18 exon 10 fused to SSX1 exon 6. The cell section, S6A caption and blot heading use HS-SY-II; the in-vitro methods and S6C caption also contain HY-SY-II, recorded here as a source naming inconsistency. Dataset uses HS-SY-II from the assay caption. S6A caption explicitly identifies wild SS18 C-terminal and fusion-junction primer regions and GUSB internal control. It states mean of triplicates and SD. NC is plotted near 1, but the precise Ct transformation is not reported. Source narrative says the experiment was performed in the same manner as the B4N experiment; it does not independently repeat a transfection instrument for SS. No separate cell line is used here as a same-cell parent control.

Exact B4N nucleotide boundary/alignment has not been independently established from the retained XML and supplement set; the main Fig. 2 sequence graphic is not among these seven PDF members. S6A visually shows its exon junction and tiling, but no verified genome-wide parent matching or common exact-breakpoint score is available for both series. Accordingly no junction centrality or parent matching feature was computed. The two frozen compositional descriptors require only the verified duplex tables. S3's wild NUTM1 primer listing does not supply a NUTM1 outcome. No second-parent outcome is plotted for either screen. Protein, viability, mouse and blood panels are excluded; source prose/caption inconsistencies in selected protein findings do not change the qPCR bar assignments.

Pixel origin is the top-left of the native image, zero-based. S5 calibration: y=356 is 0, y=52 is 1.6, hence 190 pixels/unit; intermediate ticks run in 38-pixel increments. S6A calibration: y=353 is 0, y=41 is 2, hence 156 pixels/unit; intermediate ticks agree within one pixel. Mean coordinates are colored bar tops; neutral upper SD caps are separately retained. Four-pixel mean half-widths include edge/antialiasing and axis-reading ambiguity: +/-0.0211 S5 and +/-0.0256 S6 units. SD is upper-cap height minus mean height, with a six-pixel reading half-width. These are conservative reading ranges, not a calibration confidence interval or source statistical uncertainty. All 61 mean markers and 61 upper-cap markers were visually checked on retained overlays. The darkest vertical error line can obscure a mean edge, so median colored-column detection aided manual verification. Legend swatches were explicitly excluded; the exploratory color scan is not the authoritative coordinate dataset.

It is excluded from numeric correlation rather than assigned the ceiling as a mean. Its design and endpoint row remain in coverage. All other means and labeled SDs are recoverable to the stated image-reading precision. No biological replicates were reconstructed.
