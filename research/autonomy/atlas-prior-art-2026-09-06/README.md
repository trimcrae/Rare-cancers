Dulken et al. established CHRNA6 RNA chromogenic ISH evidence; it is not protein IHC. GSE24369 is reused discovery material. The newly recovered PRAME publisher supplement contains five EMC entries negative for both IHC readers, four with documented NR4A3 confirmation and one without an ancillary result. Preserve this direct negative tissue evidence alongside RNA contrasts; it does not prove universal PRAME absence or absent peptide-HLA presentation.

The bounded source audit did not verify direct EMC cellular/spatial CSPG4 protein evidence. A new rank-effect analysis contributes qualified cohort/platform RNA evidence and comparator sensitivity; it does not discover a validated surface target or treatment. Historical myxoid-chondrosarcoma imaging/phase-I labels do not automatically establish molecular EMC, receptor subtype or clinical efficacy. The published Hofvander cohort must be explicitly attributed; no exhaustive claim that every supplementary plot was searched is made.

The public original-source-audit.zip retains the primary-source data, audit, exact PRAME sheet rows and search limitations, with one publisher subscription-preview HTML page omitted. public-export.json records the original and public archive hashes and proves every retained member is unchanged. The original complete archive and its history remain preserved locally. file-manifest.json, preservation-manifest.json and historical review receipts describe that original packet; they are not relabelled as hashes of the public counterpart. citation-anchors.json retains source-returned identifiers/title/abstract and source-table evidence. The factual source workbook remains unchanged for independent checking. This source/novelty checkpoint does not itself establish publication clearance.
