Independent FDA ADC-antigen review: shelving supported

The fixed six-gene panel has no surviving useful RNA allocation contrast. Official source checks support the eight FDA-approved solid-tumor ADC biologics and six target genes as of 2026-10-05. All 31 returned label records, including four unharmonized records, and eight original approval dates were checked independently.

The frozen 29-file owner packet matches the retained originals across all 13 EMC RNA conditions, six expression-array EMCs, ten old-ratio EMC conditions, four 3SEQ EMCs and three authenticated cultures. All fixed comparator conditions were retained. Rank effects, year weighting, all-sample/deletion checks and exploratory multiplicity agree. Conditional bootstrap bounds were checked; the draws were not regenerated independently. Nine primary rows after known overlaps were removed are not proven independent donors. Distinct assays and their units were not pooled.

HER2 and MET are appreciable in bulk RNA, but their comparator and array contrasts fail. FOLR1 heterogeneity remains visible and was already reported in primary EMC research. These results do not establish accessible protein, therapeutic safety, dependency or ADC efficacy. Failure of this operational panel does not justify a negative therapeutic paper.

The omission challenge preserves an EMC-labelled patient in the trastuzumab deruxtecan trial, with individual treatment, HER2 and response linkage still pending; two published FOLR1 outliers among five RNA-evaluable Davis cases; a tissue-factor plasma-microvesicle EMC observation; and six reported HER2-negative rows in a breast review. Those breast rows include a confirmed metaplastic carcinoma and molecularly unresolved diagnoses. They cannot provide an authenticated six-of-21 EMC negative denominator. Other public EMC measurements and clinical/protein source gaps remain explicit.

The preliminary model transcript query was invalid because GeneID translated to AllFields. Its results were rejected before freeze. Corrected official Gene-to-RefSeqRNA associations, all 58 human RefSeq summaries, the noncoding exclusion and the final mapped culture values were independently verified. Historical accession completeness remains unresolved; mapped zeros are not whole-gene or protein absence.

Decision: shelve this fixed RNA panel and retain its unfavorable/inconclusive evidence. Reopening requires a useful authenticated protein/clinical contrast or a prospectively justified different question with complete relevant evidence. No manuscript or promotion is warranted. The broader EMC discovery search remains active and unexhausted; TMEM266 remains shelved.
