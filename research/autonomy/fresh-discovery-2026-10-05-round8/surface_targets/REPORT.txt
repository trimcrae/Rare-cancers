Validity and value decision
All three fail the frozen appreciable, reproducible EMC tissue-contrast rule.
SEZ6 has no persuasive common-expression replication; NCAM1 lacks the relevant
sarcoma contrast and its CD56 phenotype is prior art; DLL3 is low and its array
direction is unfavorable. Exploratory Holm-adjusted three-comparator IUT p
values are1/0.259/1 for DLL3/SEZ6/NCAM1; these do not undo previous broad-screen
selection or validate biological value. Uncertainty, all deletion checks and
raw-scale projections are in RESULTS.json/MEASUREMENTS.tsv. The negative
allocation result tests our hypothesis, not an established clinically useful
EMC claim, so no negative-result paper is justified.

Expression cannot establish accessible surface protein, antigen-specific
binding, dependency, uptake, normal-tissue safety or treatment benefit. Every
favorable, unfavorable and missing condition in the evaluated sources stays
visible. Other suitable public tissue/model sources and exact prior gene lists
remain pending in COVERAGE.json; this failed bounded lane cannot claim complete
public EMC evaluation or global antigen absence. Advancement would require
specific new authenticated evidence with useful cell-localized protein/RNA
comparisons, independence/overlap resolved and full relevant-source evaluation.
An unchanged rerun, inventory repair, new title or selected high specimen is
not a sufficient reopening condition.
