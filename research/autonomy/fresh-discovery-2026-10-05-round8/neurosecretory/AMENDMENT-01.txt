Scientific terminology correction before final freeze

The original frozen plan and early script used independent-primary terminology. Three known historical discovery overlaps and one local recurrence were excluded from the Hofvander primary subset, leaving nine rows. These exclusions do not prove nine independent donors, complete cross-study independence, or canonical fusion status per row. Final results call this the primary nonoverlap subset after known exclusions; unknown overlaps remain. All thirteen source-labelled EMC conditions are retained and evaluated individually. No primary scientific decision or threshold was changed to improve an outcome.

The broad source search was deliberately retained as incomplete exploratory metadata rather than a full-source coverage claim; complete refined exact-disease/clinical/vesicular query results do not establish global absence of measurements. Barresi publisher HTML is a subscription preview, not full-text assay access. No receptor-positive measurement is inferred from the SSTR2 keyword.
