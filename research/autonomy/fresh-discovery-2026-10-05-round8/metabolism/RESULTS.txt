Shelve the standalone EMC lipid-redox/ferroptosis paper. No new publication-worthy finding survives this bounded pilot, and the public evidence search is not exhausted.

The frozen question asked whether authentic EMC has a measured lipid-redox vulnerability with a useful control or rescue comparison. The complete published USZ and NCC drug screens contain real viability observations, but no acquired lipid-peroxidation or mechanism-specific rescue readout establishes that comparison. Repeating their measurements does not create a new finding.

Sorafenib has NCC normalized viability of 28.42% (SD 13.11 percentage points), while its tosylate has 70.31% (SD 20.16). USZ20 reports the ordinal category “none,” which its legend defines as >71% viability. No shared dose, exposure or salt equivalence was established, so these values cannot be pooled or interpreted as ferroptosis or resistance. Other retained NCC observations are conoidin A 92.64% (SD 4.22), artemether 69.27% (SD 8.15), arsenic oxide 75.70% (SD 33.97), PRIMA-1: 78.07% (SD 19.16), and 5-ALA 137.16% (SD 24.01). These source values are unclipped; values above 100% do not prove growth stimulation. This descriptive compound selection followed roster inspection and is not a prospective mechanistic endpoint or a new hit ranking. Exact formulations, CAS identifiers and source rows are retained.

All 40 USZ drug labels, 221 NCC screening rows and 24 selected IC50 rows were audited. The declared canonical GPX4/xCT/rescue aliases were absent from these literal rosters. Unreported CAS identifiers, brands and uncertain spellings prevent treating that as exhaustive chemical-target coverage. The source measurements are viability and ATP, rather than direct lipid flux or ferroptosis measurements.

All 13 Hofvander and all six GSE24369 EMC metadata rows were retained, including overlap and nonprimary-specimen flags. Bulk RNA can supply expression context but cannot establish a metabolic dependency. No genes were mined to rescue the hypothesis, and historical eligibility flags were not used to remove unfavorable or overlapping specimens from this record.

The recovered primary studies provide real GPX4 and rescue controls in other cancers. The UPS BET/GPX4 study reports lipid peroxidation and also reports mouse combination toxicity. The 5-ALA light/dark study uses SW872 liposarcoma and MG63 osteosarcoma. Those experiments cannot substitute for EMC. Previously published EMC CAM fluorescence, PPARG expression/regulation, beta-enolase promoter activation and disputed-model drug measurements remain evidence at their actual resolution; they do not establish a new native EMC lipid-redox vulnerability.

Important gaps remain: the full broad CTRP model crosswalk, generic model/organoid identities, CIVO individual drug/control mappings and the ascorbate/MRI histology source. The failed supplement response and reCAPTCHA HTML were retained; no browser challenge or private route was used. Initial broad catalogue queries were partial. Focused searches and 302 mesenchymal-study metadata records plus 76 PubMed records were completed, but metadata screening is not evaluation of all underlying experiments. These gaps prevent promotion and are not negative EMC measurements.

Reopen only for authenticated EMC perturbation, lipid/iron and rescue measurements with useful matched controls, complete relevant public coverage and an independent scientific-value challenge. A future laboratory comparison would need documented genotype, media, dose and time, with antioxidant or genetic rescue and suitable controls; this program has performed no such experiment. No manuscript was drafted. Scripts, source hashes, observations and coverage are in this packet. Full sources and derivatives remain ignored and preserved, and shared inputs were not copied.
