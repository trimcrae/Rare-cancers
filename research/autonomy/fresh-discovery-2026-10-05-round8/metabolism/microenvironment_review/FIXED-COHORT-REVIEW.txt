The fixed ligand transcript scores and adjusted coefficients are reproducible. This is a conditional transcript observation; a publication-worthy EMC finding has not yet been established.

I independently replayed all 704 samples by 13 fixed genes against the Hofvander TPM source, all 42 array samples by 13 released probes against the original GSE24369 source, and the original GPL6244 unique-symbol/common-probe selection. All 13 EMC samples and 46 closest-histology controls were retained, with 59 distinct recorded patient groups. All six array EMC observations and 17 LGFMS controls were retained. No pooled cross-platform effect was calculated.

The largest RNA cohort has a weak unadjusted rank contrast: probability EMC exceeds a control is 0.587. The unadjusted mean difference is 0.323 log-score units, with descriptive OLS 95% CI -0.395 to 1.042. The full fixed content-adjusted coefficient is 0.764, with the owner's case-bootstrap CI 0.106 to 1.474. Independent fixed-covariate diagnostics show that the estimate and uncertainty depend on the adjustment model: only the myeloid-plus-T-content and full models have descriptive OLS intervals entirely above zero among the eight combinations of the three already specified content covariates. This does not prove an artefact, but the adjustment remains part of the result's meaning.

The nine primary samples without recorded historical MDB overlap still have a positive full-model coefficient, 1.030, with descriptive OLS CI 0.151 to 1.910. Known overlap alone therefore does not erase the conditional signal. Exact donor linkage between the historical MDB records and GSE24369 remains unproved. Restricting the RNA comparison to LGFMS gives 0.519, with CI -0.672 to 1.710; it does not independently establish the array's stronger EMC-versus-LGFMS contrast. None of these exploratory peer diagnostics is a new discovery or an independent cohort.

The two cohorts' score components also differ. After the same full adjustment, RNA has myeloid-ligand coefficient +0.207 and T-ligand coefficient -0.558; array has +1.364 and +0.294, respectively. The component estimates have substantial uncertainty. A positive balance cannot therefore be described as a reproducible absolute myeloid-ligand increase, and neither cohort measures immune recruitment or identifies the cells producing the ligands.

I checked the proposed TempO estimator against the cached full probe manifest: all 22,537 probes have length 50, and all 17 probes covering the 13 fixed target genes are uniquely assignable in both orientations. The 12 unique run records total 2,704,945,123 compressed bytes. The nine target genes omitted from the published filtered matrix are missing observations, rather than measured zeros. Completion, allocation calibration, target-specific low-count limitations, sum-versus-mean probe sensitivity and scientific value remain to be assessed after the owner's complete 12-library freeze. Any failed estimator benchmark would concern this allocation method and would not invalidate the assay or show biological absence.

The complete relevant published coverage and novelty/value assessment remain necessary. Previously reported macrophage polarization or bulk heterogeneity does not make this fixed-score reanalysis a new contribution. The EMC-only TempO cohort can check within-cohort measurements and estimator behavior, but cannot itself establish a between-histology difference. No recruitment, cell fraction, tumor-cell secretion, dependency, prognosis, response, efficacy or safety claim is supported by this review.
