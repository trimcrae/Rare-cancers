The complete official GSE6481 deposit contains **105 sample records, with no sample annotated as EMC or any chondrosarcoma**. Its source diagnosis counts reproduce the original publication's 105-tumor roster. This contradicts the later article's description of that accession as 19 EMC plus 128 other chondrosarcomas. It does **not** establish which dataset the later authors actually analyzed. A wrong accession, an incorrect figure label, or another unresolved provenance issue remains possible.

The independently inspected Figure S6B has an additional label discrepancy: its heading states GSE6481 (n=147), while every plot labels the groups Other Chondrosarcomas (n=59) and EMC (n=19), totaling 78. Labels alone do not reveal the underlying sample selection.

## Bounded conclusion

The cited accession cannot currently substantiate the described EMC-versus-chondrosarcoma comparison from its deposited annotations. The source gate requires the actual accession, complete selected GSM roster with diagnoses, and analysis input or code resolving the discrepancies. Until that provenance is established, this audit neither reconstructs the later analysis nor decides the truth of its expression findings. It supports a precise clarification/correction need; manuscript merit remains a separate assessment.
