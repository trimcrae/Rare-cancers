## Boundaries

The paper already explicitly reports severe overfitting (optimism-corrected C-index0.10), exploratory status, the absence of meaningful external survival validation, and technical-ROI limits for the two-specimen imaging data. Those acknowledged limitations are not new audit findings. No scientific inference about efficacy, safety, therapeutic sensitivity or patient management is drawn here.

The original article's Data Availability section refers to Supplemental Files and PRJNA1357027. The downloaded archive has one internal-cohort expression workbook and two document tables, plus figures; it contains no analysis script or external sample-by-gene table. The article's two GitHub links name general imaging tools, not this study's external-validation analysis. This describes the inspected public packet, not proof that the required information does not exist elsewhere. Source XML/ZIP were previously retrieved from Europe PMC; SHA256s and selected exact paragraphs are in source-inspection.json.

The strongest possible coherent contribution would clarify what each external evidence layer actually establishes, with a corrected source-backed cohort map and a precise boundary on the cross-platform inference. A single source typo would normally merit clarification/correction, not a standalone research paper. Paper merit remains undecided pending independent original-accession verification and an independent assessment of that distinction. No model fitting or expression effect recalculation has been allocated.
