# Preserve the distinction between expression and drug-response evidence

The MDM2 route is not supported by the original expression argument, but its pharmacology remains unresolved. The two-platform observation of lower p53 transcriptional output is unchanged. Inferring antagonist inactivity from that observation was an additional, untested step. The current record now separates those claims.

The retained Bangerter main text reports an ordinal HDM201 sensitivity band in one EMC model, USZ20-EMC1. HDM201 did not receive the separate validation performed for carfilzomib, doxorubicin and venetoclax. The venetoclax screen response did not reproduce as a monotherapy response on validation. These observations limit the weight of the HDM201 result; they do not prove it false or establish efficacy. The inspected passages do not provide an HDM201 concentration anchor, a normal-cell comparison or resolved TP53 status. The paper's compound-target annotation is not independently verified here. Its broad chromosome-1 gain annotation is not a focal MDM4 amplification call.

The same accepted S55 correction supplies two missing citations. PU-H71 sensitivity in an EMC model belongs to the existing weakest clientship-evidence category: a viability response does not establish that the fusion protein is an HSP90 client. The route's earlier blanket literature-absence statement is narrowed to the bounded review's scope, acknowledging the retained viability evidence. The apoptosis route now cites the source for its already-recorded venetoclax screen/validation reversal and monotherapy/combination distinction. Its proposed explanation involving another anti-apoptotic family member is explicitly a hypothesis, not a diagnostic signature of that dependency. The mixed-sarcoma dependency fractions do not identify the guardian in EMC. Grade categories, numerical findings and next actions are preserved; the chaperone grade wording is unchanged.

This is an internal evidence correction, not a new therapeutic recommendation or paper. The MDM2 route remains parked; the previously recorded hematologic-toxicity qualification remains. A useful reopening input would be a specific source or measurement resolving a relevant genotype/coverage, validated response or mechanism question. New wet-lab work is not the only possible input. The historical supplementary-file fetch proposal is not revived, and no source access, contact or experimental authority follows from this note.

The accepted S55 memo and proposed correction, exact retained primary-source blob and inspected line locations are bound in PROVENANCE.json. It also preserves the complete prior route records, including the superseded inactivity inference. No expression values, source analyses, preprints or historical evidence files changed.
