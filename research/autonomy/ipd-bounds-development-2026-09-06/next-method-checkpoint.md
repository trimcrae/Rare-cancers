The independently checked grouped-time oracle is useful but insufficient. All four harder releases exhausted node/path budgets; current partial results revert to universal bounds. Actual published reconstructions differ in risk counts, event totals and rounded curves as well as continuous support. Removing only the support constraint would not create a shared compatibility model. No superiority, held-out utility or paper readiness is established.

The independent read-only methods review by `/root/next_allocation` recommends a joint reachable-state calculation followed by an interval-time release extension. The coordinator accepts this as the next development path. No additional paper inventory or new endpoint is needed.

## First executable checkpoint

Use the existing 18 development releases and four fixed stress releases. Keep the current exact enumerator unchanged as an oracle. Joint states contain time, both risk counts, remaining event totals and exact rational KM levels. Feasible event/censor transitions add ordinary logrank score U and variance V. Equal rounded KM values do not imply identical future feasible paths; preserve exact levels initially.

Histories sharing a structural state may share future transitions only while their complete accumulated (U,V) sets are retained or conservatively enclosed. Outward rational boxes are permitted. Replacing histories with an average or one representative, or combining independently favorable U and V without enclosure, is invalid. Merging boxes can weaken bounds but must not discard attainable values.

Account for every unexpanded frontier branch. With eA and eB remaining events, safe suffix enclosures are delta-U in [-eB,eA] and delta-V in [0,(eA+eB)/4]. Each tied-event variance increment is at most d/4. Add these enclosures to the accumulated score/variance region. For strictly positive variance, Q lower is squared distance of the U interval from zero divided by V maximum; Q upper is maximum squared U divided by V minimum. A zero lower variance generally requires an infinite upper bound. Treat the valid U=V=0 convention separately. Bounds crossing zero or the threshold must remain conservative.

A certificate before exhaustive completion requires all feasible terminal and unresolved frontier regions to imply the same decision under the existing rational critical-value enclosure, plus an actual compatible witness establishing nonemptiness. Loose bounds crossing the threshold do not prove that opposing witnesses exist. If execution stops mid-expansion, retain the unprocessed portion of that branch; do not lose it from the union. A recorded timeout alone cannot justify a certificate.

Required output: mathematical coverage argument, executable solver receiving only releases, independent containment against oracle extrema, forced-exhaustion checks exercising partial expansion and nonemptiness, actual memory/time and stress bound widths. Demonstrating more certificates is not required to pass this development checkpoint; valid bounds and measured usefulness or weakness determine the next action. No new synthetic-case search or held-out evaluation is authorized by this checkpoint.

## Subsequent model and comparison requirements

Preserve the original observed-IPD logrank target when admitting time uncertainty. A separate schema may release sample sizes, available event totals, exact risk counts/times and sparse right-continuous curve observations with horizontal/vertical uncertainty intervals. Freeze the observation mechanism; a coordinate-noise model is not automatically a complete raster-digitization model.

Partition time by observation-window and exact risk/query boundaries. Inside each cell, account for admissible pooled weak orders, true event ties and censor placements. An interval is a location constraint, not a time at which all exits tie. Preserve observation ordering/existence constraints. Relaxing constraints can give conservative outer bounds; excluding admissible orders cannot. Verify tiny order-class cases independently before moderate development. Do not bin continuous times and silently substitute the grouped-time statistic.

Run unchanged IPDfromKM and CIFresolve approximate mode on the same nominal coordinates and available counts; preserve preprocessing, failures and inconsistencies. Their original-observed-IPD decision accuracy and development-calibrated margin abstention can be compared without forcing reconstructed times onto the certificate model. CIFresolve's optional MIQP remains unexecuted and must be disclosed.

For information acquisition, predefine exact candidate times and compare adaptive queries with a fixed equally spaced sequence under identical count budgets. Supply each answer to the original baselines too. Choose using only releases and hypothetical answers, not hidden originals. Graph boxes are enclosures, not equally likely patient paths: avoid the old class-count criterion after approximate merging. A conservative worst-case unresolved-decision indicator followed by bounded p width is one candidate to freeze before evaluation.

Known-IPD benchmark metadata/archive were recovered, but empirical contents and held-out cases remain unopened. Realistic uncertainty may make valid bounds unhelpfully wide, and queries may add no value beyond fixed counts or simple abstention. Those are outcomes to measure, not limitations to hide or reasons to change the target.
