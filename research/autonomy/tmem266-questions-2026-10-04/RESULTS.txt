TMEM266 remaining-question investigation — 4 October 2026

The additional investigations justify revising the exploratory EMC RNA letter.
They do not support a protein-target, dependency or diagnostic-performance paper.
The earlier evidence packets and draft remain unchanged.

Demonstrated additions — unranked

• Published USZ-23_EMC3 data contain TMEM266 transcript estimates summing to
  133.52 TPM in an EMC-derived cell preparation with EWSR1–NR4A3 sequence support.
  This supplies a concrete model for transcript experiments, but does not establish
  complete RNA, protein, dependency or expression in patient tumor cells.

• A fixed raw-read search recovered one paired fragment across an annotated
  alternative TMEM266 splice boundary in that cell preparation.
  This adds local sequence evidence relevant to transcript identity, but one
  fragment cannot identify a complete or predominant isoform.

• Historical FFPE checkpoints retain the exact TMEM266 detector sequence in
  five consistently identified libraries and one with the known Si22 conflict.
  This corroborates the assay target in published tissue, but these amplified
  detector products do not sequence native RNA or establish tumor positivity.

• Actual CEL headers resolve all six EMC specimen identifiers and reveal
  complete separation of EMC and LGFMS scan dates.
  This clarifies cohort overlap and weakens the array comparison's biological
  interpretation because scan batch and histology cannot be separated.

Include the model evidence, cohort clarification and batch limitation in the
main text; retain the one-fragment observation and detector counts principally
in the supplement. These are additions to one integrated RNA study, not four
proposed papers. The last item corrects interpretation rather than establishing
an independent biological discovery.

Other completed checks and publication decisions

• CHRNA6 already separates EMC from all four examined comparator groups in
  these reused data. TMEM266 overlaps OFMT; no diagnostic increment was
  demonstrated. Keep this context and all individual measurements.

• A fixed five-gene mature-muscle contrast is positive in all 40 tumor arrays,
  not specifically EMC. It cannot exclude entrapped muscle or estimate purity;
  preserve it only as a limited supplementary source-confounding check.

• Removing flagged probes and subtracting fixed GC controls preserve the
  upstream signal direction. Neither resolves complete scan-date confounding;
  these are technical sensitivities, not independent validation.

• All 30 array probes also match a predicted transcript with a different coding
  annotation. A signal in a canonical coding region does not identify a protein
  product, and quantifier allocation does not prove isoform predominance.

• RNA BLAST supports intended transcript compatibility and identifies the
  already segregated antisense-sharing probes. Hit caps and partial matches
  prevent an exhaustive specificity claim; the genomic search remained queued
  at the bounded stopping point, so it is unresolved rather than negative.

Promising but unfinished biological questions

Which cells in fusion-confirmed patient tissue express TMEM266? No usable new
public EMC spatial, RNA-ISH or single-cell measurement resolved this; upstream
and downstream localization with muscle/stromal controls remains necessary.

Which complete RNAs and proteins are produced? The local alternative boundary
cannot phase the full molecule, and shared probes cannot establish translation.
Transcript-resolved sequencing and protein measurement remain distinct needs.

Does TMEM266 have a functional role or useful diagnostic increment? No dependency
or clinical experiment establishes either, and the present CHRNA6 comparison
shows no added diagnostic value.

Rejected or unavailable routes

Explicit subtype lists in Burns proteomics and CellSARCTx contain no EMC.
ProCan public metadata contains generic sarcoma labels but no authenticated EMC;
its separate ISKS cohort remains private. The pulmonary single-nucleus study
lacks usable subtype identities/accession, and a retracted proteomics study was
excluded. These outcomes are source-access findings, not negative TMEM266 biology.

DNA methylation cannot answer RNA isoform questions. Brenca patient/run mapping,
controlled-access Hofvander raw reads and the complete earlier CHRNA6 candidate
list remain unresolved. No identities were guessed and no first-discovery claim
is made. See source-gates.txt and the reproduced ProCan metadata gate.
