Anchor EMC-1: Agaram et al., 2014, Extraskeletal Myxoid Chondrosarcoma with non-EWSR1-NR4A3
Variant Fusions Correlate with Rhabdoid Phenotype and High Grade Morphology.
[Primary report, PMCID PMC4015728](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4015728/),
[PMID 24746215](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24746215/).
Locations: Abstract; Introduction; Results, FISH and Molecular findings; Discussion.
Verified against the primary report on 2026-09-06; disease evidence only, no registry refresh.

The report identifies EMC as a soft tissue sarcoma with NR4A3 rearrangements. EWSR1::NR4A3 and
TAF15::NR4A3 are supported EMC fusion routes; TCF12::NR4A3 is also documented. NR4A3 aliases
include CHN, TEC and NOR1. EWSR1, TAF15 and FUS belong to the shared TET/FET protein family,
but family membership does not imply interchangeable tumor fusions. The reported series does
not establish FUS as an EMC fusion partner. Thus a FUS-only entry criterion is not automatically
satisfied by EMC; neither is an EWSR1 requirement universal across EMC subtypes. Gene mention
alone does not establish a required fusion, mutation, partner or extra biomarker.

Interpretation rule, not an experimental finding: broad soft tissue sarcoma or solid-tumor
language can encompass this diagnosis unless the same cohort restricts it. A narrow histology,
partner or alteration requirement must be checked explicitly. This anchor supports disease
identity and possible molecular subtypes; it establishes no drug sensitivity, patient eligibility,
clinical benefit or currently available cohort.
