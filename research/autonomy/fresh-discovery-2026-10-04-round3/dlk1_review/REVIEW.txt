Decision: SHELVE the standalone DLK1 RNA paper. Independent checks confirm the prespecified broad-expression gate fails. This is a failure of our proposed hypothesis, not a useful new refutation of an established EMC DLK1 claim. No full manuscript is warranted, and the two high specimens must not be recast as a post-hoc target-positive subtype.

Scientific value and controls

The biological premise was reasonable enough for one pilot: direct DLK1 work in other tumors shows a differentiation-associated protein with possible membrane and soluble forms. It does not establish this biology in EMC. Primary RMS2013 (PMC3618045) measured multiple DLK1 splice products in human RMS cells and control placenta/muscle; primary normal human myoblasts had state-dependent DLK1 expression. Sarcoma2020 (PMC7449862) directly reported lack of RNA/protein correspondence in tested sarcoma lines, including sw872 and A204. MPNST2025 (PMC12081192) combined tissue/culture/spatial, serum and perturbation evidence in MPNST, not EMC. Thus normal/progenitor state, stromal mixture and other sarcoma expression were real pre-value alternatives, not excuses invented after failure. RNA evidence alone could potentially nominate an EMC-specific measurement, but these data do not support a shared broadly expressed EMC DLK1 phenotype sufficiently to do so as a standalone contribution.

Primary neuroblastoma2024 (DOI10.1016/j.ccell.2024.10.003) and ACC2024 (PMC11482787) were identified as direct protein/functional context, not evidence of EMC efficacy or safety. The ACC study's DLK1 panel reused META-PRISM; neither its broad 'sarcoma' group nor other-cancer targetability is transferable to EMC. No membrane isoform, tumor-cell localization, fusion regulation, dependency, imprinted-locus state, serum secretion, or response was inferred. No replacement gene was selected.

Independent omission check

All 1,031 META-PRISM public clinical sample rows were retrieved by five specific attributes, including 947 RNA sample IDs. The complete compact mapping and raw-response SHA256 receipts are in metaprism-identity.json. There is no literal EMC/extraskeletal/myxoid-chondrosarcoma label. Forty keyword-selected rows include sarcomatoid/carcinosarcoma diagnoses and must not be reported as 40 sarcoma donors. MP-0003 and MP-0126 remain Sarcoma,NOS/undifferentiated; MP-0120 is labelled undifferentiated uterine sarcoma and MP-0674 fibrosarcoma. MP-0255's 'Fibromyxosarcoma' is explicitly mapped to Myxofibrosarcoma. Broad diagnoses and other unknown-primary categories do not establish absence of occult/mislabelled EMC. No fusion or expression values were requested. A complete diagnostic/fusion crosswalk remains unresolved if anyone later advances a pan-public EMC claim.

The owner evaluated the Subramanian2005 full SAM workbook; its recorded no-alias hit is only absence from a selected gene list. It is not absent RNA or proof of novelty. GSE4303 likely shares that source study and must not be counted as independent confirmation. Other already identified gaps include Filion/Sjogren source completeness, ambiguous GSE71119 labels, PeerJ12 gene filtering, unassessed raw targeted counts, and possible authentic-model/old-study overlaps. H-EMC-SS/FANTOM and generic 'EMCS' labels cannot be accepted as authentic EMC without the prior identity checks. These gaps would block any advanced complete-coverage claim; they do not require further work to rescue the explicitly shelved proposal.

Reopening requires an independently motivated question and new suitable evidence that resolves cell identity or protein/isoform localization in authentic EMC, or an important externally established DLK1 claim that these data can directly test. The two high bulk specimens alone, a narrower title, or another threshold cannot reopen the paper. No current therapeutic or biological subtype claim survives.
