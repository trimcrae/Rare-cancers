Decision, 4 October 2026: SHELVE the standalone functional-discovery paper.

No new EMC biological finding survived this bounded source pass. No new
authenticated EMC response dataset was identified that supports a worthwhile
analysis beyond the already evaluated Bangerter, NCC-EMC1-C1 and Planas-Paz
measurements. This is a scoped feasibility result, not evidence of drug resistance
and not an assertion that all public functional evidence has been exhausted.

What was actually evaluated

1. Kuo et al., Science Advances 2026, DOI10.1126/sciadv.adz3351.
Recovered the full 62-page supplement through Europe PMC, streaming the 11.74 MB
PDF without retaining the large original. Source PDF SHA256, archive hash and
all page text are retained. All 220 Table S3 rows (pp20-35) were extracted and
checked, not just the abstract's 15 tumor types. There are 215 distinct literal
source Patient ID labels, with repeated rows and suffixed samples; neither
220 nor 215 is an independent-donor count. Authors report 191 patients.

No row is labelled EMC or an EMC alias. Most rows are explicitly carcinomas,
melanoma or benign cultures, but three CUP rows remain unresolved: WCM2527,
WCM2902_A and WCM2902_B. Table S2 calls the latter two parental tissues
"carcomatoid" (source spelling) with more solid/epithelioid PDO growth; it does
not establish EMC. No DNA sequencing is listed for these three, and their S3
RNA-seq columns are blank. The two WCM2902 cultures are not separate independent
patients. The public cBioPortal study URL and tested GitHub clinical files
returned404, so no diagnostic fusion crosswalk was recovered. These are
unresolved diagnoses, not EMC-negative measurements. No talazoparib or other
response value was tested as an EMC result.

2. Broader directly relevant functional cohorts.
The 2019 patient-derived sarcoma DST main Table1 identifies all14 sarcoma PDCs
and five nonmalignant controls. None is labelled EMC; K-MFS1 is explicitly
"Myxoid fibrosarcoma", not a licence to relabel it. The2025 Breunis tumoroid
study specifies14 Ewing tumors/13patients and4CIC::DUX4 tumors/3patients.
The2025 Ringnalda functional screen specifies21lines/14patients:10EWSR1::FLI1,
6CIC::DUX4,2BCOR-rearranged,2EWSR1::ERG and1EWSR1::FEV. Those diagnoses and
fusion partners do not authenticate EMC. Other cancers' MCL1/mTOR effects
cannot establish EMC dependence. No new EMC drug calculation was justified.

3. Historical PDX and explant evidence was not mistaken for response.
The NCC2015 original annual report explicitly names EMC among29 PDX implantation
attempts and reports9 newly established models across all tumor types. Pages159-
160 (printed146-147), including all46direct-culture and37PDX-culture table rows,
do not identify which successful PDX, if any, was EMC, or provide an EMC drug
response. No9/29 or zero count was assigned to EMC. Old XenoSarc evaluation was
reused: five EMC implantation attempts, initial engraftment reported for all,
but no established EMC model; this is not five independent drug-tested PDXs.

Muff2016 Tables1-4 report broad culture categories and a small cytogenetic subset;
uncertain-differentiation and chondrogenic aggregates do not expose an EMC case
crosswalk. Their establishment yields are not EMC drug susceptibility.

4. False functional leads and explicit remaining gaps.
JCI2014 EWSAT1 Figure3C does contain published EMC RNA expression, already
reported low in non-Ewing tumors. Its PSS090 xenograft and perturbations are
Ewing sarcoma. The genuine EMC expression measurement is unsuitable for the
present functional question, not absent evidence.

UCLA2024 organoid study retains the earlier verified uncertainty about individual
194-specimen identities; bone chondrosarcoma cannot be substituted for EMC.
Tempus2025 PARIS poster reports19sarcoma records within91samples/85patients,
but the accessible text does not supply their full histologic identities.
Two normal direct-PDF requests returned429; a web screenshot returned only a
reference and was not visually read. The sarcoma heatmap remains unevaluated,
not a proven no-EMC cohort. CIVO2020 patient4 is a published EMC case after
neoadjuvant radiation, but the prior supplement evaluation and fresh403 access
failure leave its categorical Figure2M and quantitative drug/control response
unresolved. Missing measurements are not evidence of resistance.

Scientific value

The source gate prevents false disease attribution but is not a discovery about
EMC. The sole additional authentic disease mention, the old NCC PDX attempt,
does not supply a new phenotype. Published drug responses in authentic USZ/NCC
models remain prior art with their positive, negative and discordant conditions
preserved in earlier packets. No full manuscript or new target ranking was made.

Reopen only with an authenticated EMC specimen/model-to-assay crosswalk and
accessible response/PD measurements that answer a consequential unresolved
question beyond authors' original findings; predefine that question and its
defeating comparison before relevant values. Resolve every eligible condition,
controls, assay units, donor overlap and technical replication before promotion.
The CUP/PARIS/UCLA/CIVO identity or measurement gaps are concrete reopening
routes, not claims that they contain usable EMC observations.
