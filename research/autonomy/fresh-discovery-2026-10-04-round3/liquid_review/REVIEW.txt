Why shelving remains appropriate
Combining previously published singlecase positives and non-detections does
not estimate a disease detectionrate across incompatible assays, markers and
clinicalsettings. It also does not challenge an established importantEMCclaim
of uniformshedding or validatedmonitoring, because no suchclaim was identified.
A narrowed historicalevidence note is not automatically a useful newpaper.
Reopen only with an authenticated EMC blood/tissue/serialdisease crosswalk,
adequate analyticcontrols and a consequential unansweredcomparison whose result
could change practice of measurement or the next researchtest. Complete all
eligibleEMCconditions and independent donor/overlap checks before promotion.

This review used exact retainedsources and limited fresh primarydiscovery,
