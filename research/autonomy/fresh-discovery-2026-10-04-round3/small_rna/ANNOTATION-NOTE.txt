NCBI Gene 494330 identifies MIR381 at 14q32.31, GRCh38.p14 chromosome 14 positions 101045920–101045994. Its indexed official source was readable in search. The ordinary HTML download named MIR381.html is an HTTP-200 browser-check page, not a usable gene record, and must not be parsed as annotation. A primary locus study, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6406543/ , explicitly assigns miR-381 to the DLK1–DIO3 domain. The other Sápi targets, miR-206, miR-671-5p and miR-765, do not constitute additional members of that domain. A four-marker assay with one domain member cannot establish coordinated locus expression.

Mature miRNA arms, precursor names, host transcripts and mRNA are distinct measurements. Neither the precursor's genomic coordinates nor host-gene RNA counts are mature-miRNA abundance. The relevant published small-RNA assays cannot establish altered imprinting, causality, dependency or treatment efficacy. No full annotation set was chosen retrospectively to fit the reported selected markers.

The original prior-art context includes non-EMC sarcomas with locus-related miRNA patterns. This prevents treating domain coordination itself as an intrinsically new general-cancer discovery. EMC specificity, suitable tissue comparisons and practical biological value would still have to be demonstrated if a cohort is recovered.
