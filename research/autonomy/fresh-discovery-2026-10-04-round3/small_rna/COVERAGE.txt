1. Sápi et al., Genes Chromosomes Cancer 2016, DOI 10.1002/gcc.22379, PMID 27223121.
Status: evaluated published aggregate; unavailable individual evidence. All ten author-labelled EMC cases are represented in the published qPCR summary. Selection required complete or partial loss of SMARCB1 staining. Four selected miRNAs were tested; miR-381 is the only one within the proposed domain. The result is that at least one of miR-206, miR-381 or miR-671-5p exceeded a threefold threshold in each selected EMC. It does not identify which marker was elevated in each patient. The 96 SMARCB1-positive STS controls lack an exposed subtype roster, so additional EMC controls cannot be ruled out. EMC fusion confirmation, donor IDs, normalization, technical replication and individual values remain unverified. The original abstract and author affiliations were read; Wiley's full paper and observed 23.8 KB Table S1 link failed. See sapi2016-core.json, followup3-receipts.json and SEARCH-LOG.txt. This panel cannot by itself demonstrate coordinated domain expression, even if all values are recovered.
