2026-10-04T18:31Z — reassessment outcome
Neither additional question survives the decisive feasibility and scientific-value checks; no new proposal is advanced.

1. Telomere maintenance as an EMC phenotype.
Actual primary measurements exist, so this was not rejected for lack of any EMC data. Koelsche2014 (DOI10.1186/1756-9966-33-33; PMC4022359) reports eight EMC cases, internal IDs203–210, all wild type at the assayed TERT promoter hotspots; the original supplementary workbook was downloaded and all eight rows evaluated. A ninth text hit is the abbreviation legend, not a patient. Liau2015 (DOI10.1038/modpathol.2015.114; PMID26428317) Table2 directly reports no ATRX loss in three EMC and no ALT phenotype in the two tested by telomere FISH. These are already published, tiny, non-linked cohorts; no fusion-partner or outcome pairing supports a new clinical relation, and TERT hotspot negativity does not establish absence of telomerase activity.
The fresh pan-sarcoma telomeric-content study (DOI10.1038/s41525-023-00369-6) was then examined with all four small supplemental workbooks. Its public tables combine chondrosarcoma or use broad bone/soft-tissue labels; no individual authenticated EMC telomeric-content/fusion/outcome crosswalk is released. The primary paper states that Foundation data require a study review, author contact and data transfer agreement; survival data additionally require licensing. No outreach, paid resource or access bypass occurred. We cannot turn that inaccessible individual information into a measured EMC association. Existing published negatives are not a new discovery, and merging them as if independent paired ALT/TERT assays would be invalid. Reopen only with linked EMC measurements, biological variation and a consequential unanswered contrast.
Primary sources and exact retrieved bytes are in reassessment/receipts.json, tert2014.xml, tert2014-supplement.xlsx, alt2015.html, alt-table2.html and telomeric-content2023.html. The review was used only to find the original Liau source; primary table values determine this decision.

2. DNA methylation subgroups linked to fusion partner or phenotype.
Koelsche2021 (DOI10.1038/s41467-020-20603-4; GSE140686) releases measured methylation arrays and clinical sample annotations. All four supplementary workbooks were examined, including all ten reference EMC rows. All ten are listed as primary tumors and FFPE, with tissue site, batch, supplier and a tumor-content estimate; no individual fusion partner, outcome or recurrence condition is released. Thus the prespecified subgroup/phenotype relation lacks its decisive explanatory variable or endpoint. Most EMC reference cases share a Jena source and array batch, so an unsupervised split without genotype/clinical metadata would also risk a batch artifact.
The validation table contains one initial EMC diagnosis, VALIDATION_SAMPLE54, which the original investigators reclassified to sclerosing epithelioid fibrosarcoma with EWSR1::CREB3L4 support and a strong SEF classifier score. It is not silently counted as an authentic additional EMC or presented as our discovery. The author's class description already reports largely flat EMC copy-number profiles and a distinct diagnostic methylation class. Recreating that classification or finding a cluster without an interpretable independent phenotype would be a methodological exercise. No full methylation matrix was downloaded because the proposed novel biological contrast fails the metadata gate. Raw data remain accessible and unevaluated for other future questions; this is a scoped rejection, not a claim of corpus exhaustion.
All individual reference EMC and validation-label rows are retained in reassessment/feasibility-results.json and methylation-tables.json. evaluate_reassessment.py reproduces row eligibility and receipt-hash checks.
