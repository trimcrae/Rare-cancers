# Round 3 immune-state and treatment pilot

Decision on 2026-10-04: **Shelve the standalone paper.** No new EMC immune-state/response relation or within-patient treatment-induced change was demonstrated. This is a failure of the available measurement linkage to answer our exploratory question, not a negative biological result or evidence that immunotherapy cannot benefit EMC.

The original question and stopping rules remain in `PLAN.txt`. Before examining a new molecular contrast, the pilot required authentic EMC identity, an individual assay/outcome crosswalk, and a comparison that could distinguish the proposed relation from treatment, assay, and donor differences. No newly recovered source passed those gates. No response-rate pooling, outcome-trained biomarker threshold, regression, or expression reprocessing was performed.

## Actual evaluations

- Subramanian et al., Nature Cancer 2024, DOI [10.1038/s43018-024-00743-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-024-00743-y): every GSE213065 library was evaluated: 87 libraries from 54 explicitly identified people, with LMS/UPS/SS/LPS/MPNST labels and no EMC. Its seven single-cell and four spatial specimens also contain only LMS/UPS. All 22 supplement sheets were inspected; clinical tables S18/S19/S21 contain only those five non-EMC histologies. Gene NR4A3 appearing in a result table does not authenticate a tumor as EMC. Distinct single-cell specimen titles were initially counted as individuals by a fallback; the final script corrects that field to null when no individual field is provided. The seven single-cell specimens are not claimed to be seven independent patients.
- Moura et al., CCR 2024, DOI [10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-24-1782](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-24-1782): the full author manuscript, all five supplementary figures and four supplementary tables were evaluated, including visual inspection. Four EMC patients belong to IMMUNOSARC I; three EMC are listed in the paired transcriptomic subset and three in the paired blood subset. Each is 3/4 of the EMC cohort; that arithmetic is only assay coverage, not a biological finding, and the two sets of three cannot be assumed identical. Public paired plots do not identify EMC points or supply sample IDs. The paper explicitly states individual data are not public because consent did not permit it. Its experimental PBMC/tumor cells all come from leiomyosarcoma patients, not EMC. The pan-sarcoma increases in CD8 and six transcripts/decrease in NR4A1 were already reported by the authors and cannot be reassigned to EMC. The patent describing the same cohort is not independent evidence.
- Starzer et al., JITC 2021, DOI [10.1136/jitc-2020-001458](https://doi.org/10.1136/jitc-2020-001458): all 35 patient listings in supplement S1/S2 and both supplement containers were evaluated. Center 1 case 7 is explicitly labeled extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma, male 54, hip primary, pembrolizumab, best iRECIST PD, PFS 3 months. Center 1 case 5 is labeled only myxoid chondrosarcoma of the “rips,” male 65, pembrolizumab, PD, PFS 5.9 months; its EMC identity remains unresolved. No patient-specific numerical immune values or methylation matrix are supplied. Main figures 2/3 provide only broad soft-tissue/osteosarcoma categories and no patient IDs; the heatmap cannot be linked to case 7. These patients may overlap Schur2017 Vienna; they are not counted as independent validations. The published methylation classifier selected differential probes using response labels, so reusing its fitted clusters as validation would also be circular.
- Galitskiy et al., Annals of Oncology 2025, DOI [10.1016/j.annonc.2025.08.310](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2025.08.310): the abstract identity, BostonGene JSMO announcement, author list, and public patient-page trail were checked. The actual regimen, response measurement, assay numbers, and patient overlap are not available in the retrieved public material. A signup form was not submitted. A title about effective treatment is not an evaluable biomarker-response observation.
- Calukovic2024 Tübingen thesis: the full public thesis reports a nivolumab responder described as “extraskeletal chondrosarcoma,” high TMB24.7 mutations/Mb and germline MSH6; treatment stopped after 10 cycles due to serious adverse events. It does not authenticate myxoid/NR4A3 identity or provide sufficient individual biomarker/outcome crosswalk for this EMC analysis. The cohort-level MSS statement is not converted to a case-level MMR-protein/biallelic conclusion. The Japanese EMC26 PMS2 Q160* case evaluated by the genomics worker has no individual MSI/TMB/MMR-protein/allelic information and no immune-therapy outcome; its aggregate pazopanib outcomes cannot supply this missing link.
- Pollack et al., JAMA Oncology2020, DOI [10.1001/jamaoncol.2020.3689](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2020.3689): after the lead expanded the storage allowance, the10.46MB archive was streamed in memory and the relevant1.72MB Supplement2 retained. Its16pages and main response figure were evaluated, with visual checks of the IHC/response/gene tables. One EMC is enrolled, but eTable2 gives aggregate PD-L1/TIL counts for29assessable specimens, eFigure4 pools “Chondro,” and the main waterfall pools all other histologies. The serum assay has technical duplicate measurements, not independent donors. No EMC-specific measured immune value, pre/post pair, or response point can be assigned from these published outputs.
- Kelly et al., JAMA Oncology2020, DOI [10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.6152](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.6152): the main source confirms oneEMC and describes a potentially relevant individual PD-L1/histology/outcome eTable4 in Supplement1 (Supplement2 is the protocol). The actual432.8KB Supplement1 remains unevaluated after source-linked PMC/EuropePMC/publisher attempts returned challenge HTML,403or404. No proof-of-work challenge was bypassed. The missing table is material; baseline/on-treatment immune-state feasibility remains unresolved for this case. All named responders in the main text have other histologies, but that does not recover the EMC-specific biomarker values or stable/progressive disease distinction.

## Value and validity

There is no estimable new EMC-specific relation in this packet. A re-expression of known progressions, or a collection of a few selected responders and nonresponders on different regimens, would not establish a biomarker or efficacy difference. The original GSE213065 lead is unsuitable for this question because it contains no EMC; the IMMUNOSARC and Starzer evidence is relevant but insufficiently linked, not negative. Broad sarcoma biology and non-EMC ex vivo experiments cannot substitute for EMC measurements.

Reopen only with an authenticated patient-level crosswalk linking EMC, pre-specified immune measurements, timing and actual response, or paired before/during-treatment EMC biopsies with units/assay provenance and donor identity. A single selected paired case could support a descriptive measured change, but cannot by itself establish prediction, causation, efficacy, or generality. Galitskiy case details or public IMMUNOSARC individual data could change feasibility; the resulting contribution would still require a fresh novelty/value assessment.

## Coverage limits

`COVERAGE.md` distinguishes evaluation, unavailable linkage, unsuitable assays and unfinished accessible analysis. Pollack's relevant supplement is now evaluated; Kelly's relevant supplement remains unassessed after the documented retrieval failures, and other identified primary-trial evidence remains pending. These gaps block any manuscript advancement. This shelved pilot does not claim exhaustive immunotherapy literature coverage or prove that no accessible individual EMC crosswalk exists. Source files with successful HTTP status but HTML challenge content are flagged as failed containers in `source-evaluation.json`, never treated as PDF/XLSX evidence.

Reproducible checks: `inspect_sources.py`, `inspect_tables.py`, `audit_final_sources.py`; outputs `geo-eligibility.json`, `subramanian-table-eligibility.json`, `moura-supp-tables.json`, `starzer2021-supp-text.txt`, and `source-evaluation.json`. Retrieval scripts/receipts retain dates, URLs, hashes, limits and actual failures. Original round 2 evaluations of Rosenbaum2026, Schur2017, IMMUNOSARC2020 and Hindi2025 were reused without new efficacy calculations. Independent review corrected the description of IMMUNOSARCII: it is a second-stage/extension under the same NCT03277924 registration, so the name does not establish donor independence. Starzer case7 is authenticated only by the authors' histologic label, without reported fusion confirmation.
