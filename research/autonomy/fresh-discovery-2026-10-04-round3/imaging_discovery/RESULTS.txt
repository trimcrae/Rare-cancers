2026-10-04 — public imaging discovery

Decision: SHELVE this scoped standalone imaging inference. No new EMC biological finding was demonstrated, and no authenticated accessible patient-level EMC imaging contrast survived the source gate. This is not evidence that EMC lacks an imaging phenotype or that all public imaging evidence has been exhausted.

The complete TCIA Soft-tissue-Sarcoma clinical workbook contains 51 individually labelled patients and no EMC or chondrosarcoma diagnosis. STS_022 is myxoid fibrosarcoma (MSKCC category MFH), STS_043/044 are mixoid liposarcoma, and extraskeletal osteosarcoma/Ewing rows are different diseases. Generic or undifferentiated labels remain the supplied diagnosis, not covertly assigned EMC. The collection consists of pretreatment FDG-PET/CT and anatomical MRI, so it also cannot supply the proposed paired treatment contrast. No DICOM was downloaded.

QIN-SARCOMA is currently public in the official collection page; the old restricted wiki status is obsolete. Its entire 2168-series manifest contains 15 patients and 38 studies, but none has an admitting diagnosis or case-linked histology. All patients and their available conditions are preserved in eligibility-evaluation.json. The cited Meyer2013 report has 16 clinical patients, 11 entering MRI, and no reported EMC histology; it cannot automatically identify the 15 deposited patients. The directly related Huang2016 paper reports all 20 individual histologies, with no explicit EMC; generic spindle/pleomorphic classes and the missing collection-to-paper crosswalk prevent a definitive hidden-case exclusion. The studies also describe different early treatment timing, so visits cannot silently be equated. This is an authentication gap, not an MRI biological null.

Three source-linked imaging studies explicitly include EMC, and their actual primary articles and original supplements were evaluated. Benhabib et al.2025 (DOI10.1002/jmri.29691) includes 12 EMC within 322 malignant myxoid lesions; results and supplemental tables pool these with other myxoid sarcomas. Its illustrated individual feature maps are myxoma and myxofibrosarcoma, not EMC. Crombé et al.2024 (DOI10.1038/s41698-024-00616-8) includes 6 EMC among 225 MRI cases, 4 with RNA; MRI/radiomics and individual crosswalks are available only on author request. The other 2024 multimodal study (DOI10.1038/s41698-024-00695-7) includes 4 EMC among 287, with 2 in training and 2 in test; its individual data are controlled at MSK. Its four illustrative test images are not histologically identified and cannot be assigned to the two EMC test cases. These cohort counts must not be summed as independent donors without crosswalks.

Crombe availability was tested, not accepted from the paper's declaration. GSE262937 currently returns an HTML statement that the accession is not yet approved by GEO curators, with no sample data. All 14 pages of the main supplement and all 5 pages of the reporting summary were inspected. ST4-ST7 and the deep-learning annex point to five public OneDrive links, each actually returning HTTP403. Those are access failures, not proof of no underlying data. The reporting summary still describes ongoing GEO uploads, and no alternative public specimen mapping is supplied. No private upload path or reviewer token was guessed, and no outreach was performed.

Prior art removes the value of claiming that functional imaging changes can accompany or precede dimensional response. Stacchiotti2012 (DOI10.1186/2045-3329-2-22) already reports two EWSR1-CHN-positive EMC cases, including a complete PET response after four weeks of sunitinib and reduced CT size/contrast uptake, interruption-associated progression and response after restarting. Those published observations are retained as prior knowledge; they are neither a new analysis nor proof that every functional imaging metric measures viable cell number. A useful future study would require a new case-linked contrast with biological meaning beyond these known responses, appropriate therapy timing and controls, and uncertainty at the donor level.

The prospective conditional pilot therefore did not open. No radiomic feature mining, invented histology, pooled treatment efficacy, unvalidated surrogate for viability, or classifier demonstration was performed. The reusable substantive output is source/case authentication, with a visible stop and all gaps. Older clinical MRI-risk and CPTAC eligibility evaluations remain frozen; their relevant scope is reused with receipts.
