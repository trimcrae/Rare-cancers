TNT EMC follow-up — 2026-10-04

Decision: SHELVE this standalone immune-response inference. No new disease finding was demonstrated. This bounded evaluation closes a source omission; an additional published observation is not itself discovery.

The primary 2023 TNT study (Chawla et al., DOI 10.3389/fonc.2023.1116937; NCT03886311; PMC10206273) enrolled one histologically labelled EMC among 50 patients. Its Figure 1 includes one bar labelled Extra myx Chondro among 39 patients with week-6 imaging, with a small decrease in target-lesion summed diameters (visually approximately 4%; no exact numerical value provided). The independent reviewer read the same label, direction, timepoint and approximate magnitude. Molecular diagnostic testing is not reported; this uncertainty does not justify discarding the authors' EMC diagnosis.

This is the actual available EMC measurement: a single baseline-to-week-6 target-lesion contrast during combination trabectedin, nivolumab and intratumoral talimogene laherparepvec. It cannot establish overall RECIST stable disease or progression because no EMC-linked new-lesion/non-target assessment is released. The paper names its three partial responders as uterine leiomyosarcoma, dedifferentiated liposarcoma and undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma. The EMC bar is not a partial response. The cohort median PFS, OS, disease-control proportion and surgical outcome must not be assigned to this individual.

Every main table, all five original supplementary tables and both longitudinal imaging figures were inspected. Table 3 provides 39 numbered treatment histories, but no histology crosswalk. The spider plot has no case/histology mapping. Supplemental death, withdrawal and last-contact lists are also unlinked to EMC. Consequently, exact drug exposure, adverse events, durability, PFS and OS cannot be reconstructed for the EMC patient. Figure 5's paired immune portraits, 70% to 34% malignant-cell fractions, surgical necrosis and sustained remission explicitly belong to rhabdomyosarcoma. They provide no measured EMC immune response or mechanism.

The registry has no posted results. The 2020 abstract describes protocol/enrolment, the 2021 report is an overlapping interim TNT analysis, and the 2025 report evaluates LMS/LPS only. None releases an additional EMC-specific measured condition. The same-center 2018 trabectedin plus nivolumab paper contains two generically labelled chondrosarcomas, including one partial responder, without myxoid/fusion subtype, identifiers or overlap crosswalk. Their EMC eligibility remains unresolved; they cannot be counted as two additional EMC cases or as independent of later trial participation.

Scientific value: these observations do not test an important EMC immune mechanism, identify a predictive biomarker, or isolate immune therapy from the two other treatment components. There is no disease-specific controlled comparison, case-linked paired biology or useful response denominator to analyze. No p-value, pooled response estimate or invented precision was generated. Reopening requires a materially new, EMC-linked measurement/outcome crosswalk capable of answering a useful disease question; more rewording of this published bar is insufficient.
