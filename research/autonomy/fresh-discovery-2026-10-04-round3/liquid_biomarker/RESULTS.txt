2026-10-04. Decision: shelve the standalone liquid-biomarker contribution.

No new useful EMC shedding or monitoring finding was demonstrated. The observations below are published measurements authenticated during this campaign, not new discoveries.

Eastley 2018 (DOI 10.18632/oncotarget.24278; PMC5828212) includes donor 8, a 72-year-old woman with histology-labelled EMC, progressive metastatic disease and summed target lesions of 97 mm. Total cfDNA was 40.6 ng/mL, but the hotspot panel detected no ctDNA. Only donors 1–5 had matched tumor tissue. Without a verified target in donor 8, panel nondetection does not show absent shedding. Preserve donor 4 separately: a man aged 86, labelled 'Soft Tissue Chondrosarcoma', with progressive disease, 44 mm target lesions, cfDNA of 9.0 ng/mL and reported ctDNA evidence. His plasma PIK3CA I391M and synonymous TP53 R213R calls were absent from matched tissue and blood cells. Neither EMC identity nor a clean tumor origin for those calls is established.

Eastley 2020 (DOI 10.3390/ijms21124483; PMC7349923) includes patient 3, a man aged 63.1 with author-labelled ExMC and a tumor volume of 588 cm3. His tissue VWDE Cys1302Arg variant had 42% VAF, but was not detected at one intraoperative and four postoperative plasma timepoints. Total cfDNA fell from 15 to 7.8 ng/mL at the first postoperative draw, a 48% decrease reproduced by descriptive arithmetic. He had planned marginal surgery, reported adjuvant radiotherapy and no recurrence during 30.1 months of follow-up. These are five dependent observations from one donor, without a recurrence-positive condition or publicly exposed VWDE dilution series, sample-specific detection limit or droplet counts. Total cfDNA includes normal-cell DNA; its decrease is not tumor-DNA clearance. The initial LPHN2 label in a brief to the lead was wrong and was corrected in AMENDMENT-01. Sex and age distinguish this donor from the 2018 EMC donor; shared control sourcing is not independent validation.

McConnell 2020 (DOI 10.3390/cancers12123627; PMC7761870) evaluated 12 selected metastatic donors with paired tissue and plasma. M2 was a man aged 65 with a chest-wall primary, lung metastases and radiotherapy three months before sampling. Preserve the authors' diagnosis 'Extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma' alongside their MEAF6-PHF1 tissue/plasma fusion: no NR4A3 co-fusion, original pathology report or subsequent reclassification was located. The matching thesis table reports plasma VAF of 0.24% with the same donor IDs and sequencing depths, so it is the same case. MEAF6-PHF1 has been measured in ossifying fibromyxoid tumors (Antonescu 2014, DOI 10.1002/gcc.22132), but a gene alone cannot authorize reclassification. A single observed detection is not an experimentally established limit of detection, and no EMC serial trajectory was released.

Czachor 2025 (JCO supplement e23559; DOI 10.1200/JCO.2025.43.16_suppl.e23559) reports patient 9 with low-grade, author-labelled EMC, a 14.3 cm primary on MRI, clear chest CT and a baseline personalized ctDNA value of 0.92. The unit is not specified in the recovered primary abstract/table. Later blood and MRI values are N/A; the patient awaits postoperative follow-up. The abstract's aggregate ctDNA disappearance cannot be assigned to this patient. Different targets, assays and disease states prevent pooling this positive baseline with the prior negative assays as a sensitivity estimate.

DR6 2012 (DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0036525; PMC3342254) measured serum from three EMC cases: two refractory to chemotherapy and one chemotherapy-naive. Figure 3D pools them with 14 other undifferentiated sarcomas. The 41 serial pairs and two third samples during sirolimus/cyclophosphamide treatment have no EMC crosswalk in text or figures. Duplicate western blots and normalized integrated intensity are technical measurements, not validated concentrations or independent donors. Independent review of the original figures confirmed this limitation.

Heinhuis 2020 (PMC7352477) includes two generic 'Extraskeletal chondrosarcoma' platelet samples, one in training and one in algorithm evaluation, with none in independent validation. Their myxoid subtype, fusion identity and individual scores cannot be authenticated. Pastuszak 2021 (PMC8486571) explicitly reuses that cohort with different quality filtering; its four supplementary tables provide sample splits but no subtype crosswalk. GSE158508 deposits the new ovarian cohort. The Heinhuis S2.xls is described as a transcript classifier panel, not a patient clinical table. Its original bytes are retained; the binary workbook was not decoded because the required reader is unavailable. No values are asserted from it.

Asano 2019 (DOI 10.1038/s41467-019-09143-8; PMC6428849) and GSE124158 were evaluated through both platforms' complete sample metadata (1,412 records) and the 18-page supplement. Labels provide broad malignant, intermediate and benign categories, not a usable EMC subtype crosswalk. This does not establish absence of EMC. Expression remains accessible but was not analyzed without authenticated identities. The authors already reported that their serum-miRNA classifier did not increase with tumor burden; their paired tissues were not EMC.

Axi-STS 2023 (DOI 10.1038/s41416-023-02416-6; PMC10628187) enrolled one patient as angiosarcoma who was later reclassified as EMC by local molecular histology and excluded from response analysis. The full appendix was evaluated. Proteomic discovery used eight leiomyosarcomas pooled into four groups, with technical replicates. Wider ACTG1 ELISA measurements from 93 patients and CRP/albumin analyses do not expose an EMC patient ID or histology crosswalk. Trial eligibility for sampling does not show that this person's assay was completed or released. No protein value or outcome was assigned to EMC.

The strongest alternatives remain differences in assay coverage, DNA input, preanalytics, identity and disease state. A monitoring claim requires serial tumor-specific measurements during actual disease changes, with sampling times and detectability controls. Repackaging discordant single-case assays or calculating the already published total-cfDNA decrease does not establish such a relation or justify a paper. Pending evidence and identity gaps remain in COVERAGE.txt. This decision is not evidence that EMC cannot shed detectable DNA.
