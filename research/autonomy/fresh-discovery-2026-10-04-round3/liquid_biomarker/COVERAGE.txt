Coverage dated 2026-10-04. Each status refers to actual evaluation or an explicit limitation. This bounded rejection is not a claim that all possible public EMC evidence has been exhausted.

Evaluated EMC or potentially EMC measurements

- Eastley 2018, PMC5828212: original Tables 1 and 2, methods and all donor labels were examined. Donor 8 is EMC-labelled; donor 4 has ambiguous 'Soft Tissue Chondrosarcoma' identity and is retained separately. Panel/tissue constraints and both plasma observations are evaluated in RESULTS.txt.
- Eastley 2020, PMC7349923: original main paper and both original DOCX supplements were examined. Patient 3 has one intraoperative plus four postoperative blood observations, one verified tumor variant, no reported recurrence and 30.1 months of follow-up. Sample-specific droplet counts, detection limits and full individual sampling dates are not exposed. Supplementary assay examples from patients 6 and 22 are not this EMC donor.
- McConnell 2020, PMC7761870: all 12 tissue/plasma pairs were examined for eligibility. M2 is retained under the authors' EMC diagnosis, with a MEAF6-PHF1 fusion conflict and no exposed NR4A3 co-fusion or diagnostic report. The thesis is the same donor series, not a second cohort: https://pure.qub.ac.uk/files/203508765/FINAL_Thesis_18.4.20_pdf.pdf . Its Table 3.5 was read through indexed primary PDF text; the full thesis was not downloaded. Antonescu's primary OFMT study (PMID 24285434; DOI 10.1002/gcc.22132; PMC4053209) confirms MEAF6-PHF1 in that other tumor type but does not reclassify M2. The initially drafted comparator DOI was corrected before packet freeze. The earlier five EMC tissue tests in assay development do not establish five measured plasma cases.
- Czachor 2025, DOI 10.1200/JCO.2025.43.16_suppl.e23559: the indexed primary table and author-institution mirror were examined, including patient 9's baseline and unavailable later values. Primary link: https://www.asco.org/abstracts-presentations/251429/ . Retained mirror: https://www.researchwithrowan.com/en/publications/correlation-between-circulating-tumor-dna-ctdna-and-disease-statu/ . Unit, fusion and postoperative measurement are unreported in the recovered evidence.
- DR6 2012, PMC3342254: all three EMC entries in Table 2 and original Figures 3D and 4 were examined, including independent visual review. No supplementary-material nodes exist in the source. Pooled histology and unlabelled serial bars cannot be assigned to EMC individuals.
- Heinhuis 2020, PMC7352477: original cohort and both generic extraskeletal-chondrosarcoma entries were evaluated. S1.xlsx and both supplementary figures were read. S2.xls was retained but not decoded; its original caption describes the classifier transcript panel. Individual subtype, fusion, scores and serial linkage remain unavailable.
- Pastuszak 2021, PMC8486571: the Heinhuis cohort is explicitly reused, with different quality filtering. All four supplementary workbook sheets were inspected; the sample-split table lacks subtype labels. GSE158508's original record describes the newly deposited ovarian cohort. No independent EMC cohort is established.
- Axi-STS 2023, PMC10628187: the one molecularly reclassified EMC entrant, main paper and all six appendix sections were evaluated. The eight-donor discovery proteomics experiment is exclusively leiomyosarcoma. ACTG1, CRP and albumin measurement linkage to the EMC entrant is not exposed; do not assume either inclusion or absence.

Pending identity, access or linkage

- Asano 2019, PMC6428849/GSE124158: all 1,412 metadata records (42 on GPL18941 and 1,370 on GPL21263) and the original supplement were examined. Broad malignant/intermediate/benign labels prevent EMC authentication, despite the paper's general data-availability statement. The series lists raw arrays but no separate clinical crosswalk. Accessible expression remains unevaluated for EMC; subtype, donor and classifier-split identities must be established before a meaningful pilot.
- Tsoi 2021, PMC8479566, DOI 10.1302/2046-3758.109.BJR-2021-0014.R1: main paper and the six tumor-informed cases were evaluated (three MFS, one UPS, one myxoid liposarcoma and one osteosarcoma). Twelve 'other' soft-tissue sarcomas in the total-cfDNA arm remain unresolved. The Europe PMC supplement response did not provide the mentioned Supplementary Table ii. Original publisher/table follow-up remains pending; this is not proof of zero EMC.
- Bui 2023, DOI 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-23-0250: the abstract describes 30 advanced sarcoma donors and serial personalized ctDNA. The primary PDF is listed as available to purchase; DOI web retrieval failed. Associated Subramanian/GSE213065 tissue data contain no EMC, but that subset does not establish coverage of all 30 trial donors. Full trial subtype and supplement linkage remain unresolved. No efficacy pooling was performed.
- Anderson 2025, PMID 38905450, DOI 10.1097/CORR.0000000000003161: indexed primary results name several non-EMC histologies among 13 plasmas. Full XML returned HTTP 500 twice, BioC returned 404 and the PMC page showed a browser challenge. All original Table 1 rows were not evaluated, so eligibility remains unresolved rather than verified zero EMC. No challenge bypass was attempted.
- Takami, Hoshi, Nakata and Okamoto, SICOT 2019 poster 53737: the original program lists an EMC case with high serum hyaluronic acid. Actual values, assay, serial conditions and poster body were not located by exact-title, author or Japanese/English marker searches. Program: https://www.sicot.org/sites/default/files/images/Muscat/eposter-list.pdf . This is unavailable potentially relevant evidence, not evaluated numerical support.

Sources unsuitable for an EMC blood-monitoring inference

- Braig 2022, PMC9499617: all three measured donors are MFS, LMS or UPS; 25 serial plasmas contain no EMC donor.
- Furukawa 2025, PMC11963259: all ten measured donors are three Ewing sarcomas, six myxoid liposarcomas and one synovial sarcoma.
- Joch 2025, PMC11974511: all 34 donors comprise 18 Ewing, seven synovial sarcoma, four alveolar rhabdomyosarcoma, two myxoid liposarcoma, one DSRCT, one CIC-DUX4 and one EWSR1-NFATC2 case. The supplement lists EMC fusions as possible panel targets, but none is underlined as represented. Actual underlining was parsed from the DOCX, not inferred from a keyword hit.
- Demoret 2019, PMC6966562: all 24 completed cases are six each of LMS, GIST, dedifferentiated liposarcoma and UPS. Three failed enrolled tests have no exposed EMC identity.
- Park 2025 abstract 11511 and 2026 PMID 42240449, DOI 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-26-0323: the primary abstract reports 32 donors with 228 plasmas, consisting of 12 MFS, ten UPS, six dedifferentiated liposarcoma, two pleomorphic liposarcoma, one myxoid liposarcoma and one LMS. The later report is not an independent cohort. EGA data are controlled, not accessible public EMC evidence.
- Omori 2022, PMC9527174: fusion-confirmed EMC pleural metastasis with VEGF/TGF-beta tissue staining. Tissue staining and nondiagnostic fluid cytology do not measure a circulating blood biomarker.
- Lung case 2012, DOI 10.1186/1746-1596-7-112: severe anemia and negative blood NSE despite tissue NSE staining, without molecular EMC identity or a tumor-specific blood assay. Buttock case 2023, DOI 10.3389/fonc.2023.1249928: routine normal blood tumor markers without a serial comparison. These known case observations do not establish tumor-specific monitoring.

Verified reuse and search scope

The previous frozen immune packet's GSE213065 evaluation includes all 87 libraries from 54 explicit donors and all 22 Subramanian supplement sheets. Source/result hashes are recorded in evaluation.json. This supports no EMC in that deposited subset only; no expression analysis was repeated.

Searches covered EMC, extra-skeletal/extraskeletal, myxoid and NR4A3 with ctDNA, cfDNA, circulating fusion, plasma, serum, hyaluronic acid and DR6; exact primary titles and DOIs; McConnell's thesis and fusion-pathology trails; primary citations in broader sarcoma liquid-biopsy reviews; all Asano GEO platform metadata; Heinhuis/Pastuszak original supplements; Axi-STS reclassification; and Tsoi/Bui/Anderson source trails. Reviews were used to find primary sources, not as measurements. The initial bounded GEO download failed because of size; compact streaming subsequently evaluated every metadata record while stopping before expression values. Bound failures are not scientific exclusions. Access failures and hashes are preserved in receipt JSON files. No UI, browser automation, paid access or outreach was used.
