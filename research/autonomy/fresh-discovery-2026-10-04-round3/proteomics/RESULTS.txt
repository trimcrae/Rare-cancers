Scientific value
There is no authenticated EMC mass-proteomics contrast here to distinguish a
tumor-cell program from tissue composition, platform effects or diagnosis.
No protein-expression differences, dependency, drug sensitivity or clinical
inference were estimated. A failed source gate is not a publishable challenge
to an existing EMC biological claim. Older EMC immunohistochemistry and the
previously evaluated targeted model measurements still exist; this packet
does not describe those observations as absent or repeat them as discoveries.

Reopen only with specimen-level authenticated EMC metadata linked to measured
proteins (including a resolved generic case or publicISKSrelease), sufficient
controls/assay information and a consequential question not already answered
by the original investigators. Freeze that specific biological hypothesis
before protein values, evaluate every eligible EMC condition and resolve
donor/cohort overlap. Unresolved eligible accessible measurements block any
manuscript advancement; no manuscript is proposed by this packet.
