## Select the venue for the manuscript

At each release, record the manuscript and version, distinct contribution, chosen venue, current
primary policy URLs and access date, compatibility with its intended journal, and existing public
or pending copies. Evaluate current author eligibility, article type and subject scope, screening,
fees, DOI/versioning, relevant indexing, duplicate-posting rules and the actual AI-use policy.
Describe Tristan's present affiliation accurately as independent; past University of Rochester
affiliation and prior publications do not establish a current institutional role. Do not invent
a qualification, oversight arrangement or AI-use permission to complete a form.

Research Square is a candidate for completed computational research fitting its accepted types.
Its [article-type guidance](https://support.researchsquare.com/support/solutions/articles/6000271850-what-article-types-do-you-accept-),
read 2026-09-10, accepts research articles, validated method articles and data notes, among other
categories; it excludes hypotheses, opinions, theories, commentaries and ordinary literature
reviews. Screening still applies. Its earlier rejection of the extended ASO report is specific
evidence about that manuscript, not eligibility proof for another manuscript or a program-wide
ban. Conversely, acceptance of another paper does not reverse that rejection.

The [Research Square editorial policy](https://www.researchsquare.com/legal/editorial) returned
HTTP 403 on this workflow update. Its current full screening and AI-use terms therefore remain
unverified here. Recover and read the applicable policy before selecting it for a release; a
missing page or a marketing statement is not permission. If its terms do not fit the actual work,
select another suitable venue per manuscript. There is no blanket replacement venue in this decision.

Zenodo may support preservation under the existing grant and actual deposit conditions. It is
not an unconditional fallback for AI-generated outputs or a substitute for reaching the field.
Its [AI-use policy](https://support.zenodo.org/help/en-gb/13-policies/227-what-is-your-usage-policy-for-generative-ai-for-depositors),
read 2026-09-10, requires a verifiable basis in human-conducted research, accountable human authors,
and disclosure of substantive AI use. It permits assistance such as verified code generation and
data analysis. Check the actual research basis and current terms for each deposit; do not infer
compliance merely from possessing code or a DOI.

## Release when the evidence and package are ready

Follow [the operating protocol](OPERATING_PROTOCOL.md), the existing publish bar and venue-specific
requirements. Before declaring readiness, verify the distinct scientific contribution,
reproducible computation and source provenance, appropriate uncertainty and limitations, and a
truthful disclosure of which tools did what and what the human author verified. AI acceleration
does not establish correctness, clinical efficacy or safety. Preserve negative results and
consolidate overlapping analyses into one coherent paper where appropriate.

The existing independent ultra-reasoning review remains required for each submitted paper or
material scientific revision; preserve its execution and reviewed-artifact evidence. Complete
the existing full publication checks on the frozen release candidate. A venue switch does not
clear scientific blockers or expand publication authority.

Schedule announcements and reviewer outreach for reader attention separately from scientific
readiness. Do not introduce a general three-week interval between distinct completed papers or
delay a needed correction just to suggest a slower research pace. Record actual venue timing
requirements for the selected release and decide fit accordingly.
