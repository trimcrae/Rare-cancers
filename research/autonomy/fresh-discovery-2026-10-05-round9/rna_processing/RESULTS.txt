This is a failure of the proposed endpoint on the processed matrix, not a finding of absent APA and not invalidation of 3SEQ.

The original Brunner2012 study and its Beck2010 protocol establish a poly(A)-selected, anchored oligo-dT library sequenced from the opposite fragment end. Reads normally start upstream of the RNA end; source descriptions give approximately 275bp upstream enrichment and 100–200bp fragmentation / 200–300bp fragments. Original expression peaks are pooled clusters of read starts, with coordinates at the extreme starts, and coding classification means overlap with any coding exon. Normalized expression is per-sample-mean scaled and square-root transformed. Squaring can reverse that transform, but cannot restore cleavage sites, terminal-exon identity, unobserved sites, internal-priming exclusions or library-specific read-to-end distributions.
