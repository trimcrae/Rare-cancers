# EMC discovery outcome, 2026-10-05

The earlier stop was premature. Failed pilots did not establish that the discovery search was exhausted. The search remains open; TMEM266 remains shelved.

One narrow empirical finding now survives independent numerical, scientific-value and source-coverage review: GPNMB bulk RNA is lower in the evaluated source-labelled EMC specimens than in LGFMS. In the RNA-sequencing comparison, all 13 EMC and 13 LGFMS observations have medians **8.04 and 198.26 TPM**, respectively, with a probability-of-lower-expression score of **0.8994**. A separate deposited-array comparison, all 6 EMC versus 17 LGFMS observations, supports the same direction (medians **8.07341 and 10.33387 RMA log2**, score **0.9020**).

The valid high EMC observation LR5081-14 (280.37 TPM) remains included. Exploratory target selection, bulk cellular mixture, incomplete confounder metadata and uncertain cross-study donor overlap limit this to a comparative RNA phenotype. Three EMC specimens are known to overlap between assays; additional overlap is unresolved. Array corroboration is not certified independent-patient replication. The secondary MLPS comparison failed replication in the four FFPE 3SEQ observations. Other native contexts, missing values and sensitivity results remain in the full evidence packet.

The practical contribution is a concrete sampling contrast for future localization work: include LGFMS, ordinary EMC and high-RNA EMC, and assess malignant, myeloid, stromal and vascular/endothelial compartments. This finding does not establish malignant-cell origin, accessible protein, drug response, a diagnostic threshold or clinical benefit.

All 45 evaluated native assay conditions are accounted for, not represented as 45 independent patients. Three authenticated culture identities and their four assay records are also evaluated with incompatible quantities and partial transcript capture explicitly retained. No currently identified ready, accessible, authenticated relevant native GPNMB quantity remains unevaluated in the checked inputs. This is a bounded source-readiness statement, not exhaustion of all possible data or proof of absolute novelty.

Real remaining frontiers include Brenca's mixed RNA runs requiring an author run/case/preparation bridge; unavailable or unmapped Davis, Zullow, Ngo, Filion and Nottley inputs; generic model identities; and unavailable case/measurement/compartment links in the ADC, IHC, CAR and TIC sources. Missing or blocked sources are inconclusive, never negative expression. Access denials were preserved without alternate-body retrieval. A newly authenticated relevant native measurement or equivalent published comparison requires reassessment.

The integrating lead regards this as worthwhile for a concise exploratory RNA report, following independent challenge. No manuscript submission or publication was performed. Scientific discovery completion and authorized repository delivery are separate: this note is delivered by the subsequent verified main checkpoint.
