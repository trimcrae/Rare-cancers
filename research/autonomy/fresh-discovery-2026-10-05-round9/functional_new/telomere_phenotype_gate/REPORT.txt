Question: Can direct activity, telomere-length and ALT measurements establish an informative EMC maintenance phenotype beyond known sparse ALT prevalence?

Approach: Froze the source/assay question before new values, reused the R3 evaluations, checked primary citation identities and pursued direct historical activity/length sources. An independent reviewer checked the Liau primary PDF and assay methods. No RNA, DNA architecture, images or new EMC numerical comparison was analyzed. Full sources and search catalogues remain ignored.

Finding: No new finding survives. Liau has three author-labelled EMC cases but only two tested for ALT; neither tested case showed ALT. The third is unmeasured. The review12-case compilation concerns TERT promoter testing, not ALT or enzyme activity. ATRX retention and promoter testing cannot establish telomerase activity.

Strongest limits: Older direct TRAP/length studies have unresolved individual EMC histology/assay links or source-access gaps. The Montgomery18-case primary abstract lists no explicit EMC; its one dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma cannot be relabelled. No authenticated paired activity/length/ALT input was recovered, and sparse published negatives would add no new contribution. The standalone route is shelved without an activity-absence or exhaustion claim. Reopening requires genuine linked direct assays, controls and a useful contrast beyond prior prevalence; RNA surrogates cannot repair that gap.
