Question: Can public tissue studies support a new, replicated EMC macrophage-versus-T-cell composition contrast, with both absolute counts and relative balance?

Approach: Froze eligibility, marker pairs, controls, zero handling and defeat conditions before new EMC counts. Reused the campaign immune evaluations; checked primary methods, complete printed rosters and permitted supplement text. No images or new EMC numerical comparison were analyzed.

Finding: No new finding survives this source/value pilot. Dancsok2020 already reports sparse EMC macrophage densities and a morphological-TIL ratio; that ratio adds1 and is not a CD3/CD8 result. Umakoshi2023 has one pathology-labelled EMC among75 resections, but assays only macrophage markers. Smolle2021 assays matched CD68/CD3/CD3+CD8 across188 tumors, but25Other remain untyped forEMC and units are percentages, not absolute cells/mm2. Its supplement timedout and linkedDOCX returnedHTML rather than a source file. Public graphics remain uninspected during the daily ban.

Strongest limits: Individual donor/marker/area linkage and independent compatible replication are missing. Grade, translocation status, low cellularity, selected cores, site, treatment, preservation and near-zero T-cell denominators remain plausible alternatives. Marker-positive cells do not establish macrophage polarization or T-cell function. The standalone contrast is shelved with these gaps; recovering sources is useful progress, not a new EMC result or an exhaustion claim.
