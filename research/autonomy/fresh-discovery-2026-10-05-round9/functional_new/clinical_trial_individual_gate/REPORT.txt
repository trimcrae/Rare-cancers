Question: Can public individual trial records link an EMC participant's baseline assay, systemic treatment and outcome well enough to support a useful new biomarker question?

The source gate did not establish that linkage. SAMBA102 includes three author-labelled EMC patients, but the study retrospectively reviews heterogeneous prospective phase I trials. Its methods define clinical sequencing and per-trial response criteria; they do not establish a paired EMC pharmacodynamic protein assay. The authors already compare molecularly matched and unmatched treatment. Recovering an individual best response or match status would reproduce published evidence rather than demonstrate a new useful biomarker relationship.

All 34 baseline histology labels were checked, including every author-labelled EMC and the broad diagnosis groups that remain identity-pending. The selected-benefit Table 4 has eleven histology rows and no explicit EMC row; this does not establish absence of EMC benefit or molecular measurements. No response, survival, mutation or protein-assay values were read. The authentic linked supplementary PDF was not retrieved after one ordinary API timeout. That access failure is separate from the authors' request-only individual data policy.

The independent selinexor source lead may contain pharmacodynamic biopsies, but no accepted primary article was recovered through the two attempted public routes. EMC enrollment and biopsy linkage remain unresolved. T-DXd enrollment, SAMBA101's first-line EMC and four generic extraskeletal chondrosarcomas, all prior clinical source decisions and possible donor reuse retain their verified limits. Their patient or trial counts were not pooled.

Decision: do not open a numerical stage or advance a paper from this source gate. A genuine public participant-to-assay-to-treatment/date-to-orthogonal-endpoint map, compatible controls and a specific useful contrast beyond prior art would justify a new before-value plan and lead review. The current plans freeze source choices only; no biological hypothesis was forced. The wider campaign continues, and the public evidence search is not exhausted.
