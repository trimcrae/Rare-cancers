Question: Does genuinely new Brenca metadata resolve the native case, partner and preparation map enough to unlock useful RNA evidence?

Approach: Reuse the R3/R7/R9 source decisions and inspect complete retained BioSample identity fields plus the SRA outward-link schema. No new requests, expression values, matrices or raw reads were opened.

Finding: The23 BioSamples contain only generic isolate, age, provider, sex and tissue fields. Their links repeat the known project. The23 SRA packages link only to the known Brenca paper and project; no new author manifest emerged. The separate102-row ArrayExpress cartilage study cannot supply Brenca case labels. The review therefore endorses the current source-specific no-go before values.

Limits: The native paper has12 cases,11 engineered conditions, five matched frozen preparations and six additional expression-array conditions. These are not independent patient counts. Eight engineered aliases are already mapped; the other15 records remain unresolved. Current23 libraries do not prove that every paired preparation was released. Source labels and library metadata cannot remove partner, sex, grade or handling confounding.

Value: A valid new native metadata map might permit a useful RNA-only question beyond published partner-associated neural biology. No such new input emerged here, and the failed R8 panel remains shelved. This is not a finding of biological absence or campaign exhaustion. Reopening needs an explicit public case/run/preparation bridge followed by a useful fixed question and lead review before outcomes.
