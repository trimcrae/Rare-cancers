Question: Could any of the 116 culture preparations outside the prior tissue pilot contain identifiable native EMC evidence?
Approach: Froze a new field mask and re-extracted all 138 records directly from the cached original. Only specimen/model, condition and assay labels were used. An exact retained GEO design label supplied the parent identity of two otherwise bare clone names.
Finding: All 22 tissue RNA records are source-labelled MLPS (12) or ES/EWS-FLI1 (10). The other 116 are MLPS-labelled lines (36), MLPS-linked clone preparations (32), or mesenchymal stem-cell lines (48). Thirty culture preparations declare transcriptomic RNA, while 86 declare genomic-source assays. One record called RNA-Seq has a genomic source and cannot be accepted as mRNA from that strategy label alone. No native EMC record is authenticated at the inspected source-label scope.
Meaning: The culture records now have accountable source-level dispositions; they are not globally excluded. Their RNA observations could provide separate comparison contexts after annotation/assay review, but they cannot be substituted for native EMC. The paper’s seven EMC RNA observations still need their actual file and clinical-specimen mapping.
Limits: Author metadata are not independent pathology or model-fidelity validation. Literal name variants, technical preparations and donor identity are distinct. No GPNMB quantities, gene matrices, protocols, genomic mechanisms or clinical outcomes were read. This fresh check is independent of the earlier projection, but uses the same researcher and original source; it is not an independent experimental cohort or external scientific review.
