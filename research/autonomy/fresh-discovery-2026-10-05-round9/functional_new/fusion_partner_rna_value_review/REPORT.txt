Question: Could the complete fixed R8 neurosecretory and antigen RNA family reveal a useful native EMC association with fusion partner?

Approach: Review source labels, methods, existing decisions and published prior-art scope before new subgroup values. The full family contains six primary targets and six context genes. Previous results are known, so the proposal is exploratory; the failed R8 localization and uptake claims remain shelved.

Finding: The actual author metadata gives Driver=Fusion for all13 EMC specimens. The primary workbook gives only Fusion=Yes, gradeNA and sex9M/2F/2unknown. Neither supplies an EWSR1-versus-TAF15 partition. Brenca has12 valid published partner diagnoses, but the measurement-to-case map remains unresolved; its paired preparations and engineered conditions cannot create independent native groups. Current evidence therefore does not support a numerical comparison.

Value: A reproducible RNA-only partner association could be useful. It would need an informative complete-family addition beyond the already published neural differentiation and partner-related transcription, with valid labels and donor, sex, grade, handling and assay controls. Gene symbols missing from article text do not prove novelty. Protein accessibility, uptake, dependency and causality would remain separate questions.

Limits: All relevant public native conditions and contrary evidence stay evaluated, reused or pending at their recorded confidence. A partial historical label does not establish a comparison, and missing labels or probes are not zeros. The review reports a source-specific no-go, not lack of a biological association or exhaustion of the campaign.
