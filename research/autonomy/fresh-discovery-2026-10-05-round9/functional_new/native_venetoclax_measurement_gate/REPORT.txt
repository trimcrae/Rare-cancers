Question: Do all public native EMC venetoclax assays support a useful new test of the actual published sensitivity or combination claim?
Approach: Reused complete prior 40/221/24 drug measurements and model authentication, then inspected dose/control/assay metadata and numerical-masked published claims from the retained original. No new response quantities, plots or digitization.
Finding: The paper already states that venetoclax monotherapy did not validate in either native USZ model. Its Results and Figure6 support carfilzomib+venetoclax and carfilzomib+doxorubicin; Discussion instead mentions venetoclax+doxorubicin, which cannot authenticate an additional experiment. Both models have six-day ATP assays and source-defined dose/interaction contexts. NCC has a legitimate venetoclax screen observation, but exact exposure/control compatibility remains unverified and it is absent from the selected IC50 subset.
Decision: No new quantitative stage or standalone paper is justified by the current source gate. Monotherapy nonvalidation and model-specific combination behavior are already published; a pair-label correction is not a disease finding. Public plotted curves remain pending numerical analysis, while full processed dose matrices/replicates are unlocated. No negative response, dependency or clinical-efficacy inference is made from missingness.
Next: Reopen only for a complete compatible dose/control source or independent native combination measurements that can answer a specified consequential question beyond the published behavior. Keep all native/adverse/model and donor-overlap gaps visible. The campaign remains unexhausted.
