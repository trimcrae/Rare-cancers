Diffusion-MRI source/assay/value gate — 5 October 2026

The current subtype-error numerical stage is NO-GO before ADC values. The
2017 primary study includes two pathology-labelled EMC conditions and publishes
40 ADC/texture rows, but its workbook labels rows only by ID and benign/malignant
status. Neither the workbook nor the main body/captions supplies an explicit
histology-to-ID crosswalk. All two EMC, ten MLPS and three MFS conditions remain
unlinked. Age, sex, volume, examples or figure filenames cannot reconstruct them.
Imaging was before the procedure; prior treatment is unresolved, not assumed naive.

The conditional question was whether the source's aggregate diagnostic result
conceals a useful EMC-specific error beyond known generic myxoid ADC ambiguity.
That generic ambiguity is already primary prior art. No universal routine cutoff
is established by these studies, and no actual clinical benign-management error
is documented here. The refinement, fixed controls and conditional uncertainty/
defeaters are frozen in AMENDMENT-01; the exact compatible published cutoff must
be frozen if a future linked stage opens. No case ADC/texture cell was parsed,
no optimized classifier was fitted and no independent validation was claimed.

Coverage includes all 13-case conventional-imaging sources and the prior evaluated
30-case public clinical source at their original scope. Additional contrary
qualitative reports describe low ADC/restricted septa, high DWI/high ADC and no
restriction; these remain qualitative, with identity limits retained. The 2019
nomogram reports three ESMC conditions, 239 MRIs/236 people and explicitly reuses
40 subjects from 2017. It uses an enhancing axial ROI, rather than whole-volume
histograms. Its random validation is internal and the conditions cannot be pooled
as independent donors. The 2023 one-EMC/125 cohort reuses all 125 subjects from its
2021 source. A separate postoperative study includes one EMC-history donor with
zero recurrent-tumor and two postoperative-change lesions; these are not native
EMC tumor values. No explicit EMC numeric crosswalk was found in these sources.

Full 2024 methods/requested data, unexamined public supplements or graphics,
possible donor overlap and other eligible source identity/measurement links remain
pending or unavailable as itemized in COVERAGE. These block promotion. This gate
rejects a claimed new finding, not ADC biology or effectiveness, and does not
exhaust the campaign. Reopening requires a complete explicit measurement crosswalk
and a useful prospectively frozen comparison, with independent EMC replication
and all relevant contrary/inconclusive conditions resolved.

All original whole sources, catalogues and broad masked parsing remain cache-only.
Only compact source receipts, focused observations, coverage/decision and source-
validation scripts are exported. Validation checks exact bytes, quoted primary
language and S1 ID/benign-malignant schema only; it does not reproduce clinical
results. Independent source/value and omission reviews are bound separately.
No images/UI/DICOM/digitization, restricted lanes, outreach, manuscript or external
scientific record was opened or changed. Root alone integrates and pushes.
