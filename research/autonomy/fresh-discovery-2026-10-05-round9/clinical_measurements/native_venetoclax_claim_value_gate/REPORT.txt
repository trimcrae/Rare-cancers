The primary Bangerter study does not claim that native EMC models are generally sensitive to venetoclax monotherapy. It explicitly says that the moderate USZ20 screening response did not validate as monotherapy in either USZ20 or USZ22. Its Figure6 and direct Results declarations test carfilzomib plus venetoclax and carfilzomib plus doxorubicin, already reporting different synergy/additivity patterns across the two models. The Discussion instead names venetoclax plus doxorubicin. We preserve that inconsistency rather than inventing a third experiment or treating the wording correction as new biology.

The NCC-EMC1-C1 221-drug dataset contains venetoclax, but its selected24 IC50 dataset does not. That is not a negative drug measurement. Its exact exposure/control/readout comparability and venetoclax dose-response availability remain prerequisites. Comparing these already published labels or unmatched endpoints would not yet answer an important new all-model question. This gate therefore does not justify new response-value inspection or a numerical stage.
