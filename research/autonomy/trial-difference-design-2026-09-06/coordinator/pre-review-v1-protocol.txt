## Population, provenance and selection

The target is extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma (EMC), not every use of the acronym EMC.
The population is the archived 6,182-record query union, not all trials. H is the frozen
hierarchy ranking and A its frozen molecular augmentation. Set H100 and A100 to the NCT IDs
whose archived deterministic rank is <=100. Read every record in their symmetric difference,
without relevance-based inclusion: E=A100\\H100 and D=H100\\A100. Exact membership is derived
from rankings-EMC.json; the separate coordinator manifest enumerates it. The 83 common records
require no new labels for this estimand. There are 17 E and 17 D records. No replacement is
allowed for missing, difficult, long, non-treatment, closed or apparently irrelevant records.

Rank order is descending frozen score, then ascending NCT ID. Exact equal-score tie blocks
retain rank_min, rank_max and midrank. Verify the complete rank sequence against archived
scores (no rescoring). Both H and A cutoff records and their successors are singleton ties;
no block straddles 100. Thus membership here is also robust to alternative ordering within
existing ties. This does not license changing the rule in other experiments.

The archive and archive-manifest hashes are pinned in build_packet.py. Only named ranking,
code, protocol/amendment, freeze, version-audit, generic reading-label protocol, corpus index,
and source manifests/pages may be decoded. The original selected reference and every label
outcome remain inaccessible to this builder. Hashing the ZIP container does not decode those
members. Source pages are checked against archive entries, original freeze hashes, compressed
and decompressed page digests, byte/count metadata, complete-query ID sets and union IDs.
No title, eligibility text or relevance signal enters membership or work ordering.

For each selected NCT ID, apply the archived version rule: greatest registry
lastUpdatePostDateStruct.date, then greatest retrieved_at_utc, then lexicographically smallest
page path and canonical-record SHA256. Reconcile every candidate page and distinct canonical
digest with version-audit.json. Missing version timestamps, inconsistent hashes or an audit
disagreement stop preparation. Multiple archived versions are not silently mixed: preserve
their digests and the deterministic selected version. These are internally verified archive
versions, not independent confirmation of registry truth or present-day currency.

Each reader record is the entire selected registry study JSON object, copied as an exact
UTF-8 byte slice of the decompressed page, with all fields retained. Coordinator provenance
records the ZIP member, page hash, retrieval timestamp, JSON pointer, byte range [start,end),
raw slice digest and canonical digest. Only 34 complete records are copied, not the ZIP or
the entire corpus. Reader packet metadata omits query paths, retrieval ranks, scores and group.

## Estimand and uncertainty

For a single strategy-independent binary label y_i on the identical archived record version,
the finite relevant-record yield difference is

    Delta = sum(i in A100) y_i - sum(i in H100) y_i
          = sum(i in E) y_i - sum(i in D) y_i.

The same y_i must apply to a common record in both strategies; even unknown common labels
cancel exactly. Different strategy-specific definitions, sources, judgments or weights would
break this argument. This is a set-yield contrast at fixed K=100, not a rank-weighted metric.

The primary disease-scope target is whether the archived record supports at least one cohort
whose disease and molecular scope is compatible with EMC, conditional on ordinary remaining
individual clinical requirements. It does not assert that any particular patient meets age,
performance status, treatment history, organ function or other clinical criteria. Unestablished
additional molecular criteria are not assumed satisfied. This distinction is prespecified
and applies identically to E and D. No hypothetical patient or biomarker prevalence is invented.

Readers assign one six-category label from the generic reading-label-protocol.json plus
explicit qualifiers and supporting evidence. These are annotation categories, not new claims
about which fusions define EMC:

| Category | Operational source rule |
|---|---|
| explicit_diagnosis_compatible | At least one coherent cohort explicitly permits EMC; reconcile restrictions in that cohort. |
| defining_fusion_compatible | A cohort permits a defining fusion explicitly linked to EMC by the supplied record; an unestablished link remains uncertain. |
| fusion_class_compatible | A molecular class offers an EMC-compatible route supported by supplied evidence; gene mention alone is insufficient. |
| broad_tumor_compatible | A broad sarcoma/solid-tumor cohort permits the target disease without a conflicting closed histology list; this is weak disease-scope evidence. |
| explicit_exclusion | All possible cohorts explicitly exclude the target or impose incompatible closed disease scope; a clearly nononcology false hit has a separate task-domain qualifier. |
| insufficient_evidence | Scope cannot be resolved from the full supplied record, including unavailable external criteria or unresolved molecular language. |

Within compatible cohorts, explicit diagnosis takes precedence over defining fusion, fusion
class and broad tumor. A restrictive cohort does not establish trial-level exclusion while
another plausible cohort is unresolved. Read all arms/cohorts before aggregating. No outside
medical assumptions about mutation versus fusion, partners, classes or extra biomarkers may
resolve an ambiguity. If evidence does not establish applicability, preserve uncertainty.

Assign primary admissible bounds [l_i,u_i] as follows: compatible category with a supported
route and no unresolved disease/molecular restriction is [1,1]; explicit exclusion supported
across all plausible cohorts is [0,0]; insufficient evidence, an unestablished extra molecular
requirement, or an unresolved scope-changing external-protocol condition is [0,1]. A merely
referenced external protocol does not automatically erase an unambiguous disease-scope route:
record why missing criteria can or cannot alter scope. Unresolved ordinary clinical eligibility
alone does not change this disease-scope endpoint. Broad compatibility never means EMC-specific
targeting or molecular efficacy. Unknown category values fail validation; missing/unread
records remain [0,1] and cannot be reported as completely read.

Retain separate fields for extra_biomarker_requirement (none stated / explicit required /
unclear, with text), molecular_compatibility (supported / conditional / incompatible / unknown),
protocol_uncertainty (none identified / scope-changing / other / unknown), task_domain,
clinical_trial_purpose, study_type, raw overallStatus and snapshot enrollment availability.
Purpose distinguishes treatment, non-treatment, mixed and unknown using all relevant cohorts;
the raw registry purpose is also preserved. Status is archived status only. Site/cohort access,
hold language, external protocol availability and live slots cannot be inferred from recruiting
status. Neither purpose nor recruitment filters or changes the primary endpoint. Report their
cross-tabulations on the 34-record difference descriptively, not as total top-100 counts.

Report the conservative identification interval

    [sum_E l_i - sum_D u_i, sum_E u_i - sum_D l_i].

It is an outer bound over admissible unresolved binary values, not a confidence interval.
No independence assumption, prevalence imputation, uncertain-as-negative coding or significance
test is used. Dependencies between uncertain records can make it conservative. If all labels
resolve, the interval collapses to exact integer Delta. A strictly positive lower bound supports
positive net disease-scope yield in this snapshot; a strictly negative upper bound supports a
negative difference; an interval including zero leaves direction unresolved. Report every count
and uncertainty category regardless of direction. Do not force adjudication to remove uncertainty.

## Prospective independent reading and freezing

Work order is ascending SHA256 of a fixed domain seed, a newline and NCT ID, with NCT ID as
collision fallback. Seed and membership live only in coordinator/packet-manifest.json. Assign
case-001 onward in that order; the reader manifest contains only case IDs and source hashes.
This is reproducible procedural separation, not secure or perfect blinding: NCT identity and
content can reveal clues, and anyone accessing the coordinator files can recover membership.

Future reader sessions receive only this protocol, the copied generic reading-label protocol,
reader/manifest.json and all reader/records/*.json. They must not access coordinator files,
rankings, builder code, archive, other tasks, original labels, old reference or each other's
judgments. Use two separate fresh sessions; neither may be the preparation author or a session
that has seen the group mapping. Readers inspect full eligibility, descriptions, conditions,
design/purpose, interventions and all arms/cohorts. They record exact JSON pointers, verbatim
evidence and record hashes for every label and qualifier; no live registry or external refresh.
Truncation is not complete reading. Split long records without omitting fields.

Each reader independently writes and SHA256/timestamp-freezes its complete 34-case judgment
and evidence files before any comparison, other-reader access or group unmasking. A coordinator
verifies case completeness, source hashes, pointers, excerpts and permitted bounds mechanically.
No scientific label is produced during this preparation. After both freezes, a separately
commissioned adjudicator can inspect disagreements under the same rules and with membership
still withheld. Preserve original independent labels, all disagreements and uncertain cases;
freeze adjudicated results before group unmasking and computation. Report reader-specific and
adjudicated intervals; record failures of separation honestly. This design checkpoint stops
before all reader work and makes no claim of human clinician review.

## Interpretation and verification endpoint

The algebra identifies a legitimate finite set contrast even though the earlier ordering
screen failed. The post-ordering choice of this question, query-union ascertainment, fixed
weights, single disease and snapshot prevent confirmatory generalization. Relevance is not
established by membership verification. Absolute top-100 precision/yield requires labels for
the 83 common records; recall requires a wider relevance denominator. Delta/100 is the
corresponding fixed-budget fraction difference, not either strategy's absolute precision.
No review-time measurement, patient eligibility, enrollment opportunity, clinical benefit,
therapeutic safety or efficacy follows. No scientific winner or paper readiness is declared.

The executable verifies membership, tie rules, versions, exact raw source bytes, frozen output
hashes and reproducibility. Semantic fixtures exhaust arbitrary common binary labels and all
admissible uncertain assignments, include cancellation of uncertain common records, treatment
and status orthogonality, sign reversal, and the failure of cancellation for inconsistent
common labels. Builder mutations are limited to this new directory; verify is read-only.
No original experiment code is executed. Integrity failure stops before reading. Return the
protocol SHA256 and packet manifest to the coordinator for independent methods review.
