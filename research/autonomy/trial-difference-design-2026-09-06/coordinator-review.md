The coordinator accepts the mathematical contrast and source packet for the explicitly bounded
descriptive question. **No readers are commissioned.** The worker and separate read-only
allocation challenge both recommend holding paper investment; successful preparation has not
supplied an outcome or changed the original fourth-place scientific ranking.

The coordinator read the prospective protocol, builder, synthetic tests and limitation report.
An independently written checker, which does not import the worker implementation, checked all
53 frozen file hashes (including preserved metadata-repair revisions), reconstructed both rankings from the 6,182 stored scores, confirmed
83 common and 17 differing records per method, and verified no tie crosses either cutoff.
All 34 complete reader records equal original raw JSON byte slices from 19 archived source
pages; their parsed records and canonical hashes agree with the saved version audit. Each
selected record has one distinct canonical version in that audit. This checks the archive's
internal identity, not present-day registry status or independent clinical truth.

For one strategy-independent binary label and identical common record versions, the common
83 contributions cancel. The signed sum over the complete symmetric difference is exact.
Independent exhaustive enumeration of 243 synthetic interval patterns and 1,024 admissible
binary assignments confirms the proposed unresolved-label bounds. These are identification
bounds, not sampling confidence intervals. The worker's nine semantic tests also passed when
executed by the coordinator. No trial relevance labels, selected pilot or historical reader
outcomes were inspected in this review. The original failed pragmatic screen remains failed.

The protocol appropriately keeps molecular restrictions and unresolved external criteria
explicit, requires complete record reading, preserves independently frozen judgments and
adjudication, and limits the conclusion to this snapshot. Reader manifest fields omit groups
and ranks; this is procedural separation, not secure blinding. A later reading commission
would still require fresh readers who have not seen the group map.

The remaining issue is scientific utility. A positive broad disease-scope difference could
consist of closed or non-treatment studies. It would not by itself establish more actionable
referral opportunities, clinician time saved, patient eligibility or a useful molecular-indexing
failure. The design also supplies no absolute top-100 yield, recall denominator or external
validation. A result on this single chosen snapshot is not automatically a substantive methods
paper. Changing the endpoint after outcomes to make the result useful is not an acceptable
repair. These are reasons to withhold the next allocation, not defects in the algebra.

The next selected work is the single named protocol/SAP check recorded in
../portfolio-2026-09-05/response-protocol-allocation-2026-09-06.md. The response question originally
ranked above trial discoverability, and the prior pilot left this particular source unexamined.
It also must demonstrate useful decision evidence before any manuscript investment.
This review is scoped development verification, not the required ultra submission review or
full publication preflight. No new preprint is ready.
