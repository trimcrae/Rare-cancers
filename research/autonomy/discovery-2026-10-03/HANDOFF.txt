feature of EMC versus three defined sarcoma comparators, beyond already known
macrophage-rich/CD8-poor immune context?

                        marginal       matched by sequencing year

All directions are lower, but the frozen requirement for large differences
(all six <=.30 or all six >=.70) failed. Patient/year deletion results and

Why potentially useful: a replicated distinct processing pattern could focus

this newly proposed strong pilot criterion does not challenge those findings.

PLAGL1 tissue expression:
Question: does published EMC PLAGL1 suppression extend to strong and consistent
tissue separation from three selected sarcoma histologies across cohorts?
RNA-seq A was .085470 versus LGFMS, .079365 versus myxoid liposarcoma and
.006173 versus synovial sarcoma; EMC median 5.03 TPM versus 28.43, 21.22,
85.495. Year-matched A=0 in all three, but each contrast contains only three
EMC and conditional bootstrap zeros are not population certainty.
Separate GSE24369 arrays (6 EMC,17 LGFMS; original unique probe8129985) show
much weaker same-direction ordering, A=.411765, single-EMC deletion range
.294118-.494118. CHRNA6/NR4A3 controls retained; no probe selected by outcome.
Why potentially useful: strong replicated EMC-specific reduction might
motivate promoter/allele-specific work. It was not established here; original
2005 fusion-transfected cell and tumor-versus-chondrocyte evidence already
reported suppression, and the repository already analyzed the array arm.
This comparison cannot refute the original perturbation, show loss of
imprinting, or establish tumor-suppressor function. Retain as a limited
cohort extension, not new mechanism or publication candidate.
Files: plagl1_pilot.py, plagl1-results.json, source-audit.txt.

Paired metastatic CT attenuation:
Question: is extrapulmonary versus lung attenuation consistently different
within the same EMC patient, potentially suggesting a site-linked matrix or
lesion phenotype worth measuring independently?
Kapoor2014 Table3 permits five paired patients. Median patient-level
extrapulmonary-minus-lung difference is +3.70 HU, range -24.15 to +25.65;
four positive, exact two-sided sign P=.375. Primary tumors and local
recurrences excluded; sites are not counted as independent patients.
No consistent/new site-specific phenotype established. The pilot was
explicitly exploratory after table inspection, with selected largest-burden
CT examinations, source lung-size filtering, and unresolved site/timing/
contrast confounding. It neither establishes equivalence nor refutes known
metastatic heterogeneity. Original descriptive imaging remains prior art.
Files: ct_pilot.py, ct-results.json, ct-replay.log, independent_verify.py.
