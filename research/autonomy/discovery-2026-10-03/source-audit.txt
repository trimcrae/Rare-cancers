PLAGL1 prior art:
Poulin/Labelle2005 PMID16112421, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.canlet.2004.12.007
Includes fusion-transfected CFK2 versus parental cells, not only cultured
chondrocytes versus tumors. Tissue comparisons cannot refute fusion effects.
Sarcoma PLAGL1 variability/methylation: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3829972/
New tissue ordering alone must not be presented as new imprinting biology.
Independent review also found the PLAGL1/GSE24369 comparison already in
research/manuscripts/fusion-output/nr4a3-fusion-transcriptional-output.md
(table around line545: d=-.169, P=.431). The new cohort calculation is a
limited extension; the reused array arm is not a fresh discovery.

Clarification on 2013 PLAGL1 paper: it measured LMS/undifferentiated sarcoma,
not the exact EMC comparisons here. It found broad expression variation but
no significant median difference between those two histologies, with
site-specific CpG associations; mean methylation did not explain expression.

Search is bounded, not an exhaustive claim that no public opportunity exists.
Source identifiers, sample identities and assay availability were checked;
absence of retrieved data is not evidence of biological absence.
