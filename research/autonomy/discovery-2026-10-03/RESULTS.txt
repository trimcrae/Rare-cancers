Fresh EMC discovery fan-out — 3 October 2026

One candidate survived. This is an empirical research lead, not a validated
diagnostic test, a drug target, or a manuscript ready for publication.

Demonstrated finding

TMEM266 RNA was unusually high in the EMC tissue samples: all nine RNA-seq
samples exceeded every sample in 35 of 36 sufficiently represented tumor
groups, and a separate six-EMC array series reproduced higher expression
against all four tumor comparison groups.
This makes TMEM266 worth locating within EMC tissue, but the strongest
limitation is that bulk RNA cannot distinguish tumor cells from entrapped
normal tissue, and normal-muscle controls also expressed this transcript.

What was added

The analysis identifies a specific, reproducible EMC tissue association that
was not found in the primary literature checked. EMC's broader neural
expression program was already known; this result does not claim otherwise.
The useful next measurement is RNA in situ hybridization in fusion-confirmed
EMC, alongside normal muscle and ossifying fibromyxoid tumor controls, to
determine whether the transcript is actually in the tumor cells.

The analysis started with a fixed UniProt membrane-gene universe, tested
2,694 readable genes, and froze the 21 qualifying genes before examining
their array results. Sixteen passed the array allocation rule, but most did
not establish a sufficiently novel, useful contribution to survive review.

TMEM266's median was 28.88 TPM, with a range of 6.73–56.82 across nine EMC
samples. It passed the discovery multiplicity correction (q=0.0096).
In the array series, the probability that an EMC specimen had a higher value
than a comparator was 0.92 against low-grade fibromyxoid sarcoma, 0.92 against
myxofibrosarcoma, 0.90 against solitary fibrous tumor, and 1.00 against desmoid.
These are observed expression contrasts, not validated classifier accuracy.

Important limits

- The 36-group comparison is exploratory context within the discovery data.
- Both source studies involve Lund investigators; their patient overlap is
  unresolved. Nine and six are assay-cohort counts, not 15 proven individuals.
- Two pooled normal-muscle array controls were near the EMC median. Muscle
  marker correlations cannot exclude contamination or establish cell origin.
- RNA measurements do not establish full-length membrane protein, function,
  dependency, treatment benefit, or added value over existing EMC markers.
- Small cohorts leave uncertainty: the descriptive array interval for the
  low-grade fibromyxoid comparison was approximately 0.76–1.00.

Promising but unfinished

GPR68 also showed replicated RNA enrichment against the selected sarcomas,
but broader tumor comparisons and collagen correlations raise a substantial
stromal explanation; older supplementary-table novelty remains unresolved.
The CIVO study includes one EMC patient, but usable patient-specific drug
response measurements and controls were not recovered. A newer GEO paper
mentions seven EMC samples whose deposited measurements could not be found.
These are not demonstrated functional findings.

Rejected or not promoted

The antigen-processing pilot failed its fixed effect criteria; PLAGL1 did
not add enough beyond existing work; the small CT comparison was inconclusive.
Several expression hits were already published, four failed the array rule,
and one lacked an eligible array mapping. Other statistically replicated
genes lacked a sufficiently established novel biological contribution.
New clinical, protein, and genomic sources mostly repeated their authors'
findings or lacked EMC-resolved measurements needed for a new analysis.

Verification and handoff

Three parallel workers conducted source discovery and independent challenges.
An independent implementation reproduced all 2,694 discovery rows and the
21 nominated array-gene analyses without discrepancies; a second reviewer
separately verified the TMEM266 values, mapping, and normal-muscle caveat.
The prior-art reviewer checked official platform annotations: the influential
Filion study's U133A platform did not have an annotated TMEM266 measurement.
This narrows a specific prior-art concern; it does not prove exhaustive novelty.
