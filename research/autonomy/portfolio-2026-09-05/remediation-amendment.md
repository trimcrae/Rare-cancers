The user challenged the consecutive demotions. None of the four investigations demonstrated that its entire research direction has insurmountable blockers. Each establishes bounded limitations of a proposed contribution. The earlier no-go records remain evidence, but their immediate move-to-next-lead instruction is superseded by this amendment where a concrete repair route exists.

The coordinator placed too much weight on readiness deficiencies at a feasibility checkpoint. A short round is an execution boundary, not a paper-development deadline. Missing validation can be the next research task. Prior-art overlap limits a claim; it does not automatically defeat a useful independent benchmark. Conversely, inaccessible or unreported experimental measurements cannot be invented or replaced by computational confidence.

Classify each blocker as: demonstrated absent information needed for the exact estimand; access/provenance unresolved with named sources; achievable methodological/validation work; or contribution/novelty unresolved. Record what was actually attempted, the next specific repair, and the evidence that would make further work unproductive. Do not demand a successful full paper from each pilot or an original trial decision reversal when a distinct evidence-synthesis decision could be the legitimate estimand. Do not continue only to obtain a positive result.

Trial discoverability gets the first focused remediation assessment after the currently reserved IPD round. Parent search containment is not a sufficient stop: test whether a diagnosis-level molecular strategy changes rank or review burden against competent ordinary/hierarchy retrieval. First establish an independently checked, explicitly bounded label set and benchmark estimand without assuming all unjudged records are negatives. Determine whether available positive cases and ascertainment can support an informative comparison; no clinical eligibility or global recall claim. A bounded reference set may support a bounded conclusion. Preserve historical versus currently recruiting status and cohort-specific uncertainty. If this cannot be made informative, state the observed reason rather than merely restating absent benchmark work.

The external fusion benchmark merits a targeted source-recovery assessment before treating its data barrier as settled: the named Oxford thesis, the unparsed CTSD-IFITM10 supplement, and identified inaccessible primary reports. Assess whether resolving these could change the intended validation design, including honest partial-outcome designs. Do not silently drop parent-sparing from the claim or pool ASO/siRNA. Digitizing a plotted measurement can recover that measurement; it cannot supply an unmeasured parent outcome.

The atlas has more substantial measurement/independence limitations, but unresolved sample provenance and annotation compatibility have not been proved irreparable. A further round must target a named provenance/measurement source or identifiable contrast, not another cache inventory. Response recoverability's demonstrated extractor defects warrant correction for integrity; a standalone paper still needs a contribution beyond those defects. Its absence of observed study-decision reversals in ten purposive trials is not proof of no useful methods question.

Keep one active owner per paper, a fresh task and bounded goal for each substantive round, actual independent checks, and the ultra review/full gates before readiness. The currently reserved IPD worker is retained and not replaced or duplicated. Existing app approval state remains separate from scientific feasibility. No changes to frozen ASO/Qeios/NAT, preregistrations or publication authority are authorized by this amendment. No assistant-imposed daily pacing or total iteration cap is reintroduced.
