## Primary-literature check that changes selection

[Dulken et al., 2024, PMID 38447752](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38447752/) reports CHRNA6 RNA CISH in 25 EMC cases and 685 histologic mimics, with interpretation thresholds. This supersedes the ledger's unsupported suggestion that CHRNA6 is probably an uninteresting null-tail gene. It does not establish therapeutic accessibility, protein localization or drug dependence. The study's microarray discovery is prior art: a CHRNA6 rediscovery paper is rejected. We can use the published tissue result as a reference observation while explicitly auditing whether discovery samples overlap our arrays.

[Chaiboonchoe et al., 2026, PMID 42465974](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42465974/) describes exploratory PXN/TYMS/H1FX biomarkers in a 12-case prognosis split. This is a relevant prior-art lead, not permission to fit another survival classifier. Full-text retrieval was blocked during orientation; no detailed methods or independence verdict is claimed from the indexed abstract. [GSE4303](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE4303) already reports NMB tissue follow-up; include it in later source mapping rather than presenting tissue validation as a newly invented approach.

The [FET mechanism study](../../modalities/atr-hrd-sarcoma-series-inputs.json) supplies the experimental question, but live full-text retrieval was blocked. Existing committed source evidence and conditional sequence output establish the unresolved construct gap; they do not validate an EMC mechanism.
