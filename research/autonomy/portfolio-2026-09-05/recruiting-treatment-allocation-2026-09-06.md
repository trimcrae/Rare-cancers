A focused read-only repair has identified a more useful trial question than the held

## Fixed proposal before any new relevance judgments

Use the already frozen 6,182-record corpus, scores and selected source versions. Apply identical
structural filters before selecting results: overallStatus equals RECRUITING and designInfo
primaryPurpose equals TREATMENT at their full protocolSection paths. Missing/other values
are outside this expressly bounded population, not negative disease labels. The read-only
audit reports 737 retained records, all INTERVENTIONAL; the preparer must reproduce this.

O is the existing ordinary diagnosis-synonym score across titles, conditions/keywords,
descriptions, eligibility and interventions. H adds 0.25 times the frozen sarcoma BM25 score;
A adds to H 0.5 times the maximum qualifying molecular-expression score. Retain these original
definitions without rescoring, tuning, new queries or refreshes. H is the primary controlled
ordinary-plus-histology comparator, not a claim to represent the best clinician or registry
search. Verify actual code definitions before freezing the new protocol.

Select up to 100 positive-score results for each method using stored score order and NCT ID
tie-breaking. Never pad O with zero-score records: it has only 77 positive results. The audit
reports all 77 common to H and A, 19 further common records, four H-only and four A-only
records, with no H/A cutoff-crossing tie. Thus eight records identify the primary A-minus-H
yield difference; 96 common contributions cancel. The 27-record union beyond O could support
additional comparisons, but it is not commissioned. Preserve that distinction and all exact
membership/version evidence without exposing groups to readers.

The primary judgment must connect EMC disease/molecular compatibility and therapeutic purpose
within the same source-described cohort. Do not combine an observational EMC cohort with an
unrelated treatment cohort to make a positive. Preserve additional biomarker requirements,
unavailable scope-changing external criteria, contradictory text and cohort-specific closure
or hold language. Define the exact positive, negative and unresolved rules before reading.
Snapshot RECRUITING is a record property and never proof that an EMC cohort/site is open.
Do not invent an individual patient or assume patient-specific clinical requirements are met.

The primary result is a signed difference in source-supported therapeutic disease-scope leads
under this structural gate and review capacity. Use identical strategy-independent labels and
conservative bounds for unresolved judgments. Initially the attainable range is [-4, +4].
Report negative, zero and unresolved results as readily as a positive increment. A positive
finite difference is evidence for considering further validation, not submission clearance.
If this census does not establish useful incremental leads, stop the augmentation experiment
without tuning the rule or launching broader labels to rescue it.
