# Frozen development pilot: reporting precision and reconstruction triage

Version 1, 2026-09-06. This is a development pilot, not held-out validation, a clinical analysis or a paper. Outcome access requires the coordinator's explicit authorization covering the SHA256 of this file and source-manifest.json. Subsequent corrections are dated amendments; retain original bytes.

Question: does continuous disagreement between two published point reconstructors improve identification of erroneous realized logrank threshold decisions beyond an incumbent method's own distance from the threshold? Does additional risk-count reporting reduce decision errors? This tests a different claim from the inverse solver's 0/4 stress certificates and does not repair its scalability or establish a continuous-time certificate. Generic reconstruction/abstention novelty is not claimed; Titman/CIFresolve and KM-PoPiGo are direct prior art.

Population and split: archived Zenodo 18320575 RealIPD.zip, pinned SHA256 in source-manifest.json. Use TCGA disease cohorts only. Connect cohorts sharing exact TCGA patient IDs; retain every connected source and all its variants/seeds together. Hash-order connected groups using SHA256('20260906|'+sorted-source-names joined by '|'); first at most ten are development. Remaining components are reserved and their outcome values remain unopened. All GEO sources are quarantined because cross-series reuse and aliases are unverified. TCGA identity linkage is source-declared, not independently linked to original TCGA clinical tables. Unexpected ID formats or duplicates stop metadata preparation. New provenance ambiguity excludes/combines a group before outcomes, never selects by its results.

Pseudoarms: seeds 61001, 61002, 61003; sort each cohort's patients by SHA256(seed+'|'+patient-ID), first floor(n/2) to A, remainder B. Use all records, with no endpoint-based filtering. Validate finite nonnegative times and event coding exactly 0/1 on authorized opening; malformed source stops its entire group with reason, no recoding. Zero-time observations are supported in original statistics, but such a source is excluded from this initial package-interface pilot with explicit count because the adapter assumes pre-event origin zero; do not silently shift times. No empirical source is selected because of p value, events, sample size or ease of reconstruction. Save every seed and source failure.

Ground truth is the ORIGINAL REALIZED two-sided ordinary logrank result for that exact randomized assignment, not the population null. Random pseudoarms provide only null-generating scenarios. Preserve signed Z (positive = excess A events), Q, p, rejection p<.05, false-reject and false-nonreject directions relative to realized original IPD, and sign changes. Always-nonsignificant predictions are a required class-imbalance baseline. No clinical effect or nonnull validity follows.

Information release: use original exact event times and rounded post-event KM survival at 2 or 3 decimals, including origin and cohort-arm endpoint. This is idealized numerical probability precision, NOT figure/pixel digitization. Preserve true event/censor ties. Risk tables at 0 plus 25%, 50%, 75% of each arm's maximum follow-up (dense), or 0 plus 50% (sparse); counts are pre-event numbers at risk. Each package gets identical N, event total, coordinates and risk table. Terminal zero risk is not invented. All rounding/density variants are reported and paired by source/seed. Time units are unspecified by inspected benchmark metadata; use source times only within each original cohort, with no cross-source time comparisons or common-duration clinical interpretation.

Methods: original installed IPDfromKM 0.1.10 and CIFresolve 0.1.1 default approximate QP/rounding, existing pinned sources. No package source edits, silent repairs, tolerances tuned to outcomes or discrete-support success requirement. Record original package inputs, versions, warning/error messages and reconstructed rows. Invalid reconstructed records, timeout or package failures remain in denominators and force abstention. Compute original and reconstructed logrank independently in Python and compare every R-returned Q/p. Any unexplained material discrepancy stops interpretation.

Triage comparison: fix IPDfromKM as incumbent for both primary rules, preserving identical predictions. Own margin M=abs(Z_incumbent)-1.959963984540054 in absolute value, i.e. abs(abs(Z)-critical); disagreement D=abs(Z_incumbent-Z_CIFresolve). Higher confidence is M (margin only) or M-D (margin plus disagreement), with coefficient exactly 1 fixed before outcomes. On common dual-success records compare top 50%, 75%, 90%, 100% retention; ties break by source/seed/precision/density ID. Secondary use CIFresolve as incumbent identically. Also report each individual method's own-margin triage, binary threshold-disagreement triage (agree first, then incumbent margin), and always-nonsignificant. Primary retention is 75%. Report both common-success and attempted-case coverage so requiring two methods cannot hide failures. No rule is selected from held-out data.

Summaries: case confusion matrices and source-level counts, paired rounding/density differences, selected error/coverage counts for every rule and retention. Repeated seeds/variants are dependent: no binomial confidence interval treating them as independent patients or sources. Pilot estimates are descriptive. Distinct source groups supporting errors/benefits are explicit. All cases and failures retained.

Prospective continuation gate: before any reserved outcome access, development must have realized incumbent threshold errors in at least 3 distinct source groups, at least 8 erroneous cases overall, and dual-success coverage at least 80% of attempted cases. At primary 75% common-success retention, fixed M-D must reduce selected error count by at least 2 cases and at least 20% relative to M; gains must involve at least 2 source groups and leave-one-source-out recomputation must never reverse the direction of the improvement. These are conservative allocation thresholds, not evidence of statistical superiority. Failure yields no-go for held-out expansion of this triage rule; sparse evidence yields inconclusive/no expansion. No searching additional seeds/sources to meet the gate. A separate reporting-density finding may be retained without promoting this triage claim.

Execution cap: 10 source groups, 3 seeds, 2 rounding levels, 2 risk densities; at most 120 cases if one curve/group, 2 packages/case. Per R invocation 60 seconds; whole pilot 30 minutes, preserving completed work and unrun cases. A timeout is a failure, not permission to skip difficult cases or tune. Only this pilot is authorized after coordinator review. No manuscript, publication, full preflight, inverse-solver edits or held-out evaluation.
