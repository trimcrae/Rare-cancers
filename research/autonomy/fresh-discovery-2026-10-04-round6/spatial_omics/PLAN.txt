This first gate is a bounded primary-source search (approximately 25 minutes), not a numerical screen. Search original single-cell, single-nucleus, spatial transcriptomic and multiplex cell-protein studies, their complete sample rosters and repositories, including broader sarcoma collections and historical EMC aliases. A generic chondrosarcoma, EWSR1-positive tumor, inferred deconvolution or cultured bulk profile is not authenticated cell-resolved EMC evidence. Citation-only mentions are not measurements.

Eligibility and scientific-value requirements: actual EMC-labelled human specimen measurements, donor and condition identifiers, assay definition and available appropriate controls; fusion confirmation strengthens authentication but lack of confirmation is retained as uncertainty rather than silently reclassified. Establish every eligible EMC specimen and condition in each candidate source, including repeats and treated samples. Assess donor/cohort overlap and whether apparent cells are independent biological samples. A novel contrast needs a plausible consequence for EMC research beyond a map or marker list. Before eligible values, append a source-specific plan fixing the biological state, annotation evidence, exact sample set, comparison and uncertainty analysis; no post hoc substitution after failure.

Strongest alternative explanations: normal immune, neural or vascular cells drive bulk signatures; dissociation, platform or treatment differences drive a purported recurrent state; ambient RNA, doublets or erroneous malignant-cell annotation mimic tumor expression. Cancer-cell identity requires appropriate source-supported evidence, not NR4A3 expression alone. Spatial spots need explicit mixed-cell interpretation. Analyses must use donors, not cells or image pixels, as independent replication and must not infer causation, dependency, treatment efficacy or a therapeutic window from expression.

Stop or shelve if no authenticated suitable public measurement is identified, the desired interpretation is already established, donor/condition links cannot support the contrast, or even a technically positive result would not be useful new EMC knowledge. Missing data or limited search coverage are not biological negatives. Suitable accessible relevant evidence left unevaluated blocks advancement.
