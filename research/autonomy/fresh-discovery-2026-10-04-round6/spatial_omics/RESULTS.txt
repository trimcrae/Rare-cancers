Round 6 spatial and single-cell EMC investigation — 4 October 2026

Decision: shelve this standalone candidate at the source and scientific-value gate. No new EMC tumor or microenvironment state was demonstrated. No biological expression, cell-state or spatial association pilot was performed because no newly identified cell-resolved measurement passed EMC authentication and a useful comparison gate. This is an incomplete source-feasibility result, not a negative result about EMC biology and not evidence that public EMC measurements do not exist.

The fresh primary-source search did resolve several misleading leads. Ngo et al. (2025; DOI 10.1002/cac2.70077) measured single cells in eight epithelioid-sarcoma specimens from seven patients and spatial transcriptomics in four epithelioid-sarcoma specimens. EMC belongs to their broader bulk-RNA comparison panel; the primary paper does not convert those comparator specimens into cell-resolved EMC measurements. The current 3CA sarcoma page lists synovial sarcoma and osteosarcoma only. The previously completed Subramanian atlas audit was reused with seven source/result hashes: all 87 bulk, seven single-cell and four spatial GEO records and all 22 supplement sheets had already been checked without an EMC-labelled specimen.

A fresh repository screen recovered the 2026 BO-112/radiotherapy series. The 12 libraries in GSE313859 represent seven donors and 11 donor/time-point combinations: four paired baseline/surgery donors plus three radiotherapy-only surgical donors; the two CD45 fractions of RT001 are not independent patients. The associated four spatial samples are all patient 004, whose source BioSample SAMN53912816 explicitly identifies myxofibrosarcoma. The primary text identifies the radiotherapy-only comparison as UPS. The complete single-cell BioSample records do not provide histology for patients 010, 011 and 012; these remain unresolved, not EMC-negative. Twelve generic sarcoma labels cannot support an EMC pilot.

Two broader resources remain potentially relevant but unqualified. CASSIS reports a large flow/CITE-seq collection, yet the inspected public project pages do not expose the expanded donor/subtype roster or a usable EMC-linked matrix. The 2026 scTumor Atlas preprint's original new GEO sample is retroperitoneal leiomyosarcoma; its full inherited sample roster could not be evaluated because the source-linked supplementary PDF returned HTML instead of a PDF. Luthria et al. (2024; DOI 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-23-2976) explicitly identifies its eight frozen specimens as UPS or intimal sarcoma, but the additional fresh technical-comparison specimen cannot be fully authenticated from the unavailable S1 spreadsheet. These are visible identity/access gaps.

Scientific value and reopening conditions: a map, an unlabelled sample or a repeated published two-specimen IF comparison would not establish a new recurrent EMC state. Reopen only after a permitted public source supplies authentic EMC donor/condition labels and appropriate direct measurements supporting a consequential, prespecified biological contrast. Multiple spots, cells, sections or technical fractions cannot substitute for independent donors. Before any claim advances, resolve every relevant public EMC specimen and condition, source overlap, malignant-cell assignment, batch and normal-cell alternatives, and obtain an independent omission/value challenge. The lead will review this frozen source classification; no completed independent review is claimed in this packet.
