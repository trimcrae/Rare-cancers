Unfavorable FAPI observations were retained, including a myxoid renal diagnosis and unknown-primary brain lesion. Pabst rows 7/8 explicitly describe one participant, not two donors. Generic diagnoses remain unresolved; neither low uptake nor absence of EMC can be inferred from missing subtype crosswalks.

Broader clinical FAPI coverage: evaluated released rosters in the 43-patient three-time-point study, Hirmas2023 (324 participants, including 131 sarcomas), Hirmas2024 (115 participants) and the liver-metastasis study (76 donors, 189 lesions). Hirmas2024 explicitly excludes sarcoma and is unsuitable for an EMC measurement claim. Hirmas2023's remaining non-sarcoma leaf labels were also retained, including additional unresolved generic diagnoses; organ labels cannot exclude EMC. No authenticated EMC comparison emerged. The interobserver study's full 50-patient subtype/workbook crosswalk remains incomplete; teaching cases do not replace it. Shared registries, previously reported cohorts and unresolved donor overlap prohibit independent pooling. Recovering rosters improves coverage rather than demonstrating new EMC biology.

Single-cell/spatial data: source and specimen eligibility were examined beyond the previously evaluated bulk/immune inputs, including newer pan-cancer and pediatric/model resources. No authenticated EMC measurement contrast was established. The scTumor source versions, model-library annotations and generic BO-112 donor identities must retain their actual limitations. Two pediatric title/disease labels conflict and were not silently corrected. Multiple libraries, paired conditions and sorted fractions are not additional donors. Metadata screening is not evaluation of every underlying expression dataset and is not evidence that EMC lacks a cell-state program.

Radiotherapy/local control: audited all 15 published Fice patient rows and a ten-row primary/localized/operated subset (six with primary RT, four without). A recurrence's treatment attribution conflicts between table and narrative, one local-outcome cell is blank, and 13 table operations conflict with 12 narrative operations. The narrative margin statement cannot be linked to all table patients. No event history, censoring record, effect estimate or blank outcome was invented. Bishop's already published adjusted association has only five local events and substantial uncertainty; repeating it adds no new contribution. Broader stereotactic-RT data include two EMC donors but lack their individual treatment/lesion/outcome crosswalk. These limits do not establish RT inefficacy or radioresistance.

Diagnostic assays: evaluated the published 31-EMC/187-mimic INSM1 observations, other source-reported assay bins and relevant case panels; reused the verified CHRNA6 findings. Negative staining, threshold effects, mimic reactivity and specimen/handling limitations are already primary published observations. Focal biopsy reactivity has unspecified extent/intensity and cannot be assumed to cross the original positivity threshold. Different assays, molecular authentication and unresolved donor overlap prevent a new pooled sensitivity or biological-heterogeneity inference. Suitable accessible full sources and individual mappings remain pending for any advancing comparison.

Untreated kinetics/physiology: evaluated published observation donors, serial-lung captions, regression cases and treatment-linked numerical measurements. A reported primary diameter change from 15.8 to 18 cm is +13.92% over a nominal 12 months; it is not an untreated lung growth rate or a volume-doubling estimate. The source's presumed pulmonary lesions lack a numerical, authenticated longitudinal crosswalk. The inspected randomized subgroup has two treated EMC observations and no EMC best-supportive-care control. Published indolence, regressions and two RT/drug-confounded lesion responses are prior art; selected case reports cannot establish their frequency or attributable efficacy. Acquisition comparability was explicitly corrected rather than inferred from the presence of numbers.
