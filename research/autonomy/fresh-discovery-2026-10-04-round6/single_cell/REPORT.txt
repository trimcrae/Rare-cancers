EMC malignant-cell heterogeneity source/value pilot, 4 October 2026

Decision: shelve the standalone paper. No new publication-worthy finding survives
this lane. The search is not exhausted. No authenticated EMC malignant-cell or
spatial measurement was analyzed, so neither a positive nor a negative molecular
result was established.

The frozen question asked whether cell-level measurements distinguish malignant
axon/neuroendocrine differentiation from proliferative states beyond bulk-tissue
admixture and existing fusion/axon-guidance prior art. The evaluated public
measurements did not pass authentication for this question. A correct source
inventory, exclusion or annotation repair is not an EMC biological discovery.

Actual new source work
All 424 library metadata records from ten selected series were inspected, with one
transient 502 followed by a successful, retained retry. These are libraries and
conditions, not 424 independent patients. No expression matrix or raw sequencing
library was downloaded and no specimen was chosen because of a favorable value.

The scTumor preprint's complete 36-primary-type source roster was recovered from
its actual supplemental PDF. It contains named sarcoma/mesenchymal categories
DSRCT, MPNST, osteosarcoma, rhabdomyosarcoma, synovial sarcoma, GCTB and TGCT,
with no EMC or unspecified sarcoma class. GSE319327's one newly deposited sample
is retroperitoneal LMS. GEO's 494 samples/135,441 cells and the retrieved version2
preprint's 499 samples/135,424 cells are retained as a version discrepancy, without
pooling totals or treating cells as donors. Its generic cell-line source panels
remain a separate incomplete model-identity check.

All 59 GSE319124 libraries report RMS/EWS/OST/NBL disease labels. Two entries have
discordant title prefixes and disease fields; both source labels are retained in
ANNOTATION-CONFLICTS.json. All 277 GSE318841 sibling libraries were evaluated:
RMS150, NBL53, EWS31, OST17, WT14 and RBL12. Each released measurement is a single
RSEM gene-count vector at library level. GSE330883 is explicitly methylation,
not cell-level transcriptomics. These sources do not close the study's headline
352 single-cell/single-nucleus experiments or every donor/condition crosswalk.

GSE200529's entire 26-library roster is OS/ES/DSRCT; its two generic MDA-SA98 titles
have explicit OS cell-type fields. GSE184118's entire 11-library roster identifies
conventional central/peripheral or dedifferentiated chondrosarcoma and other
cartilaginous controls, rather than EMC. Later chondrosarcoma reanalyses reuse
that cohort. GSE243381's 21 libraries include RNA, TCR and lpWGS measurements of
the primary paper's five UPS and three intimal-sarcoma specimens, with six donor
IDs and repeated X/Y donors. GSE336655 measures immune cells in JJ012 xenografts.
The 2025 tumor-cluster study confines its scRNA experiments to SS/RMS; the 2025
TLS study's external single-cell and spatial experiments concern HNSCC/NPC.

BO-112 is an important unfinished source, not a non-EMC blanket exclusion.

Accessible processed measurements plus unresolved identity remain visible gaps;

Remaining source gaps and reopening
Resolve all six BO-112 library identities, the complete pediatric 352-experiment
crosswalk, the scTumor cell-line panel identities and the recent 89-specimen
pediatric-sarcoma preprint's full donor roster before considering corresponding
measurements eligible or excluded. These are staged unfinished eligibility work,
not established promising EMC biological findings. Other generic atlas donors
may remain unrecognized by disease metadata. Initial broad full-text queries
were capped at 1,000 results; their incomplete pagination is recorded. Tighter
title/abstract queries and repository checks improve coverage but do not prove
that no undiscovered public EMC dataset exists.

Reopening needs an authenticated processed cohort with malignant identity,
donor/specimen/condition links and at least two independent EMC donors, allowing
controls for cell cycle, stress and stromal contamination. It must answer a useful
question beyond established fusion biology. Complete public-EMC coverage and an
independent value/omission challenge precede any manuscript promotion.

Reproducibility and portability
PLAN.md preserves the original question and stop rule. Retrieval scripts and
receipts preserve dates, exact public URLs/queries, original byte counts and SHA256.
GSM-ELIGIBILITY-OBSERVATIONS.json and ADDITIONAL-GSM-ELIGIBILITY-OBSERVATIONS.json
preserve every inspected library; the separate retry record preserves the original
failure. finalize_packet.py regenerates derived observation tables and the decision
from cache-only inputs, using existing dependencies. validate_packet.py checks
source hashes, library/condition arithmetic, the entire RSEM sibling and actual
input signatures. This is source/eligibility verification, not independent
reproduction of malignant biology. Raw original API responses, papers, catalogs
and their broad search-result copies are ignored local cache, not Git evidence.
Missing caches in another session require the exact receipt/URL recovery and
version comparison; a manifest alone is not reproduction.
