Question: Do authenticated public EMC single-cell or spatial transcriptomic measurements demonstrate reproducible malignant-cell heterogeneity between axon/neuroendocrine differentiation and proliferative programs, rather than variation caused by nonmalignant admixture in bulk tissue? Such a result could refine the next cell-specific measurement or experiment. It would not establish dependency, protein accessibility or treatment efficacy.

Prior art: Filion2009 and Brenca2019 already establish fusion-related regulatory/axon-guidance programs; the submitted CSPG4 letter explicitly leaves malignant-versus-stromal attribution unresolved. The R2 regulatory packet authenticated that Ngo2025's single-cell/spatial specimens are epithelioid sarcoma: its EMC comparison is bulk, not single-cell EMC. Existing immune GSE213065 and prior bulk screens will be reused as unsuitable for the malignant-cell heterogeneity question, not redone. MUG-EMCS is not accepted as EMC without NR4A3 identity. TMEM266 remains shelved. A generic sarcoma, unspecified chondrosarcoma or NR4A3-expressing cell is not an authenticated EMC donor.

Strongest alternative: apparent states reflect stromal admixture, dissociation/stress, cell cycle or batch, or known differentiation differences already established by the primary study. Cells and spots are technical observations; biological replication is donors, with recurrences and reused cohorts linked.

Pilot: 25 minutes of quiet literature/API/source work across disease aliases (`extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma`, `myxoid chondrosarcoma`, `NR4A3`, `EWSR1-NR4A3`, `TAF15-NR4A3`, `TEC`, `CHN`), model aliases (`USZ20-EMC1`, `USZ22-EMC2`, `NCC-EMC1-C1`, `EMC005`), broader sarcoma single-cell/spatial papers, supplements, GEO/ArrayExpress/cell-atlas repositories and recent datasets. Inspect full donor rosters in credible candidates; do not choose cases by results. Source discovery itself is not disease discovery.

Eligibility: all identifiable authenticated EMC specimens/conditions with malignant-cell transcriptomes or spatial transcriptomes permitting malignant localization. Prefer processed matrices and annotations. Required identification includes pathology/fusion evidence, donor and specimen mapping, technical/batch structure, cohort overlap, assay units and suitable controls. Evaluate every eligible EMC donor/specimen/condition. Suitable accessible relevant measurements pending analysis block promotion. Ambiguous identity remains unresolved rather than a negative EMC measurement.

Before new empirical values, append a source-specific analysis amendment specifying genes/signatures, malignant identity criteria, donor-level analysis, controls and uncertainty. A useful preliminary claim requires reproducible states in at least two independently authenticated EMC donors, controls capable of distinguishing cell cycle/stress and stromal contamination, and novelty after the primary paper. Do not change that requirement to rescue a one-donor result.

Defeat/stop: no eligible authenticated malignant EMC measurement; no donor replication; inference driven by technical/batch/cell-cycle/stromal effects; or already published result with no useful extension. At the bounded source/value gate, shelve the standalone route and retain suitable pending or unavailable coverage explicitly.
