Surviving demonstrated findings: none. This pilot adds a reusable source/measurement check, not a new publication-worthy EMC observation. Shelve the standalone untreated-kinetics/metabolic-phenotype paper. The public search is not exhausted.

The primary2015/2025 reports already describe rare spontaneous pulmonary regression, and Brown2020 reports all three observation donors (50,70,126months). Carroll2025’s one primary lesion grows15.8→18cm across nominal12months without further reported systemic therapy: +13.92%. Lung sizes are not released as a numerical trajectory, presumed lung metastases are unbiopsied in that report, and debulking/prior drugs confound mechanism. This cannot establish an untreated lung growth rate, RECIST category or attributable therapeutic benefit.

All two EMC rows in Morioka2016 receive trabectedin; every BSC row is mesenchymal chondrosarcoma. There is no randomized untreated EMC control in that source. Elsayed2018’s five dated lung figures cover47months after watchful waiting starts, rather than eight years of observed untreated pulmonary growth; no image-derived dimensions are claimed. Paoluzzi2018 reports both irradiated lesion size/SUV decreases in one EWSR1–NR4A3 donor on concurrent pazopanib, now shared with the RT owner. No pooled donor rate, treatment effect, p-value or causal conclusion was calculated.

Validity: published numerical observations and constrained arithmetic are traceable and reproducible; acquisition modality/protocol and segmentation comparability are unverified, and incomplete measurements/identity and treatment conditions prevent the stronger comparison. Value: the useful lesson is an already-known design caution about indolence, rare regressions and treatment attribution. Recomputing it does not supply new useful EMC biology. Reopen only with independently authenticated, case-linked serial measurements during documented untreated intervals and a worthwhile controlled/mechanistic contrast, after full relevant EMC coverage.
