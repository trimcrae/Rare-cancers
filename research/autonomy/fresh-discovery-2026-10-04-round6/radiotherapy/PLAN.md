The question is whether released individual EMC treatment, surgical-margin and
local-recurrence histories permit a useful assessment of whether the published
radiotherapy/local-control association survives a margin- and presentation-aware
comparison. This is an observational discrimination question, not an efficacy,
safety, causality or treatment recommendation claim. The candidate useful addition
would be an authenticated contrast within comparable margin/presentation strata,
with uncertainty, or a concrete measured contradiction to the prevailing empirical
interpretation. Merely restating the published association, calculating a new
p-value on aggregate counts, or demonstrating that retrospective studies are
confounded would not warrant a standalone paper.

Primary prior art to inspect: Bishop2019 MD Anderson localized EMC outcomes;
Drilon2008 two-referral-center clinical outcomes; Paioli2021 molecularly confirmed
localized EMC; Chiusole2020 Padova/Gustave Roussy; the Fice2022 and Turkish surgical
series containing public patient rows. Relevant earlier sources include Saleh1992,
Meis-Kindblom1999, McGrory2001, Kawaguchi2003 and Ogura2012; newer institutional,
registry and broader soft-tissue-sarcoma series will be searched and authenticated.
Known Mayo late-recurrence analyses, all prior round dispositions and overlaps are
reused; no conditional distant-recurrence project is being reopened.

Strongest alternatives: indication/selection by margin, tumor size/location,
initial versus referred recurrent disease, surgical quality, baseline metastasis,
calendar era and follow-up; postoperative RT can create immortal-time bias if
time origins or treatment dates are missing. Patients reused across institutions
and publications are not independent. Generic chondrosarcoma is not authentic EMC.

Eligibility: any identifiable public EMC observation bearing on primary local
treatment, surgical margins, subsequent local recurrence and observation duration,
including EMC inside broader series, all eligible rows/conditions in an advancing
source, and published measurements without separate deposition. Historical aliases
include extraosseous myxoid chondrosarcoma and myxoid chondrosarcoma of soft parts;
NR4A3/EWSR1-NR4A3 can discover cohorts but do not authenticate a generic tumor.
No non-EMC control selection is planned. Do not infer event time from final follow-up,
reconstruct fake patient data from survival curves/aggregate counts, or treat an
unreported treatment as absent.

Initial pilot: 25 minutes of quiet API/file work starting after this freeze;
at most 64 MiB retained raw source/copies and at least 10 GiB free. Search primary
literature, citation trails, supplements and repository records. Use published
processed rows if sufficient. If a credible addition and authenticated individual
data emerge, append an analysis amendment before inspecting the comparison and
complete one bounded uncertainty/confounding check. Stop this route and shelve
if no useful new comparison is supportable, if prior art already answers it, or
if sparse/confounded/inconsistent measurements defeat it. Access or time limits
remain explicit coverage gaps, never negative measurements or proof of exhaustion.

accessible analysis; demonstrably unsuitable measurement; unavailable evidence.
Pending suitable accessible data block promotion. Decision must say whether new
