2026-10-04 — NR4A3 regulatory gate: SHELVE

No new EMC biological finding survived. The prospective question required an
endogenous NR4A3-fusion perturbation, direct binding and transcriptional output in
authenticated EMC, with a meaningful independent comparison. No new source
qualified for that numerical pilot; therefore no numerical signal analysis or
new dependency claim was performed. This is a source/value stop, not evidence that
EMC lacks a regulatory program, fusion dependence or a therapeutic vulnerability.

The fresh, completely mapped public experiment GSE11185 consists of four engineered
293-cell arrays: NOR1 or EWS/NOR1, each with/without doxycycline. GDS3481 contains
the identical four GSMs and is not replication. The original Ohkura study already
reports differential transactivation and PARP-1 relationships. Recomputing its
expression contrast would not demonstrate an endogenous EMC program or add a new
disease result (PMID18680143; DOI10.1002/jcb.21876).

Frenkel's PROD-ATAC study contains NR4A3-fusion chromatin measurements, but the
observed main study uses a shared engineered 293T context. Its fusion-dependent
chromatin conclusions are already reported by the authors. Neither its cells nor
its single nuclei are independent EMC donors (DOI10.1038/s41587-024-02347-4).
The first primary-page read was usable; subsequent access presented a CAPTCHA,
which was not bypassed. Supplemental condition completeness remains unverified.

Actual EMC observations must remain visible. Laflamme2003 reports tumor RT-PCR
coexpression of SIX3/NOR1/fusion, with mechanistic transfections in immortalized
human chondrocytes (PMID12543801). A 2004 follow-up reports 14 fusion-positive EMCs
without reported native NOR1 or SIX3 expression, while two fusion-not-detected
EMCs coexpressed them (PMID15262426; DOI10.1016/j.cancergencyto.2003.11.011).
These are existing results; despite the 2004 title referring to proteins, its
abstract does not establish the assay level. Full methods, case details and study
overlap remain unresolved. We do not reinterpret fusion-not-detected diagnoses,
explain the discordance by an assumed assay difference, or promote this contrast
as our discovery.

Verified prior EMC PPARG/NDRG2/SGK1 measurements (Filion2009), fusion-partner-associated
axon programs (Brenca2019), and Zullow2022 clinical specimen labels are reused with
hashes, not rediscovered. Their existing tissue associations and engineered-cell
mechanistic results do not fill the missing endogenous EMC perturbation bridge.

Scientific value decision: shelve this standalone proposal. A smaller title,
reanalysis of heterologous fusion activity or another target list would not change
an EMC research decision beyond the original studies. Reopen only for an
identified authentic EMC perturbation/binding/output source with explicit sample
and control linkage, or a specific new question supported by those measurements
and not already answered. All relevant accessible conditions must then be
evaluated before advancement. No global exhaustion or universal absence is claimed.
