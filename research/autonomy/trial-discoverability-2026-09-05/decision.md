Deprioritize the current standalone trial-discoverability paper after the [verified pilot](pilot.md). Molecular searches identify an EMC Phase1 candidate and a historical DSRCT candidate missed by strong disease-name searches; both are parent-sarcoma-findable. Ordinary BasicSearch omits eligibility text, whereas ConditionSearch includes more than sponsor conditions. The corrected search comparisons and hard negatives are useful retained evidence; the earlier manuscript's field assumptions and unfindability claims are not validated.

The 6182-record query union is fully enumerated for its queries, not an independently ascertained denominator. The74 screening labels are provisional; coordinator verification covers all source pointers/arithmetic and seven decision-bearing trial records, not full clinical adjudication. No patient eligibility, recall, or benefit is established.

Parent-search containment alone is not a decisive objection: narrower molecular retrieval could reduce review burden. That advantage is unmeasured, and comparing against an unranked broad pool would be a weak baseline. RareCure overlaps hierarchy expansion but lacks marginal ablation; a new benchmark could be valuable if independently labeled, tested against competent ranked ordinary/hierarchy retrieval, and capable of informing a concrete search-design choice. Preserve that reopening route, without treating every absent measurement as permanent impossibility. Current evidence does not establish enough distinct contribution to prioritize another expansion round over the remaining portfolio question.

Next conditional lead: rank5, reconstruction uncertainty at the study-decision level. Existing sparse EMC reconstructions do not support clinical-effect inference. Test whether a concrete decision-error/calibration question exceeds existing IPD reconstruction benchmarks and admits reproducible known-ground-truth validation. If prior methods already answer it or only generic digitization-error simulations are attainable, deprioritize rather than manufacture a methods paper. Use one fresh-context feasibility worker; do not enlarge the clinical corpus or alter original preregistrations.
