# Publication ranking after executed measured-data analysis

The initial four-draft selection was premature. It is superseded by completed analyses across all 45 options and eighteen written artifacts. Eight distinct focused article/letter candidates emerge, of varying strength, plus one optional consolidated resource. This is an assessment of contributions, not a promise that all will be accepted or a quota on the number of papers.

The clearest new contributions are the Foundation identifier defect and the registry extraction correction. The other focused candidates concern validation, measured-source interpretation or methods. None establishes a new EMC protein mechanism or treatment-response finding comparable to the submitted CSPG4 paper. A low priority or null result did not stop a doable analysis: its complete source-qualified work is retained in the [45-option ledger](ALL45-EXECUTED-STATUS.md).

## Optional ninth contribution and existing-paper extensions

**Priority 9: one consolidated measured resource.** The [resource research note](drafts/public-sarcoma-measured-resource-research-note.md) combines the imaging, pediatric/RMS and small-EMC expression derivatives with source-qualified identities, complete testing families and reproducibility. All 50 imaging files/450 tiles, all 42 RMS count libraries, all 15 publisher workbooks/93 sheets and all 588 fixed RMS subtype/lineage tests were completed. Corrected spatial and same-assay association families were null; human-tumor/PDX RNA contrasts are assay-confounded. All 24 fixed PeerJ clinical-label comparisons failed their full-family correction. This is a lower-priority resource candidate, not three separate discovery papers. Editorial value depends on usefulness beyond these specific reanalyses and durable access to the derivatives.

**Priority 10: extend the existing RNA/fusion-output manuscript.** The [external-expression draft](drafts/external-expression-program-narrowing-short-report.md) analyzes 704 source specimens/644 eligible specimens, nine EMCs after known overlap exclusions, and all 32 released ATAC marker panels. The established three-gene core transfers; the broader sixteen-gene component does not. This is a measured narrowing of an existing claim, not evidence for a new broad fusion program.

**Priority 11: revise the existing ASO preprint with published measurements.** The [ASO qualification draft](drafts/aso-published-measurement-qualification.md) uses twelve duplexes nested in two ASOs, complete source expression/chemistry controls and four primary-source PTEN redesigns to test a frozen sequence descriptor. This supplies actual measured criticism of the scoring assumption. It does not measure EMC knockdown, delivery, selectivity or therapeutic safety, and should be incorporated into the existing work.
