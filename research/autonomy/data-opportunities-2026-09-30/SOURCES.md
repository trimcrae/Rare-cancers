# Source inventory and scientific opportunities

As of 1 October 2026. This inventory separates executed audits, inspected metadata/code, historical repository evidence and unresolved access leads. A public accession does not establish retrieval of every required file. Specimens, patients, libraries, images and table rows retain their original units.

The catalogue evaluates 33 registered endpoints plus 12 additional questions or leads. Sources can support overlapping questions; this is not an inventory of 45 publishable papers. See [ranking](RANKING.md), [catalogue](candidate-catalogue.json) and [all-backlog assessment](backlog-assessment.md).

## Evidence and prior-art boundary

Completed article/letter material comprises the response-unit audit, serial-biopsy availability audit, archived query comparison and repaired fusion-partner correspondence. Methylation has completed feasibility findings; spatial, proteomics and external gene-set work remain prospective.

Sources listed as historical audits or access leads were not converted into newly executed results. Source pins/hashes, actual retrieval and analysis outputs, end-to-end replay and exact independent-review bindings are retained under analysis/, results/ and reviews/. Novelty searches were finite and connector-limited. Current journal instructions, rendered packets and full publication gates remain unfinished.
