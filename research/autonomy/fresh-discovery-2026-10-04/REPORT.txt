SURVIVING DEMONSTRATED FINDINGS
None. This campaign produced no new useful EMC finding that clears the requested scientific bar. Published observations recovered or recomputed here are not presented as discoveries. No paper is being advanced.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY DONE
Four concurrent roles investigated secondary genomics, functional drug response, immune biology, and additional public sources. Questions and stopping rules were recorded before relevant results where possible; amendments, negative results, contrary observations and source limitations are retained. Completed workers independently challenged another lane's arithmetic, sample coverage and scientific value. The lead pursued the later ATR source-data deposit discovered during refreshed searches before closing the functional route.

The genomic pilot evaluated all 15 EMC-labelled specimens from 13 patients in MSK50K2026, all released mutations and high-level copy-number calls, and 15,770 structural-variant annotations for NR4A3 and historical aliases. Only TP53 was altered among seven selected cell-cycle genes, in two donors; one donor contributes repeated deletion specimens. The 2/13 descriptive proportion has a Wilson 95% interval of 0.0433–0.4223, but this selected series and incomplete fusion/QC support cannot establish disease prevalence or a prognostic association. Ten of the thirteen patients overlap the older eleven-patient MSK release, and September's work already used this same current cohort. Existing secondary-genomics literature already establishes TP53 involvement; no new independent recurrent lesion or credible outcome test survived.
Primary sources: https://www.cbioportal.org/study/summary?id=msk_impact_50k_2026 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40828003/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36948401/
Reproduction and full identities: genomics/analyze_pilot.py, genomics/results.json, genomics/all_emc_samples.csv, genomics/COVERAGE.txt.

The functional pilot extracted published USZ22 drug curves and comparator models from the 2023 primary study, including distinct 8-day and 12-day experiments. Follow-up recovered numerical data for the newer USZ23 ATR study under its earlier 2025 submission title, then evaluated the explicitly EMC-labelled response, DNA-fiber and pATR measurements with comparators. Strong berzosertib response and the camonsertib fiber result prevent a broad assertion of ATR-class resistance; compound, assay and exposure distinctions matter. These are the authors' source measurements, with one EMC model per relevant experiment, not an independently discovered EMC mechanism or therapeutic window. The 2025 deposit's alignment with the final 2026 article, some units/exposure details, replicate hierarchy and unlabeled panels remain unresolved; the packet preserves those gaps rather than silently treating the versions as identical.

The immune pilot evaluated published EMC macrophage/CD47 measurements and attempted a prespecified B-cell-specificity analysis of all twelve publicly released TempO-Seq specimens. Essential markers were absent from the filtered expression matrix despite being present in the assay design. A bounded diagnostic across all twelve raw libraries failed this campaign's prospective exact-matching allocation threshold; that threshold is not an established assay-quality criterion and does not invalidate the original study. The full raw data remain suitable, accessible, and pending. Further counting alone would not establish B-cell-specific prognosis: authenticated outcome/risk labels and an informative contrast are also missing, while the primary study already reports the multiple-testing limitation. No new positive or negative biological result was demonstrated.

PROMISING BUT UNFINISHED IDEAS
No unfinished idea yet has sufficient evidence to justify a standalone paper. The following remain conditional research questions, not claimed discoveries or recommendations to publish:
• Does drug-specific DDR response recur across independently authenticated EMC donors under comparable exposures? Existing measurements make this biologically plausible, but source-version/method reconciliation, all relevant public model conditions, and independent donor identity must precede a new comparative claim. Drug response is not proof of dependency, clinical efficacy or safety.
• Does an authenticated independent EMC cohort reveal a recurrent secondary lesion associated with progression beyond already reported TP53 findings? Broader genomic datasets and patient crosswalks remain incomplete. A repeated non-TP53 lesion with adequate measurement and clinical context could reopen the question; the present cohort does not do so.
The twelve-library B-cell question remains unresolved but is not currently a credible standalone contribution. Reopening requires authenticated specimen-level outcomes/risk definitions and a justified biological specificity contrast as well as validated measurements.

COVERAGE AND SCIENTIFIC LIMIT
This is a completed bounded discovery campaign, not a claim that every public EMC dataset has been exhaustively analyzed. All identified eligible observations in the evaluated sources are retained; unresolved panel identity and unexamined source conditions remain explicit gaps, and unchanged verified earlier analyses are reused with provenance. Suitable accessible evidence remains pending in several possible future claim domains; it blocks advancement of those claims.
