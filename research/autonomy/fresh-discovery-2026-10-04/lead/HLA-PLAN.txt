homozygous/non-informative loci, retained loci and LOH; do not treat missing as intact.

Multiple samples are not independent patients. Earlier MSK studies overlap.

retain per-locus and per-sample calls, proportion with exact binomial intervals.
No positive-selection filtering. If calls are absent or annotation is unsuitable,
stop this pilot as uninformative, not a negative biological result.
If present, test alternative explanations: assay/purity qualification, copy-number
calling criteria, same-patient variation, subtype misclassification; complete public
EMC coverage beyond this dataset before advancing any general claim. A matched
sarcoma comparison would require a dated amendment before comparator values.
