FRESH EMC GENOMICS PILOT — pre-analysis contract, 2026-10-04
Recorded after literature/metadata inspection but before patient or gene-level values.
Question: In a new public clinical-sequencing release, do independently authenticated EMC tumors show recurrent pathogenic secondary cell-cycle lesions beyond the defining NR4A3 fusion, and do lesions mark greater genome disruption? This would direct mechanistic study of progression beyond fusion partner alone, without claiming dependency or treatment benefit.
Prior art: Agaram2014 links non-EWSR1 partners to high-grade morphology; Huang2023 (58 cases, PMID36948401) finds size/metastasis rather than partner independently prognostic; Suemitsu2025 (18 patients/20 samples, PMID40828003) finds 2 recurrent TP53 and SGA-survival association. Davis2017 six metastatic cases have no recurring additional abnormality. Foundation2022's 75 labelled profiles and genomic correction are existing repo work, not a discovery here. Caris2026 (33 cases; 25 SGA evaluable) is prior aggregate evidence, not validation of a new finding.
New input: MSK-50K CancerCell2026 PMID41895280, cBioPortal msk_impact_50k_2026. All clinical EMC/EMCHS labels plus all NR4A3-associated SV candidates must be captured. Histology without a detected fusion is unconfirmed, not excluded from the corpus. Not observing a fusion does not prove absence because targeted DNA assay sensitivity differs by panel and intron.
Primary pilot: retrieve all EMC sample records and mutations/CNA/SV, deduplicate exact patient IDs, retain every repeated specimen with its ID and sample type. Separate fusion-supported cases and unresolved labels. Summarize TP53, RB1, CDKN2A/B, CDK4, CCND1 and MDM2 alterations, explicit VUS vs recognized driver annotations, and all other secondary events descriptively. No broad post hoc p-value search. Genes chosen before results based on cell-cycle literature.
Genomic disruption: compare per-patient FGA and WGD descriptively by cell-cycle alteration, only QC-passing FACETS; avoid ascertainment from defining copy-number events and avoid unsupported survival inference. Survival only if time origin, sampling relationship, and repeated patient representation can be established. Primary representative: first publicly provided sample order cannot imply chronological order; sensitivity via all-specimen and one-per-patient extrema, with no pseudo-replication.
Adversarial checks: assay gene-panel coverage; NR4A3 confirmation; VUS inflation; high-FGA diagnostic contamination; purity and specimen-type confounding; duplicate donor overlap with all prior MSK releases and Suemitsu2025. No pooling of overlapping cohorts; external unknown overlap remains gap.
