Final scientific reassessment, 4 October 2026, 16:17 UTC

SHELVE THE STANDALONE PAPERS. No new demonstrated EMC finding survived this lane.

The CD47/macrophage pilot recovered and evaluated published EMC protein results,
including the very low macrophage density in the five evaluable Dancsok cases.
That is important context for future immunotherapy experiments, but it is already
in the primary study. The evidence does not support a new standalone contribution.

The B-cell pilot did not establish either a positive or a negative disease result.
The released filtered matrix lacks essential prespecified markers. Every marker
is in the actual assay design, and all twelve raw libraries are publicly available,
so these are missing processed measurements rather than absent EMC expression.

Our exact-match method gate failed its original 75% allocation criterion. This
criterion is a prospective workflow rule, not an established assay validity limit.
The bounded checks covered every library and examined read length, orientation,
probe version and mismatched half-probe pairs. They do not prove that the original
study is invalid or that usable counts cannot be obtained with a validated pipeline.

The lead proposed one further estimator-validation step using disjoint 100,000-read
prefixes and correspondence to the published matrix. Before any such data were
examined, the independent genomics reviewer and lead assessed its scientific value.
Even successful validation would not settle the biological question: correlation
between B-cell markers and PTPRC is expected from immune content, and a small,
nonsignificant marker residual cannot disprove a B-cell-specific prognosis effect.
The published risk-group labels and clinical outcomes are not authenticated here.
The original study already reports loss of significance after multiple testing.

Decision: do not run the additional 100,000-read diagnostic or full FASTQ streams
solely to produce a methods result. Neither was started; no new results selected
this decision. All twelve suitable raw libraries remain pending accessible analysis.

Reopening requires a specimen-specific published risk/outcome crosswalk and an
independently justified contrast capable of changing the biological interpretation,
alongside adequate validated marker measurements. New counts alone are insufficient.
Complete relevant public EMC evidence evaluation and independent omission challenge
remain required before any claim is advanced. TMEM266 remains shelved.
