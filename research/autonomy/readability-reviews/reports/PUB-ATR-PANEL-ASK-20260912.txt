The rewritten sentence explicitly assigns both to the protein and invites a wrong reading. Separate the questions, for example protein recruitment versus responses of cells carrying these fusions. ## Five reader explanations, independently written

**Question:** What proteins are predicted by reported NR4A3 gene joins in EMC, and what limited predictions can their sequences support about collecting at damaged DNA?

**Approach:** The study assembled joined RNA sequences from public reference transcripts, translated them into protein models, and counted retained arginine-glycine pairs. It compared these sequence features with constructs studied in other fusion cancers and compared TCF12 with the FET protein family across a common set of sequence lengths.

**Main finding:** Four reported junctions yielded models preserving the complete NR4A3 portion. Building the RNA join first predicts an extra 59 amino acids in one model that the earlier protein-level model omitted. The type-1 and type-2 models retain different amounts of RG sequence. TCF12 remains compositionally separate from FET proteins across the evaluated lengths.

**Main limitations:** These are reference-sequence predictions, not observations of patient proteins or protein recruitment. Exon numbering rests on one annotation source and patient junctions remain unverified. Sequence position between measured comparison constructs does not provide a recruitment measurement. The proposed experiments cannot uniquely attribute an outcome to RG content, and nothing here establishes ATR-inhibitor response or clinical benefit.

**Next step:** Verify the actual nucleotide junctions and then test the specified recruitment predictions with the stated comparison and partner-alone controls. The TCF12-fusion prediction cannot be tested with full-length TCF12 alone; it needs a sourced fusion junction and a corresponding construct. No laboratory work is reported by this paper.

The complete body supports these explanations. The abstract alone is not yet sufficient to recover the meaning of the reference fractions without reading the Methods.
