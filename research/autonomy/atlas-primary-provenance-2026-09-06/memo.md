The recovery resolves RNA dye orientation and the smallest common-platform sample set. It does **not** yet remove either the full reference-compatibility condition (C3) or independent-cohort condition (C12). This is a recoverable evidence gap, not evidence of duplicate patients or a defeated atlas direction.

The candidate is ten EMC arrays versus three DFSP arrays on GPL3290. All 36 GSE4303 records, across seven platforms, label channel 1 Cy3 and channel 2 Cy5. The GPL3290 reference names are CRH-mRNA for ten EMC, CRH for three DFSP, and UHR for three GIST. The series matrix and family SOFT agree on the 16 GPL3290 accessions. The family contains each GSM's primary metadata, full platform annotations and raw channel columns; separate downloads of every GSM page are unnecessary. [GEO family source](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE4nnn/GSE4303/soft/GSE4303_family.soft.gz), [matrix directory](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE4nnn/GSE4303/matrix/).

Subramanian et al. examined ten morphologically reviewed frozen EMC cases and reused 26 previously published comparator arrays. The array arm had Stanford IRB approval; procurement institutions for each EMC are not stated. The separate Stanford tissue-microarray archive is not proof of recruitment for these array specimens. The author-hosted clinical table lists STT3696 while GEO contains STT3714; the remaining nine base labels match after treating the STT2528(2) suffix as unresolved. The table calls STT2528 a recurrence despite generic GEO primary-tumor annotation. STT2003 and STT3697 share published age/sex/site/size; this is not an identity linkage. [Article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/path.1792), [author-hosted Table 1](https://tma.im/tma_portal/emc/figures/table1.htm). Publisher access returned 403; an author-uploaded full text was read through indexed web extraction, whose serialized output is archived separately from exact source bytes.

The three DFSP labels link directly to Linn et al.'s Table 1: STT3126, STT3068 and STT3053. That primary study describes collection through pathology departments at Stanford, Vancouver, University of Washington and Cleveland; individual sites are not assigned to these three cases. Its expression methods explicitly prepare Cy3 reference and Cy5 tumor cDNA from mRNA, with an eleven-cell-line reference pool. Thus the methods corroborate orientation while leaving the reference-description discrepancy unresolved. The study's outcome-selected gene filters must not be reused as an unbiased atlas feature universe. [Linn primary full text, RNA expression methods and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1892373/).

GSE24369 supplies six EMC biopsy records, EMC1–EMC6, with no public patient identifiers in the GSM metadata. The depositor and assay laboratory are at Lund; neither establishes recruitment of every patient there. Moller et al.'s recovered supplement says two additional EMC samples were used only for RT-PCR, so they must not be counted as additional array validation. The 2017 primary reuse paper explicitly reuses the six published EMC controls. Its newly recovered Supplementary Table S1 lists EMC cases 46–51, with clinical data and fusion results; it does not map them to EMC1–EMC6. Four rows cite Mitelman reference 9736 cases 3, 5, 4 and 7, respectively. [GSE24369](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE24369), [Moller DOI](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-11-0145), [original supplement](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22442952), [2017 reuse DOI](https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-17-1856), [exact Table S1 record](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/22466361).

The supplement's citation was resolved through the current Mitelman reference interface to Panagopoulos et al. (2002), PMID 12378528, DOI 10.1002/gcc.10127. Publisher full text returned 403; Lund's record supplies an abstract, not clinical methods or a sample map. This is a concrete public provenance lead for four legacy EMC cases, but not established separation from GSE4303. We do not infer clinical identities from demographic resemblance or demand an impossible universal patient crosswalk: credible specimen recruitment documentation could suffice. [Mitelman reference 9736](https://mitelmandatabase.isb-cgc.org/ref_details?refno=9736), [Lund primary-publication record](https://lup.lub.lu.se/record/110661).

No new unconfounded biological EMC-versus-DFSP contrast is certified. The smallest technically specified contrast is the **observed normalized tumor/reference ratios** for ten EMC and three DFSP on GPL3290, explicitly conditional on reference compatibility. It cannot yet be called an independent tumor-expression validation. GIST's UHR records must not silently join that contrast. No normal-tissue selectivity, therapeutic-window, efficacy or target-ranking conclusion follows.

The next justified experiment is a bounded **reference-channel diagnostic**, using the already recovered raw Cy3 channels for the same 13 arrays: inspect signal, background, flags and reference-profile batch structure against diagnosis and reference label, with the three UHR/GIST arrays as a separately labelled diagnostic control. It can falsify practical reference compatibility, but high correlation alone cannot establish identical composition or eliminate batch confounding. Do not batch-correct a diagnosis-confounded reference difference into a biological result. In parallel source work, the named Panagopoulos methods/table and an EMC1–6 to cases46–51 mapping are the precise remaining provenance targets; their public recovery could change C12.
