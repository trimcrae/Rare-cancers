Bounded source search and access decisions

Direct RNA localization
No inspected source supplied a TMEM266 measurement localized to authentic
EMC malignant cells by single-cell/spatial RNA, RNA-ISH or microdissection.
This is not an exhaustive claim that no such material exists.
- PMC12728484: the retrieved single-cell/spatial measurements were
  epithelioid sarcoma; an EMC bulk comparison was not a spatial EMC assay.
- CIVO/GeoMx material inspected: fibrosarcoma, not EMC for the proposed
  quantitative spatial measurement. Other-cancer evidence cannot substitute.
- GSE28866: bulk FFPE cores; the publication's novel-lncRNA in situ analysis
  was in breast tissue, not TMEM266 in EMC.
- Seitz pediatric atlas, https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10374394/v1 :
  available main labels did not authenticate EMC inclusion; relevant data
  access was controlled. The linked EGAS50000000393 record did not resolve
  the proposed tissue dataset. No access request or outreach was sent.
- PeerJ21497's reported imaging targets did not include TMEM266.

Additional patient tumor RNA
Brenca2019 https://doi.org/10.1002/path.5284 described tumor-rich material
with >70 percent tumor nuclei. The available s005/s006 selected-gene tables
do not contain TMEM266 or inspected aliases. A missing selected row is not
zero expression. Corrigendum https://doi.org/10.1002/path.5737 points to
PRJNA692081; no full raw patient reads were downloaded.
The reanalysis at https://doi.org/10.3389/fgene.2024.1440994 supplied eight
engineered-fibroblast libraries, not patient tumor evidence. This distinction
is retained despite measurable TMEM266 counts in all eight libraries.

Coordinate/isoform interpretation
GENCODEv26 through UCSC is the matching annotation for recount3 G026.
Current Ensembl supplies interpretive context; it was not substituted into
the frozen G026 gene-sum check. Current JSON key ordering and UCSC request
metadata can change hashes without changing sequence/annotation payloads.
https://rest.ensembl.org/lookup/id/ENSG00000169758?expand=1;content-type=application/json
Both annotations preserve multiple transcripts sharing the assay junction.

Specimen identity sensitivity
The primary article's table supplies aggregate FISH counts; its Cox-model
supplement and expression workbook lack the specimen crosswalk. Explicit
FISH_1 labels were recovered from BioSamples SAMN53073757-SAMN53073768,
linked to Si identifiers through the primary-associated PRJNA1357027 deposit.
All twelve aliases match exactly, with eight EWSR1+ and four EWSR1-.
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/api/xml/SAMN53073757
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/api/xml/SAMN53073759
Exact per-record receipts, deposited attributes and run linkage are in
fish-sensitivity-results.json. Other metadata do not fully reconcile to
the article's cohort dates/origins; retain this provenance limitation.

All retrievals were public, read-only HTTP/API or local files. No UI,
browser automation, paid access, outreach, publication or public mutation.
