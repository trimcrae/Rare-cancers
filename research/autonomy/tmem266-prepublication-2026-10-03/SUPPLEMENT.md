# Supplementary methods and evidence for the TMEM266 research letter

This document accompanies the exploratory [working draft](DRAFT.md). It distinguishes discovery, follow-up within reused cohorts, and orthogonal measurements. Sample counts below are assay-specific and are not pooled as independent patients. All paths are relative to this document unless stated otherwise.

## Discovery and selection

The dated [discovery plan](../discovery-2026-10-03/ROUND2-PLAN.txt) fixed a reviewed-human UniProt universe using plasma-membrane location SL-0039 and transmembrane keyword KW-0812 or GPI keyword KW-0336. Ambiguous multi-symbol entries were excluded before matrix inspection. The frozen universe, source query, and receipt are `round2-surface-universe.tsv` and `round2-surface-universe-receipt.json` in that folder. Exactly 2,694 symbols were readable in the source expression matrix.

The nine eligible primary EMC samples were compared with 14 myxoid liposarcomas, 13 LGFMS, and 18 synovial sarcomas. Existing specimen-overlap exclusions removed 104-92, 168-97, and 536-00; 5081-14 was excluded as a recurrence. The complete sample manifest is bound by hash in `round2-surface-hits-frozen.json`. Missing identity information was not filled from expression. For each gene, directional Mann–Whitney tests used the exact method without ties and an asymptotic tie-corrected method otherwise. The largest P value across the three comparisons was adjusted by Benjamini–Hochberg across all tested genes. Allocation required q≤0.05, marginal and same-year A≥0.85 for every comparison, EMC median TPM≥5 and minimum TPM≥1, and a median log2(1+TPM) difference≥1 for every comparator. These were research allocation criteria, not clinical cutoffs.

Same-year A is the mean of within-year A values weighted by the number of EMC specimens in each shared year. Twenty-one genes passed discovery; their list was frozen before array values were read. All were evaluated, with alias mapping specified before reading previously unmapped target values. Array follow-up required a median EMC–comparator difference>0 and A>0.5 for each of four histologies, plus A≥0.8 against LGFMS; 16 genes met that rule. TMEM266 selection from these results and subsequent prior-art appraisal remain exploratory. A discovery q value does not adjust every later selection or confidence interval.

`round2_surface_discovery.py`, `round2_surface_replication.py`, `round2_surface_context.py`, `round2_array_uncertainty.py`, and `round2_muscle_controls.py` retain the analysis. `round2_independent_verify.py` independently reproduced all discovery rows and selected array results. The wider 36-histology comparison reused discovery data and is contextual, not 36 replications. No aggregate 15-patient count is justified for the nine RNA-sequencing and six array EMC specimens: unresolved Lund overlap remains despite excluding known reused specimens and a sensitivity excluding 5241-06.

## Sequence assignment and raw-array analysis

The [current plan](PLAN.txt) recorded groups, scores, controls, sampling unit, uncertainty calculation, and stop rule before raw target intensities were inspected. [probe-map.csv](probe-map.csv) contains all 30 physical probe identifiers, X/Y positions, sequences, child-region assignments, and hg19 intervals. These were recovered from the manufacturer-derived `hugene10stv1probe_2.18.0.tar.gz` annotation archive and official GPL10739 region annotation for cluster 7985066. The original study uses GPL6244 gene-level summaries; GPL10739 supplies the finer annotation of the same Gene 1.0 ST design, not another biological cohort. The archive SHA256 is `2d2fc3d5627b0e7d5b86490f4f528a732d017278a86faf812311c81a7883c6b2`.

The primary group has 14 probes in regions 7985069–7985074, corresponding to canonical exons 2–7. Two probes (54108, 772184) cross the current 5′ UTR/CDS boundary. The strict sensitivity removes them by annotation, leaving 12 probes in five coding regions, exons 3–7. The eight downstream probes occupy regions 7985076–7985078 (exons 9–11). Two probes in region 7985075 overlap a known antisense region and were kept separately. Six remaining probes provide untranslated-region context; one of these overlaps the current first exon by only 19 of 25 nucleotides, with six upstream bases. No context probe was moved into the primary or strict group on the basis of intensity.

[probe_sequence_verify.py](probe_sequence_verify.py) verifies all 30 reverse-complement 25-mers against reference hg19 and hg38 locus sequences and maps them to current Ensembl canonical transcript ENST00000388942.9. The current CDS start is 76134287 in 0-based hg38 coordinates, 24 nucleotides later than the GENCODE v26 model. Its first mixed probe contains 24 UTR bases and one coding base; the other contains 21 UTR and four coding bases. These checks establish exact reference compatibility within the inspected locus; they do not establish genome-wide uniqueness, experimental hybridization specificity, or endogenous isoform abundance. Results are in [probe-sequence-verification.json](probe-sequence-verification.json).

[raw_array_pilot.py](raw_array_pilot.py) retrieves bounded byte ranges from the official [BioStudies E-GEOD-24369 mirror](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress/studies/E-GEOD-24369). It reads Calvin headers, the complete Intensity dataset, and Mask/Outlier datasets; it does not persist whole CEL files. All six EMC arrays (GSM600934–GSM600939), 17 LGFMS arrays (GSM600940–GSM600956), and two muscle pools (GSM600968–GSM600969) were selected from original metadata. Each contains 1,102,500 finite intensity features. Retrieval required 114,321,958 bytes in the coordinator replay, below its 120 MB budget; byte ranges and SHA256 receipts are retained per array.

The primary score is the equal-weight mean of six region means of within-array intensity percentile. Percentiles use average ranks divided by the number of finite features. Same-probe log2(raw intensity), downstream, untranslated-context, and antisense-shared groups are secondary summaries. The strict score weights its five retained regions equally. These raw-feature transformations are not RMA expression estimates or transcript abundance. Eleven of 750 target measurements carried CEL outlier flags and none carried mask flags. All target values and flags are retained; no target probe was dropped for an observed value or flag.

Controls were fixed before target intensities: 10 non-target physical probes per target, sampled without replacement within the same GC-count stratum using NumPy `default_rng(20261004)`, in the saved target order. Manufacturer annotation was deduplicated to 804,955 physical identifiers from 861,493 rows with no conflicting sequences for repeated identifiers. Sorted identifiers were sampled after excluding all 30 TMEM266 probes. Controls assess broad intensity behavior; they are not a biologically validated negative-gene set. The 300 selected identifiers are frozen in the analysis script and output.

The support rule required primary A≥0.80, bootstrap lower bound>0.5, and A>0.5 in at least four of six upstream regions. All six regions met the directional criterion. Two thousand independent within-group array bootstrap samples, seed 20261004, produced percentile 95% intervals. Array specimens, not probes or regions, are resampled. The small sample size, post-selection analysis, raw-intensity background, and residual technical confounding limit inference. The primary A=0.970588 and strict A=0.950980 are supported by the saved full [raw-array-results.json](raw-array-results.json); this analysis is not a new patient cohort.

The primary score medians were 0.707705 (EMC) and 0.420240 (LGFMS); the strict medians were 0.731434 and 0.398408. Muscle-pool primary values were 0.692764 and 0.703954; strict values were 0.694560 and 0.728668. The same-GC primary control medians were 0.524792 and 0.518514 (A=0.852941). Same-probe log2(raw intensity) yielded A=0.990196 for both primary and strict groups, whereas the downstream percentile score yielded A=0.833333. No platform scales are pooled or interpreted as cross-platform fold changes.

An independent worker parsed two arrays fixed by accession before intensity inspection, GSM600934 and GSM600940, using a separate Calvin parser and NumPy sorting/search rather than the pilot's SciPy rank calculation. All 28 primary raw values, 12 region means, and both complete Intensity hashes agreed; four composite scores agreed within 1.11×10−16. This verifies the selected extraction and arithmetic, not every flag dataset or the biological specificity of the assay.

## Orthogonal tissue and culture measurements

The preceding [tissue analysis packet](../tmem266-tissue-2026-10-03/RESULTS.txt) preserves the exact PeerJ supplementary workbook, extraction scripts, manufacturer-probe matching, and independent checks. Its all-12 summary is retained unchanged as a historical artifact, with the following qualification carried forward into this draft: deposited sample Si22 has library name Si21. [identity_sensitivity.py](identity_sensitivity.py) authenticates that conflict and reports the exclusion without guessing a relabel. The 11 remaining specimens have median Log2CPM 3.978364 and range −0.375772–7.222901. The eight EWSR1 break-apart-positive specimens are unchanged (median 5.373868; range 1.126678–7.222901). Break-apart status is not an identified NR4A3 fusion partner, and all 12 cases are not claimed to be fusion-confirmed. The all-12 median is 5.227021.

The matching official v2.0 probe TMEM266_14670 is `GTGGTGACCATACACACAGCTGGCTCTGGGACACCGCTGTCACTGCTGGG`. Its reverse complement matches the joined hg38 intervals chr15:[76202254,76202264) and [76203740,76203780), the last 10 nucleotides of canonical exon 10 and first 40 of exon 11. This junction is outside the identified antisense overlap but shared by canonical, nonsense-mediated-decay, and retained-intron models. Exact matching to the named assay design does not confirm its batch revision or validate all possible off-targets. The 9,500-row filtered export has no authenticated detection threshold; ranks and positive numbers are not tumor-positivity calls.

The article and deposited provenance disagree on some dates and identifiers. Moreover, its proposed GSE6481 external cohort consists of other sarcomas, including 19 myxoid liposarcomas, not 19 EMC specimens. We therefore do not use that claim as EMC validation. These source qualifications limit confidence in the orthogonal series; neither implies that absent export rows represent biologically absent genes or that all FFPE measurements are invalid. Primary records and bounded review are recorded in [SOURCE-GATES.txt](SOURCE-GATES.txt).

One patient-derived culture, V1-34/GSM2113301 in Davila et al., is source-labelled EWSR1–NR4A3 in supplementary Table S6. SRR3380704 and SRR3380705 are technical runs of that one biological sample. Public recount3 aligned-base coverage sums assigned to TMEM266 were 1,333 and 1,694. Full-resolution locus queries reproduce these sums in four downstream union-exon intervals, including extended/alternative transcript regions; canonical exons 1–8 have zero coverage. The shared antisense interval also has zero coverage. Neither sparse coverage nor AUC-scaled values (13.2947 and 16.3312 at a target library size of 40 million) quantify full-length molecules, TPM, CPM, or isoform predominance. Culture identity is source-authenticated, not newly validated by STR or purity testing.

## Literature scope and remaining questions

Original profiling studies and the actual submitted CSPG4 letter were read before interpreting this result. The latter provided a model for an empirical contribution with comparator and normal-tissue limitations; its manuscript status was not evidence for TMEM266. Searches included TMEM266, C15orf27, HVRP1, FLJ38190, and cluster 7985066. No direct EMC-specific TMEM266 claim was recovered, but the complete candidate set from the Dulken screen could not be authenticated. The indexed supplement was an INSM1/ENO2 figure, not an established 1,283-gene workbook. We make no claim that TMEM266 was absent from all prior screens.

Further raw patient sequencing was considered before drafting. Hofvander raw data require controlled access; public gene TPMs cannot supply read-level isoform evidence. Brenca PRJNA692081 includes patient tissue and engineered cultures with generic tissue labels, and an adequate patient/run crosswalk was not recovered. Its remaining unassigned runs were not inferred to be tumors by expression or sample-number patterns. No FASTQ prefixes were examined. H-EMC-SS was not treated as decisive EMC validation because its cataloged identity concerns differ from the source-authenticated V1-34 culture. These are unresolved evidence routes, not negative TMEM266 experiments.

The study therefore supports a comparative tissue-RNA association extending to upstream coding regions. It leaves cell of origin, full-length transcript identity, protein production, functional role, and diagnostic value unresolved. Localizing upstream and downstream RNA in the same fusion-confirmed malignant cells, with muscle and stromal controls, is the next discriminating measurement; full-length RNA and protein would require separate evidence.

## Reproduction and release status

From the repository root, use the installed Python runtime with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` and `.cache/python-deps` on `PYTHONPATH`. Scripts in the discovery and tissue folders refer to existing authenticated source inputs in the repository; they are not presented as a self-contained public package. The new raw-array script reads public byte ranges and writes only selected results. The probe-verification and identity-sensitivity scripts use small public API queries. Preserve the dated plans when rerunning; do not silently overwrite a frozen release.

The optional [probe-map recovery script](recover_probe_map.py) preserves the worker's archive-decoding and source-matching steps and can compare the recovered map and controls with frozen artifacts. Those steps were executed during the source investigation; the assembled script has been syntax-checked but has not been rerun end to end. The original reference-sequence verification and raw-array replay were run and their outputs are retained.

The local manifest binds this draft, methods, code, selected data, and verification receipts. No manuscript figure has been generated from uncommitted data. A public, portable source/code package, final author review, venue formatting, and the repository-required independent ultra review of a frozen outgoing version remain release tasks. Their absence does not change the reported measurements and means this working draft is not submission-ready.
