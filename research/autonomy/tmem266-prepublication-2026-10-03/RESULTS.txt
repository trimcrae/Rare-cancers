TMEM266 prepublication investigation and draft checkpoint
3 October 2026 America/New_York / 4 October UTC

Demonstrated candidate (one, unranked)
A new analysis of published EMC tissue arrays found that the EMC-associated
TMEM266 RNA signal extends across upstream coding regions: six EMC samples
exceeded 17 LGFMS samples with primary A=.970588 and strict coding-region
A=.950980, concordant with the existing gene-level and downstream-target data.
This makes localization of TMEM266 RNA within EMC cells a specific research
priority, but normal muscle overlaps and the arrays neither identify the
expressing cells nor phase a complete protein-coding transcript.

The new result is assay-region evidence in the reused GSE24369 cohort, not
additional patients or independent validation. Primary bootstrap95% interval
.882353-1; strict .823529-1; exploratory and conditional on prior gene selection.
All six primary regions show A>.5, not only the downstream/antisense region.
Fourteen upstream probes include two mixed current-UTR/CDS probes; the strict
12-probe group covers five current coding regions. All30 target probes,
including11/750 outlier-flagged measurements, remain in saved output as planned.
GC-matched controls show a small class shift and do not eliminate technical
confounding. Both normal-muscle pools overlap the EMC score range.

What changed the drafting decision
Before this turn, downstream junction/locus evidence did not establish
whether the comparative tissue signal extended to upstream coding regions.
Authentic source probe maps and bounded raw-CEL ranges now support that
specific extension. The clinical/novelty reviewer judged the combined
comparative-RNA contribution suitable for a narrow exploratory research
letter, with no first-ever claim and no tumor-specific target conclusion.
DRAFT.md and SUPPLEMENT.md now integrate the demonstrated results.
Three independent workers reviewed distinct claims/methods/sequence scopes;
one citation placeholder and one incompletely stated allocation rule were
repaired. This is not an ultra review or a submission-readiness decision.

Prior-art review checked original Moller2011 supplementary tables, the
Dulken2024 CHRNA6 study, TMEM266 prior biology, and prior completed repository
work. No direct EMC-specific TMEM266 claim was retrieved, but full prior-screen
candidate coverage remains incomplete. Absence in search is not proof of
novelty, and this draft makes no first-discovery claim.

The FFPE source review recovered a previously known Si22/Si21 identity conflict
that had not been carried into our preceding packet's FISH summary. Original

qualification. It does not alter the8 EWSR1-positive subgroup. GSE6481 cannot

source-reliability limits, not the proposed scientific contribution.

Promising but unfinished evidence routes
Cell-localized upstream plus downstream TMEM266 RNA in fusion-authenticated
EMC cells would resolve the main question, with entrapped muscle/stromal controls.
Full-length RNA and protein require separate evidence. Publicly recovered
materials did not provide those measurements. Available culture coverage is
sparse/downstream, and its two runs are technical replicates of one culture.
No authenticated EMC-specific spatial/single-cell/protein measurement was found.

Rejected or stopped investigations
BrencaPRJNA692081 tissue/engineered-culture runs lack an adequate crosswalk;
generic BioSample tissue labels are insufficient, so no FASTQ prefixes were
examined. Hofvander raw reads require controlled access; public gene TPMs do
not establish transcript structure. Davis2017 public reports had no usable
TMEM266 matrix. H-EMC-SS identity concerns prevent treating its name as proof
of an authentic EMC model. The existing culture and engineered-fibroblast
results cannot substitute for tissue localization or fusion-induced expression.
No additional speculative pilot was created just to occupy worker slots.
