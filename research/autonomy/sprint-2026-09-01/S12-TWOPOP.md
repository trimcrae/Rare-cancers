## What I measured

### The parameterisation (the point of the row)

```
pazopanib_phase2                 19.0 mo  ci95            [11.0, 27.0]
sunitinib_nivolumab_immunosarc2  13.2 mo  ci95            [ 5.7, 20.7]
anthracycline_italian_rcn         8.0 mo  observed_range  [ 2.0, 10.0]   ⛔ NOT a CI
chiusole_metastatic_chemo         9.0 mo  none            NO INTERVAL — carried as a point
```

`verification.zero_width_intervals_emitted` is **empty**, checked programmatically: `[9.0, 9.0]` is
never emitted anywhere at any level. The Chiusole arm carries `interval: null`, `propagated: false`
and a reason string in every block that touches it.

`observed_range` is propagated but labelled as what it is — a spread of *per-patient* times, which
**widens** with cohort size where a confidence interval **narrows**. The two move in opposite
directions; quoting one as the other inverts the meaning, which is why the kind travels with the
number rather than beside it.

**⭐ An inversion worth carrying forward.** The arm with **no** dispersion is the **best-verified**
median in the set — Chiusole's 9 months was re-acquired three independent ways at HTTP 200 (GitHub
Actions run 31276131242) after this repository had twice asserted the paper reported no median at
all. The arm with the **tightest** interval, pazopanib's 19 months (95 % CI 11–27), is read from
**a review's account of the trial**, not from the trial report; the sibling model records the same
thing independently ("the primary abstract does not state a median PFS"). Interval width and
evidentiary standing are orthogonal, and only the width is visible in a plot.

### The model

Two-population competitive Lotka–Volterra, K normalised to 1, symmetric competition, drug on `S`
only — the same structural form as the sibling so the two are comparable. Progression is **RECIST
1.1, measured from the nadir**. Explicit Euler, dt = 1 day, 25-year horizon. `r_R` fitted per arm by
bisection to 1e-9/day. Grid: `f0 ∈ {1e-4 … 0.2}` (3.3 decades) × `cost ∈ {0, 0.3}` × two burden-unit
conventions × four arms = **80 cells**.

**Scoped up front and stopped there (CLAUDE.md §5):** deterministic integration, one progression
rule, two burden conventions, one grid. No bootstrap, no Monte Carlo, no replicates, no reactive
sensitivity layer.

### Result 1 — ⛔ the hypothesis this module was built on is refuted by its own grid

The prior expectation was tidy: under continuous dosing the sensitive compartment is suppressed
early, so a median reads out the resistant compartment's growth **rate** and says nothing about its
**size** — which would mean the medians pin the parameter the adaptive question does *not* turn on.
**The grid does not show it.** Sweeping `f0` over 3.3 decades changes the rate fitted to the *same*
median by up to **2.74×**, because at the modelled initial burden (0.5 K) both logistic saturation
and the depth of the nadir depend on `f0`.

What replaces it is stronger, and it is the headline:

| what moves the calibrated growth rate | factor | who chooses it |
|---|---|---|
| burden-unit convention (RECIST diameters vs spherical volumes) | **3.0000** (exact) | the modeller, usually not stated |
| initial resistant fraction `f0`, 3.3 decades | **2.7447** (measured) | nobody — unmeasured in EMC |
| **THE DATA:** spread of the four published medians, 8.0 → 19.0 mo | **2.3750** (exact) | the published record |
| fitness cost of resistance, 0 → 0.3 | 1.2395 (measured) | nobody — unmeasured in EMC |

**Two of the three modelling degrees of freedom outrank the data.** The convention factor is exactly
`ln(1.2³)/ln(1.2) = 3` because every rate here is proportional to `ln θ`. A rate quoted from a model
of this disease without both its burden-unit convention and its assumed resistant fraction is not a
quantity.

### Result 2 — ⭐ a definitional collision, exact rather than numerical

A Gatenby-style adaptive schedule withdraws drug at burden ≤ 0.5 × baseline. **That withdrawal
defines the nadir.** Recovery toward baseline is a ratio of 2.0 above that nadir, and the largest
progression threshold either convention uses is 1.728. `2.0 > 1.728`, so **the planned rebound
crosses the RECIST progression threshold by construction** — under both conventions, at every
parameter value, whatever the biology.

Measured, and the separation is total:

| progression rule | cells where adaptive > continuous | ratio range |
|---|---|---|
| RECIST 1.1, nadir-referenced | **0 / 80** | 0.126 – 0.700 |
| baseline-referenced (the sibling model's rule) | **80 / 80** | 1.067 – 1.434 |

⛔ **This is not evidence that adaptive scheduling would shorten anyone's disease control**, and the
artifact says so: an endpoint that fires on a planned, reversible rebound is measuring the schedule
rather than the disease. ⛔ **It is also not a defect found in the sibling model**, whose outputs are
all dimensionless ratios in which a common rule largely cancels — the rule choice is reported as a
first-class result instead of being fixed silently in one direction. ⛔ **No claim is made about how
adaptive-therapy trials in other diseases define progression**; no survey has been done, and
`lint_claims` R6 is exactly the rule that would catch inventing one.

The same convention is worth a factor of 2.7 in *time*, at one fitted point: the same `r_R` gives
**19.0 months** under the nadir rule and **52.1 months** under the baseline rule.

### Result 3 — the calibration that does survive, per arm

Each median bounds the growth of whatever compartment progressed: `r_R ≥ ln θ / T_median`,
equivalently a doubling time `≤ T_median · ln2/ln θ`. It is a **bound, not an estimate**, because the
median covers time-to-nadir *plus* the regrowth phase, and unrepresentable new-lesion progression
pushes the same way. Diameter convention, upper bounds on the doubling time: pazopanib 72.2 mo
(CI-induced 41.8–102.6), sunitinib+nivolumab 50.2 mo (21.7–78.7), anthracycline 30.4 mo
(observed-range-induced 7.6–38.0, **not a CI**), Chiusole 34.2 mo (**no interval**). Divide all of
them by 3 for the volume convention.

## What the program can say now, and at what weight

1. **The specification is executed, not merely correct.** RT-SCHEDULING's grade said what exists is
   "exactly a parameters-as-intervals model with one parameter that has no interval at all". That
   was a specification. It now runs, and the parameterisation survives contact with the arithmetic:
   three dispersion kinds coexist in one output and the fourth arm propagates nothing.
2. **The headline is a negative at full strength.** The burden-unit convention (3.00×) and the
   unmeasured initial resistant fraction (2.74×) each move the calibrated rate at least as far as
   the entire spread between the four cohorts (2.38×). Calibrating to all four medians does not make
   a scheduling prediction identifiable and **a fifth median would not help**. That tells the next
   session what to stop asking for.
3. **A new, sharp, model-independent claim:** a burden-fraction withdrawal rule and a
   nadir-referenced RECIST endpoint cannot coexist. Any future adaptive-schedule proposal in this
   disease must state its progression endpoint before it states anything else. This is the one
   result that holds under both conventions and at every parameter value.
4. **The misattributed figures are unchanged and stand alone.** They need no model. Their weight is
   bounded by one check nobody has run — whether any of them reached treatment guidance — which
   RT-SCHEDULING's own `remaining_unknowns` already names as the thing that would raise this
   finding considerably.

**At what weight:** model output on published summary statistics. Four single-arm cohorts, two
retrospective, one median from a review, one from a conference abstract, one with no dispersion at
all, each identified with a single deterministic trajectory under an assumption
(`method.identification_assumption`) that is false in detail and stated rather than buried — the
median of a distribution of times is not the time of the median parameter set. **Nothing here is
evidence about a patient.** The two findings that do not depend on the burden-unit convention
(the ranking, and the definitional collision) are the strongest; every rate with a unit on it is
convention-dependent and must be quoted with its convention or not at all.

**PUB-STRATEGY-ARCH consequence.** `systems/graph/publications.json` already records that the
drafted paper covers the reachability variable only, and names RT-SCHEDULING's findings as real and
publishable but not yet in the manuscript. This adds two paragraphs' worth of publishable content to
that gap — the degrees-of-freedom ranking and the endpoint collision — and neither asserts anything
about a drug. ⛔ **I did not touch the manuscript**, and PUB-STRATEGY-ARCH is not named for any
outward-facing act.
