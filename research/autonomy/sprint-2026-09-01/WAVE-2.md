## The one instruction S17 carries that the others do not

A hedge quietly dropped to shorten a sentence is the only way this seat can do real damage, and it
is invisible to every guard in the repository — `lint_claims` reads claim STRENGTH, and an inverted
claim is still a grammatical, hedged-looking sentence at the same length. CLAUDE.md §6 records the
13 inverted claims that reached `origin/main` this way, every one of which turned a caveat into an
overclaim. So the seat's deliverable is not the count: it is **a table of every sentence changed,
original beside replacement**, so the driver audits the meaning rather than trusting it.
