2026-10-04 R7 functional models — SHELVE

No new EMC vulnerability or resistance finding survived this bounded gate. A newly
evaluated primary study contains a real published EMC-labelled tissue observation,
but it is one donor's qualitative fluorescence and does not establish a controlled
treatment response. No numerical drug-effect pilot was justified.

Bohner2022 (DOI10.1186/s13018-022-02931-x) placed tissue from a 19-year-old woman
with author-labelled EMC on chick chorio-allantoic membranes (CAM). The complete
published row reports prior chemotherapy,14 inoculated eggs,2 samples per egg,
10 evaluable eggs,7 histological confirmations and single-positive5-ALA-associated
fluorescence. These eggs/grafts are not independent EMC patients. The main source
does not provide a fusion result or case-linked untreated/PDD/PDT/dose/viability
crosswalk. Its authors explicitly state that spontaneous regression prevented
meaningful interpretation of photodynamic-treatment-associated necrosis. Benign
and borderline tumors also fluoresced. Re-extracting this already published
observation is not a new selective vulnerability, killing effect or clinical claim.

The newly checked2026 1,387-compound/20-line sarcoma screen includes HEMCSS,
CVCL_1238: the same H-EMC-SS resource with unresolved EMC authentication, not a new
independent donor (DOI10.1158/2767-9764.CRC-26-0142). INBRX-109's published binding,
viability and caspase effects also use this resource; its two treated PDXs are
explicitly conventional chondrosarcoma (DOI10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-23-0974).
Those positive measurements are preserved without relabelling the line or
claiming an authentic EMC dependency. The clinical source's one EMC-labelled case
has a diagnostic footnote and lacks a fusion/outcome bridge; the clinical owner
has evaluated that observation separately.

Fresh broader studies do not yet supply a qualified comparison. The11-patient
bone-sarcoma source explicitly studies osteosarcoma/Ewing disease. The new HDAC
study specifies UPS/LMS models. CancerModels.org S-CMO9316 is a generic metastatic
chondrosarcoma organoid listing; its cited original study evaluates conventional/
dedifferentiated bone chondrosarcoma, and no exact model-to-patient EMC crosswalk
was found. Other new organoid/cohort sources retain unresolved individual
histologies or supplement access. None is counted as a negative EMC measurement.

Validity and value are separate: the source-identification checks are reproducible,
but they establish no new disease biology. Previously evaluated USZ20/22/23 and
NCC drug measurements, including favorable and unfavorable ATR conditions, remain
prior art. They were not rerun to generate a new paper. The standalone proposal
is shelved, with no global absence/exhaustion claim and no manuscript.

Reopen only when a specific authenticated EMC model/case-to-condition crosswalk
provides actual controlled response measurements for a consequential unanswered
comparison, with every relevant public EMC condition evaluated and an independent
challenge. Uncertain labels, more technical wells or a narrower title do not meet
that condition. No credible distinct next question is supported by this lane now.
