Actual evaluation of original published measurements

Brenca2019, DOI 10.1002/path.5284: all 12 patient rows evaluated for partner and specimen eligibility; seven EWSR1 and five TAF15. Five additional matched frozen conditions (four EWSR1, one TAF15) are reported without individual identity or displayed values. Two human-tumor TAF15 variants are explicitly prior art; engineered T-N/T-N* comparisons are not new donors or endogenous isoform abundance. The main text does not provide all patient-specific RNA acceptors or native/fusion ratios. Retained original XML reused by hash, not newly downloaded after the public PMC challenge.

Filion2009, DOI 10.1002/path.2445: all three expression-array samples and all three displayed Western samples considered; one shared sample is explicitly reported. Additional four PPARG Western tumors are reported but do not supply a native/fusion RNA balance endpoint and cannot be counted as isoform validation. The native ΔC observation is protein-level, and an additional lower fusion-protein band has a degradation alternative. Three RT reactions in the separate SGK1 experiment are technical replication, not new EMC donors; that is an already published different-gene endpoint and was not substituted for the fixed NR4A3 question.

Racanelli2020, DOI 10.3389/fonc.2020.00489: all 14 EMC records evaluated, exact IDs in acc-emc-sample-audit.json. All stated assay conditions retained, including case7 cross-panel discordance, case12 partly untested panel, case100 fusion-not-detected despite NR4A3 FISH. Neither diagnostic panel-negative nor no reported extra fusion implies zero expression. No donor independence from Brenca assumed: authors/institutions overlap and an individual crosswalk is lacking. RNA quality filters and occasional retained lower-QC cases are preserved in the extracted methods.

Fusion-microarray2013, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0070649: full 16-record table checked; one EMC, sample168/97, EWSR1–NR4A3. Published chimeric boundary and intragenic expression shift are already reported. Its custom junction/intragenic-array design does not directly quantify complete transcript structures or native/fusion molecule fractions. Potential earlier reuse of this source specimen is not resolved or counted as another donor. No generic EWSR1 sarcoma is treated as EMC.
