Question: Do direct RNA measurements in authenticated EMC tumors establish recurrent fusion-partner-related NR4A3 transcript/isoform biology beyond the original authors' observations? Candidate interpretation at this source gate: partner-associated inclusion of an alternative NR4A3 acceptor/cryptic exon, or a measured endogenous native-versus-fusion isoform balance, may alter which transcript must be measured to understand EMC. These are alternatives to assess for prior art and measurement feasibility, not permission for a gene-wide fishing exercise. A source-specific single contrast must be fixed before numerical values if either qualifies.

Known prior art already defeats any simple novelty claim about the existence of alternative transcripts. Brenca2019 explicitly describes canonical and intron-2 cryptic-exon TAF15::NR4A3 constructs; Filion2009 reports abundant native NR4A3 lacking its C terminus and proposed opposition to fusion-associated PPARG activation. Existing ASO work already audited junction annotation, exon conventions and deposited sequences. Repackaging these observations, correcting our designs or finding an annotated isoform is not new disease knowledge. R6 regulatory work found no endogenous perturbation bridge and remains shelved.

The first gate inspects original studies, published specimen-level measurements, supplements and processed repositories, with complete EMC donor/specimen/condition and fusion-partner mapping. A junction name alone establishes neither relative isoform abundance nor complete transcript structure. Microarrays and gene-level bulk counts may be unsuitable for distinguishing native from fused NR4A3; 3-prime assays and targeted panels may miss alternative acceptors by design. All eligible reported isoforms within a donor and all repeated/treated samples must be retained; tissue/culture and independent donor distinctions must not be inferred from generic EMC labels.

Value criterion: a new reproducible endogenous difference must change a credible mechanism or assay a researcher would test, beyond confirmation of a published fusion or a method demonstration. Controls must address coverage/capture bias, RNA integrity, sample purity, read multimapping, annotation version, partner and center/batch confounding. Main alternatives are assay ascertainment and mixtures of native and fusion transcripts. No inference from RNA to DNA topology, protein translation, dependency or efficacy. No engineering or treatment-design work.

Defeat/stop: original authors already established the contrast; no authenticated independent partner groups with appropriate junction resolution; processed measurements cannot distinguish alternatives; or a positive computation would still only reproduce prior work.
