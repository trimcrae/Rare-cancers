The original hypothesis in PLAN.txt is unchanged. No new biological numerical pilot has been run. Source-level counts below audit eligibility and assay conditions; they are not estimates of population prevalence, isoform abundance or clinical sensitivity.

Decision: shelve the standalone fusion-transcript contribution at this bounded source/value gate. The inspected evidence does not currently support a new recurrent partner-dependent NR4A3 isoform phenotype. This is not a negative biological result and does not prove that suitable data do not exist.

The obvious presence claims are already prior art. Brenca et al. explicitly state that both canonical and cryptic-exon TAF15::NR4A3 isoforms were detected in human tumors, then compare engineered variants. Filion et al. identify native NR4A3 and NR4A3ΔC by Western blot and propose an effect on fusion activity; that is not a released native-versus-fusion RNA abundance matrix. The published observation must not be recast as a new discovery or incorrectly called direct quantitative RNA evidence.

All 14 have been extracted. The source reports only one TAF15 case, and its target-dependent fusion detection cannot resolve relative abundance or prove an unreported alternative transcript is absent. The three algorithm supplements could refine junction identity, but ordinary linked requests encountered a browser challenge; no workaround was attempted. Raw sequencing has a declared public deposit, but the bounded ENA metadata request failed with HTTP 500. These are pending/access gaps, not unsuitable biology or evidence of absence.

Brenca's five matched frozen counterparts (four EWSR1, one TAF15) are additional reported conditions, not five new donors. They are mentioned as data not shown; no public sample-level crosswalk is established here. The old 23-record deposit cannot be relabeled as 23 tumors: verified prior audit separates 12 patient tumors and 11 engineered libraries, with only eight engineered aliases resolved and 15 aliases still individually unmapped.

No large raw-data request is justified solely to regenerate known variants. Reopening requires an authenticated donor/partner/condition map and a directly measured junction-resolved contrast beyond the original studies, with adequate independent partner groups and assay controls. Suitable accessible pending evidence must then be evaluated completely before promotion. Independent review may identify an omission capable of changing this gate; it does not license inventing a replacement hypothesis.
