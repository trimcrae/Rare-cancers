RESULTS — R7 endogenous EMC fusion transcripts, 4 October 2026

No surviving new disease finding was demonstrated. Decision: SHELVE the standalone contribution. This was a source and scientific-value gate; no expression effect, isoform frequency, p value or tumor-response conclusion was computed.

What was evaluated

1. Brenca et al., J Pathol 2019, DOI 10.1002/path.5284, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6766969/ . The previously retained original XML was reread and hashed. All 12 tumor records in Table 1 are retained: cases 1–7 EWSR1 and 8–12 TAF15. Five matched frozen counterparts are additionally reported (four EWSR1, one TAF15), with results not shown and no individual crosswalk here. Engineered fibroblasts are a separate assay population. The authors already report two TAF15 isoforms found in human tumors: the canonical acceptor and an intron-2 cryptic exon encoding 25 additional amino acids before the NR4A3 start. Their engineered colony-formation comparison did not distinguish these variants under the tested conditions. The inspected article and verified selected-gene supplements do not release a complete donor-by-isoform abundance matrix.

2. Filion et al., J Pathol 2009, DOI 10.1002/path.2445, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4429309/ . Verified retained original material distinguishes three EWSR1::NR4A3-positive expression-array tumors from three Western tumors, with only one shared. Two Western tumors have type-2 fusion mRNA by RT-PCR; the first is described as cytogenetically positive. Native NR4A3ΔC abundance is a Western observation; a lower fusion band also has degradation as an explicitly stated alternative to splicing. The proposed native/fusion balance and promoter effects are already published, mostly through protein and engineered reporter experiments. The paper does not supply a partner-comparative native/fusion RNA balance dataset.

3. Racanelli et al., Front Oncol 2020, DOI 10.3389/fonc.2020.00489, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7175964/ . The complete original tables contain 14 EMC records: 7, 12, 55–65 and 100. Twelve have reported EWSR1::NR4A3, one TAF15::NR4A3 and one NR4A3-FISH-positive record with no histotype-specific fusion detected. Cases 7 and 12 have multiple assay conditions; all are preserved in acc-emc-sample-audit.json. This is an assay-validation cohort, not an unselected isoform denominator. Panel coverage, caller sensitivity, RNA quality and tumor fraction are alternatives to biological absence. The sole TAF15 record cannot establish a recurrent partner-dependent state; overlap with Brenca is unresolved. Three processed supplements remain inaccessible in the ordinary linked route and the raw-deposit metadata request failed, so no complete-junction claim is advanced.

4. Assessment of Fusion Gene Status in Sarcomas Using a Custom Made Fusion Gene Microarray, PLoS One 2013, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0070649, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3742753/ . All 16 source records were checked for eligibility; one is EMC, Swedish sample 168/97. The source already reports its EWSR1–NR4A3 boundary as 13–3 in that study's exon convention. Chimeric and intragenic array signals establish the authors' diagnostic result, not a newly measured native/fusion abundance ratio. It provides no independent partner group; its citation to earlier material leaves potential cross-publication reuse unresolved. No image digitization was performed.

5. Prior repository analyses were checked for measurement scope rather than inherited conclusions. The existing junction atlas and transcript-sensitivity calculations enumerate annotation-compatible junctions; they explicitly are not patient recurrence data. Sequence deposits, engineered patent sequences and repeated technical libraries cannot substitute for independent endogenous measurements. Brenca's raw PRJNA692081 audit and the earlier regulatory/Filion evaluations were reused by exact file hashes. No previous design repair is presented as disease knowledge.

Reproducible work

source_gate.py fetched and parsed the ACC original XML and extracted all EMC table rows and assay explanations. evaluate_sources.py asserts the exact 14-row identifier set and records original Brenca rows and all reported specimen conditions; it binds prior inputs by SHA256. additional_sources.py fetched the fusion-microarray original XML and attempted a bounded public ENA metadata query. Its HTTP 500 is preserved. One initial run of source_gate.py failed because requests was unavailable; it was changed to Python's installed standard-library urllib and rerun successfully, with no installation. No BAM, FASTQ, new runtime, plot, image digitization or numerical biological pilot was used.

Strongest limitation and reopening

The source gate is incomplete at the underlying junction-data level. Brenca raw libraries, unresolved donor aliases and paired tissue preparations, ACC processed calls/raw metadata, and broader tissue-RNA sources remain capable of changing feasibility. Reopen only with a worthwhile contrast that survives prior art, an authenticated all-specimen map, and sufficient direct RNA resolution to separate native and fusion transcripts and distinguish alternative acceptors from capture/coverage artifacts. No statement that EMC lacks an isoform or partner effect follows from this packet.
