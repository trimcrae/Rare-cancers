R7 clinical linkage — 2026-10-04
Decision: SHELVE the standalone fusion-by-treatment comparison. No new EMC finding demonstrated.

The prior-art gate already defeats a generic fusion-prognosis paper: Huang2023 studied clinical confounding, Paioli2020/2021 studied fusion-specific relapse outcomes, and Suemitsu2025 reported secondary alterations and outcomes. The fixed remaining question was whether EWSR1::NR4A3 versus TAF15::NR4A3 distinguishes antiangiogenic activity from cytotoxic activity rather than general prognosis. The available individual sources do not establish that contrast or a novel result. This is a source-level linkage evaluation, not a negative biological discovery.

Actual evaluation and preserved observations
1. Anthracycline2013 (PMC3879193): all11 published clinical rows were parsed. All are NR4A3 rearranged; table outcomes are4PR,3SD,3PD,1not evaluable. Three surgery cases are censored at resection, not progression. EWS9/11 is an aggregate in the narrative; the retained primary XML Table3 contains IHC columns only despite being cited for FISH. We did not assign the two EWS-negative cases to TAF15 or map individual EWS status from demographic similarities. The publisher PDF/table rendering has not been separately authenticated, so the precise gap is missing case mapping in the evaluated machine-readable source. The narrative also ambiguously mentions responses in4 combination-treated plus1 single-agent patient despite the4/10PR table; no regimen-specific response estimate was made.
2. Sunitinib2012 (PMC3534218): both molecularly supported EWSR1/CHN cases and their prior chemotherapy, dose conditions, interruption/rechallenge, imaging and infection were evaluated. Case1 progressed on trabectedin and later responded to sunitinib, including after reintroduction; case2 stabilized then had dimensional response after dose escalation. The original authors already reported this. Neither is a TAF15 comparator and the earlier chemotherapy responses are not a standardized paired matrix. The2014 ten-case report describes strengthening the initial2 observations; this suggests an extension, but no individual crosswalk was obtained. Case1 begins June2011 in the2012 source versus the2014 abstract's fromJuly2011. We therefore retain suspected overlap and do not count12 independent patients. A possible overlap with the Italian anthracycline series remains unresolved.
3. Temozolomide2017 (PMC5687737; DOI10.1371/journal.pone.0188116): all eight analyzed sarcoma rows and every EMC row were inspected, including molecular Table4. There is one EWSR1::NR4A3 case (patient2), with source-reported pazopanib stable disease and19months treatment, then progression and2months treatment after temozolomide addition. Prior sunitinib, radiation and nivolumab are named without individual response details. The protocol selected patients after progression on pazopanib and changed its dose when adding temozolomide; the comparison cannot isolate the cytotoxic effect. This published unfavorable result was retained, not omitted to make EWSR1 uniformly drug-sensitive. No response odds, growth-modulation index or PFS ratio was calculated.
4. Davis2017 (PMC5400622) reused original XML and DOCX: all6 molecularly supported EWSR1::NR4A3 patients were evaluated, including poor-quality biopsy RNA subsequently supported in archived primary tissue. MO-1582 has sequential pazopanib/doxorubicin, but Table1 says pazopanib2months while the original supplement dates initiation10/2014 and cessation12/2015. Both report later doxorubicin progression; we retain the discrepancy without choosing an apparent correction or converting it into a new outcome. MO-1381's long cyclophosphamide/rapamycin treatment and eventual progression are also preserved, with intervening RT and no causal efficacy interpretation. These are already published and supply no alternate-fusion comparator.
5. Chiusole2020 (PMC7308468) reused actual XML: systemic-treatment summaries and all tables do not release the individual crosswalk from the23 molecularly tested, all-EWSR1 cases to therapy lines/outcomes. Aggregate line1 and line2 outcomes were not paired into invented patients.
6. INBRX2023 (PMC10425732), independent worker's original XML: one investigator-labelled extraskeletal myxoid case in Table1 has a footnote describing chondrosarcoma/chondrosarcoma-like features. No NR4A3/partner or individual outcome mapping was authenticated here. It remains an identity gap, not a proved non-EMC case and not molecularly linked EMC treatment evidence. The preclinical model is owned by the other lane and is not used here.

Scientific value and limitations
The question would be useful if it could separate partner-specific treatment sensitivity from baseline prognosis; no eligible comparative patient mapping was established in this bounded pass. Adding isolated published cases to already known single-arm associations would not provide that distinction. No pooled donor count, survival curve, statistical test, treatment recommendation, or new manuscript is justified. Accessible unresolved mappings and incompletely obtained studies remain blockers to advancement; this packet does not claim all public evidence has been exhausted.

Reopening requires a released, authentic case-level fusion-to-treatment/outcome crosswalk with meaningful treatment conditions and a credible comparison beyond the original conclusions, plus donor-overlap resolution and full relevant public-EMC coverage. A new citation, another isolated published response, or narrower title alone is insufficient. Independent review is recorded separately when completed; original PLAN is unchanged.
