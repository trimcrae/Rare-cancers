The useful gap is authoritative CHRNA6 probe coverage and two-colour reference compatibility.
The unchanged cache omits CHRNA6 from its selected gene rows, which cannot establish absent
platform coverage. Its 16 cached annotations contain CRH/CRH-mRNA descriptors for ten EMC and
three DFSP records, and UHR descriptors for three GIST records. Different strings alone do not
prove different biological pools; matching labels alone do not prove identical pools. A ratio
contrast requires compatible reference and processing evidence before cancellation can be assumed.
