## Why Nature Communications Matters Arising is not the first choice

Current guidance seeks timely comments about its own original research,
ideally grounded in knowledge contemporaneous with the paper. Main text should
ideally be <=1,200 words, with one or two small figures/tables and about 15 references;
the opening summary paragraph serves as the submission abstract. Contributions
and competing-interest declarations are required. It encourages prior author
contact and inclusion of the correspondence. Pure error notices may become
clarifications rather than Matters Arising. There is no APC for this format.
https://www.nature.com/ncomms/submit/matters-arising

No hard numeric age cutoff is stated on this Nature Communications page.
Do not import Nature's separate 18-month rule. Nevertheless, the observed
transformation first appears in inspected 2024 curation, two years after the 2022
article. Current evidence establishes an export defect, not an error in its
published scientific conclusions. That makes editorial scope/timeliness
uncertain even though the short draft fits the format. A later editor-directed
clarification may be appropriate if authors/maintainers identify a connection;
neither that connection nor an invitation currently exists.
