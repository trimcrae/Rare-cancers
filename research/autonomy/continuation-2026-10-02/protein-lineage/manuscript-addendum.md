Target: the results section after the original-versus-additionally-quantified comparison in `drafts/sarcoma-antigen-protein-transfer-short-report.md` at baseline `da49c4e836533253825587f83656675dac4c913b`. The original manuscript and submitted CSPG4 package remain frozen. This is a new post hoc analysis, not a preplanned primary endpoint.

In a post hoc descriptive follow-up restricted to CSPG4 and L1CAM, we separated pooled rank covariance into within-histology and between-histology components using the same published measurements. For additionally quantified CSPG4, pooled rho −0.304 decomposed into −0.093 within and −0.212 between histologies; 12 of 18 paired families were Ewing sarcoma. For L1CAM, pooled rho −0.483 decomposed into −0.396 within and −0.087 between histologies. These arithmetic components use ranks from each complete support subset and are not within-histology Spearman coefficients or causal effects. For L1CAM, osteosarcoma and rhabdomyosarcoma strata with only two families each together contributed −0.290 of the −0.396 within component, underscoring sparse support. Only four gene–histology comparisons supported correlations in both subsets under the inherited minimum-three-pair rule. CSPG4 osteosarcoma (n=8 versus 3; rho 0.571 versus −1.000) and L1CAM Ewing sarcoma (n=12 versus 6; rho 0.566 versus −0.600) showed observed sign reversals; the other two did not. Most comparisons remained too sparse. These results prevent attributing both pooled negative associations solely to between-histology composition, while leaving quantification selection, measurement error and sampling variability unresolved.

Methods addition: preserve all 61 frozen primary families and published accession mappings. Form original/added support from original-protein availability, then require paired RNA and expanded-matrix protein for each correlation. Retain all 24 gene-by-histology-by-support cells, with undefined correlations for fewer than three pairs or constant measurements. Decompose the Pearson covariance of globally average-tied ranks using observed family/stratum weights; report the two signed components with a common global-rank variance denominator. No additional fitting, imputation, P values or confidence intervals were introduced. Results and the prespecified follow-up plan are in this folder.

This analysis used published experiments but did not validate an EMC-specific antigen or amend the accepted Cancer Genetics submission. Publication readiness, novelty assessment and editorial format remain separate from this executed diagnostic.
