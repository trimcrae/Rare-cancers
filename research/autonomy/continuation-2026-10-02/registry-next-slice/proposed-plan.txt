Smallest proposed methodological extension
Keep condition cancer, ResultsFirstPostDate September 1 through September 30 inclusive, at most ten evaluation trials and the same outcome inclusion rules. Add only bounded metadata pagination and a local NCT tie-break after completing the cutoff-date group. This is a newly selected slice under an explicit new order, never a retrospective claim to complete the old order.

1. Freeze the exclusion ledger before any new source acquisition. Include all 138 baselineTrialIds from the pinned acquisition receipt plus all ten trials in its selected list, regardless of whether their old outcomes were eligible. This union is 148 distinct trials. The ten are NCT04008706, NCT01520701, NCT05508867, NCT05327530, NCT04209686, NCT04557956, NCT04570956, NCT04276493, NCT02657486, NCT05700903. 2. Future metadata query: /api/v2/studies with query.cond=cancer; query.term=AREA[ResultsFirstPostDate]RANGE[2026-09-01,2026-09-30]; pageSize=10; sort=ResultsFirstPostDate:asc; format=json; fields=IdentificationModule,StatusModule,ConditionsModule. No OutcomeMeasuresModule or other outcome fields. At most 20 metadata pages, 200 returned study records, 64 KiB per page and 1 MiB aggregate. Follow only the immediately preceding response's nextPageToken with identical other parameters. No retries, alternate query, date expansion, label search or client request for an unsupported NCT sort. Freeze URL, token chain, exact raw bytes, headers, UTC times and hashes page by page.
3. Validate every record has a unique well-formed NCT ID and a valid first-results date within the window. Reject duplicates, inconsistent metadata, missing date, nonmonotone dates, access failure or byte/request-cap breach. Preserve failures; do not drop questionable records to reach ten.
4. After each page, form candidates not in the frozen ledger, ordered by (ISO ResultsFirstPostDate ascending, NCT ID ASCII ascending). Once ten exist, let D be the tenth candidate's date. Continue metadata-only pages until a returned date is strictly greater than D, or pagination ends, thereby completing the observed cutoff-date tie. Then select exactly the first ten candidates in that canonical order. If pagination ends earlier, select all remaining candidates (zero through nine); no replacement or expansion. If the finite budget ends before cutoff-tie completion or exhaustion, STOP with no outcome fetch and no cohort claim.
5. Raw metadata pages define a frozen acquisition-time frame, not an atomic or immutable API database. API insertion/update drift during pagination cannot be ruled out by local checks; report this limitation. Require source bytes and dates to match when acquiring each selected record; observable drift stops, not re-selection. If strict live-population completeness is required, the exact blocker is lack of a server snapshot/version guarantee; the smallest defensible alternative is this archived, checked metadata-frame slice with that limited claim, not a supposedly stable global first-ten population.
6. Freeze and commit candidate/exclusion decisions, ordered selected NCT IDs, dates, cutoff/tie evidence and metadata hashes before any selected outcome payload is downloaded or seen. Outcome order is study canonical order then original zero-based outcome index. No relevance-based trial replacement.

Stop on any access, identity, changed selection metadata or size failure; preserve partial evidence and do not evaluate an opportunistic successful subset. No external outcome searches or additional modules. Empty outcomes are valid retained results.
