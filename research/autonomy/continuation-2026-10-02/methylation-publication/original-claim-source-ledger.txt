## Central claim and intended format

The evidence supports a short empirical diagnostic-methods report: in this fixed public-data reanalysis, matching training support reduces the supplier-validation loss gap, and the apparent benefit of reference-fitted score sharpening depends on loss definition and case weighting. The report combines those completed results around the question of which comparisons sustain a robustness conclusion.

This is not a biological discovery. It establishes no new subtype, methylation mechanism, treatment association, EMC diagnostic sensitivity, or clinical calibration. The mathematical behavior of temperature scaling is already known; the empirical contribution is the measured interaction of training support, score coverage, weighting, and recorded exceptions in these profiles. A first-in-literature claim is not made. Whether the combined contribution merits a standalone report remains a manuscript-review decision; the provenance audit alone would be an appendix, not an independent discovery paper.
