# Venue and format decision

Recommend **Virchows Archiv, Original Article, subscription route**. This format preserves the methods and qualifications; modest, dataset-specific novelty remains an editorial risk. The official scope includes evaluation of diagnostic criteria and pathology methods. Subscription publication has no APC; open access is optional. No mandatory page charge was identified.

The checked instructions request approximately 3500 main words, a 150–250-word abstract, 4–6 keywords, ordinarily no more than 50 references and 5 combined figures/tables, editable text, numbered citations and DOI links. This package has two main tables and seven references. It uses an editable DOCX, a PDF supplement and a ZIP for code/data. Author metadata, contributions, applicable ethics/consent wording and final human approval remain pending. AI use appears in Methods. No submission has occurred.

Official sources checked 2026-10-02: [scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/428/aims-and-scope), [instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/428/submission-guidelines), [publishing and charges](https://link.springer.com/journal/428/how-to-publish-with-us).

Histopathology offers a shorter original-article alternative but requires a graphical abstract. Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing expressly excludes routine applications to public datasets without substantial innovation, making it a poor fit. Sources: [Histopathology instructions](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/13652559/homepage/forauthors.html), [MBEC instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/11517/submission-guidelines).
