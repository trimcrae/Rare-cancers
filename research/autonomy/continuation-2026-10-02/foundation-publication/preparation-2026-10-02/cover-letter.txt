Please consider “A row-ordinal sample-ID transformation in a public sarcoma rearrangement export” as Correspondence under the subscription publication route.

This report documents a fixed-file data-quality correction in the cBioPortal Datahub export associated with Gounder et al. (2022), Nature Communications 13:3406. Comparing 3,771 rearrangement records with the published workbook showed 3,770 discordant source identifiers. A separate extraction of the primary workbook confirmed the complete source mapping. A defined join using 75 source EMC identifiers retrieved 28 events from other source records and missed all 76 source EMC events.

The contribution is correction of identifiers in one pinned derivative export. Recovery retains the event-order assumption and preserves event multiplicities and all non-ID export fields. The report separates fifteen gene-text discrepancies from sample identity, including three unresolved cases. It makes no claim of assay failure, new EMC biology, patient harm, or an error in every live portal. No maintainer-approved correction or corrected deployment has been established.
