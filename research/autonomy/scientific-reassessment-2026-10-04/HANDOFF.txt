Required behavior
Before interpreting relevant results, record the proposed contribution, primary
prior art, research consequence, strongest alternative and defeating outcomes.
Before drafting, perform feasible interpretation-changing follow-up, with full
accounting of small relevant model/cohort corpora and appropriate comparisons.
On material contrary evidence, stop advancing the manuscript and independently
decide: continue toward a paper, one bounded decisive follow-up, or shelve.
Scientific validity and usefulness are separate decisions. Narrowing a claim
or adding caveats does not exempt it from the usefulness requirement. Preserve
negatives and inaccessible data distinctly. No universal model-positivity or
wet-lab requirement was introduced. Keep follow-up and reviews finite.
