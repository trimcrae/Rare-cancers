## 1 · The residue-identity resolution

**The confusion is not "one residue named two ways."** NR4A1 (P22736) and NR4A2 (P43354) are both 598 aa and are
co-numbered through the LBD, so `C505` and `C534` each name a **real, distinct site present in both paralogues**.
They differ in the only way that matters:

| site | in NR4A1 | in NR4A2 | aligns to NR4A3 | NR4A3 has a Cys there? | class |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|---|
| **C505** | ✓ | ✓ | **C536** | **YES** | conserved across all three |
| **C534** | ✓ | ✓ | **S565** | **no** | paralogue-unique (both) |
| C551 | ✓ | — | T579 | no | NR4A1-only (the celastrol site) |
| C465 | ✓ | ✓ | C496 | YES | conserved |
| C475 | ✓ | ✓ | C506 | YES | conserved |
| C566 | ✓ | ✓ | C594 | YES | conserved |

Sequence contexts (`NRIAS[C]LKEHV` for NR4A1 C534, `NKIVN[C]LKDHV` for NR4A2 C534, `PQPAS[C]LSRLL` for
NR4A1 C551) are in the JSON, from `nr4a-paralogue-unique-residues.json → reciprocal_paralogue_unique`.

**Who actually closes C397's chemoselectivity window**, over the 30 graded (`term_a_exemplar`) cells per
convention that the artifact's `verdict` block is computed on:

| convention | NR4A1 C505 (conserved position) | NR4A2 C534 (paralogue-unique) |
|---|:--:|:--:|
| **through-space** | **24 / 30** | 6 / 30 |
| **corridor** | 7 / 30 | **23 / 30** |

⛔ **So the roadmap's gloss — "a paralogue cysteine, *i.e. a cysteine the paralogues have and NR4A3 lacks*" —
is FALSE for the majority through-space closer.** The artifact's `n_closed_by_a_PARALOGUE_cysteine: 30` tests
only `not closed_by.startswith("NR4A3")`, i.e. "on a paralogue chain". That is a weaker and different statement
from the reciprocal-uniqueness one, and merging them converts a **conserved-position** closure into a uniqueness
argument.

⚠ **And `closed_by` is a tie-break, not a measurement, in 35 of the 93 rows that have a closer** (38 %): two or
more cysteines arrive at the same backbone-atom count. The honest form names the set that arrives first.

**The correct sentence** — under both conventions the first cysteine into reach is always one on a *paralogue
chain* rather than on NR4A3 (30/30 graded cells each); *which* one, and whether NR4A3 lacks that site, differs
by convention, and only the corridor majority (NR4A2 C534 → NR4A3 S565) carries the reciprocal-uniqueness
reading.

★ **The deeper finding.** C505 and NR4A3 C536 are an **aligned pair**, so the gap between them in these models
is not a sequence difference — it is a **rotamer** difference between independently built models. The artifact's
own `aligned_pair_displacement` puts that pair at ΔSG 4.06 Å / ΔCA 1.19 Å (ratio 3.4) for NR4A1 and ΔSG 3.34 Å /
ΔCA 0.35 Å (**ratio 9.5 — the largest in the whole table**) for NR4A2. The through-space closure therefore rests
on precisely the pair the artifact flags as least trustworthy.

| **b** | C420 and C559 refuted at every placement, pendant and convention | ⚠ **CORRECTED** — true of C420, **not** of C559 | `verdict.per_unique_cysteine_conformer_counts.C559.best_through_space = 2` |

| **d** | window closed by a paralogue cysteine rather than an NR4A3-conserved one | ⚠ **CORRECTED** — first half supported, gloss false for the majority through-space closer | `★_family_wide_chemoselectivity_window.by_convention[*].closed_by` × `paralogue_control.reciprocal_uniqueness` |
| **e** | per-pair ΔCA vs ΔSG + the sulfur displacement that would reopen the window | ⚠ **SUPPORTED as to existence, INCOMPLETE as to coverage** | `paralogue_control.aligned_pair_displacement` (8 pairs, all conserved positions) + `verdict.family_wide_window[*].noise_sensitivity` |

**(b), precisely.** C420 is refuted everywhere — 0 of 60 (placement × pendant) cells, both conventions, no
conformer. **C559 is refuted under the corridor convention everywhere and at 59 of 60 through-space cells**,
surviving in exactly one: `vhl|M3@term_a_exemplar | dab_branch`, in **2 of that cell's 19 conformers**. That is
the *optimistic best-of-N* anchor with the *longest* pendant, so it is the weakest possible form of a survival —
and it is still not zero. **Mechanism of the error:** `verdict()` builds `refuted_unique_cysteines` from
`best_corridor > 0` alone, so C559's through-space evidence never reaches the label even though it is recorded
two fields away. The data is honest; the label is stronger than the data.

**(e), precisely.** The reopening displacement is **6.25 Å** (derived: median 5.0 atoms of lost window ×
1.25 Å/atom) against a largest-observed aligned-pair displacement of **5.94 Å** — it clears by **0.31 Å, a 5 %
margin**. And the yardstick is built only from **aligned cysteine pairs**, so **C534 — which closes 23 of 30
corridor cells — carries no measured bound at all**, because having no aligned NR4A3 partner is exactly what
makes it paralogue-unique. The one residue the noise test most needs to cover is the one it is structurally
unable to cover.

## 3 · What is pose-dependent and what is not

**Three layers, three different exposures.** This separation had not been drawn, and it is the difference
between a claim that survives `R5` failing and one that does not.

### Layer 1 — pose-INDEPENDENT (sequence only) · survives `R5` failing outright
C397/C420/C559 are cysteines in NR4A3 and not at the aligned position in either paralogue; C534 and C551 are
cysteines the paralogues have and NR4A3 lacks; **C505 is not** (it aligns to NR4A3 C536). UniProt FASTA + two
independent global aligners, every position `alignment_robust`. **On its own this layer says nothing about
whether anything can reach those residues** — it is much weaker than the axis has been credited with.

### Layer 2 — POCKET-dependent, but **not** docked-pose-dependent
Every reach number in both artifacts: the 12-atom gate fractions, the family-wide window, the matched-construct
collision probabilities.

⛔ **This corrects the roadmap.** The map says branch 1b is *"conditional on the docked pose the anchors come
from, whose known-answer test is `V3`"*. Read from the source, **the anchors are not a docked pose.**
`nr4a3_basin_search.build_pose_ensemble` samples 12 solvent-connected anchors in a shell around the
**cryptic-pocket centroid**, and says so explicitly: *"The repo holds no cmpd19 pose in this matched-model frame
… so asserting one exit-vector point would manufacture precision the evidence does not support."*
`nr4a3-orientation-basins.json` `_limits[0]` agrees: the exit vector is *"MARGINALISED over an ensemble of
pocket-mouth anchors rather than asserted."* Both audited artifacts read from that ensemble
(`exitvec_00`…`exitvec_11`).

**What this actually inherits from `V3`:** `V3`'s failure was **site selection** on 6 of 6 pairs — and now
**0 of 14 in regime by two independent transfer routes**, against **11 of 14** for an fpocket-chosen box —
not pose accuracy (an fpocket-chosen box lands in `C14`'s PARTIAL band; the RMSD digit is deliberately not
restated, because that arm is an unseeded Monte-Carlo search whose value has been committed six different
ways and `C6` measured the **band** to be what is stable). Site selection is exactly
what these anchors rest on. So — **a pose-accuracy failure is already absorbed by the marginalisation; a
site-selection failure voids every reach number here.** The exposure runs through the **pocket**, not through a
pose, and saying "docked pose" both overstates it in one direction and hides the real dependency.

### Layer 3 — EXPOSURE-CRITERION-dependent (`EXPOSED_RSA = 0.25`, instrument `V17`)

**How much load the cutoff actually carries** (percentage points of collision probability it removes):

| linker atoms | static | unbiased release | metad (biased) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **12 (the gate)** | **0.00 pp** | **0.12 pp** | **0.29 pp** |
| 14 | 0.89 pp | 3.17 pp | 2.56 pp |
| 16 | 5.44 pp | 13.33 pp | 12.39 pp |
| 20 | 26.33 pp | 38.25 pp | 37.28 pp |

★ **This is the most useful line in the audit.** **At the 12-atom design gate the exposure filter carries almost
nothing** — reach-only collision is already 0.000 / 0.00124 / 0.00290 — so the headline gate result **does not
rest on the criterion that failed its positive control.** The filter becomes load-bearing at 16 atoms and
dominant at 20. The claim is defensible at the gate on **reach alone**, and indefensible at 16–20 without the
cutoff.

### The envelope the headline does not show

Pooled over the 75 unbiased frames per species, "could **some** construct reach this cysteine at ≤ 12 atoms":

| species | cysteine | frames inside the envelope | RSA max | clears 0.25? |
|---|---|---:|---:|:--:|
| **NR4A1** | **C465** | **68 / 75 (0.907)** | 0.2126 | **no** |
| NR4A3 | C397 | 65 / 75 (0.867) | 0.6725 | **yes** |
| NR4A2 | C465 | 25 / 75 (0.333) | 0.1226 | no |
| NR4A2 | C534 | 16 / 75 (0.213) | 0.194 | no |
| NR4A3 | C496 | 12 / 75 (0.160) | 0.0552 | no |
| NR4A1 | C551 | 3 / 75 (0.040) | 0.1333 | no |

⛔ **The paralogues are not out of range.** NR4A1 C465 sits inside the 12-atom envelope in **more frames than
NR4A3's own C397 does**, and is excluded solely by an RSA that never exceeds **0.2126 — 15 % below the cutoff**.
The categorical result is *"the same construct geometry rarely reaches both"*, **not** *"the paralogues have
nothing in range."* (These are different questions: the envelope is E3-independent — *could some construct reach
it* — while the collision probability asks whether the **same** placement reaches both.)

## 4 · The limits, measured

| id | limit |
|---|---|
| **L1 ★** | **The exposure criterion has a demonstrated false negative on the one NR4A-family covalent site with literature support — and that site is on NR4A1, one of the two paralogues reported as carrying zero exposed reachable cysteines.** NR4A1 Cys551: RSA **0.165** on the state-matched opened model, median **0.064** / max **0.223** over 25 metadynamics frames, median 0.029 / max 0.133 over 75 unbiased frames. So the same criterion that returns "zero exposed NR4A1 cysteines" would also return "the celastrol site is not exposed". **The defensible form is the threshold-free rank — C551 is 3/18 across all NR4A-family LBD cysteines, behind NR4A3's C397 and C420 — not the cutoff.** ⚠ This does **not** undermine the 12-atom gate reading (Layer 3 above); it undermines the 16- and 20-atom columns, and any sentence of the form "no exposed paralogue cysteine" that does not name its criterion. |

## 5 · What the axis licenses — and what it does not

**Licenses:**
- **A design prioritisation: prefer a short linker.** Discrimination is clean at the 12-atom gate and degrades
  monotonically with length, so a construct reaching C397 at 11–12 backbone atoms is not merely more tractable,
  it is *more selective*. This is the axis's most useful output.
- **A refutation**, which is what geometry is actually good for: C420 and C559 are not usable electrophile
  targets at chemically routine linker length (C420 absolutely; C559 with the one recorded exception).
- **A narrowed statement of target-engagement selectivity**, with its criterion named.

**⛔ Does NOT license:**
- **not degradation** — no ubiquitin transfer, processivity or turnover is modelled;
- **not affinity** — no binding free energy, potency or residence time; the axis is set membership and distance,
  not energy;
- **not efficacy, not safety, not a therapeutic window**;
- **not proteome-wide selectivity** — the comparison set is NR4A1 and NR4A2, and an electrophile does not know
  it is meant to be selective;
- **not that a covalent bond forms at all**;
- ⛔ **not a productive ternary complex.** A PROTAC needs a ternary geometry presenting a substrate lysine to
  the E2~Ub transfer zone, and this axis says **nothing** about that. Separate term, separate evidence; this
  neither supports nor substitutes for it;
- **not "the paralogues lack a reachable cysteine."** They have several. They lack an *exposed* one under this
  cutoff. Only the second sentence is measured.

**One-sentence form.** *At the 12-atom design gate, over 73,867 matched E3 placements and 300 matched
conformers, no placement that reaches an NR4A3-unique cysteine also reaches a solvent-exposed cysteine on NR4A1
or NR4A2 — a target-engagement geometry result about a construct class, conditional on the cryptic pocket being
the right site, which licenses a short-linker design preference and refutes two of three candidate handles, and
which makes no claim about binding, ternary formation, degradation, efficacy or safety.*
