## 3 · The falsifier, taken seriously: is SuFEx tyrosine real?

Row 6's own falsifier is that *"chemistry credibility is a literature label, not a computed quantity"*.

⛔ **Verification level, stated up front.** Every publisher, PMC, Europe PMC and Crossref host returned
**403** in this session — `curl` failed CONNECT to europepmc.org and api.crossref.org, and fetches of
pubmed, nature.com, pmc, europepmc, RSC and bioRxiv all returned HTTP 403. **No full text and no publisher
abstract was retrieved.** What was obtained is a search-result listing: title, host, and the DOI or PMID
carried in the returned URL. Each entry in the artifact records this, and **no entry asserts an identifier,
page range or figure that was not visible in a returned listing.**

**The label survives.** SuFEx tyrosine-targeting chemistry is real and published:

- **Chen et al., *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* — `10.1021/jacs.6b02960`, PMID `27191344`.** Aryl fluorosulfates
  inactivate intracellular lipid binding proteins through chemoselective SuFEx reaction with a
  **binding-site** Tyr residue. The anchor precedent.
- **Hahm et al., *Nat. Chem. Biol.* — `10.1038/s41589-019-0404-5`, PMID `31768034`.** Sulfur-triazole
  exchange profiles tyrosine sites at proteome scale; leaving-group tuning gives **fold enrichment** for Tyr
  over other nucleophiles — enrichment, **not** exclusivity.
- **Martín-Gago & Olsen, *Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.* — `10.1002/anie.201806037`, PMID `30024079`.** ★ The
  decisive one. Aryl fluorosulfates are **latent** electrophiles that react on activation *in particular
  binding sites*, where a ligand-binding motif supplies **residence time** and the surrounding environment
  (basic residues) **lowers the pKa** of the target nucleophile.
- **Wang et al., *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* — `10.1021/jacs.8b01087`.** Title states the cross-reactivity outright:
  a SuFEx aryl fluorosulfate reacts with proximal **lysine, histidine and tyrosine**.
- **Covalent VHL-targeted sulfonyl fluoride PROTACs, *J. Med. Chem.* 67(6):4641.** A SuFEx warhead **has**
  been carried in a bifunctional degrader — ⛔ **but the covalent bond is on the E3 side (VHL Ser110)**, not
  on the target. Not precedent for a linker-borne SuFEx warhead engaging a target-side tyrosine.
- **SuFEx PROTAC construction, PMID `40267748`.** ⚠ Here SuFEx is a **linker-construction click reaction**,
  not a protein-engaging warhead. Recorded so nobody counts it as engagement precedent.

★ **Verdict.** *Precedented* is accurate and not an overstatement. It is also **not routine**, and the
specific way it is non-routine is what matters: **the precedent set is binding-site tyrosines with a
pKa-perturbing microenvironment and ligand residence time. Y419 is not that object** — it is an
**exit-vector** residue 11.4 Å from the pocket, on one static opened conformer, with no pKa, no measured
microenvironment and no residence time modelled anywhere in this repository. The chemistry does not kill the
lead; it does not transfer to this site for free either. **Only a bench closes that gap** — no pKa predictor
validated on ligand-directed SuFEx reactivity exists here, and building one is not a `$0` job.

## 4 · The consequence `S1` drags in — measured, not asserted

`S1`'s **chemoselectivity window** is Route B's decision quantity: the interval of linker backbone-atom
counts over which the electrophile reaches C397 and reaches **no other cysteine** in NR4A3, NR4A1 or NR4A2.
Its verdict, cited from [`categorical-axis-audit.json`](categorical-axis-audit.json) → `window_verdict`:

> Under **both** reach conventions the first cysteine to come into reach belongs to a **paralogue chain**,
> in **30 of 30** graded cells per convention. Through-space, the majority closer is **NR4A1 C505** (24 of
> 30) — a position NR4A3 **shares** (C536), so no reciprocal-uniqueness reading applies to it. Corridor, it
> is **NR4A2 C534** (23 of 30) — a position NR4A3 **lacks** (S565).

⛔ **That window is computed over cysteines only.** `nr4a3_linker_covalent_reach.cysteines_in` reads atom
`SG` and no other reactive atom. A SuFEx warhead does not see a cysteine-only competitor set — the
literature above reports Tyr, Lys, His and Ser labelling for this warhead class.

**So the census, on the same three committed opened models, same cryptic-pocket reference, same
minimum-heavy-atom metric `M2` used for `dist_to_cryptic_pocket_A`:**

| warhead class | reactive atoms, 3 models | excluded as off-core | **inside Y419's own 11.4 Å shell** |
|---|---|---|---|
| cysteine-directed — *what `S1`'s window was computed over* | 18 | 4 | **7** |
| SuFEx, narrow set (Tyr/Lys/His) | 70 | 16 | **18** — **2.57×** |
| SuFEx, full set (Tyr/Lys/His/Ser/Thr) | 160 | 42 | **45** — **6.43×** |

Inside-shell composition of the full set: NR4A3 19, NR4A1 14, NR4A2 12 · Ser 22, Lys 9, Tyr 5, Thr 5, His 4.
Paralogue positions with post-fit deviation > 2.0 Å after superposition are **excluded and counted**, never
silently dropped; the superposition reproduces its own documented core (NR4A1 203/244 at 1.73 Å, NR4A2
206/249 at 1.60 Å).

⚠ **Why Ser and Thr are in the headline set.** A *competitor* is a residue the warhead can label, not a
residue anyone would design against. `M2` grades Ser *"not a handle outside catalytic serines"* — a
statement about **design intent** — while the VHL PROTAC above puts a sulfonyl fluoride's covalent bond on
**Ser110**, a serine labelled in practice. Both are true; they are different questions. The narrow set is
reported beside the headline and **the direction does not change**.

⛔ **What this is NOT.** Not the chemoselectivity window. The window is an interval in linker backbone-atom
counts from an E3 anchor and needs the placement/pendant enumeration, **which has never been run for a
non-cysteine warhead**. This is a **lower-bound diagnostic on the size of that unrun problem**. It does not
prove Y419's window is narrower — it proves the window **was never computed for this warhead, and computing
it is not optional.** `S1`'s window already closes on the *smaller* of the two populations.

second cysteine"* — and the unique-lysine term serves `R12` (degradation), a **different requirement** from

paralogue-unique, linker-reachable residue **above the family's one literature-anchored covalent site** on
the ruler this program mandates. **The gap was in the enumeration.**

⛔ **And Y419 is a candidate, not redundancy.** Three things stand between it and a second handle, and all
three are named rather than deferred:

1. **The chemistry does not transfer for free** — the precedent is binding-site tyrosines; Y419 is
   exit-vector. §3.
2. **The window has never been computed for this warhead**, against a competitor population 2.6–6.4× larger
   than the one on which the cysteine window already closes. §4 — and it is a **`$0` CPU job**, proposed as
   roadmap §10.1 row 30.
3. ⛔ **It does not unblock Route B.** Route B is blocked on **`R5`** — the site/pose question `V3` returned
   **INCONCLUSIVE** on — and *every* anchor here, Y419's included, comes from that same docked pose. A
   second handle changes the single-point-of-failure risk. It does not touch the blocker.

**Claim ceiling.** Everything above is **reagent-level**: a residue is unique to NR4A3 among its two
paralogues, is within linker reach on one static opened conformer, and belongs to a residue class with
published covalent chemistry. Nothing here says an adduct forms. No binding affinity, no ternary geometry,
no degradation, no efficacy, no safety, no therapeutic window, no clinical readiness — and **no
proteome-wide selectivity of any kind**: the comparison set is two paralogues.
