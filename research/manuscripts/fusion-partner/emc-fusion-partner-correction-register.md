# Correction register — fusion-partner stratification

**Why this is not inside the manuscript, recorded here rather than left to be rediscovered.** These
rows lived as `Appendix A` of
[`emc-fusion-partner-stratification.md`](./emc-fusion-partner-stratification.md) until round 8 of that
paper's hardening series (2026-08-27). At that point the appendix ran **31 rows and 5,476 words of a
17,647-word manuscript — close to a third of it, and more than its entire Results section (3,912 words)** —
plus 15 inline `Superseded, retained` notes and 4 internal-review attributions in the live text.
⚠ **Those counts were re-measured here rather than carried forward, and two of them moved.** Round 7's record
states 5,592 and ~3,998 for the same two spans; measured at the pin the appendix was removed from
(`git show 55471ab:research/manuscripts/fusion-partner/emc-fusion-partner-stratification.md`, then
`sed -n '955,$p' | wc -w` for the appendix and `awk` between the §3 and §4 headings for Results) they are 5,476
and 3,912. The disagreement is a boundary convention, not a dispute about the text, and it changes nothing in
the argument — but an inherited number is a dated observation (CLAUDE.md §4), so the reproducible one is
printed and the command that produces it is stated beside it.

⛔ **THE SIZE WAS THE SYMPTOM; THE MECHANISM WAS THE FINDING.** Three blind adversarial seats, reading
independently, traced round 7's blockers to the same cause: a statement was corrected by *registering a
row here* while the wrong sentence stayed in the live text, so six sections came to reason by pointing
at an appendix instead of saying what they meant. That is `paper-hardening` §1's replace-don't-append
failure — the correction was appended to the document instead of applied to the sentence.

⭐ **So the register moved and the corrections went INTO the prose.** Every row below is retained
verbatim; a correction is never silently dropped (CLAUDE.md rule 1.2). What changed is that the
manuscript no longer *depends* on this file to be read correctly — a reader who never opens it now gets
the same claims at the same strength.


Per the repository's correction rule, superseded statements are registered rather than left in the live
text, and are never silently dropped. ⚠ *Registered in **two** places, not one: a row here when the statement was load-bearing, and an inline `⚠ Superseded, retained` note at the sentence itself when the correction is local to it. Several inline notes have no row here — that is the intended shape and not an omission, but this preamble asserted a single register until a blind adversarial seat counted them, 2026-08-26.*
