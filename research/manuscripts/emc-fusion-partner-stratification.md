## 1 · What the lane claimed, and what pooling it actually shows

The lane memo's premise was right that four independent lines converge and right that nobody had pooled them.
Pooling changes the conclusion's **strength**, not its **direction**, and it surfaces four things no source
says out loud — three findings below plus one attribution correction in §3.

**The pooled result.** Across every published EMC report that gives an objective-response outcome by NR4A3
partner — there are exactly **two**, the sunitinib named-use series (PMID 24703573) and the pazopanib phase 2
trial NCT02066285 (PMID 31331701) — **no TAF15::NR4A3 patient has ever responded to an antiangiogenic TKI.**
That is 0 of 3 to 0 of 5 patients, depending on how the overlap between the two reports resolves. The 95 %
Wilson upper bound on the TAF15 response rate is **43.4 % to 56.1 %** — which sits **above** the comparator
arm's point estimate in both analyses (37.0 % and 21.1 %). Post-hoc Fisher exact two-sided p = 0.155 pooled,
1.0 for the trial alone.

**So the honest statement is a direction, not a difference.** Consistent across two cohorts, two drugs and
five years of accrual (July 2011 – January 2017); and the data cannot exclude a TAF15 response rate equal to
the EWSR1 one. Anyone who has written "TAF15 tumours do not respond to antiangiogenics" has written a claim
the evidence does not carry — and that includes the reviews that state it flatly.

**Three findings that are not in any source.**

1. **The metastasis signal runs backwards in the one cohort that publishes counts.** In Agaram 2014
   (PMID 24746215), distant recurrence is **6/16 (37.5 %) with EWSR1::NR4A3 and 2/7 (28.6 %) with
   TAF15::NR4A3** — the opposite direction to the claim repeated across the review literature
   (PMC12398172, PMC12376927, PMC7563993, PMC9131214 all state lower metastasis or better
   metastasis-free survival with EWSR1). The TAF15 arm's mean follow-up is about half the EWSR1 arm's
   (21.7 vs 43.3 months), which is enough to explain the reversal on its own; but the same truncation makes
   the **death** excess harder to observe, not easier, which is why the mortality contrast survives it. The
   one cohort that treated metastasis as a *time-to-event* endpoint (Paioli 2021, n = 67) trends the other
   way and does not reach significance (DMFS p = 0.09). Crude proportions and DMFS are different estimands
   and these two readings are not in contradiction — but **neither establishes the metastasis claim the
   reviews make**.
2. **Two independent series, both larger than any cohort that could be pooled, find the partner is not
   independent of tumour size.** Huang 2023 (PMID 36948401, n = 58): TAF15::NR4A3 shortens disease-specific
   survival on univariate analysis and **loses significance on multivariable analysis** — only size > 10 cm
   (P = .004) and metastasis at presentation (P = .032) remain independent, and TAF15::NR4A3 is itself
   associated with size > 10 cm (78 %, P = .025). Paioli 2021 (PMID 32572850, n = 67, Italian Sarcoma
   Group): NR4A3-EWS shows only a **trend** for DFS (p = 0.08) and DMFS (p = 0.09), in the same analysis
   where **size reaches p = 0.004**. Neither publishes per-partner event counts, so neither could be pooled.
   The partner may be a marker for a big tumour rather than for a biology.
3. **The only matched model pair says the partner does *not* determine drug response in general.**
   The lane memo records that "even the carfilzomib synergy splits by partner" in the USZ pair
   (PMID 36316541). **The primary source says the opposite.** Verbatim: *"Both models independent of the
   NR4A3 fusion partner showed high sensitivity to carfilzomib"* and *"Similar drug responses were seen in
   both models, independent of the fusion partner from NR4A3."* The only partner-differential reported is a
   scoring-threshold one — the carfilzomib combinations scored **synergistic** in the EWSR1 model and
   **additive** in the TAF15 model, one model per arm, no statistical claim. Neither model was tested against
   an antiangiogenic TKI. This does not refute the clinical correlation; it **bounds** it, to the drug class
   where it was observed.

**Prevalence.** Pooling four independent molecular series (MSKCC, Taiwan, Czech Republic, Italian Sarcoma
Group) gives TAF15::NR4A3 in **28/154 = 18.2 % (95 % CI 12.9–25.0)** of partner-assigned EMC. That is the
population any partner-stratified recommendation would move — large enough to matter, small enough to
explain why every series' TAF15 arm is 2–10 patients. Note that the pooled figure is **below the "27 %" the
field usually quotes**, which comes from the single referral-centre cohort (Agaram 2014) that is the high
outlier of the four (29.2 % vs 15.8–18.2 % elsewhere).

**The overlap that holds back the headline.** The sunitinib series and the pazopanib trial cannot be shown
non-overlapping, and the risk is specific rather than theoretical: Istituto Nazionale Tumori Milan ran the
series and was a trial site under the same senior investigator; the trial's entry criterion —
NR4A3-translocated advanced EMC with RECIST progression in the previous six months — is satisfied **by
definition** by the series' two progressors, who are exactly its two TAF15 patients; and neither report
states whether prior antiangiogenic therapy was permitted. So POLICY-evidence §2.3 holds the smaller cohort
out of the headline, and the artifact reports both the conservative and the assume-independent analysis.
Worst case, **the world's entire TAF15 antiangiogenic experience is three patients.**

**What would resolve it, and it is not free:** the Lancet Oncol 2019 full text (paywalled; `isOpenAccess N`,
`inEPMC N`) carries both the trial's full partner distribution and its prior-therapy table. The Huang 2023
and Paioli 2021 full texts (both paywalled) carry the per-partner survival event counts that would let the
two largest outcome cohorts be pooled. None is reachable from CI, and `NCT02066285`'s eligibility text is
absent from the cached ClinicalTrials.gov v2 field set.

## 3 · The hedge, stated at full strength

The investigators who reported the correlation do not believe the fusion causes it:
**"Even in EMCS the fusion-protein is unlikely to be related to sunitinib sensitivity"** — they read the
partner as a **surrogate for something downstream**.

⚠ **The quote is from the 2012 two-case report (PMID 23058004), not the 2014 series (PMID 24703573) it is
usually attributed to — including in this repository's own lane memo.** Verified against the cached full
text of both; the 2014 abstract does not contain it, and says instead that "genotype/phenotype analyses
support a correlation between response and EWSR1-NR4A3 fusion". This changes what the hedge *is*: written in
2012, when the authors had two EWSR1-positive responders and no TAF15 patient at all, it is their **prior**
that the fusion is not the mechanism — by analogy to alveolar soft part sarcoma — not a retrospective caveat
added once the correlation appeared. That makes it stronger context, not weaker.

Three later observations are consistent with that reading, and all are above: the prognostic effect is
absorbed by tumour size in the two largest series, Paioli 2021 cannot reach significance on it at all, and
the matched ex vivo pair shows partner-independent drug response for the one class it tested.

Every arm in this synthesis is small. Every TAF15 arm that carries an actual outcome is 2–7 patients — the
only larger TAF15 groups (Huang 2023's 9, Paioli 2021's 10) publish no event counts at all. The pooled
intervals in
[`emc-fusion-partner-pooling.json`](./emc-fusion-partner-pooling.json) are wide because the evidence is thin,
and they are printed at that width deliberately.

## 4 · What a paper on this would claim — and would not

**Would claim.**

- That four independent lines converge on the NR4A3 5′ partner as a candidate stratification variable in EMC,
  and that **this is the first synthesis to pool them under a single stated method**.
- That the pooled objective-response evidence is **0 of 3–5 TAF15::NR4A3 patients**, with a 95 % upper bound
  of 43.4–56.1 %, i.e. **directionally consistent and statistically inconclusive** — a distinction no existing
  review makes.
- That the prognostic literature contains a **reversal nobody has reported**: distant recurrence is higher in
  the EWSR1 arm of the only cohort publishing partner-stratified event counts, while the review literature
  states the opposite.
- That the partner's prognostic effect **does not survive adjustment for tumour size** in either of the two
  largest series to test it (Huang 2023, Paioli 2021), in both of which size is the significant variable.
- That TAF15::NR4A3 affects **18.2 % (95 % CI 12.9–25.0)** of partner-assigned EMC — **below** the ~27 %
  the field routinely quotes from a single referral-centre series.
- That the field's own hedge is **misattributed**: the sentence everyone quotes as the sunitinib series'
  caveat is from the 2012 two-case report, written before any TAF15 patient existed.
- A concrete, cheap ask of the field: **report NR4A3 partner alongside response in every EMC systemic-therapy
  report, and publish the partner-stratified counts from the trials and series that already have them.**
  Three paywalled tables (Lancet Oncol 2019, Mod Pathol 2023, Ann Surg Oncol 2021) would more than double the
  world's poolable evidence on this question at zero new patient cost.

**Would not claim.**

- **Not** that TAF15::NR4A3 predicts non-response to antiangiogenic TKIs. Zero of five is not a rate.
- **Not** that EWSR1::NR4A3 patients should receive, or TAF15::NR4A3 patients should be denied, sunitinib,
  pazopanib or any other agent. No treatment recommendation follows from this analysis, and the comparator
  arm of the trial is **non-TAF15**, not verified EWSR1.
- **Not** that the fusion partner is mechanistically responsible. The primary authors say the opposite, the
  mechanism paper shares their consortium, and the one matched model pair shows partner-independent drug
  response.
- **Not** that TAF15::NR4A3 is an independent prognostic factor. Huang 2023 says it is not once size is
  adjusted for, and Paioli 2021 does not reach significance at all.
- **Not** a survival model, a hazard ratio, or any individual prognosis. These are crude during-follow-up
  proportions with mixed follow-up and no censoring (POLICY-evidence §2.4).

**Endpoint, one sentence, for [`systems/views/L3-publications.md`](../../systems/views/L3-publications.md):**
*the NR4A3 5′ fusion partner is a candidate — not established — stratification variable in EMC, whose entire
published antiangiogenic-response evidence base is three to five TAF15 patients, and the field should start
reporting it.*

**What would kill it** is in the artifact under `what_could_kill_this`; the shortest version is **one
published TAF15::NR4A3 objective response on any antiangiogenic TKI.**
