# Ruling on the four open decisions

**Evidence:** [`four-open-decisions-2026-08-07.md`](./four-open-decisions-2026-08-07.md) — the dossier.

## ⛔ 0 · Why these were taken here rather than escalated

**They were escalated first, and that was the error.** [CLAUDE.md §3](../../../CLAUDE.md) reserves a
reviewer block for **(a)** major program-shifting decisions, **(b)** GPU spend over $50, or **(c)** an
outward-facing or irreversible act — and it carries a dated correction recording that this exact
over-escalation had already happened once. Tested against the tree rather than assumed, on 2026-08-07:

| test | reading | source |
|---|---|---|
| Is anything here published? | **No.** Zero DOI / submission markers in the SI; the roadmap's own §5 says *"Nothing is in print, which is the whole opportunity"* | `nr4a3-degrader-paper-SI.md` |
| Is anything here spending? | **No.** `n_instances: 0` | [`ternary-vast-account-census.json`](../../modalities/ternary-vast-account-census.json) |
| Is anything here irreversible? | **No.** All four are text and register state in a git repository | — |
| Is anything here goal-changing? | **No.** None moves the north star, the portfolio, or which paper is written | [roadmap §13](../nr4a3-program-map.md) |

⇒ **None of the four clears the bar.** Three are settled by evidence already in hand and the fourth is
the application of a standing rule. Escalating them spent the reviewer channel on work that was
self-doable, which is the thing §3 exists to prevent.

⚠ **This does not retire the dossier's own recommendations — it adopts all four of them.** The dossier
did the reasoning; what was wrong was routing the *conclusion* through a human instead of taking it.

## 1 · `C14` / SI §S1 (row 10) — **ADOPTED: do not amend `C14`; withdraw the four clauses**

**Why it is not a judgement call.** The dossier's arithmetic closes it: **no reachable `C14` amendment
restores readability.** Scoring against the nearest deposited copy gives 8 of 10, not 10; widening to
the existing `partial` band gives 7 of 10. All three failing receptors still fail under every option
that is not forbidden outright. A decision among options that all fail is not a decision.

**And `C14` is shared.** The same 2.0 Å line produces `V3`'s protocol-ceiling miss and `V22`'s 0 of 6,
so moving it to rescue the anti-target panel would convert a documented miss into a pass elsewhere.
That is the frozen rule's own prohibition, not a preference.

⛔ **Option 3 is declined as a rescue, and the reason is not squeamishness.** A uniform receptor
re-sourcing rule is defensible as an *instrument* question. It buys nothing *here*, because the second
block survives it: the NR4A3 ΔG denominator those margins subtract is committed nowhere in this repo,
so **even a 10-of-10 pass leaves the published margins non-re-derivable.** Running it as a rescue would
spend effort on an outcome that cannot change the clauses' status.

**REVERSED BY:** the NR4A3 ΔG denominator landing as a committed artifact **and** a receptor-sourcing
rule, fixed in writing *before* the run, returning 10 of 10. Both, not either.

## 2 · Arm F (row 7) — **ADOPTED: retire explicitly; promote its trigger to a numbered row**

The dossier found the work state was **already** classified (⏸ parked, §6b, trigger named) and that the
row's "unclassified" implication traces to a stale inventory row. Correcting a stale row is maintenance.
The substantive half — that the reopening trigger has **no rung, no gate and no price anywhere on the
board**, so it can be neither refused nor costed nor sequenced — is a gap, and closing a gap by giving
it a row is exactly the promotion rule §10.1a already applies to everything else.

**REVERSED BY:** the trigger firing — a ternary alchemical free-energy method passing the valB
known-answer control.

## 3 · Two-branch template (row 8) — **ADOPTED: decline promotion; register as CI-guarded exploration**

Row 8's standing complaint was that it *"has never been put to trimcrae"*. That was true and is now
spent: it has been analysed, and the analysis answers it. It buys **one** admissible chain rather than
a design space, at a mass above the committed library's entire range, on windows **transferred** from
single-branch records that no two-branch chain has ever had measured. It invalidates nothing.

⭑ **The decisive point is that the experiment it exists for does not exist.** Rung `5a-KS` structurally
forbids a two-mechanism molecule, and the covalent route was measured on 2026-08-03 not to need two
branches at all. Declining a capability with no consumer is not a program choice.

**REVERSED BY:** an experiment appearing that requires two mechanisms in one molecule, **or** two-branch
windows being measured directly rather than transferred.

## 4 · `term_a_feasibility_envelope` (row 28) — **ADOPTED: regenerate the field alone; correct the row**

Row 28's stated premise is **falsified**: the 3-ball kernel is a no-op for this field, and what moves
the answer is Monte-Carlo convergence — which was already recorded in a sibling artifact on 2026-07-25,
eight days before the row was written. ⛔ The direction is **not** conservative, which is what the row
claimed: `geometrically_closed` is a *closure*, so an under-claiming reach estimate makes it **more**
confident.

**A falsified premise sitting in the roadmap is a defect, not an open question**, and rule 1.2 already
prescribes the remedy — correct it, register the superseded values in the same commit, keep the
narrative out of the live text. That is a rule being applied, not a choice being made. ✅ The routed
edit is **applied**; the regeneration is $0 and takes 8.6 s.

⭑ **What is not at risk, and it is why this is safe to take:** `C397` reads 10 atoms in every seed, both
kernels and both budgets, and `C397` is the only cysteine that survives downstream. No gate verdict, no
basin nomination and no committed downstream number changes. The exposure is two statements about
`C420` and `C559`.

⚠ **The wider exposure is NOT taken here.** `term_a_union`, every per-basin `electrophile_reach` and the
Tier-2 gate count route through the same kernel. They stay **frozen and registered as pre-fix-kernel**.
Regenerating them is a second enumeration, and a second enumeration of a preregistered artifact is a
different kind of act from repairing one unconverged field.

## 5 · What genuinely remains for trimcrae — and it is not on this page

Two things, neither of which is one of the four:

1. ★ **The trial-eligibility notice.** [`fet-fusion-trial-eligibility-notice.md`](../fusion-direct/fet-fusion-trial-eligibility-notice.md)
   §4 is a draft addressed to patients and clinicians. It is **outward-facing**, it concerns real people
   acting on it, and a stale registry status is the one error here that could mislead someone about
   their own options. Its reviewer block is §5 of that file. **This one is correctly escalated and stays
   escalated.**
2. **The deliverables framing** ([roadmap §13](../nr4a3-program-map.md)) — which paper gets written.
   That is goal-level and is trimcrae's by construction. ⚠ Decision 1 above touches it indirectly: it
   withdraws four clauses from `P6`'s SI, which widens the gap §13 already records between `P6`'s title
   and its Limitations section. That is a *consequence* to weigh in §13, not a reason to have withheld
   decision 1.

⛔ **Nothing on this page licenses a selectivity, efficacy, safety, therapeutic-window or clinical claim,
and none is computed anywhere in it.** Decision 1 makes the paper weaker on purpose; decision 3 declines
a molecule the program could otherwise name. Both read as self-inflicted losses and both are correct.
