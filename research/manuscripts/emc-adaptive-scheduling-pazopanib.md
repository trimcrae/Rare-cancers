# Adaptive scheduling of pazopanib in EMC — the five grounds checked, and an inverted answer

> ⛔ **NOTHING HERE ASSERTS EFFICACY, SAFETY, A THERAPEUTIC WINDOW OR CLINICAL READINESS.** Adaptive
> scheduling has never been tested in EMC or in any sarcoma. Deliberately reducing the dose of an
> active drug outside a trial can shorten disease control. This document describes what would have
> to be true for a hypothesis to hold. **It contains no predicted survival for any patient and is
> not treatment advice.**

## 0 · The headline, which is an inversion

[`emc-unexplored-treatment-lanes.md` §3.8](./emc-unexplored-treatment-lanes.md#38--adaptive-scheduling)
names its own falsifier and asks for it to be the centrepiece:

> *"anti-angiogenic resistance may not be fitness-costed, because **the drug's target is host
> endothelium, not the tumour cell** — so resistance could be microenvironmental, and competitive
> suppression would have nothing to work with."*

**The model does not agree with the first half of that sentence, and the disagreement is the most
useful thing this lane produced.** Sweeping the two-population competitive Lotka–Volterra model
over its parameter region (`competition_model`):

| axis swept | range | adaptive gain ratio moves |
|---|---|---|
| **initial resistant fraction f₀** | 1e-4 → 0.3 | **1.43 → 1.00** — the entire range |
| **fitness cost of resistance c** | 0 → 0.5, at fixed f₀ | **≈1 % of the ratio** — it barely moves |

**The adaptive gain runs on the initial resistant fraction and is very nearly independent of the
fitness cost.** A cost raises time to progression in *both* arms by roughly the same factor and
cancels in the ratio.

**Why:** in this model form competitive suppression operates through **competition for space** — the
`(1 − S − R)` term — not through a growth-rate differential. Killing the sensitive population frees
capacity the resistant population then grows into faster, and that mechanism is present whether or
not resistance is costly.

**Consequence for the memo's open question.** The task set for this lane was to work out whether
EMC's mutational quietness (matched-trio WGS, PMC11285543) cuts **for** or **against** the premise.
It cuts **FOR** (`mutational_quietness.net_verdict`), and it does so *because of* the inversion:
quietness is an argument that the pre-existing resistant fraction is **small**, and the small-f₀
region is exactly where the adaptive gain is largest. The route by which quietness would have cut
against — non-genetic resistance being cost-free — acts on the axis that turns out not to drive the
result.

⚠ **Held honestly:** whether a cost of resistance is *required* is a property of the competition
kernel, not a theorem, and it is contested in the adaptive-therapy literature. A different kernel
can restore the requirement. What is robust across every sweep run here — drug potency 1–12 × r_S,
cell turnover 0–0.5 × r_S — is the **rank**: f₀ dominates cost in all of them.

**Status: the question SURVIVES**, well-posed and narrower than the memo left it. It is not resolved
and cannot be, because no rate parameter of the model has ever been measured in EMC in vivo (§4).

## 1 · The five grounds, checked one at a time

`five_grounds`. Four hold; one is overstated and survives only in a weaker form.

### (i) "There is one active systemic class, so preserving it beats deepening it" — **PARTIALLY OVERSTATED**

**Against:** anthracycline-based chemotherapy in 10 evaluable EMC with a documented *NR4A3*
rearrangement gave **4 partial responses (40 %) and median PFS 8 months** (PMID 24345066,
PMC3879193 `[FT]`). That is a
second class with measured activity, so *"one active class"* is not literally true. Sunitinib
(PMID 24703573, `[secondary]`) gave 6 PR / 2 SD / 2 PD in n = 10 — same class as pazopanib, but it
means the anti-angiogenic option is not a single agent either.

**What survives:** after anthracycline failure the anti-angiogenic class is the only one with
prospective EMC evidence, and anthracycline's own 8-month median PFS is short. Losing the
anti-angiogenic class is close to losing the last measured option. **The weaker form is enough to
carry the lane; the strong form should not be written into a paper.**

### (ii) "ORR ~18 % with median PFS ~19 months is control without shrinkage" — **SUPPORTED, and now quantitative**

Primary-verified from the phase 2 abstract (NCT02066285, PMID 31331701 `[API]`): 26 patients
entered, 23 met modified-ITT eligibility, **22 evaluable, 4 (18 %, 95 % CI 1–36) had a RECIST
objective response**, median follow-up 27 months (IQR 18–30). Median PFS 19 months is `[secondary]`
via the 2025 review — **the primary abstract does not state one**, and that should be recorded
rather than smoothed over.

⭐ **The fact the memo did not use, and it is the strongest support any of the five grounds has.**
Eligibility required *"RECIST progression in the previous 6 months"* — verbatim from the primary.
**Every enrolled patient had documented growth within six months of starting**, and the
on-treatment median PFS was 19 months. The drug's measured effect is a change of **tempo**, not
cytoreduction: 18 of 22 evaluable patients never met the response threshold. That is precisely the
regime in which dosing to maximum tolerated buys the least.

⚠ **And the same criterion is a caveat the memo does not make.** The trial cohort is *by
construction* the **progressing** subset of EMC, not the indolent one. An adaptive-therapy argument
that leans on "EMC is indolent" (ground iii) while calibrating on this trial is mixing two
populations.

### (iii) "The natural history is long enough for oscillation to run" — **SUPPORTED** `[secondary]`

*"Median OS for patients with metastatic EMC is in the order of 5–7 years in modern series, and the
disease often follows an indolent course even without immediate systemic treatment"*; *"In some
cases, watchful waiting or delayed therapy is used for slow-growing M1"* (PMC12504171 `[FT]`,
secondary). The second quote matters more than the first: **an existing practice of withholding
therapy is the half of an adaptive schedule that is hardest to introduce**, and EMC already has it.

### (iv) "Burden is countable — lung nodules on volumetric CT" — **PLAUSIBLE, UNTESTED**

The compartment is right: pulmonary metastases are *"present in up to 50–90 % of patients at some
point"* (`[secondary]`). The instrument is not tested: **no EMC study reports volumetric nodule
tracking, and no EMC study reports a measured in-vivo growth rate at all.** And the anchor used a
different class of signal — adaptive abiraterone ran on weekly PSA, while CT is discrete, expensive
and carries dose, so the control loop's sampling interval is months. Whether that is fast enough
for EMC's tempo has never been analysed.

⭐ **This is the ground most easily converted into real work** — see §5.

### (v) "Halving cumulative dose is a claim on its own" — **FOLLOWS FROM (ii) + (iii)**

Anchor: adaptive abiraterone held 10 of 11 patients in stable oscillation at **47 % of standard
cumulative dose** (PMID 29180633, `[secondary]`). There is a real toxicity burden to halve: grade 3
hypertension **9/26 (35 %)**, ALT rise **6/26 (23 %)**, AST rise **5/26 (19 %)** in the EMC trial
(`[API]`), over a median PFS measured in years. ⚠ A cumulative-dose endpoint is not a survival
endpoint, which is why it is both the cheapest to power and the least persuasive alone.

## 2 · The model, and where it puts the weight

`competition_model`. Two-population competitive Lotka–Volterra, carrying capacity normalised to 1,
**symmetric** competition, drug acting only on the sensitive population, Gatenby-style AT-50 rule
(withdraw at 50 % of baseline burden, resume at baseline), progression at 1.2 × baseline, forward
Euler dt = 0.5 day, 20-year horizon.

**Symmetric competition is the conservative choice and is stated as such:** an asymmetric kernel in
which sensitive cells suppress resistant cells more than the reverse *manufactures* the adaptive
advantage by assumption. No EMC datum supports either direction, so the reported gains are a lower
bound.

**Time is reported in sensitive-population doubling times, never in months.** EMC's in-vivo growth
rate is unmeasured (§4), so an absolute time axis would be fabricated.

**Gain ratio by initial resistant fraction** (min–max across the whole cost sweep at each f₀):

| f₀ | 1e-4 | 1e-3 | 0.01 | 0.05 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gain | 1.42–1.43 | 1.38–1.40 | 1.29–1.33 | 1.19–1.24 | 1.11–1.19 | 1.09–1.10 | **1.00–1.07** |

**Gain ratio by resistance cost** (min–max across the whole f₀ sweep at each c): every row spans
**1.00 – ~1.43**. The cost axis does not separate anything.

**The degenerate corner.** At f₀ = 0.3 the adaptive rule **never fires**
(`adaptive_rule_never_fired`): the drug cannot halve total burden because the resistant compartment
alone is already near the trigger, so the adaptive arm is identical to continuous dosing *by
construction*. **A high resistant fraction does not make adaptive therapy worse — it makes it
unimplementable.**

**Where the cost does act.** Not on time to progression, but on the resistant fraction itself:
`t_resistant_fraction_50pc_*` shows the cost delaying the moment the tumour becomes
majority-resistant (at f₀ = 1e-4, from 4.4 to 5.2 doubling times under continuous dosing and from
18.7 to 35.8 under adaptive, as c goes 0 → 0.5). That matters for containment and for second-line
options; it does not drive the gain.

**Robustness.** The rank survives a drug-potency sweep (δ = 1–12 × r_S; the gain saturates above
about 3 ×) and a cell-turnover sweep (moderate turnover *increases* the gain). High turnover pushes
both arms past the 20-year horizon, where a ratio of 1.0 is a **censoring artefact** and is flagged
as one rather than reported as equality.

### 2a · The second model — resistance as reversible phenotype

`plasticity_model`, fitness cost fixed at **zero**, f₀ = 1e-3, with drug-induced switching S→R on
drug and reversion R→S off drug:

- no switching at all: gain **1.38**
- switching **without** reversion: gain falls to **1.12** — every day of drug exposure manufactures
  resistance, so a schedule dispensing less drug helps less than one dispensing none
- switching **with** reversion: gain **1.24** in the strongest uncensored cell, and the one cell
  with slow switching (0.5 × r_S) and fast reversion (2 × r_S) runs **past the 20-year horizon
  entirely** (nominal ratio 8.97, censored — a lower bound, not a measurement)
- reversion of a **pre-existing** resistant pool with no drug-induced switching at all is stronger
  still: gain **3.06** at μ_off = 0.5 × r_S, and censored at 2 × r_S
- ⚠ at the fastest switching rate tested (2 × r_S) progression arrives before any holiday can fire
  and the adaptive arm is identical to continuous dosing — the same degenerate corner as a high f₀

⭐ **This is a second, independent rationale for intermittent dosing — resensitisation rather than
competitive release — and it has a different observable.** Competitive release predicts the
resistant fraction falls during a holiday because sensitive cells outgrow it; resensitisation
predicts it falls because resistant cells become sensitive again, which shows up clinically as **a
second response on re-challenge**.

## 3 · The falsifier, restated — the memo named the wrong one

`falsifier`. The memo's sentence conflates two separable claims and **only one of them is fatal**:

| claim | fatal? | why |
|---|---|---|
| *"resistance is not fitness-costed"* | **No** | The cost cancels in the time-to-progression ratio. Competitive suppression here works through competition for space, not a growth-rate differential. |
| *"resistance is microenvironmental, not a tumour-cell trait"* | **Yes** | It means the model's second **state variable does not exist**. There is no resistant population to suppress at any cost. |

**The memo reached the right conclusion by the wrong mechanism, and the difference decides which
measurement is worth making.**

**The primary falsifier, as it should be stated:** *does a heritable, tumour-cell-intrinsic
resistant compartment exist in EMC under pazopanib, and is the tumour close enough to carrying
capacity for the two compartments to compete?* Both halves are **preconditions for the model to
describe anything at all**, whereas every parameter in §4 merely sets the size of the effect — and a
precondition that fails cannot be compensated by a favourable parameter value.

**The second falsifier the model promotes:** *is the initial resistant fraction small?* The gain
vanishes as f₀ approaches 0.3 and the schedule becomes unimplementable there. This is the axis the
memo did not weight and the one that decides the size of the prize.

**Discriminating observations, cheapest first:**

1. **In patients who progress on pazopanib and stop it, does the growth rate of previously
   responding lesions differ from the on-drug progression rate?** A slower off-drug rate is direct
   evidence of a fitness cost; no change is evidence of c ≈ 0. *Cost: retrospective imaging
   re-read. No laboratory, no new patients.*
2. **Does pazopanib re-challenge after a holiday produce a second response?** A second response is
   evidence of reversion (the resensitisation mechanism); no second response is evidence of durable
   heritable resistance. *No EMC re-challenge data of any kind was found in this pass.*
3. **Paired biopsy or ctDNA at progression: clonal selection, or none?** Clonal selection supports a
   heritable resistant compartment and therefore the two-population model. *Requires tissue — the
   only item here that is not desk-computable.*

**The counter-evidence that keeps the premise alive.** The matched trio is the one EMC dataset
showing heritable divergence *does* occur: *"the pelvic metastasis had more unique SVs with
especially increased mutational burden of SVs in chromosome 2 … different molecular drivers appear
in more advanced, relapsing EMC"* (PMC11285543 `[FT]`). **n = 1, uncontrolled, not tied to any
treatment exposure** — but it is direct evidence against "EMC does not evolve", and a tumour that
evolves has a resistant compartment for the model to describe.

## 4 · What has actually been measured in EMC: none of it

`identifiability`. **Every rate parameter of this model is unmeasured in EMC in vivo.** That is why
this lane emits no predicted progression-free survival in months — a number with a time unit on it
would be a statement about the assumed r_S, not about EMC.

| symbol | meaning | measured in EMC? | closest available | how much it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **r_S** | growth rate of the sensitive population | **no** | ex vivo doubling times **5.09 d** (USZ20-EMC1) and **6.05 d** (USZ22-EMC2), PMID 36316541 `[FT]` | sets the whole time axis |
| **c** | fitness cost of resistance | **no** | nothing in EMC; nothing in any sarcoma for an anti-angiogenic | **less than the memo assumes** — cancels in the ratio |
| **f₀** | resistant fraction at treatment start | **no** | indirect only: 4/22 RECIST responses bound the sensitive fraction at the *lesion* level, not the cellular one | **most** — the axis the gain runs on |
| **δ** | drug kill rate on sensitive cells | **no** | the trial's tempo change, which constrains δ only jointly with r_S | saturates above ~3 × r_S |
| **μ_on / μ_off** | phenotype-switching rates | **no** | nothing; no EMC re-challenge data found | decides which of the two mechanisms applies |

⛔ **The ex vivo doubling times cannot be transplanted.** 5–6 days per doubling is irreconcilable
with a disease whose metastatic median OS is 5–7 years. Using a culture rate in an in-vivo model is
how a simulation acquires false precision, and it is refused here rather than adjusted.

## 5 · What this is, honestly, and the one free step

**Survives, narrower.** Four grounds hold, one is overstated, the model inverts the memo's weighting
of its own falsifier, and the parameter region is mapped — which is exactly the deliverable §3.8
asked for: *"here is the parameter region in which it would [work], and here is exactly what you
would have to measure to know which region EMC is in."*

**Blocked on data, not on compute.** ⛔ **The one free step that moves this lane is a volumetric
re-read of serial chest CTs in any EMC cohort with archived imaging.** It would produce the first
in-vivo EMC growth rates, answer the secondary falsifier about control-loop speed, and — in patients
who stopped pazopanib at progression — test the primary falsifier by comparing on-drug and off-drug
growth rates. **One piece of desk work moves three questions.** It needs imaging access, which this
program does not have, so the honest state is *"blocked on data only a collaborator can supply"*,
not *"blocked on compute"* and not *"open"*.

**The two re-grades this owes the lanes memo are routed, not applied** — this pass computes and does
not edit another document's canonical section. Ready-to-apply, anchors verified verbatim:
[`emc-rt-adaptive-lanes-map-edits.json`](./emc-rt-adaptive-lanes-map-edits.json) → **E2** (the
shortlist row) and **E5** (the falsifier restatement, which reverses the mechanism the memo names
while keeping its conclusion).

**The paper this is for.** A modelling paper with a measurement request attached: *"What would have
to be true for adaptive scheduling to work in extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma."* One sentence
for the field's record: **in a two-population competition model the adaptive gain in EMC is governed
by the initial resistant fraction and is almost independent of the fitness cost of resistance, so
the standard objection to adaptive therapy against an anti-angiogenic — that resistance is not
fitness-costed — is not the objection that bites; the one that bites is whether a tumour-cell
resistant compartment exists at all.** What is missing: every rate parameter, and any EMC
re-challenge datum.

## 6 · Provenance and limits

- **Retrieval.** The sandbox egress proxy blocks `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, `www.ebi.ac.uk` and
  `europepmc.org` on CONNECT. Every source used here was read from the repository's existing
  `literature-cache` branch; per-file paths are in `sources[*].read_from`.
- **Verification levels** follow the lanes memo's convention: `[FT]` full text read, `[API]`
  structured Europe PMC record read, `[secondary]` read in a review with the primary named. The
  pazopanib **median PFS of 19 months is `[secondary]`** — the primary abstract does not state one.
- **The model is a model.** It has two compartments, one drug, a logistic kernel, no spatial
  structure, no immune term, no pharmacokinetics, and no vasculature — which is a real omission for
  an anti-angiogenic and is the reason §3's fatal falsifier is about the *existence* of the
  compartments rather than their parameters.
- **Whether a cost of resistance is required is contested** in the adaptive-therapy literature and
  is model-form-dependent. This analysis reports what the stated model returns, sweeps two other
  parameters to show the rank is robust within that form, and flags that a different kernel can
  change it. It does not claim a general result.
- **n.** The largest prospective EMC dataset anywhere in this analysis is 22 evaluable patients.
