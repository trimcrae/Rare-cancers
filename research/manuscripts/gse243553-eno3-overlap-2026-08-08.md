## ⛔ 0 · Two constraints, before any number

These are not caveats to be relegated to a limitations paragraph. They are conditions on reading
any sentence in this note, they are carried inside the machine-readable artifact so a reader who
quotes only the result cannot drop them, and a test fails the build if they are removed.

1. **The data is HEK293T, and accessibility is not binding.** GSE243553 expresses each fusion
   ectopically in an engineered cell line the authors call a "disease-agnostic biosensor". A
   called accessibility interval says a region became open there. It does **not** say the fusion
   protein binds there, and it says **nothing directly about EMC tumour material.**
2. ***ENO3* is this manuscript's own designated POSITIVE CONTROL** (§2.4 — "UP on both platforms
   — the positive control"). Anything found at *ENO3* therefore **validates the instrument**. It
   is not an unexpected discovery and must never be written as one.

## 1 · The result, first

**The overlap does not survive calibration, and the arm the question was asked about could not
have answered it.** Three findings, in the order they bind:

| arm | intervals | ENO3 NBREs covered | informative? | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| **EWSR1-NR4A3** | 1,119 | **0 of 4** | **NO** (2 of 203 background promoters) | ⚠ **ABSENT READING** — the zero is not evidence |
| **TAF15-NR4A3** | 8,525 | **3 of 4** | yes (16 of 203) | ⚠ **does NOT clear a geometry-preserving null** (*p* = 0.0803) |
| **TCF12-NR4A3** | 1,411 | 2 of 4 | **NO** (2 of 203) | not graded |
| **TFG-NR4A3** | 2,272 | **0 of 4** | **NO** (4 of 203) | ⚠ absent reading — **and this is the fusion the published *ENO3* evidence used** |
| NR4A3 (full-length wild type) | — | — | — | no interval file exists (§6) |
| NR4A3-EWSR1 (reciprocal) | — | — | — | no interval file exists (§6) |

**So *ENO3* does NOT move from "elevated in EMC" to "opened by the fusion".** The honest
statement is narrower and is worth having: the orthogonal instrument now exists, it was queried
at the exact coordinates on a matching genome build, and it returns **one co-location that a
proper null explains and three readings too sparse to grade.**

⭐ **The null that decided this is the whole story, and it is the reason the first answer was
wrong.** Two of *ENO3*'s four exact NBREs sit **153 bp apart**, and the paper's accessibility
intervals are **500 bp wide**, so a single interval covers both. Nulls that draw four
*independent* positions therefore score that as two successes and report *p* = 0.0019 and
*p* = 0.00005. A null that slides the **whole four-site configuration at its true spacing** to a
random offset inside the same window — which carries both the promoter's own openness *and* the
clustering — gives ***p* = 0.0803 (1,605 of 20,000 draws)**. Same data, same seed discipline,
a 40-fold difference, and only the last one is a test of the claim actually being made.

body is not the payload as `ok_but_not_the_file` **with the body kept** — a different diagnosis
from `not_retrieved`, pointing at a different fix.

✅ **The publisher's own copy serves them.** Springer static-content ESM for

⭐ **Three shut doors are not an absence** (CLAUDE.md §4). Had this note stopped at the first

## 3 · The genome build, established rather than assumed

An intersection computed across builds is meaningless, so the build was read from both sides
before any interval was compared.

| side | build | how it was established |
|---|---|---|
| the deposit | **hg38** | its own `!Sample_data_processing` lines: `Assembly: hg38`, and "Reads were then aligned to the hg38 reference genome and quantified using `cellranger-atac count`" (`cellranger-atac mkfastq` 2.1.0) |
| the NBRE coordinates | **GRCh38** | `assembly` field of [`emc-ret-target-scan.json`](../modalities/emc-ret-target-scan.json) → `part_1_nbre_scan.focus_genes.ENO3` |

**They agree, so no liftover was needed** — and that agreement is recorded as a reading, in
`genome_build.builds_agree_no_liftover_needed`, rather than left implicit.

⚠ **A defect found by reading the output rather than trusting it.** The first build scanner
reported `hg38: 0, hg19: 0, GRCh38: 0` for a deposit whose own processing line printed
`Assembly: hg38` three lines above it in the same artifact. Cause: `"\n".join(text)` over a
**string** interleaves the separator between every **character**, so `hg38` was scanned as
`h\ng\n3\n8`. Fail-quiet in the worst direction — without the per-line dump printed beside it,
"no build token found" would have read as "the deposit declares no build".

## 4 · The coordinates, and where they come from

Per CLAUDE.md §1 these are **derived, never typed**. Their one home is the committed output of the
same NBRE scan §3.10 and SI §S6 report. The module reads that artifact, derives genomic intervals
from the window arithmetic, and **re-derives each hit's `offset_from_tss` as a check** — a
mismatch raises rather than emitting a coordinate nobody verified.

*ENO3*, GRCh38, `chr17`, TSS **4,948,034**, plus strand, window **4,938,034–4,963,034**
(−10 kb/+15 kb, strand-aware, fixed before any sequence was read):

| site | interval (GRCh38, half-open) | motif strand | offset from TSS |
|---|---|---|---:|
| NBRE 1 | `chr17:4939295-4939303` | + | **−8,739** (the only upstream one) |
| NBRE 2 | `chr17:4950793-4950801` | + | +2,759 |
| NBRE 3 | `chr17:4950946-4950954` | − | +2,912 |
| NBRE 4 | `chr17:4951696-4951704` | − | +3,662 |

⚠ **Two conventions in the source artifact were undocumented and are now pinned by a test**,
because getting either wrong moves every coordinate silently: the **window is strand-aware**
(upstream 10 kb / downstream 15 kb, mirrored for a minus-strand gene) while **`offset_from_tss` is
a plain genomic difference**, not strand-oriented. The first version of the derivation assumed the
opposite and was wrong by **29,657 bp on *KDR***, a minus-strand gene — caught by `--selftest`
before one byte was fetched.

⚠ **These are not the published positions, and §3.10/SI §S6 already say so.** Kim *et al.* report
**two NBREs upstream** of the *ENO3* TSS; this scan finds **one upstream and three downstream**,
because both papers numbered from their own promoter constructs. The three sites that turn out to
be covered below are **the downstream trio** — i.e. **not** the published pair.

## 5 · Method, and the four nulls

**Peak provenance.** The intervals are the **authors' own** `<FUSION>_markers.bed` calls
(median width **500 bp**), taken from the paper's supplementary data. Nothing here is a peak this
repository called.

**Query window: motif ± 250 bp**, a 508 bp interval. Justification, fixed before any count was
read: a called accessibility interval in this assay is a few hundred bp and a motif is 8 bp, so a
flank of about one interval-width asks "is the motif inside a called accessible region" rather
than "is it exactly at an interval's summit". ⭐ **The flank manufactured none of the hits**: all
three covered sites are **strictly inside** their intervals
(`4950539 ≤ 4950793 < 4950801 ≤ 4951039`; `4950539 ≤ 4950946 < 4950954 ≤ 4951039`;
`4951425 ≤ 4951696 < 4951704 ≤ 4951925`), so ±0 gives the same 3 of 4.

**Intersection is half-open on both sides**, and both boundaries are pinned by `--selftest` — an
inclusive/half-open mix is the classic silent off-by-one that turns abutting features into an
overlap.

**Informativeness gate, before any *p*.** A set that recovers (almost) no **arbitrary** gene
cannot fail to recover a chosen one, so its silence is an absent reading. The rule and its
thresholds (`min_panel_genes` 50, `min_panel_hit_rate` 0.02) are imported unchanged from
[`nr4a3-fusion-targets-occupancy.json`](../modalities/nr4a3-fusion-targets-occupancy.json), the
artifact behind §3.11's Table 9. ⚠ Those thresholds were set for **full peak catalogues**, and a
**differential marker** set is sparse by construction, so the rule is strict here. It is applied
unchanged rather than relaxed to fit, and the raw panel counts are printed beside every verdict so
a reader can regrade.

**The background panel** is the gene list this project assembled for the ATR/DDR concept universe
— the same panel §3.11 calibrates against, so the two readings are commensurable. ⚠ **203 genes
here, not 198**: the cistrome inputs cache resolves 211 on hg38 and this read removes the 8 focus
genes. The manuscript's 198 is the same panel as the **motif** scan resolved it. Same panel, two
resolutions; do not quote one figure for the other.

**Four nulls, all seeded, seeds `20260808` / `20260809` / `20260810`, 20,000 draws each,
*p* = (ge+1)/(n+1) so it can never print a zero the null size does not support:**

| # | null | what it controls for | what it misses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **arbitrary promoter, same geometry** — *ENO3*'s own 4-site configuration placed at the same relative offsets inside each of the 203 panel windows | geometry **and** clustering; deterministic, no seed | *n* = 203 caps resolution at *p* ≈ 0.005 |
| 2 | **seeded resampling in panel windows** — 4 uniform positions in a random panel gene's window | genome-wide interval density | assumes the 4 sites are independent; ignores *ENO3*'s own openness |
| 3 | **seeded resampling inside *ENO3*'s own window** | ***ENO3*'s promoter being open at all** | still assumes independence |
| 4 | ⭐ **rigid shift of the whole configuration inside *ENO3*'s own window** | **both** — openness *and* clustering | nothing material; **this is the one to quote** |

## 6 · The readings, arm by arm

### 6a · EWSR1-NR4A3 — the question cannot be answered from this instrument

**1,119 marker intervals. 0 of 4 NBRE sites covered. 0 intervals anywhere in *ENO3*'s 25 kb
window.** And that zero is **not a reading**:

- the set covers **2 of 203** background promoter windows (**0.99 %**), below the 2 % floor →
  `informative: false`;
- null 1 says an arbitrary promoter also scores 0 — **203 of 203** panel genes are at or above the
  observed value;
- 1,119 × 500 bp is **0.018 %** of the genome, so a specific 25 kb window is not expected to
  contain one.

⛔ **So "EWSR1-NR4A3 does not open *ENO3*" is exactly what this repository must not write.** The
instrument, at this depth, could not have said yes. **No *p* is emitted**, deliberately.

### 6b · TAF15-NR4A3 — a co-location that a proper null explains

**8,525 marker intervals (the paper's own text says ≈8,600), `informative: true` at 16 of 203.
Three of *ENO3*'s four exact NBREs fall inside called accessibility intervals:**

| NBRE | offset | covering interval |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | −8,739 | — (not covered) |
| 2 | +2,759 | `chr17:4950539-4951039` |
| 3 | +2,912 | `chr17:4950539-4951039` — **the same interval** |
| 4 | +3,662 | `chr17:4951425-4951925` |

| null | *p* | at or above observed |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 · arbitrary promoter, same geometry | **0.0049** | 0 / 203 |
| 2 · resampling in panel windows | **0.00005** | 0 / 20,000 |
| 3 · resampling inside *ENO3*'s own window | **0.0019** | 37 / 20,000 |
| 4 · ⭐ **rigid shift of the whole configuration** | **0.0803** | **1,605 / 20,000** |

⛔ **Read null 4 and stop.** Nulls 2 and 3 score NBRE 2 and NBRE 3 as two independent successes
when one 500 bp interval covers both — they are 153 bp apart. Null 4 preserves the spacing and the
answer changes by a factor of ~40. **`0.0803` does not clear 0.05, so the co-location is
consistent with where two intervals happen to sit in an open promoter.**

⚠ **Null 1 is significant and null 4 is not, and that is not a contradiction to resolve by
picking the smaller number.** Null 1 asks "would *another* gene's promoter have looked like this",
so it also carries *ENO3*'s promoter being unusually open — which is a fact about *ENO3*, not
about the NBREs. Null 4 asks the question §3.10 actually poses: **given that this promoter is
open, are the NBRE positions where the openness landed?** They are not, at this resolution.

### 6c · TCF12-NR4A3 and TFG-NR4A3

**TCF12-NR4A3**: 2 of 4 covered (NBRE 2 and 3, by the same interval `chr17:4950539-4951039`), but
1,411 intervals hitting **2 of 203** panel promoters → `informative: false`, **no *p* emitted**.

**TFG-NR4A3**: **0 of 4**, 2,272 intervals hitting **4 of 203** → `informative: false`.

⚠ **And *TFG*::*NR4A3* is the fusion the published *ENO3* evidence used.** SI Table S2 records
*ENO3*'s class-A evidence as Kim *et al.*, PMID 26310886 — EMSA, ChIP at the endogenous promoter,
luciferase reporter and two NBRE motifs, all in cells over-expressing **TFG-TEC**, the t(3;9) EMC
variant. **The one arm that matches the published experiment is one of the arms too sparse to
grade.** That is the sharpest thing this exercise found and it belongs in the record.

### 6d · The two zero-peak controls

The supplement carries a `<FUSION>_markers.bed` for **32** of the library's variants. **Neither
full-length wild-type NR4A3 nor the reciprocal NR4A3-EWSR1 is among them**, which is what a
variant with no differentially accessible peaks looks like here and is consistent with the paper's
report of zero for both.

⛔ **This is not a measured zero at these coordinates.** A file that does not exist was not
queried. The defensible sentence is *"the fusion arms have interval sets and the two controls have
none"*, never *"the controls were tested at the NBRE sites and came back empty"*.

## 7 · What §3.10 should now say — proposed replacement text

§3.10's current closing paragraph reads, in part: *"**A motif is not occupancy** — only a
chromatin experiment shows binding, and §3.11 records that none exists for any NR4A3 fusion."*
**The second half of that sentence is false as of 2026-08-08** and must not survive into a
submission. Proposed replacement for that clause and the sentences around it — to be integrated
by whoever owns §3.10, **not applied here**:

> **A motif is not occupancy**, and the chromatin experiment that would test it now exists, was
> run, and does not support the sites. Frenkel *et al.* expressed EWSR1-NR4A3, TAF15-NR4A3,
> TCF12-NR4A3 and TFG-NR4A3 in HEK293T inside a pooled variant library and resolved each one's
> effect on chromatin accessibility by single-cell ATAC (GSE243553; PMID 39048711). Their
> per-fusion accessibility calls were intersected with the four exact NBRE coordinates above, on
> the matching genome build — the deposit declares hg38 and these coordinates are GRCh38, so no
> lift-over was required. **Three of the four sites fall inside TAF15-NR4A3's called intervals
> and none falls inside EWSR1-NR4A3's.** Neither observation supports the motif argument. The
> EWSR1-NR4A3 set is too sparse for its zero to be a reading — it recovers 2 of 203 promoters in
> a background gene panel assembled for an unrelated question, so a chosen gene could not have
> been recovered either — and the TAF15-NR4A3 co-location does not clear a null that slides the
> same four-site configuration, at its true spacing, to a random offset within the same window
> (*p* = 0.08, 20,000 seeded draws). That null is the one that matters here because two of the
> four sites are 153 bp apart and a single 500 bp interval covers both; nulls treating them as
> independent return *p* ≤ 0.002 and overstate the result by a factor of about forty. The
> TFG-NR4A3 arm — the fusion Kim *et al.* used for the published *ENO3* result — is likewise too
> sparse to grade. ⛔ Three conditions bound all of this: the cells are HEK293T and not EMC
> chromatin, accessibility is not binding, and *ENO3* is this study's own positive control, so a
> hit there would have validated the instrument rather than discovered anything. **The sequence
> axis and the chromatin axis have now been made to meet at these coordinates, and they do not
> corroborate each other.**

**Three consequential edits follow.**

1. **§3.10's fourth caveat needs one more clause.** "A distal element outside the window is
   untested by construction" is still true; add that the elements **inside** the window have now
   been tested against an orthogonal instrument and were not corroborated.
2. **§3.11's absence claim is the one that changes most**, and that is
   [the cistrome search note](./nr4a3-cistrome-search-2026-08-08.md) §7's business, not this
   note's. This note only adds that the experiment was not merely *found* but *queried*.
3. **§4.3's discriminating experiment survives and sharpens.** It is not "a fusion cistrome" in
   the abstract; it is **occupancy of an NR4A3 fusion in EMC chromatin**. A HEK293T accessibility
   screen has now been spent on the question and returned nothing that discriminates.

## 8 · What is still open, and what it would cost

- **The 80.1 GB of fragments have not been read.** A per-arm insertion-density read at these
  coordinates is possible at $0 and is a *weaker* instrument than the peak calls used here — it
  would bypass the authors' QC and differential test. It is worth doing only if the marker sets
  are suspected of being filtered in a way that drops the *ENO3* locus.
- **A different flank or a different window would give a different count**, and the flank here was
  fixed in advance and shown not to have manufactured any hit. Sweeping it after the fact would be
  the analysis this repository's own null machinery exists to refuse.
- **bioRxiv's supplementary-material page is still `NOT RETRIEVED`** (HTTP 429 × 3). It is
  unlikely to hold anything the Springer ESM does not, but it is recorded as unread rather than
  as empty.
- ⛔ **Nothing here bears on EMC tissue.** The measurement that would is unchanged and unbought:
  occupancy of an NR4A3 fusion in EMC chromatin, which no one has deposited.
