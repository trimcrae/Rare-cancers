## Pre-existing source ambiguities for the independent reader

These were not silently settled through an editorial rewrite and are not newly established scientific findings:

1. Vaccine Section 7 says that bounding cross-locus dependence would require haplotype-frequency data, while Section 2.3 already presents distribution-free bounds from marginals. The sentence-level relationship needs reconciliation against those existing definitions.
2. FO SI S6 calls 39 one-mismatch sites “the most of any gene scanned,” while main Section 3.10 says seven background windows equal or exceed that count (maximum 43). The intended scope appears to be the three focus genes; the source wording needs an explicit scope check.
3. FO Data availability labels the Haller dataset “referenced but not used as data,” while Section 3.11 reports its processed peaks in Table 9. This is an existing reporting inconsistency, not a reason to recompute the analysis.
4. FO Section 4.3's proposed occupancy experiment uses “is driven” for a gene with expression and a binding peak. Review that wording against the manuscript's own distinction between association, occupancy and causation; no new causal inference was introduced here.
