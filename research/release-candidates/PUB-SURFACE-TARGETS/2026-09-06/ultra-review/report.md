Independent scientific submission-readiness review

Verdict: **supported**. I found no current scientific submission blocker. The paper merits preprint submission as a limited, reproducible secondary analysis that prioritizes a specific tissue-localization question and exposes important comparator and sequencing-year failures. This is not an endorsement of therapeutic efficacy, normal sparing, diagnostic performance, or general EMC specificity.

They are disclosed prominently enough that they do not invalidate the claim actually made.

Evidence and scientific assessment

- **Estimands and rule:** The probability-of-superiority estimand, half-tie convention, equal weighting of histologies, and EMC-count weighting of supported sequencing years match the implementation. Marginal and matched estimates target different empirical populations, correctly kept separate. No gene-wise P values, adjusted population interpretation, or multiplicity-controlled discovery claim is made. The threshold is an allocation rule; treating year deletion as a qualifier instead of retroactively adding it to the rule is consistent with the frozen protocol.
- **Source and specimen provenance:** I independently rebuilt all 704 metadata records from the source Table S1 and author metadata. The nine eligible EMC specimens, three historical-case exclusions, recurrence exclusion, comparator counts, original-label policy, and sequencing-year support agree with the supplied manifest. The original paper explicitly defines samples as primary unless flagged. GSE24369 has six EMC biopsy accessions, 17 LGFMS, six MFS, six desmoid, five SFT, and two pooled muscle arrays. These are not retrospectively relabeled as confirmed primary lesions or individual normal donors. Complete cross-study patient independence cannot be established from these records; the manuscript appropriately says overlap-reduced/probable rather than proven independence.
- **Prespecification:** All eight scientific file hashes match the authorization record. Its recorded authorization time, 2026-09-06 15:30:59 UTC, precedes the first recorded empirical start, 15:31:37 UTC. The preserved initial protocol and replication amendment describe the rule, matched support, and like-histology replication axis used in the results. This supports an internally documented prospective-to-the-investigator contract for the new Hofvander values. A local timestamp and hash do not independently prove that no earlier target values were seen, nor establish public preregistration. The manuscript does not claim public registration or prospective collection, and distinguishes retrospective array evidence.
- **Arithmetic:** A new implementation reparsed the compressed full TPM matrix and original GEO SOFT, independently rebuilt metadata, and used sorted-pair ranks rather than importing the authors' scientific functions. It checked 23,600 fields, including 8,448 TPM values, 504 array values, all 12 genes' main/context estimates and primary deletion calculations, all 48 array shared-histology effects, 696 array single-biopsy deletion values, and the allocation rule. There were no mismatches (numeric comparison tolerance 1e-12). All 12 selected original-platform annotation rows resolve to the reported single gene symbol. CSPG4 alone passes: marginal A=0.8945416723 and matched A=0.8111111111. The 2019-deleted matched result of 0.4333333333 and DFSP marginal result of 0.4666666667 are correct.
- **Uncertainty:** Independent multinomial resampling of histology/year stratum counts, sharing EMC weights across comparisons, used 10,000 draws and seed 2026090601. CSPG4 percentile intervals were marginal [0.8680866459, 0.9178763623] and matched [0.7527777778, 0.8611111111]. The slight marginal difference from the 2,000-draw manuscript interval is compatible with Monte Carlo variation; matched endpoints agree. These remain conditional empirical bootstrap intervals with fixed singleton cells. I do not interpret their narrowness as evidence of population precision or batch robustness. The text explicitly explains this limitation and gives the deletion reversal adequate prominence.
- **Prior work and novelty:** The CHRNA6 RNA CISH evidence is credited and kept outside the panel's discovery/pass count. The original reported 25 EMC cases and 69 below-threshold mimic cases are consistent with the [Dulken primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38447752/). Hofvander's primary article addresses transcriptomic tumor subgroups and does not name CSPG4, CHRNA6, or PRAME in its text. The present contribution is an incremental, comparator-explicit reanalysis with a new-to-this-analysis cohort and falsifying sensitivity results, not a new therapeutic target discovery. That scope is sufficient for a preprint. I did not establish exhaustive priority across all literature, and the article does not require an absolute novelty claim.
- **Contrary and normal evidence:** Original GSE28866 peak 46886 contains CSPG4 4.302301323 in EMC_STT5592 and 6.548826073 in adult-colon STT5610; the contrary individual comparison is real. Original CHRNA6 peak values are nonuniform. Original Cammareri workbook rows 128-132 are negative in both reader columns; four rows have recorded fusion/rearrangement evidence. The paper correctly distinguishes these five negative protein observations from paired RNA/protein testing or universal absence. The [Cammareri primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37477762/) also supports the comparator-context discussion.
