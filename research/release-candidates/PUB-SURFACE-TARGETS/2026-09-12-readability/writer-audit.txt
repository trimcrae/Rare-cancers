## Plain-language summary

We compared publicly available RNA measurements to identify genes worth studying in extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma, a rare cancer. CSPG4 was the only tested candidate that met the selection rule. Its RNA levels tended to be higher than those in several comparison cancers. But comparisons within measurement years involved only four distinct patients, and removing one year's measurements reversed the combined result. Another comparison cancer also gave a contrary result. Normal tissues can express CSPG4, too. The next question is where its protein sits in cancer tissue. These findings do not show that a treatment would work or spare normal tissues.
