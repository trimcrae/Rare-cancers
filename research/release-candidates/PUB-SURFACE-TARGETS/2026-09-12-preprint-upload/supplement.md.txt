# Supplementary information for CSPG4 tissue RNA enrichment in extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma

The scientific measurements, frozen protocols and original reproducibility archive are unchanged from Research Square version 1 (https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10959636/v1). The complete public code/data archive is https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-10959636/v1/b95606f3a3799c60c8ac4434.zip. ## Source inventory and specimen scope

This supplement records which specimens were included, what each assay measured, and how the comparisons can be reproduced. EMC means extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma. The comparator abbreviations are MLPS (myxoid liposarcoma), LGFMS (low-grade fibromyxoid sarcoma), MFS (myxofibrosarcoma), DFSP (dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans) and SFT (solitary fibrous tumor). HPA is the Human Protein Atlas. TPM means transcripts per million; RMA is the robust multi-array average method used for the released array signals. IHC means immunohistochemistry, and ICC/IF means immunocytochemistry/immunofluorescence. These antibody-based assays provide context on protein staining and location; they are distinct from the RNA measurements.

Each selected array probe maps consistently to one gene across all of its transcript-cluster assignments. We do not average probes, choose a best probe, substitute a gene alias or filter features according to expression. Finite signed log values are valid array signals; TPM must be finite and nonnegative. Zero observations remain ties. Missing or duplicate observations trigger a recorded technical failure instead of deletion or imputation. The actual runs completed without these failures.

The score A averages pairwise comparisons of RNA values. For EMC values x_i and comparator values y_j, A=(1/nm) Σ_iΣ_j[I(x_i>y_j)+0.5I(x_i=y_j)]. Here, n and m are the numbers of EMC and comparator specimens, and I equals 1 when its stated condition is true and 0 otherwise. Tied pairs contribute one half. Pair counts are not independent-patient counts.

The marginal summary is the mean of the 3 histology scores. For the matched summary, we first calculate the score within each exact year. Within each histology, we then weight these year scores by the number of supported EMC specimens, before averaging equally across histologies. A common EMC specimen can contribute to several histology comparisons. Bootstrap sampling preserves that dependence by sharing the specimen's resample across comparisons. No cross-platform scale or patient pooling is performed.

Bootstrap resampling used seed 20260906 and 2000 replicates within histology/year groups. The conditional 95% percentile intervals are pointwise: each describes one estimate rather than simultaneous coverage of the full family of estimates. They were not used to select genes. For CSPG4, the MLPS matched interval has identical endpoints, [0.66667, 0.66667]. This reflects sparse observed groups and the resampling design, not absence of biological uncertainty. The files identify all single-specimen groups and every patient's placement. Independent 5000-draw resampling reproduced the main matched interval endpoints. That numerical check does not remove small-sample limitations.

The rule allocates follow-up effort only to a gene meeting all three conditions: marginal composite≥0.70; each primary marginal/matched histology>0.5; and both summaries>0.5 after each single EMC and histology deletion. Removing sequencing years or comparator specimens and using revised diagnoses qualify interpretation; they are not extra pass criteria. After a deletion, the remaining supported-year weights are normalized again. A lost comparison remains undefined. Complete-case selection or imputation is not performed. No new replication success cutoff was added. The separate cross-cohort output compares score direction and size against LGFMS primarily, and MFS/SFT/desmoid secondarily.
