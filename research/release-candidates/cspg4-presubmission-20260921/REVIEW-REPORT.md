# CSPG4 pre-submission review — September 21, 2026

The revised draft retains the original nine-EMC versus 393-reference result and adds two explicitly dated exploratory analyses. Independent scientific review at actual ultra reasoning effort, numerical review, and source review found no substantiated scientific or numerical blocker within their scopes. These are AI-assisted internal reviews, not journal peer review or external expert endorsement.

## Changes made

- Added nominal 95% specimen-bootstrap uncertainty: primary A=0.84648, interval 0.70908–0.95759. Independent weighted-ECDF calculations reproduced all 50,000 draws and exact interval endpoints.
- Added one broader source-defined Malignant/Intermediate sensitivity: 489 references / 37 labels, A=0.82436, interval 0.67780–0.94274. The original 393-reference exact test and 11-gene correction are unchanged. No new P value or favorable-comparator search was added.
- Brought all 38 source Malignant/Intermediate diagnosis labels into Figure 1, with GIST and DFSP and source-class distinctions visible.
- Made Figure 2 a direct comparison of the four EMC contrasts shared across RNA sequencing and arrays. The broader external LMS heatmap is preserved as Figure S2.
- Added source molecular support for every retained EMC: 7 explicit NR4A3 fusions, 1 FUS::NR4A2, 1 without a filtered specimen-crosswalked fusion record. Source diagnoses and frozen exclusions remain unchanged. Grade/purity are unavailable for all 9.
- Clarified CHRNA6's historical control result, the rank test's common-distribution null, reference weighting and the standalone scientific contribution. Preserved adverse year-conditioned findings.
- Kept the improved structured abstract concise (222 words excluding headings), added uncertainty, standardized median rounding, and ordered references by first use. Current Cancer Genetics guide allows 250 words, 1–7 keywords and 3–5 highlights of 85 characters.

## Findings that did not justify changing the evidence

No incorrect accepted result, hidden favorable-specimen selection, external EMC replication claim, RNA/protein equivalence, or universal NR4A3 confirmation claim was established. The September 21 initial freeze pointed at retained pre-OpenPedCan top-level CSVs; it was corrected to the already accepted final paths. Accepted final tables and public archive had not drifted. No new broad source search or specimen reclassification was performed.

## Remaining limits

The contribution remains an incremental, exploratory descriptive RNA reanalysis. Nine EMC specimens limit precision. Reference definitions, incomplete cross-study patient identities, variable assays and bulk tissue composition constrain interpretation. Approximate pointwise bootstrap intervals do not correct retrospective selection or unmeasured confounding. The additional external collections contain no explicitly labeled EMC group. No diagnostic performance, protein targeting, safety or treatment claim follows.

The package is prepared for author review. Journal account handoff, current-title/date author disclosure certification, final submission approval and submission remain separate. Existing provider preprints remain historical versions; none was reposted. The earlier accepted draft and its evidence are preserved.
