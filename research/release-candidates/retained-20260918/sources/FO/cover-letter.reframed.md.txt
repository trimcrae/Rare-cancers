# Cover letter draft

Dear Editors,

Please consider “Expression portability of reported NR4A3-fusion targets in extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma” as an Original Research Article for Cancer Genetics.

This public-data reanalysis asks how expression of three reported NR4A3-fusion targets varies across EMC cohorts and comparator histologies. ENO3 remains elevated across the reported comparator and reference-pool checks. PPARG's strongest array result reuses its discovery dataset, while SEMA3C changes direction with the comparator. The contribution is a qualified account of expression portability, not discovery of ENO3 elevation or proof of fusion causation.

The heterogeneous fusion-plus-native target aggregate and its random-set comparisons remain as complementary analyses, including their negative result. They do not establish whether EMC has a fusion-driven transcriptional programme or introduce a new statistical method. The bounded chromatin search and surrogate comparisons retain their stated source limits. All underlying numerical findings are unchanged.

The underlying report is public on aiXiv, including version 1.1: https://aixiv.science/abs/aixiv.260823.000001. This journal adaptation reorganizes existing computational findings and adds no new scientific analysis. The manuscript discloses funding, competing interests—including the author’s non-financial interest as an EMC survivor—and AI assistance.

Sincerely,
