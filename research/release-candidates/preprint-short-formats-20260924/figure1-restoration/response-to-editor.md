# Response to the editor

Manuscript CG-D-26-00971

CSPG4 RNA across sarcoma types with an expanded comparison of extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma

Dear Editor,

Thank you for identifying a more suitable format for this descriptive study. I have shortened the manuscript to a Letter focused on the comparative CSPG4 RNA finding in extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma.

The revised narrative presents the source-defined comparison, its uncertainty, the all-12 and all-13 specimen sensitivities, the cross-assay LGFMS contrast and the higher-expression GIST and DFSP comparisons. The detailed methods, broader atlas and supporting results are retained in the Supplementary Information and frozen data/code archive. The principal cross-sarcoma figure is retained as Figure 1; the remaining three figures accompany the supplement. The numerical results are unchanged.

I agree that the study does not establish malignant-cell localization, CSPG4 protein abundance or functional relevance in EMC. The Letter states these limits in one concluding paragraph and identifies tissue-level protein assessment as the next biological question. It makes no claim of therapeutic suitability or diagnostic specificity.

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript as a Letter.

Sincerely,

Tristan D. McRae
