Graphical abstract. The primary catalogue contains 190 designs across 38 modelled NR4A3 exon-3 fusion junctions. For two adjacent 16-mer registers at TAF15 e6::NR4A3 e3, the longest predicted contiguous pairing spanning all six DNA-gap positions is 9 bp with mature TFG and 11 bp with mature NR4A3, respectively. Both maxima come from the same six-parent screen. The adopted 10-bp criterion is met by 45.8% of primary designs and 40.6% of artificial exon joins; this comparison does not establish an EMC-specific excess. The sequence classification does not establish cleavage or biological selectivity. Exon-2 acceptors require separately defined sequences, and correspondence to published patient-derived model junctions remains unresolved.
