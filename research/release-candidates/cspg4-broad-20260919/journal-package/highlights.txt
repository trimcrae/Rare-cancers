Nine EMC specimens were compared with 393 same-source malignant tumor specimens.
CSPG4 RNA was higher in EMC than in this defined reference mixture.
The exact rank-sum P value was 0.000130; Bonferroni-11 P was 0.00143.
High CSPG4 RNA was not unique to EMC across the full tumor atlas.
External sarcoma cohorts add context while retaining separate measurement scales.
