# ASO short manuscript format decision

The main manuscript should be a letter or brief research communication. The two concrete contributions are a reference-annotation correspondence for USZ20 and additional isoform matches in 9 of 35 existing designs. Public-data source resolution and sequence comparison alone do not warrant an expanded biological story. The author's Cancer Genetics decision on the separate CSPG4 manuscript supplies a persuasive editorial precedent for matching length to this scope, but does not constitute an invitation or acceptance for ASO.

The new author-review document uses a continuous letter narrative with four references. Detailed methods, complete result tables and adverse evidence remain in the accepted supplement. It has no abstract or figures. It is not a completed journal-specific submission package, and its page count does not predict publisher pagination.

NAT Brief Communication is an explicit alternative if page charges are acceptable. Format eligibility is distinct from scientific acceptance; limited novelty remains the substantive editorial risk.
