# Concise ASO letter document contract

The deliverable is a substantive condensation for author review. It is not a publisher-typeset article or a submission-ready journal-specific package. The accepted September 26 manuscript and all underlying analyses remain preserved. No large source archive is copied or recomputed.

## Layout

Use the same familiar Times New Roman typography and US Letter geometry as the user's Cancer Genetics letter: 0.75 inch top and bottom margins, 0.85 inch side margins, and 17 point black title. The new ASO document is created independently; the Cancer Genetics document is a typographic precedent, not a content or package template. No figures, relationships or private correspondence from that manuscript are imported.

Use 12 point body text at 1.15 spacing, with compact 10.5 point references and declarations. This intentionally differs from the precedent's 1.4 body spacing. Obtain the requested length principally by cutting the narrative, not by shrinking its font. Target two pages including title, author information, declarations and references. Preserve every scientific qualifier needed to interpret the two main findings. Detailed accepted methods and complete result tables remain in the existing eight-page supplement.

## Verification

Render with the documents skill's canonical renderer and inspect every page image. Record actual page count and final hashes. Check document text against the Markdown source. Have the existing independent advisor check only whether condensation changed claims or lost necessary qualifiers; reuse the accepted scientific analyses and ultra review. Any journal-specific abstract, title-page arrangement and mandatory declaration headings will be applied after a venue is selected. The two-page author copy cannot guarantee two published pages.
