# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider "Transcript provenance changes fusion junction antisense comparisons in extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma" as an Original Research article in Computational Biology and Chemistry.

This computational study resolves a published EMC model's fusion-junction annotation against versioned sequence references and tests how additional parent-gene isoforms change antisense sequence comparisons. Published coordinates connect USZ20-EMC1 to a reference junction already represented in an existing catalogue, despite different exon labels. The companion model remains unresolved. An expanded normal-parent corpus lengthens gap-spanning matches for 9 of 35 source-linked designs, including an accession-confirmed alternative NR4A3 splice match.

The contribution is a traceable model correspondence and reproducible, source-specific sequence audit. It is not a new general theory of antisense specificity, a patient-coverage estimate or a claim of selective cleavage. The supplementary files preserve source classes, negative results, accession versions, inputs, code and every per-design comparison.

The work substantially revises our preceding ASO resource. Public versions are recorded on Qeios, including version 4 posted on 15 September 2026 (10.32388/VL3LJR.4) and earlier versions under 10.32388/VL3LJR, 10.32388/VL3LJR.2 and 10.32388/VL3LJR.3. Preceding data are archived under 10.5281/zenodo.22229096. This revision corrects the prior interpretation of the USZ20 model figure and adds the parent-isoform analysis. The historical records are disclosed rather than treated as primary evidence for the new claims. A correction record accompanies the supporting material.

I am the sole author and an unaffiliated independent researcher. No external funding supported the work. I have no competing financial interests and disclose EMC survivorship as a nonfinancial interest. AI assistance is described in the Methods and declaration. No oligonucleotides were synthesized or experimentally tested. I request the subscription publication route and do not request paid print colour.
