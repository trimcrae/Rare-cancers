The initial 16 MiB mate-1 prefixes returned zero exact 16+16 junction anchors. They tested the primary and noncoding acceptor atlases but omitted the cryptic-exon atlas used by the Brenca T-N construct. The initial result is retained. After reading Brenca methods, add the existing cryptic-exon candidates and expand to a fixed maximum first 64 MiB per run (two runs), without reducing anchor length or quality criterion. This is a positive-evidence probe; no negative conclusion follows. No whole run or whole transcriptome is downloaded.
