# Evidence-driven corrections to carry into any future integrated revision

Existing accepted and published files are preserved. This record identifies the precise changes justified by the new evidence; it is not itself a public correction or permission to publish one.

| Previous statement or interpretation | New evidence | Required replacement |
|---|---|---|
| The Zurich publication lacks nucleotide-level information sufficient to assess catalogue correspondence. | Figure 4b contains exact positions and protein IDs; their GRCh37 transcript parents resolve the labels to the catalogue EWSR1 e12/NR4A3 e3 boundary. | USZ20 supports a reference-reconstructed correspondence. A patient consensus is still unavailable. Treat USZ22 separately. |
| No relevant transcript model can explain the reported NR4A3 exon-2 acceptor as the usual coding acceptor. | The actual displayed ENSP00000333122 maps to ENST00000330847.1, whose exon 2 starts at chr9:102590323. The previous check considered curated RefSeq isoforms, not this displayed Ensembl transcript. | Exon numbering really does explain this acceptor in the displayed annotation. The donor label also shifts because the displayed EWSR1 transcript includes an additional upstream exon. |
| The TCF12 e5/NR4A3 e3 centred design has a longest mature-parent match of 7 bp. | This remains correct for the original six-transcript corpus, but the alternative NR4A3 transcript extends it to 11 bp; two adjacent designs rise from 8/11 to 12/13 bp. | State the corpus beside every metric. Do not carry the original values into a claim about all parent isoforms. |
| Primers anchored in retained NR4A3 exon 3 structurally cannot recover an upstream cryptic-exon fusion. | Independent advisor identified that a reverse primer within retained exon 3 can amplify such a fusion with an upstream donor primer. | Remove that general explanation unless original primer orientation and sequence establish exclusion. Deposit absence still does not estimate prevalence. |

The hypothetical EWSR1 e13/NR4A3 e2 designs remain valid as conditional sequences. They are not the reference reconstruction supported by USZ20 Figure 4b. Their historical results need not be deleted; their correspondence label must be corrected wherever it was asserted.

The new work does not recompute or replace the prior 190-design aggregate (87/190 at the adopted ten-base criterion) or the artificial-join result (40.6%). Those remain results for their specified historical inputs. A complete update of that resource would require applying the expanded corpus to its full panel and controls together; this investigation intentionally reports its source-linked 35-design subset separately.
