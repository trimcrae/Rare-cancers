# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider “NR4A3 fusion-junction antisense gapmers for extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma: reagents, test articles and a pre-registrable knockdown experiment” as an Original Paper in Nucleic Acid Therapeutics.

This computational study specifies two proposed fusion-junction gapmers and screened controls, evaluates predicted sequence specificity, and sets out a pre-registrable knockdown experiment for extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma. It provides a reproducible starting point for testing fusion-directed antisense reagents in a rare cancer with limited research resources. No oligonucleotides were synthesised or tested; the manuscript makes no demonstrated potency, delivery, safety or therapeutic-efficacy claim.

Versions of the preceding preprint were posted on Qeios (10.32388/VL3LJR and 10.32388/VL3LJR.2). This submission is a subsequent condensed revision. An earlier analysis was withdrawn after a coding-versus-transcript exon-indexing error. The manuscript describes that correction history; Supplementary File 2 distinguishes the present interpretations and supplement from the preceding public analysis archive (10.5281/zenodo.22229096). The supplied files contain the current submission text and supporting data.

I am the sole author and an unaffiliated independent researcher. The work was self-funded. I have no competing financial interests; I disclose my EMC survivorship as a nonfinancial interest. The manuscript describes AI assistance, and I take responsibility for its content. Print colour reproduction is not requested.
