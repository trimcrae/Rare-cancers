Dear Editor,

Please consider this computational proposal as an Original Paper. It applies the in-silico half of step one of the off-target framework discussed in the manuscript to NR4A3 fusion-junction gapmers for extraskeletal myxoid chondrosarcoma. Its practical contribution is a reproducible design and specificity-screening procedure, two named research reagents, two screened controls (Table 2), and a proposed knockdown experiment with explicit falsification criteria.

The manuscript retains negative results and screening limitations, including parent-transcript liabilities and an exact genomic match. Experimental breakpoint sequencing is required before ordering for a test article.
