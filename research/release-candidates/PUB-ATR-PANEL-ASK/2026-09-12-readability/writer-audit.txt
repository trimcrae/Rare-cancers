## The readability failure

ASO's original source already passes the sentence ceiling, yet starts by assuming the reader knows fusion-junction gapmers, parents, catalytic gaps, transcript compartments and null ensembles. Its short sentences do not provide the conceptual bridge between binding predictions and actual RNA cleavage. The repair defines the objects and then keeps separate the molecule's DNA-gap length, the duplex-length cutoff, the search cutoff and the unmeasured biological effect.

ATR's original abstract and opening paragraphs assume the reader knows FET, RG/RGG, recruitment, the retained-RG axis and reading-frame assembly. The paper's careful caveats are present but often arrive after dense sequences of model and artifact descriptions. The repair introduces those concepts through the study's question, states the purpose of each method, and leads result paragraphs with their finding. It also separates the four possible recruitment outcomes into distinct paragraphs while keeping every causal limitation.

This confirms why a numeric readability pass is insufficient: both source manuscripts already have **zero sentences above the configured 60-word ceiling**. The scientific-writing skill was applied as an actual editorial workflow, not a score target.

## Caution accounting

Exact raw-marker counts and every paragraph-level marker decrease are retained in `verification.json`; before/after passages are in the change maps. A change in a regex token is not itself a changed qualification. No qualification was intentionally removed. The initial ATR source-to-proposal screen decreased from 70 to 66 markers; after the first independent-review repairs it was 70 to 67, and after the focused abstract continuation it is 70 to 68. Both decreases are disclosed rather than repinned or hidden.

ASO has one lexical substitution: “transcriptomics is not performed” becomes “We did not perform transcriptomics.” The null is unchanged. The all-prose screen rises from 32 to 39 markers.

ATR decreases occur in these specific expressions:

1. “does not require the driver to be drugged” becomes “could be tested without directly targeting the driver”; the conditional rationale is retained, with no treatment claim.
2. “rests neither on one assumed junction nor on one fixed window” becomes “removes dependence on one unpinned breakpoint or fixed window.” The same scope claim follows the same symmetric grid.
3. “Three reported variants are not modelled” becomes “could not be modelled from the sources held here.” All three reasons remain; this is a sourcing limit.
4. The first proposal incorrectly changed “Neither EMC type falls outside” to a statement about all EMC models. That was not equivalent: type 5 lies at 0.367, outside the reported comparator span. Independent finding ATR-R2 caught the scope broadening, which this author's initial audit missed. The repaired sentence now explicitly names the EWSR1::NR4A3 type-1 and type-2 models only. The initial files remain in `review-initial/`, and `INDEPENDENT-REVIEW-REPAIRS.json` records the repair. “does not establish that recruitment was measured” is expressed as sequence placements, not recruitment measurements, with the unchanged measured anchors.
5. The stored-field paragraph combines two “is not” clauses with “Likewise” while retaining that 174 is not the largest complete-RG-free prefix and 13 is not its headroom.
6. “cases do not carry the lesion” becomes the explicitly transferred prediction that cases “lack the proposed lesion”; this remains untested.
7. The P2/P4 paragraph replaces “is not” with “do not”/“does not” and an explicit “limitation”; the one-experiment overlap, directional/two-sided distinction and P1 equivalence caveat remain.
8. “the tested constructs do not show recruitment” becomes “the tested constructs show no recruitment under these assay conditions.” The tested-condition null is unchanged.
9. “TCF12 length mismatch is not reconciled here” becomes “A TCF12 length mismatch remains unresolved.” No reconciliation is claimed.
10. Panel B's “is not a measured recruitment range” becomes “not a measured recruitment range.” The identical limitation remains.
11. Panel C's “does not reach 177” becomes “do not sum to 177.” The error accounting remains.
12. P3's “13 is not a second convention” is expressed by stating that the two-conventions description was wrong and identifying exactly where 13 comes from. The immutable record remains unchanged.
13. “a delayed curve cannot be told from a poorly expressed construct” becomes “controls are needed to distinguish delayed recruitment from poor expression.” The same interpretability requirement remains.

Numeric token decreases in ATR involve repeated 3' labels and the 50-residue grid start removed from the abstract. The grid start and ten-residue steps remain in Methods 2.3, Results 3.5 and Table 4; the abstract now explicitly limits the comparison to sampled lengths. Full table rows, nucleotide strings and all reference entries are identical, and every result's numeric value is retained in its result paragraph or original table. The checker deliberately separates those preservation checks from a naive count of digits in identifiers and repeated explanatory labels.
