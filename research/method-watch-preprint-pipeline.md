**RNAiSpline: A Deep learning model for siRNA efficacy prediction** — PPR, 2026-02-17

> RNA interference (RNAi) is a crucial biological post-transcriptional gene silencing mechanism where small interfering RNA (siRNA) guides RNA-induced silencing complex (RISC) to bind with messenger RNA (mRNA) thereby silencing it and stopping protein formation. We exploit this process to prevent the formation of harmful proteins by silencing mRNA before it is translated into protein through an effective siRNA. There exists a need to develop a computational model that predicts the effectiveness of siRNA on a given mRNA.  Design:   ing a model is challenging, as the data availability is either scarce or biased, and existing models lack generalization ability, even though the parameters to training samples ratio is very high.
