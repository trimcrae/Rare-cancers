FAP alpha is also known as prolyl endopeptidase FAP which modulates tissue remodelling, tumor growth, invasion, and immunosuppression. Further details are as follows:

FAPα is expressed at high levels in several types of tumors, making it a promising target for imaging and therapy
Among various tumor entities, solitary fibrous tumors (SFTs) have particularly high FAPα expression. SFTs are rare mesenchymal neoplasms
FAPI positron emission tomography/computed tomography (PET/CT) imaging has demonstrated increased tumor uptake in patients with SFTs, providing an improved detection efficacy compared to fluorodeoxyglucose (FDG) PET
FAPα is primarily located on the tumor cell surface in SFTs.

Expression beyond tumors: Interestingly, FAPα is not exclusive to tumors. It is also overexpressed in benign conditions, such as wound healing, fibrosis, arthritis, and atherosclerotic plaques. Inflammatory and immune-mediated pathologies may also exhibit FAPα expression. FAPI imaging holds potential for distinguishing between benign and malignant lesions in given clinical contexts.

## Clinical Applications
Fibroblast activation protein inhibitor (FAPI) PET/CT can detect a wide range of malignancies, including head-and-neck cancers, lung cancers, colorectal cancers, esophageal cancers, and breast cancers. It has been shown to detect 28 different types of malignancies in one study.[ ]
Notably, FAPI shows low background uptake in the liver, making it useful for detecting liver primary tumors and metastases.[ ]
In summary, FAPIs versatility extends beyond tumors, offering exciting possibilities for both diagnostic imaging and potential therapeutic applications. Further research will define its role in patient care.

## Method

## Radiopharmacy
Ge 68-Ga 68 generator (iTG: iQS Ga 68 Fluiding Labeling Module-semi automatic method) was used to obtain Ga 68 FAPI (The cold kit/carrier molecule being FAPI 46).
The cold kit was dispensed in Na acetate buffer (1.25 g Sodium acetate in 50 mL high-performance liquid chromatography water (HPLC) water) and preheated to 1050 C, as the reaction temperature.
The eluent used was 4 mL of 0.05M HCl. The reaction time was 10 min.
The C-8 Cartridge was used to purify the product after its preconditioning with 70% ethanol (35 mL ethanol + 15 mL HPLC water).
The product was then diluted with 5 mL saline and 5 mL 60% ethanol before IV administration to the patient, through the secured IV line.
The administered dose was 3–4 mCi per case, based on the weight of the patient (0.05–0.06 mCi/kg of body weight).

## Scintigraphy
As Gallium-68-FAPI is a new molecule, an ethics committee approval was obtained. Informed consent from all patients undergoing the test was procured. Whole-body imaging was acquired between 50- and 60-min postradiotracer administration using Siemens Horizon 16 slice PET CT system. Images were acquired at the rate of 120 s per bed. Intravenous contrast was not used. Delayed scan after diuretic administration was acquired for the evaluation of pyelonephritis. Images were reconstructed in short axis, sagittal, and coronal planes by Fourier rebinning (Fore) + filtered back projection method. Scans were read by one specialist and validated by second.

## Discussion

Fibroblast activation plays a crucial role in tissue repair and fibrosis; here are some key points: Fibroblast activation: Fibroblasts are mesenchymal cells involved in maintaining tissue integrity and acting as local progenitor reservoirs.[ ] In response to tissue injury, fibroblasts can transition into myofibroblasts, characterized by extracellular matrix secretion and actomyosin-based stress fiber contraction.[ ] Transient activation into myofibroblasts aids tissue repair, while persistent activation leads to pathological fibrosis[ , ]
Fibroblast behavior in tumor: Fibroblasts exhibit dynamic behavior during tumor progression. The process of fibroblast evolution in tumor cells is termed “stromagenesis.” Initially, fibroblasts may restrict or suppress tumor growth, but subsequent microenvironment changes promote tumor proliferation[ ]
FAP: Fibroblast activation protein is associated with tumor development and poor clinical outcomes in various cancers.[ , ] It localizes not only in malignant conditions but also in benign ones such as inflammatory arthritis, vasculitis, and cirrhosis.[ , ] Ga-68-FAPI imaging demonstrated utility across different entities as follows.

## Lymphoma
Cancer associated fibroblasts (CAFs) overexpress FAP, which is enriched in many epithelial carcinomas and hematologic neoplasms. Ga-68-FAPI PET/CT is a novel diagnostic tool that visualizes the tumor stroma by targeting FAP. A prospective study evaluated lymphoma patients using Ga-68-FAPI PET/CT and correlated FAP uptake with immunostaining intensity. Hodgkin lymphoma (HL) lesions showed significantly elevated FAP uptake, correlating with intense FAP immunostaining (score, 3+). Aggressive non-HL (NHL) lesions (moderate-to-strong FAP staining) exhibited intense to moderate Ga-68-FAPI uptake. Indolent NHL lesions showed weak FAP staining and mild-to-moderate Ga68-FAPI uptake.[ ] Ga-68-FAPI imaging can detect FAP expression in lymphoma lesions. It may serve as an alternate method for characterizing lymphoma profiles. Notably, Ga-68-FAPI PET/CT provides valuable information beyond traditional FDG imaging, especially in cases such as liver lymphoma with lower physiological FDG uptake.[ ] illustrates FAPI avid multiple infra-diaphragmatic nodes.
 

## Pyelonephritis

Acute pyelonephritis: Acute pyelonephritis is a bacterial infection of the kidney and renal pelvis. It should be suspected in patients with pyrexia, flank pain, and laboratory evidence of urinary tract infection. Urine culture with antimicrobial susceptibility testing guides antibiotic therapy
Imaging in pyelonephritis: Contrast-enhanced CT is the preferred modality for evaluating acute bacterial pyelonephritis. CT detects focal parenchymal abnormalities, defines disease extent, and identifies perinephric fluid collections or abscesses. When contrast or radiation is contraindicated (e. g., during pregnancy), ultrasonography and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) are alternative options
FAPI imaging: FAPI imaging demonstrated intense tracer uptake in the bilateral renal cortex. Renal pelvis does not show tracer uptake in view of postdiuretic delayed scan. MRI revealed altered renal cortical signals. Dimercaptosuccinic acid renal scan confirmed bilateral renal cortical cold areas consistent with pyelonephritis [ ].[ ]

 

## Ga-68-fibroblast Activation Protein Inhibitor Positron Emission Tomography/Computed Tomography in Ovarian Neoplasms
FAPI imaging demonstrates intense tracer uptake in bilateral adnexa (ovarian regions) in ovarian neoplasms. FAP expression is found in more than 90% of epithelial ovarian cancers, including serous and mucous subtypes. Notably, FAP is absent or low in normal ovarian tissue and benign conditions. Dendl K et al. reported variable FDG uptake in ovaries based on the stage of ovulation. In contrast, FAPI PET does not exhibit such variability, suggesting it’s promising role in ovarian neoplasm.[ ] Our case demonstrates avid uptake of FAPI in bilateral adnexa in ovarian neoplasm with synchronous lesion in the left breast [ ].
 In summary, Ga-68-FAPI PET/CT holds potential for accurate imaging in ovarian cancer.

## Renal Cell Carcinoma
Renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is the most common type of kidney cancer, arising from renal tubular epithelial cells. It accounts for approximately 90% of all kidney malignancies. The common subtypes include clear cell RCC, papillary RCC, and chromophobe RCC. FAPI imaging has shown promise in detecting RCC lesions. FAP expression is elevated in RCC tissue, making it a potential target for imaging. FAPI PET/CT can provide valuable information about tumor extent and guide treatment decisions. Early-stage RCC is often asymptomatic, but as the tumor grows, symptoms may include flank pain, hematuria, and palpable abdominal masses. Surgical resection remains the primary treatment for localized RCC. In advanced cases, targeted therapies (e.g., tyrosine kinase inhibitors) and immunotherapies are used. Accurate diagnosis and staging are crucial for managing RCC effectively. demonstrates pathological fracture in the neck of femur with few FAPI avid rib deposits. There was a suspicious FAPI avid lesion in the left kidney with corresponding cortical structural alteration on CT. This was the expected primary; however, as the patient succumbed to cardiac arrest, histologic confirmation could not be done. Dong et al. published a case report of false-positive FAPI in angiomyolipoma of the kidney.[ ]
 

## Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma

## Fibroblast activation protein and pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
FAP is highly expressed in various tumor tissues, including pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC). Activated pancreatic myofibroblasts (PMFs) play a crucial role in PDAC by depositing extracellular matrix and promoting fibrosis. Key signaling events drive PMF activation, including signals from transforming growth factor-beta 1, platelet-derived growth factor, hypoxia, and extracellular matrix rigidity. FAP-expressing cells co-evolve with PDAC cells, influencing tumor proliferation by paracrine signaling. FAP expression in PDAC correlates with poor prognosis. High-FAP levels are associated with reduced overall survival and recurrence-free survival. FAP could serve as an independent prognostic factor for early-stage PDAC. Nomogram systems integrating FAP expression improve prediction of patient outcomes. Histologically, PDAC is characterized by adenocarcinoma. IHC studies reveal up to 90% FAP expression in PDAC.[ ] FAP expression is variable in normal pancreas but neoplastic lesions usually show differential increased uptake.
 illustrates a patient who presented with pain in abdomen and weight loss. FAPI showed avid uptake in the tail of pancreas with abdominal nodes. MR showed restricted diffusion in the tail of pancreas. Histology confirmed the diagnosis of adenocarcinoma of pancreas.
 In summary, understanding FAP’s role in PDAC biology offers potential therapeutic targets.

## Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma
Oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) is the most common malignant lesion affecting the oral cavity. It arises from the thin cells lining the inner buccal mucosa and other oral structures. Early detection and accurate staging are crucial for optimal management.
FDG PET/CT versus FAP-directed PET/CT: FDG PET/CT is commonly used for OSCC imaging but may show false positives due to uptake in inflammatory cells. FAP-directed PET/CT, using a probe targeting FAP α, offers an alternative. FAP expression is elevated in cancer-associated fibroblasts, making it a potential marker for OSCC. In a study comparing F-18-FDG and FAP-directed PET/CT, both methods detected primary tumors and cervical lymph node metastases. FAP-directed PET/CT demonstrated comparable sensitivity and specificity to F-18-FDG PET/CT. Histologically, FAP expression in cancer-associated fibroblasts confirmed the findings.[ ] shows avid FAPI uptake in squamous cell carcinoma of buccal mucosa. As muscles of oral cavity show physiologic uptake on FDG under variable conditions and FAPI diminishes the possibility of false-positive results. It has been shown to have high target to background activity by one group in 14 patients.[ ]
 

## Carcinoma of Lung
Lung carcinoma, including both non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) and SCLC, exhibits remarkably high uptake on Ga-68-FAPI PET/CT. The highest average SUV max (standardized uptake value) (>12) is observed in lung carcinoma, sarcoma, cholangiocarcinoma, and carcinoma of the breast. Ga-68FAPI PET/CT provides valuable insights into tumor biology and may guide clinical decisions. Our illustrated case [ ] presented with malignant pleural effusion and FAPI avid mass in right lung upper lobe, regional nodes, and extensive skeletal metastases.
 Wang et al. studied 34 patients with FAPI and FDG and observed that FAPI outperformed FDG in detection of brain, lymph node, bone, and pleural deposits.[ ]

## Carcinoma of Breast
FAPI imaging demonstrated intense tracer uptake in the left breast mass [ ]. This uptake suggests increased fibroblast activation associated with the tumor. Further evaluation, including histology, is essential for the accurate diagnosis and staging.
 The incidental liver lesion showed minimal FAPI uptake, primarily at its periphery. MRI findings indicating features of a hydatid cyst are relevant. Hydatid cysts are caused by the parasite Echinococcus granulosus and can occur in various organs, including the liver. Timely assessment and collaboration with other imaging modalities are crucial for accurate diagnosis.

## Urothelial Carcinoma and Synonym-transitional Cell Carcinoma
Urothelial carcinoma (UC) arises from the urothelial lining of the urinary tract, including the renal pelvis, ureters, bladder, and urethra; it is the most common type of bladder cancer and can also occur in other parts of the urinary system. UC exhibits variable behavior, ranging from low-grade noninvasive tumors to aggressive invasive carcinomas.
Immunohistochemical studies have revealed a high expression of FAP in UCs. FAP-based imaging with Ga68-FAPI PET/CT shows potential diagnostic efficacy in muscle invasive bladder carcinoma and metastatic UC.
 shows FAPI avid soft-tissue lesion in the wall of dilated renal pelvis that was subsequently proven to be UC. A small cohort of eight cases studied by Novruzov et al. showed higher tumor to the background ratio in FAPI as compared to FDG.[ ]
 While Ga-68-FAPI PET/CT provides valuable insights, some lesions, including RCCs, neuroendocrine tumors, pheochromocytomas, and medullary thyroid carcinomas, may show relatively weak FAPI uptake. In such cases, alternative tracers such as F18-dihydroxyphenylalanine or Ga68-Somatostatin analogs can be considered for more specific imaging.[ ]
Personalized approaches based on tumor type and clinical context are essential for accurate diagnosis and management.

## Liver Disease
While the study primarily focused on cancer detection, it is interesting to note that Ga-68-FAPI PET has shown promise in detecting the early phases of chronic liver disease. Ga68-FAPI PET/CT demonstrated high sensitivity in detecting hepatic malignancies, particularly in poorly differentiated forms with elevated FAP expression. Our case [ ] shows diffuse FAPI uptake in the liver parenchyma. A possibility of chronic liver disease was raised despite the colloid liver scan showing no obvious chronic liver disease. Histology showed mild peri-portal fibrosis. Thus, Ga68-FAPI appears to be promising in detecting early phase of chronic liver disease. Benign entities such as hepatic fibrosis, exostosis, and hemorrhoids have been reported to localize FAPI.[ ]
 In summary, Ga-68-FAPI PET shows promise not only in cancer imaging but also in detecting inflammatory liver disease.
