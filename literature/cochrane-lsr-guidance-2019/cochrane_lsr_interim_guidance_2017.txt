 Welcome 

 Cochrane Resources is the central place for content that's designed to support specialist groups like our Trainers Network and Group members. It brings together our current guidance and information for Cochrane Groups, our Trainer's Hub and some of the more in-depth information about Cochrane. For example, policies and membership numbers. 
 Whether you're looking for practical support or deeper insights into how Cochrane works, this is the place to start.
 
 This website partly replaces the Cochrane Community and Cochrane Training sites. Past versions of the sites can be seen on the archive.org website, where highlighted dates correspond to snapshots of the archived website. 
 If you are looking for content from these sites that has been retired, or if you have any other questions, you can contact us here .
