## Abstract
1.1. A compendium of reviews and minireviews in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology published in the first half of 1991 is presented. In all there are 380 titles from 81 different publications.
2.2. The compendium includes the main keywords associated with each review; over 2500 different keywords are used.
