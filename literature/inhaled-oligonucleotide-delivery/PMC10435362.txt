https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/)Myocardial cell damage is associated with apoptosis and excessive inflammatory response in sepsis. Histone deacetylases (HDACs) are implicated in the progression of heart diseases. This study aims to explore the role of histone deacetylase 9 (HDAC9) in sepsis-induced myocardial injury. Lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced Sprague Dawley rats and cardiomyocyte line H9C2 were used as models in vivo and in vitro. The results showed that HDAC9 was significantly upregulated after LPS stimulation, and HDAC9 knockdown remarkably improved cardiac function, as evidenced by decreased left ventricular internal diameter end diastole (LVEDD) and left ventricular internal diameter end systole (LVESD), and increased fractional shortening (FS)% and ejection fraction (EF)%. In addition, HDAC9 silencing alleviated release of inflammatory cytokines (tumor necrosis factor-α (TNF-α), IL-6 and IL-1β) and cardiomyocyte apoptosis in vivo and in vitro. Furthermore, HDAC9 inhibition was proved to suppress nuclear factor-kappa B (NF-κB) activation with reducing the levels of p-IκBα and p-p65, and p65 nuclear translocation. Additionally, interaction between miR-214-3p and HDAC9 was determined through bioinformatics analysis, RT-qPCR, western blot and dual luciferase reporter assay. Our data revealed that miR-214-3p directly targeted the 3’UTR of HDAC9. Our findings demonstrate that HDAC9 suppression ameliorates LPS-induced cardiac dysfunction by inhibiting the NF-κB signaling pathway and presents a promising therapeutic agent for the treatment of LPS-stimulated myocardial injury.

## Introduction
Sepsis is a life-threatening multiple organ dysfunction, which is caused by an unbalance of the body’s response to a grievous infection [ , ]. The mortality rate in patients with sepsis exceeds 50%. Inflammation, immune system dysfunction and coagulopathy are regarded as the main factors in the pathophysiology of sepsis [ ]. Myocardial injury is a prevalent complication of sepsis [ ], which is characterized by adverse outcomes and incremental mortality, and is an indicator of poor prognosis in septic patients [ ]. Therefore, it is vital to find an effective therapeutic strategy to ameliorate sepsis-induced myocardial injury.
Histone Deacetylase 9 (HDAC9) is a class IIa chromatin modifying enzyme that regulates various physiological functions [ , ]. It controls the development and function of the myocardia [ ]. HDAC9 has been reported to be involved in cardiac diseases [ , , ] and inflammatory response [ , ]. Loss of HDAC9 suppressed the release of inflammatory factor in lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-treated BV2 cells [ ]. Yan K. found that HDAC9 knockdown in CD4+ T cell-mediated autoimmunity mice reduced inflammation and produced fewer cytokine and chemokine [ ]. HDAC9 blockade also prevented heart from myocardial infarction injury [ ]. Inhibiting the expression of HDAC9 by application of antiepileptic drugs (valproate), small RNA interference or inhibitors may be a promising treatment for heart disease [ ]. However, the underlying mechanism of HDAC9 in sepsis-induced myocardial damage remains to be elucidated.
MicroRNAs (miRNAs) are a class of small noncoding RNA molecules, which inhibits mRNA translation or accelerates its degradation by interacting with the 3 ‘ untranslated regions of target genes [ , ]. They have been regarded as critical regulators of cardiovascular development, cardiac regeneration and stem cell differentiation, and are appearing as new therapies and clinical biomarkers for treatment of cardiovascular disease [ , ]. MiR-214 is located in the 1q24.3 chromosome and is a vertebrate-specific miRNA precursor family [ ]. MiR-214 has been shown to protect against H2O2-induced cardiomyocyte injury [ ]. MiR-214-3p was upregulated in cecal ligation and puncture-treated mice with myocardial injury and was associated with myocardial protection in sepsis [ ]. These studies speculated that miRNA-214-3p might act a key role in myocardial damage.
The website prediction indicated that the 3’UTR of Hdac9 has a target binding site of miR-214-3p. In the present study, we aimed to investigative the role of HDAC9 and miR-214-3p in LPS-induced myocardial cell injury and inflammatory response, as well as explore the underlying mechanism.

## Materials and Methods

## Animals
All animal experiments and procedures were approved by the Ethics Committee of Cangzhou Central Hospital (approval no. 2020-215-01 (Z)). Male Sprague Dawley rats (220–250 g) were purchased from Beijing HFK bioscience Co., Ltd. To establish the septic myocardial injury model, rats were intraperitoneally injected with 10 mg/kg LPS (Solarbio Science & Technology, Co., Ltd., Beijing, China), and rats in Control group were treated with the same volume of PBS. To establish a rat model with silenced HDAC9, the rats in LPS + shNC and LPS + shHDAC9 groups were administered with lentiviral particles containing shNC or shHDAC9 (1 × 109 TU/ml) through tail veins in a volume of 150 µl before LPS stimulation 72 h. After 6 h of LPS treatment, rats in each group were studied on an echocardiography system. The left ventricular internal diameter end diastole (LVEDD), left ventricular internal diameter end systole (LVESD), left ventricular fractional shortening (FS), left ventricular ejection fraction (EF), left ventricular posterior wall end diastole (LVPWD), left ventricular posterior wall end systole (LVPWS) values were scored. Then, the rats were euthanized with CO2, and myocardial tissues were collected for subsequent analysis. Explanation: In the first part of the in vivo experiment, rats were anesthetized with pentobarbital sodium (50 mg/kg). In the second part of the in vivo experiment, we optimized the anesthesia method. Rats were anesthetized with 2% inhaled isoﬂurane.

## RNA extraction and RT-qPCR analysis
Total RNA was isolated from cells or tissues using TRIpure lysate (BioTeke Corp., Beijing, China). RNA was reverse transcribed into cDNA using BeyoRTTM II M-MLV reverse transcriptase (Beyotime Institute of Biotechnology, Shanghai, China) and miRNA first strand cDNA synthesis (Tailing Reaction) (Sangon Biotech (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., Shanghai, China). β-actin was used for internal control. Quantification of mRNA and miRNA was carried out by applying ExicyclerTM 96 real-time quantitative thermal block (Bioneer Corp., Daejeon, Korea). The primers used were as follows:
Hdac9 F: 5′-GCGAGTGTTTGAGGTGG-3′, R: 5′-GGAAGTCCCAGGGTGATA-3′; Tlr4 F: 5′-AATCTGGTGGCTGTGG-3′, R: 5′-TGGGCTTGAATGGAGT-3′; β-actin F: 5′-GGAGATTACTGCCCTGGCTCCTAGC-3′, R: 5′-GGCCGGACTCATCGTACTCCTGCTT-3′.

## Western blot
Samples were lysed in cell lysis buffer for western and IP (Beyotime). Proteins were quantified by BCA protein assay kit (Beyotime). After complete separation by electrophoresis, the proteins were transferred to polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) membranes (Millipore, Billerica, MA, USA). Then these membranes were blocked in 5% skimmed milk (Inner Mongolia Yili Industrial Group Co., Ltd., Inner Mongolia, China) for 1 h and incubated with primary antibodies. Then these membranes were incubated with HRP-labeled goat anti-rabbit IgG (H+L) (1: 5,000, A0208, Beyotime) or horseradish peroxidase (HRP)-labeled goat anti-mouse IgG (H+L) (1: 5,000, A0216, Beyotime) at room temperature at 37°C for 45 min. Protein bands were visualized with Gel-Pro-Analyzer software (Beijing Liuyi Biotechnology Co., Ltd., Beijing, China).
The primary antibodies used were shown as following: HDAC9 antibody (1: 500, A02177-4, BOSTER, Wuhan, China), cleaved caspase-3 antibody (1:1,000, AF7022, Affinity, Jiangsu, China), Bax antibody (1:1,000, A19684, ABclonal, Wuhan, China), B-cell lymphoma/leukemia-2 (Bcl-2) antibody (1:1,000, A19693, ABclonal), IκBα antibody (1:1,000, A19714, ABclonal), p-IκBα antibody (1:1,000, AP0707, ABclonal), p-p65 antibody (1:1,000, AP0123, ABclonal), p65 antibody (1:1,000, A2547, ABclonal), β-actin (1:1,000, sc-47778, Santa Cruz, Santa Cruz, CA, USA).

## Dual luciferase reporter assay
Targetscan website https://www.targetscan.org/vert_72/ was used to predict the target genes of miR-214-3p. The miR-214-3p binding site or the mutant 3’UTR of Hdac9 was cloned into the pmirGLO vector (Promega, Madison, WI, USA) to generate the luciferase reporter plasmid. Luciferase vectors were co-transfected with miR-214-3p mimics or mimic NC into H9C2 cells (Procell Life Science&Technology Co., Ltd., Wuhan, China) using Lipofectamine 3000 (Invitrogen, Carlsbad, CA, USA), respectively. The luciferase activity was measured using the dual luciferase reporter gene assay kit (Keygen, Nanjing, China).

## Immunofluorescence staining
Cells and tissue slides were incubated with p65 antibody (1:100, A2547, ABclonal) and HDAC9 antibody (1:100, A02177-4, BOSTER) at 4°C overnight, respectively. Then, the slides were incubated with goat anti-rabbit IgG (1:200, A27039, Invitrogen). The fluorescence was captured on a microscope system (Olympus, Tokyo, Japan). Three slides were taken from each group, and one field of view was selected for each slide. The number of p65 (+) cells in the nucleus was calculated by counting of each visual field.

## Cell culture
H9C2 cells (Procell Life Science &Technology Co., Ltd.) were cultured in DMEM medium (Wuhan Servicebio Technology Co., Ltd., Wuhan, China) in a humidified atmosphere containing 5% CO2 at 37°C.

## Cell proliferation assay
Cell viability was determined by cell counting kit-8 (Beyotime). Cells (3 × 103 cells/well) were inoculated in a 96-well plate. After LPS (1.0 µg/ml) exposure for 24 h, 10 µl CCK-8 were added into each well. OD values were detected by a microplate reader (BIOTEK, Winooski, VT, USA).

## TUNEL assay
Samples were fixed and permeabilized. Thereafter, cells were treated using in situ cell death detection kit (Roche, Basel, Switzerland) according to manufacturers’ procedure. The staining effect was observed by a fluorescence microscopy (Olympus).

## Hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) staining
After deparaffinage, the sections were stained with haematoxylin (Solarbio Science & Technology, Co., Ltd.) and eosin (Sangon). Then, the samples were observed under a microscope (Olympus). The cardiomyocyte cross-sectional areas were evaluated by detecting the circumferential length of the cardiomyocyte as described previously [ ].

## Cell apoptosis
Cell apoptosis was carried using Annexin V-FITC/PI labeling apoptosis detection kit (Keygen). Briefly, exponentially growing cells were seeded into six-well plates at a density of 1 × 105 cells/well. After 24 h of incubation, the cells were centrifuged at 200 g for 5 min. Then, cells were added 5 µl Annexin V-FITC and 5 µl PI for incubation 15 min. The cell apoptosis number was determined using flow cytometer (ACEA Biosciences Inc., Santa Clara, CA, USA).

## Inflammatory and cardiac injury marker detection
The levels of TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-1β in the cell supernatant were detected using commercial ELISA kits (MultiSciences Biotech Co., Ltd., Hangzhou, China). The level of cTnI was examined using rat cTn-I/TNNI3 (Cardiac Troponin I) ELISA kit (Wuhan Fine Biotech Co., Ltd.) according to the instructions. The levels of LDH and CK-MB were detected by commercial kits (Nanjing Jiancheng Bioengineering Institute, Nanjing, China) according to the manufacturer’s procedure.

## iRNA and mimics transfection
Cells were transfected with siRNAs directed against HDAC9 or NC siRNA and miR-214-3p mimics or NC mimics, respectively. Transfections were performed using Lipofectamine 3000 (Invitrogen) for 48 h. Then cells were treated with 1.0 µg/ml LPS for 24 h. The siRNA sequence was as follows:
HDAC9 siRNA-1: GGUUAAGCAGAAGCUUCAATT, UUGAAGCUUCUGCUUAACCTT.
HDAC9 siRNA-2: GCAACUGCAGCAAGAGUUATT, UAACUCUUGCUGCAGUUGCTT.

## Statistical analysis
Data was analyzed using GraphPad Prism 8.0 software. The unpaired two-tailed Student’s t-test was applied to compare differences between two groups, while one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s analysis was used to compare the differences among multiple groups. P<0.05 was considered as significant.

## Results

## HDAC9 was upregulated in LPS-stimulated myocardial injury
LPS was intraperitoneally injected into rats to induce septic myocardial dysfunction as previously described [ ]. LPS exposure significantly increased LVEDD and LVESD, whilst reduced FS% and EF% ( ). It needs to be explained here that in this part of the in vivo experiments, we did not detect LVPWD and LVPWS, but in the subsequent in vivo experiments, we supplemented the detection of these two indicators. HE staining was performed to evaluate pathological lesions of myocardial cells. As indicated in , myocardial tissues in LPS group showed edema, abnormal myofilament, and degeneration and necrosis in a certain degree. LPS increased the cardiomyocyte cross-sectional areas as compared with the sham group. The results of TUNEL staining showed that LPS increased the number of apoptotic cells ( ). In addition, we found that HDAC9 was upregulated after LPS stimulation, as evidenced by enhanced fluorescence intensity, mRNA and protein levels ( ). Meanwhile, miR-214-3p were also elevated in LPS-treated rats ( ).

## LPS-induced myocardial cell injury could be partially attenuated by HDAC9 blockade
To determine the role of HDAC9 in sepsis-induced myocardial injury, an in vitro model was developed to imitate myocardial injury in vivo. We firstly examined the expression of HDAC9 and miR-214-3p in LPS-exposed myocardial cells. RT-qPCR analysis showed that Hdac9 expression was enhanced in LPS-treated cells in a dose-dependent manner ( ), whereas miR-214-3p level was drastically downregulated in cells with LPS treatment ( ). In addition, the results showed that with the prolongation of LPS treatment time, the HDAC9 level was observably increased and the miR-214-3p expression was markedly decreased ( ). Subsequently, the specific siRNAs were transfected into H9C2 cells to further investigate the effect of HDAC9 on LPS-induced myocardial cell injury. The results of RT-qPCR and western blot indicated that HDAC9 was successfully knock downed ( ), and miR-214-3p expression was not affected ( ). Furthermore, we detected the content of certain myocardial injury markers (cTnI, CK-MB and LDH) and inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6 and IL-1β). LPS exposure remarkably elevated these factors production in the supernatant, which was weakened by HDAC9 knockdown ( ). These results suggested that HDAC9 inhibition could ameliorated myocardial cell injury and inflammatory response with LPS treatment.

## Inhibition of HDAC9 alleviated LPS-induced myocardial cell apoptosis
The results of flow cytometer demonstrated that LPS treatment remarkably elevated cell apoptosis rate, whereas HDAC9 blockade significantly suppressed cell apoptosis ( ). We then examined apoptosis-related proteins levels including cleaved caspase-3, Bcl-2 and Bax. As expected, we observed that the increase of cleaved caspase-3 and Bax, whereas Bcl-2 was observably decreased after LPS exposure. However, the promoting effect of LPS on cell apoptosis was also reversed by HDAC9 silencing ( ). Furthermore, CCK-8 assay showed that HDAC9 knockdown significantly elevated cell viability in LPS-exposed cells ( ). These data revealed that HDAC9 knockdown suppressed myocardial cell apoptosis.

## HDAC9 silencing inhibited NF-κB signaling pathway
TLR4-NF-κB signaling pathway regulates the progression of inflammation and is closely associated with myocardial injury [ ]. The data revealed that Tlr4 level was upregulated after LPS exposure, and HDAC9 repression reversed this effect ( ). Similarly, HDAC9 silencing inhibited the LPS-induced activation of NF-κB signaling, as evidenced by the markedly reduced p-IκBα/IκBα and p-p65/p65 protein expression ( ). Moreover, the results of immunofluorescence staining ( ) showed that the number of p65-(+) cells in the nucleus was decreased after HDAC9 suppression, which demonstrated that HDAC9 silencing abrogated p65 nuclear translocation.

## MiR-214-3p targeted with HDAC9
To further clarify the connection between HDAC9 and miR-214-3p, miR-214-3p mimics or NC mimics was transfected into H9C2 cells followed by LPS treatment. The transfection efficiency of miR-214-3p in H9C2 cells was proved successfully by RT-qPCR ( ). We further examined the mRNA and protein levels of HDAC9, the result revealed that miR-214-3p markedly decreased the expression levels of HDAC9 mRNA and protein in LPS-exposed H9C2 cells ( ). Furthermore, we found that the 3’UTR of Hdac9 contained the complementary site for the seed region of miR-214-3p ( ). Dual luciferase reporter assay was applied to confirm the combination of miR-214-3p and HDAC9. The mutation of HDAC9 significantly abrogated miR-214-3p-mediated the inhibition of luciferase activity ( ). These results demonstrated that miR-214-3p directly targeted HDAC9.

## HDAC9 silencing alleviated LPS-stimulated myocardial injury in vivo
As shown in , the mRNA and protein expression of HDAC9 were successfully inhibited in myocardial tissues of rats in LPS + shHDAC9 group compared with that in LPS + shNC group. The echocardiography results showed that HDAC9 knockdown dramatically reversed LPS-impaired cardiac function via diminishing LVEDD and LVESD, and enhancing FS% and EF% ( ). Consistently, the levels of myocardial injury markers (cTnI, CK-MB and LDH) and inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-6 and IL-1β) were reduced via HDAC9 inhibition ( ). The results of TUNEL staining indicated that HDAC9 interception decreased myocardial cell apoptosis ( ).

## Discussion
Myocardial injury is a prevalent and serious complication of sepsis [ ]. Although there are some feasible therapeutic options [ ], it remains a big clinical challenge to effectively cure the disease and reduce mortality [ ]. Currently, more and more researches and clinical trials have been carried out to reduce cardiac injury in sepsis via regulating the uncontrolled immune response [ , ]. LPS is one of the most potent immune stimulatory compounds that can induce sepsis through overactivation of the innate immune system. It promotes inflammation response by activating inflammatory cells that induce the synthesis and release of pro-inflammatory factors, ultimately leading to severe septic inflammation [ ]. In this study, we aimed to investigate the relationship between HDAC9 and sepsis-induced myocardial injury. LPS was performed to induce a rat sepsis model. We found that LPS simulation reduced ejection fraction and fractional shortening. Furthermore, we observed that the levels of inflammatory factors and myocardial function-related factors were markedly enhanced in LPS-exposed cardiomyocyte. Also, the LPS-treated rats presented the pathological changes accompanied with myocardial cell edema and the disordered arrangement of fibers, which demonstrated that the success of sepsis myocardial injury model. And we detected the HDAC9 expression profile in septic rat myocardial cells for the first time. HDAC9 was up-regulated in LPS-exposed myocardial cells in a dose dependent manner.
HDAC9 is a member of Histone deacetylases (HDACs) and belongs to IIa class, which also includes HDAC4, HDAC5, and HDAC7 [ ]. HDAC4 was were highly expressed in myocardial tissues and participate in sepsis-induced myocardial injury [ ]. HDAC4 overexpressing stimulates myocardial ischemia/reperfusion injury [ ]. HDAC5 inhibited the production of mitochondrial reactive oxygen species and induced the expression of NF-E2–related factor 2-dependent antioxidant gene in cardiomyocytes [ ]. HDAC7 was involved in myocardial injury induced by necroptotic death of ischemia heart diseases [ ]. The inhibitor of class IIa HDAC decreased HDAC4 expression, and alleviated sepsis-associated acute kidney injury [ ]. These facts pointed to distinct functions of HDAC family members, indicating that HDAC9 might have similar effects in sepsis related diseases. HDAC9 deficiency induced Nrf2 activation and prevented heart from myocardial infarction injury [ ]. Mutant mice lacking HDAC9 were sensitive to hypertrophic signaling and showed stress-dependent cardiac hypertrophy [ ]. However, the role of HDAC9 in sepsis-induced myocardial injury is still unclear. In our present study, we found that HDAC9 expression was elevated in LPS-exposed myocardial tissues, and deletion of HDAC9 inhibited cell apoptosis. The relationship between HDAC9 and inflammatory diseases has been reported in recent 10 years. Treg cells lacking HDAC9 proliferate faster, leading to stronger immune suppression [ ]. Silencing HDAC9 reduced glomerulosclerosis, the release of inflammatory cytokine, podocyte apoptosis and renal injury [ ]. In addition, HDAC9 has been found to be implicated in inflammatory response in LPS-stimulated cells [ ]. In this study, we found that the deletion of HDAC9 decreased the release of myocardial injury related factors and inflammatory factors in LPS-exposed H9C2 cells. Besides, the activation of nuclear factor (NF)-kB plays a key role in the inflammatory response [ ]. Once NF-κB is activated by external stimuli, its inhibitor IkB is degraded, which causes NF-κB translocates into the nucleus and induces the expression of inflammatory response-associated genes [ ]. In our study, LPS treatment increased p-p65 ser276 and p-IκBα ser32, as well as promoted p65 nuclear expression. HDAC9 was reported to modulate NF-κB signaling. HDAC9 deficiency was associated with the reduction of p-IκBα and NF-κB [ ]. Consistent with this study, our result showed that the block of HDAC9 suppressed the levels of p-IκBα ser32 and p-p65 ser276, and decreased p65 nuclear translocation.
MicroRNAs are short regulatory RNAs that regulate gene expression at the post-transcriptional level and play significant roles in the development of physiological and pathological processes [ ]. MiR-214, originally reported for its role in tumor progression [ ], has been found to be highly expressed in the early embryonic heart of mice [ ], suggesting its role in myocardial disease. MiR-214-3p has been demonstrated to protect against H2O2-induced cardiac myocyte injury [ ]. The expression of miR-214-3p was elevated in H2O2-treated cardiac myocytes in a dose-dependent manner [ ]. By contrast, our study indicated that with the concentration increased, miR-214-3p was downregulated in LPS-treated myocardial cells. However, miRNA-214 expression was reactively enhanced at first in sepsis rat, so we think its increase was a compensatory upregulation. Then we observed the expression of miR-214-3p gradually disappears over the increase of LPS concentration. MiRNA-214 has reported to be related to inflammatory response and myocardial protection of cecal ligation and puncture mice in sepsis [ ]. However, the role of miR-214-3p in LPS-induced myocardial injury has not been revealed. In this study, we found that miR-214-3p could target HDAC9 and negatively regulate its expression, and HDAC9 blockade alleviated myocardial cell damage, inflammatory response and apoptosis with LPS treatment, indicating miR-214-3p might act its role in this process. We think miR-214-3p may also regulate myocardial injury and inflammatory response via mediating HDAC9. The downstream mechanism of action will be addressed in our future studies.
This current study revealed that the expression of HDAC9 was enhanced in myocardial injury in LPS-exposed rats, and was related to sepsis-induced myocardial protection. Knockdown of HDAC9 targeted by miR-214-3p decreased myocardial inflammatory response and inhibited cell apoptosis via suppressing the activation of NF-κB signaling pathway. Anyway, we have to admit that our current study is limited in LPS-induced septic model. Clinically sepsis can be induced by variety of pathogens including gram-negative and gram-positive microorganisms. Furthermore, multiple pathogen studies will be performed in our future research.
