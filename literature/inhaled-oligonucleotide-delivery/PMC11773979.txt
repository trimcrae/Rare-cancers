## Abstract
Esophageal cancer (EC) is one of the most common highly malignant tumors of the digestive system, with a poor prognosis under current treatment regimens. Nucleolin (NCL) is overexpressed in many tumors, and drugs specifically targeting NCL may offer a promising strategy for treating esophageal cancer. Here, we designed and prepared a novel aptamer-conjugated drug targeting NCL by AS1411 aptamer-human serum albumin (HSA)-the apoprotein of lidamycin (LDP)-active enediyne chromophore (AE), in order to achieve targeted treatment of esophageal cancer. The experimental results revealed that AS1411-HSA-LDP effectively binds to esophageal cancer cells and could be efficiently internalized by esophageal cancer cells. In the KYSE520 xenograft tumor nude mouse model, AS1411-HSA-LDP could be targeted and enriched in the tumor location for a long time. AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE exhibited a strong cell-killing activity in esophageal cancer cells, inhibited cell migration and invasion, and induced cell apoptosis. The animal studies confirmed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE exhibited a strong anti-tumor effect. These findings suggested that the novel NCL-targeting aptamer-drug conjugate constructed based on lidamycin exhibited a strong anti-tumor effect, providing a promising strategy for the targeted treatment of esophageal cancer.
Keywords: Esophageal cancer, Lidamycin, AS1411, Human serum albumin

## Introduction
Esophageal cancer is the sixth most common and seventh most lethal malignancy of the digestive tract [ ]. Globally, there were approximately 604,100 cases of esophageal cancer and 544,100 deaths in 2020. It is estimated that there will be 957,000 new cases of esophageal cancer and 880,000 deaths from esophageal cancer in 2040 [ ]. Esophageal cancer is mainly divided into two histological subtypes, namely esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) and esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC). Regardless of the subtype, the treatment effect is very poor, and most patients are in the advanced stage [ ]. In Asian populations, ESCC is the main subtype of esophageal cancer [ ]. The incidence of EAC has increased significantly in the past four decades, and the mortality rate is very high. It has become the main subtype in the West [ ]. Radiotherapy has been widely used in the treatment of ESCC, but it is easy to cause ESCC invasion and metastasis [ ]. In addition, the main clinical therapies for esophageal cancer include surgical resection and chemotherapy. However, due to the limited efficacy and serious adverse reactions of conventional treatments, the results are still unsatisfactory. Targeted therapy, as a new method, has been proven to play an important role in the treatment of esophageal cancer [ ]. Therefore, it is urgent to develop new and effective targeted therapeutic candidates for esophageal cancer.
In human plasma, albumin is the most abundant protein with good tissue compatibility, biodegradability, non-toxicity, long circulation time, and non-immunogenicity. It has become a carrier for various anticancer drugs and therapeutic diagnostic agents [ ]. The two most commonly used types of albumins are human serum albumin and bovine serum albumin (BSA). The drug delivery platform based on human serum albumin has ideal biocompatibility and pharmacokinetic properties and is rapidly developing for tumor-targeted drug delivery to improve delivery efficiency and therapeutic efficacy [ ]. The anti-tumor drug (Abraxane) developed based on albumin has been widely used in clinical practice. In preclinical studies, Xu et al. used coaxial electrospray technology to prepare multifunctional albumin sub-microspheres for the co-delivery of bufalin and nintedanib to treat liver cancer. The experimental results showed that multifunctional albumin sub-microspheres could achieve payload efficiency and stability and have excellent anti-tumor effects [ ]. The novel recombinant fusion protein developed based on domain III of human serum albumin has a high affinity with human esophageal cancer cells and could show tumor site-specific biodistribution in the KYSE520 nude mouse transplant tumor model in vivo, with targeted enrichment at the tumor site lasting up to 624 h [ ]. Artemether (ARM) has poor solubility and bioavailability. ARM was encapsulated in human serum albumin nanoparticles (HSA NPs) by the desolvation method. NPs were modified with folic acid to achieve targeting and more effective effects on tumor cells [ ]. The strategy of constructing new drugs based on albumin has become one of the most widely used methods because of its minimal side effects and high efficiency in prolonging blood retention time. Using albumin as a carrier for anti-tumor drugs has important prospects in precision medicine.
Aptamers are single-chain functional oligonucleotides that can fold into a unique tertiary structure and bind to target molecules with superior specificity and affinity [ ]. Aptamers are also called “chemical antibodies” and have the advantages of low immunogenicity and small size [ ]. Compared with antibodies, the molecular weight and size of aptamers are smaller than those of monoclonal antibodies, and aptamers may have better tissue penetration ability [ ]. Based on the above characteristics, aptamers have been widely used in the field of cancer diagnosis and treatment. Aptamer-drug conjugates (ApDCs) constructed based on aptamers have been widely studied in the field of tumor-targeted therapy. Compared with antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), they have advantages such as improved water solubility, enhanced efficacy, and reduced toxicity [ ]. AS1411 is a 26-base DNA aptamer with a G-quadruplex structure, which is commonly known as an anti-nucleolar protein aptamer. It can specifically bind to the target nucleolar protein receptor overexpressed on cancer cells and has been used as a targeting part in many drug delivery systems [ ]. Yu et al. developed an albumin-based targeted drug delivery system in which docetaxel (DTX) was loaded into albumin nanoparticles and the particles were functionalized with the nucleolin-targeted aptamer AS1411. This functionalized drug delivery system could target the delivery of DTX to colon cancer and significantly improve the anti-tumor efficacy [ ]. He et al. constructed an AS1411-triptolide conjugate as a highly effective targeted therapy for triple-negative breast cancer. The conjugate has excellent specificity and highly effective cell-killing activity against the MDA-MB-231 cell line [ ]. Chen et al. prepared a new AS1411-triptolide conjugate (AS-TP). AS1411 modification significantly improved the specificity and affinity of TP for nucleolin, and promoted the endocytosis of conjugated TP and its transport into lysosomes through macropinocytosis in a nucleolin expression-dependent manner, triggering the release of the original TP. In a human TNBC xenograft model, AS-TP selectively accumulated in tumor tissues to exert anti-tumor effects, ultimately inhibiting breast cancer growth, and had no obvious toxic effects on normal organs [ ]. Therefore, AS1411 represents a promising tumor-targeted carrier for the delivery of cytotoxic drugs to treat malignant tumors in which nucleolin is located on the cell surface.
Lidamycin (LDM) is a new anti-tumor antibiotic containing an active enediyne chromophore and the apoprotein of lidamycin. The novel antibody-drug conjugate hIMB1636-LDP-AE constructed based on LDM could significantly inhibit the growth of breast cancer and lung cancer in xenograft models [ ]. In addition, AE and LDP could be separated and recombined. LDM was an ideal payload for the preparation of ADC. A new ADC composed of a complete anti-CD30 antibody and LDM showed attractive tumor-targeting ability and anti-tumor efficacy in the Karpas299 xenograft model [ ]. The recombinant protein conjugate F-HSA-LDP-AE prepared from folic acid (F), HSA, LDP, and AE has a significant inhibitory effect on the growth of pancreatic cancer xenografts in nude mice [ ]. Therefore, conjugate drugs constructed based on LDM have great potential in tumor treatment.
In this study, as illustrated in Scheme , we designed and prepared new ApDCs targeting NCL (AS1411-HSA-LDP and AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE). AS1411-HSA-LDP has an affinity for esophageal cancer cells, and NCL-overexpressing esophageal cancer cells uptake drugs mainly through the micropinocytosis pathway. In the human esophageal cancer xenograft model, AS1411-HSA-LDP can accumulate specifically in tumor tissues for a long time. AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE exerted significant anti-esophageal cancer effects in vivo and in vitro, fully revealing its potent anti-tumor efficacy. This study provides a new preparation method for LDM-based ApDCs and a promising candidate drug for the treatment of esophageal cancer.
 

## Materials and methods

## Reagents
The 5ʹ polyA linker and 5ʹ-SH modified aptamer AS1411, with the sequence 5ʹ-SH-AAAAAA-GGTGGTGGTGGTTGTGGTGGTGGTGG, was synthesized by GENERAL BIOL Co., Ltd (Anhui, China). HSA-LDP and HSA-LDP-AE were provided by Professor Zhen Yongsu’s team from the Institute of Medicinal Biotechnology, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences, Peking Union Medical College. Tri (2-carboxyethyl) phosphine hydrochloride (TCEP) and 4-(N-maleimide methyl) cyclohexane-1-carboxylic acid sulfosuccinimidyl ester sodium salt (Sulfo-SMCC) were purchased from Bide Pharmatech Co., Ltd (Shanghai, China). Annexin V-FITC/PI double staining apoptosis detection kit was purchased from BestBio (Shanghai, China). A reactive oxygen species (ROS) detection kit, enhanced mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP) detection kit (JC-1), Calcein/PI cell viability and cytotoxicity detection kit, and BeyoClick™ EdU-488 cell proliferation detection kit were purchased from Beyotime Biotechnology (Shanghai, China). Pierce™ BCA Protein Assay Kit was purchased from Thermo Fisher Scientific (Massachusetts, USA). Rabbit anti-NCL monoclonal antibody (Cat No. R27099), HRP-labeled goat anti-rabbit IgG (Cat No. 511203), and HRP-labeled goat anti-mouse IgG (Cat No. 511103) were purchased from ZENBIO (Chengdu, China). Mouse anti-Bax monoclonal antibody (Cat No. 60267-1-Ig) and mouse anti-PARP1 monoclonal antibody (Cat No. 66520-1-Ig) were purchased from Proteintech (Wuhan, China). Rabbit anti-Bcl-2 polyclonal antibody (Cat No. WL01556) was purchased from Wanleibio (Shenyang, China). Rabbit anti-cytochrome c monoclonal antibody (Cat No. ET1610-60) was purchased from HUABIO (Hangzhou, China). All other reagents were of analytical grade.

## Cell lines and culture
The human esophageal cancer cell lines KYSE520 and KYSE70 were purchased from Creative Bioarray. EC109 was purchased from the National Experimental Cell Resource Sharing Platform (Beijing, China). KYSE150 was purchased from the Cell Bank of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (Shanghai, China). All cells were cultured in RPMI-1640 medium containing 1% penicillin-streptomycin solution and 10% fetal bovine serum at 37℃ and 5% CO2.

## Preparation and characterization of AS1411-HSA-LDP
To prepare AS1411-HSA-LDP, the thiol-modified AS1411 aptamer was linked to the amino group of HSA-LDP via the commonly used linker Sulfo-SMCC. Briefly, 10 mg HSA-LDP and 0.72 mg Sulfo-SMCC were dissolved in 3 mL PBS and reacted on a shaker for 4 h under ice bath conditions. Unreacted Sulfo-SMCC was removed by centrifugation using an ultrafiltration tube (MWCO, 30 kDa) and resuspended in 500 µL PBS. 6 µL of 100 µM AS1411 solution was added to 900 µL ultrapure water, and 55 µL of 800 mM TCEP solution was added. The mixture was placed on a shaker for 30 min under ice bath conditions. The AS1411 solution was mixed with the SMCC-treated HSA-LDP solution and reacted on a shaker overnight. The reaction product was ultrafiltered under an ultrafiltration tube (MWCO, 30 kDa) and resuspended in 1 mL PBS.
Agarose gel electrophoresis was used to detect whether AS1411 was successfully conjugated to HSA-LDP 0.5 g agarose was accurately weighed and added to 25 mL 1×TAE electrophoresis buffer solution and heated in a microwave to dissolve. Then Super Red nucleic acid dye was added to the gel. Free AS1411, AS1411-HSA-LDP, and HSA-LDP were mixed with the loading buffer and then dropped into the gel loading well. The sample was electrophoresed at 120 V for 35 min. Under UV light, the gel imaging (Tanon-1600 Gel Image) system was used to observe the gel imaging and take pictures.

## Binding affinity of AS1411-HSA-LDP to cancer cells
Flow cytometry was used to analyze the affinity activity of different concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP labeled with fluorescein Cy5 to esophageal cancer cells. Esophageal cancer cells EC109, KYSE520, KYSE70, and KYSE150 in the logarithmic growth phase were digested, centrifuged, and washed. Different groups were set up, and 3 × 105 cells were incubated with varying concentrations of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP in an ice bath for 4 h. After incubation, the cells were filtered through a 300-mesh nylon mesh and analyzed by flow cytometry (BD FACSCelesta).

## Cellular uptake
EC109, KYSE520, and KYSE70 cells in the logarithmic growth phase were seeded at 3 × 104 cells/well on 8-well chamber slides and cultured in a cell culture incubator for 24 h. The old culture medium was discarded, and 0.1 mg/mL Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP solution diluted in complete culture medium was added, respectively, and incubated at 37℃ in the dark for 4 h. Under dark conditions, 200 µL of 4% paraformaldehyde solution was added to each well to fix the cells for 20 min. Subsequently, an anti-fluorescence quencher (containing DAPI) was added to each well and reacted at room temperature in the dark for 15 min. They were observed and photographed under a confocal laser scanning microscope (200×).

## Endocytic pathways
For the study of endocytic pathways, EC109 and KYSE520 cells were seeded in 6-well plates (3 × 105 cells per well) for 24 h and different endocytic pathway inhibitors were used to study the uptake mechanism of AS1411-HSA-LDP by esophageal cancer cells, including chlorpromazine (CPZ, clathrin pathway inhibitor), methyl-β-cyclodextrin (MβCD, lipid raft-mediated endocytosis inhibitor), genistein (Gen, caveolin pathway inhibitor), and 5-N-ethyl-N-isoproamiloride (EIPA, macropinocytosis pathway inhibitor) to block different endocytic pathways. For EC109 cells, different uptake pathways were blocked for 2 h with genistein (200 µM), chlorpromazine (30 µM), amiloride (80 µM), and methyl-β-cyclodextrin (2.5 mM). Subsequently, 2 mL of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP with a concentration of 0.7 mg/mL was co-incubated with the cells for 2 h. For KYSE520 cells, 2 mL of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP solution diluted with complete medium was added to the cells, which contained 200 µM genistein, 30 µM chlorpromazine, 80 µM amiloride, and 2.5 mM methyl-β-cyclodextrin, respectively, and incubated in the dark for 4 h. Esophageal cancer cells were washed with PBS and then detected by flow cytometry. The uptake efficiency was calculated according to the mean fluorescence intensity (MFI).
To verify the specific endocytic pathway involved in AS1411-HSA-LDP in two esophageal cancer cells, EC109 and KYSE520 cells were seeded in 8-well chamber slides (3 × 104 cells per well). For EC109 cells, different endocytic pathway inhibitors were prepared and incubated with cells for 2 h. After the incubation, Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP was added and incubated with EC109 cells for 2 h. For KYSE520 cells, after adding Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP, it was immediately incubated with the four inhibitors for 4 h. Subsequently, 200 µL of 4% paraformaldehyde was added to fix the above EC109 and KYSE520 cells, and anti-fluorescence quencher containing DAPI was added to stain the cell nucleus. The cells were imaged and photographed under a confocal laser scanning microscope (200×).

## Biodistribution and imaging
EC109 and KYSE520 cells (5 × 106 cells /0.2 mL) were inoculated subcutaneously into the right axilla of female BALB/c nude mice weighing 18–20 g to establish a mouse tumor xenograft model. When the tumor size reached 500–600 mm3, the nude mice were injected with Cy5-labeled AS1411-HSA-LDP at a dose of 20 mg/kg via the tail vein. At the predetermined time point, the nude mice were anesthetized by inhalation of isoflurane, and the biodistribution of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP in the nude mice was observed and photographed using an in vivo imaging system (Tanon ABL X5). After the experiment, the nude mice were killed by CO2 method. The heart, liver, spleen, lung, kidney, and tumor tissues were obtained for in vitro imaging. Subsequently, the collected tumor tissues were placed in 4% paraformaldehyde. After dehydration with a 15-30% gradient sucrose solution, the samples were obtained and embedded in OCT. DAPI stain was added and incubated at room temperature in the dark for 10 min. After washing, an anti-fluorescence quenching sealing agent was added to seal the slides. Tumor tissue sections were obtained and images were collected under a confocal laser scanning microscope (200×).

## Preparation and characterization of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE
8.4 mg HSA-LDP-AE and 0.75 mg Sulfo-SMCC were added to 3 mL PBS and reacted on a shaker for 4 h under ice bath conditions. The unreacted Sulfo-SMCC was removed by centrifugation in an ultrafiltration tube (MWCO, 30 kDa) and resuspended in 500 µL PBS. 5 µL of 100 µM AS1411 solution was added to 750 µL ultrapure water, and 50 µL of 800 mM TCEP solution was added. Under the condition of an ice bath, the reaction was carried out on a shaker for 30 min. The AS1411 solution was mixed with the SMCC-reacted HSA-LDP-AE solution and reacted on a shaker overnight. The final product was placed in an ultrafiltration tube (MWCO, 30 kDa) and resuspended in 1 mL PBS.
Agarose gel electrophoresis was used to detect whether AS1411 was successfully coupled to HSA-LDP-AE. The gel was prepared with 1×TAE buffer solution (containing Super Red dye) containing 2% agarose. Free AS1411, AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE, and HSA-LDP-AE were mixed with the loading buffer on an ice box and then dropped into the gel loading well. The samples were electrophoresed at 120 V for 35 min. The gel imaging was observed and photographed under UV light using the Tanon-1600 Gel Image system.

## Cell viability assay
According to the reagent instructions, the viability of esophageal cancer cells was detected using the cell counting kit-8 (CCK-8) assay. EC109, KYSE520, and KYSE70 cells were seeded in 96-well plates at 4 × 103 cells/well and cultured in RPMI-1640 medium containing 10% FBS for 12 h. The cells were then incubated with different concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE. After incubation for 24 and 48 h, 10 µL CCK-8 solution was added to the wells, and incubated at 37 °C for 2 h, and the optical density (OD450) was measured using a microplate reader. Based on the data obtained, the cell survival curve was plotted and the IC50 value (50% inhibition concentration) was calculated.

## 5-Ethynyl-2’-deoxyuridine (EdU) assay
According to the supplier’s instructions, the EdU detection kit was used to evaluate the effect of drugs on the proliferation of esophageal cancer cells. EC109 and KYSE520 cells in the logarithmic growth phase were inoculated in 96-well plates at 4 × 103 cells/well and cultured for 24 h. Then AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (400 ng/mL) and AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (800 ng/mL) were added and cultured for 24 h. 10 µM EdU reagent (Beyotime, C0071S) was added to each well and incubated for 2 h. After washing with PBS containing 3% BSA for 3 times, 1 mL of 4% paraformaldehyde was added to each well and fixed at room temperature for 15 min. After washing with 3% BSA in PBS for 3 times, 100 µL of immunostaining strong permeabilization solution was added and incubated at room temperature for 15 min. Click reaction reagent was added and incubated at room temperature in the dark for 30 min. 35 µL of Hoechst 33,342 (1:1000) solution was added to each well and incubated at room temperature in the dark for 10 min. After the incubation, the washing solution was washed 3 times and the staining results were observed and photographed by fluorescence microscope (100×).

## Colony formation assay
Human esophageal cancer cells EC109 and KYSE520 in the logarithmic growth phase were seeded into six-well plates at 5 × 102 cells per well. Cultured at 37℃, 5% CO2 for 24 h. EC109 and KYSE520 cells were cultured with AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (1 ng/mL, 12.5 ng/mL, and 100 ng/mL) respectively. The culture lasted for about 1 week. The fresh cell culture medium was replaced every 2 days. Finally, the colonies were fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde for 15 min and stained with crystal violet for 30 min, then washed with water, air-dried, and photographed.

## Live-dead cell assay
Live/dead cells were evaluated and analyzed using Calcein-AM and PI dyes. EC109, KYSE520, and KYSE70 cells (4 × 103 cells/well) were incubated in 96-well plates for 24 h. AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE with a final concentration of 400 ng/mL and 800 ng/mL was added to the corresponding wells in the drug-treated groups. After 24 h, 100 µL of the prepared Calcein-AM/PI detection working solution (Beyotime, C2015S) was added, and incubated at 37℃ for 30 min. Observed and photographed with a fluorescence microscope (100×).

## Migration and invasion
Transwell experiment was used to detect cell migration and invasion ability. The experiment was mainly carried out using transwell chambers (Corning, Cat No. 3422). For migration experiments, the chambers added with 200 µL of basal culture medium were incubated in an incubator for 30 min to achieve the effect of balancing the polycarbonate membrane. After the incubation, 600 µL of complete culture medium solution containing 20% fetal bovine serum was added to the lower chamber, and different concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE cell suspension (1 × 105 cells per well) prepared with serum-free culture medium were added to the upper chamber. For invasion experiments, the matrix gel was diluted with serum-free culture medium and added to the upper chamber of the cell chamber, and gently shaken to spread the gel evenly on the membrane. Incubate at 37℃ for 2 h. Then a serum-free cell suspension containing AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (1 × 105 cells per well) was added to the upper chamber, and a growth medium solution containing 20% fetal bovine serum was added to the lower chamber. After 24 h, the cells were fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde and stained with crystal violet. After washing with PBS, all cells that migrated to the lower surface of the filter were observed under a fluorescence microscope. Random fields of view were selected and photographed (100×). After the photography, the cells were washed with 33% acetic acid, and the eluate was mixed and added to a 96-well plate. The light absorption value was measured at a wavelength of 570 nm using a microplate reader to analyze the number of cells that migrated and invaded the lower surface of the filter.

## Cell apoptosis analysis
The effect of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE on inducing apoptosis was detected using the Annexin V-FITC/PI cell apoptosis detection kit. EC109 and KYSE520 cells were seeded into 6-well plates at 3 × 105 cells/well and cultured to adhere to the wall. The culture medium was discarded, and different concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE prepared with complete culture medium were added and incubated for 24 h. The cells were washed with PBS, and all the cell pellet samples after centrifugation were collected in Annexin V binding solution (400 µL), Annexin V-FITC solution (5 µL) was added, and incubated in the dark for 15 min, followed by staining with PI solution (10 µL) and incubation in the dark for 2–5 min. Finally, all samples were detected by flow cytometry (BD FACSCelesta) to visualize the changes in cell apoptosis.

## Mitochondrial membrane potential measurement
JC-1 staining was used to detect MMP levels. Briefly, EC109 cells and KYSE520 cells were seeded in 6-well plates at 3 × 105 cells per well and cultured for 24 h. Subsequently, the cells were treated with different final concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (400 ng/mL, 800 ng/mL) for 24 h. Then, the cells were stained with JC-1 (1:200 dilution) at 37 °C for 20 min. After washing with PBS, 2 mL of cell culture medium was added to each well, and fluorescence analysis was performed under a fluorescence microscope and photographed (100×).

## ROS level analysis
According to the manufacturer’s instructions, the DCFH-DA probe was used to detect the ROS level in esophageal cancer cells. EC109 and KYSE520 cells were seeded in 6-well plates at 3 × 105 cells per well and cultured for 24 h. Subsequently, a blank control group and an AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE group were set up, and the concentrations of the drug-treated groups were 400 ng/mL and 800 ng/mL, respectively. After 24 h of incubation, 2 mL DCFH-DA diluted with serum-free medium at a final concentration of 10 µM was added to each well. Incubated in the dark at 37 °C for 20 min, washed three times with serum-free culture medium, and observed green fluorescence using a fluorescence microscope to evaluate ROS levels and photographed (100×).

## Western blot analysis
Western blot was used to detect the expression levels of NCL, PARP1, BAX, Bcl-2, and cytochrome c (Cyto C) proteins. Cells were lysed with RIPA lysis buffer containing protease inhibitors for 1 h under ice bath conditions. Protein concentration was determined using the BCA protein detection kit. Samples with equal protein concentrations were loaded onto a protein separation system, protein samples were separated by SDS-PAGE, and then the proteins were transferred to polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) membranes at 200 mA for 2 h. The PVDF membranes were blocked with 5% milk for 2 h at room temperature. After washing three times with TBST buffer, the membranes were incubated with diluted corresponding primary antibodies at 4℃ overnight. After washing with TBST buffer, the membranes were incubated with HRP-conjugated secondary antibodies on a shaker at room temperature for 1 h. The PVDF membranes were visualized using a chemiluminescent detection kit and imaged using a gel imaging system.

## In vivo anti-tumor efficacy

## Anti-tumor study of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE in KYSE520 tumor-bearing mice
Female thymus-free BALB/c nude mice were purchased from Hangzhou Ziyuan Laboratory Animal Technology Co., Ltd (Hangzhou, China). To study the effect of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE on tumor growth, KYSE520 cells (4.5 × 106 wells) were subcutaneously implanted in the right axilla of 18–20 g female BALB/c nude mice. When the tumor volume reached 60–80 mm3, the nude mice were randomly divided into 3 groups, with 5 mice in each group. The control group did not receive any treatment; the drug-treated group was given different concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (0.6 mg/kg, 1.2 mg/kg) through the tail vein once a week for a total of 2 times. The body weight and tumor volume of mice were recorded every other day. The formula for calculating tumor volume is V=(a×b2)/2 (V is the tumor volume, a is the tumor’s long diameter, and b is the tumor’s short diameter). The tumor growth inhibition rate was calculated as [1-(tumor terminal volume of the drug-treated group-tumor initial volume of the drug-treated group)/(tumor terminal volume of control group-tumor initial volume of control group)]×100%. At the end of treatment, all nude mice were killed by the CO2 method, and tumor tissues were isolated, photographed, and weighed. The main organ tissues and tumor tissues were removed were further analyzed. For the histological analysis, the heart, liver, spleen, lung, kidney, stomach, and small intestine were fixed in 4% paraformaldehyde for hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) staining. Tumor apoptosis and proliferation were studied by immunohistochemical (IHC) staining analysis in tumor sections, and nucleolin expression in different groups was analyzed.

## Anti-tumor activity in EC109 cell xenograft in athymic mice
EC109 cells (5 × 106 cells) were suspended in 200 µL PBS and injected subcutaneously into the right axilla of 18–20 g female BALB/c nude mice. When the tumor volume reached 60–80 mm3, the nude mice were randomly divided into 2 groups, 6 nude mice in each group, including a control group and a drug-treated group. The control group did not receive any treatment, and the drug-treated group was injected with 200 µL AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE (1.6 mg/kg) via the tail vein once a week for a total of 2 times in two weeks. The tumor volume and weight of the mice were monitored every 2 days. After 2 weeks of drug administration, the nude mice were killed, and the tumor tissues were collected, photographed, and weighed. The main organs of the nude mice were removed for histological analysis. The formula for calculating tumor volume and tumor growth inhibition rate is the same as above.

## Statistical analysis
All data were obtained from at least 3 independent experiments and presented as mean ± standard deviation. SPSS software was used for statistical testing and Graphpad Prism software was used for plotting. The t-test was used to compare between two groups, and a one-way analysis of variance was used to compare between multiple groups. P < 0.05 was considered statistically significant.

## Results

## Preparation and characterization of AS1411-HSA-LDP
NCL is a highly malignant biomarker that is highly expressed in cancer and is closely associated with poor prognosis. In this study, the relative expression levels of NCL in KYSE520 cells, KYSE70 cells, and EC109 cells were analyzed by Western blot. As shown in Fig.  (A-B), NCL was overexpressed on the membrane of the three esophageal cancer cells, and the expression level of NCL in KYSE520 cells was significantly higher than that in KYSE70 and EC109 cells. The expression level of NCL in KYSE70 cells was intermediate, and the expression level of NCL in EC109 cells was the lowest. This result shows that NCL can be used as a potential target for the treatment of esophageal cancer. AS1411, an aptamer that specifically targets NCL. In order to evaluate whether the aptamer AS1411 was successfully conjugated to the recombinant protein HSA-LDP, the constructed conjugate was identified by agarose gel electrophoresis. Theoretically, the speed of electrophoresis at this time is only related to the molecular weight. The larger the molecular weight, the slower the electrophoresis speed. As shown in Fig.  C, compared with free AS1411(lane 2), AS1411-HSA-LDP has a slow electrophoresis speed, resulting in a higher band position, which is due to the increase in molecular weight after coupling (lane 3). The recombinant protein without AS1411 modification does not show a signal itself (lane 4). This illustrated that the aptamer AS1411 was conjugated to the recombinant protein HSA-LDP.
 

## AS1411-HSA-LDP has binding capacity to esophageal cancer cells and can be efficiently uptaken by esophageal cancer cells with high NCL expression
The binding activity of AS1411-HSA-LDP to esophageal cancer cells was analyzed by flow cytometry. As shown in Fig.  (A-B), esophageal cancer cells treated with Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP all emerged fluorescence signals, and as the concentration of AS1411-HSA-LDP increased, the fluorescence intensity gradually increased, indicating that the conjugate could show concentration-dependent affinity with esophageal cancer cells. Afterwards, confocal laser scanning microscopy was used to analyze the uptake of AS1411-HSA-LDP by esophageal cancer cells. After 0.1 mg/mL AS1411-HSA-LDP was co-incubated with EC109, KYSE520, and KYSE70 cells for 4 h, as shown in Fig.  C, it was clear that Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP could be uptake by esophageal cancer cells. In KYSE520 esophageal cancer cells with higher NCL expression levels, the red fluorescence signal was the strongest, indicating that KYSE520 cells had the highest uptake of AS1411-HSA-LDP. In EC109 and KYSE70 cells with relatively low NCL expression levels, the red fluorescence signal was weak, indicating that the uptake of AS1411-HSA-LDP by these two cells was less. It was speculated that AS1411-HSA-LDP could interact with NCL overexpressed on the cell membrane and could be specifically uptaken by tumor cells.
 

## Endocytosis pathway investigation
It is well known that the endocytosis mechanism of drugs plays a vital role in the final therapeutic effect. In this study, four endocytosis inhibitors, including EIPA, Gen, MβCD, and CPZ were used to investigate the endocytosis mechanism of AS1411-HSA-LDP. As shown in Fig.  A and C, when EC109 cells were treated with EIPA, Gen, MβCD, and CPZ, the fluorescence intensity was weakened after Gen and CPZ treatment, indicating that in the presence of Gen and CPZ, the cellular uptake decreased; while the fluorescence intensity did not decrease after EIPA and MβCD treatment, indicating that caveolin and clathrin-dependent endocytosis pathways play a major role in EC109 cell uptake. As shown in Fig.  B and D, in KYSE520 cells, the fluorescence intensity was weakened after treatment with EIPA, Gen, and CPZ. The fluorescence intensity of cells treated with EIPA decreased most significantly, indicating that the cellular uptake was significantly reduced, indicating that AS1411-HSA-LDP mainly enters KYSE520 esophageal cancer cells through the macropinocytosis pathway, while the clathrin pathway and caveolin pathway were potential endocytosis pathways. The fluorescence intensity after MβCD treatment did not decrease compared with the control group, indicating that the lipid raft pathway has almost no effect on KYSE520 cell uptake.
 Confocal laser scanning microscopy was used to further verify the endocytosis pathway of AS1411-HSA-LDP into EC109 and KYSE520 esophageal cancer cells. A decrease in red fluorescence signal was observed in EC109 cells pretreated with Gen and CPZ, indicating that caveolin- and clathrin-mediated endocytosis pathways play a major role in the uptake of AS1411-HSA-LDP by EC109 cells (Fig.  E). In KYSE520 cells treated with EIPA, Gen, and CPZ, red fluorescence decreased, and the decrease in the EIPA group was the most obvious, indicating that AS1411-HSA-LDP mainly enters KYSE520 esophageal cancer cells through the macropinocytosis pathway, and clathrin- and caveolin-mediated endocytosis was weak (Fig.  F). The above experimental results were consistent with the results of flow cytometry.

## Biodistribution of AS1411-HSA-LDP in BALB/c nude mouse xenograft model
After confirming that AS1411-HSA-LDP can be specifically uptaken by esophageal cancer cells, we then constructed EC109 and KYSE520 xenograft nude mouse models to evaluate the tissue distribution and targeted enrichment of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP in nude mouse xenograft models. AS1411-HSA-LDP labeled with Cy5 was injected through the tail vein, and the fluorescence images of EC109 and KYSE520 xenograft nude mice from 0 to 408 h were collected using a small animal imaging system (Tanon ABL X5). Over time, the fluorescence signal at the tumor site gradually increased, and fluorescence enrichment was still observed at 408 h (Fig.  A). In order to more intuitively illustrate the targeting of AS1411-HSA-LDP, the nude mouse tumor tissue, and main organs were peeled off 408 h after injection for in vitro imaging. As shown in Fig.  (B-C), significantly stronger fluorescence signals could be detected in the tumors of EC109 and KYSE520 groups, and no obvious signals appeared in other organs. It was worth noting that because the liver and kidney were the main organs of drug metabolism and excretion respectively, especially the macromolecular drugs were easy to stay in the liver, making the weak fluorescence signal appear in the liver. Subsequently, the tumor tissues of nude mice with EC109 and KYSE520 cells xenografts were observed simultaneously. The experimental results showed that a stronger fluorescence signal was displayed at the tumor tissue of nude mouse transplanted tumor model inoculated with KYSE520 cells with high NCL expression (Fig.  D). In order to detect the penetration ability of AS1411-HSA-LDP in tumor tissue, tumor tissues were collected from nude mice 408 h after injection of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP, and tumor tissue sections were sliced and analyzed. As shown in Fig.  E, the confocal laser scanning microscope image of the tumor slices clearly showed a significantly stronger fluorescence signal of Cy5-AS1411-HSA-LDP in KYSE520 tumor tissues with high nucleolin expression levels than EC109 tumor tissues, indicating that AS1411-HSA-LDP showed significant uptake in the tumor cells of KYSE520 xenograft nude mice, which was consistent with the results of the in vitro uptake experiment. These results confirmed that AS1411-HSA-LDP had good in vivo tumor targeting ability and could specifically target tumor tissues with high NCL expression levels.
 

## AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE strongly inhibited the proliferation of esophageal cancer cells
In order to evaluate whether the aptamer AS1411 is successfully conjugated with HSA-LDP-AE, the constructed drug was identified by agarose gel electrophoresis. As shown in Fig.  A, compared with free AS1411 (lane 2), the electrophoresis speed of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE is slower and the band position is higher, indicating that its molecular weight was larger (lane 3). HSA-LDP-AE has no fluorescent signal in the gel (lane 4). The experimental results showed that AS1411 was conjugated with HSA-LDP-AE. The CCK-8 method was used to detect the inhibitory activity of the drug on KYSE520, KYSE70, and EC109 cells. As shown in Fig.  (B-D), after 24 h of administration, the proliferation activity of esophageal cancer cells was inhibited in a dose-dependent manner. In contrast, the cell survival rate after 48 h of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE administration was significantly lower than that at 24 h (Fig.  (E-G)). In KYSE520 cells with high nucleolin expression levels, AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE had a stronger inhibitory effect on cell proliferation than KYSE70 and EC109 cells at 24 h and 48 h, with the lowest IC50 value (Table  ). The results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE had specifically targeted killing activity against KYSE520 esophageal cancer cells with high nucleolin expression. We also analyzed cell proliferation using 5-ethynyl-2’-deoxyuridine staining analysis. In this experiment, the nuclei of all cells were stained blue, and the proliferating cells showed very bright green fluorescence under a fluorescence microscope. As shown in Fig.  (H, I), the green fluorescence signal in the control group was higher, while the green fluorescence signal decreased in the presence of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE, indicating that the proliferation ability of esophageal cancer cells was inhibited after administration. In addition, we also conducted a colony formation experiment. As shown in Fig.  J, compared with the control group, the number of esophageal cancer cell clones formed by AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE at a low concentration (12.5 ng/mL) showed a decreasing trend. When the concentration reached 100 ng/mL, the number of cell colonies decreased significantly. Fluorescence microscopy was used to observe the cell survival and death after double fluorescence staining with Calcein-AM (green indicated live cells) and PI (red indicated dead cells). The experimental results were shown in Fig.  (K-M). Compared with the control group, the red color of EC109, KYSE520, and KYSE70 cells incubated with AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE increased in a dose-dependent manner. The above results showed that the drug with a strong ability to inhibit the proliferation of esophageal cancer cells.
 

## AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE inhibited the migration and invasion of esophageal cancer cells
The transwell chamber assay was used to investigate whether AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could inhibit the migration and invasion of esophageal cancer cells. The experimental results shown in Fig.  (A-B), AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE significantly suppressed the migration ability of EC109 and KYSE520 cells, and showed a dose-dependent characteristic. We further evaluated the inhibitory effect of the drug on cell invasion. Esophageal cancer cells were inoculated in a transwell chamber coated with matrix gel. The experimental results shown in Fig.  (C-D), AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE inhibited the invasion ability of EC109 and KYSE520 cells in a concentration-dependent manner. The above results strongly indicate that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could inhibit the migration and invasion of EC109 and KYSE520 cells in vitro.
 

## AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE induced apoptosis of esophageal cancer cells
Flow cytometry was used to detect whether AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could induce cell apoptosis. As shown in Fig.  (A-D), compared with the control group, the drug group showed a significantly increased apoptosis induction effect, which was concentration-dependent. When esophageal cancer cells were given the same concentration of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE, the apoptosis rate of KYSE520 cells with nucleolin overexpression was higher than that of EC109 cells. The experimental results show that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could show stronger apoptosis induction ability in esophageal cancer cells with high NCL protein expression levels.
 We further treated esophageal cancer cells with AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE to explore the mechanism of drug-induced apoptosis and measured the expression of related proteins. As shown in Fig.  (E-J), western blotting analysis showed that compared with the control group, treatment of esophageal cancer cells with AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE inhibited PARP1 expression and significantly increased the expression of mitochondrial protein Cyto C. It also increased the expression of pro-apoptotic protein Bax and inhibited the expression of anti-apoptotic protein Bcl-2. Therefore, the results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE mediated apoptosis of esophageal cancer cells through the intrinsic mitochondrial apoptosis pathway.

## AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE induced apoptosis was associated with decreased MMP levels and increased ROS levels
The collapse of MMP was an important factor leading to apoptosis mediated by the mitochondrial apoptosis pathway. Changes in mitochondrial membrane potential were a landmark event for whether esophageal cancer cells underwent apoptosis. In order to investigate whether mitochondria were involved in AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE-induced apoptosis, using JC-1 probe to detect the ∆Ψm of esophageal cancer cells after AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE treatment for 24 h. The experimental results shown in Fig.  (A-B), the control group showed brighter red fluorescence. At this time, JC-1 aggregated in the matrix of mitochondria to form polymers (J-aggregates), and the mitochondrial membrane potential in the cells was higher. When the concentration of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE was 400 ng/mL, compared with the control group, EC109 and KYSE520 cells showed obvious green fluorescence. At this time, JC-1 was a monomer and the mitochondrial membrane potential in the cells decreased. When the drug concentration reached 800 ng/mL, the green fluorescence increased, and the mitochondrial membrane potential further decreased. The above results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could cause a decrease in MMP in esophageal cancer cells, leading to early apoptosis of tumor cells, which was consistent with the results of flow cytometry.
 ROS consists of superoxide radical anions, hydrogen peroxide, and hydroxyl radicals. Excessive ROS in cells promotes DNA damage and cell death. In order to explore whether AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE induces apoptosis of esophageal cancer cells by increasing ROS levels, we observed the effect of the drug on ROS generation by fluorescence microscopy. DCFH-DA is a fluorescent probe used to detect intracellular reactive oxygen species. DCFH-DA itself has no fluorescence and can freely pass through the cell membrane into the cell, where it is hydrolyzed by esterase to generate DCFH. Reactive oxygen in cells can oxidize non-fluorescent DCFH to generate fluorescent DCF, and the green fluorescence signal is proportional to the level of reactive oxygen. The experimental results were shown in Fig.  C. In EC109 cells and KYSE520 cells, the green fluorescence intensity of the higher concentration group was significantly higher than that of the low concentration group and the control group. These results indicated that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could induce apoptosis of esophageal cancer cells by increasing intracellular ROS.

## AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE inhibited the expression of nucleolin in esophageal cancer cells
After treating cells with different concentrations of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE, the drug’s ability to regulate nucleolin levels was analyzed by western blot. The experimental results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could effectively downregulate nucleolin levels in KYSE520, KYSE70, and EC109 cells in a dose-dependent manner (Fig.  A and B). The results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could reduce the level of nucleolin.
 

## In vivo antitumor efficacy of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE

## The therapeutic efficacy of the drug in KYSE520 xenograft tumors in vivo
The antitumor effect of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE was assessed in KYSE520 tumor-bearing female BALB/c nude mice (Fig.  A). The result shown in Fig.  B, AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could significantly inhibit the growth of esophageal cancer xenograft tumors. At doses of 0.6 and 1.2 mg/kg, the inhibitory effects of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE on tumor growth were 49.9% and 70.1%. During the experiment, the nude mice in each treatment group tolerated the treatment well and did not lose weight significantly (Fig.  C), indicating that the drug treatment has good safety. After the experiment, the tumor samples of the nude mice were peeled off, photographed and weighed, and the average tumor weight of each group was calculated. As shown in Fig.  (D-E), compared with the control group, the drug treatment group could slow down the growth of tumor tissue. Next, we detected the expression levels of NCL, Ki67, PARP, and cleaved caspase-3 proteins in tumor tissues through immunohistochemistry to study the mechanism of action of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE on esophageal cancer cells. As shown in Fig.  F, the control group showed strong staining of NCL and Ki67, indicating their highly positive expression in the control group; while the AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE treatment group showed decreased staining of NCL and Ki67, indicating their low expression in the drug-treatment group; the experimental results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE significantly inhibited the proliferation of esophageal cancer cells. We further confirmed the occurrence of apoptosis by PARP and cleaved caspase-3 immunostaining. The experimental results were shown in Fig.  F. After treatment with AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE, PARP decreased and cleaved caspase-3 increased.
 After the experiment, the main organs (heart, liver, spleen, lung, kidney, stomach, and small intestine) of the nude mice were peeled off for histological examination. Compared with the control group, no obvious tissue damage or morphological changes were observed in major organs in the drug treatment group (Fig.  G). The results indicated that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE with good biosafety. In summary, AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE was a promising anti-esophageal cancer drug candidate without obvious toxicity.

## The therapeutic effect of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE in EC109 xenograft tumor in vivo
After confirming that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE has a good anti-tumor effect in KYSE520 xenograft tumors, we further studied the anti-tumor effect of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE in EC109 xenograft tumors with low nucleolin expression. The experimental results were shown in Fig.  A, at the dose of 1.6 mg/kg, the inhibitory effect of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE on tumor growth was 53.8%. During the two weeks of administration, the body weight of nude mice in each group did not decrease significantly (Fig.  B). After the experiment, the tumor tissues of nude mice in each group were removed and photographed. The final average tumor weight of nude mice in the drug treatment group was lower than that of the control group (Fig.  C and D). After the experiment, H&E staining was used to observe the histological characteristics. No obvious signs of damage to important organs in the administration group were observed (Fig.  E). In conclusion, AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE, as a potent nucleolin target inhibitor, showed good anti-tumor activity, highlighting its advantages in the effective treatment of esophageal cancer.
 

## Discussion
Esophageal cancer is one of the most common malignancies in the world, with high morbidity and mortality. More than 70% of ESCC patients were already in the intermediate or advanced stage at the time of diagnosis. Concurrent chemoradiotherapy was the standard treatment for patients with advanced ESCC. However, the 5-year survival rate of ESCC patients was low, about 20% [ ]. With the vigorous development of molecular-targeted drugs and the revelation of genetic mutations in esophageal cancer, molecular-targeted therapy has become a promising strategy in the treatment of esophageal cancer [ ]. However, the effectiveness of targeted therapy for ESCC was still limited, and drug resistance still existed. There was an urgent need to develop new candidate drugs for the treatment of esophageal cancer. NCL was a multifunctional nuclear protein that was overexpressed in the cell membrane, cytoplasm, and nucleus of cancer cells and played an important role in regulating the physiological activities of cells [ ]. Considering the key role of NCL in cancer development, some studies have studied NCL as a key receptor for therapeutic agents and imaging probes. For example, the new aptamer TB-5 targeting NCL could be used for the diagnosis and treatment of bladder cancer [ ]. NCL was involved in the initiation and transduction of EGFR and CXCR4 signals, upregulates the expression of Ki67, and affects the biological behavior of ESCC, NCL siRNA could inhibit the migration and invasion of ECA109 cells [ ]. AS1411 was an anti-tumor aptamer that specifically recognized membrane-nuclear shuttling nucleolar proteins [ ]. Currently, AS1411 is widely used in the medical field as a targeting ligand for targeted drug delivery [ ]. Given the important role of NCL targets in the treatment of esophageal cancer, finding new drugs that could inhibit NCL with AS1411 as a targeting molecule may be an effective strategy to enhance the treatment of esophageal cancer.
Previous studies have found that HSA-LDP-AE conjugate could significantly inhibit the growth of lung cancer PG-BE1 xenograft tumors [ ]. However, the efficacy of HSA-LDP-AE in esophageal cancer is unknown. In order to further improve the anti-esophageal cancer efficacy and targeting properties, a novel aptamer-mediated conjugate drug AS1411-HSA-LDP without the lidamycin enediyne chromophore was first developed with nucleolin as the target. For the first time, AS1411-HSA-LDP was found to exhibit concentration-dependent affinity characteristics with EC cells, which could be specifically uptaken by EC cells. In KYSE520 cells with overexpressing NCL, AS1411-HSA-LDP was mainly uptaken by cells through the macropinocytosis pathway. In small animal in vivo imaging, we found that AS1411-HSA-LDP could selectively and long-term enrich at the tumor site of the KYSE520 esophageal cancer xenograft nude mouse model, which is related to the albumin’s ability to prolong the drug half-life. Tumor slice images showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP was significantly uptaken by tumor cells in KYSE520 tumor-bearing mice with high nucleolin expression levels, which may be due to the massive influx of drugs by esophageal cancer cells through multiple endocytic pathways.
Before clinical application, antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) had problems such as poor drug delivery efficiency, limited chemotherapy effects, severe immune responses, and drug resistance [ ]. Compared with ADCs, aptamer-drug conjugates (ApDCs) were highly effective targeted cancer therapy drugs with the advantages of smaller size, higher chemical stability, lower immunogenicity, faster tissue penetration, and easy engineering design [ ]. Based on the above situation, in this study, HSA-LDP-AE was subsequently conjugated to AS1411 through a non-cleavable SMCC linker to form an aptamer-conjugated drug AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE with an enediyne chromophore of lidamycin. Interestingly, we found that the coupling of AS1411 with HSA-LDP-AE did not change its targeting of NCL. On the contrary, the cell proliferation inhibitory activity of AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE in tumor cells was dependent on the dosage and NCL expression. Changes in the migration and invasion ability of tumor cells were another key indicator for evaluating the efficacy of tumor treatment. AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could effectively reduce the migration and invasion ability of esophageal cancer cells. Increased levels of intracellular ROS may lead to a decrease in MMP, which may lead to the release of cytochrome c in mitochondria, thereby activating caspase family apoptosis signals and inducing tumor cell apoptosis [ , ]. In this study, we found that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could effectively induce an increase in ROS, a significant decrease in MMP, and a significant increase in Cyto C protein, indicating that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could effectively induce apoptosis of esophageal cancer cells, which could also be confirmed by the results of Annexin V/PI double staining by flow cytometry. It is worth noting that the apoptosis rate of KYSE520 cells was significantly increased compared with EC109 cells. The results showed that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could specifically target KYSE520 cells to induce apoptosis.
Next, in animal experiments, we found that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could significantly inhibit the tumor volume and tumor weight of esophageal cancer xenograft tumors in nude mice. No nude mouse death or weight loss was found during the experiment, indicating that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE was a safe and reliable way to treat esophageal cancer. It was worth mentioning that compared with EC109 xenograft tumors, the AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE with a stronger inhibitory effect on NCL overexpressed KYSE520 xenograft tumors, which also confirms the specific anti-tumor efficacy of the drug in vivo. In order to study the effect of the drug on cancer proliferation and apoptosis, we performed IHC staining to detect the expression of Ki-67, cleaved caspase-3, and PARP. The Ki-67 proliferation index is an important cancer marker [ ]. Compound 7e significantly promoted the expression of cleaved caspase-3, a major marker of apoptosis, while the level of PARP decreased accordingly, further proving that 7e has a significant apoptosis-inducing effect [ ]. In animal experiments, it was found that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could significantly reduce the expression of Ki-67 and PARP, upregulate the expression of cleaved-caspase 3, which had the effect of inhibiting tumor cell proliferation and inducing apoptosis of tumor cells. In summary, AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could exert significant anti-tumor effects on esophageal cancer both in vivo and in vitro, providing a promising strategy for the treatment of esophageal cancer.

## Conclusion
In summary, we designed and prepared a new type of aptamer-conjugated drug AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE. In vivo and in vitro studies have demonstrated that AS1411-HSA-LDP-AE could exert significant anti-tumor effects, which is an effective strategy for the treatment of NCL-positive esophageal cancer. This study provides an innovative idea for the development of novel ApDCs and a promising approach for targeted therapy of esophageal cancer.
