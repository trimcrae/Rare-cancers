## Oncolytic virotherapy improves disease-free survival in prostate cancer
The viral immunotherapy CAN-2409 (Candel Therapeutics) combined with standard-of-care radiotherapy improved disease-free survival at 4.5 years by 15%, compared to radiotherapy alone, in >700 patients with localized prostate cancer. The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial also reported a 26% increase in pathological complete response rate in the experimental group.
CAN-2409 consists of a non-replicative adenovirus designed to deliver the herpes simplex virus thymidine kinase into tumors, which converts valacyclovir (administered orally) into a toxic compound.

## Complement inhibitor improved symptoms of Guillain–Barré syndrome
The C1q inhibitory MAb ANX005 (Annexon) ameliorated Guillain–Barré syndrome (GBS) compared to standard-of-care intravenous immunoglobulin therapy or plasmapheresis. A real-world study, in which each of 79 participants of a completed Phase 3 trial of ANX005 was matched with a subject from a global GBS patient registry, reported clinically meaningful improvement in muscle strength and less need for mechanical ventilation in the experimental group.
ANX005, which is administered in a single intravenous dose, targets the complement component C1q. There is no approved treatment for GBS.

## Two Covid-19/influenza combination vaccines fast-tracked by FDA
The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has granted its Fast-Track designation to two combination vaccines targeting COVID-19 and influenza in adults ≥50 years of age. Both regimens, which combine licensed vaccines, are being tested in Phase 1/2 trials.
The adjuvanted protein subunit COVID-19 vaccine NVX-CoV2373 (Novavax) is combined with either the trivalent protein influenza vaccine Fluzone High-Dose or the recombinant trivalent protein influenza vaccine Flublok (both Sanofi).

## Peptide vaccine shows signs of activity in colorectal cancer
The microbe-derived peptide immunotherapy EO4010 (Enterome) with the PD-1 and VEGF inhibitors nivolumab and bevacizumab induced stable disease in three of five patients with microsatellite-stable, unresectable metastatic colorectal cancer, as reported from the Phase 1/2 AUDREY trial. In a cohort without bevacizumab, two cases of stable disease and one partial response were observed in 17 subjects.
EO4010 consists of five microbial-derived peptides that mimic tumor-associated antigens, along with a CD4+ T-cell stimulating peptide.

## Cell immunotherapy alleviates knee osteoarthritis
The macrophage-reprogramming immunotherapeutic Allocetra (Enlivex) was safe and reduced joint pain and improved function in 12 patients with moderate-to-severe knee osteoarthritis, according to interim results of a dose-escalation, randomized Phase 1/2 trial.
Allocetra is an off-the-shelf immunotherapeutic based on donor mononuclear cells induced to a stable apoptotic state, whose injection into the knee leads to the restoration of macrophage homeostasis.

## 96% antibody persistence three years after chikungunya vaccination
The chikungunya vaccine VLA1553 (Ixchiq, Valneva) induced a 96% sero-response rate at the three-year mark in almost 300 healthy adults enrolled in the Phase 3 VLA1553–303 trial.
VLA1553, the only approved vaccine for the prevention of chikungunya virus infection, is a single-dose, live attenuated vaccine against the mosquito-borne viral infection, which is a significant public health burden in tropical regions of the world.

## Engager of innate immunity improves immunotherapy of NSCLC
The bispecific engager AFM24 (Affimed) combined with the PD-L1 inhibitor atezolizumab was safe and induced a > 20% overall response rate and >70% disease-control rate in 60 evaluable patients with heavily pretreated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). The regimen had been previously fast-tracked by FDA.
AFM24 is a bispecific MAb targeting CD16A, which is an Fc receptor present on several types of innate immune cells, and an epidermal growth factor receptor overexpressed by some solid cancers including NSCLC. It is designed to mediate antibody-dependent tumor cell phagocytosis.

## Inhaled interleukin immunotherapy shows promise in NSCLC
The interleukin-based immunotherapy KB707 (Krystal Biotech) induced an objective response rate of 27% and a disease-control rate of 73% in heavily pretreated NSCLC patients. Interim results of the dose-escalation Phase 1 KYANITE-1 trial also reported a good safety profile
KB707 consists of inhaled HSV engineered to express the cytokines IL-2 and IL-12, which induce an intratumoral immune reaction. It had been fast-tracked by FDA.

## CpG-adjuvanted hepatitis B vaccine was immunogenic in HIV patients
The hepatitis B vaccine HepB-CpG (Heplisav, Dynavax) demonstrated superior immunogenicity, compared to an alum-adjuvanted vaccine, in HIV patients and primary non-responders. The randomized, open-label Phase 3 BEe-HIVe trial tested two and three doses in >500 hIV subjects without serological evidence of response to prior vaccination. The seroprotection rates for the two- and three-dose cohorts were 93% and 99%, respectively, compared to 81% for the group receiving three doses of the control vaccine.
HepB-CpG contains the recombinant hepatitis B surface antigen adjuvanted with a 22-meric CpG oligonucleotide.

## Oral peanut allergy immunotherapy was safe in early trial
A sublingual immunotherapy tablet (ALK) for treatment of peanut allergy was safe with no serious adverse events or anaphylaxis in 30 subjects enrolled in the dose-escalation Phase 1/2 ALLIANCE trial. The optimal dose for the challenge part of the trial has been selected.
The immunotherapeutic is designed to desensitize allergic patients to the peanut protein, to which around 1–2% of children are allergic in the developed world.
