REDOX REPORTJ. YAN ET AL.YanJunjie ]. Previous studies have shown that the occurrence and development of osteoporosis are closely related to the mechanism of cellular oxidative stress [ ]. Excessive reactive oxygen species (ROS) can reduce the activities of related enzymes, thus increasing the apoptosis of osteoblasts and inhibiting bone formation [ ]. The important cause of oxidative damage is the impaired mechanism of removing reactive oxygen species, and its occurrence mainly occurs in mitochondria [ ]. The accumulation of ROS activates osteoclasts and decreases bone mineral density [ ]. At the same time, a large amount of ROS will destroy the membrane potential of mitochondria, oxidative phosphorylation uncoupling disruption, and significantly increase the apoptosis rate [ ]. Therefore, inhibiting oxidative damage is of great significance for the treatment of osteoporosis.
Kelch-like epichlorohydrin-associated protein 1 (Keap1) and nuclear erythrocyte 2-related factor 2 (Nrf2) play important roles in anti-oxidative stress [ ]. Keap1 and Nrf2 are present in the cytoplasm in a binding form that is not sensed by the body [ ]. In some cases, Nrf2 can be dissociated and transported into the nucleus to promote the transcription of antioxidation-related genes [ ]. Nrf2 is a direct downstream of ROS, which can promote the expression of antioxidant response element (ARE) -dependent genes, keep cells in a reducing homeostasis, so as to protect cells and reduce apoptosis, inhibit oxidative damage, balance oxidative mediators, and thus reduce the occurrence of osteoporosis induced by oxidative stress [ ]. Elucidating the mechanisms by which oxidative stress triggers apoptosis can provide insights into the development of evidence-based strategies for treating osteoporosis.
Studies have shown that microRNA (miRNA) are involved in the pathogenesis of osteoporosis and are considered as potential therapeutic targets [ ]. The miRNA can play a regulatory role on target mRNA by destroying its stability and inhibiting its translation [ ]. Zhao demonstrated that miRNA-200a regulates liver inflammation and lipid deposition by regulating the Keap1/Nrf2 pathway [ ]. Peng found that miRNA-200a attenuated oxidative stress, inflammation and apoptosis in colitis by activating Nrf2 [ ]. This may provide new insights into the molecular mechanisms of osteoporosis progression and reveal that miRNA may be explored as a novel therapeutic target for postmenopausal osteoporosis patients.
Histone deacetylase (HDAC) is a class of enzymes that can remove lysine residues from acetylation marks on histones and other proteins, affecting gene expression and cell signaling [ ]. HDAC inhibitors (HDI) activate acetylation of histones as well as non-histone proteins, resulting in relaxation of chromosome structure and alteration of signaling pathway transmission [ ]. Entinostat (MS-275) is a class I histone deacetylase inhibitor that selectively inhibits HDAC1 and HDAC3 [ ]. Therefore, MS-275 can continuously up-regulate the level of histone acetylation in cells, and make heritable changes in genes in the way of epigenetic modification, thereby changing cell functions [ ]. Lee showed that histone deacetylase inhibitor (MS-275) promoted the differentiation of human dental pulp stem cells into odontoblast-like cells independent of MAPK signaling system [ ]. Mota found that MS-275 quenched the inflammatory response and microglia/macrophage activation in a mouse model of permanent cerebral ischemia [ ]. Current studies have shown that MS-275 can reduce proinflammatory cytokines and reactive oxygen species, and exert anti-tumor activity against solid and hematological malignancies [ ]. Whether MS-275 is involved in protecting osteoblasts from oxidative stress injury is not clear. The aim of this study is to investigate the protective mechanism of MS-275 against oxidative stress injury in osteoblasts. Therefore, this study aims to investigate the protective mechanism of MS-275 against oxidative stress injury of osteoblasts by inducing the establishment of oxidative stress model in vitro, in order to provide a new drug target for the treatment of osteoporosis.

2.

## Material and method
2.1.

## Bioinformatics analysis
An upstream analysis of the keap1 protein was conducted in the miRDB.txt, miRTarBase.txt, and TargetScan.txt databases, resulting in the identification of miRNAs associated with keap1, which were subsequently verified through qPCR.

2.2.

## Cell culture
MC3T3-E1 cells (mouse embryo osteoblast precursor cells) were obtained from cell-cook (Guangzhou, China) and treated as follows: (1) NC group: Cells were cultured in complete Dulbecco’s Modified Eagle’s Medium (DMEM); (2) MS-275 group: Cells were co-cultured with 100 nm MS-275 for 3 days; (3) Carbonyl cyanide 3 chlorophenylhydrazone (CCCP) group: cultured CCCP 5μl/ml with cells in complete DMEM for 30 min, and then cultured with complete DMEM for 3 days; (4) CCCP+MS-275 group: CCCP 5μl /ml and cells were initially cultured in complete DMEM for 30 min, followed by replacement with complete DMEM supplemented with 100 nm MS-275 for a subsequent 3-day co-culture period.

2.3

## Cell viability assessment
MC3T3-E1 cells were inoculated into 96-well plates, and Cell viability of different groups was detected using Cell Counting Kit-8 Kit (CCK-8, Solarbio, USA). At 60% cell density, the cells were treated with CCCP and different concentrations of MS-275 (0, 50, 100, 200, 400, 800 nmol/l). The cells were incubated with 10μl /100 ml CCK-8 reagent for 2 h away from light, and OD values of the cells were measured at 450 nm using a microplate reader (BioTek Instru-ments, Inc., USA).

2.4.

## Small interfering RNA (siRNA) transfection
To silence miR-200a, cells were transfected with miR-200a siRNA (GenePharma, China) following the manufacturer's instructions. After 5 h of transfection, the medium was replaced with complete medium. To ensure accurate evaluation of transfection efficiency, a qPCR assay was conducted 48 h post-transfection, as recommended by the siRNA provider to confirm the knockdown of miR-200a.

2.5.

## Cell apoptosis assay
MC3T3-E1 cells were inoculated on a 6-well plate. When the cells reached 60%, CCCP was treated at 5μl /ml for 40 min, then replaced with a complete medium with 100 nm concentration of MS-275 for 3 days, digested and collected. Annexin V-FITC/PI apoptosis detection Kit (KeyGEN, Nanjing, China) was used to detect the apoptosis of MC3T3-E1 cells. That is, after the cells are treated, the cells are digested and collected with 0.25% trypsin. The cells were then re-suspended with 100μl 1x binding buffer and incubated with 5μl Annexin V-FITC and 5μl propyl iodide (PI, Nuclear fluorescent dye) for 15 min. The cell suspension was added to 400μl 1x binding buffer and transferred to a flow cytometry tube for flow cytometry.

2.6.

## Reactive oxygen species detection
After treating MC3T3-E1 cells with CCCP and MS-275, intracellular ROS levels were detected with Reactive oxygen species detection kit (Beyotime, Shanghai, China) according to the instructions. Subsequently, 1 ml of DCFH-DA diluent was resuspended and incubated in a cell incubator at 37°C for 20 min. After thorough washing three times, the cells were transferred to a flow cytometry tube for detection.

2.7.

## Malondialdehyde (MDA) and superoxide dismutase (SOD)
MC3T3-E1 cells were seeded onto 6-well plates and subjected to CCCP and MS-275 treatment. Following cell lysis on ice for 30 min, the levels of MDA and SOD were quantified using a commercial assay kit from Nanjing, China, which employs ELISA technology for its high specificity and sensitivity.

2.8.

## Alkaline phosphatase (ALP) and Alizarin red S (ARS)
MC3T3-E1 cells were inoculated on 24-well plates. When the cells reached 60% confluent, they were treated with CCCP and MS-275. Osteogenic differentiation was detected with ALP staining kit (Beyotime, Shanghai, China) on day 7, and osteogenic differentiation was detected with ARS staining kit (Solarbio, China) on day 21, and then observed under microscope.

2.9.

## Western blot
After MC3T3-E1 cells were treated with CCCP and MS-275, RIPA cracking buffer (Beyotime, China) was added to each sample and cracked on ice. Or, according to the instructions, use the nuclear and cytoplasmic protein extraction kit (Beyotime, China) to separate the nuclear and cytoplasmic protein components. Centrifuge the lysate at 12,000rpm for 15 min to obtain the supernatant. Add the sample buffer to the water bath at 100°C for 10 min. Proteins of different molecular weights were separated using SDS-PAGE and transferred to PVDF membranes. The membrane was closed and incubated with primary antibody at 4°C overnight. Then the second antibody was incubated for 2 h, TBST washed 3 times for 10 min each time and developed.

2.10.

## COIP
After MC3T3-E1 cells were treated with CCCP and MS-275, the cells were lysed according to immunoprecipitation kit (Protein A+G magnetic bead method) (Beyotime, China), antibodies were bound to magnetic beads, immunoprecipitation, and elution. Then blot test was performed.

2.11.

## Luciferase assay
MC3T3-E1 cells were seeded in 12-well plates. Upon completion of transfection, the cells were thoroughly lysed, and subsequently, 20μl of the lysate was dispensed into a black microplate. Both firefly luciferase and renilla luciferase reaction solutions were then added for detection purposes.

2.12.

## RNA FISH assay
MC3T3-E1 cells were seeded on confocal small dishes and cultured overnight. On the second day, cells were loaded with probes according to the experimental instructions. After completion, the images were observed and photographed using a confocal microscope.

2.13

## In vivo analysis
Fifty SD (Sprague Dawley) rats (200±25 g) aged 8 weeks were collected from Qinglongshan Experimental Animal Center of Hangzhou and kept in the Animal Experimental Center of Wannan Medical College (temperature 21–24°C, light and dark cycle for 12 h). Each cage housing four rats was provided with sufficient food and water on a daily basis. This study was approved by the Laboratory Animal Committee of Wannan Medical College, and all experiments were conducted in accordance with the ‘Guidelines for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals’. Animals are anesthetized using isoflurane inhalation anesthesia. Following a one-week adaptation period, 35 rats underwent bilateral ovariectomy, while 15 rats received sham surgery. After 4 weeks, 5 rats from each group were taken for imaging and histological examination of femur. In 40 rats, titanium nails were inserted into the left femoral head, while a bone defect was identified in the contralateral right femoral head. They were divided into three groups, Sham group, osteoporosis group and MS-275 group. Sham group and the osteoporosis group received intraperitoneal injections of PBS, while the MS-275 group was administered MS-275 intraperitoneally (with the in vivo drug concentration converted to the in vitro concentration at a ratio of 3.76 mg/kg). After 5 weeks, the rats were killed and both femurs were taken.

2.14.

## Microstructure analysis of distal femur
In assessing the microstructural changes at the distal femur, micro-computed tomography (micro-CT) was employed to generate a 3D reconstruction image of the region of interest (VOI) located 2 mm proximal to the femoral epiphyseal line. This technique is pivotal in measuring critical bone morphometric parameters, including bone mineral density (BMD), trabecular separation (Tb.Sp), trabecular number (Tb.N), trabecular thickness (Tb.Th), and the ratio of bone volume to tissue volume (BV/TV), as detailed in recent studies.

2.15.

## Histological staining
Bone tissue was fixed in 10% paraformaldehyde for 48 h, immersed in 10%EDTA decalcification solution, cut lengthwise into 4μm thick slices, and stained with hematoxylin–eosin (HE) and Masson and Trap staining.

2.16.

## Statistical analysis
SPSS software was used for statistical analysis. After normality test, the measurement data conform to normal distribution, and the experimental data are represented by mean ± standard deviation. One-way ANOVA was used for comparison between experimental groups, and q test was used for pairwise comparison. Test level α = 0.05.

3.

## Result
3.1.

## MS-275 inhibited CCCP-induced apoptosis in MC3T3-E1 cells
The CCK8 assay results indicated that MC3T3-E1 cells exhibited peak activity when treated with MS-275 at a concentration of 100 nm for 48 h, as shown ( (c)). The following experiments were treated with this concentration and time. Flow cytometry results indicated that CCCP-induced apoptosis was enhanced, and pretreatment with MS-275 significantly attenuated this effect, as evidenced by a substantial decrease in the number of apoptotic cells ( (a and b)). Western blot analysis showed that CCCP treatment increased the protein levels of Bax and Cleaved caspase 3, and decreased the protein level of anti-apoptotic protein Bcl-2, the Bax/Bcl-2 ratio increased. Following pretreatment with MS-275, a notable decrease in the levels of the pro-apoptotic protein Bax and the activated form of caspase 3 was observed, accompanied by a significant elevation in the anti-apoptotic protein Bcl-2. Consequently, the Bax/Bcl-2 ratio, a critical determinant of apoptosis initiation, was reduced ( (d and e)).
 

3.2.

## MS-275 inhibited CCCP-induced oxidative stress in MC3T3-E1 cells
Oxidative stress is one of the main mechanisms of osteoporosis, which leads to the decline of osteoblast function. Therefore, we investigated the effect of MS-275 on the production of reactive oxygen species (ROS) in CCCP-exposed MC3T3-E1 cells. Flow cytometry analysis revealed that CCCP treatment markedly elevated intracellular ROS levels, while low molecular weight Tiron mitigated the ROS production induced by CCCP. Furthermore, MS-275 treatment reduced ROS generation from superoxide ( (b and c)). CCCP significantly increased malondialdehyde (MDA) and decreased superoxide dismutase (SOD) in the cells, consistent with findings that oxidative stress is exacerbated by conditions such as intermittent hypoxia and strong magnetic fields, as seen in studies with human vascular endothelial cells and rat islet cells, respectively. Conversely, MS-275 was observed to reverse these CCCP-induced changes in MDA and SOD levels, as also noted in the context of COPD patients where SOD levels are inversely related to the severity of hypoxia ( (d and e)). Western blot results showed that oxidative stress significantly reduced the intracellular antioxidant proteins HO-1 and SOD2, and MS-275 pretreatment restored the effect of CCCP on antioxidant proteins, and the expression levels of intracellular HO-1 and SOD2 were significantly increased ( (f and g)). Membrane potential levels were significantly reduced and green fluorescence was enhanced in the CCCP group, whereas mitochondrial membrane potential levels increased after treatment with MS-275 ( (a)).
 

3.3

## MS-275 restored the osteogenic function of MC3T3-E1 cells after oxidative stress
Oxidative stress can inhibit the osteogenic differentiation and mineralization of MC3T3-E1 cells. The results of ALP staining showed that compared with the NC group, the blue color of MC3T3-E1 cells was weakened after CCCP induction, while MS-275 deepened the blue color of cells ( (a and c)). The results of ARS staining showed that the red calcium nodules around the cells were significantly reduced after CCCP-induced oxidation compared with the NC group, while MS-275 restored the number of calcium nodules around the cells that were reduced due to oxidative stress ( (b and d)). Western blot analysis revealed a significant reduction in the levels of intracellular osteoblast-related proteins, including BMP2, Collagen 1, and OPN, following oxidative stress induced by CCCP, as compared to the control group. However, after MS-275 treatment, the inhibition of osteogenesis-related proteins by oxidative stress was weakened, and the protein expression of BMP2, Collagen 1 and OPN in the cells was significantly increased ( (e and f)).
 

3.4.

## MS-275 promoted miRNA-200a expression
In the analysis of databases miRDB.txt, miRTarBae.txt, and TargetScan.txt, miR-200a was identified as a MicroRNA closely related to keap1 mRNA, which is significant in the context of diabetic arterial endothelial damage as indicated by recent studies ( (a)). The presence of miRNA-200a in the cytoplasm was confirmed by fluorescence in situ hybridization ( (c)). MS-275 also promoted the expression of miRNA-200a in CCCP-induced oxidative cells ( (b)). We then predicted that miR-200a might target the 3′-UTR (positions 131–138) of Keap1 mRNA in MC3T3-E1 osteoblasts ( (d)). The expression vector of miR-200a was established and transfected into mouse osteoblasts. Wild-type 3′-UTR or mutant 3′-UTR target sequences were cloned into luciferase reporter vector. Transfection of vectors into mouse cells overexpressing miR-200a revealed that miR-200a significantly decreased the activity of wild-type Keap1 3′-UTR reporter cells, whereas the activity of mutant Keap1 3′-UTR reporter cells remained unaffected. This is consistent with findings from studies such as the one detailed in Reference, which suggest that miR-200a can modulate oxidative stress responses by interacting with the KEAP1/NRF2 pathway. The negative control did not affect the activity of either wild-type or mutant Keap1 3′-UTR reporter cells ( (e)). MS-275 significantly reduced the wild-type Keap1 3 ‘-UTR reporter activity, but not the mutant Keap1 3′-UTR reporter activity, in osteoblasts after oxidative induction with CCCP ( (f)).
 

3.5.

## MS-275 has been shown to activate the Nrf2 pathway, potentially through the upregulation of miRNA-200a expression
The results of luciferase assay showed that MS-275 significantly reduced the activity of wild-type Keap1 3, -UTR reporter after oxidative induction. Keap1 forms a complex with Nrf2 and remains quiescent and inactive in the cytoplasm, where Nrf2 is explained. The COIP results showed that the amount of Keap1-Nrf2 complex was reduced in osteoblasts induced by CCCP oxidation after MS-275 pretreatment ( (a)). Western blot results also showed that MS-275 reduced Keap1 protein in CCCP-induced osteoblasts, restored the reduction of Nrf2 caused by CCCP, and promoted Nrf2 nuclear translocation to play an antioxidant role, which was consistent with the results of COIP ( (b–d)). However, these effects were reversed by interfering with miRNA-200a ( (e–g)). These results indicated that MS-275 activated Nrf2 by promoting the expression of miRNA-200a.
 

3.6.

## MS-275 protects osteogenesis by promoting the expression of miRNA-200a
After inhibition of miRNA-200a, the promoting effect of MS-275 on CCCP-induced intracellular osteogenesis-related proteins (BMP2, OPN, Collagen1) was reversed ( (a, c)). At the same time, the trend of anti-apoptotic proteins (Bcl2) and pro-apoptotic proteins (Bax, cleaved-caspase 3) was also reversed ( (b, d)).
 

3.7.

## Therapeutic effects of MS-275 in OVX rat models
Micro-CT analysis revealed a diminished trabecular bone structure in the ovariectomized (OVX) group relative to the sham-operated controls, confirming the successful establishment of an osteoporosis model. Following the intraperitoneal administration of MS-275, there was a significant recovery of trabecular bone in the treated grou ( (a)). These changes were quantified, trabecular bone microstructure was analyzed and expressed as BMD, BV/TV, and Tb.Sp ( (b–d)), and MS-275 restored the effects of bilateral ovariectomy on bone mineral density, bone volume fraction, and trabecular separation compared with the sham-operation group. The analysis of the femoral epiphysis revealed that H&E staining outcomes were consistent with those obtained from micro-CT, indicating that MS-275 treatment effectively mitigated the damage to the femoral trabeculae caused by ovariectomy ( (e)). The results of Masson staining showed that the rate of new bone formation was significantly attenuated in the OVX group compared with the Sham group, whereas intraperitoneal injection of MS-275 restored new bone formation in the distal femur of rats ( (e)). Concurrent immunohistochemical analysis revealed significantly reduced protein expression of Bmp2, Collagen1, and OPN in the bone tissue of OVX rats compared to the sham-operated group, a finding that was mitigated by treatment with MS-275 ( (f)). Trap assay showed that the bone oxidative damage was obvious and the number of osteoclasts increased in OVX group, and the number of osteoclasts decreased after MS-275 administration to rats ( (g)).

## Discussion
The structure and function of bone depend on the growth and development of osteoblasts, and when they are compromised, they lead to bone loss, which causes osteoporosis [ ]. Oxidative stress is closely related to the occurrence of osteoporosis, leading to significant cellular damage, such as cell necrosis, autophagy, and apoptosis [ ]. MS-275 plays a role in clinical practice by inhibiting oxidative stress in tumor and drug resistance diseases [ ], but the mechanism of MS-275 on oxidative stress in osteoblasts is still unclear. Tiron is an orally active antioxidant, a cell-permeable analogue of vitamin E, and acts as a hydroxyl radical and superoxide scavenger [ ]. In vitro experiments, the oxidative stress model of osteoblasts was induced by CCCP, and the results showed that mitochondrial membrane potential was damaged, its potential was at a low level, oxidation indexes such as ROS and MDA increased significantly, and SOD decreased significantly, indicating that the cellular oxidative stress model was successfully established. Cell viability assay showed that the cells treated with 100 nm MS-275 for 2 d had the strongest cell viability. ROS assays showed that low molecular weight Tiron reduced CCCP-induced ROS production, and MS-275 treatment reduced ROS produced by hydroxyl radicals and superoxide. Moreover, MS-275 treatment also reduced CCCP-induced oxidative damage, as shown by increased mitochondrial membrane potential, decreased MDA content and increased SOD content. This is consistent with MS-275 inhibiting oxidative stress in tumors, suggesting that MS-275 can ameliorate oxidative damage in osteoblasts.
HO-1, an essential antioxidant enzyme, primarily facilitates the breakdown of heme into ferrous iron, carbon monoxide (CO), and biliverdin. This enzyme plays a crucial role in cellular stress response and has been implicated in various biological processes, including the regulation of cell growth, apoptosis, and iron metabolism, as evidenced by studies on breast cancer and liver disorders [ ]. The degradation of heme groups helps to prevent oxidation, and biliverdin and its reduced bilirubin have effective ROS scavenging activity to resist peroxide, hydroxyl and superoxide free radicals [ ]. Western blot results showed that CCCP induction reduced the expression of HO-1 and SOD2 in osteoblasts, while MS-275 improved these changes. These results indicated that MS-275 improved the oxidative damage of osteoblasts by promoting the expression of HO-1.
Excessive accumulation of reactive oxygen species may trigger oxidative injury, which in turn causes destruction of important biopolymers such as proteins, leading to lipid peroxidation [ ]. At the same time, oxidative damage reduces the antioxidant substances, which weakens the activity of antioxidant enzymes and promotes the apoptosis of osteoblasts [ ]. At the same time, the ratio of Bax/Bcl-2 determined the intensity of apoptosis inhibition. In this study, flow cytometry results showed that the apoptosis rate of osteoblasts was significantly increased after CCCP treatment. Western blot results also showed that after CCCP treatment, the levels of anti-apoptotic factor Bcl-2 were decreased, the levels of pro-apoptotic molecules Bax and Cleaved caspase 3 were significantly increased, the Bax/Bcl-2 ratio was also increased, and the apoptosis rate was also increased. However, when MS-275 was co-cultured with oxidized osteoblasts, the cell apoptosis rate was significantly decreased, which is consistent with its known effect on inducing apoptosis in various cancer cell lines, the expression of Bcl2 was enhanced, and the expression of Bax and Cleaved caspase 3 was decreased, the Bax/Bcl2 ratio also decreased, indicating that MS-275 could reverse the CCCP-induced apoptosis phenomenon. Therefore, MS-275 can inhibit osteoblast apoptosis caused by oxidative stress.
The strength of bone structure is determined by the balance between osteoblasts, which support bone construction, and osteoclasts, which participate in bone destruction [ ]. However, the accumulation of reactive oxygen species (ROS) during oxidative damage will over-activate osteoclasts, but promote osteoblast apoptosis [ ]. Oxidative damage is a major factor in the pathogenesis of osteoporosis [ ]. In this study, ALP staining and ARS staining results showed that the osteogenic and mineralization ability of osteoblasts was markedly diminished following oxidative damage caused by CCCP, with western blot results further indicating a significant decrease in osteogenesis-related proteins, including BMP2, OPN, and Collagen 1, in the damaged cells. When co-cultured with oxidative damage cells, MS-275 significantly enhanced the osteogenic and mineralization capabilities of the oxidized osteoblasts, along with a notable increase in osteogenic-related proteins. This suggests that MS-275 improves the osteogenic capacity of osteoblasts with oxidative damage.
Keap1/Nrf2 is one of the major intracellular antioxidant mechanisms [ ]. Keap1 and Nrf2 are a pair of complementary proteins that together form a whole and are present in the cytoplasm in an inactive state at this time, and Nrf2 is degraded at this time [ ]. Keap1 and Nrf2 can be separated by certain stimuli, Nrf2 free entry into the nucleus is significantly increased, and the expression of antioxidant elements related genes is increased, so the antioxidant damage is enhanced [ ]. Studies have shown that the expression of Keap1 mRNA is closely related to miRNA [ , ]. The miRNA, as small non-coding RNA fragments, plays a crucial role in the late regulation of gene expression [ ]. These small RNA molecules finely regulate gene expression by reducing the structural stability of their target mRNA and interfering with its translation process [ ]. In this study, we found that miRNA-200a was closely related to Keap1 mRNA by bioinformatics analysis, and FISH experiment showed that miRNA-200a was involved in the regulation of antioxidant pathways through its interaction with Keap1 mRNA. Then we predicted the binding sites of miRNA-200a and Keap1 mRNA. Dual luciferase assay showed that miRNA-200a could bind to the 3′-UTR(131-138) of Keap1 mRNA and inhibit the activity of Keap1 mRNA.
Histone deacetylation promotion is catalyzed by HDAC and results in repression of gene expression [ ]. In turn, the elevation of histone acetylation is attributed to the function of HDAC inhibitors, which promotes the opening of chromatin conformation and thus gene expression [ , ]. MS-275 is a class I HDAC inhibitor, which mainly inhibits HDAC1 and HDAC3, and regulates histone acetylation to change gene expression [ ]. In this experiment, MS-275 did not change the expression of miRNA-200a in normal cells, but when cells were subjected to oxidative damage, MS-275 promoted the massive expression of miRNA-200a. The results of dual luciferase assay showed that MS-275 promoted the expression of miRNA-200a and inhibited the activity of Keap1 mRNA by binding to the mRNA site of Keap1, and the expression of Keap1 protein was also reduced. Keap1 and Nrf2 exist in the cytoplasm in a bound form and are one of the main pathways of oxidative stress. After CCCP-induced oxidative damage in osteoblasts, intracellular Nrf2 was significantly reduced. After being cultured with MS-275, the level of intracellular Keap1 protein decreased significantly, whereas Nrf2 increased markedly, both within the cell and in its translocation to the nucleus. The results of immunoprecipitation also showed that the complex of Keap1 and Nrf2 existed in the cytoplasm, and the dissociation of Keap1 and Nrf2 was increased in the cells after MS-275 co-culture with oxidative damage, which was consistent with the results of Western blot. Furthermore, the effects of MS-275 were found to be inhibited when the expression of miRNA was interfered with. These results suggest that MS-275 exerts its anti-oxidative effect by activating the Keap1/Nrf2 pathway through promoting the expression of miRNA-200a. The stability of bone structure depends on the balance of bone formation and resorption, and when this balance is disrupted, the pathological state of osteoporosis may occur [ ]. Estrogen plays a key role in maintaining bone health by regulating a variety of cytokines and growth factors produced in bone marrow and osteocytes, thereby affecting the process of bone remodeling [ ]. As women enter menopause, the significant decline in estrogen levels is a primary factor contributing to postmenopausal osteoporosis, a condition that is not only accompanied by the loss of bone mass, but also increases the risk of fracture [ ]. In vivo studies frequently employ the ovariectomized (OVX) rat model to simulate and investigate the progression of human osteoporosis. In order for the bone volume to be fully evaluated and analyzed, the distal femur was used in our experimental procedure. Bone mineral density (BMD) is commonly used in the clinical diagnosis of osteoporosis, because BMD is the gold standard for bone volume [ ]. In order to better simulate the osteoporotic fracture and post-fracture repair, the corresponding femoral defects of the rat were implanted with titanium nails. Micro-CT results indicated that the femur bone mineral density in the ovariectomized (OVX) group was significantly reduced compared to the sham-operated group, and H&E staining results also showed that the number of bone trabeculae was significantly reduced, and the trabecular bone results were disordered and absorbed, which suggested that we had successfully established the rat osteoporosis model. Following intraperitoneal injection of MS-275 over a specific duration, the femoral BMD of the rats exhibited recovery, albeit remaining below that of the Sham group; concurrently, there was an augmentation in the number of trabecular bone structures. At the same time, Masson staining revealed a notable decrease in both new and mature bone tissue surrounding the femoral defect in the OVX group, whereas MS-275 administration reversed these adverse effects. Immunohistochemical analysis demonstrated a marked reduction in bone-related protein levels within the OVX group, while the expression of success protein was enhanced in the MS-275 group, which was also consistent with the in vitro analysis. The Trap experiment showed that after the removal of both ovaries in OVX group, the bone oxidative damage was obvious and the number of osteoclasts increased, while the number of osteoclasts decreased in MS-275 group. The results of in vivo experiments show that MS-275 can promote bone formation of osteoblasts, inhibit bone resorption of osteoclasts, and improve osteoporosis. Furthermore, the results indicate that MS-275 has the potential to enhance bone trabeculation and alleviate osteoporosis in ovariectomized rats. In conclusion, MS-275 inhibited CCCP-induced oxidative stress injury in osteoblasts in vitro. MS-275 promotes trabecular bone formation and osseointegration in osteoporotic rats in vivo, which opens up a new idea for oxidative stress-induced osteoporosis and provides a new method for clinical treatment of osteoporosis. However, we haven't done genetic testing in animals, and that will be our next goal.

5.

## Conclusion
MS-275 could protect osteoblasts against oxidative stress by activating Keap1/Nrf2 axis through enhancing the activity of miRNA-200a.
