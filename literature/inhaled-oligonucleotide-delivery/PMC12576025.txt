## ABSTRACT
Idiopathic Pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a debilitating lung condition marked by chronic progression and reduced lung function. This study aimed to explore the functional role and regulatory mechanism of circular RNA circ_0044226 in the pathogenesis of pulmonary fibrosis. The expression of circ_0044226 was analyzed in peripheral blood and TGF‐β1‐stimulated RLE‐6TN cells using qRT‐PCR. The cellular location of circ_0044226 was analyzed using FISH. The expression levels of circ_0044226 and METTL3 were evaluated in cells and clinical samples. Protein levels of GPX4 and FTH1 were examined using Western blot, while the levels of MDA, Fe2+ and ROS were determined using commercial kits. The association of circ_0044226 with m6A modifications was validated by Methylated‐RNA immunoprecipitation assay (MeRIP), RNA pulldown, RIP and dot blot assay. Finally, a Bleomycin (BLM) treated rat model was also utilized to verify the function of circ_0044226 in pulmonary fibrosis. The primary localization of circ_0044226 was within the cytoplasm and was found to be upregulated in blood samples from IPF patients and TGF‐β1‐treated RLE‐6TN cells. Knockdown of circ_0044226 reduced the accumulation of free iron and MDA, and suppressed α‐SMA and FN1 in TGF‐β1‐induced cells. Furthermore, METTL3 mediated the m6A modification of circ_0044226. Downregulation of METTL3 mitigated ferroptosis by inhibiting hsa_circ_0044226. The rat pulmonary fibrosis model further demonstrated that METTL3 knockdown alleviated pulmonary fibrosis by blocking ferroptosis through circ_0044226. Our findings demonstrated that targeting the METTL3/circ_0044226 axis attenuated pulmonary fibrosis by inhibiting ferroptosis in the studied models.
Keywords: circ_0044226, ferroptosis, m6A modification, METTL3, pulmonary fibrosis

## Introduction
Pulmonary fibrosis is a kind of lung disease marked by the gradual and continuous development of fibrous scar tissue in the lungs [ ]. Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) has been reported to have a prevalence of 14–63 cases per 100,000 individuals, with a 5‐year survival rate ranged from 20% to 40% [ ]. While medication, pulmonary rehabilitation, supplemental oxygen therapy, and lung transplantation are potential treating approaches for patients with pulmonary fibrosis [ ], the available therapeutic options are restricted, and the prognosis for many patients remains poor. Therefore, it's of great significance to investigate the molecular mechanism underlying pulmonary fibrosis and identify new potential targets for its treatment.
Iron deposition is involved in the pathogenesis of lung fibrosis. Excessive iron accumulation in the lung tissue can cause cellular damage and oxidative stress, creating a pro‐fibrotic environment [ ]. Studies have reported that iron‐mediated cellular injury and the generation of ROS contribute to the activation of fibroblasts and the deposition of extracellular matrix, ultimately leading to fibrotic changes in the lung tissue [ ]. Peight et al. discovered that increased intracellular Fe2+ accelerated the transformation of fibroblasts into myofibroblasts and suppression of ferroptosis and iron accumulation attenuated pulmonary fibrosis [ ]. Elevated iron accumulation aggravated iron‐driven oxidant injury in lung epithelial cells, triggering further cell autophagy during epithelial‐mesenchymal transition [ ]. Previous research has also shown that iron stimulates the release of profibrotic cytokines and growth factors, exacerbating the fibrotic process.
CircRNAs constitute a category of noncoding RNAs that have been implicated in various biological processes and diseases [ ]. Recent study illustrates that circRNAs serve as key regulators in the pathogenesis of fibrosis. For instance, silencing circ_0047339 inhibited the proliferation and fibrosis of urethral scar fibroblasts, thus affecting the progression of urethral stricture [ ]. Another study demonstrated that upregulation of circ_0001861 suppressed α‐SMA, E‐cadherin, collagen III and fibronectin 1, indicating an inhibitory effect of increased circ_0001861 on TGF‐β1‐induced fibrosis in pulmonary fibroblasts [ ]. Our previous study revealed that depletion of circ_0044226 alleviated pulmonary fibrosis by suppressing CDC27 [ ]. Nevertheless, the functional role of circ_0044226 and its molecular mechanism underlying pulmonary fibrosis still remains uncertain.
Recent evidence suggests the critical role of N6‐methyladenosine (m6A) modification in regulating the biogenesis, stability, and cellular functions of circRNAs. For example, Xia et al. reported that N6‐methyladenosine‐modified circSAV1 stimulated ferroptosis of lung epithelial cells, triggering airway remodeling and emphysema in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease [ ]. Another study demonstrated that m6A‐mediated elevation of circMDK accelerated the advancement of hepatocellular carcinoma through miR‐346/874‐3p‐ATG16L1 axis [ ]. Moreover, m6A modification has been identified to regulate the activation and differentiation of fibroblasts, thereby influencing the development and progression of pulmonary fibrosis [ ]. In fact, m6A‐related genes have shown potential as biomarkers for IPF [ ]. Additionally, elevated levels of m6A methylation were found in crystalline silica‐induced silicosis [ ]. Furthermore, METTL3 mediated m6A modification of CDH1 enhanced PM2.5‐stimulated pulmonary fibrosis via epithelial‐mesenchymal transition (EMT) progression [ ]. Based on these findings, we speculated that m6A modification of circ_0044226 may alleviate pulmonary fibrosis by blocking ferroptosis.
This study intended to investigate the function of circ_0044226 and its regulating mechanism in pulmonary fibrosis. Our findings revealed that silencing METTL3 alleviated pulmonary fibrosis by blocking ferroptosis through circ_0044226 m6A modification in pulmonary fibrosis. These findings indicate that targeting the METTL3/m6A/circ_0044226 axis may be a potential treating option for pulmonary fibrosis.

## Methods
2.1.

## Patients and Blood Samples
Peripheral blood samples were obtained from individuals diagnosed with IPF (n = 10) and healthy controls (n = 10), and the nuclear cells were isolated using Red Blood Cell Lysis (TIANGEN, China) according to manufacturer's instructions. All participants provided written informed consent. Furthermore, the research protocol was reviewed and approved by the Medical Ethics Committee of Anhui Institute of Medicine.

2.2.

## Clinical Sample Information
This study evaluated 10 patients with IPF and 10 healthy controls (Supplementary Table  ). The IPF group consisted of 7 males (70%) with a mean age of 64.83 ± 7.54 years. There were no significant differences between the IPF and control groups for gender and age; however, the proportion of smokers was significantly higher in the IPF group compared to the control group (80% vs. 30%, p = 0.025). In the IPF group, 80% of patients experienced varying degrees of dyspnea, the mean FVC was 72.44 ± 8.83%, and the mean DLCO was 62.35 ± 6.74%. The median GAP score was 3 (range 0‐8), and 70% of IPF patients were receiving drug therapy. Additionally, 70% of the IPF patients exhibited UIP pattern on high‐resolution computed tomography (HRCT).

2.3.

## Cell Culture and Treatment
RLE‐6TN cells were bought from ATCC (USA). RLE‐6TN cells were cultured in DMEM containing 10% FBS and 100 μg/mL penicillin‐streptomycin (Thermo Fisher Scientific, USA) at 37°C with 5% CO2. Subsequently, the cells were cultured in a medium containing 10 ng/mL TGF‐β1 (Sigma Aldrich, USA) for 48 h before conducting further experiments.

2.4.

## Cell Transfection
The shRNAs targeting circ_0044226, METTL3 and NC, as well as oe‐circ_0044226 and the vector were purchased from GeneCopoeia (Guangzhou, China). Once the RLE‐6TN cells reached a confluency of 30‐50%, they were transfected with either sh‐circ_0044226, sh‐METTL3, oe‐circ_0044226 or their corresponding negative controls by lipo6000 reagent.

2.5.

## Isolation of Cytoplasmic and Nuclear RNA
In brief, the extraction and purification of cytoplasmic and nuclear RNAs were conducted by a cytoplasmic and nuclear RNA purification kit (Sigma‐Aldrich). U6 and GAPDH were utilized as internal references genes.

2.6.

## Immunofluorescence Staining
α‐SMA and FN1 were detected by immunofluorescence staining. After fixing with 4% paraformaldehyde and blocking with 10% goat serum (Abcam, USA) for 2 h. Subsequently, the samples were subjected to the incubation with primary antibodies against α‐SMA (ab7817, 1/500, Abcam) or FN1 (ab45688, 1/250, Abcam) overnight at 4°C. The cells were then exposed to a secondary antibody (ab150077 or ab150116, Abcam) at room temperature for 1 h. After labeling the nuclei with DAPI, the slices were visualized using a fluorescence microscope (Olympus CX23 Tokyo, Japan).

2.7.

## Fluorescence In Situ Hybridization (FISH)
The location of circ_0044226 in RLE‐6TN cells was detected using a FISH assay. A biotin‐labelled antisense probe targeting the junction sequence of circ_0044226 was designed and synthesized (GenePharma; China). To perform the FISH assay, RLE‐6TN cells were treated with 4% paraformaldehyde for fixation and permeabilize using 0.5% Triton X‐100. The labeled circ_0044226 probe was then incubated with fixed cells at 37°C in the dark overnight for hybridization. Subsequently, the samples were then washed by PBS. DAPI staining was used to label the nuclei, and the images were examined using confocal microscopy.

2.8.

## Methylated‐RNA Immunoprecipitation Assay (MeRIP)
In brief, 10 μg of mRNA was fragmented into oligonucleotides using RNA Fragmentation Reagents (Invitrogen) in a Thermomixer. The Magna ChIP Protein A/G Magnetic Beads were incubated with 5 μg of m6A‐specifc antibody at 4°C for 2 h using immunoprecipitation bufer. Subsequently, the mixture was incubated with MeRIP reaction solutions at 4°C for 1 h. The RNA was then eluted and purified for qRT‐PCR analysis.

2.9.

## RNA m6A Quantification
The relative m6A content was measured using the EpiQuik m6A RNA Methylation Quantification Kit (EpiGentek, USA) according to the manufacturer′s instructions, and the absorbance was read at 450 nm with a microplate reader.

2.10.

## RNA Pulldown
A biotin‐labeled circ_0044226 RNA probe was prepared and mixed with cell lysate to form RNA‐protein complexes overnight at 4°C. The cell lysate was then added with magnetic beads coated with streptavidin to capture the RNA complex labeled with biotin at 25°C for 2 h. After washing with wash buffer, the RNA‐protein complexes were released from the magnetic beads and the complex was purified with Trizol (Takara, China). The enrichment level of circ_0044226 was analyzed.

2.11.

## Dot Blot
The extracted RNA was denatured by heating at 72°C for 5 min and then spotted onto a solid Amersham Hybond™‐N + membrane (GE Healthcare, #RPN303B). The RNA was crosslinked to the membrane twice by UV light using UVP Crosslinker Analytik‐jena. After blocking with nonfat dry milk, the samples were underwent incubation with an m6A‐specific antibody (ab314476, 1/5000, Abcam) and a subsequent secondary antibody (ab7090, Abcam). Methylene blue stained membrane was used as a control reference. The signal on the membrane was analyzed using autoradiography film.

2.12.

## Measurement of Fe2+

The content of free iron in RLE‐6TN cells was determined using an iron assay kit (Abcam, USA). Briefly, RLE‐6TN cells in the assay buffer were sonicated, and the supernatant was collected after centrifugation. The sample was then incubated with an iron probe provided in the kit. After washing with PBS, iron content was detected using a microplate reader.

2.13.

## Measurement of MDA and Lipid ROS
MDA content was detected by a MDA assay kit (Colorimetric/Fluorometric) from Abcam (USA). For the detection of lipid ROS, RLE‐6TN cells were washed with PBS and incubated in working solution containing 5 μM BODIPY581/591 C11 (Sigma‐Aldrich, USA) at 37°C for 0.5 h. After removing the dye solution and washing with PBS, the fluorescence signals of BODIPY581/591 C11 within the cells were observed under a fluorescence microscope and analyzed by Image J software (NIH, USA). Quantitative data are presented as the ratio of intracellular green/(red+green) fluorescence intensity.

2.14.

## Real‐Time Quantitative PCR
Total RNA was isolated utilizing Trizol reagent (Invitrogen, USA), followed by reverse transcription of the collected RNA into cDNA using a Prime‐ScriptTM one step qRT‐PCR kit (Takara Bio, China). RT‐PCR was conducted on an ABI7900 Real‐Time PCR System (Applied Biosystems, USA). The gene expressions were normalized relative to GAPDH, and the relative quantitative analysis was conducted using method. Primers sequences used were listed:
circ_0044226 F, 5′‐ATGCATGTACAACACCTCAGGTAT‐3′
R, 5′‐CTTCTGAAATGATGGAAGAGTCC‐3′;
METTL3 F, 5′‐CTATCTCCTGGCACTCGCAAGA‐3′
R, 5′‐GCTTGAACCGTGCAACCACATC‐3′;
GAPDH F, 5′‐ATGGGCAGCCGTTAGGAAAG‐3′
R, 5′‐GACGGTGCCATGGAATTTGC‐3′;

2.15.

## Western Blot
The extraction of proteins was performed using RIPA buffer and quantified by a BCA Protein Assay Kit (Beyotime, China). The specimens were loaded onto SDS‐PAGE gel electrophoresis and then transferred to PVDF membranes. After blocking with BSA, the samples underwent the incubation with primary antibodies against GPX4 and FTH1 at 4°C overnight and secondary antibodies (ab7090, Abcam) at 37°C for 45 min. GPX4 antibody (ab125066, 1/1000, Abcam) and FTH1 antibody (ab65080, 1/1000, Abcam). Protein band images on the membrane were taken and quantified by Image‐Pro Plus software (Media Cybernetics Inc., USA).

2.16.

## Animal Studies
A total of 25 Wistar rats (male, 6 w, weight 180–200 g) were bought from Nanjing Biomedical Research Institute of Nanjing University (China) and kept in standard cages with a humidity range of 50%–70%. The rats were allocated into six groups (n = 5/group): Control, BLM, BLM+sh‐NC+oe‐NC, BLM+sh‐METTL3 +oe‐NC, BLM+sh‐NC+oe‐circ_0044226 and BLM+sh‐METTL3+oe‐circ_0044226. To induce pulmonary fibrosis, the experimental groups were subjected to a sole intratracheal administration of BLM (50 μL, 1 U/kg) dissolved in sterile saline, while the control group was administered an equivalent volume of normal saline solution. After BLM treatment, the rats were administered with lenti‐METTL3‐shRNA, lenti‐oe‐circ_0044226 or control vectors (0.2 mL/rat) via tracheal injection. After 4 weeks, the rats were euthanized, and lung tissue samples were harvested and fixed in 4% formaldehyde for subsequent experiments. This study received approval from the Institutional Animal Care Committee of Anhui Institute of Medicine.

2.17.

## Hematoxylin‐Eosin (H&E) and Masson Staining
Paraffin embedding was performed on the BLM‐induced fibrotic lung tissue samples obtained from the rats. The tissue sections were cut into 3 μm thick slices and stained with H&E staining kit (Beyotime, China) and Masson staining kit (Beyotime, China).

2.18.

## Statistical Analysis
All results are expressed as mean ± SD and analyzed and drew with SPSS20.0 (IBM, USA). Statistical comparisons were analyzed using one‐way analysis of variance (ANOVA) followed by Tukey's post hoc test. A p‐value less than 0.05 was deemed statistically significant. All experiments were conducted at least three times.

## Results
3.1.

## Hsa_circ_0044226 Is Upregulated in Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis
The structural characteristics of the circRNA are illustrated in Figure  , indicating that circ_0044226 originates from exon 9 to exon 13 of the CDC27 gene. PCR detection revealed amplified bands for the divergent primers amplified bands in both cDNA and gDNA, while the convergent primers only produced a band in cDNA (Figure  ), suggesting the presence of circ_0044226 in RLE‐6TN cells. Furthermore, circ_0044226 transcripts were more stable compared to CDC27 mRNA (Figure  ). Consistently, FISH detection indicated that circ_0044226 was primarily localized in the cytoplasm (Figure  ). Additionally, the level of circ_0044226 was evaluated in both IPF patients and cells. It was observed that circ_0044226 levels were elevated in nuclear cells of blood samples from IPF patients and also elevated in TGF‐β1‐induced RLE‐6TN cells (Figure  ). These results indicated that circ_0044226 was upregulated in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.
 
3.2.

## Knockdown of hsa_circ_0044226 Eliminates Accumulation of Fe2+ and Inhibits Ferroptosis in TGF‐β1 Induced RLE‐6TN Cells
Since ferroptosis exhibit regulatory impact on the progression of fibrosis, this study investigated the functional role of circ_0044226 in ferroptosis. sh‐circ_0044226 was transfected into TGF‐β1‐induced RLE‐6TN cells to knockdown circ_0044226 expression. The results showed that the silence of circ_0044226 suppressed TGF‐β1‐induced increase of circ_0044226 expression (Figure  ). Further evaluation revealed that MDA content was significantly increased in TGF‐β1‐stimulated RLE‐6TN cells, which was reduced by circ_0044226 knockdown (Figure  ). Moreover, downregulated circ_0044226 could eliminate the accumulation of Fe2+ in TGF‐β1‐induced cells (Figure  ). Notably, TGF‐β1 treatment enhanced the fluorescence intensity of lipid ROS, while suppressed circ_0044226 reduced the fluorescence activity of lipid ROS in cells (Figure  ). Immunofluorescence staining exhibited that downregulation of circ_0044226 abolished the elevation of fibrosis activation markers α‐SMA and FN1 in TGF‐β1‐treated cells (Figure  ). Additionally, TGF‐β1 reduced the expression of GPX4 and FTH1 proteins, which were reversed by silencing circ_0044226 (Figure  ). In conclusion, these findings demonstrated that knockdown of circ_0044226 reversed TGF‐β1‐induced accumulation of intracellular Fe2+ and inhibited ferroptosis in RLE‐6TN cells.
 
3.3.

## METTL3 Mediates the m6A Modification of hsa_circ_0044226
Circulating evidence reveals that dysregulated m6A modification exerts multifaceted effects on the pathogenesis of pulmonary fibrosis [ ]. As a type of RNA methyltransferase, METTL3 also contributes to the progression of fibrotic diseases [ ]. In this study, we analyzed m6A methylation level and METTL3 in IPF, and found that m6A content and METTL3 level were increased in IPF patients (Figure  ), indicating that METTL3‐mediated m6A modification was correlated with pulmonary fibrosis. The presence of m6A methylation sites in circ_0044226 was predicted by SRAMP. RNA pull‐down assay and m6A RIP assay proved that circ_0044226 contained m6A methylation modified sites (Figure  ). After the methylation site mutation, the the enrichment level of circ_0044226 by m6A antibody was significantly reduced (Figure  ). Based on these data, it was concluded that METTL3 might mediate the m6A methylation modification of circ_0044226. To investigate the regulatory effect of METTL3 on circ_0044226, METTL3 was knocked down in RLE‐6TN cells. The suppression of METTL3 significantly reduced circ_0044226 expression (Figure  ). Additionally, METTL3 inhibition provoked a reduction in the enrichment level of circ_0044226 (Figure  ). Overall, these results demonstrated that METTL3 mediated the m6A modification in circ_0044226.
 
3.4.

## METTL3 Depletion Attenuates Ferroptosis in TGF‐β1‐treated RLE‐6TN Cells by Inhibiting hsa_circ_0044226
The molecular mechanism of METTL3 for regulating ferroptosis and fibrosis was further elucidated. Compared with the control group, circ_0044226 expression was increased in TGF‐β1 stimulated RLE‐6TN cells, which was further upregulated by circ_0044226 overexpressing vector but reduced by METTL3 inhibition. The inhibitory effect of silencing METTL3 on circ_0044226 was partly abolished by circ_0044226 overexpression (Figure  ). Overexpressed circ_0044226 accelerated the upregulation of MDA production, Fe2+ accumulation and lipid ROS generation in TGF‐β1‐treated cells. METTL3 depletion exerted contrary regulation for these factors, which was reversed by the elevation of circ_0044226 (Figure  ). Immunofluorescence assays identified that TGF‐β1 induced enhancement of fluorescence activity of α‐SMA and FN1, and western blot detection proved that TGF‐β1 exhibited inhibition on the proteins of GPX4 and FTH1. However, the regulating effects of TGF‐β1 treatment on fibrosis activation markers and ferroptosis‐resistant genes were counteracted by of METTL3 downregulation and enhanced by circ_0044226 upregulation. Furthermore, overexpression of circ_0044226 could reverse the functional role of silencing METTL3 on fibrosis and ferroptosis (Figure  ). Therefore, it was concluded that downregulation of METTL3 mitigated TGF‐β1‐induced fibrotic responses and ferroptosis in RLE‐6TN cells by inhibiting hsa_circ_0044226.
 
3.5.

## METTL3 Knockdown Ameliorates Pulmonary Fibrosis by Blocking Ferroptosis Through hsa_circ_0044226 in a Rat Pulmonary Fibrosis Model
Finally, the function of METTL3 was investigated in IPF rat. circ_0044226 upregulation exacerbated alveolar septal thickening and inflammatory cell infiltration in BLM‐induced lung fibrosis. However, the deletion of METTL3 attenuated inflammation in BLM‐induced lung fibrosis, while oe‐circ_0044226 rescued the function of METTL3 in tissue samples (Figure  ). Masson's trichrome staining showed that the collagen deposition and fibrosis in BLM mouse was aggravated by circ_0044226 overexpression but mitigated by the silence of METTL3, which was counteracted by overexpressed circ_0044226 (Figure  ). Furthermore, BLM treatment triggered a significant increase in α‐SMA and FN1 and an obvious decrease in GPX4 and FTH1, which was enhanced by oe‐circ_0044226 and alleviated by METTL3 downregulation. Moreover, the cotransfection of oe‐circ_0044226 could further reverse the silencing METTL3‐induced regulation on ferroptosis and fibrosis (Figure  ). Hence, we concluded that downregulation of METTL3 attenuated pulmonary fibrosis by blocking ferroptosis through hsa_circ_0044226 in a rat pulmonary fibrosis model.
 

4.

## Discussion
Inflammatory responses, dysregulated repair processes, and genetic and epigenetic factors drive pulmonary fibrosis progression [ ]. Despite considerable research, the molecular mechanisms underlying pulmonary fibrosis remain unclear, and the prognosis for patients is still unsatisfactory. Our study hypothesized that m6A modification of circ_0044226 is involved in pulmonary fibrosis. Our findings indicated that circ_0044226 knockdown reduced TGF‐β1‐induced accumulation of Fe2+ and inhibited ferroptosis. Mechanistically, we observed that METTL3 knockdown attenuated key features of pulmonary fibrosis in experimental models by suppressing hsa_circ_0044226‐mediated ferroptosis.
Previous studies have extensively investigated the alterations and functional roles of circRNAs in the pathophysiology of pulmonary fibrosis. As reported, an increase in circ_0035796 was observed in TGF‐β1‐induced HFL1 cells, and its downregulation suppressed the generation of IL‐6 and TNF‐α, as well as oxidative stress, indicating a potential inhibitory effect of reduced circ_0035796 on pulmonary fibrosis [ ]. Another study demonstrated that the overexpressed circ_0008898 aggravated TGF‐β1‐indueced fibrogenesis in HFL1 cells [ ]. Additionally, circ_0044226 knockdown could alleviate fibroblast‐to‐myofibroblast transition and fibroblast proliferation in an IPF mice model [ ]. These studies indicate that the abnormal upregulation of circRNAs might exacerbate pulmonary fibrosis. Consistently, our study found circ_0044226 was significantly increased in both IPF patients and TGF‐β1‐induced RLE‐6TN cells. Moreover, the depletion of circ_0044226 abolished TGF‐β1‐induced ferroptosis in RLE‐6TN cells.
Ferroptosis is closely associated with pulmonary fibrosis. Dysregulated circRNA exhibited positive regulation for endoplasmic reticulum stress, ferroptosis and cellular senescence in cystic fibrosis [ ]. Studies have also reported that urotensin II‐induced upregulation of circ_0004372 could enhance the production of ROS, MDA and 4‐HNE, reduced the generation of SOD, GST and GR, and promoted the accumulation of Fe2+ in vascular remodeling diseases [ ]. Overexpressed circ_0001944 intensified ferroptosis features in LX‐2 cells in liver fibrosis [ ]. Nevertheless, up to now, no research has demonstrated the association between circ_0044226 and ferroptosis in pulmonary fibrosis. Our findings for the first time revealed that knockdown of circ_0044226 eliminated TGF‐β‐induced Fe2+ accumulation and suppressed ferroptosis in RLE‐6TN cells.
Methyltransferase‐like protein 3 (METTL3), a crucial role in m6A RNA modification [ ], modulates pulmonary fibrosis progression through multiple mechanisms. Overexpression of METTL3 was found to enhance hsa_circ_0131922 expression through m6A modification in papillary thyroid carcinoma [ ] and stabilize circ_00006.2 expression via m6A modification in lung cancer [ ], aligning with our observation that METTL3 drove hsa_circ_0044226 m6A modification in pulmonary fibrosis. Notably, METTL3‐mediated m6A exerts dual effects on pulmonary fibrosis. Specifically, METTL3‐regulated m6A modification of lncRNA E230001N04Rik accelerated arsenic‐induced pulmonary fibrosis via promoting myofibroblast differentiation [ ]. Additionally, METTL3‐mediated m6A modification promoted pulmonary fibrosis by enhancing LR‐MSC differentiation into myofibroblasts via miR‐21/PTEN signaling [ ]. Furthermore, METTL3 facilitated epithelial‐mesenchymal transition in pulmonary fibrosis by suppressing TSC1 via m6A modification [ ]. Another study demonstrated that METTL3‐mediated m6A‐modified HIF‐1α contributed to ferroptosis in alveolar epithelial cells [ ], while the inhibition of METTL3 reduced fibroblast activity and collagen fiber deposition via METTL3/CTGF pathway, thereby ameliorating IPF lesions [ ]. Paradoxically, METTL3 inhibitor promoted the fibroblast to myofibroblast transition, enhancing bleomycin‐induced pulmonary fibrosis [ ]. In our pulmonary fibrosis model, the silencing of METTL3 alleviated pulmonary fibrosis by blocking ferroptosis through m6A‐medified hsa_circ_0044226. These findings suggest that the dual roles of METTL3 in pulmonary fibrosis may arise from its cell type‐specific actions, divergent downstream targets, and disease stage‐dependent context.

5.

## Conclusions
In conclusion, our finding demonstrated that METTL3‐mediated m6A modification promoted ferroptosis via hsa_circ_0044226, thereby exacerbating pulmonary fibrosis pathology. These results suggest targeting the METTL3/circ_0044226/ferroptosis axis as a novel therapeutic strategy for mitigating ferroptosis‐driven pathology in pulmonary fibrosis. Therefore, subsequent research with larger sample sizes is required to confirm our findings.
