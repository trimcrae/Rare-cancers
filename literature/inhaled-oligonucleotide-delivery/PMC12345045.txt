## Background
Diabetic Kidney Disease (DKD) represents the most prevalent secondary kidney condition that progresses to end-stage renal disease globally. Empagliflozin (EMPA) effectively reduce blood glucose levels to mitigate the impact of DKD.

## Methods
Researchers extracted kidney tissues from type 2 diabetic (T2DM) rats and performed transcriptome analysis to identify differential gene expression.

## Results
The findings indicated a link to ferroptosis, closely associated with renal fibrosis. Subsequent cellular validation of T2DM and human renal proximal tubular (HK-2) cells under high-glucose conditions revealed that Collagen type I alpha 1 (COL1A1) and alpha -smooth muscle actin (α-SMA) levels increased in high-glucose group but decreased following EMPA treatment. These observations imply that EMPA mitigates renal fibrosis. Transcriptomic analysis revealed varied SLC7A7 expression in T2DM versus normal groups, and was verified by qPCR and WB. Significant changes in ferroptosis-related proteins, specifically acyl-CoA synthetase long-chain family member 4 (ACSL4) and glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4), emerged at the protein level. Further exploration revealed that EMPA suppressed ferroptosis by downregulation of SLC7A7 via the AMP-activated protein kinase/Glycogen synthase kinase 3 Beta/Nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2 (AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2) signaling pathway, thereby reducing renal fibrosis.

## Conclusions
EMPA treatment could inhibit iron death to alleviate renal fibrosis, and the process was found to be related to the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway in subsequent mechanistic studies. EMPA is a proven treatment for diabetes. Understanding EMPA’s mechanism may uncover new drug targets and innovative therapies.

## Introduction
Diabetic Kidney Disease (DKD), a frequent complication in individuals with diabetes mellitus, stands as the primary reason for end-stage renal disease, posing a significant challenge due to the few available strategies for averting or treating it, thereby burdening patients and society alike [ ]. Therefore, effective treatment of DKD is essential. An in-depth exploration of its pathogenesis could help develop new therapeutic strategies. As important membrane transport proteins, solute carrier proteins (SLCs) play a critical role in cellular substance transport and maintaining homeostasis, particularly due to their extensive distribution and significant physiological implications in the kidney [ ].
Persistent high blood glucose levels have been proven to stimulate the generation of harmful free radicals, contributing to chronic inflammation [ , ]. Suppressing blood glucose levels helps to decrease oxidative stress and inflammation, as well as lessen the risk of developing kidney fibrosis [ ] both in DKD mouse and high glucose-cultured human renal proximal tubular (HK-2) cells. The formation of reactive oxygen species (ROS) has been extensively researched as a primary mechanism of glucose-induced cytotoxicity [ – ]. There is accumulating evidence that excessive production of ROS is a shared characteristic linking changes in renal metabolic processes and disruptions in renal hemodynamics (which are known to be connected with DKD) [ ]. Tubulointerstitial fibrosis is the ultimate common pathway in many kidney diseases, including DKD [ – ]. And excess ROS also lead to different types of cell death, such as autophagy, apoptosis and ferroptosis [ ]. Ferroptosis is a form of programmed cell death that depends on Fe2+, and is marked by an excess of Fe2+, buildup of ROS, and lipid peroxidation. Endoplasmic reticulum stress activates ferroptosis-induced epithelial mesenchymal transition (EMT) leading to tubulointerstitial fibrosis in DKD [ , ]. The regulation of ferroptosis by SLCs are intricately intertwined with oxidative stress and renal pathologies. The neutral amino acid transporter SLC38A2 confers protection to the renal medulla against hyperosmolarity-induced ferroptosis [ ]. SLC7A11 promotes cystine uptake and glutathione biosynthesis, thereby preventing oxidative stress and cellular ferroptosis [ ]. These findings underscore the critical role of SLCs as potential therapeutic targets for intervening in oxidative stress - associated renal diseases by precisely controlling ferroptosis. Several studies have confirmed that ferroptosis promotes renal fibrosis and that inhibition of ferroptosis alleviates renal fibrosis [ – ].
Empagliflozin (EMPA) is a prominent drug of sodium-glucose co-transporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors. SGLT2 inhibitors play an important role in both diabetic and nondiabetic nephropathy treatment. Regarding DKD, SGLT2 inhibitors synergistically protect the kidneys through multiple mechanisms, including improved insulin secretion, normalization of glomerular hyperfiltration, diuretic effects, anti-inflammatory and oxidative stress, sympathetic enhancement, increased GLP-1 expression and erythropoietin production [ , ]. EMPA may aid in weight reduction and positively influence renal and cardiovascular outcomes. It has been confirmed SGLT2 inhibition by EMPA attenuates myocardial oxidative stress and fibrosis in diabetic mice [ ]. EMPA is involved in inhibiting the glycogen synthase kinase-3β (GSK-3β) pathway to protect the kidney [ ]. It is commonly utilized in clinical settings as a glycemic control agent.
To elucidate the molecular mechanism of reducing fibrosis by EMPA, we initially conducted a transcriptome analysis on diabetic rat kidneys to pinpoint genes with differential expression post-EMPA therapy. The results indicated that alleviation of renal fibrosis through EMPA exhibits a significant correlation with ferroptosis. Notably, transcriptome data revealed SLC7A7 as a key differentially expressed gene, which aligns with the known role of SLC family members in regulating ferroptosis and renal pathologies. This finding prompted us to hypothesize that SLC7A7 may serve as a critical mediator in EMPA’s antifibrotic effect via ferroptosis regulation. The molecular mechanism of regulating ferroptosis is highly intricate. Various compounds have been identified to impede ferroptosis through diverse molecular pathways, ultimately ameliorating renal fibrotic damage. The mechanism by which EMPA mitigates renal fibrosis in DKD, and its potential correlation to ferroptosis, present pressing concerns and a challenging issue requiring immediate resolution. Subsequently validation in high glucose-cultured HK-2 cells confirmed that EMPA alleviates fibrosis by activating the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway, which downregulates SLC7A7 to impede ferroptosis. This mechanistic link underscores SLC7A7 as a pivotal node connecting EMPA treatment to ferroptosis suppression in DKD.

## Materials and methods

## Reagents
COL1A1, COL1A2, ACSL4, α-SMA, SLC7A11, SLC7A7, GPX4, NRF2, Lamin B, AMPKα1, p-AMPKα1, GSK-3β, p-GSK-3β and β-actin were purchased from Affinity Biosciences (Melbourne, Australia). PCR transcription kit was purchased from Accurate Biology (Changsha, China). CCK8 commercial kit was purchased from Beyotime (Shanghai, China). The ROS detection kit was purchased from Solarbio (Beijing, China).

## Animals and treatment
All rats were housed in the Bengbu Medical University, and the experimental procedures were conducted in accordance with the ethical standards set forth by Experimental Animal Management and Ethics Committee of Bengbu Medical College (No. [2022] 340). Total of 36 male 200–300 g SD rats were purchased from Hangzhou Ziyuan Laboratory Animal Technology Co. LTD. Rats were housed in a SPF environment at 24 °C with a 12-hour light/dark cycle. The SD rats were fed a normal diet, while T2DM model rats were fed a high-fat diet. The study comprised three groups: (1) normal control (NC) group; (2) T2DM group; (3) T2DM + EMPA group. The NC group was fed with normal diet, and the remaining rats were fed with high-fat diet (2.5% egg yolk powder, 10% cooked lard, 20% sucrose diet) for 4 weeks. The T2DM model was established by intraperitoneal injection of STZ (30 mg/ kg dissolved in 0.1 mol/ L, pH4.5 citrate buffer). Tail vein blood samples were detected on the third day after STZ injection, and the fasting blood glucose (FBG) level was higher than 16.7 mmol/L, which proved that the model was successfully established. The rats with successful modeling were randomly selected and fed with high-fat diet and divided into T2DM group and T2DM + EMPA group. Rats in the T2DM + EMPA group were given EMPA by gavage, and the EMPA intervention dose was 1.0 mg/kg once daily. The follow-up test was performed after 4 weeks of continuous intervention.
Once the experiment had reached its conclusion, the rats were permitted to die by CO₂ inhalation, after which their kidneys were collected. 4 kidneys from each group were immediately snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen and subsequently stored at -80 °C for transcriptomics RNA extraction. 3 kidneys from each group were fixed in 2.5% glutaraldehyde for transmission electron microscopy (TEM) examination. The remaining kidneys were stored in liquid nitrogen for subsequent experiments.

## Microarray analysis
Total RNA was extracted from kidney tissues and quantified and assessed for RNA content as well as integrity using a NanoDrop ND-2000 spectrophotometer (Thermo Scientific) and an Agilent Bioanalyzer 2100 (Agilent Technologies). Microarray analyzes were conducted by Biomedical (Hefei, China). The sample labelling, microarray hybridisation and cleaning were conducted in accordance with the manufacturer’s standard protocols. In particular, the extracted total RNA was reverse-transcribed into cDNA, which was then transcribed into cRNA. The cRNA was labelled with Cyanine-3-CTP and hybridised to microarrays. Following washing, the arrays were scanned using an Agilent Scanner G2505C (Agilent Technologies).

## Bioinformatics analysis
In this study, feature extraction software (version 10.7.1.1, Agilent Technologies) was employed to obtain the raw data and analyze the array images. Genes were identified as significantly up-regulated and significantly down-regulated when the fold change (FC) was ≥ 1.5 and the p-value was ≤ 0.05. The differential genes were analyzed using Gene Ontology (GO), Clusters of Orthologous Genes (COG) database and Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes (KEGG) [ , ]. Ultimately, distinguishable patterns of gene expression between samples were demonstrated by hierarchical cluster analysis.

## Histopathological analysis
For histopathology, the fixed kidney tissue was embedded in paraffin and cut into 4 μm sections. The collagen fibers in the kidney sections were stained using the Masson trichromatic method. The fibrotic area was measured as the percentage ratio (%) of the collagen fiber area divided by the kidney area of each rat. Kidney tissue sections were stained with hematoxylin and eosin (H&E) to evaluate kidney injury and morphological changes.

## Quantitative real-time PCR (qRT-PCR) analysis
Rat kidney tissue was immersed in Trizol (Accurate Biology, China) and total RNA extracted following thorough grinding. Reverse transcription was then performed using a reverse transcriptase kit (Eppendorf, Germany), with RNA converted into cDNA. Primers for qRT-PCR were designed using the software Primer Premier of the National Center for Biotechnology Information (Table ). The primers, samples and SYBR Green qRT-PCR master mix were combined in accordance with the instructions, and then tested using the StepOnePlusTM real-time PCR system (Applied Biosystems, USA). The results were normalized according to the β-actin level of the reference gene, and the relative values were obtained. Three replicates were performed for each qRT-PCR analysis.

## Cell culture and treatment
The HK-2 human renal tubular epithelial cell line was purchased from Shanghai Cell Research Center (Shanghai, China). The cell culture medium for HK-2 human renal tubular epithelial cell was used for the experiments (Gibco, USA). The cells were cultured in a biochemical incubator at 37 °C with 5% carbon dioxide. Passage culture was performed every 1–2 days. The specific treatments of four groups in the experiment were as follows: 1) siNC + D-glucose: HK-2 cells were treated with 30 mM D-glucose for 48 h and transfected with negative control siRNA; (2) siSLC7A7 + D-glucose: HK-2 cells were treated with 30 mM D-glucose for 48 h and transfected with siSLC7A7; (3) D-glucose + EMPA: HK-2 cells were treated with 30 mM D-glucose and 500 mM EMPA for 48 h. (4) siSLC7A7 + D-glucose + EMPA: HK-2 cells were treated with 30 mM D-glucose and 500 mM EMPA for 48 h and transfected with siSLC7A7.

## siRNA transfection
HK-2 cells were cultured in medium containing 30 mmol/L D-glucose for 48 h, followed by the addition of small interfering RNA (siRNA) transfection. The siRNA was synthesized by Biomedical (Hefei, China). The siRNA sequence is shown in Table . The transfection reagent (Biomedical, China) was transiently transfected into HK-2 cells and assayed for interference efficiency. HK-2 cells were treated with EMPA for 48 h after transfection.

## Transfection with overexpression plasmid
HK-2 cells were seeded in 6-well plates at a density of 4 × 105 cells/well for 12 h. Thirty minutes before transfection, the medium of each well was replaced with 1 mL of complete medium containing serum and antibiotics. According to the manufacturer’s instructions, 5.2 ug of DNA and 8 µL of NanoTrans reagent were each diluted in 132 µL of serum-free DMEM, and the diluted NanoTrans reagent was added to the diluted DNA solution at one time immediately after mixing. NanoTrans-DNA complexes were formed by incubation for 10 min at room temperature. NanoTrans-DNA complexes were dropwise added to the medium in each well to allow uniform distribution. 8 h after transfection, the medium containing the NanoTrans-DNA complex was discarded and replaced with fresh intact medium containing serum/antibiotics. Cells 48 h after transfection were used for subsequent experiments.

## Inhibition of the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway
To restrain the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway, dorsomorphin (DM) (final concentration, 10 µg/mL) was added to the medium and the incubation was continued for a further 2 h at room temperature.

## CCK-8 assay
HK-2 cells were seeded onto 96-well plates at a density of 5*103 cells/well. After being adherently cultured on the plates, cells cultures were performed with 30 mM D-glucose for 48 h, and then two sets were performed with EMPA, other two sets were added the serum-free culture media. At last, four sets were transferred with siRNA or serum-free culture media. Four sets of experiments on cell cultures were performed: with siNC + 30 mM G-glucose, siSLC7A7 + 30 mM D-glucose, EMPA + 30 mM D-glucose, and with siSLC7A7 + EMPA + 30 mM D-glucose. To each well, CCK-8 (10 µl) was added for 2 h of exposure in an environment of 37 °C with 5% CO2. A microplate reader determined the OD value at 450 nm. Cell viability was calculated by the formula, cell viability % = (OD experiment-OD blank) / (OD control-OD blank) × 100%.

## Measurement of ROS level
Following transfection of the HK-2 cells, cell clusters were obtained at the conclusion of the EMPA intervention. The cells were then resuspended in ROS fluorescent probe staining solution (10 µM) in accordance with the instructions provided in the ROS assay kit and incubated at 37 ℃ for 30 min, protected from light. The intensity of red fluorescence was subsequently detected with the aid of a flow cytometer (Agilent, USA), and the resulting flow data were finally analyzed using FlowJo.

## Mitochondrial structure observed by TEM
Four sets of experiments on cell cultures were performed: with siNC + 30 mM D-glucose, siSLC7A7-OE + 30 mM D-glucose, EMPA + 30 mM D-glucose, and with siSLC7A7 + EMPA + 30 mM D-glucose. To each set, the HK-2 cells were fixed with 2% glutaraldehyde for 12 h, washed with PBS and then fixed with 1% samarium tetroxide for 2 h. Then the cells were subsequently dehydrated with ethanol and stained with toluidine blue. The morphology of mitochondria was observed under TEM.

## Western blot
The protein content was obtained and assessed with BCA assays (Beyotime, China). Equal amounts (20–40 µg) of proteins were then loaded onto a 10% SDS-PAGE gel and transferred to a nitrocellulose membrane (Yamay, China). Nitrocellulose membranes were incubated overnight at 4 ℃ with specific primary antibodies against COL1A1, COL1A2, ACSL4, α-SMA, SLC7A11, SLC7A7, GPX4, NRF2, Lamin B, AMPKα1, p-AMPKα1, GSK-3β, p-GSK-3β and β-actin. The membranes followed by a second incubation with HRP-conjugated secondary Abs for 2 h at RT. ECL reagent (Proteintech, China) was used to detect the protein bands, and Image J software was used to measure the bands. Each experiment was repeated at least three times.

## Statistical analysis
Experimental data were expressed as mean ± standard deviation (SD). The experiment was statistically analyzed using SPSS 19.0 software, and comparisons between multiple groups were first performed using one-way analysis of variance (ANOVA), followed by Tukey Least Significant Difference (LSD) post-hoc test to determine the significance of the differences between groups. P < 0.05 was considered statistically significant differences (*: P < 0.05; **: P < 0.01; ***: P < 0.001).

## Results

## Transcriptome sequencing analysis of the kidneys of type II diabetic rats after EMPA intervention
A total of 520 differentially expressed genes (DEGs) were identified, comprising 293 down-regulated genes and 227 up-regulated genes in the kidneys of T2DM rats compared to the NC group (Fig.  A). In comparison to the NC group, the kidneys of T2DM rats exhibited 1657 DEGs following EMPA intervention, comprising 939 up-regulated genes and 718 down-regulated genes. Conversely, the kidneys of EMPA-intervened rats displayed 786 DEGs, including 264 up-regulated genes and 522 down-regulated genes, in comparison to T2DM rats.
As illustrated in the Wayne diagram of DEGs (Fig.  B), there were 309 common genes between “T2DM vs. NC” and “T2DM + EMPA vs. NC” (the overlapping part of yellow and purple) (Fig.  B). Additionally, there were 44 genes in common among the DEGs of these 3 groups. It is worth noting that SLC7A7 and SLC17A9 gene are upregulated in the renal tissues of T2DM rats, while Slc2a1 is downregulated (Fig.  C-D). After EMPA treatment, SLC7A7 and SLC17A9 were downregulated, while SLC2A1 was up-regulated (Fig.  E-F). Compared with untreated rats, the expression of SLC7A7 in the renal tissues of EMPA-treated T2DM rats remained unchanged, and SLC2A1 was upregulated (Fig.  G-H). The opposite changes of SLC7A7 in T2DM rats after treatment with EMPA suggest that it may play an important role in the occurrence and treatment of this disease.

 

The differential genes were functionally annotated, including GO, KEGG, and COG. There were more terms of biological process and cellular component of GO functional aggregation of differentially expressed genes in the kidneys of T2DM group vs. NC group than in the kidneys of T2DM + EMPA group vs. NC group (Fig.  ). The KEGG pathways that were identical in both sets of differential gene enrichment were regulation of actin cytoskeleton, PI3K-AKT signaling pathway, MAPK signalling pathway, Rap1 signaling pathway, cAMP signaling pathway, ribosome, pathways in cancer, purine metabolism, axon guidance, and so forth. The COG functional classification of differentially expressed genes in the kidneys of T2DM group vs. NC group clustered in R: General function prediction only and E: Amio acid transport and metabolism. In contrast, the COG function classification of differentially expressed genes in the kidneys of T2DM + EMPA rats and NC rats was clustered under R: General function prediction only, T: Signal transduction mechanism, and O: Posttranslational modification, protein turnover, and chaperones.

 

Furthermore, we conducted a Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA) that was not contingent on the expression of differentially expressed genes. Subsequently, GO, KEGG, and COG enrichment analyses were conducted. The GO terms that were significantly enriched in the T2DM group compared to the NC group were mitochondria (GO 0005739, 0005758, 0005762) and ribosomes (GO 0005840, 0003735), calcium ion binding and hydrolase activity (Fig.  A-G). The KEGG pathways were Oxidative phosphorylation (KO 00190), Glycine, serine and threonine metabolism (KO 00260), and PPAR signaling pathway (KO 03320) (Fig.  H-J). Whereas the gene set of the T2DM + EMPA group with the NC group is mainly enriched in the Golgi (GO 0005795), endoplasmic reticulum (GO 0070971), and intracellular signal transduction, cell morphogenesis, and the same for calcium ion binding (Fig.  A-E). KEGG pathway has Rap1 signaling pathway, cGMP-PKG signaling pathway, ECM-receptor interaction (Fig.  F-H).

 

 

## EMPA attenuates renal fibrosis and ferroptosis in type II diabetic rats
The rat model of type 2 diabetes was successfully established 28 days later. Twenty-eight days after modelling, EMPA treatment was added, and the changes in body weight and blood glucose of the rats were measured. The body weight of type 2 diabetic rats after modelling decreased significantly compared with the NC group. Meanwhile, compared with the initial body weight of the rats after successful modelling, the body weight of the rats in the NC group showed an upward trend, that of the rats in the T2DM group showed a downward trend, and that of the rats in the T2DM + EMPA group fluctuated, with a slight increase in the final body weight (Fig.  A). The blood glucose levels of type 2 diabetic rats after modelling exceeded 33.3mmol/L, which exceeded the detection limit. After EMPA intervention, the blood glucose of rats in the T2DM + EMPA group decreased significantly, the blood glucose of rats in the T2DM group remained at a relatively high level continuously, while there was no significant change in the NC group (Fig.  B).
Based on Masson’s trichrome staining, compared with the NC group, significant fibrosis was observed in the kidneys of rats in the T2DM group. EMPA treatment effectively alleviated renal fibrosis in these rats (Fig.  C). HE results showed that in the T2DM group, the interstitial spaces between renal tubules in many areas of the kidneys were widened, with a large amount of pink extracellular matrix deposition (red arrows), indicating accumulation of collagen-like substances, a sign of fibrosis. Irregular deformation and atrophy of local renal tubules were observed, and some epithelial cells were shed or arranged disorderly, suggesting progressive damage to the renal tubules (yellow arrows). After EMPA treatment, the renal tissue structure was relatively regular, and no obvious fibrosis or significant aggregation of inflammatory cells was found (Fig.  D).
The qPCR and WB results in this study showed that the renal fibrosis-related biomarkers COL1A1 and α-SMA were elevated in the kidneys of T2DM rats. However, the intervention of EMPA reversed this elevation (Fig.  E), suggesting that EMPA has the ability to alleviate renal fibrosis in T2DM rats.
To further explore the mechanism of attenuating diabetic nephropathy by EMPA from the RNA sequencing results, we screened the genes with opposite expression in T2DM rats and T2DM rats after EMPA intervention as the beginning of the mechanism exploration and validated the genes with significant changes. The results showed that the expression of SLC7A7 and SLC17A9 mRNA levels were elevated in the T2DM group compared with the NC group and decreased after EMPA intervention (Fig.  F).
The results of protein level expression validation were consistent with the mRNA results, and the protein expression of SLC7A11 was decreased in the kidneys of rats in the T2DM group compared with that in the NC group, and the protein expression of SLC7A11 was restored to the normal level in the kidneys of T2DM rats after the intervention of EMPA (Fig.  G). SLC7A11 are closely related with ferroptosis closely [ , ]. Therefore, we tested ACSL4 and GPX4, two ferroptosis biomarkers. The results showed that ACSL4 and GPX4 mRNA levels did not change significantly in the kidneys of rats in the T2DM group compared with the NC group (Fig.  G). However, compared with the NC group, ACSL4 protein expression was up-regulated and GPX4 protein expression was decreased in the kidneys of rats in the T2DM group, and both of them were restored to normal levels after EMPA intervention (Fig.  H-J). The above results suggested that EMPA intervention could attenuate the occurrence of ferroptosis in the kidney of T2DM rats.

 

## EMPA attenuates kidney cell injury in type II diabetes by inhibiting SLC7A7
The expression of SLC7A7 was elevated in the kidney of T2DM rats, and EMPA inhibited overexpression of SLC7A7. The study used HK-2 cell in vitro to further explore the mechanism. The knockdown of SLC7A7 in HK-2 cells was verified by qPCR and WB using siRNA. Compared to the siNC group, the knockdown efficiencies of siSLC7A7-1, siSLC7A7-2, and siSLC7A7-3 were 47%, 72%, and 67% respectively (Fig.  A-C). The siSLC7A7-2 with the highest transfection efficiency was selected for further experiments.
The CCK-8 results showed that compared to the siNC + D-glucose group, the cell viability of siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group and D-glucose + EMPA- group elevated in HK-2 cells. Furthermore, the cell viability of siSLC7A7 + D-glucose + EMPA group increased significantly relatived to D-glucose + EMPA group (Fig.  D). The results show that the combined use of EMPA and siSLC7A7 further reduced cell death caused by high-+D-glucose.
Similarly, compared to the siNC + D-glucose group, the expression of fibrosis markers (COL1A2 and α-SMA), ferroptosis-promoting protein (ACSL4) and ferroptosis inhibitors (SLC7A11 and GPX4) all decreased in the HK-2 cells in the siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group or D-glucose + EMPA group. And the effect was even more pronounced when EMPA and siSLC7A7 were used together (Fig.  E-G).
The elevation of SLC7A7 may mediate the increased ROS and mitochondrial structural damage in HK-2 cell. The results showed that compared to siNC + D-glucose group the level of ROS decreased in the siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group and D-glucose + EMPA group (Fig.  H and I). What’s more, the study shows that in the siNC + D-glucose group severe damage to mitochondrial structure, with most of the mitochondrial matrix swollen and the cristae and inner membrane structures disappearing (blue triangles). While in the siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group and D-glucose + EMPA group the part of mitochondrial structure returned to normal, the mitochondrial cristae and double-membrane structures appeared, and the number of mitochondria increased. And the expression of siSLC7A7 + D-glucose + EMPA group group was the same as the siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group and D-glucose + EMPA group. These results suggested that EMPA reduces renal damage by inhibiting SLC7A7 in T2DM (Fig.  J).

 

The study further confirmed the antagonistic effect of SLC7A7 on the protective role of EMPA by overexpressing SLC7A7 in HK-2 cells. The CCK-8 results showed that compared to the D-glucose group, cell viability was significantly increased in the D-glucose + EMPA group. However, compared to the vector + D-glucose + EMPA group, cell viability was significantly reduced in the SLC7A7-OE + D-glucose + EMPA group (Fig.  D). TEM images showed that the mitochondrial morphology in the D-glucose + EMPA and vector + D-glucose + EMPA groups was partially restored, including increased mitochondrial number and the presence of cristae and double-membrane structures. However, in the SLC7A7-OE + D-glucose + EMPA group, most mitochondria showed matrix swelling and loss of cristae and inner membrane structures (Fig.  E). Similarly, the ROS level in the SLC7A7-OE + D-glucose + EMPA group was significantly higher than that in the vector + D-glucose + EMPA group (Fig.  F-G). These findings further suggest that SLC7A7 overexpression may lead to increased ROS levels and mitochondrial damage in HK-2 cells, and that EMPA alleviates renal injury in T2DM by inhibiting SLC7A7.
Western blot analysis of fibrosis and ferroptosis-related proteins showed that compared to the vector + D-glucose + EMPA group, the expression levels of SLC7A7, COL1A1, α-SMA, and ACSL4 were increased in the SLC7A7-OE + D-glucose + EMPA group, while the expression of SLC7A11 and GPX4 was decreased (Fig.  H-J). These results suggest that SLC7A7 overexpression attenuated the protective effect of EMPA against high glucose-induced ferroptosis and fibrosis in HK-2 cells.

 

## EMPA attenuates type II diabetic kidney injury by inhibiting SLC7A7 elevation through the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway
The study associated proteins of SLC7A7 through the String database and drew a protein interaction network map to search for proteins that interaction with SLC7A7. The results showed that SLC7A7 interacts with SLC7A14, SLC43A1, and SLC3A1 (Fig.  A). Then, we conducted GO and KEGG enrichment analysis on these interacting proteins. The GO enrichment analyzes showed that it is closely related to the transport of amino acids and organic acids, and the KEGG enrichment analyzes showed that it is closely related to protein digestion and absorption, glutamate synapse, bile secretion, and ferroptosis (Fig.  B-C). This is consistent with the fact that overexpression of SLC7A7 participates in glucose-induced ferroptosis in HK-2 cells.
Next, we wanted to explore the molecular mechanisms that how SLC7A7 mediates ferroptosis in HK-2 cells. The results showed that knockdown of SLC7A7 and EMPA increase the expression of NRF2 and reduce the phosphorylation of AMPKα-1 and GSK-3β in HK-2 cells nuclear (Fig.  D-E). Moreover, Dorsomorphin (DM), an AMPK inhibitor, significantly reduced the cell viability and increased the protein expression levels of COL1A1, α-SMA and ACSL4 compared with siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group. SLC7A11 and GPX4 protein expression levels were decreased (Fig.  F-H). These results suggest that EMPA inhibits the expression of SLC7A7 through the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway and alleviates renal injury in T2DM.

 

## Discussion
DKD is one of the most common and serious complications of type 2 diabetes. The pathogenesis of DKD has not been fully elucidated. EMPA, an SGLT2 inhibitor, has been shown in several studies to have favorable renal protective effects in patients with DKD [ – ]. It delays the progression of renal disease in patients with type 2 diabetes [ ], reduces urinary albumin levels [ ], and is the drug of choice for the prevention of renal complications and related deaths in patients with high-risk T2DM [ ]. However, the mechanism by which EMPA works remains to be further clarified. In addition to DKD, EMPA has been reported to improve renal ischemia-reperfusion injury [ ], proximal tubular epithelial cell migration and epithelial mesenchymal transition [ ]. Glomerulosclerosis is a typical pathology in DKD, accompanied by increased ECM. In particular, α1 type IV collagen (Col4) was significantly increased, and Smad1 is a transcription factor for Col4, thus is critical for glomerulosclerosis in DKD. EMPA modulates ECM homeostasis in DKD, which may regulate the development of thylakoid matrix expansion in early DKD through the AngII-Src-Smad1 pathway [ – ].
We detected the occurrence of fibrosis and ferroptosis in renal tissues of diabetic rats and verified the mitigating effect of EMPA, so the present study focused on the mechanism of ferroptosis in the treatment of diabetic renal fibrosis with EMPA. In this study, elevated gene and protein levels of COL1A1 and α-SMA were used as renal fibrosis biomarkers [ ], and both gene and protein expression levels were significantly reduced after the intervention of EMPA, confirming that EMPA effectively alleviated renal fibrosis in type II diabetic rats in vivo. The expression levels of ferroptosis-related molecules (SLC7A11, ACSL4, and GPX4) in the kidneys of type II diabetic rats were restored to the normal level after the intervention of EMPA. It is suggested that the intervention of EMPA can alleviate renal injury by inhibiting the occurrence of ferroptosis in the kidneys of type II diabetic rats. We found that SLC7A7 played important mediating roles in EMPA attenuating ferroptosis and fibrosis in renal proximal tubular cells in diabetic rats, and no previous studies have confirmed the mechanism between SLC7A7 and ferroptosis. Considering that renal tubular cells are most frequently involved in patients with type II DKD, we mainly focused on the injury of HK-2 cells, and it remains to be further investigated whether the mechanism of injury of other cells, such as glomerular cells, is different.
Consistent with our findings, there are increasing data demonstrating that high glucose promotes HK-2 cell death [ ], and that EMPA attenuates HK-2 cell death in high glucose models [ ]. Apoptosis, autophagy, necrosis, and pyroptosis have been reported to play a role in the progression of diabetic nephropathy by possibly contributing to renal tubular epithelial cell injury [ – ]. In addition, according to previous studies, ferroptosis at the tubular level due to glucose overload may also contribute to the development of diabetic nephropathy [ , ]. Ferroptosis is a distinct type of programmed cell death characterized by iron-dependent, ROS production and lipid peroxidation, and the systems involved in iron death include SLC7A11, the GPX4 regulatory axis, and the ACSL4, ALOX15 axis [ , ]. Our results showed that EMPA inhibited the elevation of the ferroptosis-promoting protein ACSL4 and restored the elevation of the ferroptosis-antagonist proteins SLC7A11 and GPX4, suggesting that EMPA inhibited the process of ferroptosis in the high-glucose model HK-2 cells. This is consistent with other studies [ ]. Furthermore, SLC7A7 plays a key role in the regulation of the incidence of diabetic nephropathy [ ]. Similarly, we found that EMPA attenuated ROS elevation and mitochondrial structural damage in type II diabetic nephropathy cells by inhibiting the expression of SLC7A7, whereas SLC7A7 overexpression abolished the protective effects of EMPA. In conclusion, EMPA attenuated type II diabetic renal cell injury by inhibiting SLC7A7. Targeting mitochondria and ROS has been recognized as an important target for the treatment of DKD, and the transcriptomic data from this study illustrate that EMPA alters numerous mitophagy-related DEGs in the kidneys of type II diabetic rats (Figure ) [ ].
It has been established that SLC7A7 is an important target, and that EMPA attenuates type II diabetic kidney injury by inhibiting the expression of SLC7A7, but the exact mechanism needs to be explored. Our results showed that ROS levels were significantly elevated in HK-2 cells in a high-glucose environment, which is consistent with Ma et al.‘s study, which showed that oxidative stress is an important mechanism in the pathogenesis of diabetic nephropathy [ ]. NRF2 is an important transcription factor for maintaining cellular redox homeostasis [ ], which is associated with the development of ferroptosis [ ]. Therefore, we explored the signaling pathway of EMPA in attenuating diabetic nephropathy through SLC7A7 from NRF2. AMPK, as one of the targets of diabetes therapy, regulates glucose energy metabolism and promotes the nuclear accumulation of NRF2, which allows it to exert antioxidant effects [ ]. The expression of nuclear NRF2 and p-AMPKα1 was found to be elevated in siSLC7A7 + D-glucose group, and the protective effect of siSLC7A7 was offset by AMPK inhibitor, suggesting that SLC7A7 attenuates tubular ferroptosis in diabetic patients through the AMPK/NRF2 pathway [ ]. GSK-3β, a downstream molecule of AMPK, is also closely related to nuclear NRF2 expression, and the knockdown of SLC7A7 in the present study also reduced GSK-3β expression in this study. Similarly, a study demonstrated that EMPA attenuated renal ischemia/reperfusion injury in mice by inhibiting the phosphorylation of GSK-3β [ ].
We detected several identical KEGG pathways enriched in DKD and EMPA-treated DKD rats in our RNA sequencing results, and although we did not conduct research on them in the current mechanistic study, they are closely associated with T2DM nephropathy. It has been shown that the PI3K-AKT signaling pathway [ , ] is associated with numerous complications of T2DM, which is consistent with our study. MAPK, as an important transmitter of intracellular signals, it has been shown that Ginsenoside Rg [ ]and theaflavins [ ] alleviated renal fibrosis by inhibiting activation of the MAPK pathway in T2DM mice, and they exerted the same effect as the engeletin used in this study. The Rap1 signaling pathway has been enriched in several bioinformatics and network pharmacology studies on T2DM [ – ], and is a potential target for studies of DKD mechanisms.

## Conclusions
DKD is the most common secondary kidney disease, and renal fibrosis is the ultimate pathway of nephropathy. In the present study, transcriptome analysis revealed that EMPA treatment could inhibit iron death to alleviate renal fibrosis, and the process was found to be related to the AMPK/GSK-3β/NRF2 pathway in subsequent mechanistic studies. Exploring the mechanism of action of EMPA can help expand the therapeutic strategy of diabetic nephropathy.
