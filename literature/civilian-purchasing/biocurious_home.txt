 Welcome to BioCurious!

 At BioCurious, we believe that innovations in biology should be accessible, affordable, and open to everyone.
We’re building a community biology lab for amateurs, inventors, entrepreneurs, and anyone who wants to
experiment with friends.

 BioCurious is a complete working laboratory and technical library for entrepreneurs to cheaply access equipment, materials, and co-working space. We have a training center for lab techniques, with an emphasis on safety, and offer a meeting place for citizen scientists, hobbyists, activists, and students to innovate.
