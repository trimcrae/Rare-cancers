 Way more than just plasmids

 Whether you’re sequencing PCR products or whole genomes, our line of services eliminates the sequencing-related headaches scientists assume are unavoidable, delivering a reimagined experience you didn’t know you needed. 

 RNA-Seq

 Genome scale insight at the speed of qPCR. Send cells and get analyzed transcriptomes in as fast as 3 days.

 Whole Plasmid

 Catch errors in your backbone before they wreck your cloning. Send us purified plasmids or colonies and get answers overnight. 

 Amplicon

 Simple setup and fast results for amplicons of any size.

 Whole Genome

 Fast and complete genome sequences for nearly any organism. 

 Microbiome

 Species level identification directly from raw environmental samples or PCR products. 

 AAV

 Go from intact capsids to full-length AAV genome sequences in 3 days and identify potential issues other sequencing methods can miss.
