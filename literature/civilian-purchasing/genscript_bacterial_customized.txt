 Customized Bacterial Expression Services 

 Overview

 GenScript offers a comprehensive E. coli protein expression platform designed
 to meet your needs from high-throughput expression to pilot-scale production and
 scale up production, with GMP capability available, our platform supports expression
 from 4 mL to 5000 L .

 Our expert team leverages a robust toolbox to tackle challenges such as
 low yield, poor solubility, and hard-to-express proteins , ensuring high
 success rates across diverse targets. With over 50,000 projects successfully delivered,
 we’ve produced a wide range of proteins including enzymes, cytokines, antibody
 fragments, artificial proteins, antigens, and beyond . 

 In addition to our broad capabilities in recombinant protein development, we
 offer a specialized Cas nuclease production service with a guaranteed
 service package - tailored to accelerate your CRISPR research with confidence.

 Case Studies

 Case 1: Professional refolding_Protein 

 Background 

 rhBMP9 protein, 3 inner-chain disulfide bond and 1 inter-chain disulfide bond

 Key Challenge 

 Hard to express in soluble condition when expressed in E.coli 

 Up to 2mg/L in CHO cell

 Optimization 

 Inclusion body treatment and denaturation process

 Refolding buffer screening by dilution refolding way

 Result 

 Final Yield: 73mg/L

 SEC-HPLC purity: Up to 99%

 Case 2: Enzyme manufacturing 

 We have extensive experience with high-quality enzyme production, including vector/sequence design, upstream and downstream process optimization, customized QCs as well as activity assays.

 For different stages in enzyme production, from screening and evolution to supply, our solutions can achieve ideal and customized delivery by expressing at high-density in large volumes, providing high purity.

 Our scientists are dedicated to solving any existing technical barriers, from cloning strategy to protein purification. For in vitro diagnostics and gene and cell therapy applications, we have successfully delivered various enzymes, including Exonuclease III, TelN Protelomerase, Cas9/Cas13a, MAD7, T4 RNA Ligase, DNA polymerase, BsaI, Benzonase, etc. 

 Case 3: Cytokine_Interleukins-10 

 Background 

 A recognized inflammatory and immunosuppressive factor 

 Key Challenge 

 Hard to express in soluble condition when expressed in E.coli 

 limited output 

 Optimization 

 Proper incubation to get more protein 

 Inclusion body utilization: wash and refolding 

 Result 

 Total Amount: 75.15 mg 

 Expression Scale: 50 L 

 Pure and correct folded protein
