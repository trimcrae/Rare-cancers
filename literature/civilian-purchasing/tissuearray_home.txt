 Paraffin Tissue Sections 
 
 We have pre-cut histology paraffin (FFPE) tissue sections, including normal human paraffin sections HuFPT , human cancer paraffin tissue sections, HuCat and normal mouse/rat paraffin tissue sections. 
 Formalin fixed, paraffin embedded histology tissue sections are ideal choices for rapidly localizing DNA, RNA and protein markers. 
 The tissues were fixed by formalin no longer than 48 hours, and then processed to be ready for sectioning. 
 Two serial tissue sections with 5 µm thickness are mounted on a SuperFrost Plus glass slide. 
 TissueArray.com histology paraffin tissue section is perfect fo fast detection of genes and proteins expression in specific tissues of different species.
