 Ordering & Payment

 How do I set up Plasmidsaurus as a vendor for my institution?

 If we're not yet a vendor at your institution, please have your buyer or lab manager reach out to us at ar@plasmidsaurus.com . Our onboarding team is very efficient and we can get set up very quickly. Here is our W-9 form .

 How can I get a quote?

 Please sign in and click the “ Quote ” button on the top menu bar.

 What are my payment options?

 You have several payment methods available:
 Credit Card: Direct payment at the time of order.
 Purchase order (PO): You can enter the PO number when you order.
 Payment Link: Convenient for quick order submission with a credit card payment link sent directly to your purchasing officer.
 Dinocoins: These are prepaid sequencing credits that you can purchase at any time for later use. They are added to your account and can be utilized by an individual or shared among a lab group. The balance of Dinocoins is always visible in your account. Buy 1,500+ Dinocoins to get 5% extra bonus Dinocoins.
 The most convenient purchase options are Dinocoins and blanket POs (also known as standing POs). These allow you and your lab members to share the same PO or pool of Dinocoins without the need for a new quote or PO each time an order is placed.

 How do I receive an invoice?

 Using a Purchase Order: We invoice monthly and send out invoices at the beginning of each month for transactions from the previous month. If you require a different invoicing schedule, please contact us at ar@plasmidsaurus.com .
 Paying by Credit Card: You will receive a transaction receipt via email. Additionally, you can access and print both the invoice and the receipt from the "Order Information" page.
 Paying via Payment Link: A receipt will be emailed to the address provided during the payment process. You can also access and print both invoices and receipts from the "Order Information" pages.

 How do I cancel my order?

 You can cancel your order before we have received your samples. To do so, please sign in, navigate to your Dashboard via the top menu bar, scroll down to the “Order History” section, and click the red “X” button to cancel an order.

 What discounts do you offer for bulk purchases or long-term commitments?

 We offer the following pricing incentives for bulk sequencing purchases:
 •Orders of 96 or more samples through your Dashboard (for Whole Plasmid, Amplicon, Genome, AAV, and Microbiome Sequencing) will automatically receive a 5% direct discount on the total price of that order.
• If you purchase >$1,500 in Dinocoins, we will credit an extra 5% free credit of Dinocoins to your account.
 For questions or to explore other options, please reach out to sales@plasmidsaurus.com .

 How can I connect with sales?

 We'd love to hear from you! We can set up demos, lunch and learns for your team, support, and volume pricing. Please reach out to us at sales@plasmidsaurus.com .

 Dinocoins

 How do I purchase Dinocoins and add them to my balance?

 Log into your account, navigate to the order page and click on "Buy Dinocoins" 
 Select your method of payment for the purchase of Dinocoins:
 • Credit Card payment: Direct payment at the time of order. You will be directed to a secure portal. 
 • Credit Card link: You will receive a secure credit card payment link in your email. This link can be sent to other emails as well.
 • PO (Purchase Order) Number: You can enter a PO number when you order. If you are using a new PO that is not already in our system, please also email your PO documentation to our Finance Team at ar@plasmidsaurus.com . 
 Upon order submission, you will receive a 6-digit code that confirms your Dinocoin purchase.
 You should immediately see your Dinocoin balance listed at the top of your Dashboard.

 How do I submit a sequencing order using my Dinocoin balance?

 First make sure you have Dinocoin balance available in your account. Then, when submitting a sequencing order through your Dashboard, just select “Dinocoins” as your payment method.

 Where do I find my Dinocoin balance?

 You can find your Dinocoin balance by clicking on your name on the right side of the menu bar. Your balance can be viewed under Payment Methods - Dinocoins.

 How do I share dinocoins among members of my lab or group?

 Dinocoins can be added to either an individual or lab wallet. You need to be associated with a lab in our system for Dinocoins to be lab owned and shared with lab members. You may establish a lab when placing an order in the 'Order Review' page. 

 Can I transfer my Dinocoins to another user?

 Yes, you can contact our Finance Team at ar@plasmidsaurus.com to get your Dinocoins transferred to another user (such as a labmate).

 Can I pay for a sequencing order using both Dinocoins AND a credit card or PO?

 Orders can only use one payment method.

 When I cancel an order or sample (or when it is marked empty upon receipt by the lab), will its value be refunded in Dinocoins?

 If you originally paid for your sequencing order with Dinocoins or with a credit card, you will receive Dinocoins refunded to your account balance.
 If you originally paid for your sequencing order with PO, you will receive a refund to the balance of your PO (no Dinocoins are refunded).

 Do Dinocoins expire?

 Yes, Dinocoins expire five years after purchase.

 Are Dinocoins refundable?

 No, Dinocoins are not refundable, and they cannot be exchanged for cash value. Any balance of Dinocoins that is not used before the 5-year expiration period will be forfeited. If you’re coming up on the expiration date but have no planned orders, you can contact our Finance Team at ar@plasmidsaurus.com to get your Dinocoins transferred to another user (such as a labmate).

 Data retention and retrieval

 How does data storage work at Plasmidsaurus?

 Your results zip file is always instantly retrievable from your order page. Larger raw read files (FASTQ zips and BAM zips) are moved to archived storage after a certain amount of time. Archived files are still safely retained, but they need to be “retrieved” before they can be downloaded or reanalyzed.
 We never permanently delete archived data. Raw read files (FASTQ, BAM, POD5) are retained in archive storage indefinitely, unless requested otherwise. 

 Does archiving affect my sequencing results, reports, or analysis files?

 No. Your final sequencing reports, analysis outputs, and processed results are always instantly accessible on your order page. Archiving only applies to large raw read files (FASTQ and BAM).

 Why do you archive raw files?

 Archiving older files helps us keep our infrastructure efficient so we can continue investing in faster turnaround times, new services, and better tools - without raising prices.

 How long does it take to retrieve archived data files?

 Thawing of archived files typically completes in a few hours. Once your files are thawed, everything runs as normal — no further action needed on your end.

 How does data archiving impact RNA-Seq reanalysis (UUS)?

 If you'd like to add a UUS to rerun alignment on an RNA-Seq order older than 45 days, please contact support@plasmidsaurus.com and we'll restore your data and queue the reanalysis. Turnaround is typically 8–16 hours from when we receive your request.

 I need to download the raw read zip for an older order. What do I do?

 If it's been more than 90 days since your order was sequenced, just contact support@plasmidsaurus.com and we'll retrieve and rezip your raw reads for you at no cost! Plan for roughly 8–16 hours from when we receive your request to when your raw read zip is ready to download.

 How can I avoid data retrieval delays?

 If you know you'll want to keep raw read files long-term - for example, for downstream analysis, publication archiving, or lab records - we recommend downloading them within the first 45 days after sequencing for storage.

 Shipping

 Where are shipping instructions now located?

 We've updated our website! Shipping instructions are now located at the bottom of every sample prep page for a service. These have been tailored to your specific service, making it easier for you to follow along!

 How do I ship my samples internationally?

 Depending on where you're shipping to and from, you'll likely require some forms. Templates for each service and location are available in the sample prep instructions for each service. 

 Can you tell me more about the forms I need for international shipping?

 Read more about the forms you need directly from the carrier's website:
 Commercial Shipping Invoice 
 Detailed Description US 
 Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA) Certificate 
 Detailed Description UK 
 To access specific shipping instructions, head over to the sample prep instructions for your service!

 Will you send back my sample?

 Plasmidsaurus does not return samples to customers due to the volume of samples we handle and the sequencing processes.

 How do I request a new dropbox be added in my area?

 Please use this form to request a new dropbox and we'll be in touch soon!

 About Plasmidsaurus

 How should I cite Plasmidsaurus sequencing services in publication?

 For proper citation, we recommend the following phrase: “[Type of Service] was performed by Plasmidsaurus using [Sequencing Technology] with custom analysis and annotation.” Please change [Type of Service] accordingly to Whole Plasmid Sequencing, Linear/PCR Sequencing, Premium PCR Sequencing, RCA Sequencing, Bacterial Genome Sequencing, or Custom Sequencing. Please change [Sequencing Technology] to Oxford Nanopore Technology for all services except RNA-Seq, or to Illumina Sequencing Technology for RNA-Seq.

 I have a suggestion about how you can improve your service. Who can I contact?

 Our goal is to accelerate progress in biological research and we welcome any suggestions about how to improve our service. Please contact support@plasmidsaurus.com with any suggestions or comments.
