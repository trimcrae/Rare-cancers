 Join us in the lab and start a project that meets the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention’s Biosafety Level 1 guidelines. Lab Members receive access to Genspace's facility, equipment, and basic lab training from our staff. 
 Become a member and choose a plan that works for you. The Community Membership is geared toward collective hands on learning revolving around selected topics in the Genspace community. If you have your own research idea, an Individual Membership would best membership tier. For businesses, we offer a a Premium Membership, which includes dedicated storage space in the lab. Read more about our membership tiers below .

 Applications for membership are reviewed on a rolling basis. After receiving your responses, our lab manager will reach out to schedule a meeting and lab walk through. If you are interested in becoming a member but have no previous lab experience, we strongly recommend taking our Biohacker Boot Camp class.
