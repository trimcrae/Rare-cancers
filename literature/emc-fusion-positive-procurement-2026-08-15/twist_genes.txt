 1.8 – 3.2 kb: 13¢/bp (Low/Medium) | 18¢/bp (High)

 3.2 – 7.0 kb: 18¢/bp (Low/Medium) | 23¢/bp (High)

 Cloning: Cloning vector: $35 | Expression vector: $55 | Custom vector: $75

 Turnaround time (in business days): Express: 4 – 7 | Standard: 10 | High complexity: 15

 Customization options: Animal-Origin-Free prep, BSE/TSE certification, Transfection grade preps, Endotoxin free, Target yield, Poly-A QC

 Error rate: 100% sequence perfect, NGS verified
