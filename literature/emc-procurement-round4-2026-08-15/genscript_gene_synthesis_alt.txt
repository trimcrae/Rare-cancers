 How is GenScript's FLASH Gene so fast and different from
 competitors? 

 FLASH Gene redefines the future of gene synthesis, offering the
 fastest
 turnaround time and a unique flat-rate pricing model. Powered by the GenGenius™
 automation platform, FLASH Gene is shipped in just 4 business days 
 without
 compromising quality.

 Does FLASH Gene's turnaround time include shipping?

 The turnaround time indicates production time only. FLASH Genes are
 shipped overnight or via express shipping from local production facilities.

 What is FLASH Gene's Flat Rate Pricing model? What does it include?

 Our flat rate pricing makes budgeting easy and ensures cost-effective
 DNA
 synthesis. This model covers DNA synthesis, cloning, and default 4ug plasmid
 preparation — all for a fixed price starting at just $89 per
 construct .
 No
 surprises, just clear and predictable costs.

 What if my sequence doesn’t qualify for FLASH Gene? 

 Our GenSmart™ 2.0
 platform automatically checks sequence eligibility. If your sequence doesn’t
 qualify, we offer Premium Gene for more advanced synthesis needs. You can also use our
 free codon optimization tool to improve
 your sequence and increase the chance of qualifying for FLASH Gene.

 How does GenSmart™ Codon Optimization help with FLASH Gene
 qualification?

 Our industry-leading, free codon optimization tool adjusts your
 sequence using host-preferred codons to improve expression and increase eligibility for
 FLASH Gene. Try GenScript’s free GenSmart™ Codon Optimization Tool here .

 What are the default and available plasmid quality control options?

 The standard quality control for FLASH Gene includes Sanger or NGS
 sequencing. Additional quality controls can be bundled with plasmid DNA
 preparation .

 Why is vector onboarding necessary, and can I onboard my own vector?

 Vector onboarding ensures the fastest turnaround with ready-to-clone vectors. Even

 your vector is already with us, it needs to be archived again for the quickest
 turnaround.

 If you choose to onboard your own vector, please note that it will add 5 business

 to the turnaround time. To speed up your project, we also offer over 170 FLASH Gene
 vectors for you to select from.

 Typically, the synthesis success rate is mainly determined by DNA structures including repetitive sequences and GC content, and the stability in host cells including the toxicity for specific host cells. To 
 improve the synthesis success rate of your gene sequences, we have summarized the main factors that we take into account for sequence analysis.

 The main factors are including:

 Global GC content less than 20% or higher than 80%

 Various GC content within a DNA sequence

 Homopolymers

 Repeats of ≥ 20 bp

 The number and the length of small repeats

 Palindromic sequence

 With over 18 years experience on nucleotide synthesis, GenScript has fully developed state-of-the-art platform to handle both easy and difficult gene sequences. For the coding sequence, we strongly recommend codon optimization to reduce the difficulty. If you have any questions on our Gene Synthesis Services, please contact your technical account manager for details.
