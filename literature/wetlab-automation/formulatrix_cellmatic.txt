 Built for Next-Gen Cell Culture 

 The Cellmatic is a fully automated cell culture system that handles your entire cell culture workflow continuously, from feeding and imaging to passaging and plating, without manual intervention. Define your protocol, load your cells, and walk away. The system manages the rest, around the clock, with consistency and reproducibility across every run.

 Designed for everything from routine cell line maintenance to complex, month-long iPSC differentiation protocols, the Cellmatic scales to your throughput and adapts to your workflow.

 Truly Walk-Away Cell Culture 

 Load your cells, define your protocol, and step back — the system runs 24/7, unattended 

 Many Cell Lines in Parallel 

 Dynamic and versatile scheduler with flexible reagent handling supports 50+ cell lines in culture at once 

 Built to Stay Sterile 

 HEPA filtration, positive pressure access points, and hydrogen peroxide vapor sterilization 

 Key Features 

 Fully Automated Cell Culture Workflow: Automate the entire cell culture process, including feeding, splitting, expansion, plating, and differentiation, freeing researchers to focus on science instead of manual execution.

 High-Capacity Plate Handling: Each Incubator can support up to 200 SBS-format plates (1- to 384-well formats), enabling parallel processing of multiple cell lines and high-throughput experiments simultaneously.

 Intelligent Scheduling and Parallel Processing: An advanced scheduler allows multiple users and protocols to run simultaneously, automatically interweaving tasks without scripting or programming.

 Versatile Application Support: Supports stem cell differentiation, cell line development, high-throughput plating, permeability assays, and more — all on a single platform.

 Smart Liquid Handling with Minimal Waste: Each Cold Dispense unit can support 5 media solutions in a sterile, refrigerated environment. Low dead volumes (500 µL) and reusable tips minimize reagent waste and running cost.

 A Plate Storage Rack Housed in the Incubator 

 Integrated Cold Storage System: Provides robotic-access refrigerated storage for up to 96 SBS deep-well blocks, supporting large-scale reagent management in automated workflows.

 Automated Labware Handling with Stack ® : The Integrated Stack robotic arm moves plates and labware between subsystems inside the sterile enclosure, coordinated automatically by the Cellmatic scheduler, eliminating manual handling and reducing contamination risk.

 Advanced Environmental and Contamination Control: HEPA filtration, hydrogen peroxide vapor sterilization, UV, and fully enclosed, CO₂, temperature, and humidity controlled Incubators maintain optimal, sterile growth conditions throughout operation.

 Intuitive, Web-Based Software: User-friendly, no-code interface with real-time monitoring, scheduling, and data export. Access the system and receive alerts from anywhere via web-based software.

 FAQs 

 What is Cellmatic, and what does it automate? 
 Cellmatic is a fully automated cell culture system that performs cell culture workflows from start to finish, including feeding, splitting, expansion, plating, and differentiation. The system continuously monitors cell growth and executes culture tasks based on predefined schedules or confluency triggers, reducing manual labor while improving consistency and reliability in cell culture workflows. 

 &nbsp;

 What hardware components are integrated within the Cellmatic?
 Cellmatic integrates liquid handlers (FLO i8 PD and the high-throughput Reagent Exchanger), for automated feeding, passaging, and plating, an imager for monitoring cell growth, a CO₂-controlled incubator for culture plate storage, and a refrigerated dispenser for media and reagents. Labware storage cabinets support external plate and consumable loading, while autonomous Rover plate handlers move plates between subsystems. All components are coordinated through intelligent scheduling software for seamless automation. 

 &nbsp;

 How does the Cellmatic handle plate movement and storage?

 Cellmatic offers flexible plate storage capacity that can be configured to meet your needs. Wireless Rover robots transport plates gently and spill-free between incubators, imagers, and handling stations, with adjustable slow or normal speed motion. A built-in scanner ensures accurate plate detection and placement throughout the system. 

 &nbsp;

 What cell culture workflows does the Cellmatic support? 
 The Cellmatic excels across stem cell differentiation, cell line development, high-throughput plating, permeability assays, immunology studies, and routine maintenance and expansion, making it a versatile solution for adherent cell workflows. 

 &nbsp;

 What liquid handling workflows are supported?
 Cellmatic supports seeding, media exchange, harvesting, cell counting, reagent mixing, and plating. The FLO i8 PD liquid handler is used for dissociating cells, passaging, and cell counting, while the Reagent Exchanger performs gentle, but fast, media exchanges, compatible with organoids and stem cells. 

 &nbsp;

 How scalable is the Cellmatic system for long-term workflows? 
 The Cellmatic’s scheduler can support long-term formats like stem cell differentiation over weeks to months. Users program triggers like confluency or time-based splitting, and then leave the system to manage the process. Month-long differentiation protocols can be easily defined with the intuitive software user interface. 

 &nbsp;

 Does Cellmatic support high-throughput experiments? 
 Yes, Cellmatic can plate many cell lines in parallel, reduce manual labor by using plates instead of flasks, and support multi-user workflows efficiently. The Reagent Exchanger also enables high-throughput processing with its 96-nozzle dispenser, allowing rapid media exchange and plating across multiple wells simultaneously. 

 &nbsp;

 Can the Cellmatic be configured for regulatory compliance? 
 Currently, Cellmatic is designed for research and development use. Work is underway to develop features that will support GMP and regulatory-compliant workflows in the future. 

 &nbsp;

 What support and validation resources are available?
 Cellmatic includes continuous CCTV surveillance and is monitored 24/7 by the FMLX support team. If any errors occur, the team is immediately notified and can log in remotely to resolve issues, with 95% of errors fixed within 30 minutes without disturbing the cells. Additional resources include webinars, specification documents, and expert guidance from product managers to support the implementation and optimization of workflows. 

 &nbsp;

 How does the scheduling software work? 
 The Cellmatic uses intuitive, dynamic scheduling that enables users to define operations like feeding intervals or to trigger passaging based on confluency without the need to code. Users can run multiple workflows in parallel, with Cellmatic interweaving tasks automatically for efficient operation. 

 &nbsp;

 Can researchers monitor and control workflows remotely? 
 Yes, Cellmatic’s web-based interface allows researchers to monitor workflows in real time and receive notifications by email or text. Integrated CCTV cameras inside the system provide additional visual monitoring, helping track progress and ensure smooth operation even when accessing the system remotely. 

 &nbsp;

 How does the Cellmatic minimize reagent waste? 
 Cellmatic minimizes waste by filling reservoirs on demand inside the refrigerated storage, delivering only the media or reagent needed for each workflow. This eliminates long tubing from reagent bottles to liquid handlers, reducing flushing and conserving valuable media while maintaining precise, low-dead-volume dispensing. 

 &nbsp;

 How does Cellmatic monitor cell growth? 
 Cellmatic includes a built-in imager that captures daily images of cell cultures, allowing researchers to track growth, confluency, and overall culture health in real time. 

 &nbsp;

 What environmental controls does Cellmatic have? 
 Cellmatic maintains sterile and consistent culture conditions with HEPA filtration, VHP sterilization, and precise CO₂, temperature, and humidity control in the incubators.
