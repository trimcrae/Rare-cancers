 Build Your Lab of the Future—One Module at a Time
 Connect, analyze, and automate all of your dry and wet lab data in one secure, modular platform.

 Connect
 Ganymede’s platform offers hundreds of canned connectors and makes it easy to build new ones with a variety of connectivity tools.

 Automate
 Automated data capture and calculation of analysis via Lab-as-Code allows writing your results straight into your ELN or LIMS or other tools like MES, no human required.

 Analyze
 Our innovative flow-based web IDE allows you to write Lab-as-Code automation and build pipelines directly against your integrated data, or even build your own integrations.
