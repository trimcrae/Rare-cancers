 Sequences 

 Specification Value Details 

 Product format 
 Circular dsDNA 

 Length 
 0.3 kb – 7.0 kb 

 Complexity 
 Low | Medium | High 
 
 A sequence’s complexity score is based on the combined presence, frequency, and length of various features within the design of a sequence. Impacts the ease and speed of production.

 High complexity sequences are produced though a series of specialized manufacturing workflows. Supported features include:

 Short tandem repeats: 3–9bp repeats in up to 100bp stretches

 Long repeats: repeats up to 200bp

 Inverted (palindromic) repeats: up to 100bp

 Localized GC extremes: 10–90% within a 50bp window

 Sequence-wide GC extremes: 25–75%

 Stable secondary structures: hairpins, G-quadruplexes

 Codon optimization 
 100s of target organisms 
 Large language model-driven for protein expression.
