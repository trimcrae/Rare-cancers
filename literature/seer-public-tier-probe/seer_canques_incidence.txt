 Welcome to the Cancer Statistics Explorer Network

 Find the cancer statistics tool best suited to your needs.

 SEER*Explorer

 Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results Program

 Cancer statistics from SEER data by race, age, sex, stage, and cancer subtypes, covering 46% of the U.S. population.

 Go to SEER*Explorer 

 NCCR*Explorer

 National Childhood Cancer Registry (NCCR)

 Childhood, adolescent and young adult cancer statistics from NCCR data, ages 0-39, covering 75% of the U.S. population, and using International Classification of Childhood Cancer (ICCC).

 Go to NCCR*Explorer 

 CONCORD*Explorer

 A collaboration with the Cancer Survival Group at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine to present childhood cancer statistics at an international level.
 
 The CONCORD*Explorer features childhood cancer statistics from CONCORD data, including patients diagnosed in more than 70 countries.

 Go to CONCORD*Explorer
