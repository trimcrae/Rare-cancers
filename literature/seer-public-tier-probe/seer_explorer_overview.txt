 Overview

 SEER*Explorer is an interactive website that provides easy access to a wide range of SEER cancer statistics. It provides detailed statistics for a cancer site by sex, race, calendar year, age, and for a selected number of cancer sites, by stage and histology.

 Preliminary Estimates of SEER Incidence Through 2024

 SEER*Explorer now includes preliminary estimates of age-adjusted rates and trends through 2024 based on delay-adjustment modeling of SEER Incidence data. These series preview patterns expected in the upcoming April 2027 data release. These estimates will be removed from the application in April when the official SEER estimates through 2024 are released. See Preliminary Cancer Incidence Rates and Trends, 2000-2024 for more details.

 How Can I Provide Feedback?

 The goal is to improve SEER*Explorer with each new SEER data release, we are interested in receiving feedback from users. Please send comments to SEER Website Support .

 Citing SEER*Explorer

 SEER is supported by the Surveillance Research Program (SRP) in NCI's Division of Cancer Control and Population Sciences (DCCPS) . SRP provides national leadership in the science of cancer surveillance as well as analytical tools and methodological expertise in collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and disseminating reliable population-based statistics.
