June 22, 2004 
Errata for ICD-O-3 Site/Type Validation List 
The ICD-O-3 Site/Type Validation list has been  modified to account for behavior. If a histology is listed in the table with a /0, /1 or /2 and not a /3 then the site/histology 
combination is not valid with a behavior of 3. However, all histologies listed with a /3 are also valid with a /2. 
The following site/histology combinations were removed from the list 
C440-C449 !PAPILLARY SEROUS CYSTADENOCA 846 8461/3 Serous surface papillary carcinoma 
The following site/histology combinations were added to the list 
C500-C509 PAPILLARY ADENOCARCIOMA 826 8261/3 Adenocarcinoma in villous adenoma 
C540-C549 !PAPILLARY SEROUS CYSTADENOCA 846 8461/3 Serous surface papillary carcinoma 
C619 DUCT CARCINOMA 850 8500/3 Infiltrating duct carcinoma, NOS 
C670-C679 PAPILLARY ADENOCARCINOMA 826 8261/3 Adenocarcinoma in villous adenoma 
The ICD-O-3 Site/Type Validation list has been updated to match the NAACCR Implementation Guide 2004 Appendix D in preparation for the collection of Benign Brain 
tumors. For a complete list of the valid site/type combinations please reference the following website: 
http://www.naaccr.org/filesystem/pdf/2004%20Implementation%20Guidelines%201103%20Errata%200204.pdf
