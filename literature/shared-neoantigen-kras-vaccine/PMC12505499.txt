## Virus-based immunotherapy improved outcomes in prostate cancer
The oncolytic immunotherapeutic aglatimagene besadenovec (CAN-2409, Candel Therapeutics) combined with radiation therapy improved disease-free survival by 30%, compared to standard-of-care radiation therapy alone, in 750 patients with intermediate- to high-risk localized prostate cancer. The Phase 3 trial also reported an 80% pathologic complete response rate after 2 y versus 64% in the control group.
CAN-2409 consists of a non-replicating adenovirus designed to deliver into tumors the herpes simplex virus thymidine kinase, which converts valacyclovir (administered orally) into a toxic compound.

## Bispecific antibody holds promise for lung cancer
First-line treatment with the bispecific antibody Pumitamig (BNT327/BMS986545, BioNTech & BMS), which targets PD-L1 and VEGF-A, combined with chemotherapy induced a 76% overall response rate with median progression-free survival of 7 months in 38 evaluable subjects with extensive-stage smallcell lung cancer enrolled in the randomized Phase 2 BNT327-01 trial. Pumitamig was safe with a discontinuation rate of 14%.
Pumitamig, which is being tested in 20 clinical trials and various solid tumors, targets both the immune checkpoint ligand PD-L1 for increased antitumor T-cell response and the angiogenesis factor VEGF-A to hamper intratumoral vascular development.

## KRAS vaccine is highly immunogenic in solid cancers
The therapeutic vaccine ELI-002 (Elicio Therapeutics) targeting mutated KRAS protein induced specific T-cell responses in 89 of 90 patients involved in the Phase 2 AMPLIFY-7P trial. About 85% of evaluable subjects generated both CD4+ and CD8+ responses.
ELI-002 is a CpG-adjuvanted vaccine targeting mutated KRAS protein, which is one of the most common tumor-associated neoantigens.

## Latest COVID-19 vaccine is immunogenic
The mRNA vaccine adapted for the latest circulating SARS-CoV-2 lineage, mNEXSPIKE (Moderna), induced a >16-fold increase in neutralizing antibodies in subjects 12 y of age and older. The vaccine targets the currently dominant LP.8.1 strain of the virus.
mNEXSPIKE was tested as a post-marketing verification, as the vaccine has been approved both in US and EU.

## Alzheimer’s disease immunotherapy was safe in early trial
The Alzheimer’s disease vaccines ACI-35.030 (JNJ-2056) and JACI-35.054 (both Janssen & AC Immune) were safe and immunogenic in patients with early-stage disease in a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, dose-escalation Phase 1/2 trial. Subjects receiving the highest dose of ACI-35.030 reported significant drops in plasma and brain Tau levels.
Both immunotherapeutics target the pathological forms of the Tau protein, including phosphorylated Tau. ACI-35.030 consists of a liposome with an anchored Tau peptide plus T-cell epitopes as adjuvants. In JACI-35.054, the Tau peptide is covalently linked to the carrier protein CRM197.

## CAR-T cell therapy shows signs of activity in multiple sclerosis
The CAR-T cell therapeutic KYV-101 (Kyverna Therapeutics) was safe with no serious adverse events in four patients with non-relapsing progressive multiple sclerosis who completed a Phase 1 trial. Furthermore, CAR-T cells penetrated into the cerebrospinal fluid by day 14, and disability score was stable or better in three evaluable subjects at the 6-month mark.
KYV-101 is an autologous CAR-T regimen targeting the Bcell marker CD19, designed to deplete central nervous system-resident B cells.

## Fast-track designation for a HER2-targeting vaccine
The US Food and Drug Administration has granted its fast-track designation to the HER2-targeting therapeutic vaccine GLSI-100 (Greenwich LifeSciences) as second-line treatment of HER2-positive breast cancer with HLA-A *02 genotype. The decision is based on long-term Phase 2 data showing 80% reduction in cancer recurrence, compared to 20–50% reduction seen after standard-of-care treatments.
GLSI-100, which consists of a nine-amino-acid peptide derived from the HER2 protein and the GM-CSF adjuvant, is administered in six injections in the first 6 months and then a booster dose every 6 months.

## Chikungunya vaccine demonstrates long-term immunogenicity
A 95% sero-response rate after 4 y was reported from the Phase 3 VLA1553-303 testing the chikungunya vaccine VLA1553 (Valneva) in 250 healthy adults.
VLA1553, the only approved vaccine for the prevention of chikungunya virus infection, is a single-dose, live attenuated vaccine against the mosquito-borne viral infection, which is a significant public health burden in tropical regions of the world.
