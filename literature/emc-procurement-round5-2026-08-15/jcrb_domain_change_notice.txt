 【ImportantNotice】Domain Name Change (from April 1st)

On April 1st, 2025, the abbreviation of our corporation, to which JCRB Cell Bank belongs, will be changed from NIBIOHN to NIBN. (Release) 

Due to a system update related to this, our service will be temporarily suspended as follows.

April 9th, 2025, from 12:00 to 13:00 (UTC +9)

Accordingly, JCRB Cell Bank’s domain will be updated as follows. Access to the old domain will be automatically redirected to the new address.

[E-Mail: Updated on April 1st, 2025 (Tue)]

OLD: jcrb-cell@nibiohn.go.jp

NEW: jcrb-cell@nibn.go.jp 

[Website: Updated on April 9th, 2025 (Wed)]

OLD: https://cellbank.nibion.go.jp

NEW: https://cellbank.nibn.go.jp 

Thank you for your understanding.
