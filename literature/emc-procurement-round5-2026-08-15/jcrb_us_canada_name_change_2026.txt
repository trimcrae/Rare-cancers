Notice of Company Name Change and email contact change

Please be informed that the company currently operating as FUJIFILM Irvine Scientific, Inc., the exclusive North American provider of JCRB’s comprehensive catalog of cell lines, will officially change its name to FUJIFILM Biosciences Inc., effective January 1, 2026. Meanwhile, the email address will change to FLBILSorders@fujifilm.com. 

This change will not affect our business operations, or the excellent service you have come to expect. Our address, telephone number, fax number will remain the same:
