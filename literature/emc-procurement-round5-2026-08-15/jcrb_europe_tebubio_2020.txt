 To customers in Europe (Tebubio as the new distributor)

As of July 8, 2020, we are pleased to announce that we have added Tebubio as our European official distributor in the following countries: 

Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway(New!) Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, and UK.

Hereafter, new orders from Europe are accepted through Tebubio. JCRB’s frozen cell lines are shipped from Tebubio’s warehouse in France to customers. Tebubio has their local office in 9 countries in Europe, so please contact your local Tebubio to order JCRB cell lines. For details and contact information, please refer to the “Flow of distribution of cells” page. (set region to EU, Norway, Switerland and UK at the top of page.)

If you have already placed orders through Sekisui XenoTech which are not shipped yet, the current orders are continuously processed by Sekisui XenoTech. If you have any question about current orders, please contact Sekisui XenoTech or inquire from “Contact us” page. 

We ask for your kind understanding and cooperation. Thank you for your constant support until now and in the future.

If you need further information, please inquire to JCRB Cell Bank from the “Contact us” page.
