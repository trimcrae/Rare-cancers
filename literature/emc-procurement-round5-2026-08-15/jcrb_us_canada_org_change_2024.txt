 Organizational change of the US/Canada official distributor

FUJIFILM Wako Chemicals U.S.A. Corporation, which has been responsible for the distribution of JCRB cells in the United States and Canada, is integrated into its sister company Fujifilm Irvine Scientific (FISI) on October 1, 2024.

Accordingly, FISI is the official distributor contact point for the JCRB cell bank from October 1, 2024. 

Contact address including the phone number and the email address is not changed.
