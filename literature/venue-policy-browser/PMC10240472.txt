Eleven papers based on the presentations at ODF’22 are published in this issue or have been published in the prior issues. These papers were reviewed and accepted according to the editorial policy of Optical Review.
ODF is an international forum for engineers and scientists in the field of optics-photonics design and fabrication to exchange their ideas and achievements to promote future progress, and it has been held biyearly since 1998.
This time, ODF’22, CLEO-PR 2022 and ISOM'22 were jointly held at the same venue (CLEO-PR 2022: the 15th Pacific Rim Conference on Lasers and Electro-Optics, ISOM'22: International Symposium on Imaging, Sensing, and Optical Memory 2022). The papers were presented in a hybrid format due to COVID-19 expansion. ODF’22 was organized by Optics Design Group of OSJ and sponsored by JSAP (OSJ: the Optical Society of Japan, JSAP: the Japan Society of Applied Physics). We sincerely thank 23 other organizations for their cooperation.
At ODF’22, provided were the plenary session, a joint session with ISOM’22 on “Advanced imaging technologies,” a special session on “Optics for life sciences,” and regular sessions on four categories: Optical Design/Simulation, Optical Components/Devices, Optical Systems, and New Technologies.
The total number of papers reached 93 from 12 countries, and the total number of participants was 143, excluding extra participants from CLEO-PR 2022 and ISOM'22. The conference was successful and is followed by ODF’24 to be held in Arizona, USA, on July 10th to 12th, 2024.
Finally, we would like to express our sincere gratitude to all who contributed to ODF’22 and the editor’s thank goes to honest reviewers for their fair judgments.
