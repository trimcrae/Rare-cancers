## Abstract
In recent years, under the background of exam-oriented physical education reform, the government has issued a lot of physical education documents and policies. As a result, many schools have made fundamental changes in physical education activities. These policies are mainly aimed at getting young people to be more active and autonomous in school sports. This requires physical education training teachers must adhere to the people-oriented, student-oriented, so as to highlight the subject status of students. Based on this, students should establish a good sports values and ethics and form a good habit of lifelong sports, so as to further promote the sustainable development of youth sports activities. Based on the ecological theory of sustainable development as the background, this study constructs a unified theory of Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT) model for the sustainable training and development of youth skating sports training institutions. This study selects the youth skating sports training institutions in Beijing, China as the investigation object, covering small, medium and large institutions of different sizes, and analyzes based on the statistical data in 2023 and the results of field research. The results show that the application of this model not only increases the willingness of youth skating training institutions to continue to participate by 3.66%, but also promotes the diversified development of youth skating training. In addition, this study also discusses the environmental protection measures implemented in the process of skating training to ensure that the training activities are sustainable while reducing the impact on the environment, so as to build a sustainable development of the new ecology of skating sports training.

## Introduction
Under the current educational policy changes, the sports training industry has undergone significant changes, which provide people with new opportunities. Making clear the positive impact of these changes on the youth sports training industry is the basis of analyzing the opportunities it provides for the development of the industry. Based on this background, this article studies the new ecological and environmental protection measures of youth skating training. The research shows that these changes bring new development opportunities to the skating training industry, but also bring many challenges. Exploring these challenges and actively seeking development strategies is of great practical significance for promoting the healthy and sustainable development of youth skating training industry. The purpose of this article is to promote the development of the youth skating training industry towards a more diversified and environmentally friendly direction based on the ecological theory of sustainable development.
Youth skating training is very important to the quality and sustainable development of physical education. Based on the analysis of the impact of compulsory education on the development of youth education, this article finds that education is returning to its essence. While increasing the development of skating training, attention should also be paid to market demand. At present, social sports training has gradually replaced scientific training and occupied the mainstream position. Therefore, exploring the new ecology of skating training and implementing environmental protection measures are of great significance to the healthy and sustainable development of youth skating training.
Major contributions:
1) By constructing a sustainability model of youth skating training based on UTAUT model, a new perspective is provided to analyze and predict the willingness of youth to participate in skating training.
2) The study proposes to integrate environmental protection measures into skating training practice, aiming at achieving ecological sustainable development of sports training activities.
3) In response to parents’ anxiety and adolescent health problems, strategies are proposed to alleviate parents’ concerns and promote the improvement of adolescents’ physical fitness by improving the transparency and quality of training.

## Related work
Now there are relevant researches on adolescent sports training by scholars: Gebel A proposed that adolescent sports training was literally the general term of training and cultivation, that is, the training and cultivation were regarded as the fundamental way for students to acquire certain skills. According to the definition of United Nations Education Scientific and Cultural Organization, training was a activity to achieve a certain purpose or complete a certain task to impart relevant knowledge, skills and attitudes . Drezner J A emphasized that youth sports training was to train young people in social training institutions for the rest of their time after school sports and extracurricular activities through coaches’ teaching and students’ practice . Homare discussed the concept of sports training. He believed that sports training was to improve the quality and skills of young people, and also to meet the needs of young people in some aspects. Its training forms were flexible and diverse, and the training content was rich and colorful . Mccarthy P J gave the following definition of physical training: it referred to a kind of planned and step-by-step training and cultivation aimed at sports consumers. It was mainly to impart knowledge, skills and methods of sports activities, and supplemented by physical exercise, which strove to enhance its athletic ability based on previous research results . Whitley M A gave a definition of youth sports training, which was mainly based on the service objects, training methods and training purposes. With the progress of the times, parents were no longer confined to the consciousness of “emphasizing intellectual education and neglecting sports”. They were willing to spend more money to improve their children’s physical quality and seek coordinated development in many aspects . The improvement of teenagers’ requirements for sports training is not only the change of people’s ideas, but also the result of social and economic development. In general, youth sports training is different from school sports, which aims to improve sports skills, master sports knowledge and meet individual needs through the combination of coaches’ teaching and students’ practice.
Some scholars have conducted relevant research on physical education. Cheng Q believed that physical education has become the focus of general attention, which is conducive to the healthy and orderly development of the entire sports industry; the field of sports has obtained a rare opportunity for rapid development, providing greater space for youth sports activities . Dai Y pointed out that in order to effectively promote physical education in schools, educational policies must be fully implemented. At the same time, after-school training institutions should be supervised to safeguard the interests of parents and promote the all-round growth of teenagers . Lin X believed that schools can invite parents to participate in students’ physical education to form an education model of home-school cooperation, so that parents can support the school’s work and further promote the all-round development of students . According to Rogol A D’s actual survey, many parents felt less pressure from sports and less financial burden on their families. With the decrease of after-school delayed service activities, youth’s leisure time has increased, but students still lack professional equipment and sports venues in terms of sports activities . Jackman P C proposed that physical education teachers should make reasonable arrangements for students to make use of existing school resources and strengthen physical exercise for teenagers . Parents should also update their teaching concepts to adapt to the development of The Times and improve their ability in many aspects.

## Investigation of youth sports training
At present, the youth sports training industry is developing in full swing, which attracts the attention of many scholars. Relevant scholars empirically discuss the youth sports training market from the perspective of supply and demand. The motivation of youth to participate in sports training mainly includes four aspects: requirements for entering a higher school, benefits for physical and mental health, satisfies children’s interests, and enriches their spare time life , as shown in Fig.  . The current problems in the youth sports training market are inadequate laws and regulations in the training market, low professional level of management personnel and coaches, and simple promotion methods of sports training institutions.
 Youth sports training institutions have good prospects for development, which is mainly because of clear market positioning, brand strategy, market diversification and strong government support . Although the physical, emotional and social abilities of teenagers are very important, their psychological abilities can not be ignored. The coaches of youth sports training institutions should help students enhance their psychological abilities to promote their individual growth.
Some scholars analyze the influence factors of youth sports training by analyzing the situation of youth participating in sports activities in different fields. It is pointed out that the physical growth and emotional needs of young people, the professionalism and diversity of sports training institutions, the support and encouragement of others, the publicity of mass media and the demonstration effect of celebrities, standard equipment and venues, a good sense of sports atmosphere and other factors play an important role in young people’s selection of sports training institutions . Some scholars also discuss youth skills training by means of literature analysis and policy research, and believe that other conditions such as unclear training characteristics, low barriers to entry into the industry, and decentralized market scale are the main factors restricting the development of youth sports training market . If young people’s needs for teaching and service quality, facilities and safety, cost-effectiveness and other aspects can be fully met, the comprehensive service quality of young people’s sports training institutions can be significantly improved, so as to better maintain customer stickiness. By sorting out the relevant literature on youth sports training in the world, it is found that Chinese scholars mainly focus on youth sports training market and institutions. The research on the influencing factors of youth sports training is much the same with little difference, which is mostly related to the demand for youth to participate in sports training and the market environment for youth sports training . This article explores the sustainable participation of youth in skating sports training, enriching the body of research on the subject while also serving as a valuable reference for other scholars. Under the background of sustainable development, it has important theoretical value and practical significance to study the new ecological and environmental protection measures of skating sports training.

## Investigation and evaluation of youth sports training institutions
(1) Investigation of scale and business model.
The data of this study came from in-depth interviews with 20 registered youth skating training institutions in Beijing. These institutions include small, medium and large skating schools, and involve three models of independent operation, rental operation and cooperative operation. According to relevant statistics, as shown in Fig.  , the average number of students of the 20 skating training institutions interviewed is temporarily no more than 550. Among them, the number of students in six training institutions is between 150 and 250, accounting for the majority. As can be seen from the data in Fig.  , the training institutions for young skaters are mostly small and medium-sized institutions. However, large training institutions have more trainees, which is more conducive to the healthy development of the market.
 Good sports facilities can not only ensure the normal development of skating sports training activities , , improve the quality of teaching service of youth skating training, but also one of the key factors related to the future development of skating training institutions. According to the operation mode, it can be divided into three modes: independent operation mode, lease operation mode and cooperative operation mode.
The independent operation mode means that the skating sports training institution has its own skating facilities , , can arrange the time and place of training by itself, and optimize the management of various facilities and resources in the venue, and the training is not limited by the venue and time. This self-management mode is generally more suitable for large and medium-sized skating training institutions. The investment cost is higher, but the service management is better.
Leasing operation mode means that skating training institutions sign long-term or short-term contracts with venues to conduct teaching and training activities within a specified period of time. Skating activities are limited by time , . The leasing method is flexible and can be divided into long-term leasing and short-term leasing. The advantage is that it does not require maintenance facilities and can focus on skating training activities.
The cooperative operation mode means that the venue only provides corresponding facilities, and the other party manages the venue as a whole and obtains part of the equity from it , . Operators are responsible for hiring managers, coaches, employees, and students, and bear the risk of profit and loss.
(2) Qualification of training institutions.
The youth skating sports training started late, but developed rapidly , . Therefore, a variety of skating sports training institutions have appeared in the market, and many students and sports enthusiasts have participated in the course. This makes the service level of the entire skating sports training market uneven, and it is difficult for the relevant government departments to regulate the number of training institutions, as shown in Fig.  . The survey shows that most of the skating sports training institutions interviewed are only registered in the industrial and commercial sector, with a total of 10, accounting for 50%. Only eight are registered in the education sector, and only two are registered in both the business and education sectors. This situation leads to the regulatory confusion of various regulatory departments, and it is difficult to effectively manage and supervise skating training institutions.
 (3) Investigation of coaches in the youth sports training market.
In skating sports training, coaches spend the longest time with teenagers, and their words and deeds have a subtle impact on teenagers. In this study, 35 coaches of skating sports training institutions are selected as the research objects, and the education background of coaches is investigated by random investigation method. According to the data in Fig.  , the cultural level of skating coaches is uneven, and the overall cultural level needs to be improved.
 (4) Nature and source of coaches’ work.
According to the data survey in Table  , the coaches of skating youth training institutions mainly come from sports-related majors and universities, with a total of 20, accounting for 57.14% of the total number of the survey. The students of sports related colleges and majors are generally influenced by pedagogy, psychology and other disciplines, and have a better understanding of the growth law of youth sports , ; retired athletes have stronger special skills, but they are mainly concentrated in sports colleges and universities for competition training, to achieve higher sports results. If it is applied to the teaching of youth skating sports, it may neglect the research in other fields. “Self-training members of society”, “self-training institutions” and “general sports enthusiasts” have not received formal physical education, and often ignore the importance of the law of youth physical education in teaching.
 It can be seen from Fig.  that the training level of primary school students increases from kindergarten to primary school, and then decreases again in the transition to junior high school and senior high school. This may be because pupils learn relatively easily and have a long time after school. In order to build a social activity platform for their children, most parents are willing to send their children to various training classes for training, which can not only enable them to get good physical exercise, but also enhance their social skills. After entering junior high school, under the influence of examination oriented education in the past, teenagers often bear the pressure of academic promotion. Their leisure time is getting less and less, and it is difficult to squeeze out time to carry out sports.
 (5) Investigation of trainees’ motivation for training.
Training motivation refers to the reason and motivation for athletes to participate in training and persevere , . It plays a decisive role in cultivating interest, perseverance, motivation and sustainability. According to the data in Table  , the top three motivations of the respondents are physical exercise, hobbies and sports skills improvement, which are all subjective motivations and are conducive to the continuous conduct of intensive training and youth sports training. Other motivations, such as parental demands and decompression, are relatively passive and the proportion of overall choice is small. There are also some motivations, such as the training of professional athletes, the need for college entrance and examination, etc. This is due to the increasing proportion of sports in the secondary school entrance examination and college entrance examination. Training institutions can adjust training methods, enrich training content and introduce different training course models to adapt to the participants’ motivation.
 (6) Selection of trainee training program.
In the survey of trainees in the youth sports training market, the selection of trainee training programs is shown in Table  .  (7) Investigation of difficulties encountered by trainees in training.
Skating sports training industry belongs to sports service industry, and its development should meet the market demand. The difficulties encountered by the trainees in the training process can reflect their training requirements , . Training institutions can adjust the teaching and management in response to these difficulties, so that the entire training and management mode is more in line with the needs of students, improves the service quality of institutions, and achieves the goal of coordinated development of supply and demand, so as to promote the rapid growth of the youth skating sports training market. As can be seen from the data in Table  , the difficulties encountered by students in skating training include poor physical fitness and insufficient training time, which are also related. For example, skating has high requirements for physical fitness, and some other sports also have certain requirements for physical fitness. Most students feel that the training time is not enough, because after the weekend training, parents have to arrange other tutoring classes for them, and it is difficult to arrange other training time. However, changes in physical fitness can only be achieved through quantitative changes, requiring the accumulation of training. Therefore, due to the lack of training time, it is often difficult to improve physical fitness. A small number of students also express their views on the venue facilities and the level of coaches. This shows that most students are satisfied with the services provided, but the overall service level of the skating sports training market, venue hardware and other aspects need to be improved.
 

## UTAUT model based continuous participation behavior of youth sports training students
(1) Theoretical basis of integrated technology acceptance and use model.
Unified Theory of Acceptance and Use of Technology (UTAUT) model is developed on the basis of Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), which is a unity. This includes seven models: technology acceptance model, theoretical behavior theory, planned behavior theory, incentive model, innovation diffusion theory, social cognition theory and computer use theory. It is mainly composed of influencing factors of use intention and use behavior: performance expectation, effort expectation, social influence and promotion conditions. It also points out that gender, age, experience and voluntariness as control variables significantly affect key factors, as shown in Fig.  . Performance expectation is the perceived usefulness of users using the system. It involves five key model variables, namely perceived usefulness, external motivation, work consistency, comparative advantage and outcome expectation. Effort expectation is how much effort users need to make when using the system. It consists of three key model variables, namely perceived ease of use, complexity and ease of use. Social impact refers to the extent to which individuals think they are affected by the surrounding environment. It consists of three key model variables: subjective norms, social factors and social image. The promotion condition emphasizes the individual’s perception behavior control of relevant technology and equipment, objective reinforcement condition and compatibility.
 Aiming at the sustainable development of skating sports training, this study discusses how to provide professional and personalized training services for teenagers while protecting the environment. The research shows that participation in skating training can not only improve the skill and level of sports, but also increase the theoretical knowledge of sports, which has important educational value. The pleasurable level of the training experience is closely related to the trainees’ attention to the training process, and also affects their participation expectations and confidence. The trainees’ trust in the ability of coaches and the system of training institutions directly affects their understanding and mastery of the training content, and then affects the training effect and the willingness to continue to participate. As shown in Fig.  , the conceptual model proposed in this study shows that adolescents’ cognitive interest in skating sports training has a positive impact on their effort expectations and willingness to continue to participate. At the same time, the actual situation of skating training also affects the students’ expectations and willingness to participate. Contextuality plays a mediating role between these expectations and wishes, regulating students’ performance and participation in decision-making. The perceived degree of training pleasure mediates the expectation of effort and the willingness to continue to participate. Finally, on the basis of the trainees’ trust in the training institutions, the effort expectation and performance expectation have a positive impact on their willingness to continue to participate in the training, so as to promote the youth’s continuous participation in the skating training behavior.

Results and evaluation of the experimental data of the new ecology of sports training and environmental protection measures

The sustainable development of youth skating training involves many aspects. The quality of training institutions, the quality of training coaches, the purpose of youth training, the content of training programs, and training difficulties may all affect the sustainable willingness of youth skating training. In order to further explore the deep influencing factors of the sustainable development of youth skating training, this study selects the sustainability of youth skating training as the dependent variable q; the quality of training institutions is an independent variable q1; the quality of training coaches is an independent variable q2; the purpose of adolescent training is independent variable q3; the training content is q4; the training difficulty factor q5 is an independent variable. The multiple regression model is constructed. The discrete variables q1, q2, q3, q4 and q5 are further logarithmically processed and then added to the regression formula for analysis.
(1) Data correlation.
Table  shows the results of correlation analysis among various variables. It can be concluded that the correlation between variable q and q1, q2, q3, q4, q5 is large and each variable has a strong correlation, so the next step of multiple regression analysis can be carried out.
 (2) Standard deviation.
The standard deviation is the deviation of each data from the average value. The larger the standard deviation, the greater the distance between each data point and the average value of data distribution. It can be seen from Fig.  that youth training purpose q3, training project content q4 and training difficulty factor q5 have standard deviations greater than 0.8.
 (3) Skewness.
The skewness test aims to measure whether the data are asymmetric or evenly distributed. When the deviation exceeds 0, the data distribution is left biased and characterized by the right long tail. When the skewness is below 0, the data distribution is right skewed and shows a long left tail. It can be seen from Fig.  that the skewness analysis results of youth training purpose, training project content and training difficulty factors are larger and more directional.
 (4) Kurtosis.
The kurtosis specifically describes the data distribution, and the peak value of the standard normal distribution is at 0. In the case of scattered data distribution, the kurtosis below 0 is a low peak. When the data distribution is relatively centralized, the peak is when the kurtosis is above 0. It can be seen from Fig.  that the peak values of youth training purpose and training project content are greater than 1, which indicates that these data values are relatively concentrated in a certain range.
 The regression model analysis shows that the independent variables such as the purpose of youth training, the content of training programs, the factors of training difficulties, the quality of training institutions, and the quality of training coaches have a significant impact on the sustainable intention of youth skating training. In other words, the more active the purpose of youth training and the more diversified the content of training programs, the more sustainable participation behavior of youth skating training can be improved. In general, the construction of the model not only improves the sustainable participation willingness of youth skating training by 3.66%, but also creates a good policy environment for the development of youth skating training.
In order to test the generalization ability of the model, five sports such as basketball, swimming, track and field, football and tennis are selected for corresponding experiments. Multiple regression analysis is used to assess the sustainability of youth training in different sports and to compare the key influencing factors in these sports. Data is collected for each variable (q1, q2, q3, q4, q5), and the regression coefficient of each independent variable is calculated to evaluate its impact on training sustainability (q). The results of the evaluation are shown in Table  .
 Table  shows the impact of key factors in five sports on the sustainability of youth training. The results show that q2 plays an important role in several sports, especially in football, swimming and basketball, indicating that good coaching can significantly increase youth’s willingness to participate in the long-term. q3 is evident in athletics and tennis, demonstrating that clear personal goals are crucial to maintaining motivation to participate. q5 has a large impact in basketball and track and field, and appropriate challenges can help improve participation. Overall, the emphasis of the influencing factors in each sport is different, reflecting the unique needs of each sport.

## Conclusions
Based on the investigation of the current situation of youth skating training, this study puts forward some suggestions for youth skating sustainable training, and constructs a UTAUT model of youth skating sustainable training willingness. This model can significantly improve the participation willingness of youth skating training institutions. The study also highlights the importance of implementing environmental measures in skating training to ensure the sustainability of training activities while reducing the impact on the environment. The contribution of the article is to provide new ecological and environmental protection measures for the youth skating training industry. In response to parents’ anxiety, the study recommends that it is alleviated by improving training transparency and communication efficiency, and providing parents with regular training progress reports and health data to enhance parents’ trust in training providers. At the same time, by providing professional sports training and health guidance, children’s physical fitness and health can be effectively improved. The future research direction can further explore the impact of different types of sports training on adolescent mental health, and how to enhance the personalization and efficiency of sports training through scientific and technological means. Research can also be extended to other types of youth physical activity to provide a more comprehensive strategy for industry development.
