## Introduction
It has been over 40 years since China’s reform and opening-up policy in 1978. During this period, China’s economy has developed rapidly and achieved remarkable achievements. Data shows that in 2023, China’s GDP was 126.06 trillion yuan, approximately 17.89 trillion US dollars, accounting for approximately 17% of global GDP. Obviously, China has become an important force in global economic development. Therefore, Chinese economic development is achieved at the expense of environment , , and caused seriously environmental damage.
Data from “World Energy Statistical Yearbook (2023)” jointly released by KPMG China and UK Energy Institute shows that the CO2emissions generated by energy in Chinese Mainland in 2022 are 10.55 billion tons, which is the largest in the world, accounting for 30.69% of the global total. This indicates that China’s economic growth model of high growth and high energy consumption has led to increasingly serious environmental problems, and is contrary to the global trend of sustainable development . Nowadays, the global economy is facing significant challenges of green and sustainable development, and countries are increasingly concerned about balancing environmental protection and economic development. The Paris Agreement provides a clear carbon emission schedule, which has brought both new pressure and great motivation to China’s green development. Under the background of global economy’s sustainable development, green total factor productivity (GTFP) as a combination strategy under the dual goals of economic growth and environmental protection has around great attention. Meanwhile, as the important component of the global economy, China’s green development is of great meaning. China is a big country and the urban economy is the sub-unit of China’s economy. That is, cities are not only the mainstay of China’s economic growth, but also the main source of various pollutant emission and environmental pollution problems. Therefore, studying the urban green total factor productivity (UGTFP) of China is essential for achieving green and sustainable development both for China and for the whole world.
Many scholars have conducted researches on how to improve UGTFP. And recently, the digital economy is booming and has provided new opportunities to promote urban’s green growth . The digital economy is a new kind of economic form, and aims to promote a more unified fairness and efficiency . The digital economy has driven great changes in production methods and has become a key strength in restructuring global factor resources, reshaping global economic structure, and changing the global competitive status .
The new changes in economic practice have driven the rapid growth of related research. However, due to different research perspectives and research methods, the research conclusions are undefined in both theoretical and empirical on whether and how the digital economy affects green economic growth, thus resulting in a “Digital Paradox”. On the one hand, some argue that the digital economy has a positive impact on green economic growth . Data elements, digital technology, and digital platforms are important components of the digital economy. As an efficient production factor, data elements contribute to green economic growth. Digital technology can increase the efficiency of resource utilization, and promote green economic growth . Digital platforms can innovate business models, reduce transaction costs, and achieve green growth. On the other hand, some other scholars have proposed that the digital economy requires high investment in infrastructure, and its impact also lags behind. Therefore, in the short term, there is no significant positive impact or even a significant negative impact on green economic growth .
Based on related existing researches, it can be summarized as follows. Firstly, green growth is a comprehensive concept, and different measurement methods lead to inconsistent research conclusions. Therefore, the impact of the digital economy (DIGE) on urban green total factor productivity (UGTFP) has become a new worthful research direction. Secondly, the potential impact of digitization on urban green development is diverse, the essence of digital economy’s green effect is to influence economic entities to optimize production processes, improve production efficiency, and reduce transaction costs through digitization. The difference in technological innovation capabilities, human capital structure, and economic development levels among different cities can stimulate varying degrees of “digital green driving effects”. Therefore, it is necessary to explore this effect according to heterogeneous urban factors, further test the impact mechanism and then provide a reasonable explanation on this impact according to Chinese urban samples from a suitable perspective. Thirdly, existing researches mostly focuses on the regional or industry level, with insufficient research on the urban level – , particularly, there are relatively few researches on the impact mechanism of DIGE on UGTFP , . Thus, exploring the mechanism by which DIGE affects GTFP at the urban level is of great significance.
The achievements can provide basic references for further study, and the deficiencies of present research also provide new possibilities for further study. This study focuses on the urban level, mainly considering the driving factors of urban heterogeneity innovation, aiming to explore the boundary conditions and applicable situations of digital economy’s green driving effect, test the impact mechanism of DIGE on UGTFP, and provide theoretical supplements and empirical supports for the “digital green driving effect”. Empirical research is based on panel data from 252 cities in China 2011–2019. The empirical results indicate that: (1) The development of DIGE has a significant positive impact on UGTFP, and the research results are robust through sample reduction, extreme values removing, dependent variable’s one-year lagging, and independent variable algorithms changing. (2) Urban technological innovation level (UTI) and urban human capital structure (UHC) both play the intermediary role. (3) Heterogeneity analysis indicates that the impact mechanism varies depending on the city’s region, and the city’s resource-based type. Therefore, decision-makers should formulate differentiated digital economy development strategies, while promoting green and sustainable urban economic growth by enhancing UTI and UHC.
The marginal contributions of this article are mainly as the following: (1) At the urban level, it verifies the impact of DIGE on UGTFP in the current year and one-year lagging, expanding the explanatory scope of the “digital green driving effect”. (2) From the perspective of factor inputs that affect UGTFP, using two indicators of UTI and UHC as the mediating variables, clarifies the mechanism of DIGE affecting urban’s green growth, which supplements relevant theories. (3) Heterogeneity discussion based on the city’s region and the city’s resource-based type can provide targeted suggestions for different cities green growth.

## Literature review and hypothesis

## DIGE and UGTFP
The research on the impact of DIGE on urban green growth has become a hot issue, sparking in-depth research. By reviewing relevant literatures, the impact of DIGE on urban green growth can be divided into three dimensions of factor inputting, efficiency improving, and environmental protecting. Firstly, one significant feature of DIGE is that digital factors as a new form of production factor is included in the production process . On the one hand, this expands factor sources of and optimizes factor allocation, which helps to promote urban green growth . On the other hand, digital elements themselves possess clean and low-carbon green characteristics, which can empower urban green growth. Secondly, the digital economy represents technological progress, which can reduce production and transaction costs through scale economy, scope economy, long-tail effects and knowledge spillover effects, thereby improving green production efficiency . Thirdly, the development of DIGE has promoted environmental protection level. Specifically, on the one hand, from the perspective of expected output, under the background of the digitalization, the level of resource recycling has been promoted, the overall efficiency of resource utilization has been improved, and the efficiency of urban green economy has been improved , . On the other hand, from the perspective of unexpected output, under the background of the digitalization, the intelligent transformation of traditional industries has accelerated the process of energy structure changing towards clean and low-carbon, this help to reduce the emissions of three wastes, thereby promoting urban green growth .
Although the research of DIGE on urban green growth is still in the exploratory stage, the related researches of the green effect of the Internet has shown valuable research results. Based on panel data from 285 cities in China 2005–2019, the research found that network infrastructure construction is helpful to improving urban ecological efficiency . According to the panel data of 30 provinces in China 2006–2017 and 196 cities in China 2011–2018, the article explicated that the Internet facilitate the cross-regional flow of production factors, then significantly improved the efficiency of energy utilization, and this effect has a spatial spillover character . Data from Exponential Climate Action Roadmap (2018) show that the increasing use of digital technology can dramatically increase energy efficiency, and reduce global carbon emissions by at least 15% by 2030 .
The above analysis indicates that research at the urban level still needs to be deepened. Based on the “Follow Cost Theory”, DIGE will lead to digital input increasing especially in the short term, thus inhibit the UGTFP. However, according to the “Innovation Driven Theory”, DIGE can stimulate innovation activity and technological improvement in the long run, thus helpful UGTFP. Consider that the main objective of this article is to explore the impact of DIGE on UGTFP and promote urban economy’s growth. Thus, based on the “Innovation Driven Theory”, it is believed that DIGE can promote enterprises green growth. In addition, even if there exists a negative impact, the empirical results will show a reverse support.
Thus, hypothesis 1 is proposed.
H1. DIGE has a significant positive impact on UGTFP.

## DIGE, UTI and UGTFP
DIGE not only has a direct impact on urban green growth, but also influences urban green growth by influencing technological innovation . Firstly, DIGE is characterized by the widespread use of digital technology, which provides a broader space for technological-innovation. Information and knowledge resources have driven the strategy of improving efficiency. The development of DIGE has promoted the application of digital technologies such as artificial intelligence, blockchain, cloud computing and big data, resulting in improved production efficiency and continuous optimization of new technologies, thereby promoting the green transformation. The empirical research based on panel data of 277 cities 2011–2018 found that DIGE has improved the efficiency of green economy by increasing technological-innovation .According to the system GMM and intermediary effect model test of China’s provincial data 2006–2017, the results indicated that the development of the Internet helps to decline energy consumption by improving R&D investment .
Secondly, DIGE enhances GTFP by improving technological efficiency, narrowing the technological gap, and reducing dependence on fossil fuels , , , . DIGE has constructed new ways of information and resource exchange, broken down traditional spatial barriers, improved resource utilization efficiency, promoted technological cooperation among innovative entities, maximized the effect of scale and scope economy, improved innovation efficiency, and promoted technological progress . According 284 cities data 2001–2018, the research results showed that urban innovation level has a dramatically raising with the influence of Internet .
Thus, hypothesis 2 is proposed.
H2. DIGE has a significant positive impact on UGTFP by influencing urban technological-innovation-level.

## DIGE, UHC and UGTFP
DIGE can also influence UGTFP by influencing urban human capital structure. Firstly, the digital economy has driven the emergence of new industries, new business forms, and new developing patterns. The intelligent development it brings will replace some low-quality labor and increase the demand for high-quality labor, and this provides more opportunities for high skilled labor and changing the human capital structure. The empirical results indicated that the employment-creation-effect generated from digitalization can significantly improve the employment level of cities . Based on the city data in China 2010–2018, the results of the dynamic space Durbin model indicated that human capital played an important double threshold effect in the process of digital economy affecting carbon reduction .
Secondly, the development of DIGE has led to Skill-Biased Technological Change, and further optimizing the industrial structure . This can increase high-quality employment opportunities and increase the income of medium and high-skilled labor, and thereby improving the urban human capital structure. According to the data of 45 developed and developing countries 2000–2017, GMM analyzing results showed that ICT increases wealth inequality .
Thirdly, changes of human capital structure contribute to the labor efficiency improvement and technological efficiency diffusion, thereby increasing total factor productivity. In relevant researches, many references focus on the impact of human capital structure on industrial structure upgrading and manufacturing production efficiency, and few studies analyze the human capital structure and GTFP within the same framework – . On the one hand, labor is an important input factor of GTFP. On the other hand, the human capital structure is an important element that affects production efficiency and technological efficiency. Therefore, it is essential to consider human capital in the impact of digital economy on UGTFP.
Thus, hypothesis 3 is proposed.
H3. DIGE has a significant positive impact on UGTFP by influencing urban human-capital-structure.

## Research design

## Sample selection
The samples used this research are panel data of 252 Chinese cities 2011–2019, and the reasons are as following: (1) In 2011, the American consulting firm Gartner defined digital transformation, and given that digital business transformation is the process of exploiting digital technologies and supporting capabilities to create a robust new digital business model. Thus 2011 is usually considered as the new stage in the study of digitalization. (2) From the stage division of information technology development, 2011 also marks the transition from the mobile Internet era to the digital era. Therefore, many scholars consider 2011 as the opening year of the digital economy. (3) There are many missing or unpublished data at the urban level in 2020 and beyond. The lack of data will affect the objectivity of research results, so the data until 2019 should be used as a stage. (4) In 2020, the COVID-19 broke out across the world. The occurrence of this special event will seriously affect the original trend of the relationship between the above two. Therefore, using data until 2019 as the end can eliminate the extreme impact of special events, further ensuring the objectivity of empirical analysis results.
On this basis, this study further screened and supplemented the research samples. On the one hand, remove urban samples with severe data missing. On the other hand, linear interpolation is used to complete a small amount of missing data. Thus, 2268 pieces of sample cities panel data over the period 2011–2019 are obtained.
The data using in the paper comes from China Urban Statistical Yearbook (CUSY), China Regional Statistical Yearbook (CRSY), China Statistical Yearbook (CSY), China Urban National Economic and Social Development Statistical Bulletin (CUNESD), China Environmental Yearbook (CEY), China Energy Statistical Yearbook (CESY), China Digital Economy Index White Paper (CDEIWP), as well as China Digital Inclusive Finance Index (CDIFX) jointly prepared by the Digital Finance Research Center of Peking University and Ant Financial Group.

## Variables measurement and data sources

## Dependent variable
Green economy usually refers to the developing mode that adopting the concept of coordinated development between economy and ecology, minimizing environmental damage and natural resource loss, improving input-output levels, and promoting sustainable economic development . Presently, three kinds of methods are mainly used to measure the green economy. Firstly, single indicator measurement algorithm, which usually uses GDP and CO2emissions to calculate. This has been widely applied in the early stage and the calculation is not accurate enough . Secondly, constructing a comprehensive evaluation system. This method can measure green economy more accurately, but it requires multiple indicators and the proportion of composite indicators is controversial. Thirdly, the non-parametric calculation methods for GTFP, including traditional DEA models, Super Efficiency SBM models and other related methods, which have become the mainstream calculation methods. GTFP integrates resource and environmental factors into a unified analytical framework, which coincide with the green development orientation – . Therefore, GTFP can better measure the level of green economy . The Super Efficiency SBM model is fully consider expected output, unexpected output, and multiple input factors, integrating the advantages of traditional DEA and SBM models, and thus can further make efficiency calculations for decision-making units at the forefront . Firstly, the calculation logic of SBM model is more systematic and comprehensive. At the same time, there are many academic literatures use the SBM model to measure green total factor productivity, and the measurement methods are relatively mature. In addition, the data required to calculate this indicator at the urban level can all be obtained from the city statistical yearbook. Therefore, this article uses the Super Efficiency SBM model to calculate UGTFP of 252 Chinese cities 2011–2019, and the production possibility set containing resource and non-resource input, desirable and undesirable outputs , .

## Independent variable
The independent variable of this article is urban digital economy (DIGE). Referring to relevant researches , , it can be measured from five sub-indicators of Internet popularity rate, Internet related practitioners, Internet related output, mobile phone popularity rate, and digital inclusive finance. The sub-indicators are measured by the following methods. Firstly, five specific indicators are used to measure every sub-dimension including the number of Internet users per 100 people, the employees’ proportion of information transmission, computer services and software industry, the total amount of telecommunications services, the number of mobile phone users per 100 people, and the digital inclusive financial index. Secondly, the entropy weight TOPSIS method is used to standardize the data and make dimensionality reduction, ultimately obtaining the comprehensive index of the digital economy, denoted as DIGE. The original data for the first four indicators mentioned above is gained from CUSY and CDIFX.

## Mediating variables
The previous theoretical analysis shows that urban technological-innovation-level (UTI) and urban human-capital-structure (UHC) are the most important influencing factors in the impact of DIGE on UGTFP , . Therefore, these two factors are selected as intermediary variables to make further mediating effect analysis.
Firstly, the mediating effect of UTI. It is generally believed that patents are an important indicator of innovation status. On the one hand, compared with industrial design patents and utility model patents, invention patents usually show higher degree of difficulties, higher technical requirements, and have more leading and exploratory characteristics, thus can better reflect the status of UTI. On the other hand, compared with patents under application, authorized patents measure innovation capability from the perspective of innovation results, thus more accurately reflecting the current level of UTI. Based on the above considerations, this article measures UTI by the number of authorized invention patents in that year. Relevant data are from CUSY.
Secondly, the mediating effect of UHC. Human beings are an important factor of production, and the structure of human capital not only affects production efficiency, but also affects the absorption and application of new technologies such as digitalization. Therefore, human capital structure positively affects green efficiency . The proportion of scientific researchers in the tertiary industry reflects not only the attractiveness of the city to experts, but also the speed of urban economic development, talent vitality, and overall quality. Therefore, this article uses the proportion of scientific researchers in the tertiary industry in the current year to measure the structure of urban human capital. Relevant data are from CUSY.

## Control variables
In order to eliminate the possible influence of other factors on the experimental results and more accurately display the relationship between the dependent variable and the independent variable, it is necessary to set control variables. The controlled variables should be highly relevant to the research topic and have independence and operability. Referring to relevant literatures, this article selects the following variables as control variables , , , , which are urban economic development (UED), urban environmental regulation (UER), urban financial development (UFD), urban fiscal expenditure (UFE), urban science and technology support (UTS), urban opening-up level (UOR), rate of urbanization (ROU), and urban industrial structure (UIS). The detailed measurement can be seen in Table  . Relevant data are from CUSY.

## Grouping variables
The impact of DIGE on UGTFP may vary due to the existence of urban heterogeneity characteristics. Therefore, this article makes a further heterogeneous analysis according to two most important characteristics as grouping variables. These two grouping variables are city’s region (Reg) and the city’s resource-based type (Res) , .
China has a vast territory, and there are apparent differences in resource endowments and policy environments among different regions. Therefore, making a further study on the differential digital economy’s green environmental effects in different regions is of great practical significance. The city’s regions can be divided into four categories of eastern, central, western, and northeastern. The classification is based on the economic zone classification standards released by China National Bureau of Statistics (CNBS). Among them, the 252 research samples of this article include 79 cities located in the eastern region, 76 cities located in the central region, 64 cities located in the western region, and 33 cities located in the northeast region.
Urban’s green development is closely related to their industrial structure. Whether a resource-based city reflects the industrial structure of the city, thus is also an important grouping variable that should be considered when studying the green environmental effects of urban digital economy. The city’s resource-based type means whether the city is a resource-based city, and it can be divided into two categories of yes and no. The division is based on “The Notice on the Issuance of the National Sustainable Development Plan for Resource-based Cities (2013–2020)” (NSDP), which issued by the State Council of China. Among them, the 252 research samples of this article include 102 resource-based cities and 150 non-resource-based cities.

## Results and analysis

## Baseline regression
The benchmark regression results of DIGE affecting UGTFP can be seen in Table  . The first column indicates the regression results without control variables. The second column indicates the regression results with control variables. The third column indicates the regression results with both control variables and individual fixed effects. The fourth column indicates the regression results with both control variables and two-way fixed effects, which are individual fixed effects and time fixed effects simultaneously.
As shown in every empirical result, DIGE has a significant positive impact on UGTFP. More specifically, the impact of DIGE on UGTFP is positively significant at the 5% level, showing that DIGE improves the overall environmental efficiency of cities. That is, empirical evidence reveals that DIGE has a direct and apparently positive empowering effect on UGTFP. The result indicates that although there exists the viewpoint of “Digital Paradox”, the green environmental effects of digitization is still significantly positive at the urban level. This can not only provide important practical support for the green development and digital transformation of Chinese cities, but also helps to provide some reference for promoting the digital and green coordinated development of the global economy.
The regression results also showed the influence of control variables involved in this study. Firstly, UER has a significant negative impact on UGTFP at the level of 1%. Due to the fact that UGTFP calculated in the article is short-term data, this empirical result reflects that in the short term, UER increases environmental governance costs, and then has a negative influence on UGTFP. Thus, the “follow cost” theory has been confirmed. Secondly, urban financial development, urban science and technology support, and rate of urbanization all have a significant positive impact on UGTFP at the level of 1%, which reflects the positive driving effect of finance, technology, and human capital on urban green development.
The above results indicate that H1 is positive supported.

 

## Robustness test
This research conducts robustness test through four approaches.
Firstly, robustness testing is conducted by eliminating some samples . That is, we will test whether the results are still significant by adjusting sample size. Being a provincial-level administrative unit in China, municipalities are directly managed by the Central People’s Government, and usually have better resource conditions in politics, economy, technology, culture, transportation, and other aspects than ordinary prefecture-level cities. This can also easily lead to municipalities particularity in the digitalization background, which may probably affect the stability of the regression results. Therefore, this article attempts to eliminate the influence of special administrative status on the relationship between the above two by excluding urban samples of municipalities. The empirical results can be seen in column (1) of Table  . Obviously, after eliminating the sample of municipalities, the impact of urban digital economy on UGTFP is not only significant at the 1% level, but also the impact coefficient increases. This result confirms the robustness and reliability of the research conclusions.
Secondly, robustness testing is conducted by reducing the impact of extreme values . Considering the extreme-values may have a special impact on the research results, this article uses the Winsorized method to handle all continuous variables, that is, all continuous variables were Winsorized at the 1% and 99% levels. The empirical results can be seen in column (2) of Table  . Obviously, the impact of urban digital economy on UGTFP remains significant at the 1% level, which once again confirms the robustness and reliability of the research conclusions.
Thirdly, robustness testing is conducted by setting the dependent variable one-year lagging . According to the literature review, some scholars believe that there was a lagging impact of digital economy on UGTFP. Hence, we did the regression test based on UGTFP one-year lagging. On the one hand, by the dependent variable one-year lagging, endogeneity issues can be addressed to some extent, which ensuring the stability of regression results. On the other hand, by the dependent variable one-year lagging, the lagged impact of digital transformation can be tested, which providing practical support for the rationality of the “Innovation Driven Theory”. The results can be seen in column (3) of Table  . Obviously, all variables maintain the same impact direction and trend, and pass the significance test. Thus, the research conclusions are reliable and robust.
Fourthly, robustness testing is conducted by replacing the explanatory variable calculation . The level of urban digitalization is the independent variable, and its different measurement method is an important reason for the inconsistent research conclusions in existing literatures. Therefore, this article recalculates the urban digital economy through principal component analysis and conducts regression tests again. The results can be seen in column (4) of Table  . After changing the calculation method, the influence of DIGE on UGTFP remains significant. This confirms the robustness of the results.

 

## Heterogeneity analysis

## Heterogeneity of the city’s region
As a big country, China has lots of geographical area and there exists apparently differences among different regions. Therefore, it is necessary to analyze the differentiated impacts generated by different regions where cities are located , , . According to the economic belt classification standards released by CNBS, this study divided 252 sample cities into four categories. Among them, there are 79 cities in the eastern region, 76 cities in the central region, 64 cities in the western region, and 33 cities in the northeast region. The heterogeneous regression results are shown in columns (1), (2), (3), and (4) of Table  . The data shows that DIGE has no significant impact on UGTFP in the eastern region, has a more significant impact on UGTFP in the central and western regions, has the most significant impact on UGTFP in the northeast region. The possible reason is that the green environmental effect of digitalization may exists the trend of Diminishing Marginal Utility. The eastern region has a higher level of technological development and a more optimized industrial structure, and the green environmental effect of digitalization is not significant. The implementation of the strategy of “Rise of Central China” and the “Western Development” has enabled the central and western regions to undertake many heavy industries from the eastern region , . The heavy industry emits high levels of pollutants during the production process, so digitalization has a more significant impact on energy conservation and emission reduction, and its green environmental effect is more prominent. The Northeast region is the Old Industrial Area in China dominated by heavy industry, so digitalization has the most significant positive impact on its UGTFP.

## Heterogeneity of the city’s resource-based type
As an important input factor, resource inputting is crucial in affecting UGTFP. Different cities have formed different characteristics of industrial mode and factor input due to their different resource endowments. Therefore, it is of great significance to analyze the differential impact of DIGE on whether the sample cities resource-based type. Based on “The Notice on the Issuance of the National Sustainable Development Plan for Resource-based Cities (2013–2020)” issued by the State Council of China, this research divided 252 cities into two categories of resource-based and non-resource-based. Among them, there are 102 resource-based cities and 150 non-resource-based cities. The heterogeneous regression results are shown in columns (5) and (6) of Table  . According to the results, for resource-based cities, the impact of DIGE on UGTFP is significant at a 1% level, and the coefficient of influence is much higher than that of non-resource-based cities, while the impact of DIGE on UGTFP for non-resource-based cities is still significant. Analyzing the reasons, the level of digitalization can change the resource-based industrial structure of cities, promote the transformation and upgrading of traditional economies; besides, digitalization can promote the green and high-quality development of emerging industries. Resource-based cities consume a large amount of energy, leading to greater pressure of low-carbon and green development than non-resource-based cities. Therefore, the overall impact of resource-based cities DIGE on UGTFP is more significant.

 

## Mediating mechanisms
The estimation results of Eq. ( ). and Eq. ( ). are shown in columns (3) and (4) of Table  . Obviously, both urban technological innovation level (UTI) and urban human capital structure (UHC) have a significant positive impact on UGTFP. This shows that, on the one hand, UTI measured by the number of authorized invention patents is an important factor in improving UGTFP; on the other hand, UHC calculated by the rate of scientific researchers in the tertiary industry is an important factor in enhancing UGTFP.
There are differences in the impact of some control variables. In the impact of UTI on UGTFP, UED, ROU, and UIS are all positively impact on UGTFP, while UFE are negatively impact on UGTFP. The empirical results show certain rationalities. Firstly, the level of UTI may increase the resource utilization efficiency and production efficiency, thereby stimulating or amplifying the empowering effect of economic growth, foreign investment utilization, and industrial structure on UGTFP. The level of UTI may also “squeeze out” the urban budget expenditures, leading to the financing constraints on UGTFP in short term.
In the impact of UHC on UGTFP, both UED and UIS have a significant positive impact on UGTFP, while UER’s negative impact on UGTFP is no longer significant. Therefore, on the one hand, the optimization of UHC can amplify the active empowering effect of economic development and industrial structure on UGTFP. On the other hand, UHC can weaken the negative effect of environmental regulations on current UGTFP.
According to the theory and method of testing the mediating effect, UTI and UHC probably play the mediating role in this process of DIGE impacting UGTFP. To verifies the mediating effect, models (6) and (7) were further tested, and the results are shown in columns (5) and (6) of Table  .

 

The regression results indicate that after considering UTI and UHC in the benchmark regression model, DIGE still has a significant positive impact on UGTFP. That is, both UTI and UHC exhibit a mediating role in the process of DIGE affecting UGTFP.
This further indicates that in the short term, DIGE has both “technology innovation compensation effect” and “human capital optimization effect”, which in turn helps to improve UGTFP. This result expands the explanation scope of relevant theories. Meanwhile, the research results clarify the impact mechanism of DIGE on UGTFP, providing theoretical basis and practical supports for regional innovation and green development.

## Conclusions and limitations

## Conclusions and policy implications
The digitalization has become an important opportunity for Chinese economy’s green and high-quality growth. Based on panel data from 252 cities in China 2011–2020, this article uses the Super Effect SBM model to calculate UGTFP, establishes a two-way fixed effects model to examine the impact of DIGE on UGTFP, utilizes the mediation effect model to clarify the impact mechanism of DIGE on UGTFP, and further explores the heterogeneity caused by urban region difference and urban resource-based type. Then, the following research conclusions are drawn.
Firstly, DIGE has significantly improved UGTFP, and has become an effective impetus in promoting high-quality development in China in the new era. Furthermore, by eliminating some samples, reducing extreme values, setting the dependent variable one-year lagging and replacing the explanatory variable calculation, the research conclusions remain robust and reliable. At present, researches on UGTFP mainly focus on industrial structure adjustment, industrial agglomeration effects and environmental policy regulation , , . The conclusions of this article are valuable supplements to the above literature, not only enriching the literature of urban UGTFP, but also providing new explanations for UGTFP in the era of the digitalization.
Secondly, the positive impact of DIGE on UGTFP shows regional heterogeneity and urban resource-based type heterogeneity. DIGE has a greater impact on UGTFP in undeveloped areas such as the central, western, and northeastern regions. DIGE has a greater impact on UGTFP for resource-based cities. The research findings provide guidance for formulating different digital economy policies.
Thirdly, UTI and UHC play the mediating role in the impact of DIGE on UGTFP. Existing researches mainly analyzed the impact mechanism from the perspective of industrial structure . Considering the factors influenced UGTFP, this article focuses on the input factors, selects two mediating variables of UTI and UHC, and analyzes their mediating effects. This expands the exploration of the impact mechanism of DIGE on UGTFP.
The policy implications are as following.
Firstly, the urban government should actively propose policies to improve DIGE, unleashing greater dividends of digitalization and sparking social creativity. Policy supports is the most important factor in promoting the development of DIGE. The digital economy represents a new economic form and growth mode. There are significant differences between digital economy and traditional economy in terms of infrastructure, technology investment, talent demand, production methods, etc. All these various resources should have the overall effective arrangement. Therefore, the government should have the overall planning, give an overall arrangement, and promote the coordinated development of DIGE. Meanwhile, the government should strengthen the evaluation of the development status and adjust the policies according to its changing. In addition, the government policies should focus on both the short-term and long-term aspects, promoting sustainable development of DIGE. In summary, DIGE can significantly improve UGTFP, so the local government can completely seize the dividend period of digitalization and fully release the positive role of the digital economy in improving UGTFP .
Secondly, both Chinese and other countries’ government should implement a more flexible digital economy strategy according to local conditions. As a big country, China has lots of cities, and there are differences both in the development foundation and resource endowment. Therefore, cities should implement targeted digital economy policies based on their different regional characteristics and resource endowment conditions . Specifically, for resource-based cities and cities located in the central, western, and northeastern China that have undertaken the industrial transfer should vigorously promote urban digital economy growth by strengthening the digital infrastructure construction. For cities located in the eastern China and cities non-resource-based, optimizing the digital platform is the core to achieve urban sustainable developing with greater quality and efficiency. At the same time, the government should fully take into the effect of macro-control, grasp the opportunity of digitalization, actively promote cooperation and positive interaction among regions, and achieve regional green growth.
Thirdly, governments around the world should continuously focus on strengthening UTI and UHC, and try to improve the level of urban digital economy ultimately , . The government can fully utilize the driving role of UTI and UHC in the process of DIGE influencing UGTFP, achieving synergy development in economy and ecology, and fostering new driving force of economic growth simultaneously. On the one hand, the government should further attach great importance to the role of technological innovation, take into the positive role of digitalization in reducing transaction costs, improving production efficiency, and promoting knowledge spillovers, further enhancing UTI, and finally promote UGTFP. On the other hand, the government should try to stimulate the potential role of UHC by implementing attractive talent policies, building a scientific personnel training system, and related effective measures to improve UHC, and increase UGTFP.

## Limitations and future research
This research draws some valuable conclusions, but some limitations remain.
Firstly, this article mainly explores the effect of DIGE on UGTFP based on the two-way fixed effects model. However, there may exist a bilateral dynamic relationship between DIGE and UGTFP, which has not been explored in this article. Therefore, future researchers can further discuss it.
Secondly, the environmental effects generated by the development of DIGE have the spatial spillover characteristics. Digitalization is flourishing globally, which brings new possibilities for green growth. Although this article explores the impact and mechanism of DIGE on UGTFP, it didn’t discuss the spatial spillover impact. Future researchers can further explore the spatial spillover effects.
Thirdly, the research conclusions in this article are all gained according to Chinese listed companies’ data 2011–2019, the relations between DIGE and UGTFP should be further test by samples around the world. Thus, subsequent researchers can further expand the sample sources and verify the applicability of our research conclusions.
