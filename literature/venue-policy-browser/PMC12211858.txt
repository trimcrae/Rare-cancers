## Background
With the acceleration of the global aging process, Subjective Well-being (SWB) of the elderly which focuses on their inner true feelings and reflects the meaning and happiness of their lives, has drawn extensive attention from all walks of life. However, due to factors like the flow of the labor force, the miniaturization and flattening of family structures, the traditional function of family care has been continuously weakened, and it’s hard for families to take on the all-round care for the elderly.

## Methods
This study utilized the micro-panel data from the China Longitudinal Aging Social Survey (CLASS) in 2018 and 2020, covering a sample of 8,179 elderly people aged over 60 in China. The fixed effects model was employed for the benchmark regression. The two-stage least squares method (2SLS) was used to overcome the endogeneity problem. Replacing variables and samples as well as the propensity score matching (PSM) was adopted for the robustness test, and the mediating effect model was utilized for the mechanism analysis.

## Results
The findings of this study are as follows: (1) Community nursing increases the SWB of the elderly by 0.101 standard deviations. (2) Mechanism analysis demonstrates that community nursing can improve the SWB of the elderly by means of health improvement (0.029/0.003/0.006), social participation (0.014), and social identity (0.018/0.012). (3) Heterogeneity analysis discloses that for different types of elderly groups, community nursing plays a more crucial role in enhancing the SWB of vulnerable elderly individuals with low incomes (0.145, p < 0.01), low educational attainment (0.092, p < 0.01), living in rural areas (0.447, p < 0.01), and not living with their children (0.296, p < 0.01).

## Conclusion
This study has confirmed the crucial role of community nursing in enhancing the SWB of the elderly. Moreover, this positive influence has a multi-dimensional impact mechanism and shows characteristics of group differences. Based on this, Special training programs for community nurses can be carried out, with a focus on enhancing their capabilities in elderly health assessment and planning, humanistic qualities, as well as communication and coordination abilities. Community institutions can implement a stratified nursing strategy, designing customized interventions for vulnerable elderly groups.

## Introduction
Against the backdrop of accelerating global population aging, the subjective well-being (SWB) of the elderly has attracted increasing attention. As an overall cognitive and emotional judgment of individuals about their living conditions, SWB not only reflects the objective material living conditions of individuals but also people’s sense of gain, happiness, and pursuit of a good life. Despite its significance, the weakening of the traditional family-based elderly care model and the shortage of institutional elderly care resources pose significant challenges to improving the SWB of the elderly in many countries. Consequently, the elderly increasingly confront socioeconomic vulnerabilities, including financial insecurity, inadequate daily care, emotional isolation, and deteriorating mental health marked by rising depression rates and suicidal ideation. Moreover, the cumulative effect of factors such as declining physical functions has limited their mobility, making the community the primary setting for daily activities. Given these circumstances, community nursing, as a critical bridge connecting family and social services, offers a path for improving the SWB of the elderly through diverse services such as health management, emotional support and social participation. Therefore, a comprehensive evaluation of the impact of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly is imperative. Specifically, does community nursing help to improve the SWB of the elderly? Through which mechanisms does it operate, and which subgroups of the elderly benefit most? This study is designed to provide a scientific basis for promoting the development of community nursing by answering those questions, thereby helping the elderly lead a fulfilling and dignified life in an aging society.
SWB refers to an individual’s cognitive and emotional evaluation of their own life and is an important dimension reflecting the quality of life [ , ]. Since Easterlin put forward the famous “happiness paradox”, research on the influencing factors of the SWB of the elderly has gradually expanded from wealth or income [ , ], to individual or family factors such as health status, psychological resilience, self-esteem, religious beliefs, Internet usage, neighborhood relations, family size, children’s structure, intergenerational support, and living arrangements [ – ]. Compared with other groups, the most obvious characteristics of the elderly group are relatively high age, poor health conditions, and a relatively small range of daily activities. For most elderly people, the community where they live is the scope of their daily activities. Therefore, the community environment is particularly important and is regarded as one of the key factors affecting the welfare of the elderly [ , ]. In recent years, with the increasing attention paid to the community environment in the field of public economy, scholars’ research focus on the determinants of the SWB of the elderly has gradually shifted from the aforementioned internal personal and family factors to external community factors. For example, Xu et al. found that community social capital has a positive impact on the SWB of the elderly [ ]. An age-friendly community environment is conducive to enhancing the elderly’s self-perception of environmental safety, improving their self-care ability, reducing their dependence on others in daily activities, and effectively improving their quality of life [ , ]. Moreover, the improvement in safety and comfort will further reduce the risk of depression and enhance the SWB of the elderly [ , ]. In terms of community participation, participating in community activities such as music, art, culture, social, and sports clubs has a positive effect on the SWB of the elderly [ , ]. However, there are two aspects in the existing research that need to be further expanded. First, although some scholars have studied the community environmental factors that affect the SWB of the elderly, there is a relative lack of research on the causal relationship between community nursing and the SWB of the elderly using large sample micro-data. Second, research on the mechanism of how community nursing affect the SWB of the elderly is relatively lacking. Most of the relevant literature mainly focuses on directly verifying the influence relationship between variables, while there are fewer studies that conduct theoretical associations and mechanism tests from a logical perspective.
China’s unique eldercare context makes community nursing a critical policy instrument in addressing the problem of population aging. By the end of 2024, China’s population aged 60 and above reached 310 million (22% of the total population), including 220 million individuals aged 65 and over (15.6%), according to data from the National Bureau of Statistics. Remarkably, China transitioned from an aging society to a moderately aged society in just 25 years, a process that took 50–100 years in developed countries such as France and Germany. This unprecedented demographic shift has placed enormous pressure on the healthcare system. Meanwhile, influenced by the strong cultural tradition of filial piety, an essential part of Confucianism, the majority of Chinese elderly prefer home-based care. However, the One-Child Policy has led to smaller family sizes and weakened traditional family support systems, resulting in 49.7% of elderly households being classified as empty-nest families in 2024 (Ministry of Civil Affairs). Within this complex context, community nursing demonstrates unique adaptive advantages. Institutionally, it optimizes healthcare resource allocation through hierarchical medical systems, alleviating hospital bed shortages and improving health service utilization. Professionally trained nurses provide essential services including emergency response and chronic disease management, effectively reducing hospitalization rates. Socially, community nursing satisfies elders’ environmental attachment needs while addressing care challenges faced by one-child families through shared service models. In recent years, policy documents such as the National Nursing Undertaking Development Plan (2016–2020), the Medium- and Long-Term Plan for the Prevention and Control of Chronic Diseases in China (2017–2025), and the Technical Specifications for the Health Management of the Elderly, which have been successively introduced in China, all propose to vigorously develop community health services to meet the strong demand of the elderly for community nursing services. This offers a valuable research opportunity to investigate the impact of community nursing interventions on elderly SWB.
This paper employs the data from the China Longitudinal Aging Social Survey (CLASS) in 2018 and 2020 to conduct an empirical analysis on the impact of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly and its mechanism. This paper may have the following marginal contributions: In terms of empirical data, for the first time, it carries out an empirical analysis on the impact of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly based on the CLASS data, which is an authoritative micro-survey database of the elderly at the national level in China, thereby providing new empirical evidence for relevant research. In terms of the empirical strategy, multiple causal identification methods are adopted to relieve potential endogeneity problems as much as possible for conducting empirical analysis, such as the instrumental variable method and the propensity score matching method, so as to accurately identify the causal relationship between community nursing and the SWB of the elderly. In terms of mechanism analysis, the mechanism of how community nursing affect SWB is analyzed and tested from the aspects of the health of the elderly, social participation, and identity recognition, which enriches the discussion on the mechanism in existing research.
The structure of the remaining part of this paper is organized as follows: In the second section, a theoretical analysis is carried out and research hypotheses are put forward. The third section details the research design of this paper, covering data, variables, and econometric models. The fourth section presents the empirical results. The fifth section conducts the mechanism test. The sixth section focuses on the heterogeneity analysis. Finally, the discussion, limitations, and conclusion are provided.

## Theoretical analysis and research hypotheses
The present study analyzes the causal relationship between community nursing and the SWB of the elderly based on the social support theory. Social support refers to various kinds of help and necessary resources that people obtain from others in the social relationship network. According to the structural level, the social support structure can be regarded as a nested system of “inner layer—middle layer—outer layer”. Specifically, the inner layer represents the support from intimate relationships, the middle layer refers to the support from social networks, and the outer layer indicates the support provided by the community [ ]. Currently, the elderly in China generally face the dilemma of insufficient inner-layer social support and middle-layer social support for their elderly care needs. As a specific form of community support in the field of nursing, community nursing can serve as an effective supplement to these two types of social support. In terms of the inner—layer social support, along with the shrinking of family size in China, the separation of intergenerational living, and the flow of the labor force, family resources for elderly care have become relatively scarce. The traditional way of providing elderly care support solely relying on children is no longer sustainable. Against the backdrop of the continuous weakening of the guarantee function of the traditional family-based elderly care mentioned above, the elderly group is faced with difficulties such as the lack of care. According to the social support theory, community nursing can provide services such as health monitoring, rehabilitation guidance, disease prevention for family caregivers, better balance the complementary relationship between formal care and informal care received by the elderly, and thus better meet the elderly care needs of the elderly so as to improve their SWB. Regarding the middle—layer social support, as the elderly age, their physical and cognitive functions gradually decline, and their contact with members of the social network gradually decreases. The combination of these factors results in a narrowing of the elderly’s travel range, and consequently, the community has become the main venue for their daily activities. Thanks to its remarkable advantages in terms of distance and cost, community nursing provides a guarantee for the elderly to obtain nursing services at a relatively low cost without having to leave the community. According to the community care theory, community nursing can enable the elderly to gain the greatest degree of autonomy and take control of their own lives, thereby meeting the elderly care needs of the elderly and further enhancing their SWB.
Hypothesis 1: Community nursing can enhance the SWB of the elderly.
Health is of vital importance to the SWB of the elderly [ ]. As physical functions gradually decline with age, most elderly people regard health as the greatest happiness [ ]. Community nursing is an important health resource source for improving the health level of the elderly. On the one hand, community nursing provides support for the elderly to maintain their physical health. For instance, regular health monitoring can help the elderly detect potential health problems in time. Through health lectures, community nurses or other professionals can disseminate health knowledge to the elderly, such as knowledge of nutrition, disease prevention and mental health. On the other hand, community nursing can encourage the elderly to exercise to improve their health. With the help of community monitoring and feedback, the elderly can have a clearer understanding of their own physical conditions, thus forming regular exercise routines to improve their disease resistance. Meanwhile, many communities also set up incentives to motivate the elderly to exercise, and the elderly will also take the initiative to increase healthy activities. These behavioral changes directly promote the improvement of the health level of the elderly.
Hypothesis 2: Community nursing can enhance the SWB of the elderly through health improvement.
Promoting the social participation of the elderly is an important mechanism through which community nursing exerts a positive impact on SWB. Social participation refers to non-isolated activities that are carried out in the social environment and are connected with others, which has a significant positive impact on the SWB of the elderly [ ]. This positive effect is mainly achieved through the following mechanisms. Firstly, the social role compensation mechanism. The participation of the elderly in social activities can bridge the negative emotions caused by the loss of roles, and then help them obtain a clearer self-awareness and group identity, thus effectively enhancing their SWB [ ]. Secondly, the social network expansion mechanism. The improvement of the degree of social participation helps the elderly maintain their social networks and increase social support, and then meet the needs for emotional care [ ]. According to the community support theory, community nursing plays a positive role in promoting the social participation of the elderly. Specifically, the elderly can obtain material support, emotional support and information support from community nursing, which helps the elderly become more independent, improves their social adaptation and encourages them to carry out social activities with a more positive and autonomous attitude, thereby improving their level of social participation and having a significant promoting effect on the SWB of the elderly.
Hypothesis 3: Community nursing can enhance the SWB of the elderly through social participation.
Community nursing is capable of enhancing the SWB of the elderly through social identity. The social identity refers to the process by which individuals or groups evaluate and classify themselves or others based on the groups to which they or others belong. Since the social identity can influence the attitudes, emotions and behaviors of individuals or groups, it can stimulate the positive emotions and behaviors of the elderly, promote their self-actualization and self-development, and thus enhance their SWB. Community nursing can not only benefit individual elderly people but also help create a good social atmosphere of “respecting and caring for the elderly”. Thus, by exerting the positive effect of social identity, it can provide strong support for improving the SWB of the elderly. Firstly, Community nursing itself reflects the respect and care of society for the elderly, which directly enhances the social status and sense of social value of the elderly and makes them feel the social identity and respect. Secondly, the respect and care of society for the elderly group are conducive to building extensive social trust and have a positive impact on the happiness of the elderly [ ]. Finally, the community environment of “respecting and caring for the elderly” created by Community nursing can enhance the sense of community dependence among the elderly and help them form a healthy lifestyle [ ].

## Research design

## Sample and data sources
The micro data employed in this article is sourced from the China Longitudinal Aging Social Survey (CLASS). CLASS represents a large-scale national social survey project on the elderly, jointly designed and implemented by the Institute of Gerontology at Renmin University of China. This project adopts a meticulously designed stratified multi-stage probability sampling method, covering over 30 provinces, more than 400 village-level units, and over 11,000 elderly individuals aged 60 and above, thereby ensuring excellent national representativeness of the data. The CLASS questionnaire encompasses a wealth of information regarding the well-being of the elderly, as well as personal and family circumstances, providing sufficient samples and appropriate indicators for the research on aging issues in China. The latest data from the two waves in 2018 and 2020 have been selected as the empirical analysis samples for this article.

## Variable specification

## The dependent variable
The dependent variable in this article is the subjective well-being (SWB) of the elderly. In light of the characteristics of the CLASS data and drawing on the common methods in existing research [ , ], this article employs life satisfaction as the measurement indicator of the SWB of the elderly. The reasons are as follows: First, life satisfaction serves as a core dimension of SWB, directly reflecting individuals’ holistic evaluation of their quality of life. This aligns with the essence of SWB, which emphasizes personal self-perception rather than external objective criteria. Second, within collectivist cultural contexts such as China, older adults tend to express their well-being through relatively implicit indicators like life satisfaction, rather than directly discussing emotional experiences (e.g., happiness or depression). Using life satisfaction as a measure respects this cultural nuance while still capturing meaningful evaluations of well-being. Third, single-dimensional measures like life satisfaction demonstrate strong validity and reliability in assessing SWB [ ]. In the CLASS questionnaire, the question about life satisfaction is “Overall, are you satisfied with your current life?” and the corresponding answers are: very dissatisfied, relatively dissatisfied, average, relatively satisfied, and very satisfied. In this article, the aforementioned responses are successively assigned values ranging from 1 to 5, where a higher value indicates a better state of the elderly’s SWB.

## Key independent variable
The key independent variable in this article is community nursing. In the CLASS, the interviewed elderly are successively asked whether they have used a series of community nursing, including home nursing care, house calls, rehabilitation training, rental of rehabilitation aids, free physical examinations, establishment of health records, and health lectures. If the interviewed elderly use community nursing, assign the value of 1 to the core explanatory variable; otherwise, assign the value of 0 to it.

## Control variables
To mitigate the endogeneity problem caused by omitted variables, it is necessary to further control other factors that might affect the SWB of the elderly. Various covariates that affect the SWB of the elderly are included in this study, including (a) demographic characteristics including gender, age, marital status, educational attainment; (b) health characteristics including number of chronic diseases and activities of daily living (ADLs) [1]; (c) economic characteristics including personal income, whether enrolled in health pension insurance, and the residence area; (d) family characteristics including number of family members living with the interviewee and whether living with sons or daughters.

## Discussion on endogeneity
The conclusions drawn from the benchmark regression may be interfered by two types of endogeneity issues, namely reverse causality and omitted variables. Firstly, the issue of reverse causality. Compared with the elderly who enjoy relatively good health and do not require care from others, those elderly individuals with limited abilities in daily activities and in need of elderly care support from others have a stronger motivation to utilize community nursing. Moreover, due to their relatively fragile physical and mental health conditions, their SWB will also be negatively affected [ ]. In such a situation, the positive impact of the utilization of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly may be underestimated, yet it will not affect the direction of the estimation. Secondly, the issue of omitted variables. Although this paper incorporate the characteristic variables that influence the SWB of the elderly and has also included year fixed effects and individual fixed effects, there may still be unmeasurable omitted factors that could simultaneously affect the utilization of community nursing and the SWB of the elderly.
To reduce the endogeneity problems that may be caused by reverse causality and omitted variables, this paper further adopts the instrumental variable method for estimation. Drawing on the practices of relevant studies [ ], this paper selects the proportion of the use of community nursing in the entire district or county sample, excluding the sample itself, within the same district or county as the instrumental variable. Because within the same district or county, factors such as economic conditions and living environments are similar, which meets the relevance constraint of the instrumental variable. Meanwhile, the instrumental variable also needs to meet the excludability constraint, that is, the instrumental variable is not correlated with the disturbance terms that affect the explained variable. From a theoretical perspective, the instrumental variable selected in this paper is the proportion of the use of community nursing in the same district or county and cannot affect the SWB of an individual elderly person through channels other than the endogenous explanatory variable. Columns (1) and (2) in Table  reports the regression results of the instrumental variable method. The estimated coefficient of the key independent variable is consistent with the direction of the benchmark regression, which supports the causal relationship that community nursing can enhance the SWB of the elderly. In summary, the above instrumental variable method enhances the internal validity of the effect estimation, indicating that the core findings of benchmark regression are reliable. 

## Robustness test

## Robustness test for substituting variables
Selecting inappropriate variable measurement methods may affect the estimation results. Therefore, the dependent variable (SWB) is replaced to test the robustness of the benchmark regression results. The dependent variable in the benchmark regression adopts “SWB intensity”. In this section, the dependent variable is replaced by “whether to feel satisfied” (1 = very satisfied or relatively satisfied, 0 = average, relatively dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied). Unlike the intensity-based measure, this binary variable captures the impact effect in a simpler yet more intuitive way, which is crucial for re-evaluating the impact of community nursing on the SWB from a different angle and ensuring that our benchmark regression results are not influenced by the specific measurement approach of the variables. The substitution described above allows us to re-estimate the impact of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly, offering a more comprehensive and reliable validation of the benchmark regression results. Column (1) in Table  provides the regression results of replacing the variables, showing that community nursing has a significant positive impact on the SWB of the elderly. Based on the above test results, it can be concluded that the benchmark regression results of this paper are robust. 

## Robustness test for replacing sample
This paper further screens out the interviewed elderly who have a demand for community nursing as samples according to their responses in the CLASS regarding whether they need such nursing services, and then conducts the regression anew. The internal logic behind this is that the interviewed elderly who are insensitive to whether the community provides community nursing may inherently possess a higher level of SWB. For this group of elderly people, their higher SWB level may not stem from the community nursing. Consequently, including this group of elderly people in the sample might overestimate the effect of community nursing. By contrast, if only the interviewed elderly who have a demand for community nursing are taken as samples for regression analysis and the estimated coefficient of the key independent variable remains significant, it can better prove the positive impact of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly. As shown in column (2) of Table  , the coefficient of community nursing is significantly positive at the 5% level, which is consistent with the results of the benchmark regression.

## Robustness test for sample selectivity bias
The use of community nursing by the elderly may be the result of self-selection, and their utilization of community nursing does not conform to random sampling. In this section, a method combining propensity score matching (PSM) model and fixed effects (FE) model is further adopted for the robustness test. Specifically, this section first divides the survey sample involving the elderly into two categories: the elderly who use community nursing and the elderly who do not. The interviewed elderly who use community nursing are set as the treatment group, and the remaining elderly who do not use community nursing are set as the control group. Second, the control variables including the demographic characteristics, health characteristics, economic characteristics, and family characteristics are incorporated into the PSM model. Finally, after a 1:1 nearest neighbor matching is conducted based on the propensity score values, an individual fixed effects regression is carried out to re-estimate the community nursing on the SWB of the elderly.
Figure  presents the balance test results obtained after PSM matching. It can be seen that after matching, the standardized differences of most control variables are reduced, and the absolute values of the standard deviations of all variables are less than 5%. The above test results indicate that the characteristics of the treatment group and the control group are similar. Further, it can be found from Fig.  that after matching, the propensity score distribution curves of the treatment group and the control group are closer to each other. In summary, the PSM model has enhanced the comparability and provides good samples for studying the causal relationship between community nursing and the SWB of the elderly. 
Column (3) in Table  provides the regression results based on the samples after the above nearest neighbor matching, showing that the effect of community nursing on improving the SWB of the elderly remains significant. This indicates that the benchmark regression results are robust and are not driven by systematic differences in observable individual characteristics.

## Mechanism test
The previous results of the benchmark regression and a series of robustness tests have confirmed that community nursing can improve the SWB of the elderly. But by what means is this effect achieved? This demands a more profound examination of its impact mechanism. Section 2 has already led to the theoretical hypothesis that community nursing can enhance the SWB of the elderly through improving the health conditions, promoting the social participation, and enhancing the social identity effect. This section will verify whether these hypotheses hold true.

## Mechanism test based on health improvement
Since self-rated health is a comprehensive indicator for measuring physical health conditions and has good reliability and validity [ ], this paper uses self-rated health to measure the physical health conditions of the elderly. Specifically, in the CLASS, the item asking about self-rated health is “How do you think your current physical health condition is?” The response options include “very unhealthy”, “relatively unhealthy”, “average”, “relatively healthy”, and “very healthy”. The above options are assigned values from 1 to 5 in turn, indicating that the health condition ranges from very unhealthy to very healthy. In addition to health conditions, this paper further examines whether community nursing has an impact on the physical exercise of the elderly, mainly focusing on two aspects: the duration of a single physical exercise session (1 = more than half an hour on average each time, 0 = otherwise); the frequency of physical exercise (1 = more than once a month on average, 0 = otherwise). It should be noted that due to the lack of relevant data, this part only uses the data from 2020 as samples for regression analysis, and all regressions incorporate regional fixed effects at the community level.
The regression results in columns (2), (4), and (6) of Table  show that community nursing significantly improves the self-rated health of the elderly and increases their exercise frequency and exercise duration. After further incorporating the mediating variables into the regression equation, as shown in columns (3), (5), and (7) of Table  , the estimated coefficients of the mediator variables are both significant at the 1% statistical level. Moreover, the regression coefficient of community nursing has decreased compared with that in column (1), indicating that the mediation effect test is passed. In summary, community nursing significantly enhances the SWB of the elderly by improving their health conditions, which verifies Hypothesis 2. The above research findings hold significant guiding implications for nursing education. In the curriculum system of nursing majors, the courses related to the health management of the elderly should be strengthened. Through methods such as case-based teaching and simulated practice, nursing students can gain an in-depth understanding of the operation mechanism of community nursing interventions in enhancing the elderly’s health and promoting their physical exercise. In practice, community nursing institutions can implement proactive health promotion initiatives such as health education workshops to empower the elderly to take an active role in health management. 

## Mechanism test based on social participation
In the CLASS, the social participation includes activities such as community security patrols, taking care of other elderly people, environmental sanitation protection and mediating disputes. For each activity that the interviewed elderly participate in, it is counted as 1, and then these numbers can be added up to obtain the intensity of social participation.
Table  reports the regression results of the social participation mechanism. The results in columns (2) show that community nursing significantly promotes the social participation of the elderly. The regression results in columns (3) indicate that social participation can effectively improve the elderly people’s SWB. Overall, community nursing significantly improves the SWB of the elderly by promoting their social participation, thus verifying Hypothesis 3. The above research findings hold significant practical implications. Community nursing curricula should, through project-based learning, integrate content that promotes social engagement among the elderly, equipping nursing students with the ability to plan and carry out activities aimed at enhancing the elderly’s social participation. In addition, specialized communication skills training programs should also be developed to provide specialized training to teach community nurses how to effectively guide seniors in social interactions. Community service organizations can regularly organize social activities and assign nurses to provide follow-up services, thereby stimulating the elderly’s enthusiasm for social participation. 

## Mechanism test based on social identity
This paper selects two indicators, namely the social atmosphere of “respecting the elderly” and the subjective social perception of the elderly, to test the impact effect of social identity. Specifically, the paper uses the “respecting the elderly” atmosphere perceived by the elderly to directly measure the social atmosphere of “respecting the elderly”. The corresponding item in the CLASS is “How satisfied are you with the atmosphere of respecting the elderly in your community (village or neighborhood)?” The response options are “very dissatisfied (=1)”, “relatively dissatisfied (=2)”, “average (=3)”, “relatively satisfied (=4)”, and “very satisfied (=5)”. The item corresponding to the subjective social perception of the elderly is “Do you think the current social changes are increasingly unfavorable to the elderly?” The response options include “completely in line (=1)”, “relatively in line (=2)”, “average (=3)”, “relatively not in line (=4)”, and “completely not in line (=5)”.
Table  reports the regression results of testing social identity. Firstly, it can be seen from columns (2) and (3) that community nursing significantly improves the social atmosphere of “respecting the elderly” perceived by the elderly. This indicates that community nursing not only directly benefits the elderly themselves but also strengthens the respect for them throughout the whole society. Then, as shown in columns (4) and (5), under a good “respecting the elderly” atmosphere, the elderly are more adaptable and optimistic about current and future social changes, which helps the whole society jointly deepen the concepts of “active aging” and “healthy aging”. In summary, community nursing significantly enhances the SWB of the elderly by improving their social identity, thus verifying Hypothesis 4. The findings suggest that efforts should be made to improve the communication skills of community nurses, guiding the elderly to view social changes correctly. Community nursing institutions can incorporate the creation of a social atmosphere of respecting the elderly into their service objectives. They can popularize the importance of respecting and caring for the elderly among community residents by regularly carrying out community publicity activities and other means. 

## Heterogeneity analysis
To further examine the differences in the impact of community nursing on the SWB of different types of elderly people, this paper selects variables such as the gender, age, and marriage of the elderly and uses a strict interaction term form for heterogeneity testing. As shown in Table  , community nursing has a more significant impact on the SWB of disadvantaged elderly people such as those with low income, low education level, living in rural areas, and living alone. Specifically, first, for elderly people with low income, community nursing can reduce the economic burden and enable limited funds to be used for other necessary expenses. Moreover, free health check-up services provided by community nursing can also help them maintain good health under low economic pressure, thereby enhancing their SWB. Second, elderly people with a lower education level can benefit more from community nursing. This may be because services like health lectures within community nursing can help them acquire more knowledge and guide them to develop good living habits, thus improving their SWB. Third, for elderly people in rural areas, due to the relatively scarce economic and medical services in their regions, community nursing can make up for the lack of basic services and thus have a more significant impact on their SWB. Fourth, community nursing has a greater impact on the elderly living alone. This may be because community nursing can compensate for the lack of family care for the elderly living alone and increase their social interaction to overcome loneliness, thereby effectively enhancing their SWB. Findings from the heterogeneity analysis indicate that nursing curriculum design should integrate differentiated content for various elderly groups. For instance, specialized course modules should be established to cultivate nurses’ capacity to formulate cost-effective nursing plans for low-income seniors. Community nursing institutions can tailor precise nursing service strategies for stratified and classified care based on these analysis results. For example, training programs should be set up to teach community nurses how to popularize health knowledge in an easily understandable way to less-educated elders. 

## Discussion
Based on the micro-data of the elderly from the CLASS in 2018 and 2020, this study investigates the effects of community nursing on the SWB of the elderly. We obtained empirical evidence indicating that community nursing can significantly enhance the SWB of the elderly. After a series of tests, including the Two-Stage Least Squares (2SLS) method, variable and sample replacement, as well as propensity score matching, this result remains reliable and valid. The core findings of this paper are somewhat in line with previous studies at the community level. Those studies have revealed that community-based elderly care services, community elderly care facilities, and community age-friendly renovations and the like have a positive impact on the SWB of the elderly [ , ]. Nevertheless, the distinguishing feature is that few previous studies have focused on the welfare effects of community nursing. In addition to filling the research gap, the core findings of this paper also hold practical significance for aging countries represented by China. Through this study, the strategy of addressing the issue of aging by increasing resource investment in community nursing to compensate for the lack of family nursing functions has obtained empirical support. At the policy level, policy makers can increase resource input in the field of community nursing. At the community level, several measures are advisable. First, more medical equipment should be allocated. Second, efforts should be made to recruit and train specialized geriatric nurses. These actions can jointly enable the elderly to access nursing services more conveniently.
Based on the mechanism analysis, this study has uncovered three mediating effects of community nursing in enhancing the SWB of the elderly: health improvement, social participation, and social identity. First, in terms of health improvement, this study found that community nursing can improve the elderly’s self-rated health status and increase the frequency and intensity of their exercise. This provides reliable empirical evidence for the views of many scholars. For example, Dai et al. believe that regular screening and health monitoring of the elderly based on the community can detect and prevent the further development and deterioration of diseases, which is an effective way to prevent and control diseases [ ]. Feng and Chen hold that in daily life, poor health-related lifestyles and behaviors are important incentives for the occurrence and development of various diseases. Community nurses can use their professional knowledge to help the elderly change these bad behaviors and lifestyles and guide them to conduct self-health management [ ]. Second, regarding social participation, community nursing can promote the elderly’s participation in social activities such as community patrols and environmental protection. The satisfaction of social needs helps relieve the elderly’s sense of loneliness, enhance their sense of belonging, and thus improve their SWB [ , ]. Beauchet et al. have found that group-based intervention measures are more conducive to expanding the social circle and maintaining the physical and mental health of the elderly [ ]. Therefore, as the main place where the elderly live, it is necessary for the community to organize relevant activities to provide safe and effective ways for the elderly to socialize and promote their social participation [ ]. Previous studies on the impact of the community environment on the elderly’s social participation have seldom involved community nursing. However, this paper’s empirical analysis confirms its promoting role in this regard. Third, as for social identity, community nursing improves the social atmosphere of “respecting the elderly” and enhances the elderly’s adaptation to society. The need for respect is one of the five-level needs in Maslow’s need theory. Existing studies have pointed out that improving the community environment can meet the elderly’s need for respect [ ]. As an embodiment of society’s respect and care for the elderly, community nursing can improve the community atmosphere, enhance the social status of the elderly, and promote the improvement of the SWB of the elderly [ ]. Based on the mediating effects mentioned above, an integrated “health management + social promotion” service model could be designed to maximize the effectiveness of community nursing. For example, incorporating interactive sessions into health lectures or organizing health-related group activities can serve as practical ways to facilitate social interaction among the elderly, thereby establishing a practical link between health improvement and social participation.
The heterogeneity analysis shows that there are differences in the impact of community nursing on the SWB of different types of the elderly. Specifically, community nursing has a more pronounced effect on vulnerable elderly groups, including those with low incomes, low educational attainment, living in rural areas, and those living alone. Vulnerable elderly groups generally have greater care needs [ ]. They are in urgent need of social support to compensate for insufficient care. The absence of social support reduces positive emotional experiences among these elderly people and impairs their mental health, leading to issues such as depression, loneliness, and social isolation [ ]. Some scholars have put forward the suggestion that community-level care services should be offered to vulnerable elderly groups [ , ]. The results of our heterogeneity analysis lend support to these academic viewpoints. For the elderly with low incomes, low educational backgrounds, living in rural areas, or living alone, community nurses should offer more targeted services. For instance, simple health management programs can be designed for rural elderly, and regular home visits and social activities can be organized for the elderly living alone. This approach further strengthens the role of community nursing in enhancing the SWB of vulnerable groups. Furthermore, exploring the “Internet + community nursing” model holds great potential. Online platforms can be utilized to popularize health knowledge, arrange nursing service appointments, and conduct remote health monitoring. By doing so, the constraints of time and space can be broke through, significantly improving the efficiency and accessibility of community nursing.
This study also has beneficial implications for enhancing the competences of community nurses. In China, the nurse education system encompasses both academic education and vocational training. In terms of academic education, it is categorized into secondary vocational, junior college, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels. According to data from the National Health Commission, by the end of 2023, more than 80% of nurses in China held a junior college degree or higher. This indicates a substantial improvement in the educational structure of nurses in China. Regarding training, the “Training Outline for Newly Recruited Nurses (Trial)” issued in 2016 stipulates that newly recruited nurses in China must undergo standardized training. This training covers 27 clinical operation skills, as well as laws, regulations, and communication skills and so on. With the accelerating aging process, community nursing in China is evolving towards specialization. Currently, some universities in China have established the specialty of community nursing. Its curriculum system centers around basic nursing knowledge and practical skills, covering subjects such as physiology, fundamentals of nursing, and clinical nursing skills. According to the “Healthy China 2030” Plan Outline, the competency requirements for nurses in community nursing differ from those in hospital settings. Community nursing places greater emphasis on health assessment, promotion of health education, and coordination of resources. In the academic community, scholars propose that the core competencies of community nurses should include nursing practice skills, critical thinking, communication and coordination, professional development, legal and ethical practice, and management skills [ ]. Since the elderly are the primary service recipients of community nurses in China [ ], some scholars have specifically defined the core competencies of community nurses for elderly care. These include the ability to assess and plan for elderly health, provide nursing practice, offer educational guidance, improve service quality, engage in interpersonal communication, pursue professional development, adhere to legal and ethical standards, and demonstrate humanistic qualities [ ]. A survey by Cao et al. on the core competencies of elderly care among 738 community nurses in Shanghai found that their overall ability was at a medium level [ ]. This indicates that although Chinese community nurses possess certain practical skills in elderly care, there remains significant room for improvement.
The research findings of this paper emphasize the importance of enhancing three core competencies of community nurses. First, improving the ability to assess and plan for elderly health, such as conducting health assessments, providing disease prevention guidance, and managing chronic diseases, can directly improve the health status of the elderly. Second, enhancing the humanistic qualities of nurses, such as providing psychological counseling and emotional support, can help the elderly develop a sense of belonging and identity in the community, strengthening the positive impact of the social recognition mechanism on SWB. Third, enhancing communication and coordination competencies of nurses, such as organizing community activities and coordinating social resources, can encourage the elderly to participate in social activities, increasing the frequency and depth of social interaction and realizing the promoting effect of social participation on SWB.

## Limitations
This study may have the following limitations. First, the data were collected from the Chinese mainland. Given that the Chinese mainland has a vast territory and a unique political, economic, cultural, and social background, certain aspects such as differences in healthcare systems, social welfare policies, and cultural attitudes towards aging may influence the research findings. As a result, the research conclusions based on the data from the Chinese mainland may not be directly generalizable to other countries and regions. Second, cross-sectional data only reflect the situation at a specific point in time and are unable to capture the impacts that may occur over time. This limitation restricts the ability to understand the dynamic changes and long-term effects, thus limiting the universality of the research conclusions across different time periods.

## Conclusion
This study explored the causal relationship between community nursing and the SWB of the elderly. It was found that community nursing can have a positive impact on the SWB of the elderly through the mediating mechanisms of health improvement, social participation, and social identity. Moreover, this positive impact is more pronounced among vulnerable elderly people with low incomes, low educational attainment, living in rural areas, and living alone. The findings suggest that special training programs for community nurses can be carried out, with a focus on enhancing their capabilities in elderly health assessment and planning, humanistic qualities, as well as communication and coordination abilities. This is to facilitate the channels through which community nursing affects the SWB of the elderly, enabling community nursing to exert a greater positive effect. Community institutions can implement a stratified nursing strategy, designing customized interventions for vulnerable elderly groups. For example, community institutions can provide services such as free basic health check-ups and lectures on disease prevention knowledge for the elderly with low incomes, low educational levels, and those living in rural areas. For the elderly who live alone, are disabled, or suffer from dementia, establish exclusive health records and arrange regular home nursing services, including daily care and rehabilitation nursing. In this way, while better bringing into play the SWB enhancement effect of community nursing, the gap in welfare levels among the elderly groups can be narrowed. In the future, long-term longitudinal studies could be conducted, and due consideration should be given to the disparities in community nursing models under diverse cultural backgrounds and regional characteristics. This approach will serve as a foundation for the continuous refinement of community nursing.
