## AIDS 2012: 19TH INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE AT WASHINGTON, DC
AIDS, due to miserable fate of its patients and its socioeconomic influences, is a major world health issue. Countable efforts are being taken to expand the fight against AIDS worldwide. On the same line, Geneva-based International AIDS Society in partnership with a number of international bodies and local partners is intended to organize the conference AIDS 2012. The International AIDS Conference is the premier gathering for policy makers, those working in the field, and individuals committed to ending the pandemic. The conference programme will consist of abstract-driven sessions featuring the latest HIV research; non-abstract-driven sessions (with invited speakers) focusing on the latest issues in HIV science, policy, and practice; and workshops that will develop professional skills and knowledge. It will also include programme activities, including the global village (open to the public) and the youth programme. For details about schedule, registration, accommodation, transportation, and scholarships, please visit www.aids2012.org

## GLOBAL AYURVEDA CONGRESS TORONTO, CANADA
Late Dr. Ramesh N. Modi in 1988 started ‘Ayurvedic LifeStyles Inc’ (ALI) in Canada, which is now a well-recognized organization. ALI and National US INDIA Chamber of Commerce along with their partners have organized a ‘Global Ayurveda Congress’ from 8th to 10th June 2012 in Toronto, Canada. The venue for the event is International Centre, 6900 Airport Road, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada L4V 1E8. For more details, please visit www.globalayurvedacongress.com

## SEMINAR ON ‘HERBAL PRODUCTS: REGULATORY ASPECTS’ AT MUMBAI
The Dept of Clinical Pharmacology, TNMC & BYL Nair Ch. Hospital, Mumbai are conducting a 2-day Seminar on “Herbal Products: Regulatory Aspects” on 29th and 30th June 2012. The objective is to review the current regulations on herbal products, and to update health care professionals/stakeholders involved in herbal research/industry with respect to the regulatory requirements for evaluating the safety, efficacy and quality of herbal products. This interactive seminar will aid in facilitating better understanding of the current regulations pertinent to herbal medicines/products. The seminar is open to the researchers working in academia, pharmaceutical and clinical research industry.
The seminar will give emphasis on regulations to launch herbal product (Traditional Medicine/Food/Health Supplement/Raw material), regulatory requirements regarding quality of herbal products, regulatory requirements for safety data documentation/generation of herbal products, recent notifications related to herbo-mineral formulations in India, claim substantiation; regulatory requirements, AYUSH GCP and IPR issues related to herbal products.
