Unwillingly to School: The Place of the Child Guidance 
Clinic in the Treatment of School Phobia 
By J H Kahn, Consultant in Charge of the Community Mental Health Service and 
Medical Director of the Child Guidance Clinic, West Ham, and Jean P Nursten, 
Psychiatric Social Worker, West Riding County Council Child Guidance Service 
School refusal is a clinical problem of the individual; it is a problem of inter- 
personal relationships for the family; an educational problem of the school; and, 
a social problem of the community. 
This book will be of interest to all those concerned with the diagnosis and 
treatment of the specific problem posed by those children who refuse to go to 
school, and it is written for each of the professional workers drawn into the 
situation: general practitioners, school medical officers, teachers, education 
welfare officers, children's officers, probation officers and magistrates, as well as 
the staff of child guidance clinics and child psychiatry in-patient units.
