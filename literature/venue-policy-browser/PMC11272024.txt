## Academy of Family Physicians of India Foundation Day: National Family Medicine Day (NDFM) 27th September
During the general body meeting of AFPI organised at the 5th National Conference of Family Medicine and Primary Care (FMPC), Hyderabad, it was decided to observe 27 September as Foundation Day and National Family Medicine Day (NFMD) in India. It is celebrated to mark the distinction of family medicine and celebrate the contribution of family physicians within India’s health system. Members of AFPI organise health camps, workshops, CMEs, meetings, social events, social media campaigns, videos, patient education events, community engagement events, meeting political leaders and politicians, and so on.

## Purpose and Significance

Raising Awareness: The day aims to increase public awareness about the role and benefits of family medicine and physicians. It highlights the comprehensive, continuous, patient-centered care family physicians provide.
Recognising Contributions: NFMD celebrates the dedication and hard work of family physicians who play a crucial role in managing the health of individuals and communities. It acknowledges their efforts to provide holistic care, manage chronic conditions, and emphasise preventive health.
Advocacy and Policy: The day is a platform to advocate for better support and resources for family medicine. It encourages policymakers and healthcare leaders to invest in primary care infrastructure and education.

## Activities and Celebrations
AFPI members are encouraged to display their creativity and professional work to disseminate the importance of family medicine.

Educational Events: Workshops, seminars, and conferences are organised to discuss recent advancements, challenges, and innovations in family medicine. These events provide family physicians with continuing medical education (CME) opportunities.
Community Outreach: Health camps, free medical check-ups, and public health awareness campaigns are conducted to engage with the community. Family physicians and healthcare organisations participate in activities that promote preventive care and healthy lifestyles.
Recognition and Awards: Awards and honours are given to outstanding family physicians and practitioners for their exceptional service and contributions to the field. Testimonials and success stories of family physicians are shared to inspire and motivate others.
Media and Social Media Campaigns: Media campaigns, including articles, interviews, and social media posts, are used to spread awareness about the importance of family medicine. Hashtags and online events engage a broader audience and create a collective voice supporting family medicine.

## NFMD 2022: Launch
The first NFMD was launched in 2022 at the India International Centre, Lodhi Road, New Delhi. [ ]. The NFMD was launched in the presence of dignitaries such as Prof. Surekha Kishore, Executive Director, AIIMS Gorakhpur, Member, National Medical Commission (NMC); Shri Alok Saxena, Additional Secretary, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Director General, Central Government Health Scheme, Director General, National AIDS Control Organization (NACO); Prof. Suneela Garg, Chair Program Advisory Committee National Institute Of Health and Family Welfare (NIHFW), National President OMAG, National President IAPSM (2021), Professor of Excellence, Ex Head (CM) Sub Dean, MAMC and Head Community Medicine, FMS New Delhi; and Dr. Sanghmitra Pati, Scientist G and Director, ICMR Regional Medical Research Center, Bhubaneshwar, Department of Health Research, MOHFW [Images and ]. The event was simultaneously observed as AFPI Foundation Day. AFPI organised a “National Consultation on Undergraduate Family Medicine Education” during the launch event. Experts from all over India from different domains of medical education were invited to deliberate on the thematic session [ ]. The consultation concluded that a distinct and mandatory Department of Family Medicine must implement the MBBS curriculum in its true spirit. The event received widespread media coverage [Images and ].
 

## AFPI Foundation Day Oration Award
The first AFPI Foundation Day oration was delivered by Prof. Shiv Kumar Sarin, Chancellor, Institute of Liver and Biliary Sciences (ILBS), New Delhi and President, National Academy of Medical Sciences. In his talk, Prof. Sarin emphasised that family physicians play a pivotal role in preventing disease and their complications [Images and ]. The second Foundation Day oration was delivered at the inaugural state conference of AFPI Rajasthan by the author, Dr. Raman Kumar, as Founder and National President of the Academy of Family Physicians of India, in Jaipur. Dr. Kumar discussed the recent updates on the status of family medicine in India in his oration [Images and ].
 

## Impact and Future Directions
NFMD strengthens the identity and recognition of family medicine as a critical medical specialty. It fosters a sense of community and solidarity among family physicians. The day encourages medical students and young doctors to consider a career in family medicine by showcasing its impact and rewards. Highlighting role models and career opportunities can attract more talent to the field. Policy and Healthcare System Integration: Ongoing advocacy efforts on this day can lead to improved policies and integration of family medicine into the broader healthcare system. It can drive initiatives that enhance access to primary care, especially in underserved areas. NFMD is a significant event that underscores the essential role of family physicians in providing accessible, comprehensive, and continuous healthcare. By celebrating this day, the healthcare community and the public can work together to support and advance family medicine, ultimately leading to better health outcomes.
