## Abstract
As the Philippines celebrated the 2nd Asia Laboratory Animal Day (ALAD) last November 29, 2025, the Philippine Association for Laboratory Animal Sciences (PALAS) reflected on a year marked by growth, collaboration, and renewed commitment to ethical and scientific excellence in animal‐based research. Throughout 2025, PALAS has continued to strengthen the laboratory animal science community by advancing education, capacity building, policy advocacy, and regional cooperation. These efforts underscore PALAS’ vital role in shaping a culture of responsible animal research aligned with international standards.
Keywords: animal welfare and ethics, laboratory animal practice, laboratory animal science

1.

## STRENGTHENING PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION AND CAPACITY BUILDING
A central pillar of PALAS’ work in 2025 has been the expansion of education and training programs for students, researchers, veterinarians, and animal care personnel. In response to the evolving scientific landscape, PALAS delivered a wide range of lectures, workshops, and continuing professional development activities covering animal welfare, experimental design, biostatistics, biosafety, and ethics, in partnership with different institutions and organizations.
A highlight of the year was the sustained focus on training programs designed for researchers, laboratory technicians, and institutional staff involved in animal‐based studies. These programs addressed core competencies such as proper animal handling, anesthesia and analgesia, humane endpoints, and recordkeeping. Additionally, training modules on ethics review processes, particularly the roles and responsibilities of Institutional Animal Care and Use Committees (IACUC), were emphasized to strengthen institutional compliance and ethical oversight during the Pre‐Conference Workshop of PALAS.
PALAS further supported early‐career scientists and students by engaging universities and training facilities in collaborative seminars and mentoring opportunities. This effort aimed to develop foundational knowledge in laboratory animal science while inspiring future professionals to value both scientific rigor and animal welfare. By bridging academic learning with practical application, PALAS helped ensure that the next generation of researchers is well‐equipped to meet international expectations.

2.

## THE PALAS 2025 CONFERENCE: A PLATFORM FOR INNOVATION AND DIALOGUE
One of the most anticipated events of the year was the PALAS Annual Conference 2025 with the theme “Beyond the Human Body – Understanding Diseases through Translational Animal Research,” which served as a meeting point for researchers, veterinarians, educators, and regulators from across the country. The conference focused on emerging trends in laboratory animal science for elucidating human diseases, and the continuous integration of the 3Rs: Replacement, Reduction, and Refinement, into research practices.
Key discussions addressed innovations in animal care, alternatives to animal testing, welfare assessment tools, and quality assurance systems in research facilities. The conference also highlighted local research studies demonstrating responsible use of animals in biomedical and veterinary investigations, reinforcing the importance of good science supported by sound welfare practices. In addition, the conference provided a venue for networking among institutions and organizations that share a common mission of advancing research standards. Panel discussions allowed participants to explore real‐world challenges, including resource constraints, regulatory harmonization, and biosafety, offering practical insights and collaborative solutions.
The PALAS conference reaffirmed the organization's role as a national leader in laboratory animal science. By bringing together diverse stakeholders, the conference strengthened professional relationships and laid the foundation for multi‐institutional cooperation in research and education.

## INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERSHIPS AND REGIONAL ENGAGEMENT
In 2025, PALAS expanded its partnerships with academic institutions, research organizations, and allied professional groups. These collaborations resulted in shared training activities, knowledge exchange programs, and collective advocacy for ethical research practices. Importantly, PALAS continues to build strong regional ties with other laboratory animal science societies and organizations in Asia. Through active participation in the Asian Federation of Laboratory Animal Science Associations (AFLAS), PALAS has aligned Philippine practices with international benchmarks, contributing to regional discussions on animal welfare, policy development, and scientific advancement.
Partnerships with regulatory bodies and educational institutions also strengthened PALAS's influence in promoting standardized protocols and ethical review procedures nationwide. , , By fostering dialogue between researchers and policymakers, PALAS helps ensure that local regulations evolve in harmony with international best practices.
Furthermore, industry collaborations have supported the acquisition of equipment, access to training resources, and the dissemination of scientific literature. These partnerships enable PALAS members to remain updated on technological advancements and methodological innovations essential to modern laboratory science.

4.

## PREPARING FOR ASIAN LABORATORY ANIMAL DAY 2025
Asia Laboratory Animal Day provides an opportunity to recognize the contributions of laboratory animals to scientific advancement, while reaffirming commitment to humane care and responsible research practices. For PALAS, ALAD 2025 is not merely a commemorative event but a platform for advocacy, education, and public awareness.
Leading up to November 29, 2025, PALAS activities emphasized public engagement by raising awareness of the ethical frameworks guiding laboratory animal use. Educational campaigns highlighted the significance of animal welfare, the value of veterinary professionals, and the scientific benefits of ethical research. This outreach aimed to promote transparency, address misconceptions, and foster public trust in the scientific community.
To celebrate ALAD 2025, PALAS did a web‐quest activity entitled “Guardians of Humane Science: ALAD Mission” (https://www.facebook.com/photo?fbid=1334212278400130&set=a.541303797690986). This web‐quest enabled participants to read more about the AFLAS, PALAS, pertinent laws and regulations about laboratory animal science in the Philippines, as well as different activities involving laboratory animal use. This activity was envisioned to bring together researchers, students, administrators, and partners in a shared commitment to ethical excellence and collaboration.

5.

## LOOKING FORWARD AND BEYOND
As PALAS looks beyond ALAD 2025, the organization remains focused on sustainability and progress. Plans are underway to expand training programs, strengthen accreditation systems, and encourage research innovation grounded in humane science. Continued partnerships at both national and regional levels will be essential in achieving these goals. Through its dedication to education, collaboration, and ethical leadership, PALAS has proven itself as a driving force behind the advancement of laboratory animal science in the Philippines. The accomplishments of 2025 demonstrate that meaningful change arises from shared commitment and cooperation.
Asia Laboratory Animal Day 2025 thus stands not only as a milestone but also as a reflection of the collective efforts of the PALAS community. It is a reminder that scientific progress and animal welfare go hand in hand, and that through partnership, integrity, and excellence, the future of laboratory animal science in the Philippines is promising.
