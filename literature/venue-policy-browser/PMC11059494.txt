## Introduction
The inauguration of the Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality section signifies a pivotal moment, as it highlights both the journey thus far and the envisaged path forward for this scholarly initiative. Heliyon distinguishes itself through rigorous scientific scrutiny and an expansive interdisciplinary focus as an open-access publication. The journal's venture into the dynamic tourism and hospitality research field broadens its scholarly purview, underscoring a commitment to advancing research that could influence industry practices and societal conventions.
Central to Heliyon's esteemed reputation is its unwavering commitment to research integrity [ ], upheld by a stringent peer-review process, an open-access model for global dissemination of findings, and a focus on fostering innovation and cross-disciplinary cooperation. The journal values scientific accuracy and methodological rigor, prioritizing these over mere novelty while remaining open to new ideas and approaches. Such a strategy positions Heliyon as a foremost venue for global tourism and hospitality research community contributions.

2.

## The vision and mission for tourism and hospitality section
The launch of the Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality section recognizes the significant influence of this domain on worldwide economic, societal, cultural, and environmental realms. Its mission is to publish scholarly works that deepen understanding of the tourism and hospitality industry, demanding commitment to academic rigor, innovation, and collaboration. Strict criteria for research methodologies, data analysis, and theoretical foundation are enforced to ensure that studies expand the scholarly body of knowledge and contribute novel insights.
This section prioritizes advanced research methodologies and theoretical frameworks that challenge existing paradigms and explore novel aspects of the field. This approach positions Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality at the forefront of academic investigations in the sector. Additionally, fostering interdisciplinary collaboration between researchers, industry practitioners, and stakeholders guarantees that its outputs are academically robust and have practical relevance, tackling the concrete challenges faced by the tourism and hospitality industries.
Per its foundational values of quality, innovation, and collaboration, Heliyon aims to transcend the traditional limits of academic publishing. It seeks to become a pivotal resource and dynamic platform fostering progress in tourism and hospitality. This dedication to fostering an ecosystem characterized by excellence, innovation, and collaborative inquiry establishes Heliyon as a premier venue for scholars committed to making substantial contributions to the tourism and hospitality field.
Moreover, the tourism and hospitality industry is acknowledged for its significant economic contribution and role in environmental conservation, cultural preservation, and social inclusivity. The Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality section captures this field's intricate and varied nature, addressing many themes. These include sustainable practices, technological advancements, destination management, climate change mitigation strategies, health tourism, and tourism and the economic impacts of tourism and hospitality.
The goal is to cultivate an academic environment where rigorous research strengthens theoretical frameworks and yields actionable insights for policy formulation, industry implementation, and community engagement. By incorporating diverse methodological approaches, ranging from empirical studies to theoretical analyses, and prioritizing a balance between quality, innovation, and collaborative research, Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality seeks to advance the boundaries of existing knowledge and tackle the evolving trends and challenges in the tourism and hospitality field.

3.

## Who is welcome to submit?
The Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality section seeks submissions from a wide range of contributors with expertise in tourism, hospitality management, leisure studies, and tourism economics, among other pertinent areas. This call extends to faculty members and researchers who contribute significantly to these domains' theoretical development. Postdoctoral fellows and graduate students are also encouraged to submit their research, either under the guidance of senior faculty or as lead authors, bringing fresh perspectives and methodologies to the field. Professionals from the hospitality industry, including those working in hotels, resorts, restaurants, and travel agencies, are invited to contribute insights based on their extensive industry experience, focusing on practical applications and case studies that bridge theory with real-world practices. Consultants specializing in tourism and hospitality consulting are prompted to submit articles that discuss industry best practices, current challenges, and data-driven analyses, providing valuable information for practitioners and academics. Independent scholars engaging in tourism and hospitality research outside of conventional academic environments are also welcome, as their unique viewpoints and specialized research areas enrich the diversity and depth of knowledge within tourism and hospitality studies.
Interdisciplinary collaboration, merging academics with a focus on tourism theory, industry professionals with empirical experience, and potentially scholars from ancillary disciplines like psychology or sociology, is set to deliver comprehensive insights to augment the field's knowledge base. Such collaborative efforts are likely to produce research that reinforces the theoretical foundation of tourism and hospitality and yields significant practical advantages for sector stakeholders.

## A recipe for a structured and readable paper
The architecture of an academic manuscript is fundamental in efficiently communicating research findings, bolstering your thesis, and enhancing the likelihood of publication. An effectively structured paper ensures a clear and systematic journey through the research narrative, maintaining a focused discourse and minimizing diversions. This organization guarantees a cohesive integration of the manuscript's components—literature review, methodology, results, and discussion—thereby strengthening the central thesis. Sequential coherence among sections fosters a robust and convincing conclusion. A logical arrangement underscores the research's importance and lends a professional tone to the manuscript, favorably influencing reviewers and editors. Additionally, a well-organized submission facilitates the review process by simplifying the evaluation of the manuscript's contributions.
Crafting an influential academic manuscript requires careful attention to its organizational structure, given that its structure significantly influences its acceptance and effectiveness in disseminating knowledge. Guidance draws on the scholarly work of Reuber & Sharma [ ], Belcher [ ], Creswell & Creswell [ ], Kotzé [ ], and Delias et al. [ ], outlining the essential elements and structure of a research paper in the Tourism and Hospitality fields. Please also consult Heliyon's guidance on the types of articles considered for publication [ ].
Precision and clarity in language are crucial in manuscript preparation, requiring adherence to academic norms and field-specific terminology. Authors should rigorously proofread their texts to eliminate inaccuracies and promote transparency. For non-native English speakers, consultation with native speakers or professional editing services is advisable to achieve linguistic quality. Observance of the journal's language and formatting guidelines is essential to avoid submission setbacks or rejections. Diligent language and comprehensive proofreading are vital for improving the manuscript's academic integrity, readability, and likelihood of acceptance.
The careful composition of each segment, from a compelling title and concise abstract to a thorough literature review, transparent methodology, integrated results, discussion, and a summarizing conclusion that projects future research avenues, facilitates clear communication of the research, significantly enhancing publication prospects. Linguistic precision and adherence to academic and formatting standards are critical to augmenting the manuscript's credibility, improving its readability, enriching scholarly dialogue, and easing its acceptance and dissemination within academic and professional circles.

5.

## Conclusion
The launch of the Heliyon Tourism and Hospitality section marks a significant moment for impacting research in tourism and hospitality. This initiative welcomes contributions from scholars, practitioners, and policymakers worldwide, seeking to cultivate diverse scholarly articles that address present challenges and anticipate future trends in the tourism and hospitality industries.
Guided by the Section Editor and Associate Editors, we uphold the utmost academic integrity and excellence standards, advocating for an interdisciplinary methodology to foster a vibrant scholarly milieu. Our objective is to compile research that profoundly explores the intricate influence of tourism and hospitality on global economies, social structures, and environmental practices.
The initiation of this section signifies significant advancement in scholarly dialogue, providing an open-access venue for disseminating substantial research. We welcome scholarly, methodologically sound contributions of practical relevance, aiming for our collective research to deliver insightful and beneficial outcomes for the worldwide tourism and hospitality community. Embarking on this innovative path, our goal is to foster and achieve a more sustainable and prosperous future for global tourism and hospitality.
