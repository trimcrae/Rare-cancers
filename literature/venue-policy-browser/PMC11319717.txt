However, judging from the actual development situation at present, the sports industries of China is generally still in the primary stage, and lags behind developed countries, thus playing a minor role in the national economic landscape. Despite government initiatives proposing relevant laws and regulations, it still lacks the guidance of concept for low-carbon industry development. Meanwhile, in the process of the development of sports industry in China, there is the phenomenon of unreasonable allocation of market resources. For example, the internal structure of sports industry is unreasonable, and the proportion of talent allocation is low. Hence, the development of China’s sports industry is still excessively depended on the extensive mode, which is characterized by high energy consumption, high pollution, high emissions and low output value , such as the lower green production in the manufacturing process of sports equipment, and the high carbon emissions of sports buildings. Otherwise, due to the differences in resource conditions, economic development level and other factors, the development level of sports industry in different regions of China is uneven, which would have a poor effect on the coordinated development of low-carbon sports industry in whole China. Considering the aforementioned condition, the development of sports industry in the China’s various regions will not only be limited by the continuously increasing cost, but also will pose a threat to the local ecology.
As the document from the state council general office, named “Opinions on promoting the national fitness and sports consumption to promote high-quality development of sports industry” , issued that the sports industry can not only take new impetus to the economic growth, but also play a vital role in driving the development of other industries, such as increasing the social employment, optimizing the industrial structure, and promoting the transformation of economy . In view of this, accelerating the low-carbon transformation of sports industry in China can not only achieve the sustainable industry development, but also add new momentum to promote the sustainable growth of national economy and open up new economic growth points. Meanwhile, there is still obstacle in promoting the coordinated development of the low-carbon transformation for the sports industry in China because of the development gap among regions. Hence, it is more urgent to formulate targeted polices to accelerate the low-carbon development of sports industry in China’s various regions and promote the coordinated development for low-carbon sports industry between regions. As entering the period of the 14th five-year plan period, China’s ecological civilization construction has also entered the key period for focusing on the synergy of pollution reduction and carbon reduction, and promoting low-carbon transformation of sports industry . However, it is worth noting that more targeted policymaking requires not only full consideration of previous experience, but also a precise assessment of future trends . Therefore, how to provide the accurate evaluation results and the high-precision prediction results will play an important role in this study. The main aim of this study is to address the following questions: (1) To forecast data corresponding to each index of various regions in the China under small sample, how can the prediction performance of classical grey prediction model (GM (1,1)) be further improved by a series of optimal techniques? (2) To achieve the comprehensive and accurate evaluation, how to choose a proper assessment model tailored to take into account the unexpected output. (3) To promote the coordinated development of low-carbon sports industry among regions in China, how to accurately reflect the spatial correlation characteristics of development efficiency for low-carbon sports industry among regions.

## Literature review

## Data forecasting
The more accurate predicted results could identify the future trends, while playing a pivotal role in the data forecasting . In the recently stated researches, many scholars made full use of related methods for data prediction. For instance, the univariate autoregressive was applied for data prediction of electrical consumption in Lebanon ; Some scholars used evolutionary algorithm to forecast the demand for electricity in the Australia, Taiwan and Spain, respectively – ; Other researchers proposed that time series techniques could also be applied for data prediction , . However, incomplete information provided by small sample sizes can influence the prediction accuracy of these methods. Hence, the grey prediction model (GM) was firstly proposed by Deng to forecast data while the sample was small. Thereinto, the classical GM (1,1) model which could provide accurate short-term predictions was widely applied to forecast CO2 emissions , . And some scholars proposed out that increasing the smoothness of raw sequence could mitigate the disturbance caused by external factors. For example, the variable buffer operators are introduced to weaken and strengthen the intensity of the change in data to make data smooth , . Further, Wang and Dang pointed that buffering operators with invariant weight could not realize slight adjustment. Also, Wang stated that the latest data should be given more weight according to the principle of new information first. At the same time, He and Pei has made full use of accumulative transformation and translation transformation to increase smooth of raw sequence. It indicates the feasibility of combination of evolutionary algorithms and translational optimization to increase prediction accuracy.

## Regional disparity analysis
Due to various factors such as the resource endowment and geographical location, the different types of disparity between regions in China has been significant for a long time . In view of this, how to identify the regional disparity, explore the spatial interaction, thereby formulating the differentiated policies, is growing awareness of governments. The spatial econometric model could be used to explore whether there is spatial dependence between those research objects, namely the spatial autocorrelation and spatial heterogeneity . One of the most important features for the spatial econometrics, which is different from traditional econometrics, is that the spatial factors are fully considered in the research of economic data . And, the analysis of spatial effects of data is emphasized, which could make up for the spillover effects of economic activities on geographic space neglected by traditional economics . In the current, the application scope of spatial econometrics has expanded to technology development, energy development and economic development. The spatial effects mainly include the spatial dependence and the spatial heterogeneity . Thereinto, spatial dependence indicates that the observed values of research objects within a range are similar to some extent, and those values would affect the observed values of their neighboring research objects. Due to the influence of many factors in the geographical space, the observed objects could show spatial heterogeneity, namely spatial heterogeneity, which is usually manifested as the omission of relevant variables or the error of model setting, or the difference of variables, parameters and variances due to different spatial positions. Hence, the exploratory spatial–temporal data analysis method, which mainly takes the panel data combined with time and space as the research object, could reflect the spatial distribution law among the objects studied, and deeply explore the distribution pattern, thereby providing the theoretical reference for the differentiated policy formulation .

## Indicators selection
According to the aforementioned analysis, the index system should be considered comprehensively based on the total factors from two perspectives of input and output. Meanwhile, the three principles of representativeness, data accessibility and objectivity should also be introduced in the establishment of index system. From two perspectives of production input and industrial output, the index system consists of seven subindexes. Both production input and industrial output are expanded from three aspects: ecology, economy and autonomy. Production input expressed only by the capital investment and manpower resource would fail to reflect input substitution and autonomy, which could be more important bases for sports industry to achieve low-carbon development. Hence, the investment of capital stock and manpower resource should be further segmented to research & development (R&D) investment, technological import cost, amounts of the sports institutions and full time equivalent of R&D personnel. The output should not only include economic benefits, but also involves pollution emissions. Hence, based on some literatures, the output of sports industry should be divided into two parts, namely the expected output and undesired output. Thereinto, the expected outputs could consist of the operating income and added values. ## Methods

## Efficiency evaluation methods
Data envelopment analysis (DEA) is originally introduced by the CCR model proposed by the researchers Charnes, Cooper and Rhodes (CCR), which has assumed constant returns to scale (CRS) and evaluates the relative efficiency of decision-making units (DMUs) by constructing efficiency frontiers . Subsequently, the researchers Banker, Charnes, and Cooper (BCC) proposed the BCC model, which assumes variable returns to scale (VRS), enabling DEA methods to deal with scale effects problems . As the application range of DEA continues to expand, researchers have proposed many improved and extended models to meet different practical needs. For example, Super-Efficiency DEA model proposed by Andersen and Petersen, which is used to distinguish DMUs with an efficiency score of 1, so as to achieve finer sequencing . At the same time, to analyze the specific improvement direction of a specific target after efficiency evaluation, the Directional Distance Function (DDF) is proposed by Chambers, Chung and Fare, which fully considers the changing direction of input and output, and uses the direction vector to guide the adjustment, making the efficiency analysis result more closed to the actual situation . Taking into account the undesirable output, the non-radial and non-angular DEA model, namely Slacks-based Measurement (SBM), has been proposed by Tone, which includes relaxation variables of input and output, making the efficiency measurement more accurate . Then, Zhou et al. . has evaluated the efficiency of collaborative governance for CO2 and haze in China based on the super-efficiency SBM model, further verifying the applicability of this model. In 2007, Färe et al. has found that many actual production and service systems are multi-stage, with internal structure and intermediate products, and proposed a network DEA model to decompose the multi-stage production process into multiple interrelated stages or nodes, and achieve a separate efficiency assessment of each stage or node, thus providing a dual perspective of the global and local. Based on the previous research experience, Liu et al. has found that the use of different technologies would cause efficiency gaps, and the timeliness of decision-making could be increased through dynamic timing, thus introducing the Meta-Frontier Window Network DDF DEA model by scientifically combining with DDF function, meta-frontier analysis, window analysis and network DEA . In general, the Meta-Frontier Window Network DDF DEA model proposed by scholar Liu et al. is more suitable for analyzing complex and dynamic efficiency evaluation scenarios. Considering the homogeneity of decision-making units such as input–output in the process of low-carbon transformation of the sports industry in various provinces in China, as well as the inevitable undesirable outputs such as carbon emissions generated in the process, this study would select the super-SBM model to evaluate the efficiency based on the full reference of scholars' research experience. ## Conclusions and policy implication

## Conclusions
According to the aforementioned empirical results, the main conclusions of this study are as follows: (1) The optimal VBO-GM (1,1) model has the highest accuracy by comparing the prediction performance among these prediction models. (2) The curves of development efficiency for 30 regions will have a significantly increasing trend from 2022 to 2025. (3) The curves of development efficiency for low-carbon sports industry have presented an increasing tendency, but the proportion of regions with values greater than 1 have been relatively low from 2006 to 2020. (4) The development efficiencies of low-carbon sports industry for 30 regions are generally the H–H clustering feature and L-L clustering feature during the China’s three five-year plan periods. And, the regional dissonance will become serious in the coming years.

## Policy implication
The recommended reference could be provided for the more targeted policies formulation. (1) Adhering to the green-innovation-oriented development mode to accelerate the low-carbon transformation of the sports industry in the China’s each region. (2) Promote the comprehensive reform for the allocation of resource factors in the sports industry to accelerate the flow of green and high-quality resource factors, thereby realizing the structure optimization and upgrading for the sports industry. (3) Constructing a new pattern of coordinated development between regions, thus gradually narrowing the gap of development efficiency for low-carbon sports industry.
