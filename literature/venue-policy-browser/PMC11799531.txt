The rapid development of digitalization provides new opportunities for the improvement of manufacturing industry’s green total factor productivity (IGTFP). However, there exists two different perspectives of “digital promotion effect” and “digital inhibition effect” presently, which causes “digital paradox”. That is, digitalization may have a non-linear effect on IGTFP. Therefore, this study focuses on the industry level and aims to test the threshold impact of digitalization on IGTFP. Furthermore, this study tries to explore the boundary conditions and applicability of the green driving effect of digitalization on sub-industries from the perspective of heterogeneous technological innovation abilities in different industries. The empirical study is based on the panel data of 29 manufacturing industries in China 2012–2019. The empirical results show that: (1) Digitalization has a significant threshold impact on IGTFP, and the research results are still robust according to changing the threshold variable algorithm and carrying on a Winsorize test. (2) Industry technological innovation (ITI) plays a threshold role in the process of digitalization impact on IGTFP. (3) Heterogeneity analysis shows that the impact mechanism varies depending on differentiated industry factor intensity and industry pollution degree. Therefore, policymakers should formulate differentiated industry digitalization strategies.

## Introduction
The main goal of current world economy is green and sustainable development. 11 of the 17 sustainable development goals proposed by the United Nations are related to environment and economy. The Paris Agreement signed by 178 countries around the world has set a clear carbon emission reduction agenda for the whole world. China’s economy is an important and indivisible part of the world economy. According to data from the National Bureau of Statistics of China, in 2023, China’s GDP is approximately 17.89 trillion U.S. dollars, accounting for approximately 17% of global GDP. However, current development path of Chinese economy is at the expense of massive energy consumption . According to the “World Energy Statistical Yearbook (2023)”, in 2022, the carbon dioxide emissions generated by mainland China are 10.55 billion tons, accounting for 30.69% of the world’s total, far exceeding other areas. Therefore, the green development of China’s economy is crucial to the green development of the whole world . That is, the green development of China’s economy is not only China’s own development issue, but also a global development issue. As the key position connecting economic growth and green development, researches on manufacturing industry’s green total factor productivity (IGTFP) has become an urgent requirement to promote the whole world’s green development.
Manufacturing is the foundation of the real economy and an important driving force for economic development. In addition, manufacturing is not only the main body of economic development, but also the main body of energy consumption. The total energy consumption of China’s manufacturing industry has increased from 4,283.34 million tons of standard coal in 2014 to 5,720 million tons of standard coal in 2023. Although in recent years, the total energy consumption of China’s manufacturing industry has gradually shown a relatively stable state, its overall energy consumption is still high. Therefore, manufacturing industry is the key factor for China to achieve green development. IGTFP is an indicator that comprehensively considers an industry’s pollution emissions and energy consumption, and is an important factor in evaluating an industry’s green development. Research on IGTFP plays an important role in optimizing resource allocation, encouraging environmentally friendly production, and promoting green economic development .
The rapid development of digital technology provides both technical support and development guidance for the improvement of IGTFP. However, there are different conclusions both in theoretical and empirical studies on whether and how digitalization affects IGTFP, and there exists two main viewpoints of “the promotion effect of digitalization” and “the inhibitory effect of digitalization”.
Relevant researches show the results that digitization has a positive promoting effect on IGTFP , . Firstly, the application of digital technology can optimize resource allocation, reduce energy consumption and waste emissions, thereby promoting green development , . Secondly, digitalization can promote the intelligence and automation of manufacturing industry, which enable the prediction and adjustment of energy consumption in the production process, and achieve environmentally friendly production . Thirdly, digitalization has driven innovation of manufacturing industry, especially in green and clean energy technologies, which can greatly reduce carbon emissions in their production process – . Fourthly, digitalization provides new business models for the manufacturing industry, which encourage the recycling of resources, reduce the demand for new resources, and can achieve green production , . Therefore, digitalization can provide positive driving forces for the green development of manufacturing industry.
Although digitalization has brought many positive impacts on the green development of manufacturing industry, there are also some negative effects , . On the one hand, in the process of digitization, the excessive reliances on digital devices usually lead to excessive consumption of resources and energy, resulting in the “carbon lock-in effect” , . On the other hand, the “energy rebound effect” caused by the improvement of energy efficiency originated from digitization will also stimulate the increase of total energy consumption, which may ultimately cause an increase rather than a decrease in carbon emissions . Besides, the development of the digital economy may have a “siphon effect”, that is, it can improve the green growth of the regions with higher levels of economic development, but it may inhibit the green growth of the regions in surrounding areas .
Theoretical and empirical studies have not yet reached a consensus, possibly for two reasons. First, the impact of digitalization is comprehensive. According to the “innovation compensation effect”, digitalization means data become a new production factor, which can greatly reduce resources and energy consumption in traditional industrial production , and thereby improving GTFP. However, according to the “R&D crowding-out effect”, digitalization inevitability needs more infrastructure investment which may lead to insufficient investment in other production factors and imbalanced internal resource allocation , , thereby decreasing GTFP. In fact, both the “innovation compensation effect” and the “R&D crowding-out effect” are reasonable . The reason for the contradiction is that digitalization can both increase investment costs and reduce energy consumption, thus the impact of digitalization on IGTFP may be non-linear. But the current discussion of the non-linear impact mechanism, especially the critical point, is still insufficient . Second, the existing research mainly focuses on the regional level, while there are fewer studies on the industry level . As heterogeneous individuals, the impact of digitalization in different industries is significantly different.
Manufacturing is the key component of the national economy. Digitization of manufacturing industry has not only changed production methods, but also had a profound impact on resource utilization efficiency and environmental status. However, the increase of energy consumption and the existence of energy rebound effects in the digitalization process pose new challenges to IGTFP. Therefore, it is necessary to explore the impact of digitalization on IGTFP from the industry level.
This study focuses on the manufacturing industry level, considering the heterogeneous innovation driving factors of different industries, aiming to explore the boundary conditions of digital economy’s driving effect, examine the impact mechanism of digitalization on IGTFP, and provide theoretical supplement and empirical support for the viewpoint of “digitalization’s green driving effect”. The empirical study is based on panel data of 29 manufacturing industries in China from 2012 to 2019. The empirical results show that: (1) Digitalization has a significant threshold impact on IGTFP, and the research results are still robust according to changing the threshold variable algorithm and carrying on a Winsorize test. (2) Industry technological innovation (ITI) plays a threshold role in the process of digitalization impact on IGTFP. (3) Heterogeneity analysis shows that the impact mechanism varies depending on differentiated industry factor intensity and industry pollution degree. Therefore, policymakers should formulate differentiated industry digitalization strategies.
The main contributions of this article are: (1) Focusing on the manufacturing industry level, exploring the impact and mechanism of digitalization on IGTFP based on different sub-categories of manufacturing industry. (2) Focusing on the manufacturing industry level, viewing ITI as the threshold variable to clarify the panel threshold mechanism and determine the threshold value. (3) Heterogeneity analysis is conducted based on industry factor intensity and industry pollution degree. The research conclusions can provide valuable reference for manufacturing industry’s green growth in the environment of digitalization from both related theoretical research and policymaker’s decision-making.

## Literature review

## Digitalization and industry green total factor productivity
The study of the impact of digitalization on IGTFP has further attracted researchers’ widespread attention and current studies present some new features. First, researchers’ discussion of the correlation between the two has changed from the early linear correlation to focusing on the exploration of non-linear correlation. Some scholars believe that digitalization may lead to resource allocation or technology adaptation problems , which will hinder IGTFP, other scholars believe that digitalization improves the efficiency of resource and energy utilization – , which in turn increases IGTFP. Second, most of the current research on the impact of digitalization on IGTFP focuses on regional and other macro levels , . There are few relevant studies at the industry level. Compared with the “siphon effect” between regions, there is a unique “cohort effect” at the industry level. Some studies have found that companies in the same industry can promote digitalization of “leader” companies and help them develop in the long term. Other companies can also learn from the relevant experience of “leader” companies to help themselves achieve high quality development. The “cohort effect” drives the digitalization improvement of the entire industry. The impact of digitalization is more comprehensive and extensive – , and may show unique effects in different industries.
Green total factor productivity refers to the ability to effectively utilize input factors such as labor, capital, and technology to achieve economic output, while simultaneously taking into account environmental factors such as resource utilization efficiency, environmental impact and ecological sustainability . This indicator is measured from the perspective of input-output, which can cover all enterprises and better reflect green development. It is an effective indicator for measuring the green development of the manufacturing industry and has significant implications for its development. According to the “following cost theory”, when a company chooses innovation, it will inevitably lead to some production factors’ input in the technology research department, while reducing the production factors’ input in other departments. Therefore, leading to “R&D crowding out effect”. However, at the same time, digitalization in manufacturing enterprises can further improves the competitive advantage and market competitiveness, which can compensate for the losses caused by increased R&D investment , and thus generates “innovation compensation effect” .
The rapid increasing digitalization has provided new opportunities and challenges for the green development of the manufacturing industry . But there are still differences in empirical research conclusions. On the one hand, the increasing digitalization has led to the improvement of manufacturing production efficiency and technological advancement , and promoted the improvement of IGTFP in the manufacturing industry. Based on Chinese A-share listed companies’ penal data from 2004 to 2020, the empirical study found thatdigitalization has a significant positive impact on enterprise GTFP . On the other hand, digitalization inevitably increases the production and operation costs in the short term, and increases R&D investment and related investment in the long term, which in turn hinders the improvement of IGTFP. Based on the data of relevant countries from 1985 to 2012, the research gave the opinion that digitalization will lead to an increasement in energy consumption . The research using the Structural Factor Vector Autoregressive Model (S-FAVAR) and data from five countries of France, Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom and the United States, showed that the improvements in energy use efficiency bring about an economic rebound effect, which in turn offsets the expected cost savings . In addition, digitalization may also have a non-linear impact on IGTFP. Based on the panel data of 277 cities in China 2013–2019, the research using the spatial Durbin model shows that the digitalization has an inverted U-shaped impact on IGTFP . Furthermore, according to the panel data of 100 Chinese cities 2011–2019, the research results found that digitalization of financial industry has an inverted U-shaped relationship on IGTFP .
The empirical research conclusions on the green environmental effects of digitalization in manufacturing industry are inconsistent. The possible reason is that the magnitude of the “R&D crowding out effect” and “innovation compensation effect” affects the direction of the impact of digitalization on IGTFP. However, the existing research lacks the exploration of the boundary of these two simultaneously existing effects at the industry level, and the mechanism of digitalization affects IGTFP is not yet clear, which cannot guide manufacturing industry’s green development by way of differentiated digital policies. Therefore, it is necessary to examine the green environmental effects of digitalization from a nonlinear perspective .
Based on the above analysis, it is showed that there may exist a threshold impact of digitalization on IGTFP, so Hypothesis is proposed.

## Hypothesis 1
Digitalization has a threshold impact on IGTFP.

## Industry technological innovation and industry green total factor productivity
The impact mechanism of digitalization on IGTFP needs to be further clarified, but the effects of “cost reduction, efficiency improvement, and knowledge spillover” brought about by digitalization are widely recognized. Firstly, data itself is a kind of valuable resource, digitalization breaks the time and space gap, thus it not only expands the data source, but also greatly reduces information searching cost and is helpful for building competitive advantages , , . Secondly, digitalization can promote the simplification and intelligence of work processes, which is conducive to improving operational efficiency, reducing operational risks, and creating greater value , . Thirdly, digitalization can reduce information asymmetry, expand the source and quality of knowledge, promote knowledge flow and technological change, and improve competitiveness , .
Digitalization breaks down information barriers through the investment of data elements, and improves the flexibility, convenience, and timeliness of technological innovation , , thereby shows positive impacts on IGTFP , . However, the mechanism of innovation costs and innovation benefits both brought about by digitalization is still unclear. Schumpeter’s innovation theory proposes that innovation is the essential requirement for economic development. Thus, the heterogeneous technological innovation level of different sub-industries is an important factor affecting IGTFP . Furthermore, it is necessary to further explore the impact of technological innovation on GTFP from the industry level.
According to the “learning curve theory”, in the early stage of new technology application, employees need time to master new techniques, which inhibits the improvement of production efficiency, and furthermore IGTFP decreasing. Therefore, companies have to increase their R&D investment, and resulting in the “R&D crowding out effect”, which is detrimental to green development in the short term. However, in the long term, technological innovation brought about by digitalization can form integrate resources, establish dynamic competitive advantages, and generate “innovation compensation effect”, thus conducive to green development. Empirical results based on 27 provinces in China show that technological innovation is positive to the green development .
The heterogeneous equilibrium point of “innovation compensation effect” and “R&D crowding out effect” in different industries affects the differences in environmental performance obtained from digital transformation, and the level of technological innovation is an important factor affecting the digital green environmental effect of manufacturing industry . The impact of digitalization on IGTFP is multidimensional and complex, and the existing researches still lack exploration of the heterogeneity equilibrium point between the two effects. Therefore, future researches need to conduct in-depth analysis of the impact of digitization on IGTFP in sub-industries of manufacturing, as well as under what conditions the above two effects can reach equilibrium. This can help to give more suitable policies and achieve green development. Therefore, it is necessary to explore the role of industry technological innovation in the impact of digitalization on IGTFP.
Based on the above analysis, Hypothesis is proposed.

## Hypothesis 2
Industry technological innovation plays a threshold role in the impact of digitalization affecting IGTFP.

## Research design

## Variable description and data sources

## Dependent variables
The dependent variable of this article is industry green total factor productivity (IGTFP). This paper constructs a production possibility set including energy and non-energy input, desired and undesired output, and then uses the EBM model to calculate IGTFP of manufacturing sub-industries – . The related indicators are shown in Table  .
Specifically, input indicators include labor input, capital input, and energy input . The labor input is measured by the average number of employees in the segmented manufacturing industry. The capital investment is calculated by the perpetual inventory method according to sub-industry’s fixed assets investment. Energy input is measured by sub-industry’s total electricity consumption. Output indicators include desired and undesired output. The desired output is measured by the actual GDP at constant prices in 2011. The undesired output is measured by industrial wastewater, SO2 and carbon emissions of segmented manufacturing industry. The relevant data comes from China Statistical Yearbook (CSY), China Labor Statistical Yearbook (CLSY), China Environmental Statistical Yearbook (CESY), China Industrial Statistical Yearbook (CISY), China Electricity Council Annual Compilation of Electric Power Industry Statistical Data (CECA), National Economic and Social Development in provincial level (NESD).

 

## Independent variable
The dependent variable of this article is the digitalization of manufacturing sub-industries. According to “China Digitalization Development Index Report (2022)” (CDDI) and the standards for measuring digitalization by OECD, this paper constructs a comprehensive evaluation system of digitalization including 4 first-level indicators and 9 s-level indicators , which are listed in Table  . Based on these indicators, the entropy method is used to calculate the digitalization level.

 

## Threshold variables
The threshold variable of this article is industry technological innovation (ITI) of manufacturing sub-industries. The number of patents is widely regarded as an important indicator to measure it. Compared with design patents and new practical patents, invention patents are most closely related to technological progress and innovation capabilities, furthermore, authorized patents can measure innovation level from the results dimension. Therefore, this article uses the number of authorized invention patents in manufacturing sub-industries to measure ITI.

## Control variables
Referring to relevant literatures, this article selects the following control variables: average enterprise scale (SCA), capital intensity (CI), industry competition condition (HHI), market structure (STR), and industry average return on assets (ROA). Among them, SCA is measured by the ratio of the industry total output value to the total number of large and medium-sized enterprises in the industry. CI is measured by the ratio of industry total assets to the number of industry employments. HHI means Herfindahl-Hirschman Index, which is the sum of the squares of the ratio of the main business income of every company in the industry to the whole industry’s main business income. STR is measured by the total number of large and medium-sized enterprises in the industry. ROA is measured by the industry average ratio of the sum of net profit and financial expenses to average assets.

## Grouping variables
Industry heterogeneity characteristics are an important factor in the differential impact of digital transformation on green total factor productivity in manufacturing subdivision industries. Industry factor intensity (Indus) and industry pollution status (Pollu) are grouping variables in this article. On the one hand, referring to relevant researches, the manufacturing industry is further divided into three subcategories of labor-intensive, capital-intensive and technology intensivebased on the relative intensity of labor, capital, and technology in each industry. On the other hand, the manufacturing industry is divided into three sub-categories of heavily-pollution industry, moderately-pollution industry, and lightly-pollution industry according to their comprehensive pollution emission.
Variables summary is shown in Table  .

 

## Sample selection and data source
The sample used in this study is panel data of 29 manufacturing industries in China from 2012 to 2019. The reasons are as follows: (1) In 2011, the American consulting company Gartner defined digitalization for the first time, thus 2011 also can be considered as the beginning of the digital era, and 2012 is the first full financial year contained digitalization data. (2) Most of the related data in the year of 2020 and after are missing or unpublished. Therefore, 2019 is the latest year in which data of IGTFP can be measured more accurately. The data used in this article come from the CSY, CISY, CESY, NBSC and CSMAR.

## Threshold regression
Firstly, testing whether the single-threshold or double-threshold effects exists. The results show that, taking ITI as the threshold value, digitalization has a significant single-threshold impact on the IGTFP. The results of the threshold effect test are shown in Table  . Single threshold effect diagram is shown in Fig.  .

 

 

Secondly, analyzing the threshold impact of digitalization. The regression results are shown in column 1 of Table  . Obviously, digitalization has a significant single-threshold impact on IGTFP, and industry technological innovation plays the threshold role. If ITI < 7.0411, DIG has a significant negative impact on IGTFP. If ITI ≥ 7.0411, DIG has a significant positive impact on IGTFP. That is, as the increasing of technological innovation, the inhibitory effect of DIG on IGTFP changes to a promoting effect. In other words, with the improving of technological innovation, the higher degree of DIG, the more conducive it is to guiding and promoting the industry to carry out green production. This result proves that DIG has a “U-shaped” impact on IGTFP, and the “innovation compensation effect” and “R&D crowding out effect” of digitalization exist simultaneously.

 

## Robustness test
In order to test the stability of the threshold regression results, this paper further conducts the robustness test in two ways. Firstly, changing the threshold variable algorithm. Considering that R&D investment is also an important indicator that can measure the level of technological innovation, we re-calculate technological innovation of manufacturing sub-industries according toR&D investment and carry on the threshold regression again. The results are shown in column 2 of Table  . Secondly, shrinking the data. All continuous variables are Winsorized and then re-perform the threshold regression. The results are shown in column 3 of Table  . Both of the above two robustness tests indicate that the research conclusions are reliable and robust.

## Heterogeneity analysis

## Heterogeneity of industry factor intensity
This paper divides the samples into three subcategories of labor-intensive, capital-intensive and technology-intensive based on industry factor intensity . The classification criteria include two indicators which are the proportion of fixed assets to total assets and the proportion of R&D expenditures to employee salaries payable. The regression results are shown in columns 1, 2, and 3 of Table  .
The regression results show that the threshold impact of digitalization on IGTFP is varying due to different sub-industries factor intensity, and this impact is most obvious in labor-intensive industries. For labor-intensive industry, if ITI is lower than the threshold value, digitalization has a negative impact on IGTFP, if ITI is higher than the threshold value, digitalization has a positive impact on IGTFP.
This reflects that being the traditional manufacturing industry, labor-intensive industry usually has to face the difficulties of lacking high-tech talents and advanced equipment. In the early stages, the “R&D crowd-out effect” of digitalization is more significant. However, with the increasingly R&D output, the level of ITI is improved simultaneously, which makes the “innovation compensation effect” of digitalization more significantly, and thus promotes IGTFP. For capital-intensive and technology-intensive industries, the threshold effect of digitalization on industry GTFP is not significant. Owing to their abundant capital and advanced technology, they have basements and advantages in green production equipment and technology, which will furthermore have a higher IGTFP.

## Heterogeneity of industry pollution status
This paper divides the samples into three subcategories of heavily-pollution, moderately-pollution and lightly-pollution based on the comprehensive pollution emission levels of manufacturing sub-industries . The regression results are shown in columns 4, 5, and 6 of Table  . According to the regression results, the threshold effect of digitalization on IGTFP is varying due to different sub-industries pollution status. For heavily-pollution industries, if ITI is lower than the threshold value, digitalization has a significant negative impact on IGTFP, which indicates that heavily-pollution industries are highly polluted and their digitalization are relatively slowly and arduous. For moderately-pollution industries, digitalization does not show a significant impact on IGTFP. For lightly-pollution industries, digitalization shows a significant positive impact on IGTFP, this may due to the particularity of their products which usually with less pollutants emitted and high extent of green development. Thus, the impact of ITI is relatively lower in lightly-pollution industries, and digitalization always shows a positive impact on IGTFP.

 

## Conclusions and limitations

## Conclusions
The green and sustainable development has become a trend. It is of great significance for developing countries including China to grasp the opportunities of the digitalization to achieve green growth. In order to clarify the nonlinear impact of digitalization on IGTFP, based on existing theoretical and practical research literature, this article establishes testable hypotheses about digitalization, ITI and IGTFP. Furthermore, this article examines the impact of digitalization on IGTFP based on panel data of 29 manufacturing sub-industries in China 2012–2019. The main conclusions are as following: (1) Digitalization has a single-threshold impact on IGTFP, and this impact is “U-shaped”. (2) ITI plays a threshold role in the impact of digitalization affects IGTFP. If ITI is lower than the threshold value, the negative impact is significant, which indicates that in this stage, the “R&D crowding out effect” is higher than the “innovation compensation effect”. If ITI is higher than the threshold value, the positive impact is significant, which indicates that the “innovation compensation effect” is higher than the “R&D crowding out effect”. (3) The threshold impact of the digitalization on IGTFP is due to the heterogeneity of industry factor intensity and industry pollution level. There are industry factor intensity and industry pollution status differences in the impact of digitalization on IGTFP. This single threshold effect is more significant in labor-intensive industries and heavily-pollution industries, and the threshold role of ITI is more obviously.
The empirical analysis results confirm the non-linear relationship between digitalization and IGTFP, which offers a beneficial expansion of current industry perspective researches. The empirical results also confirm the threshold role of ITI on the impact of digitalization on IGTFP, which not only distinguishes the differentiated impacts of “R&D crowding out effect” and “innovation compensation effect” at different stages, but also provides a new perspective for understanding the relationship between technological innovation and green productivity. The heterogeneity of industry characteristics provides a theoretical basis for the green transformation of segmented industries.
All these conclusions have valuable policy implications for formulating differentiated policies to promote the green development of manufacturing sub-sectors more in-depth. Firstly, the digitalization process of the manufacturing industry is not necessarily better the faster. The government should establish a flexible and differentiated digital development policy system to adapt to the heterogeneous responses of different industries to the impact of digitalization, and improve green productivity. Secondly, manufacturing enterprises should increase their R&D investment to increase the “innovation compensation effect”, and improve IGTFP through technological innovation. In addition, related policies should encourage the coordinated development of digitization and green level, especially for labor-intensive and high-pollution industries. This can help them achieve digital transformation and improve green efficiency by way of technological innovation.

## Limitations
On the one hand, digitalization usually requires lots of investments and thus may cause hysteresis impact, but this article does not analyze the lagged impact. Future research can further explore this. On the other hand, the rapid development of digitalization has brought unprecedented opportunities and challenges to the green development both for manufacturing industry and for manufacturing enterprises. This article focuses on the discussion at the industry level and does not consider enterprise heterogeneity factors. Therefore, further research can conduct an in-depth exploration from the enterprise level.
