University of Oklahoma 
Bioprocessing Core Facility 
External User Acknowledgment Form 
In consideration for using the services of the Bioprocessing Core Facility, including the opportunity 
to use equipment in the Bioprocessing Core Facility, the External User hereby agree as follows: 
A. Liability 
1. The External User shall indemnify, defend, and hold the University, its Regents, officers, 
employees, students, volunteers, agents and representatives harmless from and against liability for 
any and all claims, demands, damages, liabilities, ﬁnes, penalties, losses, expenses, costs, and fees 
of any nature, including but not limited to, bodily injury, death, personal injury, illness, product 
liability, and property damage, arising from the External User’s use of the Bioprocessing Core Facility. 
The University will give the External User notice of any claim it receives within ten (10) business days 
of receipt of a claim by the University.  
3. The External User hereby agrees to release, waive, forever discharge, indemnify and covenant not 
to sue the University, its Regent s, officers, employees, students, volunteers, agents and 
representatives for any and all loss, damages, claim, demand, action or right of action, arising from 
the External User’s use of the Bioprocessing Core Facility.  
3. The University makes no representations and extends no warranties of any kind, either express or 
implied, including without limitation warranties of merchantability or ﬁtness for a particular purpose, 
and assumes no responsibility whatever with respect to the External User’s use of the Bioprocessing 
Core Facility. All services and equipment are provided “AS IS. ”  
4. The University shall not be liable in all events for any consequential, special, incidental or indirect 
damages arising out of the External User’s use of the Bioprocessing Core Facility even if the University 
has been notiﬁed of the possibility thereof. 
B. Insurance 
1. The External User shall, at its sole expense, carry and maintain a policy of comprehensive general 
liability insurance, including without limitation, bodily injury and death, and property damage , as 
follows: A limit of $1,000,000 for bodily injury (including death), $1,000,000 for personal injury and 
$1,000,000 for property damage. 
2. The External User shall  include in its insurance policy  “the Board of Regents of the University of 
Oklahoma” as a certiﬁcate holder and submit such certiﬁcate of insurance to the University within a 
reasonable time after execution of this Acknowledgement Form and before commencing any use of 
the Bioprocessing Core Facility.  
C. Use 
1. The External User shall comply with all rules, regulations and policies applicable to the University 
and the Bioprocessing Core Facility , including but not limited to the Bioprocessing Core Facility  

Operation Rules and Regulations, which is published on the Bioprocessing Core Facility ’s website 
and may be updated from time to time. 
2. The External User shall conduct its use of the Bioprocessing Core Facility  with accepted 
professional standards and will exercise reasonably best care for the equipment of the Bioprocessing 
Core Facility. At the University’s option, the External User shall promptly pay for or reimburse the 
University for all repairs to any equipment damaged during the External User ’s use of the 
Bioprocessing Core Facility or if unable to be repaired, to replace such equipment with equipment of 
similar value and speciﬁcations. 
3. The External User shall pay for its use of the Bioprocessing Core Facility in accordance with the 
Bioprocessing Core Facility Fee Schedule, which is published on the Bioprocessing Core Facility ’s 
website and may be updated from time to time. 
D. Acknowledgement 
1. Acknowledgement of the Bioprocessing Core Facility is encouraged and appreciated. The External 
User may acknowledge the Bioprocessing Core Facility in public statements, reports and other print 
and online publication  in accordance with the Bioprocessing Core Facility  Acknowledgment 
Requirement, which is published on the Bioprocessing Core Facility’s website and may be updated 
from time to time.
