 THE PROBLEM THIS SOLVES

 Hidden friction in sourcing the right partner is holding your science back

 The scientific capabilities required to advance a program frequently exist somewhere in the global marketplace. The challenge lies in identifying the right partner, qualifying them, contracting with them, onboarding them into internal systems, and managing performance over time. While science itself has modernized, much of the operational infrastructure that supports research and development has remained fragmented, manual, and slow to evolve.

 Enter: our purpose-built R&D marketplace 

 HOW IT WORKS

 From manual searching to intelligent matching

 Intelligent Search and Filtering

 Search by capability, methodology, therapeutic area, geographic location, compliance certifications, and more. A conversational agent surfaces optimal partners based on requirements and performance data from across the network.
 What you get: Find specialized suppliers in minutes, not weeks.

 Robust Legal Model

 All suppliers are pre-contracted and pre-qualified: risk intelligence screening, compliance assessments, and capability verification completed before joining the network. Start working immediately under a single MSA (Master Services Agreement).
 What you get: Skip the contracting and onboarding. Access new suppliers fast.

 Real-time Analytics

 See supplier ratings, on-time delivery metrics, quality scores, and past project outcomes from across the Science Exchange network.
 What you get: Make informed decisions based on actual performance, not marketing claims.

 See How a Request Comes Together

 Watch a scientist search the Marketplace, add the same item from two suppliers, and submit a single request form that reaches both at the same time. The whole process takes under a minute, and it works the same whether the request goes to one supplier or five.

 Because every supplier in the network is already contracted and qualified, there is nothing to negotiate before the first quote arrives. One form goes out, suppliers respond with competitive bids, and the scientist compares on price, quality, and speed without ever leaving the platform.
