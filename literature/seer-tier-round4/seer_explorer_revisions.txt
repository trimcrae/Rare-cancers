 SEER*Explorer Revision History

 The following list details updates to the data and features available in SEER*Explorer.

 Jun 
 22 
 2026

 Preliminary Incidence Rates through 2024 were added to the application. See Preliminary Cancer Incidence Rates and Trends, 2000-2024 for more details.

 May 
 12 
 2026

 The confidence intervals for the annual survival estimates in Long-Term Trends for Survival were not correct. The estimates were corrected.

 Apr 
 22 
 2026

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, prevalence and risk of diagnosis estimates based on the SEER November 2025 submission and includes the 2023 diagnosis year.

 The application was updated to include mortality and risk of death estimates through 2024.

 Due to the Impact of COVID on the SEER Data Release , the 2020 incidence rates are not used in the fit of the Joinpoint trend line(s). Where applicable, the 2020 incidence rates are still displayed on the graphs and data tables.

 Jan 
 8 
 2026

 A Data Archive page was added to the application. This page contains the SEER*Explorer estimates from past SEER submissions (November 2021 SEER submission through November 2023 SEER submission) consolidated into downloadable Excel files. These files can be used to reference the estimates that were shown in previous releases of the SEER*Explorer application.

 The Rural/Urban Trend graphs for Incidence and Mortality were updated to allow a second variable on the graphs that can be added using the Additional Comparison option. This allows users to show graphs by both the Rural Urban area classification and a second variable of their choice.

 The Recent Trends and Long-Term Trends graphs for Survival estimates were modified to clarify when the regression line for a series is based on observed or projected survival estimates. The regression line now appears as a dashed line when based on projected survival estimates.

 The functionality for comparing cancer sites was changed. Cancer Site is now available as an option in the Compare By ribbon of the application. The Get Started with a Cancer Site control no longer includes an option to compare cancer sites.

 Jul 
 2 
 2025

 Preliminary Incidence Rates through 2023 were added to the application. See Preliminary Cancer Incidence Rates and Trends, 2000-2023 for more details.

 Long-Term trends in Survival, 1975-2021 were added to the application.

 U.S. Mortality trends and 5-year age-adjusted rates by Rural Urban Continuum Code were added for selected cancer sites as part of the release. The estimates can be found under Rural/Urban Trends and Rural/Urban Rates where available.

 The Rates by Age graphs for Incidence and Mortality were updated to show the rates for ages 85+ split into the age groups 85-89 and 90+.

 Apr 
 18 
 2025

 A correction was made to the limited duration estimates for Non-Hispanic White. No other race/ethinicity groupings were affected by the update.

 Apr 
 16 
 2025

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, and prevalence estimates based on the SEER November 2024 submission and includes the 2022 diagnosis year.

 The application was updated to include mortality estimates through 2023.

 SEER Incidence trends and 5-year age-adjusted rates by Rural Urban Continuum Code were added for selected cancer sites as part of the release. The estimates can be found under Rural/Urban Trends and Rural/Urban Rates where available.

 Due to the Impact of COVID on the SEER Data Release , the 2020 incidence rates are not used in the fit of the Joinpoint trend line(s). Where applicable, the 2020 incidence rates are still displayed on the graphs and data tables.

 Nov 
 5 
 2024

 Preliminary Incidence Rates through 2022 were added to the application. See Preliminary Cancer Incidence Rates and Trends, 2000-2022 for more details.

 Recent Trends and Long-Term Trends for Colon and Rectum Cancer (excluding Appendix) were added to the application. The estimates previously labeled as Colon and Rectum are now labeled as Colon and Rectum (including Appendix).

 Sep 
 26 
 2024

 The Average Absolute Change in Survival estimates shown for Recent Trends in Survival were not correct for any detailed stage at diagnosis series. The estimates were corrected. The Average Absolute Change in Survival estimates shown for 'All Stages' series were correct.

 Jun 
 27 
 2024

 Risk of developing cancer and risk of dying from cancer estimates were added to the application.

 Apr 
 17 
 2024

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, and prevalence estimates based on the SEER November 2023 submission and includes the 2021 diagnosis year.

 The application was updated to include mortality estimates through 2022.

 Due to the Impact of COVID on the SEER Data Release , the 2020 incidence rates are not used in the fit of the Joinpoint trend line(s). Where applicable, the 2020 incidence rates are still displayed on the graphs and data tables.

 The Risk of Diagnosis/Death estimates that are typically part of the April updates are still in development and will be added to SEER*Explorer when complete.

 Nov 
 16 
 2023

 Delay-adjusted rates and trends were added for the majority of the cancer sites presented in this application. 

 Jul 
 31 
 2023

 Median age at diagnosis, based on 2016-2020 SEER 22 data, and median age at death, based on 2016-2020 NCHS data, were added to the application.

 Jun 
 8 
 2023

 Recent Trends in Survival, 2000-2019 were added to the application.

 The labels for prevalence percentages were updated to reflect that they are the percentage of the population for the displayed cohort.

 Apr 
 19 
 2023

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, prevalence, and lifetime risk estimates based on the SEER November 2022 submission and includes the 2020 diagnosis year.

 Due to the Impact of COVID on SEER Cancer Incidence 2020 data , the 2020 incidence rates are not used in the fit of the Joinpoint trend line(s), and the risk of developing cancer is based on underlying estimates for the year range 2017-2019.

 Survival estimates are now based on the SEER 22 registries (excluding Illinois and Massachusetts). Prior releases of SEER*Explorer included survival estimates based on the SEER 17 registries.

 The Purchased/Referred Care Delivery Area (PRCDA) county definition used for American Indians/Alaska Natives estimates was updated from the PRCDA 2017 definition to the PRCDA 2020 definition.

 The Recent Trends in Survival that are typically part of the April updates are still in development and will be added to SEER*Explorer when complete.

 Mar 
 7 
 2023

 When working with the Compare Cancer Sites selection from the Get Started with a Cancer Site dropdown list, sex-specific cancers sites such as Cervix Uteri cancer and Prostate cancer can now be included in the results when 'Both Sexes' is the selected sex value. The population based statistics for the sex-specific cancer sites will be limited to the applicable sex.

 Sep 
 1 
 2022

 The application was updated to include mortality estimates through 2020.

 Recent Trends in Survival, 2000-2018 were added to the application.
 The survival and prevalence estimates were updated to reflect corrections made to the underlying SEER data release. 

 The ability to show the number of cases for incidence and survival estimates, and the number of deaths for mortality estimates was added to the application.

 Buttons to 'Select All' and 'Clear All' options were added to the Select Cancer Sites to Compare window.
 
 The display of prevalence estimates were updated to present the data table by default with the prevalence count and prevalence percent both shown in the table. The graphs for prevalence are still available, but only include the prevalence percent in the graph.

 Jul 
 13 
 2022

 The application was not presenting estimates correctly when multiple cancer sites were compared with the sex or stage at diagnosis variables. Estimates may have appeared in the wrong rows within the data table. The issue has been addressed. Any estimates obtained from this type of cross-comparison since the April 15, 2022 release should be reviewed.

 When a user copies the URL link of the selected estimates, the display settings for either the graph or data table are now preserved when the link is shared. 

 Apr 
 15 
 2022

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, prevalence and lifetime risk estimates based on the SEER November 2021 submission and including the 2019 diagnosis year.

 The SEER areas included in the SEER*Explorer data sources have changed due to changes in which registries are included with this data submission. The groupings are now referred to as SEER 8, SEER 12, SEER 17, and SEER 22. Refer to Registry Groupings in SEER Data and Statistics for details. 
 SEER's recommendations for producing statistics by race and origin changed. SEER*Explorer statistics align as much as possible with the new recommendations which include five mutually exclusive categories: Non-Hispanic White, Non-Hispanic Black, Non-Hispanic Asian/Pacific Islander (API), Non-Hispanic American Indian/Alaska Native (AI/AN) and Hispanic. Refer to Race and Hispanic Ethnicity Changes for more information.
 
 The ability to compare multiple cancer sites in a single graph or data table was added for the majority of available statistics. Select Compare Cancer Sites from the Get Started with a Cancer Site dropdown list.

 The Recent Trends in Survival that are typically part of the April updates are still in development and will be added to SEER*Explorer when complete.

 Feb 
 1 
 2022

 A new feature was added to the Recent Trends and Long-term Trends graphs for Incidence, Mortality, and Survival statistics. An information panel can now be displayed next to the graph that shows the Annual Percent Change (APC) estimates for Incidence and Mortality statistics, or the Average Absolute Change in Survival (AAC_S) estimates for Survival statistics. To show the APC or AAC_S, check the 'View APC' (or 'View AAC_S' for Survival) checkbox located above the graph.

 Sep 
 27 
 2021

 The application was updated to include mortality estimates through 2019.

 Aug 
 9 
 2021

 The survival by survival time and conditional survival estimates presented for stage specific cancer estimates presented the wrong year range of 2000-2017. The year range has been corrected to 2004-2017.

 Jul 
 12 
 2021

 The confidence intervals presented for the annual survival rates under survival recent trends were incorrect. The estimates have been corrected.

 Apr 
 23 
 2021

 The age-adjusted prevalence percentages presented for age groups smaller than 'All Ages' were not properly age-adjusted to the 2000 U.S. Standard population. The estimates have been corrected.

 Apr 
 15 
 2021

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, prevalence and lifetime risk estimates based on the SEER November 2020 submission.

 Subtypes for Brain and Other Nervous System and Non-mailgnant Brain and Other Nervous System were added to the application based on the new SEER Brain/CNS Recode .

 Starting with the November 2020 data submission, SEER now uses "Behavior code ICD-O-3" rather than the "Behavior recode for analysis" field in SEER*Stat and any published statistics. For more information, please see Behavior Variable Changes .

 Conditional survival estimates were added for a majority of the available cancer sites.

 Feb 
 10 
 2021

 A new option was added to the Choose a Statistic to Explore options, Incidence and Mortality Comparison, which allows users to dispaly SEER Incidence and U.S. Mortality series on several of the existing graph types.

 Sep 
 14 
 2020

 The application was updated to include mortality estimates through 2018.

 Delay-adjusted rates, when available, are the default rate type shown for SEER Incidence statistics.

 Apr 
 15 
 2020

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, mortality, prevalence and lifetime risk estimates based on the SEER November 2019 submission.

 A new user-interface for the application is also part of this release.

 Apr 
 15 
 2019

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, mortality, prevalence and lifetime risk estimates based on the SEER November 2018 submission.

 Statistics for several subtypes of larger cancer groupings including:
 
 Breast Cancer

 Esophagus Cancer

 Lung and Bronchus Cancer

 Thyroid Cancer

 Charts for complete and limited-duration prevalence were added to the application.

 Charts for 5-year recent rates were added for incidence and mortality.

 Apr 
 16 
 2018

 The application was updated with incidence, survival, mortality and lifetime risk estimates based on the SEER November 2017 submission.

 Survival Trends charts were added to the application.

 Charts for lifetime risk estimates were added to the application.

 Relative survival for Asian/Pacific Islanders and Hispanic ethnicities were added to the five-year survival and survival by time since diagnosis charts.

 Dec 
 1 
 2017

 The user interface was updated based on feedback from usability testing.

 May 
 18 
 2017

 The relative survival estimates available under Survival->5-year survival were corrected. Previous estimates were cause-specific survival estimates. The estimates are now relative survival estimates.

 Apr 
 14 
 2017

 The application was updated with incidence, survival and mortality estimates based on the SEER November 2016 submission.

 Apr 
 15 
 2016

 The application was released with incidence and mortality estimates based on the SEER November 2015 submission.
