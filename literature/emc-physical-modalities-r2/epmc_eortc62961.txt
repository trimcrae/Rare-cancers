Soft tissue sarcomas (STSs) comprise a group of rare malignancies with anatomic- and histologic-specific patterns of local and distant recurrence. The aim of this review is thus to provide a comprehensive overview of diagnostic and treatment options for localized extremity and retroperitoneal STSs. Methods
A literature search was conducted to identify articles related to the diagnosis and management of localized extremity and retroperitoneal STSs.
