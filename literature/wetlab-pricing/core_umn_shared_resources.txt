 Shared Resources

 The Masonic Cancer Center, University of Minnesota, provides access to an extensive network of Shared Resources that support and advance cancer research across the entire continuum. These resources are designed to promote collaboration, enhance scientific rigor, and provide investigators with access to specialized expertise, state-of-the-art instrumentation, and high-quality services. 
 Supporting excellence in cancer research
 MCC’s Shared Resources play a vital role in the success of our members’ research by offering: 
 Cutting-edge technologies and instrumentation 
 Expert consultation and training 
 Assistance with experimental design, data analysis, and interpretation 
 Centralized services that ensure consistency, quality, and cost-efficiency 
 These resources are available to all MCC members and other investigators, with prioritized access and discounted rates for MCC-affiliated researchers.

 Access and acknowledgement
 Required acknowledgment for Shared Resource use 
Research supported by the MCC Cancer Center Support Grant, including work using MCC Shared Resources, must cite the cancer center and each Shared Resource used.
 “This work was supported in part by NIH P30 CA77598 utilizing the following Masonic Cancer Center, University of Minnesota shared resource(s)..." 
 
 Public access compliance 
Investigators must follow the NIH Public Access Policy by submitting the final peer‑reviewed manuscript to PubMed Central and including the PMCID in all applicable publications.

 Antibody Engineering

 Biostatistics 
 Offers rigorous statistical design and analysis across the cancer research continuum to ensure the integrity and impact of research findings.

 Cancer Genomics 
 Offers advanced genomic technologies and expert guidance, including specialized support in Cytogenomics and access to the University of Minnesota Genomics Center .

 Cancer Informatics 
 Offers comprehensive, integrated data support for cancer research, leveraging both Bioinformatics and Clinical Informatics .

 Comparative Pathology 
 Provides comprehensive pathology support and expertise to investigators who use laboratory animals in their research.

 Exposure Testing 
 Offers services supporting environmental exposure research, chemical analysis, and biomarker development.

 Flow Cytometry 
 Provides high ‑dimensional, multiparameter analysis of single‑cell populations defined by surface and intracellular immune and cancer markers.

 Genome Engineering 
 Applies state-of-the-art genomic engineering strategies and whole-genome knockout screens to various cell types.

 Mouse Genetics
