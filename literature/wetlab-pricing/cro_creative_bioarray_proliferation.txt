 Cell proliferation and viability assays are indispensable tools in the exploration of novel therapeutic agents. These assays play a crucial role in providing a comprehensive understanding of cellular behavior. In addition to quantifying the number of viable cells, modern in vitro proliferation assays go beyond, incorporating various methods to assess cell nuclei, measure metabolic activity, evaluate cell redox potential, and scrutinize mitochondrial integrity. These multifaceted approaches ensure a thorough and nuanced characterization of cell health, enabling researchers to make informed decisions in the pursuit of innovative and effective therapeutic solutions.

 Creative Bioarray offers specialized tumor cell proliferation and viability assay services, precisely tailored to determine the IC50 values of your compounds of interest in comprehensive whole cell assays. Our services extend across a diverse array of over 100 normal and tumor cell lines, providing a robust platform for cell screening and proliferation assays. This expansive selection ensures that you have the flexibility to choose the most relevant and representative cell lines for your specific research needs. Trust Creative Bioarray to deliver accurate and insightful data to advance your understanding of compound efficacy and optimize your drug discovery process.
