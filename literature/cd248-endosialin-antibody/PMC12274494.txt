Correction to: Scientific Reports 10.1038/s41598-024-65435-0, published online 21 June 2024
The original version of the Article contained errors.
As a result of an error introduced during the final figure formatting stage, the Vimentin western blot data panels were mistakenly duplicated across replicates for both NCI-H460 and A549 during figure assembly.
The NCI-H460 Vimentin condition in Figure  B was mistakenly a duplication of the A549 Vimentin condition in Figure   A. In the Supplementary Figures file, the NCI-H460 Vimentin condition in Supplementary Figure 3 was mistakenly a duplication of the A549 Vimentin condition in Supplementary Figure 2.
 The original Figure  and accompanying legend appear below.
The original Article has been corrected.
