# NR4A3 method-watch — week of August 28

**Real one this week: a fusion-targeted peptide vaccine just showed it can work in a related sarcoma.**

- **EWSR1-FLI1 peptide vaccine, Ewing sarcoma.** A published trial reports durable clinical and immunologic response to an off-the-shelf peptide vaccine targeting the EWSR1-FLI1 fusion breakpoint. That's the closest real-world precedent yet for the EWSR1::NR4A3 junction-vaccine route's core bet — that a fusion breakpoint is immunogenic enough to target. Worth reading in full and likely worth citing in vaccine-construct.json.
- **Daraxonrasib (RASONQUE) approved for pancreatic cancer** — first pan-RAS(ON) inhibitor to reach approval, called practice-changing by multiple outlets. Doesn't bear on any of your routes (not a degrader, ASO, or vaccine); flagged only because it's a first-in-class oncology approval.
- **Degrader-class drugs kept racking up clinical activity**: first patient dosed in a new molecular-glue trial for prostate cancer, plus Fast Track for the pan-RAS molecular glue ERAS-0015. Incremental added precedent for the NR4A3 degrader route, beyond last week's CELMoD/vepdegestrant approvals.
- **FDA Fast Track for SOT106** in soft tissue sarcoma — sarcoma-specific regulatory movement, no data yet.
- **Merck/Moderna's melanoma neoantigen vaccine** (last week's headline) is now getting "approval push" coverage — momentum, not new data.
- **Funding:** still nothing newly open to a solo researcher; same institutional-eligibility DOD/NSF grants as before.

No direct NR4A3/EWSR1::NR4A3 advance, and no new tool release beyond what was already flagged.
