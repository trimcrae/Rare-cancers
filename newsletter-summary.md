# NR4A3 method-watch — week of October 30

Nothing new on NR4A3/EMC, but the vaccine and sarcoma news is worth a look. News items are press leads, not confirmed; primary sources still to be read.

- **Merck/Moderna personalized melanoma vaccine, follow-on** (KOLD, 10/29: awaiting FDA approval after the late-stage trial; last week's Reuters/STAT reports of a 42% lower risk of death or recurrence remain press claims). An approval would be the strongest precedent yet for the junction-vaccine route. Read the primary data before citing.
- **Fusion-breakpoint vaccine precedent:** a case report of a durable clinical and immunologic response to an off-the-shelf EWSR1-FLI1 peptide vaccine in metastatic Ewing sarcoma (PMC13452805, 8/8). One patient, but the closest fusion-sarcoma analogue to the EWSR1::NR4A3 junction idea. A personalized mRNA vaccine trial in refractory rhabdomyosarcoma (NCT06932861) was updated 10/26, still recruiting.
- **Sarcoma:** Iovance reports about a 50% response rate for lifileucel (TIL) in refractory UPS and liposarcoma (press, 10/26–28). Not EMC; the registry sweep found no EMC or fusion-sarcoma trial recruiting.
- **Degraders:** an AACR item (10/18) reports a first-in-class protein degrader approved for ESR1-mutated breast cancer. It supports the modality, but the target is ESR1, not NR4A3.
- **Oligonucleotides:** nothing in solid tumours; the Merck KGaA–Silence deal (blood cancer) is unchanged from last week.
- Quiet: no new tool or model release, no individual-eligible funding that fits, and the literature hits were noise.
