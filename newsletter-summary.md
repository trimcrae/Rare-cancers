# NR4A3 method-watch — week of November 20

The big item this week is reported Phase 3 survival data for a cancer vaccine. The news items below are press leads, not confirmed results; read the primary sources before citing any of them.

- **SELLAS cancer vaccine reportedly improves overall survival in a Phase 3 AML trial** (BioPharm International, 11/17; Cancer Health, 11/19). If confirmed, this is a Phase 3 survival win for a peptide vaccine. That is precedent for the junction-vaccine route, though this vaccine is off-the-shelf, not individualized.
- **Vepdegestrant (PROTAC) Phase 3: results now posted on ClinicalTrials.gov** (Pfizer, NCT05654623, updated 11/18). This is the registry record for a degrader's pivotal readout, so it is clinical precedent for the degrader route. Also reported: a second molecular-glue IND for Evotec/BMS, and early tumour shrinkage for ERAS-0015 in Phase I.
- **EWSR1-FLI1 peptide vaccine: durable response in metastatic Ewing sarcoma** (PMC13452805, August). This is a fusion-breakpoint vaccine in a FET sarcoma, the closest precedent the junction-vaccine route has. Check whether the vaccine manuscript cites it.
- **First siRNA approved for myasthenia gravis** (Regeneron's Livsayis; MedPage Today and others, 11/19). It is not a solid tumour, so it does nothing for the ASO route's delivery gate.
- Other cancer news, none of it touching your routes: the FDA reportedly rejected ivonescimab in lung cancer, and the oral SERD giredestrant (Roche's Wilserdly) was reportedly approved in breast cancer.
- No new tool releases and no funding open to individuals that fits. The direct NR4A3/EMC literature query was **unread** this week (Europe PMC returned a 503 error).
