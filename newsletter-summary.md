# NR4A3 method-watch — week of August 24

**This week: real clinical movement, twice over.** Two readouts land directly on route precedent — worth a look.

- **Melanoma neoantigen vaccine hits Phase 3.** Merck/Moderna's intismeran autogene (V940) + pembrolizumab met its primary endpoint in resected high-risk melanoma (recurrence-free survival) — reported by 10+ outlets as the first personalized cancer vaccine to clear a Phase 3. Press-release only, no primary data yet, but this is direct precedent for the EWSR1::NR4A3 junction-vaccine route's modality.
- **Two degrader-class drugs crossed real clinical milestones.** BMS's CELMoD (ZENBEXUS) got accelerated FDA approval in multiple myeloma — a debut for that drug class — and Rigel's vepdegestrant (VEPPANU), a bona fide PROTAC, is now commercially available for ER+/HER2- breast cancer. Both are precedent for the NR4A3 degrader route reaching the clinic, though neither targets NR4A3 itself.
- **ASO/solid-tumor and sarcoma/EMC watch: quiet.** No pivotal ASO/siRNA solid-tumor readout; one unrelated EMC case report only.
- **BioEmu v1.4.0 shipped** — the cheap generative-ensemble tool IDEAS.md names as the cryptic-pocket-atlas enabler.
- **Funding: nothing newly open to a solo researcher.** Listed grants (ONR YIP, NSF solicitations, DURIP) still read as institutional/faculty-eligibility, not individual.

No EMC-specific advance this week; nothing else material in literature or tools.
