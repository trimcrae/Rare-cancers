# NR4A3 method-watch — week of November 13

Two approvals this week land in modalities your routes use, and one new model is directly runnable. News items are press leads, not confirmed; read the primary notices before citing.

- **Iberdomide approved in multiple myeloma** (FDA; Cleveland Clinic, 11/12). A CELMoD/molecular-glue degrader clearing approval is the degrader route's clinical precedent — it bears on NR4A3 degradability as a class, not on the transcription-factor target itself.
- **Pimicotinib (Pimjetmi) approved for tenosynovial giant cell tumour** (Targeted Oncology / Pharmaceutical Executive, 11/11–11/12). A soft-tissue tumour approval; useful as a rare-sarcoma regulatory precedent, no route read-across.
- **GENESIS: single-cell perturbation prediction near experimental reproducibility** (PMC13734124, 11/1). The most actionable item here — if it holds, it is the virtual-cell tool for the EWSR1::NR4A3 fusion-dependence test the degrader route turns on.
- **EVX-01 plus pembrolizumab shows neoantigen-specific T-cell responses in advanced melanoma** (OncLive, 11/7). Continued immunogenicity support for the junction-vaccine premise; a response readout, not an outcome.
- **AlphaFold3 shipped v3.0.5** (10/30) — worth a rerun if the ternary-complex modelling comes back up.
- Quiet elsewhere: no EMC or fusion-sarcoma trial newly recruiting, no direct NR4A3 advance (the only hit is a case report), and no newly-opened individual-eligible funding that fits.
