Where the off‑targets are expressed

Both junction gapmers in the ASO panel carry an off-target load of near-matches. This asks the question the five sequence screens cannot: is the transcript carrying each near-match actually present in a tissue the drug reaches?

Every hit behind this page sits at 14/16 identity — two mismatches in a 16‑mer, the loosest class the screen admits. Whether such a duplex is an RNase‑H1 substrate at all is an affinity question, and no screen here and no expression value anywhere answers it.

So expression is a necessary condition for an off-target effect and never a sufficient one. Every figure below is evidence about a gene, not about an oligonucleotide. There is no risk column on this page, deliberately: the step from “this gene is expressed in liver” to “this reagent does something in liver” needs an argument nothing here supplies.

Two compartments, held apart

A gene can be high in a dosed organ and absent on site, or the reverse. The two questions are never summed, averaged, or reduced to one score anywhere in this work.

Exposure — liver and kidney

Systemically dosed phosphorothioate gapmers distribute predominantly to liver and kidney, so these organs carry the exposure question. Read as GTEx v8 median TPM in Liver, Kidney Cortex and Kidney Medulla.

On site — the tumour compartment

EMC arises in deep soft tissue of the extremities with a myxoid stroma. No reference atlas contains that tumour, so normal soft tissue is a labelled proxy — and the real tumour reading sits beside it: array percentile in archival EMC.

Did the instrument work?

known-answer controls

A shifted column in a wide expression matrix produces an artifact whose numbers are internally consistent and completely wrong. Three genes with textbook tissue restriction are carried through the same parse as every locus below; if any lands in the wrong tissue, the run emits no exposure figure at all.

  • ALB → Liver 25,201 TPM PASS
  • UMOD → Kidney Medulla 2,116 TPM PASS
  • MYH7 → Heart Left Ventricle 4,514 TPM PASS

This gate has already earned its place: an earlier run fetched perfectly and still withheld every figure, because a label mismatch (Kidney Medulla against Kidney - Medulla) failed two of three controls. A false negative on a safety gate is the cheap direction of being wrong.

Two uncharacterised loci

LOC105374140 and LOC105370997 carry 9 records between them and return nothing from three independent instruments — no GENCODE gene model, zero HPA rows, no array probe on either platform. Their exposure question is not answered here, and no absence of data is written up as absence of expression.

Registers counts how many of the five tiling designs at this seam return the locus. It is a second axis and not the record count: a locus every register returns is there wherever the window is put, while one register may be a property of that window. Neither may stand in for the other.

both compartments

NRP1

The only locus returned by all five registers, on just five transcript records — and it reads positive on both axes: 6.6–17.8 TPM across liver and kidney, 104.7 in fibroblasts, and the highest EMC array percentile in the panel at 0.96. Record count would have buried it at rank eleven of seventeen.

exposure-concentrated

SLC17A3

Its whole-body top three tissues are, in order, Kidney Cortex, Liver and Kidney Medulla — a urate transporter essentially restricted to the two organs a systemic phosphorothioate distributes to. On site it is near the bottom of the array distribution (0.17). Four records, two registers.

on site only

LAMA4

Low across the exposure organs (1.3–6.1 TPM) and the highest soft-tissue value in the whole panel at 268.6 in fibroblasts, 132.8 in tibial nerve, with an EMC array percentile of 0.95. The exact case the compartment split exists to keep visible.

clean on exposure

ZFPM2

Flagged as a priority for recurring across registers, and the exposure column does not support that framing: 0.4–0.6 TPM in all three organs, with its whole-body maximum in cerebellum (39.2) and ovary (27.0). Three of five registers, but not an exposure-organ transcript.

What public data cannot settle

Seven of twenty-three loci carry no exposure reading at all. Six are uncharacterised or non-coding entries with no GENCODE gene model, no Human Protein Atlas record and no probe on either EMC array platform; one is a readthrough transcript. Three independent instruments returning nothing is not a measurement of absence — it is the absence of a measurement, and it is reported as such on every row.

  • The affinity question is untouched. Nothing here re-aligned a hit, computed duplex stability, or distinguished these two-mismatch matches from one another. A gene being expressed is not a predicted cleavage event.
  • The tumour compartment has no atlas. GTEx contains no EMC and no sarcoma of any kind, so the soft-tissue column is normal tissue of the right anatomical region, and the only genuine tumour reading is an array percentile in six and ten archival tumours.
  • Both array platforms are probe-limited. Where a probe does not map, the read could not be taken — on GPL3290 that includes ZNF667, CHST5 and GBP4, none of which is thereby silent in EMC.
  • Nothing on this page is an efficacy, selectivity, safety, therapeutic-window or clinical-readiness statement about any sequence.

How it was measured

arm A · exposure

GTEx v8

Gene-level median TPM across 54 tissues. Liver and kidney read from the same matrix as every other tissue, so the comparison sits inside one instrument.

arm B · on site

GSE24369 · GSE4303

The two readable EMC series, read through the existing panels pipeline so this cannot disagree with it about what a probe mapping is. Reported as within-array percentile.

arm C · identity

NCBI Gene

What each locus is, so an absence from the expression atlases can be attributed to an uncharacterised NCBI-only locus rather than left as an unexplained gap.

arm D · cross-check

Human Protein Atlas

Tissue-specificity calls and consensus nTPM. Not independent of GTEx — HPA's consensus incorporates it — so agreement is a transport check, not replication.

Corroboration — two GTEx endpoints, same numbers

The exposure column was read twice by different routes: once from the GTEx portal API and once from the published v8 release matrix, in separate runs. Across all 57 gene × exposure-tissue pairs the two agree to floating-point precision — worst relative difference 2×10−14 %. That rules out a transport or parsing artifact in these figures. It says nothing about GTEx itself being right, and it is not a second measurement: it is the same underlying release reached two ways.

Locus sets are derived from the committed deep screens rather than typed, at the screen's own gap-paired class.
